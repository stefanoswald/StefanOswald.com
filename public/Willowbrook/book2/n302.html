Night 302

The Coming Home Opposite

Month 10: Opposites

Morning. The door.

Out: coat, hat, mittens on their string, boots. Zip.

Cold in the nose. The step: crunch.

Bye, house.

The lane. The library. The bakery smell. Rosa's wave through the glass.

Evening. The same door.

In: warm in the nose. Lamp. Soup.

Papa's face does the thing it does when she comes in.

"Boots off," says Mama. "Then on the mat."

Boot one, tug. Boot two, tug. Both on the mat.

Two things, in order. Done.

Coat on the low hook. Her hook.

"Home."

Out is the whole town.

In is this.

The boots drip on the mat, snow going to water, a small puddle, all evening.
