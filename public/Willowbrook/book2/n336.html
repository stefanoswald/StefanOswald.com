Night 336

The Pajama Parade

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Two pajamas on the bed.

The star ones. The duck ones.

"Which?"

Olivia pats. "Dis one."

Stars. Stars win most nights.

Now the parade.

Leg one... in!

Leg two... in!

Arms up. Through the tunnel...

Where is Olivia?

Where did she go?

Pop! Head out. There she is!

Every night, the same surprise.

Every night, still funny.

Last: the zipper.

Mama starts it.

Olivia finishes. Zzzzip. To the chin.

The duck ones go back in the drawer.

"Tomorrow," Mama tells them.

The ducks don't mind.

They have waited before.
