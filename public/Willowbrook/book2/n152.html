Night 152

The Banana Opens

Month 6: Yum

The banana wears a coat.

A yellow one.

To eat the banana,

The coat must come off.

Mama starts it. Crack!

Now Olivia pulls.

One strip... two strips...

Down they fold, like petals.

And inside: the banana!

Pale and sweet and ready.

It was wrapped up the whole time.

"Nana," says Olivia.

Bite. Sweet.

Banana on the chin. Banana on the wrist.

Banana goes everywhere.

The empty coat lies on the tray.

Olivia pats it flat.

"Bye," she says. To the coat.
