Night 154

Warm Soup Night

Month 6: Yum

Rain on the window. Soup on the stove.

It rained all afternoon,

So the pot cooked all afternoon.

Now: bowls!

A big one for Papa.

A middle one for Mama.

A little one for Olivia.

Careful, warm.

Blow on it first. Whoo, whoo.

Now hunt with the spoon.

Dip... a carrot! Got it!

Dip... nothing. Try again.

Dip... a potato! Got it!

Some soup rides the spoon up.

Some soup goes back to the bowl.

Some soup is on Olivia.

The spoon is new. It is trying.

The broth at the bottom:

Tip the bowl, Mama helps.

Warm all the way down.

Outside, still raining.

Inside, three empty bowls.
