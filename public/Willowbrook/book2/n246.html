Night 246

Three Bears at Bedtime

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Tonight we count to three.

We need three things.

Bears. Line them up on the blanket.

Bobby Bear: one. Tap his head.

The little bath bear: two. Tap.

The... cow?

The plush cow from Grandpa's.

Is a cow a bear?

Olivia looks at the cow for a while.

"Cow bear."

Tonight, yes. A cow bear.

Three!

One, two, three bears.

Count them again, the other way.

Cow, bath bear, Bobby.

Still three. Same bears.

Three heads to pat goodnight.

Pat, pat, pat.

Then a fourth head, curly.

Mama pats that one.

"Nigh bears."

The cow stays a bear till morning.
