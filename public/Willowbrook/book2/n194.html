Night 194

The Swing Goes Up

Month 7: Outside!

Into the bucket swing.

Legs through. Hands on.

Ready?

Push!

Up goes Olivia...

Eeek, says the chain.

Back she comes...

Hello, Mama's face!

Up again. The trees! The sky!

"Sky!"

Back again. Mama.

Sky, Mama, sky, Mama.

The tummy does a little flip at the top.

Then Mama grabs the swing. Stops it.

"All done. Home."

Too fast. Olivia arches. Wails.

Mama's hand goes soft.

"I stopped it with no warning. Three more. Then home."

One. Sky. Two. Mama. Three. Sky.

Out she comes.

The swing swings empty.

Slower. Slower. Still.
