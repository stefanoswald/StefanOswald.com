Night 286

Happy and Sad

Month 10: Opposites

Blocks. Red ones, blue ones.

Olivia piles them. Red ones, blue ones, all in.

Then a tower. Red, blue, red. Three high. Four.

Wobble.

Crash. All of it.

Her face goes still. Then it folds.

Crying. The hot kind.

Mama gets low. She does not build.

"The tower fell. You're sad."

"Sad," says Olivia. Wet.

Mama's hand on her back. The crying goes on a while. Mama lets it.

Then smaller. Hiccup. Sniff.

Olivia picks up a red block. Puts it down. Picks up a blue one.

Red. Blue. Red.

Three high.

She looks at Mama. A small smile, just starting.

"Happy?" says Mama.

Olivia thinks about it.

"Sad," she says, and puts the fourth block on.
