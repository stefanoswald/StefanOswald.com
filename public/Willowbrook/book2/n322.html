Night 322

The Shape Hunt in the Snow

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Fresh snow. A whole blank yard.

Boot print: stamp. A little rectangle with ridges.

Stamp, stamp, stamp. A row of them.

"Papa foot," says Olivia, at the big one next to hers.

Mitten print: a mitten shape.

Now the big stick. Papa drags it, walking a ring around Olivia.

A circle. The biggest one in the yard.

"Big circle."

Three steps this way. Three steps that way. Three steps back.

A triangle, made of feet.

Noses red. Fingers cold.

"More," says Olivia.

One more. A wobbly line to the back door.

Inside, boots off, the window shows it all: row, circle, triangle, line.
