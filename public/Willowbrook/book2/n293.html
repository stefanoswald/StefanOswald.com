Night 293

Empty Lane, Full House

Month 10: Opposites

Look out.

The lane: empty. Blue. Snow on every porch and nobody on them. Even the gold dog is in.

Mrs. Henderson's window: one square of yellow. She waves. Olivia waves.

Now look in.

The house: full.

Lamp. Soup. Papa stirring. Mama's slippers. The bear on his little chair.

People: Olivia points. Papa. Mama. Then she pats her own front.

"Livy."

Three.

Empty out there. Full in here.

Snow can have the lane.

Mama pulls the curtain. The lane goes away.

Three bowls. Papa's spoon: big. Her spoon: little. Same soup.

Steam off all three.
