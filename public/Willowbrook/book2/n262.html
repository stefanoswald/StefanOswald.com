Night 262

The Domino Line

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Papa is building something odd.

Blocks. Standing up. In a line.

Not a tower. A line.

One, two, three, four, five.

Five blocks, all standing.

"Ready?" says Papa. "Just a tiny push on the first one."

Push.

Click.

Click-click-click-click.

They ALL fell.

One push. Five falls. The falling ran down the line.

"'Gain!"

Stand them up. Slowly. Papa's hands, then hers.

Four standing...

The cat walks through.

Click-click-click-click.

The cat did the push.

The cat leaves. The cat has done enough.

Stand them up again.

This time Olivia pushes before Papa says ready.

Click-click-click-click-click.

Same sound. Still works.

Papa stands one block up, alone.

Olivia leaves it standing.

Just the one.
