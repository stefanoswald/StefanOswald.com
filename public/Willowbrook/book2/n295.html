Night 295

Awake and Asleep

Month 10: Opposites

Awake: eyes open. One more page. One more wiggle. One more "More?"

Asleep: not yet.

In between: the yawn.

Ahh-hum.

Mama yawns too. Yawns go around.

Olivia yawns again, bigger. "Ahh-humm."

The bear's ear, in her hand.

Eyes at half.

"Nigh-nigh," from the crib, small.

"Nigh-nigh," from the chair.

Wiggle. Smaller wiggle. Toes still.

Breath going slow and long.

Awake on this side. Asleep on that side.

And Olivia... over.

Mama stays one more minute. In case.

Then the chair creaks, and the door is left open a crack.
