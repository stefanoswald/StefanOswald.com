Night 330

The Shapes Go to Sleep

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Bedtime for the shapes.

The circle piece: click. Home in its round bed.

The square: click.

The triangle: click, after a turn.

The blocks go in the box, squares with squares.

"Nigh-nigh, bock," says Olivia.

The ball rolls to its basket. It gets a nudge. Balls dawdle.

The star cutter, the one Rosa sent home with her.

That one stays out on the sill, facing the window, where the real one can see it.

They like to sleep in sight of each other.

"Star stay," says Olivia.

Star stays.

All shapes home. All corners resting. All circles still.

And in the crib, one more shape: a small warm curl, with a bear in the bend of it.
