Night 355

The Birthday List

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

The birthday is close.

Time to plan.

Mama has paper. Papa has coffee.

"What do we need?"

"CAKE!"

(Written down. Rosa's.)

"WIWI!"

(Lily. First guest. Written.)

"BOON!"

(Balloons. Red. Written.)

"Duck?"

(The pond ducks. Mama writes it down.

Papa raises an eyebrow. Mama writes it anyway.)

Grandpa Joe. The aunts. Rosa.

Mateo, from two porches down.

The same names as last year,

Plus a few.

"Anything else?"

Olivia thinks hard.

"More."

Mama writes it down.

Papa reads the list back.

"Cake, Lily, balloons, ducks, more."

It is a good list.
