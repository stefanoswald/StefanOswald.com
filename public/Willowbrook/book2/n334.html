Night 334

The Shape of the Month

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Last one. Quiet.

Mama at the crib. The curtain open a hand's width.

Out there, over the roofs: the Moon.

Not half. Not a thin boat.

Round. All of her tonight, like a plate.

"Big moon," says Olivia.

Big moon. The same one. The whole of her came round.

And beside her, small and gold: Stella. Five points, if you look hard.

Olivia looks hard.

In the crib: the bear, the heart on his foot.

On the sill: the star cutter.

Downstairs, on the fridge: the hand with the bump.

Olivia's eyes go to the window. Close. Open. Close.

"Mmm," hums the round Moon.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
