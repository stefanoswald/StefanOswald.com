Night 32

Bobby Bear Learns to Share

Month 2: Animal Friends

When Olivia sleeps, Bobby Bear dreams.

Olivia is asleep. Olivia is watching.

Dreams are like that.

In the dream: a berry.

One red berry. Just one.

A little bird hops close. Hop, hop.

The bird looks at the berry.

Bobby Bear looks at the bird.

One berry. Two friends. Hmm.

Bobby Bear holds it out.

The bird takes a peck.

The bear takes a bite.

Bird's turn. Bear's turn.

Bird's turn. Bear's turn.

Bird's turn. No berry.

The bird sings a small song.

Bobby Bear dreams it all night long.

Olivia's eyes stay shut.

She sees it anyway.
