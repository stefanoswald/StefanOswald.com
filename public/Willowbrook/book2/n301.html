Night 301

Inside Voice, Outside Voice

Month 10: Opposites

The park. Snow on everything. Nobody else. Crows.

"Outside voice," says Mama. "Ready?"

Ready.

"HELLOOOO!"

It rolls across the snow. The crows go up.

Olivia's turn.

"HI!"

Not big yet. Again.

"HIIII!"

Bigger. The crows come back to look.

"WIWIII!"

Lily is not here. The name goes out over the snow anyway.

Mama does one more. The whole park hears it. Probably the farm too.

Home. Boots off. Mama's finger to her lips.

"Inside voice."

"hi," says Olivia. Tiny.

"hi," says Mama.

The outside voice stays on the porch, in the snow, till tomorrow.

Whispers all the way to supper. "soup." "bear." "more."

Then Papa comes in and says HELLO in his outside voice, and everybody shushes him.
