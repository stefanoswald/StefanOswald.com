Night 356

Two Candles Wait

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

On the shelf: a little box.

Inside: candles.

Olivia grabs.

"No!" says Mama. Too fast. Too loud.

Olivia's lip goes.

Mama kneels. "That was loud. Sorry.

Candles are for the cake, but you can hold the box."

The box is fine. The box is great.

Last year: one candle.

One candle for one year.

This year, count them out.

One...

"Two!" says Olivia. Two fingers up.

Two candles. Two years of her.

Two springs. Two summers.

They go in the box, side by side.

Olivia closes the lid.

"Soon," she tells the box.

The box goes back on the shelf.

Higher this time.
