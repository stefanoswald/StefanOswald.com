Night 311

The Egg's Special Shape

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Grandpa's kitchen. The stove is going. Out the window, the barn roof is white.

On the table, one brown egg. Henrietta's. Just laid.

Olivia holds it in both hands.

"Egg."

Not a circle. One end rounder. One end pointier.

Grandpa Joe rolls it across the table. Wub, wub, wub.

It doesn't roll away. It curves. Round in a circle and back to his hand.

Olivia's eyes go wide.

"'Gain."

He rolls it again. Round and back.

Olivia rolls it. Round, and it bumps the cup. Tok.

"Egg home," she says.

Grandpa Joe winks.

"Still warm," he says.

She puts it against her cheek.

It is.

Then hands at the sink. Egg hands, off.
