Night 204

The Caterpillar on the Fence

Month 7: Outside!

On the fence rail: a traveler.

Small. Striped. Fuzzy.

A caterpillar!

Inch... scrunch... inch...

That is how caterpillars walk.

Scrunch and stretch. Scrunch and stretch.

We look. We don't touch the fuzzy ones.

Olivia follows the whole journey.

Fence post to fence post.

A long way, at caterpillar speed.

"You know a secret?" says Mama.

"Someday this little one gets wings."

Wings. Like Bella.

Olivia looks up. No wings up there yet.

Inch on, little traveler.

Off the end of the rail,

Into the leaves.
