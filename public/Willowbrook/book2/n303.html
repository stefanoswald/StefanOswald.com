Night 303

Little Word, Big Word

Month 10: Opposites

Little words. She has a lot now.

"Hi." "No." "Up." "Ta." "Mine."

Pebbles. "No" is the hardest pebble.

Tonight, at the lamp, Mama remembers the month, and Olivia puts the pebbles in.

The boot inside the boot. "Boot gone."

The swing, arms up. "Down."

The bowl of snow, going to water. "Cold."

The tower on the rug. "Sad." And the fourth block.

The coat and the door. "Mama back."

The blue block, and Lily. "Livy turn."

The laundry basket. "Dere!"

Then the big word. From the bear book, the page with the wings.

"Buttahfly."

Three pieces, carried with both hands, like the pail.

Mama says it back, whole, and turns the lamp off.

In the dark, small: "Buttahfly."
