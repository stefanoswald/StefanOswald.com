Night 364

From Two to Three Begins Tomorrow

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Look at the shelf.

This book, our book, is almost done.

Papa tells it back. He gets things wrong.

"You called from the crib and Mama turned on ALL the lights."

"No!" says Olivia.

No lights. Just Mama. Just the hum.

"Papa said I'll be back, and then he never came."

"Papa back!"

He did. Every time.

"The milk spilled and the plow never came."

"Da TRUCK!"

"The candles. Mama blew them out."

"Livy!"

Herself. Twice.

"And the balloon went down."

"Up!"

Up. Gone gold.

Papa gives up being wrong.

Beside our book, another one.

"From Two to Three."

Pat the old book. "Ta, book."

Pat the new one. "Hi, book."

One more night in this one.
