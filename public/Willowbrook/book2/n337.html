Night 337

Checking on Everyone

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Before bed: the rounds.

Olivia leads. Mama follows.

Bobby Bear? In the crib. Ear up.

"Bear." Check.

The bath bear? Dry on his shelf. Check.

The plant? Still green. Check.

The sill museum?

Rock. Leaf. Feather. Shell.

She touches each one.

All there. Check.

The window?

Snow on the lane, blue in the dark.

Porch lights: on.

The gold dog: gone in.

"Dog gone," says Olivia.

Check.

Star: there. Moon: up.

Everybody where they belong.

One more.

"Livy?"

Mama lifts her.

Into the crib, next to the ear.

"Check," says Mama.

The blanket goes over both of them.
