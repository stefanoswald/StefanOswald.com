Night 251

Counting the Chickens

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Dusk at the farm. Early now.

The grass crunches. First frost coming.

The hens go in to roost.

Count them in with Grandpa.

"One!" In hops a red one.

"Two!" The speckled one.

"Two!" Another red one.

"Two!" Henrietta, bok, announcing herself.

Every hen after two is two.

Grandpa Joe does not correct this.

And last, taking her time,

Pausing at the door for effect:

The Duchess.

"Two!"

The Duchess considers it. Goes in.

"Six," says Grandpa.

That is all he says tonight.

He shuts the door. The latch. The wink.

Inside, a rustle, a hmph, and quiet.

Everybody home.

Olivia pats the door.

"Nigh hens."
