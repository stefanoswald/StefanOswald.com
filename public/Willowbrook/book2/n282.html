Night 282

Day and Night

Month 10: Opposites

The window. Afternoon, and already going.

Day: the lane. The snow. The gold dog on his route. Mrs. Henderson's blue car.

Then grey. Then blue. Then bluer.

Lights come on. Porch. Porch. Porch. Mrs. Henderson's kitchen, one yellow square.

Night comes early this month. Before supper.

Olivia's nose on the glass. Fog. Wipe.

"Dark."

"Dark," says Mama. "Supper in the dark tonight."

First star. Point.

"Della."

Inside: lamp, soup smell, warm.

Outside: blue, snow, nobody.

"Bye day."

Olivia waves at the window.

In the dark glass, a small girl waves back.
