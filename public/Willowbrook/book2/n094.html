Night 94

Green

Month 4: A World of Colors

Green is the growing color.

The grass: green.

The leaves: green.

The brook bank: green, green, green.

Olivia lies down in it.

Mama lies down too.

Between Olivia and the water.

Green tickles her ears.

And look. Something greener.

A frog. THE frog.

Freddy, bright as a new leaf.

"GREEN?" says Freddy. "I INVENTED IT."

He did not.

But he wears it best.

Hop. Plop. Gone.

Green into green.

The whole summer is made of it.

Olivia looks at the grass where he was.

Still green.
