Night 248

One Duck, Two Ducks, All the Ducks

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

The pond is cold now.

The ducks don't mind.

Mama's hand holds the back of Olivia's coat.

Let's count the ducks.

"One duck!" There he is.

"Two duck!" Got them.

"Free..."

The third one swims behind the first one.

Start over.

"One!"

"Two!"

"Fr..."

Now they're all moving.

Ducks will not hold still. It is not in them.

They mix. They dive. One is gone.

Up he pops, somewhere else.

Mama tries. "One, two, three, four, fi..."

Mama gives up too.

Olivia spreads her arms as wide as they go.

"Lots!"

Lots of ducks.

Overhead, a long V of geese goes honking south.

Lots of geese.

"Lots," says Mama, and pulls Olivia's hat down over her ears.
