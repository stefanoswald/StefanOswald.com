Night 164

Trying the New Thing

Month 6: Yum

On the plate tonight:

The usual friends.

Bread. Cheese. Peas.

And... a new thing.

Small. Just one piece.

"Dat?" Olivia points.

"Zucchini," says Mama. "From the garden."

In August there is always more zucchini.

Everybody in Willowbrook knows this.

Olivia looks at it.

She pokes it.

She sniffs it.

Nobody says eat it.

Nobody says anything.

Mama eats hers. Gone.

Olivia picks hers up.

One lick.

Down it goes, back on the plate.

A lick counts.

The bread goes first, as usual.

The zucchini sits and watches.
