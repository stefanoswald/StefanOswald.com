Night 265

The Muffin Tin Count

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

The muffin tin has holes.

Six little rooms.

And rooms want somebody inside.

One rock, the big one from the windowsill. In a room.

One block. In a room.

One rolled-up sock. Room three.

One wooden egg. One big spoon. One mitten.

Every room: full.

"One, two," counts Olivia, and stops. Two is the top.

Mama taps the rest, singing. "Three, four, five, six."

Now: dump.

Crash-rattle.

Empty rooms.

Fill them again. Differently.

Two socks in one room this time. Squeezed.

"Two!"

The mitten gets a room of its own. It is soft and needs one.

Six rooms, full, carried carefully down the hall to show Papa.
