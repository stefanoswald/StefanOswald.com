Night 268

The Last Cracker

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Snack is nearly done.

The plate has one cracker left.

One.

Two friends.

Everyone sees it.

Lily looks at Olivia.

Olivia looks at Lily.

Somebody's hand moves.

It's Olivia's.

She picks up the cracker.

"Mine."

Bite.

Crack. Crackers break. That is what crackers do.

Half in her mouth. The other half lands on the plate.

Lily's hand.

Gone.

Munch. Munch.

Olivia looks at the plate. Empty.

She looks at Lily. Chewing.

She looks at her own hand. Crumbs.

"Ta," says Lily, to the plate.

Nobody planned any of that.

Mama brings the box, and the plate is full again.

Everybody forgets the last cracker at once.
