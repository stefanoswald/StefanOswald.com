Night 347

What the House Hears at Night

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

When everyone sleeps,

The house stays half awake.

Listen.

Tick... tick. The clock.

Crick. A beam, settling.

Houses stretch at night too.

Mmmmm. The fridge, humming to the leftovers.

Whumm. The furnace, taking its turn.

Plip... plip. Cold rain, starting.

Rain on snow. The snow will get smaller.

The house hears the family too.

Papa's snore. Rrr-shhh.

Mama's page, turning.

The cat, jumping down from somewhere she shouldn't be.

And one small sleeper's breath.

In... out... in... out...

The house hears that one best.

Plip. Tick. Rrr-shhh.

Sleep on.

The rain has the roof.
