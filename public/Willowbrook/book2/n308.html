Night 308

The Star Shape

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

The star.

Five points, going out. A little burst.

Mama draws one. Point, point, point, point, point.

"Star," says Olivia.

Now hers. The crayon goes everywhere, out the side, off the paper.

"Star," says Olivia.

Mama looks. It has eleven points and a tail.

"It has a tail," says Mama. "Stars don't usually."

It stays a star. Olivia said so.

Where else does this shape live?

The back of Grandpa's chair. Carved.

The cake, in the photo on the fridge. Gold.

And out the window, low. The gold one.

"Della!"

Olivia holds the paper to the glass, so the star can see.

Twinkle.
