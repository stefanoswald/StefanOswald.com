Night 112

The Market of Colors

Month 4: A World of Colors

Saturday market!

A rainbow you can eat.

Red strawberries, in little boxes.

Orange carrots, in a bunch.

Green peas, in their pods. Pop!

Purple beets, with muddy tails.

Yellow lemons, in a bowl.

Olivia points from the carrier.

"Dat!"

The strawberries.

"Dat!"

The strawberries again.

The stall lady laughs.

"She knows what she likes."

One box rides home in the bag.

One strawberry rides home in Olivia.

In little pieces.

Red on her chin. Red on Papa's shirt.

Red in the bag, for tomorrow.
