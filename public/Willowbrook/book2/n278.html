Night 278

Open and Closed

Month 10: Opposites

Her cupboard. The low one, by the sink.

Open.

"Hi pots." Hi lids. Hi funnel.

Closed.

"Bye pots."

Open. Eeee, says the door. It has a squeak. Mama keeps saying she will oil it. She does not. The squeak is part of the game.

Closed. Eeee.

Eyes do it too.

Mama's eyes: closed. Olivia pats them. Open! "Boo."

Olivia's eyes: closed. Squeezed. One of them peeking, a little.

Open. Mama is still there.

Open, eeee, closed, eeee.

Supper time. "Last open," says Mama. "Then closed till morning."

Open. Bye pots. Bye lids.

Closed. Click.

The funnel stayed out. It is coming to supper.
