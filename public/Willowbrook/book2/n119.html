Night 119

Every Color Home

Month 4: A World of Colors

Bath done. Pink cheeks.

Now, before bed, the color round.

"Red?" says Papa.

Olivia points. The cup, on the table.

"Yellow?"

The blanket, in the crib.

"Green?"

The rug, under her feet.

"Orange?"

Olivia points at the door.

The marigolds are out there, on the step.

Mama says, "Brown?"

Bobby Bear, standing by.

"Blue?"

Lily's block. She left it. Again.

Purple? The lilac smell, nearly gone now.

Grey? That was the rainy day. It left.

The lamp: gold. It glows.

Outside the window: black sky, white stars.

Every color made it home.

Sometimes they visit the sky.

Then they come back here.

Olivia, in the middle of all of them.
