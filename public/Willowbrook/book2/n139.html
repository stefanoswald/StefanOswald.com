Night 139

The Tickle Map

Month 5: All About Me

The body has secret buttons.

Tickle buttons.

Papa knows the map.

The ribs? Tickle-tickle.

Ha ha! Found one.

The neck? Tickle.

Hee hee! Another.

The feet? The FEET?

HA HA HA! The best one.

"No!"

Papa's hands stop. Right away.

Up in the air, where she can see them.

Olivia breathes. Wipes her eyes.

"'Gain."

Tickle-tickle.

Hee hee!

"No."

Stop. Hands up.

Now her turn. Papa's map.

Under the chin. In the beard.

"HO HO." Found it.

"Stop," says Papa.

She stops.

Mostly.
