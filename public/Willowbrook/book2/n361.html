Night 361

The Balloon Went Up Again

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

The party is over. The chairs are stacked.

Grandpa Joe stayed to stack them.

Now he sits on the porch step, hat on.

One balloon is left. Red. Long string.

Olivia holds it.

It tugs. Gentle. It wants up.

Mama, at the door: "Keep it? Or let it fly?"

Olivia looks at Grandpa.

Grandpa looks at the balloon.

That is all he does.

She holds it close. One second. Two.

Then...

Open fingers.

Up.

Red against the dusk.

Over the lane. Over the roofs.

Toward the willow, where the evening goes gold.

"Bye-bye!" Both arms.

"Yep," says Grandpa Joe.

The string is gone from her fingers.

Her hand stays open a while.
