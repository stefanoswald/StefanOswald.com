Night 219

The Brook Wade

Month 8: Splash

The wading day.

Boots on. Red ones.

The brook is cool now.

Leaves ride by on it, little yellow boats.

One hand tight in Papa's.

"WELCOME!" yells someone green from a rock.

"I TAUGHT HER EVERYTHING!"

He did not. But he is a very good yeller.

Step...

IN.

Ooh! The boots go dark.

Cold comes right through them.

"Cold!" says Olivia.

"Cold," says Papa. "Cold and moving."

The water pulls at the boots, gentle.

Another step. Pebbles wobble.

Papa's hand holds.

The brook hurries on around her,

Like it has somewhere to be.

Out.

Two boots dripping on the bank.

The hand does not let go yet.
