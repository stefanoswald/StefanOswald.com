Night 331

Ellie Is Every Shape

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Sunday. The farm, white to the fence line.

And out by the far fence: Ellie.

She does not think much of snow.

Her feet: big round circles.

"Big foot," says Olivia.

Her ears: grey triangles, going fwump, fwump, slow.

Her trunk: a curl. A spiral, like the shell on the sill.

Her tail: one line, one red circle.

"Ellie!"

The trunk lifts. Curls. Reaches over the fence.

It takes Grandpa Joe's hat right off his head.

Grandpa Joe holds out his hand.

The hat comes back. Snow in it.

"She's been waiting on you since the frost," he says, and puts it on, snow and all.

The red circle bobs.
