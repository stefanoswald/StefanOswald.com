Night 284

New and Old

Month 10: Opposites

A box came, from the aunts. Inside: a rabbit. New.

Stiff ears. Bright fur. Smells like the box.

"Bunny."

Bobby Bear watches from the crib. Old. Fur gone soft. One ear flopped.

Olivia sits them side by side and looks at both for a long time.

Rabbit: pat. Bear: pat.

Pretend supper. The rabbit gets a spoon. "Mmm." The bear gets a spoon. "Mmm."

The rabbit gets the little chair. The bear gets the lap.

Bedtime. Mama holds them up, one in each hand.

"Bear."

The rabbit goes on the shelf, where he can see everything. His first night.

In the crib: one bear, one floppy ear, one hand holding it.

The rabbit's ears stick straight up all night.
