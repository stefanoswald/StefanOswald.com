Night 304

The Opposite of Alone

Month 10: Opposites

One more opposite. The last one this month.

Alone: one. The box with the flaps shut. The tower, built by one girl. Fine, for a while.

The opposite of alone, tonight:

A girl.

A bear in her arm, one ear held.

Mama's hand on the blanket.

Papa in the doorway, leaning.

The tiny moon on the wall.

Stella at the window, low, looking in.

The lane outside, porch lights going out. One. One. One.

Snow coming down on all of it, slow.

Stella twinkles once.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
