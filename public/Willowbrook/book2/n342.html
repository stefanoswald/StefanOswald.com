Night 342

Bobby Bear Goes First

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Bobby Bear goes first.

Olivia does it. All of it.

Bath: a wipe with the cloth. "Eyes."

Pajamas: he has none. Pretend. Zzzip.

Milk: the little red cup, empty.

He drinks it anyway. "Mmm."

Teeth: scrub-scrub, on his snout.

Story: she opens the bear book. Upside down.

She turns the pages. All of them. Fast.

Song: "Nigh-nigh, nigh-nigh."

Then the blanket. Over him. Pat, pat.

"Shh," she tells him.

Mama watches from the chair.

Not one word. It is not her turn.

Now Olivia.

Bath, pajamas, milk, teeth.

Story, right side up.

Song, with the cow in it.

Blanket. Pat, pat.

Mama's turn now.

"Shh," Mama tells her.
