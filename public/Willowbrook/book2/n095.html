Night 95

Orange

Month 4: A World of Colors

Papa's turn to pick a color.

"Orange," says Papa.

Where is orange? The kitchen has it.

The oranges in the bowl: orange. Of course.

The carrots in the fridge: orange, with green hair.

The marigolds on the step: orange.

Olivia holds the carrots by their hair.

It tickles her chin.

"Mine," says Olivia.

Papa peels one. Two. Three.

Into the pot. Bubble, bubble.

Soft, soft.

Olivia eats them with her fingers.

Orange was her first mash, long ago.

She wore most of it.

Orange on her chin now.

Orange on the step, in its pot.
