Night 187

The Wind Plays Tag

Month 7: Outside!

Someone is playing with Olivia.

Someone she can't see.

It lifts her hair. Whoosh!

It tugs her shirt.

It rolls a leaf right past her feet.

Chase it!

The leaf stops.

Reach down...

Whoosh! Away it goes.

It takes Papa's hat next.

"Gone!"

Papa runs. The hat runs faster.

Olivia laughs so hard she sits down.

The wind is playing tag.

The wind always wins.

Lean on it. Lean!

It holds you up. A little.

Papa comes back with the hat.

Hair everywhere. His and hers.
