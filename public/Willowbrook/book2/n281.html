Night 281

Full and Empty

Month 10: Opposites

The little jug. The red cup.

"Livy pour."

Mama pours instead. Quick. Full to the top. Done.

Olivia's mouth opens. No sound yet. Then a big one.

Mama looks at the jug in her own hand.

"That was your pour. Sorry."

She tips the cup back into the jug. Empty again. She hands the jug over.

Pour. Wobble. Splash on the table.

Some in the cup. Half full. Half is plenty.

Drink. Glug, glug, glug.

Empty. Tip it: nothing. Look in: nothing. Nothing is funny, every time.

"All gone."

Where did it go? Pat the tummy. In there.

Jug: empty. Cup: empty. Girl: full.

Table: wet. Olivia gets the cloth and wipes it, mostly.
