Night 300

Three Hundred Nights of Both

Month 10: Opposites

Three hundred nights. This book. This lap.

Papa's lap tonight. Mama in the doorway.

The book is thick. The read pages have gone soft at the corner.

The girl on the lap:

Big. Big to the baby picture on the fridge.

Little. Little to Papa's boot by the door, to the top mark on the door frame, which is Mama's.

Loud, all day. Quiet, now.

Fast, in the hall. Slow, on the stairs, one hand in Papa's.

Happy at supper. Sad at the tower. Happy again before the blocks were picked up.

All of it, one girl, one lap.

"More?"

One more page. Papa reads it. Mama listens from the door.

The lamp goes off.

Three hundred. Tomorrow, one more.
