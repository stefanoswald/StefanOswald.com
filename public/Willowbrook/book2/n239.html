Night 239

Brushing Teeth Is Water Play Too

Month 8: Splash

Last stop before stories:

The teeth!

The little brush. The tiny dab.

Papa's turn first. That's the deal.

Brush, brush, brush.

Front ones. Side ones.

The new back ones, way back...

Too far. Too fast.

Olivia turns her head. "No!"

Papa stops.

"Too rough. Sorry," says Papa. "Softer."

Softer. Better.

Now her turn. Brush, brush. Mostly the tongue.

Now the cup. A little water.

Swish...

And the big finish:

The SPIT.

Ptooey!

Mostly dribble.

Rinse the brush. Tap, tap.

Into its cup, standing up.

Papa has foam on his nose.

Olivia points. He leaves it there.
