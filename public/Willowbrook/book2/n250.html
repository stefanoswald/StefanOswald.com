Night 250

The Piggy Toes

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Socks off. Five toes on this foot.

And they are piggies.

This little piggy went to market.

The big toe. The bold one.

This little piggy stayed home.

He's shy.

This little piggy had roast beef.

This little piggy had none.

Poor piggy. He gets a kiss.

And THIS little piggy...

Wait for it...

Wait for it...

Went wee, wee, wee, all the way home!

Tickle-run up the leg.

Shrieks.

"More!"

Olivia lifts the other foot herself.

"Dis foot."

Five more piggies. Same market.

The last piggy on this foot is tired.

He does not run home.

He walks. Slowly. Up the leg.

And stops at the knee, for a rest.

Wee. Very small.
