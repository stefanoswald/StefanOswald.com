Night 114

The Puddle Mirror

Month 4: A World of Colors

After rain, the lane has mirrors.

Puddles!

Look down into one.

What is there?

The sky. Down in the ground!

Blue, with clouds going by.

And someone else.

A small girl with a red bow.

"Baby!" says Olivia.

Papa grins. He does not say who.

The puddle holds the baby and the sky

In the same little cup.

Stomp!

Everything wobbles. Splash.

Wait.

It all comes back.

Sky. Bow. Baby.

Stomp!

Wait.

Sky. Bow. Baby.

This could go on all morning.

It does.
