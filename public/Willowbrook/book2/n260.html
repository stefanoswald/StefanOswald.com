Night 260

The Three-Step Race

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Hallway race. Papa versus Olivia.

But Papa is big.

So: head-start rules.

Papa takes three giant steps. "One... two... three!"

He is nearly at the end already.

Ready...

Papa whistles for go. Pfff. Nothing. Papa's whistle has never once shown up.

"GO," he says instead.

Zoom.

Here she comes. Small legs, top speed.

Papa runs, oh no...

His shoe is untied...

He must hop...

Oh NO...

She passes him.

She wins.

"'Gain!"

Papa has lost every race this month.

His shoes are always untied. Always. A mystery.

He ties the shoe.

Three giant steps. "One... two... three!"

"Go!" says Olivia, before he is ready.

The shoe comes undone at step four.
