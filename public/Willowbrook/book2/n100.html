Night 100

Black

Month 4: A World of Colors

Black is the night color.

In June the dark comes late.

Later than Olivia, most nights.

Tonight she is up.

Up the stairs, Papa's hand around hers.

Creak. Creak. Creak.

To the window.

Black has a job.

It holds the stars.

Without the dark, no twinkle.

The lane below: black.

A cat crosses it. Black cat, black lane.

Only her eyes show. Two little lamps.

The sky: black velvet.

And on it: Stella.

Shining her best.

"Star," says Papa.

Olivia points. Up. There.

The cat is gone.

Stella stays.
