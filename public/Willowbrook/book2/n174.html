Night 174

When Tummies Are Full

Month 6: Yum

The tummy talks at the start.

Rumble, rumble. Feed me.

And the tummy talks at the end, too.

It says: enough.

Not with a rumble.

With a feeling.

Olivia knows the feeling.

She pushes the plate. Gently.

Hands up: all done.

"One more bite," says Mama. Spoon out.

Olivia turns her head.

Mama looks at the spoon. She puts it down.

"Sorry," says Mama. "You said all done."

The tummy knows its size.

The plate goes away. No fuss.

Three bites are left on it.

The tummy did not order those.
