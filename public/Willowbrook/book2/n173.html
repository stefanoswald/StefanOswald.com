Night 173

The Honey Spoon

Month 6: Yum

Tonight, Mama's tea gets a visitor.

Honey.

Watch it pour.

Slow... slow... slooow.

A golden thread!

It spirals down.

It takes its time.

Honey never hurries.

Bees are patient. So is the jar.

The bees made this.

Flower by flower. All summer long.

"Bzz," says Olivia. She knows bees.

Stir, stir. The thread is gone.

Olivia's cup: warm milk. Just milk.

Sip. Sip.

Then the toothbrush.

Scrub, scrub, up and down.

Milk does not get to sleep on teeth.

The spoon waits in the sink, a little gold left on it.
