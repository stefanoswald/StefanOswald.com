Night 180

The Thank-You for Food

Month 6: Yum

Before dinner, a little pause.

Just a beat.

"Thank you for the food," says Mama.

Papa has more.

"Thank you, garden, for the tomato."

"Nana," says Olivia. She has one too.

"And the banana tree. Thank you."

"Thank you, Henrietta, for the eggs."

"Bok!"

"Thank you, Rosa, for the crust."

"Ta!"

Thank you, Grandpa, for the carrot and the berries.

Thank you, bees, for the honey.

Thank you, rain and sun, for all of it.

And the corn, and the orange, and the zucchini nobody ate.

So many helpers in one bowl of soup.

Olivia pats the table.

Pat, pat.

That is her amen.

Papa pats the table too.

Now: soup.
