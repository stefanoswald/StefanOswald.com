Night 88

The Sound of Rain Boots

Month 3: The Sounds of Everything

Rain leaves puddles.

Rain boots find them.

Splash!

Splosh!

Every step talks.

Olivia marches down the lane.

Splish-splash, splish-splash!

The trees are still dripping. Plip. Plip.

Papa's boots go SPLOSH.

Big steps. Big splash.

The biggest puddle is by the gate.

Olivia stomps in.

Sits down in it.

"Uh-oh."

"Uh-oh," says Papa. He holds out a hand.

Up she comes, dripping. Squelch, squelch.

Back home, the boots come off.

Two small puddles on the mat.
