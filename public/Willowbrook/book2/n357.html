Night 357

The Remember Game, Age Two Edition

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Tonight: the remember game.

This whole year.

Mama and Papa take turns.

Olivia gets the last word.

"The night you turned one, who moved in?"

"Bear."

"The brook. You wore the red boots. It was..."

"Cold!"

"Yellow and blue made..."

"Geen."

"The worm on the path, and you were so..."

Olivia strokes the air. Gently.

"Every puddle in town..."

"Jump!"

"Lily's sad day. What did you do?"

Pat, pat, on Mama's arm.

"The milk, the plow..."

"Maybe!"

"And the mirror. Who's that?"

"Livy."

That was the year.

"'Gain," says Olivia.

The whole year? Again?

"'Gain."

Papa starts over. Slower.

Her eyes close before the puddles.
