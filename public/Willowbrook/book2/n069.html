Night 69

The Crunch Walk

Month 3: The Sounds of Everything

The park path is made of little stones.

And little stones have a secret:

They crunch!

Stomp! Crunch!

Stomp, stomp! Crunch, crunch!

Olivia finds the crunchiest patch.

The loose grey bit by the bench.

CRUNCH!

That was a good one.

Papa stomps too. His crunches are huge.

They crunch the whole path round.

Past the pond. Past the swings.

The trees are all new green, and they do not mind the noise.

Home time.

Papa carries her the last bit.

Her feet keep going in the air.

Crunch, crunch, on nothing.
