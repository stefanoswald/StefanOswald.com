Night 71

Boom Says the Sky

Month 3: The Sounds of Everything

Far away, the sky is drumming.

Boom.

Booom.

Olivia goes still.

"Boom," says Papa. "That's the sky."

He picks her up.

They watch from the window.

Boom, says the sky.

"Boom!" says Olivia.

She shakes her fist at it.

The rain comes marching in.

The drums roll past, bigger, then smaller.

Boom... booom... gone.

"More?" says Olivia.

"The sky's all out of booms," says Papa.

Just the rain now.

Papa keeps holding her anyway.
