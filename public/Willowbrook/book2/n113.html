Night 113

The Flower Bed Changes Its Mind

Month 4: A World of Colors

All spring, Mrs. Henderson's flower bed wore yellow.

Tulips. Every day. Only yellow.

Then the tulips went.

Green for a while. Just green.

Now, on the morning walk. Red!

Poppies. Big red cups, wide open.

And pink ones, at the edge.

The flower bed changed its mind.

No one asked it. It just did.

"Look at that," says Mama.

Olivia points. And points.

A bee lands in a red one.

Bzzz.

"Bee!" says Olivia.

The poppy nods. Up, down.

The bee goes to the next one.

Olivia and Mama go to the next street.

Behind them, the poppies nod.
