Night 289

Together and Apart

Month 10: Opposites

Mama has her coat on.

Rosa is here, flour on one cheek, and Lily.

Olivia knows the coat.

"Mama no."

Mama gets low. Both hands on Olivia's arms.

"Store, then back after snack. Rosa has you."

One kiss. "Bye, Livy."

Door. Click.

Apart.

Olivia's mouth goes square. Loud.

Rosa picks her up, does not say much, and walks to the window. The lane. Snow. Mama's back, small, smaller, gone.

Crying. A while. Rosa sways. Lily pats Olivia's boot.

Then, on the rug: the pots. Lily has the funnel on her head.

Snack. Crackers.

The door. Click. Boots stamping off snow.

"Mama back!"

Olivia holds Mama's neck, hard. Then wriggles down to show her the cracker.
