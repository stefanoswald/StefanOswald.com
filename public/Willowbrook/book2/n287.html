Night 287

First and Last

Month 10: Opposites

Sunday. Papa at the stove. The stove is Papa's.

The first pancake: wonky. A blob with a tail.

Whose?

"Mine."

Hers. First pancakes always are.

First boot on. First step out onto the snowy step. Crunch. Her print, the first one on the whole white step.

The day goes by in the middle.

Last: the last of the cocoa, tipped up, gone.

The last page of the bear book.

The last of the bath water. Glug.

The last light. Off.

And the last kiss.

"More kiss."

That one, then. Now that one was the last.

Mama's hand on the door. The last look.

The tiny moon night light was on first, before all of it. It stays on.
