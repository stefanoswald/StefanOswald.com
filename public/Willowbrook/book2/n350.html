Night 350

Tomorrow Is Getting Ready

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

While Olivia sleeps,

Tomorrow gets ready.

Behind the hills, the sun warms up.

Stretches. Not yet.

The birds practice with their beaks shut.

The frost draws ferns on the window.

One... then another. It never hurries.

The bakery ovens dream of dough.

The snowplow man checks his lights.

Blink. Blink. Good.

Tomorrow's puddles, waiting under the ice.

Tomorrow's words, lining up on the tongue.

Even a brand-new one.

Everything getting ready

For one girl

Who will wake up and say:

"Hi!"

Stella goes pale. Her turn is nearly done.

Sleep on.

The ferns are almost finished.
