Night 196

The Sandbox Kingdom

Month 7: Outside!

The sandbox is a kingdom.

You can pour the ground!

Scoop the sand. Pour it out.

Shhhhh. A golden waterfall.

Scoop, pour. Scoop, pour.

Fill the cup. Tip it over. A castle!

A small one. A good one.

Lily is here too.

Lily digs. Olivia pours.

Nobody looks at anybody.

Two castles. Neighbors.

Lily reaches for the cup.

"Mine!" Olivia holds it tight.

From the bench: "That one's Olivia's. Here's the blue one."

Lily takes the blue one. Fine.

Dig, pour. Dig, pour.

Sand in the cuffs. Sand in the socks.

Sand goes home with everyone.
