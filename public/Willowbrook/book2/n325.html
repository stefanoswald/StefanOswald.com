Night 325

Everything Is Made of Shapes

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Four o'clock, and the lamps are on up and down the lane.

Walk home with shape eyes.

The houses: square walls. Triangle roofs, with snow on.

The doors: rectangles.

The doorknobs: circles.

Mrs. Henderson's window: a square of yellow light. She waves from inside it.

Olivia waves back. Mitten up.

Scrape, scrape. The plow truck comes down the lane.

Mama's hand holds tight. Up on the bank they stand.

A big rectangle on circle wheels. Its light turns, orange, orange, orange.

"Big truck!"

Then it's gone round the corner.

Their own house. Square. Triangle. Rectangle door.

And in the square window, a round face with a beard.

"Papa!"

The door opens. Warm comes out.
