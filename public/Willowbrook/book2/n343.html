Night 343

The Whole Town Goes to Bed

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Olivia is not the only one.

The whole town goes to bed.

Watch from the window.

The bakery: ovens cooling.

Rosa's light: off.

The library: lantern low.

Molly, in the wall, on her last page.

The farm: barn shut. Coop murmuring.

One straw hat on its peg.

The square: the fountain under its winter cover.

Asleep till spring.

The lane: porch lights out.

One... by one... by one.

"Light gone," says Olivia.

Gone. Somebody tucked in.

Another gone. Somebody else.

Snow on every roof, blue.

And one star over all of it.

Over this window too.

Mama pulls the curtain.

The star stays out there, on its own.
