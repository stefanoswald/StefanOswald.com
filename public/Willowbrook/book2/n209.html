Night 209

Mud Pies

Month 7: Outside!

Mud. Good mud. Thick mud.

The garden corner is full of it.

And Olivia has an old tin.

Mud pies!

Scoop the mud. Slap it in.

Slap, slap, slap.

Smooth the top.

Now decorate:

One leaf, in the middle.

Three pebbles, around.

Beautiful.

A pie for looking, not for eating.

(Very important rule.)

Mama leans in. Opens her mouth wide...

"No!" says Olivia. Hands over the pie.

Mama laughs. "Right. Looking only."

The pie goes on the step to dry.

One leaf. Three pebbles.
