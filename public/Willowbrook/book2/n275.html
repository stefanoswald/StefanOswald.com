Night 275

Up and Down

Month 10: Opposites

Snow on the park. Snow on the swing. Mama wipes the seat with her mitten.

In she goes, coat and all.

Push.

"Up!"

Sky. Bare branches. Tummy flip.

Back she comes.

"Down."

Mama's face. Boots. Whoosh.

"Up!" Push.

"Down." Whoosh.

Mama does the up. The swing does the down. That is the deal.

A crow goes up out of the big tree. Up, up, a dot, gone.

"Up," says Olivia, at the crow, and gets a push.

Cold nose. Cold hands, even in the mittens.

The swing slows. Slower.

Olivia holds up both arms.

"Livy down."

Arms up, to get down. Mama lifts her out.

The empty swing keeps going a little, by itself.
