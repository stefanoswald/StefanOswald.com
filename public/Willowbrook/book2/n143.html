Night 143

The Shadow Friend

Month 5: All About Me

Evening at the farm.

The sun is low and gold.

Someone is on the barn wall.

Someone flat. Someone dark.

Olivia waves.

It waves.

Olivia stomps.

It stomps. Not a sound.

A shadow.

It is stuck to her feet.

Where she goes, it goes.

Next to hers, a tall one. With a hat.

Grandpa Joe's.

His shadow lifts its hat.

Puts it back.

Lifts it.

"Hat!"

Grandpa Joe winks. "Mm-hm."

Tips the hat again.

A cloud comes over. Both shadows go.

"Gone."

The cloud passes. Back.

The sun sinks lower.

The shadows stretch longer and longer,

All the way to the chickens.
