Night 214

Esquire the Duck

Month 8: Splash

This is Esquire.

The duck.

Papa named him. Nobody knows why.

He has been in this bath

Since Olivia was small.

Push him under.

Pop! Up again.

Push him under.

Pop!

Every time.

Squeeze him. Eek!

Squeeze again. Eek!

He floats. He squeaks. He looks.

One face. It works for everything.

Baths, storms, Tuesdays.

"Duck," says Olivia.

She pats his yellow head.

Esquire bobs.

Mama's hand rests on the tub's edge.

Esquire bobs at that too.
