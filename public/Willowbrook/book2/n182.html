Night 182

The Door to Everywhere

Month 7: Outside!

This is the front door.

Inside it: home.

Outside it: everywhere!

Boots on. Left. Right. Stomp, stomp.

Mama turns the handle.

Click... creeeak...

Light! Air! Birds!

The outside was there all along.

Waiting on the step.

Waiting all night.

"Out!" says Olivia.

Out it is.

One step. Two steps.

The step is warm already.

Behind them, the door swings.

Slow... slow... click.

The cat watches from the window.
