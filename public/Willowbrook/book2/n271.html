Night 271

Three Friends on the Rug

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Too cold for the park blanket.

So: the library rug.

Olivia. Lily.

And today, one more.

Mateo, from two porches down. He builds things.

Three on the rug.

Olivia stacks her blocks. One, two. Wobble.

Lily turns the pages of a book with a bang each time.

Mateo makes a ramp out of books. His car goes down it. Zoom.

Three jobs. One rug.

Everybody plays next to everybody.

"One, two, three," says Mama from the bench, counting coats on the hooks.

Mateo's car comes off the ramp and bumps Olivia's tower.

Crash.

Olivia looks at the car.

Mateo looks at the tower.

"Uh-oh," says Mateo.

Olivia stacks again. One, two.

The car goes down the ramp the other way.
