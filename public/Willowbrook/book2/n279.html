Night 279

Fast and Slow

Month 10: Opposites

The hallway. Two ways down it.

Fast: ZOOM. Arms out, hair back, done.

"'Gain!"

Zoom.

Slow: Papa shows. One foot. Wait. Other foot. Wait.

"Sloooow," says Papa. Tessa Turtle slow.

Olivia tries. One step... two steps... zoom.

Slow is a hard speed. It does not last.

Fast: snow sliding off the porch roof. Whump.

Slow: one snowflake, past the window. So slow you can pick one and watch it all the way down.

They pick one. Papa's finger follows it. Olivia's finger follows Papa's.

All the way to the sill.

"Down."

Zoom, to the kitchen. She wins. Papa was not racing. She wins anyway.

Then back the other way, slow. Tiptoe. Tiptoe. Tiptoe, to Papa's knee.
