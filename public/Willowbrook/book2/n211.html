Night 211

Bringing the Outside In

Month 7: Outside!

The walk is over.

But look what came home.

One sparkly rock. Of course.

"Where did this come from?"

"Papa." The step. The row. The ooh.

One gold leaf.

"The wind took this one. And Papa's hat."

"Gone!" She laughs all over again.

One grey feather, from Grandpa's hens.

"Bok," soft.

They go on the windowsill. In a row. Just so.

The outside, sleeping over.

"Night, rock. Night, leaf. Night, feather," says Mama.

"Nigh-nigh," says Olivia. Pat, pat, pat.

Through the window behind them: the real outside.

The lane. The big tree with the low branch.

"Up!"

"You climbed it. And came down when you said."

The lawn, dark. No blinks tonight.

The crickets are still at it.
