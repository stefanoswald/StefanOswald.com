Night 306

The Square Window

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Frost on the window this morning. White ferns, corner to corner.

The window is a square. Four sides. Four corners.

Mama's finger on the cold glass. Eeek.

Across the top. Corner!

Down the side. Corner!

Across the bottom. Corner!

Up the side. Corner!

Home again.

Where the finger went, the frost is gone. A square of clear glass.

Olivia's finger now. A small squeak.

"Cold," she says. She does it anyway.

Across. Down. Across. Up.

Two squares in the frost. One big. One small.

Look through the small square.

The lane. The snow. The gold dog, going by.
