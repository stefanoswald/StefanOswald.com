Night 333

The Blanket Fort Blueprint

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

The big build. The fort.

Papa is the crane. Olivia is the architect.

Walls: the square cushions.

Roof: the big blanket, couch to chair. A triangle.

Door: the round pillow, rolled aside.

Inside, the lamp glows gold through the blanket.

Papa brings things in out of his sweater.

The puzzle circle. "Remember? You slid it till it dropped."

"Click," says Olivia.

The paper with two hands. "Remember the bump?"

"Papa hand."

The bear's foot. Bump, bump, point.

"And the moon," says Papa. "It was square."

"No! Half!"

"Half. Then it came back."

Through the fort door, through the window: there it is.

"Moon back."

"Good house," says Olivia, and lies down in it.
