Night 305

The Circle Sun

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

The first shape is the circle.

Round and round, no corners.

Where does it live?

Look up!

The sun! Low and gold over the snow.

A circle. The brightest one there is.

"Sun!" says Olivia. Both hands up.

Now look around.

The breakfast bowl: circle.

The clock's face: circle. Tick.

The little red cup's rim: circle.

Mama's finger goes round the rim.

Round and round.

Olivia's finger goes round the bowl.

Into the oatmeal. Out again.

Round and round.

Round goes the spoon too.

The sun climbs. The bowl empties.
