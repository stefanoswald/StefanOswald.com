Night 327

Star Light, Star Bright

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

First star tonight. There. The gold one, low.

Mama knows the old words. So did her mama.

"Star light, star..."

"Bright!" says Olivia.

"First star I see to..."

"Night!"

"I wish I may, I wish I..."

"Night!" says Olivia. Close enough.

"Have the wish I wish tonight."

Now the wish. Close your eyes.

Mama closes hers.

Olivia closes one. The other one watches Mama.

She puts a hand over it. Now both.

Squeeze.

What did Mama wish? She won't say. Wishes work best quiet.

What did Olivia wish?

She opens her eyes and points. At the star. At Mama. At the bear.

Out the window, the gold one. Twinkle.
