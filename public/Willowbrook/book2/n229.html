Night 229

Tears Are Water Too

Month 8: Splash

Today there were tears.

The tower fell.

Then the banana broke in half.

It was a wrong-shaped day.

"Broke," says Olivia.

Tears came. Big ones.

Down the cheeks. Drip.

Mama got low.

"You're sad. The banana broke."

She caught one tear with her thumb.

"Tears are water too," she said.

Then nothing. Just arms.

The crying rained a while.

Mama held her through it.

Slower. Slower.

Hiccup. Sigh.

Wet face. Wet shirt. Both of them.

The banana was still broken.

Two halves.

She ate them both.
