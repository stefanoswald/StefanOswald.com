Night 307

The Triangle Tree

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Papa and Olivia at the corner, looking up at the pine.

Wide at the bottom. A point at the top. A triangle.

Snow sits on the point like a hat.

"Hat!" says Olivia.

Papa makes a triangle with his hands, fingers tipped together.

Olivia tips her mittens together. A mitten triangle.

The roof of the house: triangle, with snow.

The far hill: triangle, white.

Papa pats the pine. Fump. Snow slides off the point, down his neck.

"Oh," says Papa. "Cold."

Olivia pats the low branch. Fump. Snow on her mitten.

"Hat gone," she says.

The tree stands there, bare on top.

Papa shakes out his collar.
