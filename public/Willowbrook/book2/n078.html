Night 78

The Giggle

Month 3: The Sounds of Everything

Of all the sounds in the house,

One is the best.

It is not the kettle.

It is not the bell.

It starts when Papa puts a sock on his ear.

Olivia giggles. Hee-hee.

So Papa giggles. Ho-ho.

So Olivia giggles MORE.

So Papa laughs for real.

So Olivia falls over laughing.

The sock falls off.

Nobody notices.

The giggle feeds itself now.

Round and round it goes.

It slows down. Hee. Hee.

Two people on the rug, out of breath.
