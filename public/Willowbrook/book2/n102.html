Night 102

Bella's Wings Have Everything

Month 4: A World of Colors

The rainbow went home.

But somebody kept the colors.

On the fence post, in the sun.

Bella.

Her wings are open.

Red. Orange. Yellow.

Green. Blue. Purple.

All of it. All at once.

A rainbow folded small enough to carry.

Olivia comes close. Not too close. Shh.

Bella fans her wings. Slow. Slow.

Olivia fans her hands. Slow. Slow.

A little wind comes.

Up goes Bella.

Over the fence, over the garden, off.

The fence post is just a fence post again.

Olivia fans her hands anyway.
