Night 332

The Bakery Window Shapes

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Morning. The bakery window, fogged at the edges from the warm inside.

The buns: circles. Rows and rows.

The braided loaf: a long oval, with a twist down it like Tessa's back.

And the special tray. Star cookies, sugar on top. A whole sky of them.

"Star," says Olivia, nose on the glass. Mama's hand on her hood.

Jingle, says the door.

Rosa comes round the counter, flour on one cheek.

"One star?"

"One star."

Olivia eats it point by point.

"One. Two." Then, arms wide: "Lots."

Sugar on her chin. Flour on Rosa's cheek. Even.

Out the door. Jingle.

Behind the glass, the tray of stars, one short.
