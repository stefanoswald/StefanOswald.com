Night 346

The Bear's Job at Night

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

What does Bobby Bear do all night?

You think he sleeps?

He does. Bear-sleep.

One ear stays up.

(The floppy one. It hears best.)

His jobs:

Being held. The big one.

Being soft.

Keeping the warm spot warm.

Catching the bumpy dreams

Before they land.

Letting the good ones through.

And if she wakes in the middle:

Being right there. First reach.

"Ear," says Olivia, half asleep.

The ear is there.

It is always there.

It has been squeezed flat more nights than anyone can count.

It does not mind.

It springs back by morning.
