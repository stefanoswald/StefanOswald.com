Month 3 · The Sounds of Everything

For the grown-up

Nights 60–90

This month is all sound. She is fourteen months old, walking well, and the world has started answering her: the cow at the fence, the door with Papa behind it, the bridge that says her name back. She says one word at a time, and will for a while yet. Listen to what she can already do, though. Moo, boom, choo, whoo, bzz, pop. Each of those is a word trying itself out, and when you make one back, she hears that sounds go between people. That is talking, before the words come.

Point at the drum, the bell, the bees, and make the sound before you say the name. Leave a gap after "the cow says" and see what fills it. No questions after the story. Make the sounds inside it. She will make them back when she is ready.

If she wants the sneeze story ten nights running, that is not a rut. She is learning its shape until she can hold it herself. The night she says "choo" before you get there, it worked.

One thing to try: put the wrong animal in the bedtime song and wait. One thing not to worry about: the jump she gives at thunder or the truck. A startle is not a fear. Stay easy, and she will copy that too. And her first loud no is the age, not the child.
