Night 189

The Climbing Tree's Low Branch

Month 7: Outside!

The big oak has many branches.

High ones for birds.

Higher ones for squirrels.

And one low one.

Low as a chair.

Olivia looks at it.

She looks at Papa.

Papa's hands come close. They wait.

Foot on the bump. Hands on the bark.

Pull...

"Up!"

She is IN the tree.

Sitting in its old wooden hand.

The bark is rough under her legs.

The leaves move. She does not.

Papa's hands stay near. Just near.

A long sit.

A bird lands, higher up.

Then: "Down!"

Now the hands come.
