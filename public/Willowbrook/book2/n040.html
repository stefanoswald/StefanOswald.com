Night 40

The Gold Dog's Patrol

Month 2: Animal Friends

Every evening, the gold dog walks.

Down the lane. Past every gate.

It is his patrol.

He checks on everything.

The hedge: fine.

The mailboxes: fine.

The puddle by the Hendersons' gate: fine, and worth a drink.

At Olivia's fence, he stops.

Mama is on the step. Watching.

"Woof!" says Olivia.

His tail goes wag, wag.

He sniffs her hand, flat, through the slats.

She keeps it there. Mama's hand on her shoulder.

She waves with the other one.

Then on he walks, into the dusk.

Porch lights come on behind him, one by one.
