Night 99

White

Month 4: A World of Colors

The dandelions have gone white.

Round and soft. Little white moons, all over the park.

White is everywhere today.

The clouds: white.

The daisies in the grass: white.

Grandpa Joe's mustache: white.

He is at the farm. It counts.

Papa picks one white moon.

"Blow," says Papa.

Olivia blows. Fwoo.

Nothing.

Papa blows. Fwooo.

Everything!

White bits go up. Up, up.

Tiny umbrellas, riding the air.

Olivia grabs at them. Gone.

She finds another moon.

Fwoo. Two seeds go.

One lands on Papa's nose.

He leaves it there.
