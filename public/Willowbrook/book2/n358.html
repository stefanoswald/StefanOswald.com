Night 358

The Quiet Before the Party

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Tomorrow: the party.

Papa is up the ladder with the banner.

It says TWO.

"Up," says Olivia, from the doorway.

"Not now." Tape in his teeth.

She waits. And waits.

Papa comes down. Tape on the step. Ladder folded.

"I said not now. Sorry. Up."

Up she goes, onto Papa's shoulders.

From up high: the cups, out. The plates, out.

Two candles on their shelf.

But tonight is just tonight.

Bath. (Splash contest. A tie.)

Pajamas. (Stars.)

Story. (The bear book. Nobody skips a page.)

Song. (The cow. Moo.)

The last bedtime of being one.

Mama holds her a little longer.

Papa's watch at the door runs late.

The banner says TWO in the dark.
