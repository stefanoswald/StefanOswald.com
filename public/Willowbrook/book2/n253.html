Night 253

The Countdown to Blastoff

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Dark already, and it's not even supper.

Good. Dark is for rockets.

The rocket is Olivia.

Papa holds her sideways, both hands, tight.

The big bed is the landing pad.

Begin countdown.

"Three!"

The rocket wiggles.

"Two!"

The rocket squeals.

"One!"

Wait for it...

"BLAST..."

"Off!" says the rocket.

Whoooosh. Across the room, slow, in Papa's hands.

Landing: pillows. Soft. Everywhere.

The rocket survives.

The rocket demands relaunch.

"More go!"

Three, two, one, off. Whoooosh.

Three, two, one, off. Whoooosh.

The rocket is out of fuel.

So is Papa. He lies down flat on the pad.

The rocket climbs onto his tummy and sits there.

It is a good place to park.
