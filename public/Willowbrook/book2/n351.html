Night 351

The Night Before Winter's End

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Listen. Outside. Very soft.

Drip.

Hear it?

Drip.

The icicle at the eave.

It is melting.

The first melt.

Winter is getting sleepy.

It has been up a long time.

Under the snow, the bulbs are stirring.

Under the fence, a leaf-covered someone

Is dreaming of trundling.

Down the pasture, a balloon

Waits for a warm wind.

Spring is on its way.

Drip by drip.

"Nigh-nigh, winter," whispers Olivia.

Mama closes the curtain.

Drip.

Drip.
