Night 280

Wet and Dry

Month 10: Opposites

Bath. Mama's sleeves rolled. Mama's hand on the tub edge, where it stays.

Wet: hair. Wet: hands. Wet: Esquire the duck. He is always wet. He likes it.

Splash.

Wet: Mama's front, a little.

"Wet Mama."

"Wet Mama," Mama agrees.

Out. The towel with the bear ears.

Rub, rub, rub.

Dry: toes. Dry: hair, sticking up. Dry: the bear ears.

The duck stays in the tub.

"Duck wet."

"He's a duck," says Mama. "He's fine."

The water goes down the drain. Glug, glug, gone.

Bye, water.

Warm jammies. Dry all over.

Except one curl at the back, still wet, hiding. Mama finds it with the towel.
