Night 249

Two Hands Full

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Olivia has two hands.

Block one: left hand. Got it.

Block two: right hand. Got it.

Now Papa holds out block three.

Hmm.

Two hands. Three blocks.

She grabs with a full hand.

Block one drops. Bonk.

"Uh-oh."

She picks it up. Two hands full again.

Papa still holds out block three.

He does not help. He waits.

Think... think...

She tucks block one under her arm.

Squeezes.

Takes block three.

Three blocks. Two hands. One elbow.

"You put it under your arm," says Papa. "Huh."

Papa holds out block four.

Olivia looks at him.

"No."

Papa keeps block four.

The tower goes up: one, two, three.

Papa puts the fourth one on top, when she nods.
