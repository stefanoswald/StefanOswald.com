Night 345

The Lullaby Museum

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

The sill has a museum.

Rock, leaf, feather, shell.

Bedtime has one too.

Songs.

First: Papa's song. The one with the cow.

He sings the wrong sound. "The cow says baa..."

"Moo!" says Olivia.

Every night he gets it wrong.

Every night she fixes it.

Second: Mama's hum.

No words. Hmm-hmm-hmm.

Third: Grandpa's tune.

The one he sings to the hens.

It works on people.

Fourth, the newest one:

"Nigh-nigh, nigh-nigh,"

Olivia's own. Sung to the bear.

Her tune. Nobody taught it.

All four, one after another.

The wrong cow. The hum. The hen tune.

And last, hers, very small.

The bear gets that one.
