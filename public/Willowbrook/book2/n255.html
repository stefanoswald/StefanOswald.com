Night 255

Counting Sheep

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Can't sleep?

There's an old trick. The oldest.

Counting sheep.

The blanket is the fence. See the bump?

Mama's hand is the sheep.

Fluffy. Bouncy.

Hop. Over the fence.

"One sheep."

Here comes another.

"Hop!" says Olivia.

"Two sheep."

"Hop!"

"Three sheep."

"Hop."

"Four sheep..."

"Hop."

"Five..."

The eyes close. Open. Close.

The hand-sheep slows down.

The hop gets smaller.

"Six," Mama whispers.

The hand-sheep stops on her tummy and stays.

The counter is asleep.

The sheep is very good at its job.
