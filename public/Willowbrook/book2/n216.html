Night 216

Washing Bobby Bear's Face

Month 8: Splash

Bobby Bear does not take baths.

Bears like him stay dry.

So the bath-bear goes in instead.

A little rubber one.

Olivia has a little cloth.

First his face. Round and round.

"Eyes," she tells him.

That is what Mama says to her.

Behind the ears. Squish.

Under the chin. Squish.

There. Clean bear.

"Done," says Olivia.

She pats his head.

Mama, on the bath mat, pats hers.

The bath-bear floats off, clean.

Bobby Bear waits in the crib.

Dry. As always.
