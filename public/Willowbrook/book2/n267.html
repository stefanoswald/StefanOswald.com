Night 267

The Whole Hand

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Bedtime song. Just one tonight, Mama says.

Olivia has other plans.

"Two songs."

Two fingers. Like this.

Olivia tries.

Up goes... the whole hand.

All five.

"Five songs?" says Mama. "Nobody has five songs."

Giggles. "No!"

Try again.

The thumb helps. It holds down the extra fingers.

Wobble.

Two. Two fingers.

"Two!"

"Two songs," says Mama. "The cow one and the twinkle one."

The cow one first. Moo in the right place.

"Moo," says Olivia, in the right place too.

Then the twinkle one, slower.

The two fingers are still up when it ends.

Then one.

Then the hand is just a hand, open on the blanket.
