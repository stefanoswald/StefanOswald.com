Night 159

The Bread Rosa Bakes

Month 6: Yum

Jingle! The bakery bell.

"There's my girl!"

Rosa has flour on one cheek.

She always does.

The ovens are warm behind her.

And here comes the ritual.

The crust end.

Warm. Fresh. Hers.

Rosa saves it every visit.

"For my best customer."

Olivia gnaws it.

Gnaw, gnaw. Crumbs down the front.

"Thank you, Rosa," says Mama.

"Ta," says Olivia. Mouth full.

"Both welcome," says Rosa.

And is that a little flour

On Olivia's cheek now too?

It is.

Jingle, wave, home.
