Night 234

The Fountain in the Square

Month 8: Splash

In the middle of town: the old fountain.

Old stone. Always burbling.

Water leaps up in the middle,

Falls back, leaps again.

Olivia runs to it. Mama's hand catches up.

Look in the bottom.

Coins! Shiny ones.

And leaves now. Yellow ones, sunk.

"Last week for the fountain," says Mama.

"Then it sleeps till spring."

The town turns it off before the cold can crack it.

Olivia has no coin.

She has... a pebble. From her pocket. A good one.

Toss.

Plip.

She points at the fountain.

"More."

More fountain. Mama nods.

"In spring," says Mama. "It comes back."

Burble, burble.

The pebble lies down with the coins.

It will wait there all winter.
