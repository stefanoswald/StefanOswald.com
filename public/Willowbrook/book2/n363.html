Night 363

What Two Feels Like

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

The morning after. The willow walk.

Does two feel different?

Running: faster? Up the hill path. Zoom.

Maybe faster.

Jumping: both feet. Up... and...

Both boots left the mud. A real jump.

Talking: bigger?

"Livy jump!" Two words at once.

At the top, the tree.

The Great Willow, bare still, gold at the tips.

Papa finds the place in the bark.

A word. New this year.

"H-O-M-E," reads Papa. "Home."

"Home," says Olivia. She pats it.

On the bark beside it, something opens and shuts.

Dark wings, gold at the edges.

The first butterfly.

"Wings," whispers Olivia.

"Our fuzzy one from the fence?" asks Papa.

"Maybe," says Mama.

The wings open once more.

Then Papa's shoulders, all the way down.
