Night 224

The Puddle That Stayed a Week

Month 8: Splash

After the big rain,

A puddle moved in.

The big pothole by the gate.

Day one: STOMP. Magnificent.

Day two: still there. A leaf in it. STOMP.

Day three: a bird was bathing in it.

They waited. Birds first.

Day four: Mateo stomped it from his side.

Olivia stomped it from hers.

Two stompers. No talking.

Day five: just a splash left.

Both boots at once? Bend. Push.

One boot goes. Then the other.

Splash, splash.

Day six...

Gone.

Dried up. Gone to the sky.

It will come back with the next big rain.

"Puddle?" says Olivia.

She checks the pothole. Twice.
