Night 316

The Spiral Shell

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

On the sill: the shell the aunts sent.

A line that goes round, and round, and IN.

A spiral.

Olivia's finger goes round and round and in.

"Round," she says. "Round round."

Hold it to your ear. Shhhhh.

That night, in her dream, somebody slow comes over the snow.

Tessa. Her shell has the very same swirl. A cinnamon roll with legs.

Olivia's finger goes round and round and in, on Tessa's back.

"Slow is also a speed," says Tessa.

She walks her spiral in the snow. Round. And round. And in.

At the middle, she tucks in her head.

On the sill, the shell keeps its middle too.
