Night 348

The Goodnight Kiss Travels

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Watch where the kiss goes.

It starts on Mama's lips.

It has been there all day, waiting.

Here it comes...

Mwah.

Landing: Olivia's forehead. Right in the middle.

It stays there. All night.

Kisses don't wash off. They soak in.

Now watch this one.

It starts on Olivia's lips.

Mwah.

Landing: the bear's snout.

Now another.

Mwah. Mama's cheek.

Mama puts her hand over it.

"I'll keep that."

One more, on a flat hand.

Blow it...

Off it goes, out the door,

Down the hall,

To Papa, doing the dishes.

"Got it," calls Papa.

He always catches them.

Nobody knows how.
