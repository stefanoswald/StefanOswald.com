Night 60

Moo Says the Cow

Month 3: The Sounds of Everything

The cow has one word.

It is a good one.

"Moo," says the cow.

"Moo!" says Olivia.

The cow looks up. Interested.

"Moo," says the cow again. Slower.

"Mooooo," says Olivia. Slower too.

Olivia looks at Grandpa Joe.

Grandpa Joe leans on the fence.

"Yep," he says.

That is all he says all morning.

The cow and Olivia do the talking.

Moo. Moo. Mooooo.

"Moo," says Olivia at dinner.

"Moo," says Olivia in the bath.

"Moo," says Olivia to the dark.
