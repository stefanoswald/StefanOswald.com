Night 105

Grey Day

Month 4: A World of Colors

Some days are grey.

The sky: grey.

The rain: grey. Tick, tick, on the glass.

The puddles: grey mirrors.

Grey days are soft days.

Nothing shouts. Nothing hurries.

Olivia and Mama sit at the window.

Blanket. Tea. Toast. A book.

Mama's phone buzzes. Mama looks at it.

Olivia taps the book. Tap. Tap.

Mama turns the phone face down.

"Sorry, Livy. Book first."

"Frog," says Mama.

Olivia points. There.

"Duck," says Mama.

There.

Grey outside. Toast inside.

Toast crumbs in the blanket.

Rain on the glass.
