Night 296

Freddy Explains Opposites (Loudly)

Month 10: Opposites

In Bobby Bear's dream tonight it is summer at the brook, and Freddy is awake.

"OPPOSITES!" announces Freddy. "I WILL DEMONSTRATE!"

Nobody asked. As usual.

"WET!" Splash. He's in the brook.

"DRY!" Hop. He's on the rock.

"WET!" Splash!

"DRY!" Hop!

"LOUD!" That one, he nails.

"...quiet."

Two seconds. Then loud again.

"FAST!" Zip, around the rock!

"SLOW!" He points at Tessa.

"Excuse me," says Tessa. Slowly.

"UP!" Jump! "DOWN!" Land!

"THE END! APPLAUSE!"

The brook burbles.

Tessa nods. Eventually.

The dream goes quiet, the way dreams do.

Under the real brook, under the mud, Freddy sleeps till spring, and has no idea he was in it.
