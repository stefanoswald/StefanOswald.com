Night 312

The Moon Changes Shape

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Something is different tonight.

Papa lifts Olivia up to the window.

The Moon is half.

Half a moon. Straight down one side.

"Moon gone?" says Olivia.

"She does this," says Papa. "Watch her all month."

Some nights: a thin curl, like a nail clipping.

Some nights: half, like now.

Some nights: full and round, a big plate over the roofs.

Then back the other way. Slow.

Olivia looks hard at the dark side, where the rest should be.

"Dere," she says, and points at nothing.

"Dere," says Papa. "Resting."

"Mmm," hums the half-Moon.

Olivia waves at the half she can see.

And the half she can't.
