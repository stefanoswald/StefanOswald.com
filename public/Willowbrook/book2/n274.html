Night 274

Big and Little

Month 10: Opposites

Boots by the door. Snow tonight, Papa says.

Papa's boot: BIG.

Olivia's boot: little.

She stands them together and looks.

Then the little boot goes into the big boot. All the way down.

"Boot gone."

Papa tips the big boot. Out slides the little one. Bonk.

"'Gain."

In. Gone. Tip. Bonk.

Mama's boots join the line. Big, big, little. Olivia moves hers to the end. Little goes last.

Supper. Big spoon, little spoon. The little spoon goes in the big spoon. Nearly.

Papa's hand on the table: big.

Her hand on top of it: little.

Papa's hand closes. Gone.

Opens. There.

"'Gain."

It closes.
