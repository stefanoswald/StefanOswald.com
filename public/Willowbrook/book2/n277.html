Night 277

Loud and Quiet

Month 10: Opposites

The pot. The spoon.

BANG. BANG. BANG.

Loud.

The cat leaves the room.

Mama puts her hands over her ears and makes a face. Olivia bangs harder. That is how it works.

Then Mama's finger goes to her lips.

"Shhh."

Olivia's finger goes to her lips. "Shh."

Tap. Tap. The spoon, tiny, on the pot's edge.

Then BANG. Giggles.

Loud: the pot. The truck in the lane. Papa's laugh.

Quiet: the snow coming down. The cat's feet. Papa's whistle. His whistle is the quietest thing in the house. It makes no sound at all.

BANG. "Loud!"

Tap. "Shh."

BANG.

Tap... tap...

The spoon goes in the pot. The pot goes in the cupboard.

The cat comes back.
