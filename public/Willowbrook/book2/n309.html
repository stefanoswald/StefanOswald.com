Night 309

The Heart on the Bear's Foot

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

One more shape lives right in the crib.

Look at Bobby Bear's foot. The left one.

A patch, stitched on. Two bumps on top. A point below.

A heart.

Trace it. Bump, bump, point.

Olivia traces it. Bump, bump, point.

"Bear foot," she says.

Mama sewed it back on one night, when it came loose.

The stitches are a little crooked. They hold.

Now find Olivia's heart. Hand flat on her chest. Wait.

Thump. Thump. Thump.

Find Mama's. Thump. Thump.

Find the bear's.

Quiet. Bears keep theirs on the outside.

"Nigh-nigh, foot," says Olivia.

Pat, pat. The bear's foot, up by her chin.
