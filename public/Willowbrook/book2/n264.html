Night 264

Two Ears, One Mouth

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Grandpa Joe counts the farm way.

Slow. With one finger.

Out of the wind, in the barn. The old tractor sleeps here for the winter.

"Ears," says Grandpa. Tap. Tap.

"Two."

"Mouth." Tap.

"One."

"Eyes." Tap. Tap.

"Two ears," says Olivia, and taps her mouth.

"Nose." Tap. "One."

Now Olivia counts Grandpa.

Nose: one. Big.

Mustache: one. White. Tickly.

Hat: one.

She takes the hat.

"Livy hat."

It comes down over her ears. Both of them.

Grandpa Joe winks at nobody in particular.

The tractor has seen this before.

Under the hat, a small voice: "Two."
