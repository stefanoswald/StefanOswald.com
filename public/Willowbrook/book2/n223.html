Night 223

The Drain Goodbye

Month 8: Splash

The bath is over.

Out, and wrapped, and warm.

Now the last part.

Mama pulls the plug.

GLUG.

Olivia goes still.

The drain is loud tonight.

Gurgle, gurgle.

She grabs Mama's leg. Tight.

Mama stays low. Her face stays easy.

"That's the drain," says Mama. "Loud tonight."

She does not move.

Gurgle.

Olivia watches from behind the leg.

The water spins. Round and round and down.

Where is it going?

Down the pipes. Under the house.

Off toward the brook, Mama says.

The last of it: gurgle... gulp.

Quiet.

"Bye," says Olivia. A small wave.

Mama waves too.

Tomorrow it will gurgle again.

Tomorrow the leg will be there again.
