Night 247

Counting Stairs

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

The stairs like to be counted.

Every trip up: count.

One hand in Papa's hand. That's the rule.

"One!" Step.

"Two!" Step.

"Free!" Step.

"Fo!" Step.

Four is new. Wobbly. Fine.

"Fi!" Step.

"Free!" Step.

"Two!" Step.

The numbers go on adventures after four.

"Four," says Papa. "Not two. Four."

Olivia stops. The stairs go quiet.

Papa hears himself.

"Sorry. Your count, not mine."

"Two!" Step.

"Fi!" Step.

Papa counts the true numbers, under his breath.

Olivia counts her wild ones, loud.

Both counts get to the top.

Twelve steps. Or two. Or a hundred.

The gate clicks shut behind them.

Olivia sits down on the landing, to rest.
