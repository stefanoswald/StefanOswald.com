Night 299

The Sun Sets, the Moon Rises

Month 10: Opposites

Coats. Hats. Out to the porch, quick, before it's gone.

West, over the willow hill: the sun. Low. Orange. Going.

"Bye sun."

Wave.

Turn around. East, over the roofs: the moon. Big. Coming up. The bottom of it still behind the Hendersons' chimney.

"Hi Moon."

Wave.

One going down. One coming up. Both at once. Papa says it does not happen every night. Tonight it does.

Breath in clouds. Papa's, big. Hers, small.

The sun: gone. The hill goes dark.

The moon: all the way up, clear of the chimney.

It hums. Once. Low. Papa does not hear it. Olivia does.

"Hmm," she hums back.

In. Door. Cold noses in the warm.

The moon comes along, in the window.
