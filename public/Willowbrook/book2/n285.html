Night 285

In and Out

Month 10: Opposites

A box. A big one, empty. The best kind.

IN. Leg over. Leg over. Flop. Sit.

"In!"

Just her eyes over the top.

OUT. Leg over. Leg over. Bump.

"Out!"

In. Out. In. Out.

In, and the flaps close. Dark. Quiet. A box with a girl in it.

Where is Olivia?

The flaps open. "Boo!"

In: the bear too. Snug.

In: the bear and the blanket. Snugger.

In: the bear, the blanket, and one boot.

No room for Olivia.

Out comes the boot.

Out comes the blanket.

In goes Olivia.

Now it fits.

She sits in the box with the bear and waits for somebody to come and find her.
