Night 57

The Swallows Come Back

Month 2: Animal Friends

The first warm evening of the year.

Everyone is out on the lawn.

Swoop!

Something flies over. Low and fast.

Swoop! Another!

The swallows are back.

All winter they were somewhere else.

Now they are here, catching bugs for supper.

Olivia reaches. Whoosh, gone.

Reaches. Whoosh, gone.

"Up!" says Olivia.

Up into Papa's arms. Reaches.

Whoosh, gone.

Swallows do not stop for anybody.

Papa tries a swallow whistle.

It comes out as wind.

Mama laughs. Papa bows.

The sky goes rose. Then grey.

The swallows go wherever swallows go.

Olivia's arms come down.

Papa carries her in, still looking up.
