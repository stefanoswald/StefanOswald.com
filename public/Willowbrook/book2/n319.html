Night 319

The Kite Above the Hill

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Cold wind. Bright sky.

The big kids are on the hill with a kite.

Red. A diamond, with a long rag tail.

Up it goes. It swoops. It climbs. The tail goes flap, flap, flap.

"Kite!" says Olivia.

A diamond: a square standing up on one corner.

The kite dips at them. Olivia ducks. It climbs again.

"Up! Up!"

Head all the way back. Further back.

Down she goes, into the snow, on her bottom.

"Uh-oh."

From down there, the kite looks even higher.

She stays down. Papa sits down in the snow too.

The wind pulls at his hat.

Up there, the red diamond wiggles.
