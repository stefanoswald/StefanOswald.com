Night 183

The Walk to the Corner

Month 7: Outside!

Today's adventure: the corner.

One block away.

One block is very far

When you stop for everything.

Stop! A pebble. Pick it up.

Stop! A fence. Pat each slat.

Stop! A crack. Step over it. Twice.

Stop! A gate. Does it creak? It does.

Papa looks at the corner.

So far. So slow.

He scoops her up. "Let's just get there."

Olivia goes stiff as a board.

"Down!"

Papa stops.

"You're walking this one. I forgot."

Down she goes.

Back to the gate. Does it still creak?

It does.

Then, at last: the corner.

Pat the mailbox. Made it.

Now the walk home.

Same pebble. Still there.
