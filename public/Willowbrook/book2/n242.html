Night 242

Every Water Goes Home

Month 8: Splash

Lights low.

Where did this month's waters go?

The bath water: down the drain. Gurgle.

"Bye," said Olivia. Remember?

The brook: cold on the boots. Remember?

"Cold," says Olivia.

The brook rolls to the river.

The river rolls to the sea.

The sun sips the sea up. Slowly.

Up, up, into clouds.

The clouds sail back over the hill,

And rain. On the puddle by the gate.

On the fountain, asleep now.

Into the brook again.

Around and around. The same water. Forever.

The tears, the banana day: water too.

Tonight's bath might hold a drop the dinosaurs drank.

Water never says goodbye.

It only says see you soon.

Splash. Sleep. See you soon.
