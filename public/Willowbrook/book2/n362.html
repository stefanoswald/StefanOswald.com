Night 362

Bobby Bear's Birthday Letter

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

A letter came. Bear-sized.

Papa reads it.

"Dear Olivia,

Two! I watched the whole party from the good chair.

One year ago tonight I moved into this crib.

Best move a bear ever made.

You talk to me now. About ducks. About Lily. About Maybe.

I understand every word.

Next year, big things.

Your feelings will get names.

A girl called Lily will crash her cart into yours. It will be the best day.

And somebody small is coming to the house.

I will be there for all of it.

Happy birthday, my girl.

Your bear, Bobby."

Olivia pats the bear.

"Bear," she says. He knows.

Papa folds the letter.

It goes in the drawer with the others.
