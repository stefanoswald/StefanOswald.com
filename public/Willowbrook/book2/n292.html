Night 292

Lost and Found

Month 10: Opposites

Bedtime. Crib. No bear.

Not on the pillow. Not on the little chair. Not under the table.

"Bear gone."

"Where did you put him?" says Mama. Sharp. Tired.

Olivia's lip.

Mama hears herself. "Sorry. Not your fault, bears wander."

They look.

Couch: no. Toy box: no. Pots cupboard: pots, lids, funnel, no bear.

The big box from this morning: no.

Olivia checks under the rabbit. No.

The laundry basket, by the stairs.

A towel. A sock. A floppy ear.

"Bear dere!"

Found. He rode in with the towels. Bears do that.

He smells like the dryer. Warm.

Squeeze. A long one.

Back to the crib. Bear first. Girl second.
