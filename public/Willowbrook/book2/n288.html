Night 288

Over and Under

Month 10: Opposites

Snow day. No park. Papa builds a fort. Papa's forts are famous on this street.

Cushions: the wall. Blanket: the roof. Two chairs.

"Over the wall," says Papa. "Then under the roof."

Climb. Knee up. Tummy on top. Flop. Over.

Crawl. Head down. Under.

Both, in that order.

Inside: dark, warm, and Papa's socks.

"Over," says Olivia, and goes over. "Under," and goes under.

The cat goes under. Then over. Then sits on the roof. The roof sags.

Papa tries over the wall. Too big. Papa has to go under.

Over, under, over, under, over...

The roof comes down on all of them.

Under the blanket, in the dark: a giggle, then Papa's big laugh.
