Night 354

The Spring Peepers Return

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

A new sound tonight.

Papa opens the window a crack.

Cold comes in. So does...

Peep!

Peep-peep!

PEEP-PEEP-PEEP!

The peepers.

The tiny frogs of the brook, back for spring,

Singing their one song with everything they've got.

(Somewhere down there,

A certain green someone is awake too.

He is louder than all of them.

"WELCOME BACK!" Nobody asked him.)

All winter the nights were quiet.

Snow-quiet.

Now: peep-peep-peep.

"Peep," says Olivia.

"More peep."

Papa leaves the crack open one more song.

Then shut. Cold out.

Through the glass, smaller: peep... peep.

It goes on all night.
