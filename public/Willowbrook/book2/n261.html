Night 261

How Many Kisses

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Goodnight kisses. How many tonight?

Count them.

Forehead: "one."

This cheek: "two."

That cheek: "free."

Nose: "fo!"

Chin: "fi!"

This ear. That ear.

The count has lost its place.

The count does not care.

Olivia points to where the next one goes.

"Nose."

Nose again.

"Hand."

The hand. The other hand, to be fair.

"Bear kiss."

The bear gets one. On the good ear.

"Mama kiss."

Olivia gives that one. It lands mostly on the chin. Wet.

Mama wipes her chin with her sleeve and does not mind at all.

That was the last one.

Mama says so.

The door stays open a crack.
