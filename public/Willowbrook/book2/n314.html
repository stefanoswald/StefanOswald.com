Night 314

Snack Shapes

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Lunch. Look at the plate.

The crackers: squares.

Crunch. Now it's a broken square. New shape. Still a cracker.

The cucumber, sliced thin: circles.

"Wheel!" says Olivia.

Green wheels. Right.

The sandwich, cut corner to corner: triangles. Two of them.

Sails on a bread boat.

Olivia eats the point first. Then the wide part.

The cheese, in small bits: little squares.

The banana, in slices: circles.

One goes on her nose. Falls off.

Mama tries one on her nose. Falls off.

A cucumber wheel stays on Mama's nose.

"Nose wheel," says Olivia.

Lunch takes a while today.
