Night 146

The Runny Nose Day

Month 5: All About Me

Today the nose is runny.

Sniff. Sniff.

A little cold came to visit.

Even in July. Colds do that.

The nose runs. The tissue chases.

Wipe... "No!"

Mama stops.

"Sorry. That came at you fast."

"This tissue or that tissue?"

That one. Okay. Wipe.

Better.

Runny nose days are couch days.

Blanket. Books. Water in the red cup.

The body is busy inside,

Shooing the cold away.

It knows how.

It needs rest, and water, and somebody warm to lean on.

Mama is warm.

The fan turns its head. Left. Right.

Olivia leans. Sniff.
