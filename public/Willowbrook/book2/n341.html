Night 341

Papa's Watch at the Door

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Story read. Song sung.

Papa stands up.

"I'll be back."

He goes to the door.

Olivia's eyes go with him.

He steps out.

One... two... three.

He steps back in.

"Back."

"Papa," says Olivia. Not worried. Checking.

"I'll be back."

Out. One... two... three... four... five.

Back. "Told you."

Her eyes are heavier now.

"I'll be back."

Out. Longer this time.

The kettle, the dishes, the sock on the stairs.

Back.

Her eyes are shut.

Papa stands at the door anyway.

He watches her breathe. Up. Down.

Just because.

Then the door pulls to. Soft click.
