Night 335

The Same Every Night

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

"No bed," says Olivia.

Two words. New ones.

Mama nods. "Not yet. First bath."

Every night goes the same way.

Bath: splish.

Pajamas: zzzip.

Milk. Teeth: scrub-scrub.

Story. Song.

Olivia knows the whole road.

She is already at the tub before the water is.

She knows where the road ends, too.

At the crib, she stops.

"No bed."

"Bed," says Mama. "Bear's already in."

The bear is already in.

His ear is up, waiting.

Olivia looks at the ear.

She lifts her arms.

Up, over, in.

Her hand finds the ear.

Same as last night.
