Night 310

Rolling Things

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Papa has a question. What rolls?

The ball: rrrrr, all the way down the hall. Rolls. Of course.

The orange: rolls. Wobble, wobble. Bumps into the wall.

The cup, tipped on its side: rolls in a circle, all around itself. Then stops.

The block.

Push. Flop. Sits.

Push harder. Flop. Sits.

"No roll," says Olivia.

Papa tries. Flop.

The cat comes to see. The cat pushes the ball. Rrrrr.

"Ball go!"

Olivia goes after the ball. The cat goes after Olivia.

The block stays where it is.

Olivia comes back and sits on it.

It stays.
