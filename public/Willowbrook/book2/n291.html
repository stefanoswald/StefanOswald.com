Night 291

Dark and Light Make Friends

Month 10: Opposites

Lamp on: the whole room. Crib. Chair. The rabbit on the shelf, ears up.

Then off.

Lamp off: dark.

Wait.

The tiny moon night light was on the whole time. It needed the dark.

Slowly, the room comes back. Grey, not gone.

The chair: there. The rabbit's ears: there, two points.

Bobby Bear's ear: here, in her hand. No looking needed.

Dark: the corners.

Light: one small gold circle on the wall.

Two green eyes at the door. The cat. She is dark too, all but the eyes.

Hall light, a stripe along the floor.

Dark room. Light stripe. Gold circle. Green eyes.

The eyes blink out. The cat has gone to her bed.

Olivia's eyes go too.
