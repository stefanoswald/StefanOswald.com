Night 315

The Zigzag Path

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

The park path by the pond does a funny thing.

It bends. This way. That way. This way again.

Trodden snow. Creak, creak, under the boots.

Olivia walks the zigs, one hand in Papa's. "Dis way."

Olivia walks the zags. "Dat way."

Papa tugs toward the corner, straight through the snow. He's cold.

"No!" Olivia stops. She points at the bend. "Dat way."

Papa looks at his own straight tracks.

"I skipped a zag," he says. "You're right."

He goes back. He walks the bend, tipping over sideways.

Zig. Zag. Zig.

The pond beside them: grey, still.

Nobody walks on that. Not even ducks.

At the end of the path they look back.

Two sets of tracks. Both bendy.
