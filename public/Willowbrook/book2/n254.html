Night 254

Half for You, Half for Me

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Rosa came by with one cookie, warm, and flour on one cheek.

One cookie.

Two friends.

Two hands out, already.

Mama holds it up.

"Half for you, half for you," she says. "None for me."

Crack.

Two pieces.

Olivia gets one half.

Lily gets one half.

They compare them. Closely.

Same? Same-ish.

Lily's half has more chips.

Lily's half is gone first.

Munch.

Munch.

Olivia's half takes longer.

She has a system: chips first, then the rest.

Lily watches the system.

Lily's face says she would like a system.

Crumbs on the table. Olivia points at Lily's chin.

"Crumb."

Lily wipes it with her whole arm.

The plate is empty. Mama takes it away.
