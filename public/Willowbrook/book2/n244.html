Night 244

One Is Olivia

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Morning. Still dark out. Lamp on.

The first number is one.

One is the finger trick.

Hold it up. There.

"One!"

One nose. Tap.

One bear, in the crib.

One red cup, by the sink.

One Mama, doing the buttons.

One Papa, in the kitchen. Eggs.

Mama lifts her up to the mirror.

One girl in there.

One finger in there too.

Olivia looks hard.

"One Livy."

Two words, stuck together. The first ones.

Mama says nothing.

Her face says plenty.

The finger taps the mirror finger.

Tap.
