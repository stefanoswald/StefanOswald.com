Night 323

Tracing Papa's Hand

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

Papa puts his hand flat on the paper.

Olivia holds the crayon. Papa's other hand helps, a little.

Round the thumb. Up the finger. Down. Up the next one.

The crayon tickles. Papa's hand jumps.

The line goes off the side.

"That was me, it tickled," says Papa. "Keep going."

Round the last finger. Down. Done.

A Papa hand. With one bump where it jumped.

"Papa hand," says Olivia.

Now hers. Inside his.

Fingers spread wide. The crayon goes round. Small thumb, small fingers, done.

"Livy hand."

A small hand inside a big one.

The bump stays. Papa likes the bump.

The paper goes on the fridge, beside the duck.

The fridge understands.
