Night 70

The Quietest Sound

Month 3: The Sounds of Everything

Some sounds are loud.

Some are little.

And one sound is so quiet

You have to watch for it.

Look.

Mrs. Henderson's tree, across the street, is letting go.

White petals. One, then lots.

Down they come.

No pit-pat. No drum.

Just hush.

They land on the grass. Hush.

They land on the path. Hush.

Quiet as the library.

One lands on the window, right by Olivia's nose.

She watches it. It does not move.

Mama turns the lamp low.

Hush. Hush.

Olivia's breath fogs the glass.
