Night 276

Hot and Cold

Month 10: Opposites

First snow. It came in the night.

Mama brings a bowl of it inside. A bowl of outside.

Touch.

"Cold!" Fingers back. Touch again. Still cold.

The stove: milk in the pot, little bubbles at the edge. Hot.

The stove is Mama's. Hands back.

Olivia puts her hands behind her back before Mama says it.

Cocoa, in the red cup. Steam.

Mama blows. Whoo. Olivia blows. Pfff.

Mama tests it on her wrist. Not yet. Then: now.

Both hands on the cup. Sip. Warm going down.

The bear gets a sip. Pretend. "Mmm."

The bowl of snow is smaller. Going. Water at the bottom.

"Snow wawa."

"The warm ate it," says Mama.

Olivia pokes the water. Still cold.
