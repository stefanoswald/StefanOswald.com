Night 111

Lily's Blue Day

Month 4: A World of Colors

Lily came to the park.

Blue jacket. Blue cup. Blue block.

But today Lily is not playing.

Lily is crying.

Nobody knows why.

Not Rosa. Not Mama. Not Lily.

Rosa sits close.

Waaah, goes Lily.

Olivia stops.

She looks at Lily for a long time.

Then she comes over.

Pat.

One pat, on the blue jacket.

Waaah, goes Lily.

Pat. Pat.

"Wiwi," says Olivia.

Lily cries a little less.

Then a little less.

Then a hiccup. Hic.

Then nothing.

Two of them on the blanket.

The blue block between them.

Nobody says anything about it.
