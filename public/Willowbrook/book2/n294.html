Night 294

The Middle

Month 10: Opposites

Bath. Mama runs it.

Hand in. Too hot. More cold.

Hand in. Too cold. More hot.

Hand in.

"Just right," says Mama.

Olivia's hand in, Mama holding her steady.

"Warm."

In she goes.

Porridge this morning: too hot. Then it sat by the window. Too cold. Then the middle, and eaten.

Papa's chair: too big. The bear's chair: too little. Her chair: the middle.

The three bears in the book know all about it.

The water goes cool.

One more minute, then out.

Just right for one more minute.

Olivia lies back in it, up to her ears.

Mama's hand stays under her head.
