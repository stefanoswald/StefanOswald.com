Night 283

Heavy and Light

Month 10: Opposites

Snow at the farm. The hens stay in. Feed time.

Olivia wants the pail.

"Two hands," says Grandpa Joe.

Two hands. Lift.

It does not come up. She leans back. Up a little. Waddle, waddle. Down.

Grandpa waits. He does not take it.

Waddle, waddle. Down.

The coop is far when the pail is heavy.

There. Tip. Feed everywhere.

On the straw, a feather. White.

Grandpa holds it out on one finger.

She takes it. So light her hand is not sure it has anything.

Blow. Up it goes. Down, onto her hat.

Grandpa winks.

The empty pail swings back to the barn.

Hands at the pump. Splash, splash. Coop hands, off.

The feather rides home in the hat.
