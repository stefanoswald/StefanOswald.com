Night 290

The Opposite Game

Month 10: Opposites

Supper. The game.

Papa calls it. "Big voice!"

"HELLO!" Everybody. The soup shakes.

Mama calls it. "Little voice."

"hello." Everybody. The soup does not notice.

Fast bites: nibble-nibble-nibble.

Slow bites: chewwww.

Hands up! Hands down.

Eyes closed. Eyes... open. Everyone peeked. Everyone always peeks.

Olivia's turn to call.

"Big!"

"HELLO!"

"Little."

"hello."

"Big!"

"hello," says Papa, tiny.

"No! Big!"

"HELLO," says Papa. "Sorry." He gets it wrong a lot.

Happy face. Grumpy face. The grumpy faces fall apart into giggles.

One more round, Mama says. Then bath.

"Big!"

Papa does his grumpy face at the soup. The soup does not care.
