Night 256

The Counting Caterpillar

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

The fuzzy caterpillar is back.

The one from the fence, weeks ago.

Fatter now.

He is crossing the big leaf on the garden bed.

We look. We don't touch. Fuzz can itch.

And he walks in counts. Watch.

Scrunch. "One!"

Stretch.

Scrunch. "Two!"

Stretch.

Scrunch. "Two!"

He is the slowest counter in the whole garden.

He never rushes past a number.

Scrunch... Mama says three.

Scrunch... four.

Scrunch... five.

Off the leaf. Onto the dirt.

Under the leaf pile at the edge of the bed.

Gone.

One stripe shows. Then nothing.

"Gone."

"He's going to sleep till spring," says Mama. "Under there."

Olivia puts one leaf on top. Gently.

Then another.

The Counting Caterpillar has a blanket.
