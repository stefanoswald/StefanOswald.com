Night 340

When Sleep Won't Come

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Lights out. Door pulled to.

Then, from the crib:

"Mama?"

Mama comes. No light.

"It's still night. I'm here."

The blanket, re-tucked.

The bear, moved closer.

A hand on her back. Hmm-hmm-hmm.

Then Mama goes.

A minute.

"Mama!"

Mama comes. No light.

"Still night. Still here."

Blanket. Bear. Hand. Hmm-hmm.

Then Mama goes.

A longer minute.

"Ma..."

Mama comes. No light.

The blanket is already tucked.

The bear is already close.

The eyes are already shut.

Hmm-hmm, anyway.

Mama stands a moment in the dark.

The hallway light waits for her.
