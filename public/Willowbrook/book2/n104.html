Night 104

The Crayon Box

Month 4: A World of Colors

New in the house: crayons.

Fat ones.

Red crayon: scribble, scribble!

See what came out. Red!

Inside the crayon the whole time.

Blue crayon: scribble!

Then the blue one goes in her mouth.

Mama takes it out. "Crayons are for paper."

Olivia goes stiff. Red face.

"NO!"

The crayon flies.

Under the fridge, where crayons live now.

Then the crying. Big crying.

Arms up.

Mama gets down low. Close.

"You wanted the crayon. So mad."

"Red on the paper, or green on the paper?"

Olivia grabs red.

Scribble. Scribble. Round and round.

"What is it?" asks Mama.

It is a duck.

It is always a duck.

On the fridge it goes.

Above the blue crayon.
