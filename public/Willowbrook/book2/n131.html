Night 131

The Voice That Grew Words

Month 5: All About Me

Inside Olivia lives a voice.

It started tiny.

Just cries. Then coos.

Then ba-ba-ba, all day.

And now, listen.

"Mama!"

"More!"

"Duck!"

"Up!"

Words.

They come like the tomatoes on the vine.

Slow. Then all at once.

Where do they come from?

From every word she ever heard.

They all went in.

Now they are coming out.

Mama points at the window. "Bird."

Olivia looks. Looks a long time.

"Buh."

Close.

That one is still on the vine.
