Night 257

Ten Fingers of Paint

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

Paint day. Rain on the window. Cold rain.

Inside: warm, and paint.

Dip one finger. Press.

One dot.

"One!"

Dip two fingers. Press, press.

Two dots.

"Two!"

Now the whole hand.

Five dots at once. A five-dot flower.

Other hand: five more.

Count the dots.

"One, two..."

"Three, four, five," sings Mama, "six, seven, eight, nine, ten."

Ten dots. Ten fingers.

"More paint."

Dip. Press. Dot.

Dip. Press. Dot.

Then one finger goes somewhere new.

Nose.

One blue dot, on the nose.

"Nose!"

Mama looks at the nose for a long moment.

"That one doesn't go on the fridge," she says.

The paper does. Next to the duck.
