Night 353

The Maybe Story

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Tonight, the special story.

Not from a book. From the family.

"Once, we spilled the milk.

The whole cup. Remember?"

"Uh-oh," says Olivia.

"Uh-oh is right. We mopped it up.

That made us late going out.

And because we were late..."

"Da TRUCK!"

"...the snowplow came by!

The big one. Lights blinking.

You waved. He waved back."

Olivia waves at nobody. Practicing.

"So. Was the spilled milk bad?"

Wait for it.

"MAYBE!" shouts Olivia.

Maybe.

The family word. She has it now.

"Maybe," she says again, softer,

Holding it like the bear.

Mama turns the lamp down.
