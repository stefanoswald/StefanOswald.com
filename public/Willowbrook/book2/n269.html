Night 269

Counting Backward to Sleep

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

The day counts down now. Backward. Softly.

FIVE kisses.

Mwah, mwah, mwah, mwah, mwah.

FOUR pats for the bear.

Olivia does those. Pat, pat, pat, pat.

"Two," she says. It was four. Two is fine.

THREE tucks for the blanket.

Tuck. Tuck. Tuck.

TWO whispers.

"Nigh-nigh."

"Love you."

And ONE.

Click.

The lamp.

The tiny moon night light is still on. It is always on.

The bear's ear is in her hand.

Five, four, three, two, one.

Mama stands in the door a moment, counting nothing.
