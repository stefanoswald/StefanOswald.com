Night 297

Give and Take

Month 10: Opposites

Lily is here. One blue block. Two hands on it.

"Mine!"

"Mine!"

Pull. Pull.

Mama gets down on the rug. She does not take the block.

"One block. Two friends. Olivia's turn first. Then Lily's."

Olivia has it. She builds. Lily watches the block. Only the block.

Olivia stops. Pats Lily's arm, pat, pat. Goes back to the block.

"Lily's turn when you're done," says Mama.

Olivia holds on. Builds.

Mama waits. Lily waits. Lily's lip.

Olivia looks at Lily. Looks at the block.

Down it goes, on the rug.

Lily's hand. Gone.

"Lily's turn," says Mama. "Then Livy's."

Lily bangs the block twice. Drops it.

It rolls. Olivia takes it.

"Livy turn."

Lily has found the red cup.
