Night 245

Two Is a Pair

Month 9: One, Two, Three!

After one comes two.

Two is a pair.

Two hands. Tap, tap.

Two feet. Stomp, stomp.

Two eyes. Blink, blink.

Two ears. "One, two!"

The bear has two ears too.

One floppy. Still counts.

Two boots by the door, waiting for the cold.

Two socks in the drawer.

Usually. Socks wander.

Two mittens on a string.

Where is the other mitten?

Up the sleeve. There.

Two knees. Two elbows.

Two of everything, nearly.

Two, two, two, all morning.

Mama and Olivia on the rug.

"Two," says Olivia. Pat for Mama. Pat for Livy.

Mama pats back.

Two pats.
