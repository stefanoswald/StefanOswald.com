Night 329

Round and Round the Garden

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

One more circle. The oldest one.

Open your hand.

Here comes Mama's finger.

"Round and round the garden, like a teddy bear..."

Round and round the palm.

"One step..."

Up the arm.

"Two step..."

Higher.

"And tickle you under there!"

Under the arm. Shrieks. Collapse.

"'Gain!"

Round and round the garden...

The third time, Olivia takes Mama's hand and turns it over.

Her finger goes round Mama's palm. Round, round.

"One... two..."

"Tickle!"

Mama shrieks. Mama collapses. Mama is very ticklish, it turns out. Or a good sport.

Now the bear's paw. Round and round.

The bear does not shriek. Bears never do.

Round and round. Slower now.

Lamp off.
