Night 321

The Crescent Moon Boat

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

The Moon is thin tonight.

A little silver curve, lying on its back over the roofs.

Mama and Olivia at the cold window.

What does it look like?

"Nana!" says Olivia.

A banana. Points for that one.

"Or a boat," says Mama. "A little silver boat."

Who rides in the moon boat?

Dreams do. They climb in every night.

The Moon rocks them. Rock, rock.

She hums her one hum and sails them slow over the white roofs, over the white fields, over the farm, to morning.

"Boat," says Olivia. She rocks. Rock, rock.

Mama rocks too.

Into the crib. Rock, rock.

Up there, the thin silver boat waits for passengers.

Shhh.
