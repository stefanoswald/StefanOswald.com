Night 338

The Story Chooser

Month 12: Bedtime Itself

Which story?

Olivia goes to the basket.

Out comes the duck book.

Out comes the tractor book.

Out comes the bear book.

She looks. She looks again.

This takes a while.

Papa waits on the floor.

He has a guess.

Up goes one book, high.

The bear book.

(Papa guessed right. Again.)

Onto the lap.

Papa reads. He knows it by heart.

So does she.

Page three... page four... page six.

"No!" A finger on the page. "Dat."

Papa looks. He skipped one.

"I skipped the honey page. Sorry."

Back a page.

The honey page, read properly.

Olivia holds the corner.

She is ready to turn it.

Not yet.
