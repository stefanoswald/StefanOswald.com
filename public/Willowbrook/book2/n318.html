Night 318

The Shape of a Hug

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

This shape is not in the puzzle box.

Watch.

Mama's arms go round Olivia.

Papa's arms go round them both.

Olivia is in the very middle.

"Livy middle," she says.

What shape is it? Round, mostly. Warm, all through.

Squeeze.

"Mmmph," says Papa. His beard is in her hair. It scratches.

"No beard," says Olivia.

Papa moves his chin.

A bump at the bottom of the shape. The cat, pushing in.

The cat did not ask.

The lamp is on the other side of it, warm on everybody's backs.

Squeeze, one more.

Then everybody lets go. Olivia doesn't. Not yet.
