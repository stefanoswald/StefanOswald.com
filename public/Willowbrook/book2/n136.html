Night 136

Livy in the Mirror

Month 5: All About Me

In the mirror lives a girl.

Olivia has waved at her forever.

Today, sunscreen before the park.

Mama rubs it in. Cheeks. Arms. Ears.

One white dot is left on Olivia's nose.

Now, the mirror.

The girl in there has a dot too.

Olivia goes still.

She looks at that girl a long time.

Not so sure about her today.

One hand comes up, slow.

It touches... her own nose.

The dot comes off on her finger.

The girl's dot is gone too.

"Livy," says Mama, and points at the glass. "That's you."

Olivia points. At the girl. At herself.

Same finger. Same girl.

"Livy."

Mama rubs the rest of the dot in.

Nose done. Hat on.
