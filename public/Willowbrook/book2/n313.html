Night 313

The Puzzle's First Piece

Month 11: Shapes Everywhere

A new thing on the rug. A puzzle. Three pieces, three holes.

The circle piece is in Olivia's hand.

She pushes it at the square hole. No.

Mama's hand comes out to help. Mama's hand goes back.

"Sorry. Yours."

Mama sits on the couch with her tea.

The triangle hole. No.

The circle piece goes flat on the board. Slides. Slides.

The round hole.

CLICK.

Olivia looks at Mama. Mama looks at Olivia.

"In," says Olivia.

"You slid it," says Mama. "Till it dropped."

Then the square. Bump. Bump. Bump.

It does not go.

The triangle. Bump.

Not today.

Olivia tips the whole board over.

Three pieces on the rug.

"More."
