Night 198

The Lost Voice Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

On Saturday Papa went to Mateo's first soccer game and cheered so loud he lost his voice. By supper it was a croak. By bedtime it was nothing at all.

He held up the moose book and made his biggest, growliest face, and no growl came out.

"I want da growly voice!" Olivia's eyes filled. "You do da moose."

Papa nodded. He mouthed, very slowly, "I know. You love the growl." Then he put his arm around her.

When she was calmer, he mouthed one more word. "Maybe."

"Maybe what?"

Papa shrugged. He opened the book and pointed at her.

So Olivia did the moose. She did not read the words. She told what the pictures said, the way she remembered it. "Da moose is hungry. He goes to da lake. Uh-oh, no lake." She did the growl herself, low in her throat.

Papa did the moose's ears with his hands. Theo sat on Papa's knee and watched her mouth the whole time.

At the end Theo tapped his fingertips together, tap, tap. It was his sign for more.

Papa looked at Olivia. Olivia looked at Papa.

Papa, with no voice at all, turned back to page one.
