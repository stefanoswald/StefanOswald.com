Night 8

The Grocery Store Under the Table

Month 1: Pretend!

Under the table, there was a store. The tablecloth made the walls. The shelves held real cans, play fruit, and one actual potato. The sign was a crayon potato. You paid in paper money, with crayon on it.

"Papa! Da store is open!"

Up above the store, Papa was reading the newspaper.

"Mm-hm," said the newspaper.

"Papa. You can buy da potato."

"Mm-hm."

Olivia waited. She lined up the cans. She waited some more. At last she crawled out, stood up, and pushed the newspaper down with one finger.

"You not looking at me."

Papa looked at her. Then he folded the paper and put it on the floor.

"You called me, and I kept reading," he said. "I'm sorry."

"You was reading a long time."

"I was. Is the store still open?"

She checked on the potato. "Yes."

Papa got down on his knees and crawled under the tablecloth. Bonk, went his head.

"This store is smaller on the inside," he said.

He bought the potato for four paper dollars. The shopkeeper gave him nine back, because this store gives very good change.

"Thank you! Come again!"

He came again. He bought the beans. Then he bought the potato back.

The newspaper stayed on the floor till bedtime.
