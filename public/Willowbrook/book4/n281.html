Night 281

The Sign on the Door

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Olivia had built a whole town on her bedroom floor, with a shoebox bakery and a road made of books. Theo had walked through it twice.

"He's a giant," she said. "A wrecking giant."

"A small one," said Papa.

"I need a sign on my door. Dat says keep out."

So Papa wrote KEEP OUT in big letters, and Olivia drew a red hand under it, the stop kind. They taped it to her door.

"Now he has to," said Olivia.

Theo came down the hall with his cow. He looked at the sign. He looked at the red hand. Then he walked straight in and sat down on the bakery.

"FEE-OH! It says keep OUT!"

"He can't read yet," said Papa.

Olivia turned to the sign. "Den who is it for?"

"People who can read," said Papa. "I've been staying out all afternoon."

That didn't help with Theo.

So they tried another way. Olivia gave Theo a corner of his own, by the bed, with the three biggest blocks. He knocked them down and laughed. He built them up and knocked them down.

He stayed in his corner. Mostly.

"Write more," said Olivia. "Say except Fee-oh. Sometimes."

Papa wrote it at the bottom, small.

Theo was in his corner, building.
