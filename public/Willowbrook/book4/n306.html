Night 306

The Chrysalis Questions

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Olivia carried the butterfly book home from the library herself, with her own card on top where she could see it.

At the lamp, Papa found the page with the chrysalis.

"Does she eat in dere?"

"No. She ate plenty before she went in."

"Does she sleep?"

"Sort of. She's very busy holding still."

It sounded like a Papa joke. His face said it wasn't.

"Busy doing what?"

Papa read the next part to himself first, and then made it smaller. "She's changing. Almost all of her is turning into something new."

"Almost all?"

"A few tiny parts stay the same the whole time. The new wings grow from those."

Olivia put her finger on the picture. "Da Bella-est parts."

"Those," said Papa.

"Does it hurt?"

"I don't think so. It's the thing her body knows how to do."

"When does she come out?"

"Usually in spring. What happens then?"

She knew this one. "Her wings are all crumply. Den dey get big. Den she flies to da flowers."

"That's how the book goes," said Papa.

She made him read the chrysalis page again, and then once more, slower. He left the book open on the chair when he turned off the lamp.
