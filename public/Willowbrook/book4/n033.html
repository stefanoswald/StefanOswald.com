Night 33

Telling Theo About Dream-Baba

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Some news is too big to keep. Olivia lay down on the rug, nose to nose with Theo, and told him.

"Fee-oh. Listen. Baba walks. In my dreams."

Theo looked at her. He looked at the bear. Then he put a block in his mouth.

"Not dat. Listen."

He took the block out. He had one in each hand, and he banged them together. Clack. Clack. Clack.

"He says yes," Olivia told Mama.

"He likes your voice," said Mama. "I think he likes the clacking too."

Olivia told him the rest. The brook, the glowing fish, the stone that went plop. Theo listened to about half. Then he saw Papa's slipper by the couch, and he was gone, crawling fast with his bottom in the air.

Olivia crawled after him with Baba in one hand. It is hard to tell a secret to somebody who keeps leaving.

She caught up at the slipper. "Fee-oh. Does your cow walk too?"

Theo sat down hard. "Ba-ba-ba," he said.

Olivia's mouth fell open. "Mama. He said Baba."

"He said ba," said Mama. "Three times."

"I'll look for your cow tonight," Olivia told him.

Theo chewed Baba's ear.
