Night 82

Why Does Bread Rise

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Thursday afternoon in Rosa's kitchen. The dough had gone into the bowl small, and now it was puffed up to the top under its towel, round as a belly.

"Rosa. Why did da bread get big?"

"There's yeast in it," said Rosa. "Tiny living things, too small to see."

"Alive?"

"Alive. They eat the sugar in the dough and breathe out little bubbles, and the bubbles puff it up."

Olivia put her nose right down to the dough. It smelled warm and a little sour.

"Da bread is breathing?"

"In a way," said Rosa. "Now punch it down. Gently."

Olivia punched. The dough sighed and went flat.

"You pushed with your whole hand," said Rosa. "That gets the big bubbles out."

"Now what?"

"Now it rises again. We wait about an hour."

Olivia lifted the towel to look. Nothing. She lifted it again. Still nothing.

"Every time you peek, the warm gets out," said Rosa.

So Olivia sat on her hands. This was very hard. Rosa hummed, and the oven ticked, and the kitchen clock went around.

When Rosa finally lifted the towel, the dough was round again, all the way to the top.

"Dey did it again!"

Rosa had flour on her cheek. Now so did Olivia.

Neither of them wiped it off.
