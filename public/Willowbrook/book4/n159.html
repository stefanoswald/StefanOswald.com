Night 159

Buttons Gets Out

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Sam was the hamster helper that day. He opened the cage door to give Buttons a seed, and Buttons took the seed and kept going.

He ran along the wall. He ran under the bookshelf. Somebody shrieked, three children started to chase him, and Buttons shot under the easel and stayed there.

"Statues," said Miss Dana. "Everybody, right where you are."

Everybody froze. Marcus froze on one foot, which lasted about two seconds.

Olivia crouched down by Lily. "Quiet bodies," she said, very low. "He's scared of us."

They waited. The clock on the wall ticked. Nobody breathed very much.

Then a pink nose came out from under the easel. Then whiskers. Then the rest of Buttons.

Miss Dana laid a cup on its side, and Buttons walked into it as if it had been his own idea.

Sam was crying by the cage, his face all red. At snack, Olivia sat down next to him. She didn't say anything. She slid her napkin over to him.

Sam wiped his nose on it. Then he almost smiled.

After that, the cage door had a clip.
