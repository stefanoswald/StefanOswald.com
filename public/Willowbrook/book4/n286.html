Night 286

Olivia Makes a Book

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Mama folded four sheets of paper in half and stapled them down the middle. "A book with nothing in it yet."

Olivia filled it.

On the front she drew a bear with a round head, a round body and two legs, and a whistle in his mouth. Mama wrote the words she asked for across the top: THE BEAR WHO LOST HIS WHISTLE.

Inside, the whistle was gone. The bear looked under a chair, then in a boot.

"This sounds a little like the button book," said Papa.

"It's a different bear," said Olivia.

On the last page she wrote OLIVIA, as big as it would go.

At supper she held it facing out, like Miss Dana, and told each page from the pictures.

"Den he looked in da boot. No whistle. Just a foot smell. Den he finded it on his pillow."

"He found it on his pillow?" said Mama.

"He was sleeping on it da whole time."

Papa snorted into his milk.

Theo reached for the book with both hands. "More!"

She told it four more times. By the end, Theo was holding it upside down.

At bedtime it went on the shelf, next to the bear book. It was the thinnest one there.
