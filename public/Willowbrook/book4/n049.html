Night 49

The Moth Incident

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

There was a moth in the meadow one night, big and soft, glowing a little at the edges.

Baba stopped walking so fast that his flopped ear swung.

"Baba?"

"Moth," he said, without moving his mouth much. "I don't like dem."

"How come?"

"One sat on my nose once. On da dresser. All night. I couldn't move. Bears aren't allowed to move."

Olivia looked at him. His paws were shaking, just a little.

"You're scared," she said.

"Yes."

She got down low beside him and held his paw. She didn't say anything for a while. The moth bumbled along over the grass, up and down, up and down.

"We can just watch it," she said. "From here."

They watched. The moth came closer.

Olivia held out one finger, slowly. The moth landed on it. It was so light she could hardly tell it was there.

Baba leaned in, a little. The moth opened its wings, and shut them, and flew off over the brook.

"It had fuzzy feet," said Baba.

His paw had stopped shaking.

It stayed in hers.
