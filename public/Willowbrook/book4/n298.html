Night 298

The Poem That Happened

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Olivia stood at the window, talking to the snow.

"Snow comes down. Snow comes slow. Snow on da roof and on da..." She stopped and spun around. "Mama! Write dat! Quick!"

Mama grabbed a slip from the word jar pile and a pencil. "Say it again."

"Snow comes down. Snow comes slow." Olivia frowned at the window. "Den dere was more."

"On the roof," said Mama.

"On da roof and on da," said Olivia, and stopped. It was gone. It had gone right out of her head and into the snow somewhere.

"It got away," she said.

"That happens to poems," said Mama. "It happens to grown-ups too."

"Is dat a poem?"

"It has a bounce. I think it's a poem."

Olivia said it again, bouncing on her toes. "Snow comes down. Snow comes slow." She stopped. "Slow and snow. Dey sound da same at da end."

"They rhyme," said Mama.

"Rhyme," said Olivia. That one went in the jar too.

She watched the window for the rest of it all afternoon. It didn't come back.

At bedtime she had an idea. "Leave room at da end. In case it comes back."

Mama left a space at the bottom, for it.
