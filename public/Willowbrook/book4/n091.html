Night 91

The Dark Gets a Name

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

It was June. Bedtime came, the sky outside was still pale, and Olivia was not pleased about it.

"Why is it still day?"

"In summer the day stays up late," said Mama. "Watch it get tired."

They watched from the window. The light went thin, then gray, then blue. The dark came in the way it always does, from the corners first.

"I don't like da dark," said Olivia.

"The dark can feel scary," said Mama. She didn't hurry past it. "Want to know its other name?"

"What?"

"It's just the day, resting. Nothing goes anywhere. Look." Mama switched off the lamp.

The chair was still there, only dimmer. "Da chair is resting," said Olivia. The shelf was resting. The toy box was resting with its lid shut, which is how toy boxes do it.

"Baba's not resting," said Olivia. "He's working."

"What about the cat?"

The cat was on the rug, where she had been resting since lunch.

Olivia pulled the blanket up to her chin. "Da blanket is resting on me."

"It is," said Mama.

She stayed until Olivia's hands were still.
