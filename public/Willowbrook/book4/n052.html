Night 52

Teaching Baba to Whistle

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Olivia can whistle now. It took her most of a year, and it comes out thin and a little wet, but it is a whistle.

In the meadow, she decided Baba should learn.

"Make your mouth small," she said. "Like dis. Den blow soft."

Baba made his mouth as small as a bear's mouth goes. He blew.

Pffffff.

"Softer."

Pffffffffff. His cheeks puffed out and his ear flapped up.

The next night it sounded like a sneeze in a sock.

"Bears can't whistle," said Baba, and he sat down hard in the grass.

Olivia sat down next to him. "Not yet," she said. "Mine took forever."

On the third night Baba did something with his nose. Nobody showed him. A thin, wobbly note came out of it, and it was, without any doubt, a whistle.

Olivia stared. "Dat's not how."

"It's how bears do it," said Baba, and did it again.

In the morning Papa was at the sink, trying to whistle. Papa cannot whistle. It comes out as air.

"Papa. Try your nose."

Papa tried his nose. Out came a snort so big that Theo, sitting on the rug, laughed and toppled over sideways.

Papa went back to air.
