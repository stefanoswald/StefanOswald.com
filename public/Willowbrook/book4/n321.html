Night 321

The Soup Day

Month 11: Winter Wonders

A gray Sunday, cold to the bone, meant soup day. Out came the big pot.

Everybody had a job. Mama had the sharp knife and the stove. Olivia had a butter knife and a pile of mushrooms, and she cut every one into pieces her own size. Theo had a bowl and a bunch of kale.

"Theo, put it in the bowl," said Mama.

Theo tore off a leaf and put it in the bowl. Then he put a piece in his mouth, made a face, and put that in the bowl too. Mama fished that one out.

The onions went in first. "Dat's da smell dat means soup," said Olivia. Then everything else, and then the long wait while the windows steamed.

At dusk they filled jars and went out in their hats. One went across the street to Mrs. Henderson. One went to June's house, where June's mama opened the door with the new baby asleep on her shoulder. She looked at the warm jar for a long moment.

"Oh," she said. "Oh, thank you."

On the walk home, Olivia swung Mama's hand. "Why so much soup?"

"So it goes around," said Mama.

The big pot soaked in the sink all night.
