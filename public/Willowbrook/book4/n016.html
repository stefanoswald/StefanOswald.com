Night 16

The Farm Pretend

Month 1: Pretend!

On Sunday, Olivia came to the farm with her star wheelbarrow and an announcement.

"Gampa. I'm a farmer today."

Grandpa Joe looked at her boots. He nodded once and handed her a real bucket of grain.

"Hens," he said.

The hens came running before she had the gate open. They crowded her boots, all talking at once. Olivia held the bucket up high.

Grandpa stood next to her. He did not take the bucket. He just stood there.

Olivia threw a handful. The hens ran after it. She threw another. They ran the other way. She laughed.

Then there was straw to carry to the muddy run. She filled her wheelbarrow to the top and pushed. It tipped over in the mud, straw and all.

Olivia looked at Grandpa. Grandpa looked at the straw.

So she picked it up in armfuls and carried it, a little at a time. Grandpa waited.

Afterward they washed their hands at the pump and sat on the porch step with mud to their knees.

"Gampa. Is dis pretend farming? Or real?"

Grandpa looked at her boots, then at his own. They were the same color.

"Real," Olivia said.

"Yep," said Grandpa.

He gave her one egg to carry to the car. She held it in both hands, all the way.
