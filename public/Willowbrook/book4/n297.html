Night 297

Her Own Order

Month 10: Words Are Magic

In the car, Olivia practiced. "I would like da grilled cheese, please. I would like da grilled cheese, please."

At the diner the waitress came with a pencil behind her ear. "What can I get you folks?"

Olivia took a big breath.

"She'll have the grilled cheese," said Papa, "and he'll have the soup."

Olivia's face crumpled. She slid down in the booth until only her eyes showed over the table.

Papa saw her face. "Could you give us one minute?" he asked the waitress.

He bent down to Olivia's eyes. "You wanted to say it yourself, and I jumped in. I'm sorry."

Olivia came up a little.

"Want to try again?" said Papa.

The waitress came back and bent down too. "What can I get you, miss?"

Olivia's voice started small and got bigger. "I would like da grilled cheese. Please."

"Triangles or squares?"

Olivia hadn't practiced that part. She looked at Papa. Papa looked at the ceiling.

"Triangles," said Olivia.

The waitress wrote it down like any other order. Then she looked at Theo.

Theo banged his spoon on his tray. "Ball!"

"One ball," said the waitress, and wrote that down too.

When the grilled cheese came, it was in triangles. Olivia ate the points off first.
