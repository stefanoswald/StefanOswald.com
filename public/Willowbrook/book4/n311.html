Night 311

The Ice Window

Month 11: Winter Wonders

The big puddle at the edge of Maple Lane froze clear on the coldest morning yet.

Olivia got down to it in stages. Knees. Tummy. Nose.

"Mama! It's a window!"

Under the ice lay three brown leaves, stopped in the middle of floating, and a scatter of white dots.

"What are da dots?"

"Air bubbles," said Mama. "They got caught when the water froze."

"Are dey stuck forever?"

"Until it warms up. Then they float up and pop."

Olivia pressed her mitten flat on the ice. "Can I take it home?"

"You can try," said Mama. She stood on the street side of her and waited.

Olivia worked her mitten under one edge and lifted. The window cracked into three pieces, with a sound like a cookie.

"I breaked it!" Her chin went hard.

"It broke," said Mama. "Ice does that. You're mad."

"I'm MAD."

Mama crouched and stayed with her.

After a while, Olivia picked up the biggest piece and held it to her eye. Through it the sky was wobbly, and all the houses leaned.

"Da houses are wiggly," she said. "Look."

Mama looked. They were.

Olivia carried the piece home in both mittens. It was smaller by the corner, and water by the porch.
