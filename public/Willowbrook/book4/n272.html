Night 272

One Brave Step

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In the bath, Mama asked, "What was scary this month?"

Olivia blew a hole in the bubbles. "Da deep end. I held da wall."

"All the way to the corner." Mama rinsed one of her shoulders. "What else?"

"Da circle. My hand only went up halfway."

"Halfway still got you picked."

"And da storm. We counted, and Fee-oh said boom."

"What about the morning your words went away?"

Olivia sank down until the bubbles touched her nose. "Next time I look at your face."

There was more. The night at Lily's under the crooked stars. The dark basement stairs, and the furnace that turned out to be loud and warm. The sorry at the blocks, and the garage with a door that mostly opened. The lantern walk, and the helper with the stroller. The pages that stuck together, and how she kept turning anyway.

"Which part was hardest?" said Mama.

Olivia looked at her wrinkly fingers. "Da first page. Den my hands knew how."

"Brave is scared plus one step," said Mama.

"One step," said Olivia. "Den look for da next one."

Mama filled the cup, and Olivia shut her eyes.
