Night 156

The Hard Drop-Off

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The first morning was new. The second morning was fine. On the third morning, at the classroom door, Olivia held on to Mama's shirt with her whole fist.

"I don't want school. I want home."

Mama knelt down. "You want home, and you want me. That's a big want."

Olivia cried harder. Bobby Bear got squashed between them.

Mama didn't hurry, and she didn't stay long either. She kissed the top of Olivia's head. She gave her a squeeze. Then she said the part they had practiced.

"I always come back."

Miss Dana was already there, down low. "Want to watch for her at the window?"

They watched together. Mama went past on the sidewalk and waved, and Olivia waved back with Bobby Bear's paw.

"Now," said Miss Dana, "Buttons needs fresh water. I need somebody with careful hands."

Olivia carried the little water dish across the room with both hands. It sloshed, and nothing spilled. Then they washed their hands at the little sink.

At pickup, Miss Dana said Olivia had cried until the water dish, and then she was done. Mama did not say how long she had taken.

Olivia came out holding Bobby Bear by one leg.
