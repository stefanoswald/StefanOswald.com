Night 295

The Book Hospital

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The bear book came apart on a Wednesday. The back of it let go, and the pages hung out of the cover like a loose tooth.

"It's broken," said Olivia. "Da oldest book."

"It's been read a lot," said Mama. "Let's take it to Mr. Petrov."

Mr. Petrov's window says SHOES, ZIPPERS, AND MOST THINGS. He put on his glasses and turned the book over slowly, the way a doctor turns over a hand.

"This book has been read," he said. "Many times."

"Every night," said Olivia. "Since before me."

"That's the best way to break a book," said Mr. Petrov.

He got out a roll of cloth tape. Olivia held the pages flat while he pressed the tape down the spine. Her hands stayed very still. He pressed it again with the side of his thumb.

"Now it sits," he said. "Glue is slow."

While it sat, Olivia turned to the button page. The shiny tape from the time it tore was still there.

"It has two fixes now," she said.

"Only two?" said Mr. Petrov. "I have a shoe with seven."

When they got home, the book would not quite close flat.

That night Mama read it very gently.
