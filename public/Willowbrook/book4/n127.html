Night 127

The Two-Kid Errand

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Mama had one list, two children, and forty minutes until nap time.

"I'll do da list," said Olivia. She had drawn it herself: an apple, a loaf, a milk, and something that was either cheese or a door.

Theo rode in the cart seat, buckled in. His job was to hold the bananas. He held one up to his ear, like a telephone.

"Hello?" said Olivia into the other end.

"Ba," said Theo, very seriously.

At the apples, Olivia counted five into the bag. She kept one hand on the cart the whole way. That was her own rule. She made it up in the parking lot and kept it all the way to the milk.

While Mama was choosing cheese, Theo leaned out and licked an apple on the pile.

"Mama! He licked one!"

Mama looked at the apple. The apple shone back at her. "Well," said Mama. "That one's ours now."

It went in the bag by itself, to be washed.

At the checkout, Olivia told the man everything in order. "First we got apples. Den Fee-oh licked one. Den we got da cheese."

"Sounds like a big trip," said the man.

On the way home, Theo fell asleep on the phone.
