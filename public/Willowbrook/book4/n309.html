Night 309

The Quiet That Snow Makes

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Papa and Olivia went out at the end of the afternoon, in the falling snow, just the two of them, to listen.

Half a block down Maple Lane, Olivia stopped.

"Papa. Everything's gone soft."

It had. A car went by with its sound turned down. Two streets over, a snowplow went past, and it sounded like a bee in a jar. Their boots didn't crunch. They hushed.

"Why is it so quiet?"

"New snow is full of tiny air holes," said Papa. "Sounds go in and get lost."

"Da snow eats sounds?"

"Some of them."

Papa tried to whistle, to test it. As usual, nothing came out but air.

"Da snow ate your whistle," said Olivia.

"It wasn't much of a meal," said Papa.

They stood under the street lamp and listened hard. Far off, a shovel scraped. A dog said one thing and stopped. Snow landed on Olivia's hood with a sound so small she had to hold her breath to hear it.

"I can hear da snow," she said. "Tick. Tick."

Papa bent his head down close to her hood to hear it too.

They stood there like that until Olivia's nose said it was time to go home.
