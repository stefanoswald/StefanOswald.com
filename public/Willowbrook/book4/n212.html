Night 212

Behind the Clouds

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Clouds came in low over Willowbrook that night, thick as a gray quilt, and from below nobody could see a single star.

Stella waited above them. She could not dip low tonight. There was nowhere to dip.

Under the clouds, the town turned its lights down one at a time. The bakery went first, because Rosa gets up before the birds. Then the mending shop. Then the porches on Maple Lane, one by one, until only the lantern on the library was left, and a few small windows.

The very top of the Great Willow poked up through the cloud. Oliver Owl sat on it, blinking.

"Who is still awake?" he asked.

Nobody answered. Down in one of the last windows, a small gold nightlight glowed near the floor.

The Sleepy Moon hummed, low and long, and the clouds shifted in their sleep. A small gap opened over Maple Lane, just big enough for one star.

Stella slipped through it and dipped low, over the house with the gold nightlight.

Olivia was already asleep.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
