Night 203

The Wrong Gift Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Wren's grandmother gave Olivia an envelope at pickup. Inside was a card with a cake on it. A party. Wren had never invited anybody to anything.

"Wren picked ME!" Olivia danced around the kitchen, twice.

"That's wonderful," said Mama, smiling. Then she winked. "Maybe."

"Not maybe! It's GOOD!"

"It's very good," said Mama. "We'll see what else it is."

On the hall table were two presents: a puzzle for Wren, and a truck for Marcus's party next week. On Saturday they grabbed one and ran.

At the party, Wren opened it. It was the truck. A loud one, with a siren.

Olivia's face went hot. She pulled Mama down close. "It's da WRONG one."

"Oh no," said Mama, very quietly. "You wanted Wren to get the puzzle." She kept her arm around Olivia.

Olivia leaned on her. After a while she said, very small, "Maybe?"

Wren turned the truck over and looked at the bottom, very seriously. Then Wren pulled, and a wheel came off with a click. It went back on with a click.

"Wren likes the insides of things," said Wren's grandmother.

Off came the wheels. On went the wheels. Off, on, all afternoon.

Wren was still clicking a wheel on when they left.
