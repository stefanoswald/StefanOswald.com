Night 142

The Cranky Evening

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

A tooth was coming in at the back of Theo's mouth, and it was taking its time.

He fussed at supper. By bedtime he was crying the tired, sore kind of crying, with his fist in his mouth.

Mama walked him up and down the living room. The fan turned.

Olivia wanted to help. She brought his cow, and he threw it. She offered him her cozy corner, and he didn't look. Then she sang the brook song, loud, right up at his face.

"Olivia, NOT NOW!"

Olivia stopped singing. She backed up to the couch.

Mama stopped walking. She shut her eyes and took one long, slow breath, the kind she was always asking other people to take. Then she came to the couch, Theo still fussing on her shoulder.

"I'm sorry I snapped," she said. "You were trying to help me."

Olivia leaned on her arm. "Does his mouth hurt?"

"His gums hurt. A tooth is pushing through."

"Can I help? Quiet?"

"There's a cold washcloth in the fridge," said Mama. "Can you bring it?"

Olivia brought it carefully. Theo took it and chewed it and frowned.

Then Olivia sang the brook song again, the size of a whisper.

Theo stopped fussing to listen.

Mama nodded at her to keep going.
