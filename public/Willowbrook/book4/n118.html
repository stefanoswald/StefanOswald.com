Night 118

What the Nightlight Sees

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

The gold nightlight sits low, near the floor, so it sees everything from underneath.

It saw the shadow on the wall turn into a curtain, and then dance. It saw the flashlight go under the bed and come back with dust and a stripy sock. It saw Papa asleep on the rug in one sock while the storm walked away. It went to Lily's house in the side of a bag, next to Grandpa's stone, and glowed there too.

Tonight Olivia was telling Bobby Bear the daytime parts, which he had missed. She lifted his floppy ear so he could hear.

"A big dog came. I stopped my feet. We waved."

"A duck had a kid inside."

"Da pool rope says not yet."

"Papa got scared on da ladder. He went up anyway."

"Lily did da bridge by herself. Mateo borrowed my list. It came back."

She held up three fingers. "Lost is statue, shout, helper. Fire is da mailbox."

"Fee-oh doesn't like da vacuum. Not yet."

"I sent da chasing thing home. With a key."

"And da dark is busy. Fireflies."

"You forgot the sock," said Mama from the door.

"Da sock was hiding."

Bobby Bear had heard it all. He is good at that.

The gold light glowed on, low down.
