Night 154

The Night Before School

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The clothes were on the chair. The backpack was by the door, zipped, unzipped and zipped again.

Olivia was in bed with Bobby Bear, and she was not one bit sleepy.

"I feel excited," she said. "And scared. Both."

"Both at once," said Mama. "That happens before big things."

Papa sat up. "Let's practice the goodbye. You can be the mama."

Papa would be the kid. So Olivia stood by the door, and Papa, who is large and has a beard, knelt down and wrapped both arms around her leg.

"Don't go!" said Papa. "I want home today!"

Olivia looked at Mama. Mama told her the words, very quietly, and Olivia bent down to Papa. She kissed the top of his head. She gave him a squeeze.

"I always come back," she said.

Papa let go and waved with his whole arm.

They practiced it three times. The third time, Papa howled so loudly that Olivia had to sit down on the floor to laugh.

Then she climbed back into bed and said the goodbye once more, to Bobby Bear, to be sure.

She was asleep before Papa stopped sniffing.
