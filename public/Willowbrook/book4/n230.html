Night 230

Theo's First Chart Job

Month 8: Helping Hands

Laundry day was windy. The sheets on the line in the yard went snap, snap, like flags.

Mama had a bag of wooden clothespins. Today Theo had a job.

Olivia showed him first. "One. Den give Mama. One. Den give."

Theo took one clothespin out of the bag. He carried it across the grass like an egg and held it up. "Mama!"

"Thank you," said Mama, and pinned a corner with it.

Theo went back for another. He brought a clothespin. He brought a leaf. He brought a clothespin. He brought a small rock.

"Dat's a rock, Fee-oh," said Olivia.

Mama put the rock in her pocket. "Thank you," she said.

The wind billowed a sheet up and dropped it over Theo's head. He stood still underneath. Then he pulled it off, and there was Olivia.

"BOO!"

Theo squealed. He pulled the sheet back over his head, to do it again.

When the basket was empty, he held up one last clothespin. There was nothing left to pin.

"You handed me every one," said Mama. "One at a time."

That afternoon Olivia drew a clothespin on the job list, right next to Theo's purple line.

The rock stayed in Mama's pocket all day.
