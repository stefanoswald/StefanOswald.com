Night 140

The Bath Captain's Mate

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

In July, bath time comes after the sprinkler, so there is always a little grass in it.

Olivia and Theo had their baths together now. Mama knelt by the tub the whole time, with a hand near Theo, and she never left the bathroom while anyone was in the water. The towels were already waiting on the hook.

Olivia was the captain. Theo was the mate.

The captain's job was the ducks. She lined them up on the edge and pushed them off.

The mate's job was the rinse cup. He filled it. He looked at it. Then he poured it, very carefully, onto the captain's head.

"FEE-OH!"

Theo laughed so hard he sat down, which in a bath makes a wave.

"Mates don't pour on captains," said Olivia, dripping.

"He filled it all by himself," said Mama. "Next he learns where to pour."

Olivia wiped her eyes. Then she held her hands out flat in front of her. "Here, Fee-oh. Pour here."

He poured on her hands. Then on the ducks. Then on Mama.

When it was time, the captain pulled the plug. The water turned around and around and began to go.

"Bye, water," said Olivia.

Theo waved at the drain, the backward way.

Mama wrapped them up, one towel each.
