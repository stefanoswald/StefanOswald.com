Night 331

The First Green

Month 11: Winter Wonders

The cold came back in the night, with a thin new snow over the old gray one. But in the kitchen window, something was green.

"Papa! Da carrot growed hair!"

It was true. The carrot from Theo's snowman had been sitting in its saucer of water all this time, and out of its top came a little tuft of feathery green.

"It grew leaves," said Papa. "Look at that."

Theo pushed a chair toward the counter and started to climb. Papa scooped him up instead and held him to the window.

"It's da snowman's nose, Fee-oh," said Olivia.

Theo looked at the carrot. He touched his own nose. "Nose."

"Da nose is growing."

Theo reached over and checked Papa's nose too, in case.

Next to the saucer, the green room had changed. It had gone dark and thin, almost see-through, and something inside it showed, folded, like a shadow with colors in it.

"Papa. Bella's room is dark."

Papa bent close. "She's almost ready, I think."

Olivia leaned in near the parsley. "Dere's green now," she told the green room. "Just a little."

She checked it again before lunch, and again before bed.
