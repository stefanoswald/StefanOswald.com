Night 232

The Weekend Plant

Month 8: Helping Hands

Every Friday, one child in Miss Dana's class took the class spider plant home for the weekend. This Friday it was Olivia.

It came in a box with a note. Mama read it: "A little water on Saturday. A sunny window. That's all."

Olivia knew about plants. She had a fern.

On Saturday morning she gave the spider plant a drink. Then she sang to it. After the song it looked thirsty again, so she gave it another drink, and then one more, to be sure.

Water ran out of the bottom of the pot, over the saucer, across the table and under Mama's toast.

"Oh," said Mama, looking at her toast.

"It's still thirsty," said Olivia.

"I think it's full," said Mama. "Plants drink a little, then they rest."

Olivia looked at the plant. "Like me at bedtime?"

"Very like you at bedtime."

They tipped the extra water into the sink together. Mama ate the wet toast anyway.

On Sunday Olivia did not water it at all. She checked it twice and told it about her weekend. At night she tucked one of her socks around the pot to keep it warm.

On Monday the plant went back to school with the sock still on.

Miss Dana left the sock on.
