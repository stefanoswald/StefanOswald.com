Night 335

The Countdown Chain

Month 12: Almost Four

"How many sleeps till I'm four?"

"Twenty-eight," said Mama. "Let's make them into something you can see."

So they made a paper chain. Olivia cut the strips, some wide and some very wide, and gave every one a crayon squiggle. Mama taped them into rings. The chain went across the kitchen doorway, low, where a person could reach it.

"One link every morning," said Mama. "The last one comes off on your birthday."

The first morning, Olivia tore off one link. The second morning, she tore off another.

On the third morning, Theo got there first. He ran at the chain, grabbed it with both fists, and sat down hard. Three links came with him.

"Fee-oh! Now it's closer!" Olivia looked at Mama. "Is it closer?"

"You hoped it was," said Mama. "But the chain can't make the sleeps go faster."

Theo waved a link at them. "Mine."

"How could we fix it?" said Mama.

Olivia thought. "Put it high. Like da cookies."

So Papa taped the three links back on and hung the chain along the top of the doorframe. After that, every morning, he lifted Olivia up to tear one off.

Theo watched from the floor.

High up, the chain swayed.
