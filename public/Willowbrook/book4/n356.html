Night 356

The Party Preparations

Month 12: Almost Four

The party was Saturday. The birthday itself was Sunday, but Saturday is a better day for a party.

So on Friday night the whole house got ready. Papa went up the stepladder with the streamers, gold and blue. Mama hung paper stars in the window. Olivia built a story corner out of every cushion in the house.

Theo found the tape.

He ran with it. The tape unrolled behind him in a long shiny tail, around the couch, under the table, into the kitchen and back.

"Fee-oh! Dat's da PARTY tape!"

Theo stopped and looked at his tail. Then he kicked his ball, missed it, and sat down.

Mama wound the tape back up, mostly.

From the top of the ladder, Papa practiced the song. "Happy birthday to you, happy birthday to you, happy birthday, dear Mrs. Henderson."

"NO!" said Olivia. "Dear OLIVIA!"

"Dear Mrs. Olivia?"

"Just Olivia!"

Papa sang it right. Theo clapped in the wrong place.

By bedtime the streamers were up and the story corner was ready.

The cat was already asleep in it.
