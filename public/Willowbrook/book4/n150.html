Night 150

Keeping Theo

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

At bedtime, Mama and Papa sat on the end of the bed.

"What was the hardest part?" asked Mama. "Of being Theo's big sister this month?"

"When he knocked my big tower," said Olivia.

"You were so mad," said Papa. "What did we do?"

"Mama holded us both. Den I made a little one. For knocking."

"She held you both," said Papa. "And the big one went on the table."

"And my splinter," said Olivia. "I waited all day."

"Next time?" said Mama.

"I tell right away. Even if you're holding him."

"What else?"

"When he went to da aunts. Da house was too big."

"And the best part?" asked Papa.

"He does da head part now. He says uh-oh. And up. He shushed da whole library. He held my fingers. For da poke."

Papa looked at the ceiling. "And he whistled beautifully."

"NO. He did a raspberry. On you."

Papa reminded her of the rest: the fort doorbell, the telling game, the wagon, the egg on his shoe.

"So what about having a little brother?" said Mama.

Olivia pulled the sheet up to her chin. "He's a lot of work. He knocks things. He licks things."

Mama waited.

"But I'm keeping him."

Across the hall, somebody said, "Livy." Then he was asleep again.
