Night 260

Papa's Bathrobe

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Papa had hung his bathrobe on the hook on Olivia's door, because the bathroom hook was full.

At bedtime, with the lamp off, the bathrobe was a man. A tall man with no face, standing very still by the door.

"PAPA!"

Papa came fast and switched on the lamp. Then he saw the bathrobe, and he laughed. "That's just my bathrobe, silly!"

Olivia pulled the covers over her head. Under them, she started to cry.

Papa stopped laughing. He sat on the bed and didn't say anything for a while.

"I laughed," he said. "It wasn't funny to you."

"It was a MAN."

"In the dark it looked just like a man. I'm sorry I laughed."

Olivia came out as far as her eyes.

"What could we do about him?" said Papa.

Olivia looked at the bathrobe for a long time. "Take him away."

"We could. Or?"

She looked some more. "Give him a face."

So she drew one, with a big smile and three eyes, because two didn't look friendly enough. Mama brought the tape. Papa stuck the face on the collar.

With the lamp off, the man by the door had a paper face. It was smiling.

"Night, Mister Robe," said Olivia.

Papa went out and left the door open one finger, the way it always was.
