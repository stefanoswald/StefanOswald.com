Night 21

The Puppet Sock

Month 1: Pretend!

The sock drawer is full of socks that lost their other half. This week, one of them became somebody.

Mama sewed on two button eyes and a red yarn mouth. Olivia put it on her hand, and it was not a sock anymore.

"I'm Mr. Socko," it said, in a high, scratchy voice that was nothing like Olivia's.

Mr. Socko came to dinner. Theo was already asleep, so he was not interviewed. Everybody else was.

"What are you eating? Why? Is it good?"

"Peas," said Papa. "Because they're green, and yes."

"What's your name?"

"Papa."

"Dat's not a name."

Mr. Socko tried a pea and dropped it. "I don't got a tummy. I got a hand."

Then he got quiet. "Mr. Socko don't like da drain."

Mama put her fork down. "What doesn't he like about it?"

"It goes glug. Does it take socks?"

Papa talked straight to the sock. "That drain is very small. Only water fits down it."

"Not socks?"

"Not socks. Not girls either."

Mr. Socko nodded. "Okay," he said.

At bath time he sat on the edge of the sink and watched the water go. "Bye, water," he said.

Olivia fell asleep with him still on her hand.
