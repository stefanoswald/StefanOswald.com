Night 231

The Letter Writers

Month 8: Helping Hands

A letter came from the aunts, the way one came every month, with a bird stamp and a star drawn at the bottom.

Mama read it out loud. The aunts' apple tree had given them too many apples. One aunt had a cold. The other aunt said it was not a cold, it was a sniffle.

Olivia looked at the stack of letters on the shelf. It was taller than her hand.

"Do we write dem back?"

"Sometimes," said Mama. "Not as often as they write us."

"Do dey sit a long time?"

"A good long while. They sit down just for us."

Olivia thought about the aunts sitting down, just for her, with their pens.

"We write back. Now."

So Olivia told the letter, and Mama wrote it down, every word.

"Dear aunts," said Olivia. "First we raked Mrs. Henderson's leaves. Den we jumped in dem. Den Fee-oh wore a leaf."

"Anything else?"

"Thank you for all da letters."

Theo made his part with a fat green crayon. Some of it was on the paper. Some of it was on the table.

At the bottom Olivia drew a star, like the aunts do, and an O.

They walked it out to the mailbox together, and Olivia put the red flag up.
