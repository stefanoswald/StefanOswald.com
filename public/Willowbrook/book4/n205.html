Night 205

Papa's Trip Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Papa went away for work, four sleeps, to a city with bridges.

The first night Olivia cried at bedtime. "I want Papa to do it."

"You miss Papa," said Mama. "Me too." She did bedtime anyway, the wrong way, and Olivia told her so.

In the morning there was a postcard on the kitchen table. Papa had left four with Mama, one for each morning. On the front was a bridge. On the back was a duck, drawn in Papa's wobbly pen, standing on the bridge.

"Da duck went with him!"

The second night was harder. At bath time Theo splashed, and Olivia splashed, and the floor got wet, and Mama said "ENOUGH!" much too loud.

Everybody went still. Mama sat down on the edge of the tub.

"I was too loud," she said. "I'm sorry."

Then, quieter, "I'm missing Papa too."

Olivia patted Mama's knee. "Tomorrow's a duck."

"Maybe," said Mama, and smiled a little.

It was. The next duck was on a boat. The one after that rode an elevator. The last one was in a car, driving home.

On the fourth night, headlights came up the drive. Theo got to the door first, wobbling the whole way down the hall. "Dada!"

The four ducks went up on the fridge, in a row.
