Night 296

The Longest Night

Month 10: Words Are Magic

It got dark before supper. It got dark before Olivia's snack.

"Is it night?" Olivia stood at the window. "I didn't have my snack yet."

"It's the longest night of the whole year," said Mama. "The sun went down early."

"I don't want da longest one. I want da day back."

"You want the day back," said Mama. "It went too fast."

Olivia leaned her forehead on the cold glass. Nobody said anything for a while.

Papa came home with snow on his hat. "The longest night has a name," he said. "The solstice."

"Sol-stiss." Olivia would put that in the jar later. "Is it a bad night?"

Mama thought about it. "Maybe," she said.

"Maybe what?"

"Tomorrow the day gets a little longer," said Papa. "Then a little more, all the way to summer."

"So it turns around? Tonight?"

"Tonight," said Mama. "It's the year's Maybe night."

Papa put his hat back on. "Want to go see who's lit up?"

They walked down Maple Lane in the dark. Porch lights. String lights. Mrs. Henderson's wreath, with a light in the middle of the O. And far off, on top of the round library, the lantern was already lit.

"Da lantern's on early," said Olivia.

"Somebody knew," said Papa.

Back home, the porch light was on.
