Night 319

The Big Sled Hill

Month 11: Winter Wonders

For two winters, Olivia had watched the big sled hill from the little one.

"The big hill is the best," said Mateo. He was almost five and had been down it all morning.

Olivia looked up. The top was very far up. "It's too high."

"It looks high," said Papa. "We could start partway."

They started a little way up, on one sled, Olivia in front in her red helmet and Papa behind with his arms around her. Feet first, sitting up.

Whoosh. It was over before she could scream.

"Again. Higher."

From halfway, she screamed the whole ride and laughed at the bottom.

At the top, she looked down, and her tummy went small.

"I'm scared," she said.

"I know," said Papa. "You can say no. Or we go together."

Olivia held the rope in both mittens. "Together."

They pushed off. The hill went by in one long white rush, bump, bump, and Papa roared, and Olivia roared, all the way down to Mateo, who was jumping up and down.

"From the TOP!" said Mateo.

Olivia sat in the snow, dusted white, and couldn't stop laughing.

They went down from the top again and again. On the walk home, Papa pulled her on the sled. She lay back and watched the sky.
