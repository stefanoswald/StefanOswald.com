Night 38

The Daytime Wink

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The rain stopped late in the afternoon, and the sun came in low and gold across Olivia's room. She was tidying her shelf, which meant moving the sparkly rock from one end to the other and talking the whole time.

"Franklin said da tea was too hot," she told Baba. "Which is silly. It's air."

Baba sat on the shelf and listened.

"So I blowed on it for him."

She reached past him for the Duchess's feather. And in that little pause, in the gold, Baba's flopped ear moved. It slid down half an inch, slowly, and stayed there.

Olivia stood very still.

Stuffing settles. It does this in every house, at every hour, for no reason at all.

She looked at Baba. Baba looked at nothing, as he always does in the daytime.

"I saw dat," she whispered.

She did not run and tell. She finished the shelf instead, and put the feather back, and moved the sparkly rock one more time.

At supper Papa asked what she had done all afternoon.

"Tidied," said Olivia, and ate a pea.
