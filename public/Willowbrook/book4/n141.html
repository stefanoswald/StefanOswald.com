Night 141

The Checkup Buddy

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Theo had his one-year checkup, and Olivia came too. She said she was his helper. Nobody argued.

In the little room, Theo sat on Mama's lap and looked at everything with deep suspicion.

The doctor took out the little light for ears.

"Dat's da ear light," Olivia told him. "It tickles. Watch me first."

She held out her own ear. The doctor, who was a good sport, looked in it. "A very nice ear," said the doctor.

Then she looked in Theo's. He did not like it, but he did not cry. He kept his eyes on Olivia the whole time.

The doctor weighed him and measured him from his head to his heels.

Then came the pokey part. Olivia knew about the pokey part.

She held out her hand. Theo grabbed two of her fingers and squeezed. He cried a big loud cry, and she let him squeeze as hard as he wanted.

"Ow," said Olivia, very quietly. "But okay."

Mama held him close, and the crying got smaller.

"You gave him your fingers," said Mama. "He held on the whole time."

On the way out, Theo rode on Mama's hip, and Olivia held up one finger for him.

He didn't let go of her finger.
