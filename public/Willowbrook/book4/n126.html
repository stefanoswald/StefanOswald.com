Night 126

Uh-Oh

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

At breakfast, Theo held his cup out over the side of the high chair. He looked at Olivia. He let go.

Bonk.

"Uh-oh," said Theo.

Everybody stopped chewing.

"Mama! He said uh-oh!"

"He did," said Mama. "That's a brand-new word."

Olivia climbed down, picked up the cup, and gave it back. Theo held it out over the side. He looked at her. He let go.

Bonk. "Uh-oh."

"He's doing it on purpose," said Olivia.

"He's checking whether it falls every time," said Mama. "So far, it does."

Olivia picked it up. Bonk. "Uh-oh." She picked it up again. Bonk. "Uh-oh." Her cereal went soggy in the bowl.

The next time, she didn't give it back. She crawled under the tray and held out both hands.

Theo let go. The cup dropped right into them.

There was no bonk. Theo leaned over the side and stared down at her, astonished.

"Uh-oh?"

"Not uh-oh," said Olivia. "Dat's a catch."

Mama wrote uh-oh on the kitchen calendar, under the date.
