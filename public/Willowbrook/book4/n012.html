Night 12

Rain Delay Theater

Month 1: Pretend!

Rain, all day long. So there was a show.

The curtain was the big blanket, hung between two chairs. The spotlight was Papa's flashlight. The seats were the couch: Mama, Theo on her lap, and three stuffed animals in the front row.

"Break a leg," said Papa.

Olivia stopped. "No! I need my legs!"

"It means good luck in theater talk."

She checked both legs anyway. Then the show began. It was called "Da dragon who lost his rawr."

The dragon opened his mouth and nothing came out. He looked under the couch. He looked in Mama's pocket. He looked in Papa's beard, twice, which was the funny part.

Then the flashlight swung onto Theo, and Theo, who loved a flashlight more than anything, shrieked. "Aaaah!"

"Dere it is!" Olivia shouted. "Fee-oh had it!"

She took the rawr back. "RAWR!" Cape flap. Spotlight. The crowd went wild. The wildest part of the crowd was Theo, who shrieked again, because now he knew it worked.

There was a bow, and another bow, and a bow for Theo, who bowed by tipping sideways into Mama.

At dinner, Olivia had news. "Tomorrow's show is about a duck."
