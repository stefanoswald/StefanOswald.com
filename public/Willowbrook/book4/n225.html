Night 225

The Bird Feeder Round

Month 8: Helping Hands

Grandpa's feeder hung from the hook by the back porch now, with Olivia's O under its roof.

Filling it was Olivia's morning job, and Papa's too, because the seed bag was heavy and the hook was high. They went out early, before Theo woke, Olivia in her coat over pajamas.

Papa lifted her. Olivia tipped the scoop, and the seed went in with a sound like rain.

"Now da birds come," said Olivia.

Papa tried to whistle like a chickadee. Only air came out.

"You can't whistle," said Olivia.

"Not yet," said Papa.

They sat on the step and waited. Something came down the porch post. It was not a bird.

A gray squirrel swung into the feeder upside down and began to eat.

"HEY! Dat's for BIRDS!"

The squirrel went on eating.

"He doesn't know it's for birds," said Papa. "He thinks it's his breakfast."

"Somebody tell him!"

There was no telling him. So Olivia made him his own breakfast, a little pile of seed on the stump by the fence. "Dat's YOUR table."

The squirrel looked at the stump and went back to the feeder.

"He eated it all," said Olivia.

"He ate every seed," said Papa. "Tomorrow we try the thin branch by the kitchen window."

The chickadees waited in the hedge.
