Night 217

Helping That Isn't Wanted

Month 8: Helping Hands

Theo had Olivia's old wooden puzzle, the one with three shapes and a knob on each.

He picked up the circle. He pushed it at the square hole. He pushed it at the triangle hole. He pushed it at his own foot.

"Not dere, Fee-oh." Olivia took the circle and popped it into the round hole. "Dere! Done!"

Theo looked at the puzzle. Then he pulled the circle out again and threw it across the rug, with a yell.

"I helped him!" said Olivia. "And he throwed it!"

Mama got down on the rug. "He threw it," she said. "I think he wanted to do it himself."

"But he was doing it wrong."

"He was doing it slow. The slow part is his."

Olivia fetched the circle and gave it back. Then she sat on her hands, because they kept wanting to help.

Theo pushed the circle at every hole there was. At last it dropped in, clunk. He clapped for himself.

Then he picked up the triangle, tried it twice, and held it out to Olivia.

Olivia looked at Mama.

"He's asking," said Mama.

Olivia turned the triangle the right way round and gave it back. Theo pushed it in himself.

Olivia kept one hand under her leg, just in case.
