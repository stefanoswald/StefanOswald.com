Night 361

The Willow Writes

Month 12: Almost Four

At the end of the birthday, when the light went gold, the family walked up the hill to the Great Willow. The snow lay in soft patches. The willow's long branches had gone from brown to yellow, the first color on the whole hill.

Theo rode on Papa's shoulders, holding on to Papa's ears.

The branches moved, though there was no wind anywhere else.

In the bark, the old words glowed. HOME, from when she was two. FAMILY, from when she was three. And under them, new and bright, one more.

Olivia went close. She knew the first letter.

"G," she said. "Like Grandpa."

"Growing," said Papa.

Olivia frowned at the bark. "But I checked. Dis morning. I didn't."

"The tree uses a longer ruler," said Papa.

"How long?"

"A whole year long."

Olivia stood very still and looked at the word.

Theo drummed on Papa's head. He held up one finger to the tree.

"Livy four!"

"Dat's one finger, Fee-oh."

"Four!"

Everybody laughed, and the willow's branches swung once and hung still.

They walked down in the blue dusk. Olivia walked by herself, almost the whole way.

Near the bottom, Mama carried her.
