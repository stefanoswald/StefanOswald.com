Night 125

The Splinter of Being Second

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

All day, everybody watched Theo.

He walked across the yard holding Papa's fingers, and everybody clapped. Grandpa Joe telephoned and asked, "Is he walking yet?" Miss Rosa said he was a peach.

At bedtime, with the lamp on and Theo asleep, Olivia held out one finger.

"I got a splinter."

Mama looked. There it was, a thin dark line in the side of her finger. "When did that happen?"

"At lunch. On da porch step."

"At lunch? Why didn't you tell me?"

Olivia looked at her blanket. "You were holding Fee-oh."

Mama sat down close. She didn't say anything for a moment.

"You waited all day for me," she said.

Olivia's chin wobbled. "Everybody claps for Fee-oh."

"They did clap a lot today," said Mama. "And you wanted some of me, just for you."

Olivia nodded into Mama's arm.

Mama washed the finger and got the tweezers, and Olivia held very still under the lamp. It took two tries. "Ow." Then it was out.

"Next time, tell me right away," said Mama. "I can hold Theo and help you at the same time."

"Can we have just-us tomorrow?"

"At nap time. Just us."

Olivia held her finger up to the lamp. Nothing in it now.
