Night 324

The January Thaw

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Overnight, the air went soft. In the morning, everything in Willowbrook was dripping: the roofs, the trees, the mailbox, the end of Papa's nose.

"It's the January thaw," said Papa. "A few warm days in the middle of winter."

On the way to the brook, they passed the ice window puddle. It was only a puddle now, and the three leaves were floating.

"Dey got out!" said Olivia.

The brook still had its lid on, but the ice had gone gray and wet.

"Thaw ice is thin," said Papa. "We stay on the bank."

So Olivia lay on her tummy on the bank with her ear near the edge, and Papa kept a hand on the back of her coat.

Under the ice, something was moving. Blub. Blub-blub.

"Papa! Da brook is awake under dere!"

"It never stopped," said Papa. "It ran under the ice all winter."

Olivia listened harder. "Where's Freddy?"

"Frogs sleep in the mud at the bottom until spring."

Olivia put her mouth near the ice. "Freddy. It's not spring. It's just a thaw. Keep sleeping."

Papa helped her up, soaked all down the front. The woods went drip, drip. Under the gray ice, the brook kept going.
