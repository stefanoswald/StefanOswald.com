Night 278

The Picture List

Month 10: Words Are Magic

On Saturday, Mama made a grocery list, and Olivia made one too.

Mama's list had words. Olivia's had pictures. A milk jug. Three red apples. A long loaf of bread. An egg with a yellow middle.

At the bottom she drew a small yellow square with a line across it.

At the store she held her list in both hands and read it aisle by aisle. "Milk. Got it. Apples. Got it. Egg. Got it."

Then she came to the yellow square.

"What's that one?" said Mama.

Olivia turned the list sideways. "I don't know. I forget."

They looked up and down the aisles. It wasn't cheese. It wasn't bananas. It wasn't the yellow soap.

At home, Mama opened the fridge. "We're out of butter."

Olivia looked at the yellow square. It had been butter the whole time.

"Oh no. Da butter."

"The butter," said Mama. "We'll get it tomorrow."

That night they made tomorrow's list together. Olivia drew the yellow square again, very carefully.

Mama wrote BUTTER under it.
