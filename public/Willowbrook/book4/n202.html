Night 202

Two Rain Days

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

It started raining on Friday night, and on Saturday morning it was still raining.

Olivia ran to the front door. The yellow boots were ready. They had been ready all week.

Saturday was a boots day. She stamped every puddle on Maple Lane, and some of them twice. Papa said it was raining cats and dogs. Olivia looked up at the sky for a long time.

"I don't see any."

Then the cat came in from outside, shaking one wet paw, looking disgusted.

"Dere's one."

On Sunday it was still raining. The puddles were old news. The boots were wet inside.

"Da rain is too MUCH now." Olivia lay face down on the rug. "Too much."

"You've had enough rain," said Mama. "Two days is a lot of wet." She lay down on the rug too.

"Maybe," Mama said, into the carpet.

Papa came in from the mailbox and walked right across the rug in his muddy boots.

"PAPA. BOOTS."

He went back out and came in again, in his socks, on tiptoe.

After lunch nobody played anything. Olivia and Theo knelt on a chair by the window, with Mama's hand on Theo's back, and watched the drops run down the glass. They raced. Some got stuck halfway.

"Dat one's winning," Olivia whispered.

The cat slept through the whole race.
