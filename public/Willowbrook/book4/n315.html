Night 315

Cocoa at Lily's

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Preschool stayed shut for the snow, so Olivia spent the morning at Lily's, on the window seat, under the good blanket.

Miss Rosa brought cocoa with little marshmallows, already melting.

Olivia counted. "Lily got four. I got three."

"Dat's not fair," said Lily, and ate one of hers, so it was.

"This is the last marshmallow in the house," said Rosa, and dropped one more in each mug.

It was not the last marshmallow in the house. There were more after that, one at a time, each one the last.

They pressed their noses to the cold glass and picked snowflakes to race.

"Dat one's mine," said Olivia. "Da big one."

"Mine's da fast one."

Olivia's landed on the mailbox. Lily's landed on a dog, who was walking by in a red coat and did not notice.

"A DOG," said Lily. "Mine wins."

Olivia thought about it. She thought about it some more. "Okay," she said. "Dog wins."

They picked more all morning. None of them landed on anything as good as a dog.

At lunchtime Rosa found them still at the glass, with cocoa on their lips and two round fog spots where their noses had been.
