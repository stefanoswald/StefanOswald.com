Night 137

The Day He Wasn't There

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

On Saturday, Papa drove Theo to see the aunts for the day. The aunts had not seen him walk yet, not even holding on.

So Olivia had the whole house.

She built a tall tower on the rug, and nobody knocked it down. She had Mama for lunch, and for after lunch, and for after that.

By two o'clock, she was lying flat on the rug.

"Mama. It's too big in here."

"The house?"

"Everything." She looked at her tower. "It's just standing dere."

She played hide and seek by herself. She hid behind the curtain and waited, and nobody came. After a while, she came out on her own.

"I hided and nobody looked."

"You hid, and nobody came looking," said Mama. "You miss him."

Olivia nodded.

At four o'clock, she knelt on the couch with her chin on the windowsill. Across the street, Mrs. Henderson's sprinkler went back and forth. Every car was the wrong car.

Then one was the right one.

Olivia ran to the door. Papa came up the walk with Theo on his hip, and Theo saw her and leaned out so far that Papa needed both hands.

"Livy!"

The tower was still standing on the rug. She had saved it for him.
