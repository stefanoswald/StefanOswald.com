Night 219

The Tool Belt

Month 8: Helping Hands

On Sunday Grandpa Joe was building something in the workshop. It had a floor and four posts and no roof yet. He didn't say what it was.

He held up a small leather belt, old and soft, with a pencil in one loop and a sanding block in the other.

"Fits?" he said, and buckled it on.

It fit.

He gave her the little roof board and ran his finger along the rough edge. That was the whole instruction.

Olivia sanded fast and hard, like scrubbing a pot. Grandpa put his big hand over her small one and slowed it down. Back and forth, with the grain.

"Dis way?"

"Yep."

She sanded until her arm got tired. "Why does it gotta be smooth?"

Grandpa didn't answer. He took her hand and ran her fingers over the rough end. Then over the smooth end.

Olivia felt the difference. "So da birds don't get splinters."

Grandpa nodded once.

When the roof was done he ran his thumb along it, twice. Then he gave her the pencil from her own belt and turned the board over. She wrote an O on the underside, the part of her name she was sure of.

Grandpa hung the little belt on a nail next to his. Two belts, one big and one small.
