Night 65

Why Do I Have to Sleep

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

In May the sky forgets about bedtime. Olivia had her pajamas on and her teeth brushed, and outside the window it was still perfectly light. A robin was singing on the Hendersons' fence as if it were the middle of the afternoon.

"Why do I hafta sleep? It's still day."

"Your body grows while you sleep," said Mama. "It fixes your bumps, too."

"Why does it fix at night?"

"Because you hold still. It's hard to fix a thing while it's running."

Olivia thought about Papa fixing the wagon wheel. The wagon had to be upside down and not going anywhere.

"Da robin isn't sleeping."

"He goes to bed right after you," said Mama. "Listen."

They listened. The robin sang. He sang again. Then he sang a long one, with extra parts.

"He's not going to bed."

"He's saying goodnight to the whole lane," said Mama. "That takes a while."

Olivia yawned, very wide, before she could stop it.

"Dat was not a real yawn."

"Of course not," said Mama, and pulled the curtain the rest of the way.

She sat on the edge of the bed and didn't talk. Outside, the robin kept on.

Olivia was asleep before the robin.
