Night 121

The Shadow With Feet

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

It was too hot for the park, so they stayed in with the fan going. Wherever Olivia went, Theo went too.

He could not walk by himself yet. He could crawl like a beetle in a hurry, and he could cruise along anything with an edge. That was enough.

Olivia built with blocks on the rug. Theo arrived. She sat at the little table to draw. Two hands came up over the edge, and then a nose. She went to the kitchen for water, and slap, slap, slap came his hands on the floor behind her.

"Mama. He follows me all da time."

"He does," said Mama. "You're his favorite show."

Olivia went upstairs to get Bobby Bear. At the bottom, Theo reached the gate. He pulled himself up, held the bars, and rattled them.

"Livy!"

She stopped on the stairs and looked down. He looked up at her with his whole face.

"I'm just getting Bobby Bear," she said. "Wait dere."

He waited. He was very bad at it.

When she came back down, he let go of the bars, sat down with a bump, and reached up for the bear.

She let him hold one ear.
