Night 115

The Fear Museum

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

On a rainy afternoon, Olivia made a museum on the low shelf. It was a museum of scary things. Not the scary things themselves, which would not have fit.

There was a drawing of the curtain, for the shadow. There was the flashlight from under the bed. There was a drawing of a very tall duck with a boy's sneakers sticking out. There was a paper cup of dust, which was the under-bed. There was one crumb, for Crumby.

The first visitors were Mama, on the couch, and Theo, on the rug.

"Dis is a museum," Olivia told them. "No touching."

Theo crawled over, pulled himself up on the shelf, and touched everything.

"Fee-oh! No touching!"

Theo let go of the shelf. For one whole breath he stood there all by himself, with no hands, looking very surprised. Then he sat down hard on his diaper.

"Did you see dat?"

"I saw," said Mama, and her eyes were shining.

Olivia gave the tour anyway. "Dat was da shadow. It was da curtain. Dat was da under-bed. It was just fluff."

At the end of the shelf there was an empty spot.

"What goes there?" asked Mama.

"Da next one," said Olivia. "For when dere's a new scary."

By suppertime, the cat was asleep in the empty spot.
