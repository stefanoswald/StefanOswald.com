Night 79

Why Questions at Preschool Signup

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Mama had a paper from the preschool, the one Olivia would start at the end of summer, with Lily.

"What's dat paper?"

"It tells your teacher about you," said Mama.

"Why does she need to know?"

"So she knows what you like, and who picks you up."

Mama wrote. Olivia knelt up on her chair to watch.

"Why is dere a line? Why do you write small?"

"In a minute, love."

Olivia counted to ten. Then she counted to ten again, to be fair.

"Dat was a minute."

Mama looked at the clock. It had been four. She put the pen down. "You're right. Ask me."

"Why is dere a line?"

"The line shows me where to write," said Mama. "And I write small to fit."

Then Mama read the next question out loud. "Does your child have something from home that helps her feel safe?"

"Bobby Bear!"

Mama read the next part. "Comfort things are welcome at rest time."

"Bears are allowed?"

"At rest time," said Mama, and wrote Bobby Bear on the line in her neatest letters.

Olivia went upstairs to tell him. She came back down with him to be sure.

Bobby Bear sat on the form, checking it.
