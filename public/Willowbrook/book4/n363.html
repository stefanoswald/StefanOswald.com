Night 363

Bobby Bear's Letter

Month 12: Almost Four

At bedtime there was something under her pillow: an envelope, with a paw print on the back.

"It's from Bobby Bear," said Olivia. "His birthday letter."

On the front it said OLIVIA. She read that part herself. At the bottom inside it said Love, and she read that too. Mama read the middle, slowly, while Olivia moved her finger under the words.

"Dear Olivia. Happy birthday. Here is what I know about next year. In the spring, the training wheels come off. In the fall, you go to the big school, to the pre-kindergarten room. June's baby sister will learn to laugh, mostly at you. Your sunflowers will grow in a row at the farm, taller than Papa. Theo will dream louder, and he will turn two. If a small cow comes to the meadow, it is probably his. Keep the key warm. Love, Bobby Bear."

Olivia looked up. "What training wheels?"

Mama looked at Papa, in the doorway. Papa looked at the ceiling.

"Bears," said Papa.

Olivia held the letter up to Bobby Bear. "How do you know all dat?"

Bobby Bear didn't say. He never does when she's awake.

She put the letter back under her pillow, and her hand on top of it.
