Night 347

The Aunts' Package

Month 12: Almost Four

The package from the aunts came the way it always does, in brown paper and string, a week early. The aunts do not believe in waiting for the day.

Inside was a sweater, red like last year's, with gold stars across the front.

"Count them," said Mama.

Olivia put a finger on each star. "One. Two. Three. Four." She looked up. "Dey knew!"

"They always know," said Mama.

Olivia pulled it on over her head. The sleeves came down past her hands and flapped at the ends.

"It's too big."

"The aunts knit for next winter," said Mama. "You'll grow into it."

Olivia flapped the sleeves. She was a bird. Then she was a bigger bird.

There was a letter too. At the top Olivia found her own name, and in the middle she found a 4. Mama read the rest.

"Dear Olivia. Four stars, because you are four. The sleeves are long on purpose. Is Theo still eating socks? Love, your aunts."

"He is," said Olivia.

She wore the sweater to supper. She wore it to bed, sleeves and all.
