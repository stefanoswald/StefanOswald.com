Night 317

What the Green Room Hears

Month 11: Winter Wonders

"Mama. I was in your tummy once. Did you talk to me?"

"Every day," said Mama.

"Did I hear?"

"I'm pretty sure. When you were born, you knew my voice."

Olivia looked over at the windowsill. "Den Bella can hear me."

"Nobody knows," said Mama. "You could try."

So every afternoon after that, Olivia knelt on the kitchen chair by the parsley pot and told the green room the news. Bobby Bear sat next to the pot, because a visitor is nice.

"Hi, Bella. It's me. It snowed again. Da squirrel has his own stump. Fee-oh melted his snowman."

She stopped. "No. His snowman melted. Fee-oh didn't do it."

The green room hung there and said nothing. Green rooms are like that.

Some days the news was small. "I had toast." Some days it was big. "I gave my stripey mittens away. Somebody has dem now."

Theo came to listen once. He stood on his toes and said "HI!" so loud that Olivia had to tell the green room he was little.

On the day the snow came sideways, she didn't have any news at all. She just sat with Bella a while and held the bear.
