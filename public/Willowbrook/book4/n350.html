Night 350

The Town Delivers Week

Month 12: Almost Four

The week before a birthday, the town delivers. Nobody plans it. Things just turn up.

On Monday there were snowdrops on the front step, three white ones in an egg cup.

"Dat's Mrs. Henderson's egg cup," said Olivia. "It has da chip."

"Her snowdrops grow by her wall," said Mama. "She had to dig under the snow for these."

On Tuesday there were cinnamon rolls from Miss Rosa, still warm in the bag.

"Rosa got up in da dark," said Olivia, with her mouth full.

On Wednesday the Lantern Library had a book waiting at the desk with a slip on it. The slip said OLIVIA. She could read that part herself.

On Thursday Mateo came up the walk with a cardboard crown. It had a hinge on one side. Nobody knew what the hinge was for. It worked anyway.

On Friday Mr. Petrov brought her wheelbarrow back from his shop with a new star painted beside the old one.

Olivia stood on the step and looked at everything.

Then she got out her crayons.

That evening she and Mama walked the thank-you pictures up and down the lane. Mrs. Henderson opened her door and found her own snowdrops, drawn very big.

She put her hand on her heart.
