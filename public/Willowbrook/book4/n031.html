Night 31

Stella Learns the Hello Song

Month 1: Pretend!

At bedtime Olivia sang her hello song, and it drifted up over Maple Lane.

It was mostly names. Stella caught it and could sing it by the second line. Stars are quick that way.

Near midnight, she hummed it back down, very softly, over the sleeping town.

Hello, Mama. Hello, Papa. Hello, Fee-oh, in the yellow room. Hello, Baba. Hello, Lily, two streets over, by the window seat. Hello, Mateo, two porches down. Hello, Grandpa, out at the dark farm, where the hens slept in a row.

Then she came to Franklin, and stopped.

"Mmm?" asked the Sleepy Moon.

"Franklin," said Stella. "I don't know which house."

So she hummed his name softly over all of them.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
