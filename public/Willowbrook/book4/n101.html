Night 101

The Big Unknown Dog

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

A new dog came down Maple Lane on a leash. He was big, with a big bark, and he pulled toward everything: the hedge, the mailbox, Olivia.

Olivia stopped walking and took Mama's hand with both of hers.

Mama crouched beside her. "That's a big barky dog, and you don't know him. It's fine to feel scared."

The dog barked again.

"We stop our feet and keep our hands in," said Mama quietly. "Then we ask the owner."

Olivia stopped her feet. She put her hands in her pockets, all the way to the bottom.

Mama asked for her. "Can she say hi to your dog?"

"Better not," said the owner. "He's still learning his manners. You could wave."

So Olivia took one hand out and waved, very small. The owner waved back for the dog, and they went on down the lane.

"He needs more lessons," said Olivia.

"He does," said Mama. "You stopped your feet and kept your hands in. That's how we meet a dog we don't know."

At the gold dog's fence, the gold dog got up slowly and leaned on the boards.

"Dis one I know," said Olivia. His person wasn't out, so she just waved.

The gold dog thumped his tail. Then he lay down in the sun.
