Night 133

The Tattle Question

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

"Mama. Fee-oh is licking da cat."

"Mama. Fee-oh has my crayon."

"Mama. Fee-oh looked at me."

By lunchtime, Mama had heard a great deal about Theo. She sat down at the kitchen table with Olivia.

"Let's sort these out," said Mama. "Is Theo in danger, or is he just bugging you?"

"What's danger?"

"Something that could hurt him, like stairs, high places, water, the street, or something small in his mouth. You tell me those fast, every time."

"And bugging?"

"Try your words first. Then come get me."

They made it a game. Mama said a thing, and Olivia said tell or don't tell.

"He's licking the cat."

Olivia considered the cat. "Don't tell. Da cat can tell."

"He took your crayon."

"Don't tell. I say, 'Mine, please.'"

"He has a button in his mouth."

"TELL!"

That afternoon, Papa carried the laundry upstairs and left the stair gate open behind him. Olivia saw Theo cruise to the bottom step and reach up.

"MAMA! Da gate! Fee-oh's at da stairs!"

Mama came fast and scooped him up. Papa looked down from the top with the basket.

"That was me," said Papa. "Sorry, everybody."

"You told me fast," said Mama to Olivia.

Papa shut the gate and checked it. Then Olivia checked it too.
