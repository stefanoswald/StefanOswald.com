Night 288

Letters in the Snow

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The park was all new snow. Olivia, Lily and June stood at the edge of it, while Mama and Miss Rosa sat on the bench.

"Lily's Plan Snow," said Lily. "Step one, stomp. Step two, stomp more. Step three, cocoa."

"Let's stomp letters," said Olivia. "Big ones."

She stomped an O, big enough to stand in.

Lily stomped an L, one long line and one short one. The short one went the wrong way. Nobody said anything.

June didn't know hers yet. "What's mine?"

"J," said Olivia. "It's a hook."

June stomped a hook. It came out more like a snake.

"Dat's a snake," said Lily.

"It's a J snake," said June.

They climbed the slide and looked down. There they were in the snow, an O, an L and a J snake.

"Dat's us," said Lily.

June pointed at the duck pond. It was white and flat and looked like more park. "Let's do a big one dere."

"Not on da pond," said Olivia. "Ice is not for walking."

June turned around to the bench. Mama and Miss Rosa were already standing up.

"Okay," said June. "Step three."

Behind them, new snow began to fall into the letters.
