Month 5 · Little Brother Tags Along

For the grown-up

Nights 121–151

This is the month the baby starts following. Theo is just past one: he cruises along the couch, walks holding a hand, drops his cup to hear it land, copies his sister, and says one word at a time. He isn't walking on his own yet, and he has no idea a tower is supposed to stay up.

Olivia is becoming a big sister in real time. You'll see her teach him by making the job small enough, stand beside him in the sandbox, and run to tell you when he's near the stairs. You'll also see her furious when he wrecks her work, jealous when he gets the clapping, and lonely the one day he's gone. Some days she feels all of it before lunch.

The grown-ups in these stories hold both children at once, sometimes on the same lap. They name her anger before they explain the baby, and they let her stay mad a while. Twice a parent gets short, notices, and says sorry.

Leave room for the sounds: the uh-oh, the shush, the raspberry. If she starts telling you about a baby she knows, let the page wait.

One thing to try: while the little one naps, keep a small stretch of time that belongs to the big one alone. One thing not to worry about: the days she wants him sent back. They come and go, right alongside the love.
