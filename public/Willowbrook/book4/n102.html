Night 102

Mr. Socko at the Dentist

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

The dentist's chair was big and blue and went up and down. The light over it was very bright. On a tray lay a little mirror and a little hook, shining.

Olivia looked at the tray. Then she put Mr. Socko on her hand.

"He wants to go first," she said.

The dentist was a Willowbrook dentist, so she did not even blink. "Open wide, Mr. Socko."

Mr. Socko opened wide.

The dentist looked inside with her little mirror. "Let's count them. Hmm. Zero teeth." She made a note. "Zero teeth, and not one cavity."

"Dat tickles," said Mr. Socko, in a scratchy voice that came from somewhere near Olivia.

Then it was Olivia's turn. Mr. Socko sat on her tummy to watch. The chair rode up. She got a pair of sunglasses for the light, and so did Mama, and they looked like movie stars.

The dentist counted Olivia's teeth with the little mirror, tapping each one. There were twenty. Olivia held still for all of them, except her feet.

The polish tasted like berries.

"Thank you for going first, Mr. Socko," said Mama, in the car.

"Dat wasn't scary," said Mr. Socko. "Just tickly."

Olivia had a new toothbrush, a purple one. Mr. Socko had the box it came in. He wore it home.
