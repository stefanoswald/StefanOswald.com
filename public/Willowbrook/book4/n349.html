Night 349

The Remembering Game

Month 12: Almost Four

"Tell me when I was little," said Olivia at supper. "Da good ones."

Papa put down his fork. "The day you were born, the brook came unstuck. The ice cracked and the water ran, all in one morning."

"Because of me?"

"The brook didn't say," said Papa. "But we thought so."

Mama went next. "Your first word was duck. You pointed with your whole arm."

"Duck," said Olivia, pointing with her whole arm.

Theo banged his tray. He patted his own chest with both hands.

"Me! Me!"

"He wants one," said Olivia.

"Do you want to tell it?" said Mama.

Olivia sat up. She had been there.

"You came home little. And red. And loud. You couldn't do nothing. Not even sit." She thought. "You liked Mama best. And da ceiling fan."

Theo laughed as if it were the best story he had ever heard.

"Moo," he said.

"And you had a cow," said Olivia.

"Moo!"

"Dat's da whole story, Fee-oh."

Theo clapped for himself. Olivia turned back to Papa.

"Now tell mine again."

So Papa started again with the brook.
