Night 327

If She Comes Early

Month 11: Winter Wonders

On another drippy day of the thaw, Olivia asked when Bella would come out.

"Usually in spring," said Mama. "But she's in our kitchen, and our kitchen is warm."

"So?"

"So she might think it's spring already. She might come out early."

Olivia looked out at the gray, slumped snow. "In da winter? She can't fly in da cold! Dere's no flowers!"

Her voice went high and wobbly. "Dat would be bad."

"You're worried about her," said Mama.

Olivia nodded hard. Mama stood beside her and didn't hurry. After a while the wobble went out.

"Maybe," said Mama. "We don't know how it goes yet. What can we get ready, in case?"

Olivia thought. "She could stay in da kitchen. Till it's warm."

"What would she eat?"

"Um. Juice?"

"Butterflies like oranges," said Mama. "We could give her a slice on a saucer."

"And da sunny window. And da cat stays out."

"The cat will have opinions," said Mama.

So they set a clean saucer by the parsley pot, and Olivia drew a sign for the kitchen door: a cat, with a big line through it.

"If it's spring, da garden," said Olivia. "If it's winter, da kitchen."

The saucer sat by the parsley, ready.
