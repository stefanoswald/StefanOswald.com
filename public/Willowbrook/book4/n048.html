Night 48

The Bear's Request

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Baba had never asked for anything. So when he stopped at the end of a meadow walk and said, "Livy, can I ask you something?" she listened hard.

"Dere's no tulips here," he said. "Can I see a real one?"

She promised.

In the morning it drizzled. Theo was napping, so it was just Olivia and Mama. Olivia put on her raincoat and boots and zipped Baba inside with only his head out.

"Mrs. Henderson has tulips," she said. "Red ones."

Mama held her hand all the way across the street. Mrs. Henderson was on her porch with her coffee. When she heard why they had come, she came down in her slippers.

"Well," she said. "Which one does he want?"

Olivia pointed at the reddest. Mrs. Henderson bent it down, gently, and Olivia held Baba's nose right up to it.

"Sniff, Baba."

She sniffed too. It smelled like almost nothing. A little like rain.

"Tulips don't smell much," said Mrs. Henderson. "They're for looking at."

So Baba looked. He looked for a good while, with rain on his nose.

That night in the meadow he had a red petal tucked behind his flopped ear.

"Did it smell good?" said Olivia.

"It smelled like nothing," said Baba. "I liked it anyway."

He wore the petal all night.
