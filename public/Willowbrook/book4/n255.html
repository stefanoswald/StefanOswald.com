Night 255

The Apology to Marcus

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In the block corner, Olivia built a tower as tall as her chin. Marcus drove his truck right into the bottom of it.

The tower fell. Olivia's mad came up fast, and words came out before she could catch them.

"You wreck everything! Nobody wants you at da blocks!"

Marcus's face went still. He took his truck to the sand table, and he didn't play with anyone for the rest of the morning.

At home, Olivia didn't want her snack.

Mama sat down on the floor beside her. "Something feels heavy."

"I said a mean thing to Marcus. He knocked my tower. I said nobody wants him."

"You were angry about your tower," said Mama. "Then the words hurt him."

"Dey're still in him."

"What could you do?"

Olivia thought. "Say sorry."

"Yes. Say what you said, and then ask what would help."

The next morning, at the blocks, Olivia's legs felt wobbly. She went anyway.

"Marcus. I said nobody wants you. Dat was mean. I'm sorry."

Marcus looked at his truck.

"What would help?" said Olivia.

Marcus thought about it for a long time. "Build my truck a garage."

So she did. It took all of free play. It had a door that opened, mostly.

Marcus drove the truck in, and out, and in.
