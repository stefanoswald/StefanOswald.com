Night 253

Brave for Someone Else

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

A week later it was Theo's checkup, and his checkup had a shot in it too.

Olivia came along. She brought his cow, the one with the bell.

In the little room, she stood by the door. "I don't want to watch his."

"You don't have to," said Mama. "You can wait by the fish tank in the hall."

Olivia looked at the hall. Then she looked at Theo on Mama's lap, in only his diaper, playing with Mama's buttons. He didn't know about shots.

"I stay," she said.

The nurse came in with her kind face and her cold fingers. Olivia's own arm remembered the pinch. She held the cow very tight.

The shot was quick. Theo's face went surprised, then red, and then he cried with his whole mouth.

Olivia's eyes filled up too. She took one step closer. Then she held up the cow and shook it, right in front of his face.

Ding. Ding-ding.

Theo stopped in the middle of a cry. He looked at the cow. He hiccupped.

"Moo," said Olivia.

"Moo," said Theo, wet and cross.

Mama rocked him. "You stayed," she said to Olivia. "Even though it was hard to see."

Olivia carried the cow all the way to the car. She rang it every time Theo frowned.
