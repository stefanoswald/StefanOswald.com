Night 194

The Too-Short Haircut Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Saturday morning was just-us: Mama and Olivia, the hair place on the square, and a lemonade after.

It was only supposed to be a trim. But curls spring up when they dry, and these sprang up much farther than anybody meant.

At home Olivia stood on the stool at the mirror. She did not say anything at all.

"I look like a dandelion."

Then she cried.

"It's a big surprise, seeing it," said Mama. "It's shorter than you wanted."

"MUCH shorter."

"Much shorter." Mama stood behind her and held her shoulders, and they both looked.

"Maybe," Mama said, when the crying stopped.

Olivia glared at the dandelion. "Maybe," she said, not believing it one bit.

On Sunday night, after the bath, Mama combed her hair. The comb went straight through. No tangles, and no crying.

"It didn't pull!"

On Monday Theo patted her head with both hands and laughed out loud, and then patted it again.

On Wednesday Lily said, "You're bouncy."

By Friday Olivia was shaking her head at the mirror on purpose, to watch it boing.
