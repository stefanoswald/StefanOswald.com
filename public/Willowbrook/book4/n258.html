Night 258

The Getting-Lost That Happened

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In November, Maple Lane had a lantern walk. By five it was dark, and the neighbors walked the lane with paper lanterns, singing.

Olivia's lantern was a moon, with a little battery light inside.

"If we lose each other," said Papa, "what do you do?"

"Statue. Shout. Helper." She had known it since the market.

Mama carried Theo. Papa held Olivia's hand.

Halfway down the lane, Olivia let go to fix her moon. When she looked up, there were coats and legs and lanterns everywhere, and no Papa.

Her tummy dropped to her boots.

She stood still, a statue. "PAPA! IT'S OLIVIA!"

Everybody was singing.

Right beside her was a mama pushing a stroller. A helper.

"I'm lost," said Olivia.

The mama held her lantern up high. "Olivia's papa!" she called, and down the lane other people called it too.

A minute later Papa came running. He crouched down and held her.

"You stood still," he said. "You found a helper."

"Statue, shout, helper." She was shaking.

"I need a slow breath," said Papa. He took one, and she took one with him.

Then Mama came with Theo, and Olivia counted everybody.

For the rest of the walk she held Papa's coat pocket. The moon swung from her other hand.
