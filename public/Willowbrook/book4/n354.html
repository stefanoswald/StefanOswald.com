Night 354

The Jar Harvest

Month 12: Almost Four

The morning after Maybe night, the jar comes down and gets tipped out on the rug. Theo was napping, which helped.

Out slid all the little drawings from September to now, with Papa's words underneath.

Olivia picked them up one at a time.

"Mr. Socko on da post. He got lost. Somebody put him on a post."

"And then?" said Mama.

"We found him."

The next was a puddle with a small person in it.

"Fee-oh splashing. On da rain day."

The next had four ducks on it.

"Da postcards. When Papa went away." She kept that one in her hand. "I didn't like it. Den da ducks came."

She made two piles, the ones that turned and the ones that didn't. The green circle went in the second pile by itself. Underneath, in Papa's writing, it said STAYED GREEN.

"Still green," said Olivia.

"Some stay the way they are," said Mama.

The last drawing was the back fence, with brown lines under it.

"Da Spill Garden. It's under da snow."

"So we don't know yet," said Mama.

"We don't know yet."

Olivia wound string around the rest, and Mama tied the knot. Then she put the Spill Garden back in the empty jar, by itself, and set the jar on the windowsill.

One paper, waiting.
