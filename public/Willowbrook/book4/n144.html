Night 144

Theo at the Library

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Theo had been to the Lantern Library before, but always asleep. Today he was awake, on Mama's hip, and Olivia showed him everything.

"Dis is where da books live. Dat's da round window. We use hush voices here." She put one finger on her lips. "Shhh."

Theo put one finger on his lips. "SHHHH!"

It was the loudest shush the library had ever heard. Two big kids looked up from their table. Theo shushed them too.

"Not dat loud, Fee-oh."

Theo shushed a man reading a newspaper. He shushed a lady with a stack of books. He shushed June, who was on the story rug with her mama, and June shushed him right back.

"He'd make a fine librarian," said the librarian.

Down low in the wall by the picture books, there is a small round hole. Molly Mouse looked out of it, with her acorn-cap satchel over her shoulder. "Who is that?" she asked, only to Olivia.

"My brother," said Olivia. "He's new at hush."

Molly nodded, the way a mouse who has read everything nods. Then she went back in.

Olivia picked a book about a cow. Theo picked a board book and chewed its corner, which is what board books are for.

On the way out, Theo shushed the door.
