Night 185

The Wrong Library Book Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Olivia picked five books at the Lantern Library all by herself while Theo napped at home with Papa. The pony book went on top.

At story time she opened the top book and stopped.

"Dis isn't da pony one!"

It wasn't. At the desk, their pile and another family's pile had shuffled together. Somewhere in Willowbrook, somebody else had the pony.

"I wanted da PONY." She pushed the book off her lap.

"You picked it all by yourself," said Mama. "And now it's gone."

Olivia kicked the couch once, not hard. Mama waited.

"Maybe," Mama said, when the kicking was done, and she opened the wrong book anyway.

A mountain, with fire coming out of the top.

"Why is da mountain on fire?"

"It's very hot deep under the ground. Sometimes the hot comes out the top."

"Is dere one in Willowbrook?"

"No. They're far, far away."

Olivia looked and looked. "Again."

They read it three times. In the morning the oatmeal went blup in the pot, and Olivia said it was erupting. In the bath she was a volcano, Theo was the town, and Mama was the rain.

On Friday the pony book came back. She looked at every page, politely.

"Da pony doesn't even erupt."
