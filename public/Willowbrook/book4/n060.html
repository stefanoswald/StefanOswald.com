Night 60

The First Why Avalanche

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

It started with toast.

"Why is da toast brown?"

"The toaster is hot inside," said Papa. "Hot turns bread brown."

"Why?"

"Heat cooks things," said Mama. "Like pancakes. Like eggs."

"Why does heat cook?"

Papa looked at Mama over the jam jar. Mama looked at Papa. It was ten past seven, and neither of them had finished a cup of coffee.

"Heat changes the bread inside," said Mama. "What else do you see on your toast?"

Olivia looked. The butter had gone shiny and was sliding down into the little holes.

"Da butter is running away! Why?"

"The toast is still hot," said Papa.

She touched the toast with one finger. Warm. She touched the butter dish. Cool.

"Hot toast melts da butter," she said, and ate the proof.

Then came the rest of them. Why is butter yellow? Why do cows make milk? Why does jam have lumps? Papa answered two and got up to make more coffee. Out the window a robin was tugging a worm out of the lawn, and for once nobody asked why.

Olivia turned to the jam jar. "Why are you purple?"

The jam kept its reasons to itself.
