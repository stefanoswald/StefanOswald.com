Night 55

The Naptime Visit

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

"I'm not tired," said Olivia, and yawned so wide her ears popped.

"I know you'd rather play," said Mama. "Rest time is still rest time. Baba or Rabbit?"

Olivia picked Baba. She lay down with her eyes wide open, to show she was only resting.

The rain went on and on against the window.

Her eyes closed for one second.

And there was the tea rock, and there was Baba sitting on it, his feet dangling.

"You came da short way!" he said.

"How long do I got?"

"Not long. Naps are little." He patted the rock. "Sit."

So she sat. There was no time for the brook or the fish or the firefly field. Baba showed her one snail with a shell that glowed like a nightlight, and together they watched it go about as far as her thumb.

Then the dream went thin, and she was in her own bed, and it was still raining.

"Mama! I saw Baba!"

Mama came to the door. "What did you two do?"

Olivia thought about it.

"We watched a snail."
