Night 292

The Reading Fort

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Papa built the fort against the front window, where the snow was coming down. Two chairs, the big blanket, and every pillow in the house.

"Books only," said Papa. "It's a reading fort."

Olivia brought the snow book, a flashlight, and Bobby Bear. Theo brought his cow and crawled in after her.

She opened the snow book on her lap and held the flashlight on it.

"Fee-oh. Where's da bird?"

Theo looked. He put his finger on the bird.

"Yes! Where's da sled?"

His finger went to the sled.

"Where's da moon?"

Theo turned around and pointed out the window, at the real snow coming down. "No!"

"Dat's snow," said Olivia. "Not moon. But okay."

They did the whole book that way. Olivia asked, and Theo pointed. When he got it, she said, "Yes!" When he didn't, she said, "Close."

A while later Papa looked in through the gap. The flashlight was pointing at the ceiling. Theo was lying on his cow with his eyes almost shut. Olivia was telling the snow book to Bobby Bear, in a whisper.

Papa lifted Theo out, cow and all, and carried him up to his crib.

Olivia kept reading to the bear.
