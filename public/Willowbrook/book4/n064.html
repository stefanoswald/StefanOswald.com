Night 64

Why Can't I Fly

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Mateo said his cape could fly. Olivia had tested a cape back in March, off the bottom step, and it could not.

"Papa. Why can't people fly?"

"Birds have bones as light as straws," said Papa. "And wings bigger than their whole bodies."

"I got heavy bones?"

"Sturdy ones. Good for running and climbing."

"Birds can't go down slides."

"Not one bird," said Papa. "I've watched."

The swings were free. Olivia wanted to go high by herself, like the big kids. She leaned back and kicked, and leaned forward and kicked, and the swing wobbled sideways and hardly went anywhere.

"It's not going!"

"Pumping is tricky," said Papa. "Your legs don't know the timing yet."

He gave her a push. "Now kick out, going up."

She kicked out going up. He pushed. She kicked. He pushed.

"You're kicking right at the top," said Papa. "That's what makes it go."

At the top of each swing, her tummy floated for half a second.

"I'm flying!"

"Half a second at a time," said Papa.

They flew until Papa's arms gave out. Then she let the swing slow down. She scuffed it to a stop.
