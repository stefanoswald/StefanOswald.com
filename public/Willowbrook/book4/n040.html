Night 40

The Night Baba Couldn't Come

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

"He wasn't dere."

Olivia was sitting up in bed with Baba hanging from one hand. Mama was pulling clothes out of the drawer, fast, because they were late.

"Who wasn't where, love?"

"Baba. I dreamed a bus. And socks. No meadow."

"Well, it was only a dream," said Mama. "Shoes on, please."

Olivia put her shoes on. She put them on the wrong feet and did not care.

Mama was halfway out the door. Then she looked back at Olivia, and she came back in.

She knelt by the bed. "I'm sorry. I rushed right past that." She waited. "You missed him."

Olivia's chin wobbled. "I tried to dream him in."

"I know you did." Mama didn't say anything else for a while. She stayed down there by the wrong-footed shoes until the wobble passed.

"Maybe tonight," she said. "What could help?"

Olivia thought. "Hold him tighter?"

"Try it."

That night she held him so tight his ear folded over. And there was the meadow, and there was Baba on the bank of the brook, as if he had been there all along.

"Where WERE you?"

"Right here," said Baba. "You were on a bus."

She held on until morning.
