Night 165

The Show and Tell Debut

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Friday was show and tell. Olivia thought about it all week. The red cape? The shiny rock? Bobby Bear?

She chose the feather. It was speckled brown and white, and it came from the Duchess, Grandpa Joe's grumpiest hen, who does not give anybody anything.

On Friday she stood up at circle. All the faces looked at her, and her words went away.

She held the feather tight. She looked at Lily. Lily nodded at her, hard, the way Mama nods at the edge of the pool.

"Dis is a feather," said Olivia. "From da Duchess. She's a hen."

Marcus put his hand up. "Does she bite?"

"She pecks."

"Is she in your pocket?"

"No. She's at da farm."

June wanted to know if the feather could fly. Olivia held it up high and let go. It drifted down, turning and turning, and landed on Wren's shoe.

Nobody said anything. Then everybody wanted a turn to drop it.

"It went round da whole circle," she told Mama at pickup. "Nobody squished it."

That night it slept on her windowsill.
