Night 247

Speaking Up at Circle

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Sam's grandpa was the helper at preschool that week. He was tall and kind, with a big voice, and he knew the words to every song.

At circle, Miss Dana said the chairs had to go to the tables for painting.

"Strong boys!" said Sam's grandpa. "Who wants to carry chairs?"

All the boys' hands went up. Olivia's cheeks went hot. Her hand wanted to go up and also wanted to stay in her lap.

It went up halfway.

"Yes?" said Sam's grandpa.

Everybody turned to look at her. Her voice came out small. "Girls carry too. I carry da milk. At home."

"And the laundry," said Lily, who hadn't raised her hand at all.

Sam's grandpa looked at the two of them for a moment. "You're right," he said. "I said that wrong." Then he tried again. "Strong kids! Who wants to carry chairs?"

Every hand went up, even Wren's.

Olivia and Lily carried one chair together. It was heavier than milk. They bumped it into the easel, a table and Sam, and said sorry to all three.

At pickup, Sam's grandpa was holding the door. "Thank you for telling me," he said to Olivia.

Her cheeks went hot all over again. She stood behind Mama's coat, but only halfway.
