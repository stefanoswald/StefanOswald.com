Night 310

Theo and the Melted Snowman

Month 11: Winter Wonders

It was too cold for Theo outside, so Mama brought a bowl of snow in from the porch.

On a pie tin at the table, Olivia rolled a snowman three balls high. Mama gave him a carrot nose. Theo poked in two eyes with one finger and stroked the snowman's head all over.

"Man," said Theo.

He loved that snowman with his whole face. Then it was nap time.

After his nap, he went straight to the kitchen and climbed onto his chair to look.

The pie tin was full of water. The carrot lay in it on its side.

"No," said Theo. He patted the water. It stayed water. "Mine!"

Then he cried, the whole-body kind.

Mama picked him up. "You wanted your snowman," she said. "He melted."

"We can make anudder one," said Olivia.

"No!"

Olivia looked at her brother. "He wants dat one."

"He does," said Mama.

Olivia held Theo's sock foot and didn't let go.

Mama set the carrot in a saucer of water on the windowsill, beside the green room.

"Why?" said Olivia.

"Just to see."

At bedtime, Theo pointed at the dark window. "Man?"

"He melted," said Mama, the same as before.

"Melt," said Theo, trying it out. He told his cow twice, and then he was asleep.
