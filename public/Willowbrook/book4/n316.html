Night 316

Snow Angels

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Fresh snow fell in the night, and Olivia had a plan before breakfast.

"Everybody makes snow angels. Even grown-ups. Especially grown-ups."

"I'm too old for snow angels," said Papa.

"You're not old," said Olivia. "You're just long."

So everybody went out and lay down in a row. Mama swept her arms slow and careful. Olivia flapped as fast as she could. Theo lay on his back and looked at the sky, surprised by it. Then he rolled over and ate some of his angel.

Papa made the biggest angel in the yard. Then he tried to get up without wrecking it.

He sat up. He tipped back. He rolled to one side, and his elbow went through a wing.

"Papa's stuck!"

"Papa is resting," said Papa, from inside the snow.

It took Mama and Olivia on one arm each to get him out, and Theo on a boot, to help. When Papa finally stood up, there was a big round dent in the middle of his angel.

"Your angel has a bottom," said Olivia, and fell over laughing, and made another angel by accident.

From the kitchen window, the angels lay in a row, one of them with a bottom.

The yard kept them till morning.
