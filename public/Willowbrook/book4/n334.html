Night 334

Stella and the Green Room

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Snow fell again on Willowbrook, small and slow, and the town slept under it.

Maple Lane was dark, every house but one, where a little light was on over the kitchen stove.

Stella came down low to see. Behind a chimney, the Sleepy Moon opened one eye.

On the windowsill stood a pot of parsley, a carrot with green hair, and a jar of folded drawings. From the parsley hung the green room, empty now and split at the bottom. Beside it, on a slice of orange, Bella rested with her wings closed.

Upstairs, everybody was asleep. The cat slept outside the kitchen door, as close as the sign allowed.

Bella looked out at the snow for a long time. Then she looked up.

"Flowers?" she asked.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.
