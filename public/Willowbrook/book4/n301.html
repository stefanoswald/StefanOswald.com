Night 301

The Word Willow

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Mama and Olivia walked up the hill through the snow to the Great Willow. Its long branches hung down to the ground like a curtain, and there was snow along every one.

Olivia pushed through the curtain to the trunk.

The words were still there in the bark, the ones that came on her birthdays.

She put her mitten on the first one. "Dat says home."

"How do you know?" said Mama.

"You told me. When I was two." She moved her mitten over. "Dat says family."

She couldn't read either one. She knew them anyway, like the last page of the bear book.

"Mama. Da tree is writing."

"Writing what?"

"Words. One every birthday. Dat's slow."

"Very slow," said Mama. "It's a tree."

Olivia put her ear on the bark to listen. Nothing. Just cold, and a little creak from up high.

"What will it write when I'm four?"

"I don't know," said Mama. "We'll come and see."

Olivia thought about it all the way down the hill. At the bottom she stopped.

"I hope it's a long one," she said.

Up on the hill, the willow was turning gold.
