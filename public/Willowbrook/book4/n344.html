Night 344

The Spring Peepers Night

Month 12: Almost Four

It was the first mild night of the year, and it smelled like mud. Papa opened the back door and listened.

"Hear that?"

From down by the brook came a sound like a hundred tiny bells.

"Peepers," said Papa. "Little frogs, the first ones after winter."

They went down in boots, with the flashlight. Old snow still lay along the banks. The brook was running high and loud, so they kept to the path, and Papa kept her hand.

The ringing was everywhere, until they came close. Then it stopped.

"Dey heard us."

"Stand still," said Papa. "Lights off for a little, and I've got you."

Olivia did not love the dark. She held his hand with both of hers, and the light went off.

Nothing. Then one peep. Then ten. Then the whole brook, all around them, ringing.

On a stone at the edge sat Freddy Frog, with winter mud on his lily-pad hat.

"Dey're my band," said Freddy. He puffed himself up to show her how. It came out a burp.

Olivia giggled.

"What's funny?" said Papa. He could only hear frogs.

"Freddy."

Papa looked at the stone. "He does look pleased with himself."

They walked home slowly. Behind them, the ringing got smaller.
