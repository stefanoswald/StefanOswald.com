Night 214

The Thirsty Fern

Month 8: Helping Hands

The fern was Olivia's job, and for three days it did not get watered.

On the first day there were puddles to jump in. On the second day Lily came over. On the third day there was no reason at all.

Ferns do not complain. They just droop.

On the fourth morning its long leaves hung down over the pot like wet hair.

"Mama! Da fern is broken!"

Mama came and looked. "Oh. That's a thirsty fern."

Olivia's chin wobbled. "I forgotted it. Three days."

"You forgot," said Mama. "It happens to everybody."

"Is it dead?"

"Not dead, just very thirsty. What could we do?"

They gave it a slow drink, a little and then a little more. Olivia sprayed its leaves with the mist bottle until her finger got tired.

By bedtime most of the fern had lifted its head. One leaf stayed brown and crackly. Mama snipped it off.

"Will dat one come back?"

"That one won't," said Mama. "The rest are drinking."

The next morning Olivia carried the watering can to the front hall and set it down beside her boots.

On his way out the door, Papa tripped over it.

"Dat's where it lives now," said Olivia.

Papa stepped around it.
