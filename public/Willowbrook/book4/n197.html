Night 197

The Torn Page Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

The bear book is older than Olivia. Its corners are round and its pages are soft, and the best page is the one where the bear finds his button.

On Tuesday Olivia turned to it too fast, and it tore. Right through the bear.

"I tored it!" She held the two pieces apart and stared at them. "He's in HALF."

"Oh, your best page," said Mama. "You're so sad."

Olivia put her face in Mama's cardigan. Mama held the book in one hand and Olivia in the other.

"Maybe," Mama said.

She got the tape. Olivia's job was holding the two edges together, exactly, so the bear's ear lined up. Her fingers held very still. Mama laid the tape down and smoothed it with her thumbnail.

The bear was one bear again. But a shiny line went right across him, from his ear to his foot.

"It still shows."

"It still shows," said Mama. "That's where we mended it."

Olivia ran her finger along the line. It felt smooth and a little cold.

At bedtime she read the button page to Bobby Bear, one finger on the tape.
