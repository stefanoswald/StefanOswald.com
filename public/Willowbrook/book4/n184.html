Night 184

The Rained-Out Farm Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Sunday was farm day, and Sunday poured.

"The farm lane will be all mud," said Papa at the window. "No farm today."

Olivia pressed her forehead to the glass. "But da hens. But da tractor." Her lip went. "It's FARM day."

"You were all ready for the hens," said Papa. "That's a big sad." He let her lean on his leg and watch the rain.

"Maybe," he said, after.

At eleven, headlights came up the drive. Grandpa Joe climbed out of his truck with rain running off his straw hat.

"Mud," he said, and came in.

He did not sit down. He walked around the kitchen slowly, opening things. The cabinet door squeaked. He opened it again and listened.

"Hm."

He went back out to the truck and came in with his tool box.

Olivia held the screws. She held them the way she holds eggs at the farm. Grandpa fixed the squeak, then the wobbly stool, then a drawer nobody had mentioned.

"How did you know about da drawer?"

Grandpa winked.

After lunch he read the bear book in the big chair, very slowly, and fell asleep on page six. Olivia turned the pages for him the rest of the way.

"Da farm came to us," she told Mama.

The rain kept on. So did the snoring.
