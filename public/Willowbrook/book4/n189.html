Night 189

The Burnt Cookies Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Mama and Olivia made oatmeal cookies and slid them into the oven. Then the phone rang. It was Rosa, and Rosa was talking about bread.

Nobody set the timer.

BEEP. BEEP. BEEP. The smoke alarm went off in the ceiling, loud enough to rattle the spoons. Theo's face crumpled. Olivia put her hands over his ears.

Mama said goodbye to Rosa in one word and pulled out the tray. Every cookie was black.

"It's only the cookies," she said over the noise. "No fire." She opened the window and waved a dish towel until the beeping stopped.

Then it was quiet, and smoky, and sad.

"My cookies! Why did you burn dem?"

"I forgot them. That's my mistake," said Mama. "You can be cross about it."

Olivia was cross about it. Mama let her.

"The alarm is loud on purpose," Mama said, when the cross was done. "It has to wake up sleeping people."

"Even Fee-oh?"

"Even Theo. If it beeps and I don't say 'only cookies,' we go out to the mailbox."

Then she got the bowl out again. "Maybe," she said.

Batch two had a timer, and Olivia held it in her lap.

"Is it now?"

"Nope."

"Now?"

"Almost."

"NOW?"

It was.

Olivia kept the timer in her lap until supper.
