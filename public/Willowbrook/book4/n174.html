Night 174

The Harvest Party

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The preschool had a harvest party at the end of summer. Families came, and you could dress up as anything that grows, or anything that helps things grow.

Olivia went as a sunflower. She cut the paper petals herself, with Mama's hand on the scissors for the hard corners.

Theo went as a bee.

The bee suit had been Olivia's. It came out of the dress-up box squashed, so she fluffed the wings. It didn't fit her anymore.

"Now it's Fee-oh's," she said. "Bees go bzzz, Fee-oh."

Theo blew a raspberry.

"Dat's close," said Olivia.

Lily came as a baker, in a strawberry apron, with flour on one cheek that she had put there herself. Rosa took one look and pretended to faint.

The garden table had tomatoes, beans, a jar of honey, and one zucchini the size of a baby. Nobody had asked for more zucchini. Zucchini does not wait to be asked.

Theo let go of Olivia's hand and walked three steps to the table by himself. Then he sat down.

Mama held a little cup of water for him, her hand under the bottom. He drank some and wore some.

"Uh-oh," said Theo.

The bee fell asleep in the stroller.
