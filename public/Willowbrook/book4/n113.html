Night 113

Lending Courage at the Bridge

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

On Thursday the girls walked to the brook, with Mama and Miss Rosa close behind them. At the little wooden bridge, Lily stopped.

"I don't like dis bridge." She held the rail with both hands. "You can see da water. In da cracks."

Olivia looked down. You could, in thin brown stripes.

Lily was the bold one. Lily went down the big slide headfirst. But Lily was not moving now.

Olivia did not say "come on."

"You don't like da cracks," she said.

"No."

"Want my hand?"

Olivia put her hand flat under Lily's on the rail, just as Papa once had for her at the gold dog's fence.

They went one board at a time. Olivia told the water what it was doing. "It's just going by. It doesn't see us."

At the far side, Lily let go and blew out a big breath. Then she turned around. "Again. By myself dis time."

"I'll be right dere," said Olivia, and stood at the end with her hand out, in case.

Lily went across by herself, holding the rail. Halfway, she stopped and looked down, and looked up, and kept going.

"One board at a time," said Miss Rosa, catching up. "That's how you did it."

Underneath, the brook kept going by.
