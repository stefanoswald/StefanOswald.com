Night 50

Franklin Gets Dreamed In

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

"Franklin came," said Olivia at breakfast. "To da meadow. I dreamed him in."

Papa put down his toast. "What does he look like?"

"You still can't see him. Not even dere."

"Then how do you know he came?"

"Da grass bent where he sat. And da tea rock got warm."

Theo banged his spoon on the high chair tray, twice, as if he agreed.

"Did Baba like him?" said Mama.

"Dey knew each udder already. From before." Olivia frowned. "Baba won't say from where."

"What did you do?" said Papa.

"Tea. Two sugars. Den skipping stones." She held up all ten fingers, and then three of Papa's. "Franklin got thirteen."

"Thirteen skips! Did you see them?"

"You can't see Franklin's stones. Just da splashes."

Papa nodded slowly. He had once skipped a stone five times, years ago, and he still mentioned it.

That afternoon Olivia left an empty spot on the rug, next to her and Baba, in case Franklin wanted to sit.

The cat lay down in it.
