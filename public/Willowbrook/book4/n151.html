Night 151

The Watch on the Two of Them

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

The night was so warm that every window on Maple Lane stood open.

Stella dipped low over the rooftops. On the Great Willow's highest branch, Oliver Owl sat with his notebook shut.

"Two windows," said Stella. "Side by side."

In one, a girl was asleep with a bear under her chin. In the other, the yellow one, a small boy was asleep on his back, one hand on his cow.

"Were they this close last summer?" asked Oliver Owl.

"One of them was brand new last summer," said Stella. "He mostly slept."

"And now?"

"Now he follows her everywhere."

Down in the grass, the fireflies came up. A few drifted between the two windows, blinking on and off, like a string someone had hung there.

"Do you see that?" asked the owl.

"I see it," said Stella.

The boy turned over in his sleep, toward the wall. On the other side of the wall, the girl turned over too.

The Sleepy Moon hummed, very low.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
