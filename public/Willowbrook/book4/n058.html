Night 58

The Long Way to Bed

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The peas were up. Olivia found them after supper, a row of green hooks in the wet dirt.

"Papa! Dey came up!"

Papa crouched. "Even the one you dug up?"

"Twice. Dat one too."

They took the long way to bed after that. At the back door, the deputy pushed, and it was locked. At the window where they had counted the thunder, she counted to nine, for practice. At the kitchen wall, Papa pointed at the orange smudge on the map.

"Who's that?"

"Franklin, Papa."

"And who went to see the tulips?"

"Baba. Dey smelled like nothing."

"What about the night he didn't come?"

"I held him tighter. Den he came."

At the yellow room they looked in. Theo was asleep on his back with his arms up, next to the rail he had first pulled up on.

"Who showed him how to sit down?"

"Me. I bended my knees."

"You bent them slow, so he could see."

In bed she told Baba the rest: the moose, the moth with fuzzy feet, Theo at the picnic, Lily's rabbit, the cow who followed her whistle home, and the gate, still far away.

"Anything else?" said Papa.

Olivia looked at the shelf.

"No," she said.

Baba kept it too.
