Night 238

The Chart Grows a Column

Month 8: Helping Hands

Olivia knelt on a chair in front of the job list. It had her fern, her sock, her spoon. It had Theo's purple line and his clothespin.

"Where's your jobs?" she asked.

"Ours aren't up there," said Mama.

"Why not?"

"We just do them," said Papa.

"Dat's not fair."

So Olivia got the crayons and made a new column, and Mama wrote MAMA and PAPA at the top.

"Tell me your jobs," said Olivia.

Mama cooked, so Olivia drew a pot. Mama did laundry, so Olivia drew a sock. Mama found things, so Olivia drew a big eye.

"Don't forget 'in a minute,'" said Papa.

Mama gave him a look. Olivia drew a clock anyway.

Papa did the dishes, so she drew a plate. "And I carry sleeping people up the stairs," said Papa.

Olivia stopped drawing. "What sleeping people?"

"You. Most nights you fall asleep on the couch, and I carry you up."

Olivia had never once seen that job. She had always been asleep for it.

That night she did not fall asleep on the couch. She walked up the stairs by herself, all the way to the top, and turned around.

"Dat's for da other nights," she said.

Papa sat down on the stairs.
