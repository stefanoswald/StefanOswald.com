Night 3

The Box Castle

Month 1: Pretend!

The aunts' birthday package came in a box big enough to sit in.

"It's a castle. Papa, I need a door."

Papa cut a drawbridge with the big knife while Olivia watched from the couch, away from the knife. Then her crayons made the stones, gray scribbles, row on row. A paper flag went up, with a duck on it.

Queen Olivia wore her birthday crown. Sir Theo sat on the rug and was knighted with the dragon spoon. He tried to eat it.

The kingdom stood for three days. On the fourth day, the queen watered the moat.

A castle made of box does not like a moat. The front wall went soft. It leaned. Then, very slowly, it sat down.

"Why it falling?"

"Water makes cardboard soft," Papa said. "Moats are tricky."

Olivia looked at the soggy wall. Her chin wobbled once.

"Next castle, no water," she said.

"Next castle, no water," said Papa.
