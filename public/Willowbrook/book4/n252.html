Night 252

The Storm Chaser

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

The last thunderstorm of the year came on a November night, when nobody expected one.

In June, thunder had sent Olivia under the covers. Tonight she came down in her pajamas. "Can we watch it?"

"From inside," said Papa. "When there's thunder, we stay in, back from the window."

So Papa made cocoa, not too hot, and they sat on the couch across the room from the big window. Theo came too, on Papa's arm.

The sky went white.

"Count!" said Olivia. "One, two, three, four, five."

BOOM.

"Five far," said Olivia.

The rain ran down the glass in wiggles. At the next flash they only got to three.

"It's coming closer." Her cocoa hand held on tighter. She stayed on the couch.

Theo pointed at the window. "Boom!"

"Yes, Fee-oh. Boom," said Olivia. "It's loud. It can't come in."

The next boom was the biggest. The window buzzed. Olivia's shoulders went up to her ears, and Papa's arm came around her, and they came back down.

Then the booms got farther away. Four. Six. Nine. The rain slowed to a patter.

"It's going to somebody else's house," said Olivia.

"Boom," said Theo, much quieter, into Papa's sweater.

By the time the cocoa was gone, he was asleep on Papa's shoulder, and the storm was only rain.
