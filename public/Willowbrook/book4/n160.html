Night 160

The Kid Who Plays Alone

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Every free play, Wren did puzzles. She did them by herself, at the corner table, every single day.

"Is Wren sad?" Olivia asked at bedtime.

"I don't know," said Mama. "Some people like to play alone, and some are waiting for a friend."

"Which one is Wren?"

"How could you find out, without scaring her off?"

Olivia thought about Buttons under the easel.

The next day she did a puzzle at the other end of Wren's table. She didn't say anything. Neither did Wren.

The day after that, she sat a little closer.

On the third day she found a blue piece on her side of the table. "Dis is your sky," she said. She put it by Wren's elbow and went back to her own puzzle.

Wren looked at it for a while. Then she clicked it in.

On the fifth day, Wren slid a piece across the table without a word. It was a wheel from her tractor. It fit.

"Wren and me did puzzles," Olivia said at pickup.

"Did you talk?" said Mama.

"No. We did puzzles."

The tractor got finished on Friday.
