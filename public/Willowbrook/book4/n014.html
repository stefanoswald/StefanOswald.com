Night 14

When the Pretend Gets Too Real

Month 1: Pretend!

At dusk, at cleanup time, Olivia stopped in the middle of her room with a block in each hand.

"Mama. Da wild bear's in my closet."

"The snow-land bear?"

"I don't want him dere now."

Her voice was small. She did not go near the closet, and she did not put the blocks down.

Mama did not say there was no bear. She came and knelt beside her.

"You're scared of the bear in the closet."

Olivia nodded hard.

"I think the snow door is still open," Mama said.

Olivia looked at the closet.

"You made that door," Mama said. "You can close it."

"You come too."

"I'm right here."

They took one step toward the closet, together. Then one more. Olivia held Mama's hand very tight.

She drew a door in the air in front of the closet. She turned the knob. Click.

"Bear. It's night. Go home to da snow land."

She waited. "He's going. He waved."

Then she shut the door and turned the knob the other way.

Mama switched on the hall light. They sat on the bed, and Olivia leaned on Mama and breathed. Her shoulders came down, a little at a time.

Soon she got up and put the last block in the basket.
