Night 346

The Wish

Month 12: Almost Four

A birthday wish only works if you don't tell it. So Olivia was choosing hers in private, with Bobby Bear.

"A dragon," she told him. "A little one. For da bath."

Bobby Bear said nothing. He only talks in dreams.

"Or snow in summer. Or a slide to da kitchen."

She drew each one on her clipboard to see how it looked. The slide looked good.

Then she stopped drawing.

"Or Buttons back."

She looked at the bear. Mama had answered that one at the lamp more than once, always the same way. Olivia said it to Bobby Bear the same way.

"Buttons died. His body stopped working."

The bear listened.

"Dat one is just sad."

She sat still with the bear in her lap. Then she turned to a clean page and drew something new, slowly, with a lot of gold in it. She folded it into a small square before the bear could see.

She tucked the square under his arm.

"Keep it till Saturday. Don't tell."

Bobby Bear held it all night.
