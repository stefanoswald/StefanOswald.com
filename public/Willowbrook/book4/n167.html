Night 167

The Pickup Fence

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

At pickup, the grown-ups waited along the fence. Every day Rosa had flour on one cheek, and every day somebody told her.

One afternoon Olivia ran out and found Mama standing at the far end of the fence, not talking to anybody.

"Why are you by yourself?"

"I don't know anybody yet," said Mama. "I'm new too."

Olivia stopped. She looked up at Mama. "Grown-ups get new?"

"Yes. It feels wobbly."

Olivia took her hand and pulled her down the fence to Wren's grandma, who was holding a thermos.

"Dis is my mama," said Olivia. "She's new. She likes cats."

Wren's grandma said she liked cats too. Mama said their cat slept in backpacks. Wren's grandma said hers slept in the bread box. After that, the two of them talked the whole time.

By Friday, Mama knew six names at the fence and how to make plum cake.

"Were you scared at school?" Olivia asked on the way home.

"A little," said Mama. "The first day, I cried in the car."

"I cried at da door."

"You did. Then you fed Buttons."

They swung hands the whole four blocks.
