Night 11

The Restaurant of Mud

Month 1: Pretend!

Olivia opened a restaurant by the back fence, in the mud where the snow had been.

She stirred mud soup in a pot. She carried one bowl up the back steps, through the door, and across the clean kitchen floor to Mama.

Drip. Drip. Drip.

"Olivia! Look at this floor!"

It came out loud. Olivia stood very still with her bowl. Her lip wobbled. She turned around and went back outside.

Mama looked at the floor. She looked at the door. She took a slow breath.

She went out and crouched by the fence, where Olivia was stirring very hard.

"I yelled," Mama said. "I'm sorry."

Olivia stirred.

"I was cross about the floor. Not about you."

Olivia stirred slower.

"Mud stays outside. Could I eat at the restaurant out here?"

Olivia looked up. "You hafta sit down first."

Mama sat on the upturned bucket. The soup came on a leaf.

"Da soup is hot. Blow first."

Mama blew. Then she lifted the leaf toward her mouth.

"MAMA! Pretend eating!"

"Oh. Right," said Mama, and said mmm at it instead.

The bill was one pebble. Later they wiped the kitchen floor together. Olivia did the drips.
