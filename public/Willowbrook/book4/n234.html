Night 234

The Fire Station Thank-You

Month 8: Helping Hands

Miss Dana's class went to the fire station to say thank you, and also to see the truck.

Everyone had made a drawing. Olivia's was a red truck with a yellow duck driving it.

"Why is a duck driving?" asked Lily.

"He's da helper," said Olivia.

Rosa sent buns.

The firefighters came out to the bay. The class held up the drawings and said thank you all at once, which came out as "THANK-OO."

The tallest firefighter looked at every drawing. "Most people see us when something's wrong," he said. "Nobody comes just to say thanks."

Then he put on his mask. His breathing came out loud and whooshy, like wind in a pipe. Some of the class stepped back. Olivia held Lily's hand hard.

"I don't like dat face," said Olivia.

"It's a strange face," said Miss Dana. "It's still him in there."

The firefighter knelt down so they could see his eyes. He held out one big glove. Olivia touched it with one finger. Her shoulders came down.

Then everybody practiced stop, drop, and roll on the station floor. Lily rolled all the way under a bench.

On the way out Olivia looked back. The duck was taped up in the bay, next to the truck.
