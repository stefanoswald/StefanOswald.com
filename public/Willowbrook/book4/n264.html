Night 264

One Long Whistle

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Olivia and Lily had a whistle code. One whistle meant hello, and the answer was one whistle back.

On Saturday, at dusk, they made a new one.

"One LONG whistle," said Lily, "means I'm scared."

"Den what?"

"Den you whistle short. And come stand by me."

They practiced on the sidewalk while Mama and Miss Rosa watched from the porch. Lily walked three houses down. Olivia whistled a long one, wobbly in the middle. Lily whistled short and came running.

Papa came out onto the porch. "Can I be in the code?"

He whistled. Only air came out, and a little spit.

"Dat's not a whistle, Papa."

"It's a very quiet one," said Papa. So Papa's signal was the porch light instead. Blink, blink.

Lily went back down the lane for one more practice. Just then the streetlight came on with a buzz, and behind a fence the gold dog barked, deep and sudden. Lily jumped.

Then a whistle came up the lane. One long one. It wasn't practice.

Olivia whistled short. She walked down the sidewalk, fast, and stood next to Lily, shoulder to shoulder, until the barking stopped.

"Dat was a real one," said Lily.

"I know," said Olivia.

Up on the porch, the light blinked twice.
