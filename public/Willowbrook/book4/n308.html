Night 308

Fort Together

Month 11: Winter Wonders

It was packing snow, and plenty of it. Olivia, Lily and June started a fort below the kite hill. Miss Rosa watched from the bench, with a thermos and flour on her cheek.

The first wall went straight up and fell straight down.

"Lean it back," said June. "Like a hill."

The next wall leaned, and it stayed. June had a spare red mitten, so it went on a stick for a flag.

"It needs a name," said Lily. "Fort Lily."

"Fort June," said June.

Olivia put her mitten on the wall. "Fort Togedder. Den it's everybody's."

They said it out loud a few times, and Fort Together won.

"Just us three inside," said Lily.

Then Wren came across the snow with wet mittens and a red nose, and stood by the wall, not asking.

Olivia looked at the nose. "Da three of us," she said. "And whoever's cold."

Wren climbed in over the low wall, grinning. It was a small fort for four, and nobody minded knees. Lily ran to the bench and came back with warm rolls in her hat, and they broke them so it came out even.

When the snow turned blue, Rosa called them home. The fort stayed behind with its flag up and nobody in it.
