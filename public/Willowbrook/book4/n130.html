Night 130

Dat's My Brother

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

At Dandelion Park, Theo sat in the sandbox with a red shovel. He wasn't digging. He was banging it on his knee, which he thought was excellent.

A bigger boy came over with a truck. He was four, or nearly. He looked at the red shovel. Then he took it.

Theo's mouth went round. He looked at his empty hand. Then he looked at Olivia, to find out what his face should do.

Olivia stood up. She planted her feet right next to him.

"Dat's my brother's," she said. "He's little."

The boy held on to the shovel.

On the bench, Mama sat forward. She stayed where she was, for now.

Olivia looked down at her own bucket, the green one. Then she held it out.

"You can have dis. For da shovel."

The boy looked at the bucket. It was a very good bucket. He handed back the shovel and took it.

Olivia gave the shovel to Theo. Theo banged it on his knee again, delighted, as if nothing had happened.

"You want to dig a road?" said the boy.

They dug a road. Theo banged.

On the walk home, Mama said, "You stood right by him. Then you found a trade."

The green bucket had stayed behind, with the boy.

"Tomorrow I want my bucket back."
