Night 80

The Why Journal

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Mama came home from the store with a notebook, a plain one with a green cover. On the first page she wrote WHY in big letters.

"What's dat for?"

"Your whys," said Mama. "So we don't lose any."

She wrote the first one down. Why is the toast brown?

Papa read over her shoulder. "Put the moon one in." Mama wrote, Why does the moon follow the car? Underneath she wrote, Too far away to leave behind.

"And da worms," said Olivia. Mama wrote the worms.

"And where I was before."

Mama wrote that one, and left the space under it empty.

"Why is it empty?"

"Nobody knows that answer," said Mama.

"I want to write one."

Olivia took the green crayon. She drew a big circle. She gave it two eyes. She thought for a while, and gave it a mouth.

"What's the why?" asked Papa.

"Da moon has a face. Why?"

"From here, the dark patches on it look like eyes and a mouth," said Papa.

Olivia looked at her circle. It looked back.

Mama wrote the words under the drawing. Why does the moon have a face?

Then she closed the notebook on the crayon moon.
