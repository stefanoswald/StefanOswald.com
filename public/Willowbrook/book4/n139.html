Night 139

The Snack Chef for Two

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Olivia opened a cafe on the kitchen floor. It had a dish towel for a tablecloth, a cushion for a chair, and one customer.

"What'll it be, sweetheart?" she asked, with one hand on her hip. That is what Miss Rosa says at the bakery, exactly that way.

The customer said, "Ba."

"One soup," said Olivia. She stirred the empty pot and poured him an empty bowl. "Careful. It's hot."

Theo looked into the bowl. He looked at her. Then he put the bowl on his head.

"Dat's not how soup goes."

"He doesn't do pretend yet," said Mama from the counter. "He only knows the real kind."

Olivia frowned at her cafe. Then she went to Mama and asked for something real. Mama peeled a banana and cut it into little pieces, the small size Theo needs, and Olivia carried the plate down slowly.

"Real banana," she said. "Da special today."

Theo ate every piece. He did not say thank you. He held out his hand for more.

"Come again," said Olivia, the way Miss Rosa does.

The customer kept his hand out.
