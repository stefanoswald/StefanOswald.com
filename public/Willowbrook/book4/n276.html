Night 276

Theo's Letter

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Theo wanted a letter too. He made this clear by grabbing Olivia's crayon and scribbling on her name.

"Fee-oh! Dat's MINE!"

She moved her paper out of reach. Theo reached for it anyway, with both arms and a loud noise.

Olivia frowned at him. Then she gave him a paper of his own and showed him his letter.

"T," she said. "For Fee-oh. Down stick. Hat stick."

Theo made a round scribble. Then a lot of round scribbles.

"Dose are O's," said Olivia. "Dose are MY letter."

She showed him again. Down stick. Hat stick. Theo made a line that went off the paper and across his tray.

"Okay," said Olivia. "Close."

Then she got two blocks from his bucket, the T one and the O one, and held them up.

"Fee-oh. Where's da T?"

Theo looked. He put one finger right on the T.

Olivia gasped. "Where's da O?"

He pointed at the T.

"Where's da cat?"

He pointed at the T.

"He knows one," Olivia told the cat. "It's a good one."

Theo carried the T block around until supper. He fed it a pea.
