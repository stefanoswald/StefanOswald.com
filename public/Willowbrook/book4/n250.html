Night 250

The Sleepover at Lily's

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

The first time Olivia slept at Lily's house, in June, Miss Rosa sat on the bed and hummed until Olivia fell asleep.

Now it was November. It was dark before supper, and Olivia was back.

In her bag she had Bobby Bear, facing out, the gold nightlight, and the folded brave list in the side pocket.

After soup and bubbles, the girls got into Lily's bed, and Miss Rosa turned out the light.

Olivia's chin began to go. The dark was the wrong shape. A heater ticked, and it wasn't her heater.

"Lily. Your dark is different."

"I know," said Lily. "Look up."

On Lily's ceiling were glow stars, pale green, stuck on crooked.

"Dat one's a duck," said Lily. "Dat one's my mama. Dat one's you."

"Which one's me?"

"Da little one. By da duck."

Olivia looked at herself on the ceiling for a long time.

Miss Rosa looked in from the door, once. "All right in here?"

Olivia checked. "All right." Miss Rosa went back down the hall, and Olivia stayed.

The next thing she knew, the window was gray and the house smelled like rolls.

"You fell asleep first," said Lily, surprised.

At breakfast Olivia told Lily all about the night, from the beginning. Lily had been there for all of it. She listened to every word.
