Night 73

Why Do I Have Two Eyes

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

"Why do I got two eyes? One eye sees fine."

Papa scratched his beard. He thought he knew. He wasn't sure he knew.

"I don't know," he said. "Let's find out."

He held up one finger over the kitchen table. "Cover one eye. Now grab my finger, quick."

Olivia covered one eye and grabbed. She got his nose.

"Dat's your nose."

"I noticed," said Papa.

She tried again and grabbed the air right next to his finger. Then she tried with both eyes open, and caught the finger on the first go.

"Two eyes catch it!"

"Two eyes help you tell how far away things are," said Papa. "One eye makes it harder."

"Why?"

"Each eye sees from its own spot. Here's a trick."

He held his thumb up. "Look at my thumb. Close one eye. Now switch."

Olivia closed one eye. Then the other.

"It jumped!"

"It didn't move," said Papa. "Your eyes are just in two different places."

She made the thumb jump over the salt shaker. She made it jump onto the salt shaker, and off again, and over Papa's coffee cup, which he moved, just in case.

She practiced right up until the bath.
