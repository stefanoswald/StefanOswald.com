Night 71

Why Does Rosa Give Us Extra

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Saturday at the bakery. The bell over the door jingled, and the whole shop smelled like warm sugar. Miss Rosa had flour on one cheek, the way she always does.

She slid a cookie across the glass case. "Last one," she said.

There were two full trays behind her. There always are.

Olivia held the cookie in both hands. "Why do you give us extra?"

"Well, nobody buys the broken ones," said Rosa. "And I like your face when you get one."

Olivia checked. It was a little broken, on one edge.

Mama didn't have a cookie. Mama was busy counting coins for the bread.

Olivia broke the cookie in two. It didn't break fair. There was a big piece and a little piece, and the big piece had most of the raisins. She looked at it hard.

Then she held the big piece up to Mama.

Mama stopped counting. "That was your cookie," she said. "You gave me the big half."

Her face went soft all over, the exact face Rosa likes.

"Thank you," said Mama, and she ate it very slowly, to make it last.

Rosa wiped the counter and said nothing at all.

The bell rang them out the door.
