Night 67

Why Does It Get Dark

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

"Why does it get dark?"

The kitchen was full of evening sun, so Papa pulled the blinds. He got an orange, a flashlight, and a star sticker.

He stuck the star on the orange. "That's us. Willowbrook." He shone the flashlight on it. "Daytime."

He turned the orange slowly, and the star rode around into the shadow.

"Night," said Papa. "The sun stays put. We turn away from it."

"We're turning? Right now?"

"Right now," said Papa. "Hundreds of miles an hour!"

Olivia grabbed the table with both hands. "Are we gonna fall off?"

Papa put the orange down and crouched by her chair. "I made that sound scary. I'm sorry."

He waited until she looked at him. "It's smoother than the car. Nobody falls off, not even the cat."

The cat, under the table, stayed on.

Olivia let go with one hand. Then the other.

The flashlight spilled a circle of light onto Theo's tray. He slapped it. He grabbed it. He opened his fist. Nothing.

"Fee-oh is catching da day."

Papa turned the orange again. Day. Night. Day. Olivia rode her finger along behind the star.

"Somebody's having breakfast now?"

"On the far side," said Papa.

Then he peeled the orange for Olivia, and Theo got soft pear, cut small.

Willowbrook ended up in the compost.
