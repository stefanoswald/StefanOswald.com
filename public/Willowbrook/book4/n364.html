Night 364

The Year of Three

Month 12: Almost Four

Olivia wasn't sleepy yet. She held Bobby Bear up to face her.

"I'm telling you da whole year. Of three."

Mama and Papa sat on the end of the bed.

"First da meadow. Da udder side of da willow. We drew it on da wall. And da gold gate, far away."

"And who walked there?" said Papa.

"You did," she told the bear. "You walked and talked. And Fee-oh's cow said ding."

She told him about the whys, all of them, and Oliver Owl answering with questions. She told him about the storm nights, and the brave list on the kitchen wall.

"Fee-oh said uh-oh. Den he walked to me. Three steps."

"School. Miss Dana. My cubby by Lily. Den da Maybe jar. Da class jar. Da whole town is a jar."

She told him about the fern and the jobs, and the helping nobody asked for.

"Da deep end. I went past da rope."

"Scared, plus one step," said Papa.

"My library card. My own book." She got quieter. "And Bella in da green room, all winter. Quiet, but still going."

"And this month?" said Mama.

"Da chain. Da party. Da tree said growing." She yawned. "Even doh da doorframe didn't."

She lay down with the bear under her chin.

Papa turned the lamp down.
