Night 87

Why Do Stars Twinkle

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

A warm night at the end of May, way past bedtime, just this once. Papa and Olivia lay on the old blanket in the yard, and the stars came out one at a time.

"Why do da stars twinkle?"

"They don't, really," said Papa. "The air between us and them wiggles, and it wiggles their light."

"Da air is wiggling?"

"All the time, way up high. You can't feel it down here."

Olivia wiggled, to help.

"Why does dat one twinkle more?"

She meant the small gold one, low over the lane, the one with the sleepy face.

Papa looked at it for a while. "I'm not sure about that one," he said.

Olivia waved at it.

The small gold star twinkled. Once.

Papa looked at Olivia. Olivia looked at Papa.

"Did you see dat?"

"I saw something," said Papa carefully.

Olivia lay very still on the blanket. The grass was cool under her hands.

"Stella, is it you?" she whispered.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.
