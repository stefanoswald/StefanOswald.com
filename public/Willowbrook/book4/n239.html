Night 239

The Leaf Crew Rides Again

Month 8: Helping Hands

The first frost came in the night. In the morning the grass was white and crunchy, and every leaf on the sidewalk had a silver edge.

The crew met at Mateo's porch: Mateo, Lily, Olivia, and this time June too. Theo came because Olivia came. Papa and Rosa came too.

"Where to?" said Mateo. He had his tool belt on over his coat.

"Wren's grandma's," said Olivia. "Her steps got leaves. Wet leaves are slippy for canes."

So they raked Wren's grandmother's steps and her front path. The frosty leaves crunched. Lily raked in circles, as usual. June held the bag open. Mateo made a leaf scooper out of a box lid and string, and it worked, mostly.

Theo squatted on the sidewalk and picked up one leaf at a time. He carried each one to the bag and put it in. Then he took it out again.

By the time the steps were clear, the sun had come round the house and the frost on the path was gone.

Wren's grandmother opened her door in her slippers and looked at her clean steps. She put her hand flat on her chest.

"Now I can go to the market," she said, "and my knees won't worry."

Only the roof's shady side stayed white.
