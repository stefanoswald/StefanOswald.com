Night 47

When Lily Asked About the Meadow

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

At Lily's window seat on Thursday, Olivia told her everything. The meadow. The glowing fish. Baba, walking.

Lily listened with her mouth open. Then she shut it.

"My rabbit doesn't walk," she said.

"Did you look?"

Lily thought. "I never looked."

Her face had gone small. Olivia scooted closer on the window seat until their knees touched.

Then Lily sat up straight, because Lily always has a plan, and her plans always have three steps.

"Da Meadow Plan," she said. "One, we go to sleep. Two, we find da meadow. Three, pancakes."

"Why pancakes?"

"For after."

On Friday, Miss Rosa walked Lily over first thing, with flour on one cheek and a plate under a towel. Lily ran in.

"I found da meadow! Da fish glowed! And my rabbit hopped in front. Like he knew da way."

"Did you see me?" said Olivia.

Lily stopped. "No. Did you see me?"

"No."

They looked at each other. Step two had not gone right.

"Your meadow's too big," said Lily.

Olivia took her to the kitchen wall and put a finger on the map, right on the willow. "Next time, meet here."

Lily put her finger next to Olivia's.

Under the towel on Miss Rosa's plate were pancakes. Step three went fine.
