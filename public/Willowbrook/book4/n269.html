Night 269

The Big Kid Pool Jump

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

On Saturday it was just Papa and Olivia at the indoor pool. Papa left his glasses in the locker.

"Without them I can't see a thing," said Papa, from the water. "Is that you, or a duck?"

"It's ME."

"Hello, duck."

Big kids were jumping in from the edge, feet first, with a splash. Olivia wanted to jump too. Papa stood in the water right below the edge with his arms out.

Olivia stood at the edge. Her toes curled over it. The water looked a long way down.

She stepped back.

She came to the edge again, and stepped back again.

"My feet won't," she said.

"That's all right," said Papa. "Feet get a say."

So she sat down on the edge instead, with her legs in the water. That was better.

"Can I do a sitting jump?"

"There's no such thing," said Papa. "Let's invent one."

Olivia put her arms out. She pushed off with her hands and slid in, SPLOOSH, right into Papa's arms. Water went up her nose. She came up blinking and laughing.

"Again!"

She did sitting jumps until the whistle blew for the end of swim time. The standing jump stayed up on the edge, for another Saturday.

Papa still couldn't see a thing. He followed her wet footprints to the locker.
