Night 27

Bobby Bear in the Dream

Month 1: Pretend!

At breakfast, Olivia had news. She told it slowly, with her spoon in the air.

"Mama. Baba was in my dream. He was walking."

Mama put down her cup. She was thinking of the birthday letter. I am going to walk, it said. Watch for me.

"Walking?"

"On his own feet. And he talked."

In the dream there was a meadow, with the moon over it and the willow on its hill glowing gold. Baba walked beside her through the long grass, floppy ear and all. The patchwork heart on his foot shone in the moonlight.

"What did he say?" Mama asked.

Olivia counted on her fingers. "He's been practicing. He knows da meadow. He knows da willow."

"Anything else?"

"He can only walk in dreams. For now."

"For now," said Mama.

"And I hafta dream lots. So he gets walks."

That night at bedtime, Olivia held Baba up and looked at him hard. His glass eyes. His floppy ear. He did not move at all.

"I know it's you," she whispered. "See you in dere."

She tucked him under her chin, floppy ear on top.
