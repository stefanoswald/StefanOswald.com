Night 90

Oliver Owl's Favorite Question

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

After the whole town had gone to bed, Stella came down low over the Great Willow.

Oliver Owl was on his branch with his notebook shut.

"All month the little one asked why," said Stella. "Which why did you like best?"

The owl thought for a long time. He only ever answers with questions.

"Whoo could choose?" he said. "The one about the toast? The one about the wind?"

The lilacs down the hill smelled sweet in the dark. On Maple Lane one window was still lit, and then it wasn't.

"Or the one about the moon's face?" said the owl.

They both looked up.

The Sleepy Moon's eyes were closed, the way they always are. The face was right where it always is.

"Does the Moon know it has a face?" asked the owl.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
