Night 81

Why Do Dogs Wag

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

The gold dog lives behind the white fence at the corner of Maple Lane. He has known Olivia since she rode by in a stroller.

Today, when they came around the corner, he barked. One big WOOF.

Olivia stepped behind Mama's legs.

"That woof made you jump," said Mama, and stood still with her.

Olivia peeked out. The dog's tail was going back and forth.

"Why is he wagging?"

"Wagging is how dogs show how they feel," said Mama. "A big loose wag like that means he's glad."

"Glad at me?"

"Glad at you. The woof was hello."

Olivia took one step out from behind Mama. The tail went faster. She took another step, with Mama right beside her. The dog put his nose through the fence and sniffed her sneaker.

"Where's my wag?"

"Your face, mostly," said Mama. "And your bounce."

Olivia bounced. The dog wagged so hard his whole back end went along with it.

Then a bee went by, and he wagged at the bee too.

"He wags at everything," said Olivia.

They walked on. Behind them, the tail kept going.
