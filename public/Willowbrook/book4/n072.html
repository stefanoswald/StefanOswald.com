Night 72

Why Do Flowers Close at Night

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

After supper the evening went on and on, the way May evenings do. Olivia went down the garden path to say goodnight to the tulips and found them already closing.

"Mama! Da tulips are shutting!"

"They close up every evening," said Mama. "They open again when the sun comes back."

"Why do dey shut?"

"The petals keep the middle warm and dry at night," said Mama. "Like pulling up your blanket."

Olivia wanted to see the middle. She pulled one red tulip open with two fingers. Rip. A petal came off in her hand.

She stared at it. "I broke it."

Mama crouched beside her. "You wanted to see inside, and the petal came off." She waited a moment. "It won't go back on."

"Is da tulip sad?"

"Tulips don't get sad," said Mama. "It still has five petals. It can close with those."

They watched it close with five. It was a little lopsided, but it managed.

Olivia held her petal. It was soft as an ear.

"Tomorrow I look when it's open."

"I'll come and get you when the sun is on it," said Mama.

Olivia said goodnight to the other tulips one at a time, without touching.

She carried the petal up to bed.
