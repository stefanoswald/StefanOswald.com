Night 228

The Shelving Volunteer

Month 8: Helping Hands

On Thursdays Olivia went to the Lantern Library with Mama, and now she had a job there.

The librarian let her put the returned picture books back on the low shelf.

"Spines out," said the librarian. "The spine is the back, where the name goes."

"Books have spines?" said Olivia. "Like fishes?"

"Like fish, but flatter," said the librarian.

Olivia put the truck books with the truck books and the animal books with the animal books. Then she held up a book about the moon and did not know where it went.

A small gray nose came out of a gap at the end of the shelf. Then whiskers. Then Molly Mouse, with her acorn-cap satchel.

"Bottom shelf," said Molly, very softly. "By the wall. I read it at night."

Olivia slid the moon book onto the bottom shelf, right by the wall, where a mouse could reach it.

The librarian came by and frowned at it. She thought it had been put back in the wrong place. She moved it up to the top shelf.

When the librarian had gone, Olivia moved it back down.

Molly's tail went back into the wall.
