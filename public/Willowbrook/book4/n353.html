Night 353

The Fourth Maybe Night

Month 12: Almost Four

Mama lit the Maybe night candle and set it high on the windowsill, next to the jar, where Theo could see it and not reach it.

"Tell it," said Olivia.

Papa pulled Theo onto his lap. "Once, when you were a baby, it snowed in the night. In the morning, at breakfast, the cup of milk went over."

"Uh-oh!" said Theo. He didn't know the story. He knew that word.

"Uh-oh is right," said Papa. "You cried, and Mama used her cross voice."

"And den she said sorry," said Olivia.

"Then she said sorry. We mopped, and changed your clothes, and we were late."

Papa stopped there and looked at her.

"Da snowplow came!" said Olivia. "Da big one. With da yellow light. And da man went beep, beep!"

"And he waved at you," said Mama.

"So," said Papa. "Was the milk bad?"

Everybody looked at Olivia. She knew her part.

"Maybe," she said.

Theo clapped, because everybody looked happy.

Outside, the old snow lay gray along the lane. Mama let Olivia blow out the candle.

It took two tries.
