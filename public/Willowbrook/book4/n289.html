Night 289

The Word That Fixed a Day

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Olivia had painted all afternoon. A house, a tree, Lily, and a sun with long yellow arms. It was almost done.

Then her own elbow went across the wet paint.

The sun smeared into the house. Lily turned brown.

Theo saw it first. He pointed at the painting and yelled, "MAMA!" So Mama came. Then Papa came. Then everybody was looking.

Olivia threw the brush and stomped up the stairs.

At bedtime she was still quiet. Mama sat on the bed and waited.

"I'm mad," said Olivia at last. "At my elbow."

"At your own elbow," said Mama. "That's a hard kind of mad, with nobody else to be mad at."

"Yeah."

"That kind has a name. Frustrated."

"Frus-tray-ted." Olivia tried it out.

"And when everybody came to look?"

Olivia pulled the quilt up to her nose. "I didn't like dat. My face got hot."

"That's embarrassed," said Mama. "When you want everyone to stop looking."

Olivia counted on her fingers. "Frustrated. And embarrassed. Two."

"Two at once," said Mama. "No wonder it was so big."

Olivia's shoulders came down.

"Can I paint a new one tomorrow?"

"First thing."

Downstairs, the brown Lily was drying on the table.
