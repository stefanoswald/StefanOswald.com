Night 244

The One-Step Rule

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

The tall slide at Dandelion Park had eleven rungs. Olivia had counted them many times from the bench, and the bench was where she liked them.

"Today?" said Papa.

Olivia looked up. The trees were bare, and the top of the slide was very close to the sky. "My tummy says no."

"What does the rest of you say?"

She checked. "My feet say a little."

Papa crouched down beside her. "Then here's a family rule. Brave is scared plus one step."

"Just one?"

"Just one. Nobody has to do the whole ladder."

Olivia put one boot on the bottom rung. Then the other boot. She held on with both mittens. She was one rung up, and her tummy was still scared, and she stood there anyway.

Then she climbed back down.

"Dat counts?"

"That counts," said Papa.

"But I didn't slide."

"The slide isn't going anywhere. It's bolted down."

At supper she told Mama about the rung. "One step," she said, and held up one finger. Then she ate her peas that way, one at a time, to show how it worked.

It took a long while. Nobody hurried her.
