Night 172

The Buttons Funeral

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

On Tuesday morning Buttons did not come out of his cedar corner. Miss Dana looked in the cage for a long time. Then she sat down on the circle rug with the class.

"I have sad news," she said. "Buttons died in the night."

"Is he sleeping?" asked Marcus.

"No. His body stopped working, and it won't start again."

"Did it hurt?" asked June.

"I don't think so. He was very old, for a hamster."

"Can we get a new one?" asked Sam.

"Not now. Right now we're missing Buttons."

At pickup, the grown-ups came, and everybody went out under the big maple by the fence. Miss Dana dug a small hole. Buttons went in, in a shoebox, with three sunflower seeds. Olivia put dandelions on top.

That night, at the lamp, she asked, "Is Buttons dead?"

"Yes," said Mama. "His body stopped working. It won't start again."

"Is he cold?"

"No. He can't feel cold anymore."

The next night she asked again. "Is Buttons still dead?"

"Yes," said Mama, in the same voice. "His body stopped working. It won't start again."

Olivia held on to Bobby Bear's ear. "I'm sad about Buttons."

"Me too," said Mama.

Mama didn't go anywhere.
