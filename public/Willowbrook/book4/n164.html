Night 164

The Birthday Party Circuit

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Marcus turned four, and the whole class was invited. It was the first party from school, and the first of a great many.

Olivia chose the present herself. "Marcus likes trucks. And loud. Dis truck is loud."

"You thought about what Marcus likes," said Mama.

Marcus opened it at the door. It was very loud. His dad looked at Mama for a long moment.

There was a bounce house on the lawn, a sprinkler, a table of cupcakes, and a dog who ate a paper plate. For a while Olivia bounced with Lily. Then the bounce house got too full, the music got louder, and a party horn blew right by her ear.

She came and found Mama with her hands over her ears. "Too loud. I need a corner."

There was no corner outside, but there was a front step, around the side, away from the music. They sat on it. Mama didn't talk. Olivia leaned on her arm and watched an ant carry a crumb. Lily waved at them through the bounce-house net.

After a while Mama said, "Ready?"

"Not yet."

"Okay."

After another while, Olivia stood up. "Now cake."

She ate it on the step, with Mama.
