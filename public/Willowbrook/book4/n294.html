Night 294

The Catalog Reading at the Farm

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The seed catalog comes to Honeywell Farm in December, when everything outside is asleep.

Grandpa Joe opened it on the kitchen table, and Olivia climbed up beside him.

"I'll read," she said.

She could not read the words, so she read the pictures. "Dis says tomato. Big red. Dis says tomato. Little red. Dis one says yellow tomato?"

Grandpa's finger went under the name. "Yellow. Says so."

"Tomatoes aren't yellow."

"These are."

"I want to check," said Olivia, so Grandpa circled it.

On the sunflower page she stopped. There was a flower in the picture taller than the man standing next to it.

"What does dis one say?"

Grandpa's finger went under the word. It took a while. "Mam. Moth."

"Mammoth?"

"Means big."

"Dey named a flower after a mammoth!"

"Seems they did."

"Dat goes in da word jar."

Grandpa wrote MAMMOTH on the edge of the page, slow and careful, and tore it off for her.

At pickup he told Mama, "She read me the whole tomato section."

Mama raised her eyebrows.

"Pictures count," said Grandpa.

When they had gone, he left the catalog open at the sunflowers.
