Night 155

The First Day

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

They walked the four blocks holding hands. It was hot already, and the sprinklers were ticking on the lawns.

At the classroom door, Olivia stopped.

Mama crouched down. A kiss. A squeeze. Then the last part, the part they had practiced, and Mama stood up.

Olivia looked back once. Then she stepped in.

"Olivia!" Lily was at the next cubby, hanging up her backpack by one strap. "Your hook is dat one!"

It was.

The morning was short, the way first mornings are. At pickup, Olivia came out talking, and she told it in order, all the way home.

"First I hung up my backpack. Den Buttons ate a seed. Den we goed outside. Lily and me dug a hole."

"You went outside and dug a hole?" said Mama. "How deep?"

"Dis deep." Olivia held her hands apart. "And dere's a song. It has everybody's names."

"Everybody's?"

"Mine too."

Mama laughed right out loud on the sidewalk, because there was her girl, in her red backpack, with dirt on both knees.

That night Olivia sang the name song for Bobby Bear. He heard it four times.
