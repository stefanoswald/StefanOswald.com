Night 304

Molly Mouse Reads by Starlight

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The snow stopped after supper, and the sky came out clear.

Molly Mouse came up the hill from the Lantern Library with her acorn-cap satchel. Her feet made a row of tiny prints in the snow, neat as stitches.

Oliver Owl moved over on his branch to make room. "Isn't it late for a mouse?" he asked.

"I brought something to read," said Molly. From her satchel she took a book no bigger than a thumbnail.

"By what light?" asked the owl.

Molly pointed up. Stella was dipping low over the willow, bright and close.

So Molly read, and the owl listened, and down in Willowbrook the windows went dark one at a time. The bakery. The mending shop. The farmhouse, far off.

On Maple Lane, one lamp was still on in a small upstairs window. Then it went out too.

Molly closed her tiny book and tucked it back in her satchel.

"Was that the last one?" asked Oliver Owl.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.
