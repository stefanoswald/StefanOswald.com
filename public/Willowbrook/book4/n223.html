Night 223

Bin Day

Month 8: Helping Hands

The recycling was Olivia's new job. Paper went in the brown bin. Cans and cups went in the blue one. Glass jars were Papa's, because glass can break.

"Where does it go?" asked Olivia, holding up a can.

"A truck takes it to a big building," said Papa. "Machines squash the cans and melt them."

"Den what?"

"Then they make new cans out of them."

Olivia looked at her can. "Dis one comes back?"

"It could come back as a soup can. Or part of a bike."

"How will I know it?"

"You won't," said Papa. "It'll be a whole different can."

So Olivia got a marker and drew a duck on it, so she would know.

"The duck will melt too," said Papa. "But it's a very good duck."

Theo put his shoe in the blue bin. "Uh-oh," he said, pleased with himself. Papa fished it out.

On bin day the truck came up Maple Lane. Its big arm grabbed the blue bin, lifted it high and tipped it over. KA-CHUNK. CRASH. The duck can went in with everything else.

Olivia and Theo watched from the window. The driver waved, and they waved back.

The truck honked twice as it went. Olivia decided that was for the duck.
