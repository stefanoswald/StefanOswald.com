Night 340

Eleven Days

Month 12: Almost Four

The ice on the duck pond had gone gray and soft. Nobody walks on pond ice. The ducks do, but ducks are light.

Olivia and Lily sat on the bench with Miss Rosa's cocoa between them.

"When's your birthday?" said Olivia, though she knew.

"After yours. Eleven days after." Lily kicked at the snow. "You get four first. Not fair."

"I can't help it. It just comes."

Lily folded her arms. "Lily's Plan. Da Waiting Plan. Step one, you stay three. Step two, you wait for me. Step three, and den sprinkles."

Olivia thought about staying three. She did not want to.

Miss Rosa crouched down in front of the bench. "You want to be four together," she said to Lily.

Lily nodded at her mittens.

Olivia watched the pond. Then she said, "You can blow my fourth candle."

Lily looked up. "Da biggest one?"

"Dey're all da same size."

Lily thought about it. "Okay. And you blow one of mine."

Miss Rosa looked at Olivia. "A candle off your own cake," she said. "That's a real thing to give."

A duck sat down on the ice.
