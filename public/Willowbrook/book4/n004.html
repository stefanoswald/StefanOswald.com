Night 4

Tea for Everyone Invisible

Month 1: Pretend!

The tea party had a guest list. The guests you could see were Baba, Duck the doll, and Theo's cow, borrowed off his dresser. The guests you could not see got chairs too.

"Dis chair is for Mrs. Feathers. Dis one, da Balloon Man. And dis one is Franklin's."

Nobody knew who Franklin was. He had come to every party since her birthday, and he took two sugars.

The tea was air, poured from very high up so it made a sound. "Ssssss." The sugar was air too. Plop, plop, for Franklin.

Olivia did all the voices. Mrs. Feathers talked about her eggs. The Balloon Man only said "whoosh." Baba slurped.

"Baba. No slurping."

Then Franklin knocked over his cup.

"Oh no. Franklin spilled."

She got a real napkin and mopped all around his chair. She patted the air where his hand would be.

"It's okay. It's just tea."

The cow's bell rang when she poured him more. That meant thank you.

When the party was over, she put away every cup but one.

"Franklin's still drinking," she said.
