Night 106

Upstairs Alone

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

"I'm gonna play upstairs," said Olivia. "By myself."

Mama was folding towels on the couch. "By yourself. Okay. I'm right down here."

Upstairs is different when nobody else is up there. The family sounds go small and far away, and the house gets louder than the people.

Olivia lasted about eight minutes. Then she came down the stairs, not in a hurry. "I just came for a thing."

"Which thing?"

Olivia looked around the living room. "Dis thing." She picked up a spoon.

"Hello, you," said Mama, and her whole face lit up.

Before Olivia went back up, Mama told her about the house sounds. "If you hear a creak, that's the floor. A tick is the pipes, warming up."

"What if it's a new sound?"

"Then come tell me, and we'll go look."

Up she went again, with the spoon.

The second time, she stayed long enough for Mama to fold every towel and start on the socks. The hall floor creaked. From upstairs came Olivia's voice, very matter-of-fact.

"I know, floor."
