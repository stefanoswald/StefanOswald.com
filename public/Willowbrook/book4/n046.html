Night 46

Papa's Night Rounds

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Baba and the cow walk all the way around the edge of the meadow every night. Olivia told Papa about it at bedtime.

"Dey check everything," she said.

Papa nodded, as if this made perfect sense. "I do that too. Every night, before I go to bed."

"Around da meadow?"

"Around the house. Doors, lights, Theo."

Olivia sat up. "Can I be da deputy?"

So, just this once, the deputy stayed up past bedtime. Papa had the little flashlight. The deputy had Baba under one arm, and rules.

"Deputies talk quiet," she said, very quietly. "Deputies check da cat."

The back door was locked. Papa let her push on it to make sure. The porch light was off. The cat was asleep in the laundry basket, on the clean towels, which is not allowed, but deputies do not wake cats.

Then the yellow room. Papa pushed the door open a crack and pointed the flashlight at the floor, so the light wouldn't wake anybody.

Theo was asleep on his back in his bare crib, with both arms flung up by his ears, like somebody who had just won a race.

Olivia looked at him for a while.

"Checked," she whispered.

Papa walked the deputy back to bed. He left her door open its inch.
