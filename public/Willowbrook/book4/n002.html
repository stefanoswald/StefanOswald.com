Night 2

The Floor Is Water

Month 1: Pretend!

It rained all morning. At ten o'clock came the announcement.

"Da floor is water!"

Just like that, the living room was the sea. The cushions were islands. The couch was the big safe boat, and Mama was on it, folding socks.

"Don't touch da water! Dere's sharks!"

There were sharks. Invisible ones, which are the worst kind.

Olivia jumped from island to island with her arms out. Hop. Wobble. Hop.

Then she saw Theo. He was sitting in the middle of the rug, in the deepest part of the sea, chewing his cow's ear.

"Mama! Fee-oh is in da water!"

Mama looked at the sea. She looked at Theo. "Oh no. What about the sharks?"

Olivia thought hard. Theo slapped the rug with both hands.

"Bababa!" he said.

"He's a fish," Olivia said. "Dat's fish talk."

After that the sharks swam right past him. Olivia brought him a green sock for seaweed, and he chewed it.

At lunchtime, Mama carried the fish over the sea to his high chair. The sharks let her pass.
