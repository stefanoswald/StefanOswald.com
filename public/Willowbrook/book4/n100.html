Night 100

One Hundred Nights

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

It had been one hundred nights since Olivia turned three. Papa had worked it out on the calendar twice, and got a different answer the second time, and Mama said to go with the first.

"A hundred bedtimes," said Mama. "A hundred stories."

"A hundred?" Olivia sat up. "How many is dat?"

"More than all your fingers," said Papa. "More than all our fingers put together."

"I can count it." She held up her hands and started. "One, two, three, four, five, six, seven, eight, nine, ten."

She was out of fingers. She kept going anyway.

"Eleventy, twelvety, a HUNDRED!"

"That was quick," said Papa.

"I skipped da middle."

Mama snorted. Mama never snorts.

Olivia lay back on her pillow and thought about it. A hundred was a lot of stories. It had the storm in it, and the sock monster, and the shadow she could make dance.

"What comes after a hundred?"

"A hundred and one," said Mama. "Tomorrow."

Papa turned the lamp down. Olivia shut her eyes tight.

She wanted to get there faster.
