Night 358

Theo's Gift

Month 12: Almost Four

After the party the house was quiet, and half the streamers had come down on their own. It was the last night of three.

Mama lifted the card down from the top of the fridge and gave it to Theo.

Theo carried it across the kitchen in both hands, very carefully, as if it might spill.

"Livy."

He held it out. Then he pulled it back and looked at it himself, just to be sure.

Olivia waited.

He held it out again. "Here, Livy!"

Olivia took it. She had not seen it three times. She opened her eyes as wide as they would go.

"For ME? Fee-oh! Did you make dis?"

Theo nodded so hard he had to hold on to the table.

The card was purple on the front and purple on the inside and a little purple on the back. In the middle Mama had written LIVY, and Theo had scribbled on that too.

"Purple is his color," Olivia told Mama.

"It is," said Mama. She was looking at Olivia, not at the card.

Olivia stood the card by her plate. Then on the windowsill. Then, at bedtime, on the shelf by her bed.

From her pillow, she could see purple.
