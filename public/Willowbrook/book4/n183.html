Night 183

The Broken Crayon Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Theo came in from the yard with Papa right behind him, his arms up and a yellow leaf in one fist. He did not fall once, which was new.

"Livy!" He held it up to show her. Then he laid it on the table and went back out for more.

Olivia slid a fresh paper over to start a fire truck, right on top of the leaf. She pressed hard on the ladder, and the red crayon snapped in two.

"It broke! My red broke!" She threw the short piece on the floor. "I want it long!"

Mama picked it up before Theo could. "Your fire truck red. You're mad it snapped."

"Very mad."

"Very mad," said Mama, and did not fix anything.

After a long quiet, Mama said, "Maybe."

"Maybe what?"

Mama only shrugged.

Olivia took it back, scowling, and rubbed it sideways across the paper, fast and cross.

Lines came up through the red. A stem. Little branching veins. The whole leaf, right where Theo had put it.

"Mama! Da leaf came through!"

"You turned it on its side," said Mama. "The long one never did that."

Olivia did the leaf again. Then she tried her hand, which did not work, and the bottom of her shoe, which did.

Theo came back in with two more leaves.
