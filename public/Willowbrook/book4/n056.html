Night 56

The Storm on Both Sides

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The first big storm of spring came in the night.

CRACK. Olivia sat straight up with Baba squashed against her chest.

"Papa!"

Papa came to the door, half asleep. "It's just thunder, sweetheart. Go back to sleep." And he went.

BOOM. Olivia pulled the blanket over her head. It did not help at all.

Then the door opened again, and this time Papa came all the way in and sat on the bed.

"Sorry. I said 'just thunder' and walked off." He found her hand under the blanket. "You're scared of it."

Olivia nodded under the blanket.

"Want to look at it with me?"

She came out. They sat on the end of the bed, her on his lap and Baba on hers, and watched the window. The yard lit up white.

"Now we count," said Papa.

"One. Two. Three. Four."

BOOM.

"Four," said Papa. "It's far off, and it's going farther."

The next flash, they counted to six. The next, to nine. Her fists came open. Her head got heavy on his arm.

Papa carried her back to bed.

In the meadow that night there was no storm at all. Baba was lying in the grass, looking up.

She lay down next to him.
