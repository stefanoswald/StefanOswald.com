Night 333

Telling Bella

Month 11: Winter Wonders

That evening, Olivia knelt on the kitchen chair and told Bella about the winter she had missed.

"We made a fort," she said. "Fort Togedder."

"Who got to come in?" said Mama, from the table.

"Me and Lily and June. And whoever's cold. Wren was cold."

Bella opened her wings and closed them.

Olivia went on: the stripey mittens on the tree, the soup for June's baby, the icicles singing high and low. The library under the table, where the cat lives now. Papa's angel, which had a bottom.

"And the big hill?" said Mama.

"I went from da top. I was scared. Papa came on da sled."

"Papa said you held the rope in both mittens," said Mama.

Theo banged his cup. "Melt!"

"Fee-oh's snowman melted," Olivia said. "He was sad. Now its nose is growing."

Then the moon at supper, and the brook running under the ice the whole time, where nobody could see it.

"Remember when you worried she'd come too early?" said Mama.

Olivia looked at Bella, bright on her orange, with the snow outside.

"She did come early. Now I see her every day."

After supper, she drew Bella on her orange slice, and it went in the Maybe jar.
