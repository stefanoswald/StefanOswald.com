Night 53

The Quiet Bell

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The cow's bell went quiet in the dream.

He had wandered out to where the grass stops glowing, and when he shook himself, nothing rang. He turned around and around.

Olivia saw him from the bank. It was too far to run. So she whistled, her thin wet note, as loud as it would go.

The cow's head came up. He walked toward the sound, and the minute he stepped onto the glowing grass, the bell rang again.

"It wasn't broke," said Baba. "He just went too far to hear it."

In the morning, Olivia tried it on Theo.

She hid behind the couch and whistled. On the rug, Theo stopped chewing his cow's ear. He looked around. Then he came, crawling fast, hands slapping the floor, straight to the sound. He found her and shrieked.

She hid behind the big chair and whistled. Here he came again.

She hid behind the curtain and whistled. Theo started toward the curtain, stopped at the coffee table, and pulled up on it instead, very pleased.

Olivia came out. Theo let go of the table, sat down with a bump, and lifted both arms up to her.

"I can't lift you," she said. "You're too heavy."

She sat down on the rug and opened her arms.

He crawled into her lap.
