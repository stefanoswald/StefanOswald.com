Night 136

The Hand-Me-Down Ceremony

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Olivia found the old push wagon in the hall closet, behind the vacuum. It was wooden, with a handle, four red wheels, and blocks inside.

"Dat's mine," she said. "From when I was little."

"You pushed it all over this house," said Mama. "Right before you walked by yourself."

Olivia looked at the wagon. Then she looked at Theo, cruising along the couch.

"Can I give it to Fee-oh? Da real way?"

"What's the real way?"

Olivia showed her. First she wiped the wagon with a dish towel. Then she put the blocks back in, in a row. Then she stood up very straight, like somebody at a wedding.

"Fee-oh. Dis was my wagon. It's fast on da kitchen floor. Da back wheel squeaks. Now it's yours."

Theo let go of the couch and took hold of the handle. The wagon rolled. Theo went with it, holding on, one step and then another, all the way across the rug, with his mouth open.

He bumped gently into the armchair and stopped.

"He's going!" said Olivia.

"He's going," said Mama.

Olivia ran to turn the wagon around for him. Then she looked over at her tricycle by the door.

"Da trike is next," she said. "When his legs are long."

The back wheel squeaked all afternoon.
