Night 97

Theo's First Fear

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Theo was cruising along the couch, one hand over the other, when Mama switched on the vacuum.

It roared. Theo let go of the couch and sat down hard. His face went very still, and then it crumpled.

"Mama!" The whole word, for the right person.

Mama switched the vacuum off. She got down on the rug with her calm face and opened her arms, and Theo crawled into them fast.

"That was loud," she said. "It scared you."

"Why is he crying?" asked Olivia.

"He doesn't know what it is yet. He thinks it might be something big."

Theo looked up at Mama's face. It was her same face. His breathing slowed down.

Olivia came close. "Fee-oh, it's just da vacuum." She knew about loud things. "I was scared of da flush."

"You were," said Mama. "Now you flush it yourself."

Olivia patted the vacuum, which was off. "It only eats crumbs."

Theo watched her hand. He did not pat it. He put his face in Mama's cardigan instead.

"Not today," said Mama. "That's fine."

After that, Mama vacuumed at nap time with the door shut. Olivia named the vacuum Crumby, and every morning she told Theo it was sleeping in the closet, in case he was wondering.
