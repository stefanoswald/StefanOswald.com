Night 273

Frost on the Willow

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In November the dark comes early, and so does the frost. That night it came down before anyone in Willowbrook was asleep.

It whitened the roofs on Maple Lane. It found the board ramp on the back walk, and the mailbox with its red flag, and the lion on Mrs. Henderson's door. Out at the farm it found the henhouse, where the Duchess slept with her head under her wing.

On the hill, the Great Willow had let go of nearly all its leaves. Oliver Owl sat on a low branch and fluffed himself round.

Stella dipped down beside him. "Oliver. The first time you flew, were you scared?"

The owl blinked behind his spectacles. "Have you seen how far down the ground is, from a willow branch?"

"And?"

"How long do you think I sat there, deciding?"

"A minute?"

"Would you believe a whole night?"

Stella didn't ask anything else. She stayed close to him, glowing a little.

Down on Maple Lane the porch lights went out, one, then another, then the last one.

The frost went on settling, very quietly, on everything.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
