Night 152

The Backpack

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Summer was nearly over, and preschool was twelve sleeps away. Theo stayed home with Papa, so the trip to the shop on Maple Lane was just Mama and Olivia.

Olivia had a list for the backpack. She said it at the shop door.

"It has to be red. And Bobby Bear fits."

There was a blue one with a shark on it. There was a green one that lit up when you stomped. There was a small red one with a pocket on the front.

Bobby Bear went in headfirst. His flopped ear stuck out of the zipper.

"He fits," said Olivia. "Dis one."

She wore it out of the shop. She wore it past Mrs. Henderson's porch, and Mrs. Henderson said it was a very serious backpack. She wore it at dinner, sitting on the front edge of her chair so it would fit.

At bath time, there was a talk.

"Backpacks don't go in the bath," said Mama. "It can wait on the stool where you can see it."

Olivia thought about it. "Da stool. Close to da tub."

After the bath it went by her bedroom door. When the lamp went off, it was not empty.

The cat had moved in.
