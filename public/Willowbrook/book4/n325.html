Night 325

The Seed Order

Month 11: Winter Wonders

The seed catalog had been on Grandpa Joe's kitchen table since the first snow. It was soft from being looked at.

Today it turned into an order.

Grandpa set out the form and the good pencil, and pulled out Olivia's chair.

"I get to pick?"

He nodded.

She turned the pages. "Dose tomatoes." He wrote. "Dose beans. Da short fat carrots." He wrote. Then she came to the sunflowers, taller than the man standing next to them in the picture.

"Grandpa. Can we grow dose?"

Grandpa looked out the window at the white field for a while. Then he looked back at the page.

"Yep."

He wrote it in. Then he turned the form around and tapped the line at the bottom. Olivia wrote her O, big, and then the rest of her name, slower.

The envelope got a stamp, pressed on hard with her thumb.

They walked it down the farm lane to the mailbox, Olivia in front and Grandpa behind in his big boots, the fields wide and white on both sides.

"When do da sunflowers come?"

Grandpa put the envelope in the box. He pointed at the snow.

"There," he said. "Summer."

Olivia looked at the field. Then Grandpa let her put the flag up.
