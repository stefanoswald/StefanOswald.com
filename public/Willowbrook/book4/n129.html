Night 129

The Nap Gap

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Every afternoon, Theo went down for his nap, and the house went hush.

Olivia hardly napped anymore. She had rest time on her bed with Bobby Bear and her books. Then she came down the stairs on her bottom, one step at a time, as quiet as a sock.

This was the nap gap. It was hers.

"Just us?" she said, in her nap voice.

"Just us," said Mama, in hers.

They got out the puzzle with the small pieces. That puzzle only came out at nap time, because Theo put everything small in his mouth.

Olivia found all the edges. Mama found the piece with the cow's nose on it and let Olivia put it in.

"Tell me da just-me time."

So Mama told her. Before Theo, Olivia was the only baby in the house for more than two whole years, and Mama and Papa took turns just looking at her.

"What did I do?"

"Mostly you slept on Papa," said Mama. "He didn't move for hours."

Then a sound came from the monitor on the counter. "Dah."

The nap gap was over. Olivia put the small pieces back in the box, every one, and checked the floor for strays.

The box went up on the high shelf.
