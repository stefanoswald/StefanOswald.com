Night 117

Tucking In the Dark

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Bedtime in June is a strange thing. You go to bed, and the day is still out there without you.

"It's not even dark," said Olivia at the window. "Da dark is late."

"It will come," said Mama. "Mostly after you're asleep."

Olivia thought about the dark coming in by itself, with everyone asleep and nobody to say hello to it.

"I'm gonna say night-night to it. Before."

She put her hands on the windowsill and looked out at the pale sky.

"Night-night, dark," she said. "Come quiet. Don't wake Fee-oh."

Mama didn't say anything. She stood by the curtain and let Olivia finish.

"Da fireflies can stay up," Olivia told the dark. "And you rest. Like da day."

Then she let Mama draw the curtain. She got into bed and looked for the little gold light. It was on. The door was open one finger.

The dark came later, very quietly.
