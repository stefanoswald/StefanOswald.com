Night 138

Up

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Theo had a new word, and it was "up."

He said it to Mama at the sink. "Up." He said it to Papa at the door. "Up!" He said it to the cat, who did not pick him up and never would.

Then he said it to Olivia. He stood holding on to her knees and lifted both arms. "Up."

Olivia got her arms around his middle and pulled. Theo's feet came off the floor. Olivia's face went red.

Papa was there in two steps. "He's too heavy for you to lift. Picking him up is a grown-up job."

"But he said up. To me."

"I know," said Papa. "He wants you."

Olivia set Theo down. He sat on the rug, looked at her, and said "Up" again, very sadly.

Olivia thought. Then she sat on the rug, crossed her legs, and opened her arms.

"Dis kind of up."

Theo climbed in. There was a knee in her tummy and a hand in her hair. Then he turned around and sat in her lap like it was his chair.

"Up," said Theo.

From the couch, Papa said, "Anybody else need an up?"

"Me," said Olivia.

Papa carried the whole pile to the bath.
