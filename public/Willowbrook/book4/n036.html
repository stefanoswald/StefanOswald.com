Night 36

The Cow Walks

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

"Fee-oh. Your cow walked."

Olivia said it the minute she sat down to lunch. Theo was in his high chair with the straps done up, working hard on a pea.

"In my dream," she said. "He came to da meadow. On four feet. His bell went ding. Every step, ding."

Mama had squashed the peas flat. Theo chased one around the tray with his fingers. On the fourth try he got it between his finger and his thumb, slowly, the way you would pick up something small and important. He got it all the way to his mouth.

"Den he walked in da brook." Olivia giggled. "Cows don't mind wet feet."

"What did Baba do?" said Mama.

"He said, 'Dat's a cow.' Like I didn't know."

Theo picked up another pea.

"Fee-oh. He's YOUR cow. Aren't you happy?"

Theo ate the pea. Then he looked in the bowl for more.

"I think he's happy about the peas," said Mama.

Olivia watched him. "He's happy about both." She watched a little longer. "Mostly peas."

After lunch she went and told the cow instead.
