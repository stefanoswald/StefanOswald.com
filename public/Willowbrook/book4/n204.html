Night 204

The Sing Moves Inside

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

The September sing was going to be outside, under the big maple, with families on blankets in the grass. Then it was the hottest day of the whole month, far too hot for sitting in the sun at noon. Miss Dana moved everybody inside, to the gym at the community hall.

"Da blankets were da best part," said Olivia.

"I wanted da GRASS," said Lily.

Miss Dana got down on her knees in front of the line. "You wanted the blankets and the grass. Me too." She stayed down there while they grumbled.

Lily kicked the floor. Olivia took her hand. "Maybe," she told her.

The gym was big and bare, with a shiny floor and high walls. The families sat on folding chairs. Mama waved from the third row.

Miss Dana counted them in. On the first note, something happened. The walls threw the song right back at them, and twelve small voices came back sounding like a hundred.

Olivia's eyes went wide. She sang louder. The walls sang louder too.

After, she ran to Mama. "Why were we so LOUD?"

"Hard walls bounce the sound back," said Mama. "So you hear yourselves twice."

Lily hummed the song all the way to the car.
