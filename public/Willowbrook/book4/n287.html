Night 287

Unfortunately

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The pig in the hat book had a big word in it. Papa read it in his serious voice.

"Unfortunately, the hat blew away."

Olivia sat up. "Say dat one again."

"Unfortunately."

"Un. For. Chu. Nit. Lee." She said it slowly, to taste it. "What does it mean?"

"It means too bad," said Papa. "Something went wrong."

After that, a lot of things went wrong.

At breakfast: "Unfortunately, I don't like dese eggs."

At the door: "Unfortunately, my boot is on da wrong foot." It was.

At the park she told Mateo, "Unfortunately, you are on my swing."

When Theo dropped his cup over the side of the high chair, she said it for him. "Unfortunately."

Theo looked over the edge at the cup. "Uh-oh," he said, which is the short way.

On Friday, Mama said, "Bath time."

Olivia folded her arms. "Unfortunately, I am a pig in a hat."

"Unfortunately," said Mama, "pigs in hats take baths too."

The pig took its bath in a hat.

It was Papa's hat.
