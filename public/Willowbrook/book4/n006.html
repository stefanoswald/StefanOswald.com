Night 6

Theo the Dog

Month 1: Pretend!

For weeks, Theo had been getting up on his hands and knees. He rocked forward. He rocked back. Then he sat down again, surprised.

"He's a dog," Olivia said. "He got four feet."

So Theo was the dog.

"Doggy, sit!"

Theo sat. He had been going to sit anyway.

"He sitted! Mama, he sitted!"

"He sat right down," Mama said, from the floor by the couch.

Olivia took his cow to the far edge of the rug and shook it. The bell went ding-a-ling.

"Doggy, come!"

Theo looked at the cow. He rocked. Forward, back, forward. Then one hand went out, and one knee came after it. The other hand. The other knee.

Mama put both hands over her mouth.

Theo crawled four crawls, flopped on his belly, and got the cow by the ear.

"He came to me! Mama, he came!"

"He did," Mama said. "He wanted that cow, so he went and got it."

Theo chewed the cow's ear and looked at them both. "Li-li-li," he said.

"He said Livy," Olivia said. "To me."

It sounded like Livy to Mama too.

Olivia got down on all fours too. She barked. The dog laughed.
