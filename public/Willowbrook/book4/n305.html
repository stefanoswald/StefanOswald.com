Night 305

Where Did Bella Go?

Month 11: Winter Wonders

The new year came in white. Olivia stood at the kitchen window in her socks and looked for the garden. It was under there somewhere.

"Where did da flowers go?"

"Under the snow, resting," said Mama. "Roots and all."

"And da bees?"

"In their hive. They huddle up to keep warm."

Olivia breathed a cloud on the glass. "And Bella?"

"Lots of butterflies spend the winter inside a little case called a chrysalis," said Mama. "I don't know where Bella's is."

Watering the parsley was Olivia's job. The pot had come in from the porch before the frost, and it lived on the windowsill beside the Maybe jar. She knelt up on the chair with her little can, and stopped.

Under one stem hung a thing that was not a leaf. It was green, as long as her thumb, and held on by a thread.

"Mama. Dis leaf is wrong."

Mama looked. Then she looked closer.

"Oh," she said. "That's a chrysalis."

"Is Bella in dere?"

"She might be."

Olivia held still, the way you do near a sleeping baby.

"Dat's her room," she said. "Her green room."

She watered the parsley slowly, around the edges.
