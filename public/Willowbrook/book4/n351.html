Night 351

The Photo Album Night

Month 12: Almost Four

The albums came out after supper, and everybody got up on the big bed. Theo got up on it four times, because he kept getting down.

Mama turned the pages. There was Olivia, very small and very bald, asleep on Papa's chest.

Theo leaned over and put his finger on the baby. "Me!"

"Dat's ME," said Olivia. "Not you. Me."

"Me!" said Theo.

"He thinks every baby is him," Olivia told Papa.

Mama turned more pages. Olivia with a duck. Olivia and Lily, both bald, holding hands on a blanket. Olivia sitting in a bucket.

"Why was I in a bucket?"

"You climbed in," said Papa. "It took us a while to get you out."

Then came a picture of the yellow room, and a very new baby, and a small girl with curls on tiptoe to look at him.

Theo studied it. He put his finger on the girl.

"Livy."

He moved his finger to the baby. "Baby."

Then he turned back to the first picture, the bald one on Papa's chest, and patted it.

"Livy baby."

Everybody stopped.

"Two words," said Mama.

"He said two!" said Olivia. "Fee-oh, say it again!"

Theo shut the album and lay down on it.
