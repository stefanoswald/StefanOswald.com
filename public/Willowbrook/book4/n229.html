Night 229

The Flyer Crew

Month 8: Helping Hands

The Hendersons' orange cat had not come home for two nights.

Mrs. Henderson walked up and down the lane calling, in a voice that wobbled.

Olivia and Lily had an idea. Mama was on the phone, and the kettle was whistling.

"Mama! I got an idea!"

"Not now, Olivia!" It came out sharp.

Olivia went quiet and sat on the bottom stair.

Mama finished the call and came to sit on the stair too. "I said that too sharp. I'm sorry."

She waited.

"What was your idea?"

"Pictures for kids," said Olivia. "Down low. Kids look low."

So they made flyers. Olivia drew the cat. It looked like a pumpkin with whiskers. Mama wrote LOST CAT underneath.

"Lost means statue, shout, helper," said Lily. "Does da cat know?"

"Cats don't know it," said Mama.

They taped the flyers low on the lampposts, where kids look.

On the way back past the Hendersons' shed, Lily stopped. "Listen."

From inside came a small, cross mew.

Mrs. Henderson opened the shed door. The orange cat walked out covered in dust and looked at everybody as if it were their fault.

"I looked everywhere," said Mrs. Henderson, "except my own shed."

"He did da shout part," said Olivia.

By supper the cat was back on his wall. The flyers stayed up all week anyway.
