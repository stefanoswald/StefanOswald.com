Night 261

Theo's First List Entry

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Between the kitchen and the back door there was one low step. Everybody stepped over it without looking, except Theo.

All week Theo had tried to climb it. He got his hands on top and one knee up, and then he slid back down on his tummy.

"I help," said Olivia, and pushed on his bottom.

Theo roared.

"Sorry, Fee-oh." Olivia took her hands back. "Try one knee. Dat's a step."

Theo put one knee up and his hands flat. He pushed and wobbled, and the other knee came up too. Then he stood up on the step, all by himself, arms in the air.

"Up!" said Theo.

"Mama! He did da step!"

Mama crouched close. "You climbed it yourself," she said to Theo. "Down goes backward, tummy first."

Theo went down backward, tummy first. Then he climbed it again.

"He needs his own list," said Olivia. "He's bigger now."

So Olivia drew a step on the brave list, next to the tiny vacuum she had drawn for him in June. Mama wrote, Theo. The low step. Up by himself.

Olivia gave Theo the purple crayon. He made his mark across the whole corner.

He went up and down the step until supper. The cat moved to a different doorway.
