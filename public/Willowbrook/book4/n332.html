Night 332

The Morning Bella Came Back

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Olivia came down in her pajamas and went to the windowsill before anything else, as she did every morning.

She screamed, the good kind.

"She's OUT! Everybody! She's out!"

They came running, Theo in Papa's arms.

The green room had split open at the bottom, and hanging from it by her feet was Bella. Her wings were wet and crumpled, like paper that had been in a pocket.

"Her wings are all crumply," said Olivia. "Is she broken?"

"Wait," said Mama. "Watch."

So they watched. Slowly, so slowly you only saw it if you looked away and looked back, the wings began to fill. The crumples smoothed out. The colors came out one at a time in the window sun. Nobody made breakfast.

"It's her," said Olivia. "It's Bella. She kept da Bella-est parts."

Much later, when her wings were dry, Bella let go and fluttered to the glass. Outside, the snow sparkled.

"She thinks it's spring," said Olivia.

"The warm kitchen told her so," said Papa.

"Bug!" said Theo.

"Not a bug, Fee-oh. Bella."

"Bug," said Theo, sure of it.

Olivia set an orange slice on the saucer, just like the plan. Soon Bella walked onto it. Her wings opened and closed in the sun, slow as breathing.
