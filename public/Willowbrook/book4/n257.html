Night 257

The Gold Dog

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Every morning on the way to preschool, Mateo stopped at the gold dog's fence. The gold dog pushed his nose over the top, and Mateo scratched his ears.

Olivia stood back on the sidewalk and watched.

"He likes everybody," said Mateo.

"He's big," said Olivia.

"He's big and he likes everybody."

On Thursday the dog's person was out too, a lady in a long green coat.

Olivia remembered the rule from the summer. She stopped her feet. She put her hands in her pockets. Then she asked it herself. "Can I say hi to your dog?"

"He'd like that," said the lady. "Let him sniff the back of your hand first."

Mama stood right behind her. Olivia held one hand up, knuckles first, the way the lady showed her.

The gold dog sniffed it. His nose was cold and wet.

"He's sniffing my breakfast," said Olivia. "It was toast."

Then the gold dog licked her knuckle, once.

Olivia pulled her hand back and looked at it. Then she laughed and held it out again.

"One pat," she said, and patted his head with two fingers. It was warmer than she'd thought.

All the way to preschool, she kept that hand out of her pocket.
