Night 218

Supper for June's House

Month 8: Helping Hands

June's mama had a baby, a girl, very small, born in the night.

"Can we see her?" asked Olivia.

"When she's bigger," said Mama. "Today we can bring supper."

"Why supper?"

"When a baby comes, no one has time to cook. So the neighbors do."

Mama made a pan of macaroni and wrapped it in a towel. Olivia pulled it in the wagon. Theo walked beside it holding Mama's hand, and stopped to squat for every red leaf.

On June's porch Mama said the rules quietly. "Knock soft, once. Then step back."

Olivia knocked soft, once. She stepped back so far she was on the sidewalk.

June opened the door. Her hair was not brushed, and she had one sock on.

"She cries all da time," said June. "Like a goat."

Olivia thought about that. "Fee-oh cried like a kettle."

"Did he stop?"

"Mostly. Den he got fun."

June looked at Theo, who was holding up a leaf for her. She took it.

Mama handed the pan in at the door. "Nothing to do but eat it," she said. "Send the pan back whenever."

Inside, something small started crying like a goat.

June almost smiled. Then she went in to help.
