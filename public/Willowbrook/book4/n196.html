Night 196

Two Friends, One Thursday

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Mama said yes to Lily for Thursday. Then, at the park, she said yes to June's mama for Thursday too. She found out at supper, looking at the calendar.

"Oops," said Mama.

Olivia put her fork down. "But Lily plays loud. June plays quiet."

"You're worried they won't like the same game," said Mama.

Olivia nodded. She was very worried. Mama put an arm around her and let her be worried.

"Maybe," said Mama.

On Thursday Rosa dropped Lily off with flour on one cheek, and June's mama brought June. For a while nobody played anything. Lily stood on the couch. June stood by the door. Olivia stood in the middle, which was the worst place.

Then Lily held up one finger. "Plan Island. Step one, da floor is water. Step two, everybody gets a boat. Step three, muffins."

June looked at the floor. "I can sell boats."

So June ran the boat shop, which was the footstool, and sold pillow boats for three pretend shells each. Everybody needed one, because the floor was water. When Lily and Olivia both wanted to be the captain, Mama said, "Whose turn?" and they worked it out.

At five o'clock June's mama knocked. All three girls were on the couch, the only dry land.

Nobody wanted to swim to the door.
