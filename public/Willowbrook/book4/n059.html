Night 59

The Willow's Other Side

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

After the April rain, the Great Willow glowed gold at the tips, and Stella came down low to look at it.

"The children found the other side," she said.

Oliver Owl blinked his slow blink. "Whoo told them?"

"The bear did. They drew it on their kitchen wall. In crayon."

"Did they draw the gate?"

"Not yet," said Stella.

The Sleepy Moon said nothing. The Moon had always known about the other side.

Below them, Willowbrook was going to sleep. The rain had stopped. The tulips on Maple Lane had closed up for the night. In the yellow room a baby slept with his arms up, and in the next room a girl slept with a bear. Somewhere on the other side of the willow, the bear was walking.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
