Night 338

Sunflowers on the Sill

Month 12: Almost Four

Grandpa Joe came in stamping the snow off his boots, with a seed packet in his coat pocket.

"Too early," he said, and opened it.

The seeds were striped gray and white, bigger than Olivia expected. Mama had lined up paper cups of dirt along the sunny windowsill.

"Knuckle deep," said Grandpa. He showed her his knuckle.

Olivia pushed a seed down with her thumb, all the way to the knuckle, and pressed the dirt flat on top.

"I digged it to my knuckle."

"Dug it right," said Grandpa. "Now firm it."

She pressed harder. Then harder.

"Not dat firm," she said, before he could.

They went down the whole row, one seed to a cup. Olivia watered each one from the little jug. Some of the water went in the cups. Some went on the floor.

"How big do dey get?"

"Taller than your papa."

Olivia looked over at the doorframe with its pencil lines. Then she looked at Grandpa.

"Taller dan da DOOR?"

"Yep."

Grandpa put the leftover seeds on the top shelf, out of Theo's reach, and went home.

Before bed, Olivia came down to check the cups.

Just dirt.
