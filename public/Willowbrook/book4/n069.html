Night 69

Why Is Theo Little

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Olivia's game was Store. You put food in the basket, pay with paper money, and say thank you.

Theo did not play Store. He chewed the wooden carrot and crawled off with the basket.

"Why is Fee-oh so little? He can't play!"

"He's ten months old," said Mama. "He can't play Store yet."

"Why not yet?"

"He doesn't know what a store is. Come look."

She took them to the kitchen door frame. Olivia's pencil marks climbed the wood. Theo had one low mark, from Christmas.

Theo crawled over, grabbed the frame, and pulled himself up to standing.

Mama laid the pencil flat on his hair and drew a line. Higher than the old one.

Theo reached for the pencil. "No, Theo," said Mama. He stopped and held on.

"He growed!" said Olivia.

"He grew a whole hand," said Mama.

Olivia found her own mark at Theo's height. "Was I dat little?"

"At ten months. You chewed a green crayon."

"I did NOT."

"A little bit," said Mama.

Theo sat down hard. Olivia made Store smaller. She put a wooden banana in the basket and held it out. Theo took the banana out and dropped it.

"Dat's not how you pay."

She put it back. He dropped it again, and laughed.

She picked it up. Again.
