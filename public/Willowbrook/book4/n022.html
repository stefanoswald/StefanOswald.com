Night 22

The Map of Nowhere

Month 1: Pretend!

Olivia drew a map of nowhere. It had a mountain, a lake, a forest of scribbles, and a dotted line wandering through all of it. At the end of the line was an X.

"Dat's da treasure."

"What's the treasure?" Papa asked.

"We hafta go see."

So they followed the map out into the yard, and the yard went along with it. The mountain was the last gray pile of snow. The lake was the big puddle. The scribble forest was the bare lilac bushes, and they went through it bent over so the forest would not poke them.

The dotted line ended in the corner by the fence. They dug with the toy spade in the soft mud.

Papa may have been out in that corner earlier, while Olivia was in the bath. His boots were muddy.

Clink.

The treasure was a small tin. Inside were six buttons and one chocolate coin.

"Da map was true!"

Olivia held up the tin to show the whole yard. She ate the chocolate coin right there, standing on the X.

The tin went on her shelf, next to the leaf from the puddle sea.
