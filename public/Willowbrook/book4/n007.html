Night 7

The Band-Aid Hospital

Month 1: Pretend!

The hospital opened after breakfast. The couch was the ward.

Baba had a broken ear, the floppy one, extra floppy today. Theo's cow had swallowed his bell. Duck had a hot head. The rabbit was sad, and sad counts.

Mama counted out three real band-aids. "These three are for the hospital. The rest are for real hurts."

Doctor Olivia made her rounds with the toy stethoscope. She listened to every tummy, even Baba's.

"You'll be okay. I'm here. Dis will help."

Baba's ear got two band-aids. Duck got a cool washcloth. The cow had an operation with a spoon. Then the doctor got to the rabbit, and there were no band-aids left.

"More band-aids, Mama."

"Those were the three," Mama said. "You wish there were more."

Olivia frowned at the rabbit. The rabbit waited.

"What else do doctors have?" Mama asked.

Olivia thought. She bent down and kissed the rabbit on the nose.

"Da kiss kind."

Last came a new patient. Mama lay down on the end of the couch. "Doctor, I'm very tired."

The doctor listened to Mama's knee. "You need a nap. Lie still."

Mama lay still. It was the best medicine anyone had given her in eight months.

The doctor tucked a dish towel over her and tiptoed off to check on the cow.
