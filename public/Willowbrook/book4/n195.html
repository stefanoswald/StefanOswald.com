Night 195

The Power Outage Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

In the middle of supper, the lights went out. The fridge stopped humming. Out the window, every house on Maple Lane had gone dark at once.

Olivia grabbed Mama's arm. "It's too DARK!"

"It went dark all at once," said Mama. "That's scary." She pulled Olivia onto her lap.

Papa found the camping lantern by feel, and the big flashlight, and clicked them both on. The kitchen came back, yellow and small.

"Can I hold it?" Olivia held the flashlight in both hands and pointed it into every corner. The corners were only corners.

Her breathing slowed down. She leaned back against Mama.

"Maybe," said Papa, "the dark wants a show."

He put his hands in the flashlight beam, and a rabbit hopped across the wall. It wiggled its ears.

Theo stared. Then he walked over to the wall and patted the rabbit.

Olivia made a duck. It looked like a potato. Papa made a dog that ate the potato.

Then Papa sang in the dark, "The wheels on the bus go squish, squish, squish."

"ROUND AND ROUND!"

"Are you sure?"

"PAPA."

At bedtime the lights came back on with a hum. Everybody said, "Aww."

Papa turned them off again, for one more rabbit.
