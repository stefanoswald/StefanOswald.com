Night 39

Come Spring

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Grandpa Joe came on Saturday with a paper bag in his coat pocket. He shook it. It rattled.

"Seeds?" said Olivia.

Grandpa nodded.

They wheeled her wheelbarrow with the star out to the corner of the yard, where Papa had turned over a patch of dirt, dark from all the rain. Grandpa knelt, one knee and then the other. He poked a hole with his finger. He dropped in a pea. He pushed the dirt over it and patted it, once.

Then he looked at her.

Olivia poked a hole. Her whole finger went in. She dropped in a pea and patted it three times, hard.

They did the whole row that way, his one pat and her three.

When it was done she sat back on her heels. "When do dey come up?"

"Couple weeks."

Nothing was happening in the dirt at all.

On Sunday she dug one up to check. It was a pea.

On Monday she dug up the same one. This time it had a crack down the middle and a tiny white tail poking out.

"Mama! Call Grandpa!"

Mama held the phone for her. "Grandpa. It's got a TAIL."

On the other end, Grandpa Joe was quiet for a moment.

"Busy," he said. "Put him back."

She put him back. She patted him once.
