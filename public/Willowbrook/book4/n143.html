Night 143

The Room Question

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

"Can I sleep in Fee-oh's room?" Olivia asked at supper. "Just one night?"

Mama and Papa looked at each other.

"He wakes up early," said Papa. "Very early."

"I don't mind."

"He talks to himself in the night," said Mama.

"I like dat."

So on Friday, Olivia's sleeping mat went on the floor of the yellow room, beside the crib. Theo slept in his crib on his back, the way he always does, with his cow. Olivia slept on the mat with Bobby Bear, and her gold nightlight glowed low by the door.

In the dark, Theo said, "Ba. Ba-ba. Dah."

"Night-night, Fee-oh," said Olivia.

"Dah," said Theo.

They talked like that for a long time.

At five o'clock in the morning, when the window was barely gray, something soft landed on Olivia's head. It was the cow. Theo was standing at the rail, looking down, grinning.

"Fee-oh. It's still night."

Theo pulled off his sock and dropped that on her too.

Mama came to the door in her robe. "Good morning, roommates."

Olivia sat up with a cow on her head and a sock on her pillow.

"I want my own room back," she said. "But again sometimes."

Outside, the sun wasn't up yet either.
