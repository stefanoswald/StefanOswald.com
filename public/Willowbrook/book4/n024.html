Night 24

The Little Brother's First Big Game

Month 1: Pretend!

Olivia needed cargo.

"Papa, I got a truck. I need something to carry."

The truck was a box. The cargo was Theo. Papa set him in the middle of it and knelt alongside, with one hand on the edge.

"Beep beep! Da truck is going!"

Olivia pushed. The truck went across the rug very slowly. Bump. Bump. Theo held a block in one hand and passed it to the other hand, and back again.

The truck stopped at the couch. "Delivery!"

Theo looked up at her. He bounced, both hands on the edge of the box. "Ba!" he said. "Ba!"

"He wants more," Olivia said.

So the truck went again. It stopped at the kitchen. Theo bounced. "Ba!"

It stopped at the bottom of the stairs. "Ba!"

The truck needed a horn, so Papa tried to whistle. What came out was mostly air.

"Not yet," said Papa.

Theo laughed at it. He was the first person who ever had.

The truck kept going until Olivia's arms were tired. Then Papa pushed, and Olivia walked beside the cargo, saying beep beep, softer and softer, all the way to dinner.
