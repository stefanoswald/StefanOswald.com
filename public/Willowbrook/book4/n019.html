Night 19

The Longest Game

Month 1: Pretend!

On Monday the couch became a ship.

"We sail to da faraway island," Olivia said. "It takes six sleeps."

On Tuesday morning the ship was still a ship. "We sailed all night. Now dere's a storm."

On Wednesday the crew fixed the sail. On Thursday they found an island, but it was the wrong one.

Theo was the whale. He crawled around the ship very slowly, blowing bubbles.

Papa was not allowed on the ship at all.

"Ships are for sailors," Olivia said. "Not Papas."

So Papa sat on the floor. Papa sat on the stairs. On Friday he tried the very end of the couch, one small corner of it.

"PAPA. You're in da water."

Papa got up. "It was a very small corner," he said.

On Saturday they reached the right island. The treasure was under a cushion: two raisins and the hair clip Mama had been looking for since February.

On Sunday they sailed home.

"We're back," Olivia said, and climbed down. "It's a couch now."

Papa sat on it slowly, with both hands, like a man getting into a warm bath. He closed his eyes.
