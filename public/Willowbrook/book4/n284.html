Night 284

The Word Jar

Month 10: Words Are Magic

There was a new jar on the kitchen windowsill, next to the Maybe jar.

"Dis one is for words," said Olivia. "Good ones. Words dat taste good to say."

Mama wrote each word on a slip of paper, and Olivia folded it and dropped it in.

"Wobble," said Olivia. That was for Theo, who was wobbling past with a spoon.

Then "crunch," from the snow. Then "lullaby," because it sounded like it was singing itself.

Mama put in "hush."

Papa took a long time to choose. "Petrichor."

"What's dat?"

"The smell when rain lands on a warm sidewalk."

Olivia stared at him. "Dat has a WORD?"

"It does."

"Say it again."

"Petrichor."

"Pet. Ri. Kor." She said it until it stopped sounding like anything, and then a few more times.

"What else has names dat I don't know?"

"Lots," said Papa. "Keep your ears open."

Olivia held her ears open with two fingers, just in case.

Theo came back and dropped his spoon in the jar. Clink.

"Dat's not a word," said Olivia.

"Spoon is a word," said Mama. She wrote it on a slip, to be fair.

The spoon stayed in the jar until supper.
