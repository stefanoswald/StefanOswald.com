Night 240

Nobody Pays Mama

Month 8: Helping Hands

At bedtime Olivia counted her jobs on her fingers. "Fern. Spoons. Socks. Birds." She held up four fingers. "How many coins?"

"No coins," said Mama. "Family jobs aren't for coins."

"Stickers?"

"No stickers either. But I saw the fern standing up straight."

Olivia frowned. "Does Papa get coins for dishes?"

"Nobody pays Papa for dishes. Nobody pays me for socks."

Olivia looked at Mama. "Dat's sad," she said.

She took the acorn from cleanup day off her nightstand and put it in Mama's hand. "For your socks."

Mama held it in her palm. "Thank you," she said.

Olivia lay down. Then she sat back up.

"Can I have it back?"

Mama laughed and gave it back.

Olivia put it on the nightstand, where she could see it.
