Night 192

Teaching Miss Dana's Class the Jar

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

On Wednesday the Maybe jar went to preschool in the red backpack, wrapped in a sweater.

At circle, Olivia held it up. "Dis is our Maybe jar."

The class had heard the word before, on the rain day. But nobody had a jar.

"Tell us one," said Miss Dana.

Olivia told the bus one. "I spilled my milk. We were late. Den da bus waved."

"Should we have a jar here too?" asked Miss Dana.

Twelve hands went up. So did one foot.

The class jar went on the shelf by the easel, and twelve children decorated it at once. It came out mostly glue.

The first maybe arrived before snack. Marcus's block tower fell down, and Marcus kicked the rug and yelled. Miss Dana sat with him until the yelling ran out. Then he built it again, flat this time.

"It's a bridge now," Marcus said.

After snack, Wren tipped the blue paint cup at the easel and went very still. Then Wren pressed a clean paper down on the spill and lifted it up. Blue swirls, all over.

Miss Dana hung it on the wall.

At pickup, Olivia climbed into Mama's arms. "Our jar has a baby jar."

Mama carried her out to the sidewalk, jar and all.
