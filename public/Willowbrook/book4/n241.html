Night 241

The Quiet Thank-You

Month 8: Helping Hands

On a frosty morning near the end of October, the mailbox flag was up, and no one in the house had put it up.

Inside was a small envelope. On the front, in careful, old-fashioned letters, it said OLIVIA.

"Dat's me! Dat's my O!"

In the envelope was a red maple leaf, pressed flat, and a card. Mama read it out loud. "Thank you."

That was all. No name.

"Who sent it?"

"I don't know," said Mama. "Who do you think?"

Olivia thought of Mrs. Henderson, and her leaves, and her cat. She thought of Wren's grandmother and the bag with the lemons. She thought of Mr. Petrov, and the firefighters, and the aunts.

"Not da squirrel," she said.

"No," said Mama. "The squirrel has never said thank you in his life."

Olivia decided it was from all of them.

She put the leaf on the fridge next to the job list, with the duck magnet on top.
