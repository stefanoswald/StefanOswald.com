Night 226

The Broken Banana

Month 8: Helping Hands

Theo wanted the banana whole.

Mama peeled it, and it broke in half.

Theo looked at the two halves. His face went red, then redder. Then he threw himself down on the kitchen floor and roared. He kicked. He arched his back like a bridge. He smacked the floor.

Olivia stood very still, holding her own banana. "Is he broken?"

"No, he's having a big mad," said Mama. "His first real one."

Mama let her own breath out slowly. Then she sat down on the floor near him, not too close.

"The banana broke," she said. "You wanted it whole."

The roar got smaller. It turned into crying. Then Theo rolled over and reached up for Mama with both arms, and she picked him up. He put his hot face in her neck.

Olivia waited until he was quiet. Then she took his hand. "Come, Fee-oh. I show you."

She showed him her cozy corner, the cushion and the soft blanket, the place where mads go after.

"Dis is for after roaring," she said.

Theo sat on the cushion and looked around. Then he got up, went to the kitchen, and came back with the two banana halves. He put them under the blanket.

He patted them twice.
