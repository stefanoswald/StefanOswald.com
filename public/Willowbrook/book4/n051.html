Night 51

The Dream Picnic

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The picnic was Olivia's idea. She spread the blanket on the glowing grass, and Baba brought the food, which was dream food and tasted like warm.

The cow came, ringing. Franklin came, and one corner of the blanket went flat where he sat.

And then, for the first time, Theo came.

He came crawling, fast, across the grass, the way he crawls at home when somebody opens the snack cupboard.

"Fee-oh! You came!"

Theo went straight past her. He went straight past Baba. He went straight after the cow.

The cow trotted off. Ding, ding, ding. Theo crawled faster. The cow went around the willow. So did Theo. The cow went around the willow the other way. So did Theo.

Baba ate a sandwich and watched. "Dat's a fast baby."

"He's very fast now," said Olivia. "We got gates."

At last the cow sat down, puffing, and Theo caught him by the ear and laughed.

Everybody lay back on the blanket after that. The sky over the meadow went pink, then purple. Theo lay between Olivia and the cow with his fists open.

The bell rang slower, and slower.
