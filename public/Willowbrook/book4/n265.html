Night 265

The Duchess

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In November the hens laid fewer eggs. On Sunday Grandpa Joe handed Olivia the egg basket.

The first nest box had one brown egg. The second had two.

The third box had the Duchess in it.

The Duchess sat puffed up, eyes half shut, with eggs somewhere underneath her. Last time Olivia reached under her, the Duchess pecked her thumb.

Olivia put her hands behind her back. "You do dis one."

Grandpa just waited.

"She pecks," said Olivia.

"Yep."

Olivia looked at the Duchess. The Duchess looked back with one orange eye.

Grandpa held out his big hand, flat, and moved it toward the straw. Slow. Slower than slow. Then he took it back.

Olivia put her hand out flat, like his. It shook a little. She moved it so slowly it hardly moved.

The Duchess made a low noise in her throat. Olivia stopped. Then she went on.

Her hand stopped at the straw, and she waited.

The Duchess did not peck. She stood up, and there was an egg, warm as a baked potato.

Olivia held up the egg. "She moved over!"

Grandpa nodded, once. "Partners," he said.

After that the Duchess walked beside Olivia to the house. She did not do that for anybody. Olivia carried the basket with both hands. Then she washed both hands.
