Night 42

The Meadow Tour

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

"Mama. Come on da meadow tour."

Mama was at the stove. "In a minute."

Papa looked at the clock over the sink.

Olivia did not wait. She got the big paper and the crayons and started. A green scribble for the meadow. A blue line for the brook. Yellow dots for the fish that glow. Orange dots for the fireflies. Brown for the willow, right in the middle.

Mama sat down at last. Behind her, Papa held up nine fingers.

"Okay," said Mama. "Tour, please."

Olivia put her finger on the blue line. "Dis is da brook. Da fish glow. Dey're all named Gerald."

"All of them?"

"Baba says." She moved her finger. "Dis is da firefly field. Dey know Baba." She tapped the brown. "Dis is our willow. Da udder side."

"The other side?"

"Trees go all da way through. Baba says."

Mama looked at the brown willow and was quiet for a bit.

Papa leaned over the table. "Where's the bathroom?"

Olivia stared at him. "Papa. It's a DREAM."

Mama taped the map to the kitchen wall, next to the calendar, where you could see it from every chair.

"You put the willow right in the middle," she said.

"So everybody can find it."

Olivia ate her soup facing the map.
