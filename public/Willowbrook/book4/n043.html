Night 43

What the Cow Said

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The cow came back to the meadow, and this time he talked.

He talked in bells. When Olivia said hello, he said, "Ding."

"Cows say moo," she told him.

"Dis cow says ding," said the cow.

Baba nodded. "It's true. I asked him."

Olivia sat down in the grass. She had a question, and the cow was the one to ask. He sits on Theo's dresser all day and sees everything.

"What does Fee-oh like best?"

"Da cat," said the cow. "His feet. And when you sing."

"Me?"

"You. Loud."

In the morning Olivia went straight to the yellow room. Theo was up already, holding the crib rail, bouncing.

She stood where he could see her, and she sang the bath song as loud as she could.

Theo stopped bouncing. He stared at her. Then he bounced twice as hard, and squealed, and laughed so much he sat down with a bump.

"Mama! Da cow was right!"

Papa put his head around the door. "What's all this about a goat?"

"He's a COW, Papa."

"And what did the cow say?"

Olivia thought about the best way to explain it.

"Ding," she said.
