Night 112

The Morning After

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

In the morning there were pancakes with strawberries on top, cut small, because it was June and the strawberries were everywhere.

Olivia told Lily about the night, although Lily had been there for some of it. "First I was awake. Den I wanted Mama. Den your mama hummed. Den it was morning."

"I sleeped da whole time," said Lily.

"You slept like a log," said Miss Rosa. "A log that snores."

Then the doorbell rang, and it was Mama. Olivia got down from her chair so fast it wobbled, and ran, and Mama caught her and held on.

"I missed you," said Mama into her hair. "I looked in your room twice last night."

"But I wasn't dere."

"I know. I looked anyway."

Mama wanted to hear everything, so Olivia told it again from the beginning, with pancake in the middle.

"She called me and told me what she needed," said Miss Rosa. "That made it easy."

On the step, Mama held the bag. "Would you come again?"

Olivia looked at Lily. Lily looked at Olivia.

"Next time at my house," said Olivia.

The gold nightlight went home in her pocket.
