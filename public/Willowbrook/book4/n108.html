Night 108

What Papa Is Scared Of

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

The gutters were full of old leaves and maple seeds, so the tall ladder came out.

Mama held the bottom. Olivia's job was to sit on the porch step and call out if anything fell. Papa said it was the most important job, and also the one farthest from the ladder.

Papa went up two rungs. Then three. Then he stopped.

"Papa. Are you scared?"

Papa could have said no. "A little. High places make my tummy flip."

"Papas get scared?"

"Everybody gets scared sometimes." He gripped the ladder and let out a long, slow breath. Then he went up one more rung, and one more.

He looked at his feet before each step. He tried to whistle, to feel braver, and it came out as air.

"Dat's not a whistle!" called Olivia.

"I know," said Papa.

A wet clump of leaves dropped past Mama's head.

"Leaf!" called Olivia. "Big leaf! Helicopter!" A maple seed spun down beside her.

When Papa came down, his hands were sweaty, and he wiped them on his pants, and Olivia saw.

After supper she took out the crayons. "Can Papa go on my list?"

"If you'll have me," said Papa.

She drew him on the ladder. The ladder went off the top of the paper.
