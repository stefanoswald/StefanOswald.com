Night 362

Back in Grandpa's Day

Month 12: Almost Four

The birthday girl picks supper. Olivia picked pancakes, again.

Grandpa Joe came in from the truck with his hat in his hand.

"Pancakes for supper," he said. "Back in my day, we didn't have supper."

"Nuh-uh," said Olivia.

"Didn't have birthdays, either. Only every other year."

"GRANDPA."

"Odd years," said Grandpa. "I was four for two whole years."

Olivia giggled into her pancakes. "Dat's not true."

Grandpa winked at Theo. Theo tried to wink back and shut both eyes.

"What did you get?" said Olivia. "On your fourth birthday?"

Grandpa thought about it. "One pancake," he said. "For the whole farm."

"Da whole farm?"

"Cut it with a saw."

Papa said that was how he remembered it, too.

Olivia looked down at her plate, three pancakes high. She cut a piece off the top and put it on Grandpa's plate.

"For da whole farm," she said.

Grandpa looked at the piece. Then he ate it slowly, as if it were the only one.

After supper he fell asleep in the big chair.
