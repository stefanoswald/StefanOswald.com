Night 352

The Dream Meadow, Almost Four

Month 12: Almost Four

On this side of the willow it was still winter. On the other side, in the dream, the meadow was green.

Bobby Bear came walking up the path to meet her, one ear flopped, with both paws behind his back.

"Livy," he said. "You're almost four."

"What's behind you?"

"A thing."

"Is it honey?"

"No."

"Is it Gerald?"

Bobby Bear looked shocked. "Gerald is a fish, Livy. I wouldn't carry Gerald."

All the fish in the dream brook are named Gerald. It saves time.

They walked out to the far edge of the meadow, where the far gate stood, gold and shut.

Then Bobby Bear brought out his paws. In one of them lay a key, small and gold, warm when she held it.

"For da far gate," he said. "For later."

"Can I open it now?"

"Not now. Not for years."

"How many years?"

Bobby Bear counted on his paw. He ran out of paw.

"More dan dis," he said, and held it up.

Olivia closed her hand around the key.

When she woke up, her hand was still closed. There was nothing in it. She kept it closed anyway, down to breakfast.

She ate her toast with the other hand.
