Night 122

The Fort Doorbell

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Lily came over with a plan. Lily always comes with a plan.

"Da Fort Plan," she said. "Step one, we make a fort. Step two, nobody knocks it down. Step three, popsicles."

Step one went well. Two kitchen chairs, one old sheet, and the couch cushions for walls.

Step two did not. Theo crawled over, took the sheet in both fists, and pulled. The roof came down on everybody.

"FEE-OH!"

Theo laughed under the sheet. He thought that was the game.

They built it again. He pulled it down again.

"He needs a job," said Olivia.

"He can't do anything," said Lily. "He's one."

Olivia looked at Theo. He was chewing on his cow. The cow's bell went ding.

"He can be da doorbell."

So Theo sat in the fort door with his cow. Anybody who wanted to come in had to wait for the ding. Theo dinged for Lily. He dinged for the cat, who did not want to come in and was dinged at anyway.

"Rule thirteen," said Olivia. "Fee-oh is da doorbell. Not da roof."

"Now step three," said Lily.

Mama brought two popsicles for the fort, and some cold watermelon, cut small, for the doorbell.

The fort stayed up until supper.
