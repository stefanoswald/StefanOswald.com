Night 107

The Bad Dream and the Key

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

The crying came at two in the morning, the tangled kind.

Mama was there before Olivia was all the way awake. She picked her up, blanket and all, and held on.

"Something was chasing," Olivia said into Mama's neck. "I couldn't find you."

"That was a bad dream," said Mama. "You're awake now, and I've got you."

Mama turned the lamp on low. The chair was there. The shelf was there. Bobby Bear was there, squashed under Olivia's arm.

"Dreams are pretend," said Mama. "Even the bad ones."

"It was big."

Mama didn't ask how big. She asked, "Want to give it a different ending?"

Olivia thought. "It trips. And it's little. It was little da whole time."

"Where does it go now?"

"Home. To its mama."

"How does it get there?"

Olivia drew a door in the air, just like the one she drew for the wild bear. "It needs da key."

Mama handed her one, the invisible kind. Olivia turned it. "Click." The little something went through, and Olivia shut the door.

"Next dream, Baba comes."

"He'd like that," said Mama.

She kept a hand on the blanket until Olivia's breathing went long and slow.

Mama put the key on the nightstand, for later.
