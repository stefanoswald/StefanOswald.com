Night 290

The Bedtime Story, Reversed

Month 10: Words Are Magic

At supper Olivia made an announcement. "Tonight I do da bedtime. You two get tucked."

So after her bath, Mama squeezed into the reading chair, and Papa folded himself into the small chair. Olivia tucked a blanket over each of them, very tight, all the way up to the chin.

She held up the moose book, facing out. She knew every page. She told it from the pictures, and did the growl on the growl page, and turned each page slowly.

"Da end. I readed da whole thing."

"You read the whole thing," said Papa. "One more?"

"No. One story."

"I need a drink of water."

Olivia brought him the cup from the bathroom. He drank all of it.

"I need to tell you one thing."

"What thing?"

Papa thought. "I forgot."

Olivia put her hands on her hips, the way Mama does. "You want more. I know. It's sleeping time."

Papa lay very still. Then one of his eyes opened.

"I saw dat," said Olivia.

She turned to Mama for help. Mama was asleep in the reading chair, with her mouth a little open.

"She's very good at this," Papa whispered.

Olivia turned the lamp down low.
