Night 98

The Brave List

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Mama taped a big paper on the kitchen wall, low enough for a crayon.

"This is for things that felt scary," she said, "and you did them anyway."

Olivia drew the shadow first: a gray scribble with one wiggly arm. Mama wrote under it, The shadow on the wall. We looked.

Then the storm. It was a lot of lines and a lump.

"What's the lump?"

"Dat's Papa sleeping."

Mama wrote, The big storm. Papa slept through the end.

Then the big flush, from when she was two. Then the dust under the bed.

She wanted to draw Theo's vacuum too.

"That one's Theo's," said Mama. "He can have his own list when he's bigger."

So she drew it tiny, down in the corner, for later.

Three nights later, something thumped in the attic. Olivia's voice came down the hall. "Mama. I need my list."

Mama carried her to the kitchen, and they looked at it under the light over the stove. Olivia put her finger on each drawing while Mama read it out.

"I did all dose?"

"All of those."

"Dis thump can go on too."

The thump was the attic fan, starting up for summer. By breakfast it was on the list, a square with wind coming out.
