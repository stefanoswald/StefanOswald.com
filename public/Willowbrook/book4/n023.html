Night 23

The Orchestra of One

Month 1: Pretend!

On the first mild afternoon, Olivia made up a song. It was called "Da song of everything."

The instruments were two pots, the bell off her tricycle, and a jar of dried beans with the lid taped on tight. The audience was the gold dog next door and Mrs. Henderson across the street, who came out on her porch and waved. Theo saw her wave and hid his face in Mama's sweater.

Theo's drum was a bucket, upside down. He had one move. He slapped it with both hands.

The big pot made the best noise. Olivia looked at it. She looked at Theo. Then she pushed the big pot over to him and kept the little bell for herself.

"You gave him the big pot," Mama said.

Theo slapped the big pot. BONG. His whole face opened up.

The concert began. Part one was pots, loud. Part two was the bell, soft.

"Dis part is da morning!" Olivia shouted, so everyone would know.

Part three was everything at once. "Dis part is da whole day!"

The gold dog howled along, which was not in the song. Mrs. Henderson clapped from across the street.

At the very end, Theo slapped the pot once more, softly, just for himself.
