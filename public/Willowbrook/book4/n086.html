Night 86

The Hard Why About June's Abuela

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

June's abuela lives far away, in another country, and she is very sick. June told them at the park, and then she cried, and her mama carried her home.

That night, on the couch before bed, Olivia asked about it.

"Why did June's abuela get sick?"

Mama took a slow breath before she answered.

"Sometimes bodies get very sick," she said. "It isn't anybody's fault."

"But she's nice."

"Being nice doesn't stop being sick," said Mama. "It happens to nice people too."

"Dat's not fair."

"No. It isn't." Mama pulled her in closer. "I feel sad about it too."

"Is she gonna get better?"

"We don't know," said Mama. "Her doctors are taking care of her."

Olivia was quiet for a long time.

"June was crying."

"She was. She loves her abuela very much."

"What can we do?"

"We can be kind to June," said Mama. "What do you think she'd like?"

Olivia thought. "A picture. Of me and June."

"Tomorrow, then," said Mama.

Olivia leaned on Mama, and Mama stayed where she was.

Olivia's head grew heavy on Mama's arm.
