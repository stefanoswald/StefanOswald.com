Night 329

Boots on the Wrong Feet

Month 11: Winter Wonders

At preschool, getting ready to go outside in winter took longer than being outside.

Everybody sat on the floor by the cubbies and did their own. Olivia's cubby said OLIVIA, next to one that said LILY. She knew them both by their shapes.

Snowpants first. One leg went in. The other leg went into the same hole.

"Two legs, one hole," said Miss Dana, going by. "Hm."

Olivia took them off and started again. This time it worked.

Then boots. She pulled and stamped and pulled, and stood up.

Her feet looked strange. They pointed away from each other, like they were mad.

"Your boots are backwards!" said Lily.

Miss Dana crouched down. "Want to switch them, or keep them?"

Olivia took a step. She waddled. She took another step. She waddled more.

"I'm a duck," she said.

Lily sat down in the hat basket, laughing.

"Keeping dem," said Olivia.

Outside, the playground was all slush and puddles from the thaw. Olivia waddled through every one, and Lily waddled behind her, even though Lily's boots were on the right feet.

At snack, Olivia switched them by herself. Her feet stopped being mad at each other.
