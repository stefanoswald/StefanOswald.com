Night 119

Theo Turns One

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Theo was one year old, and everybody came to the back yard to say so.

Miss Rosa came with a small cake and a little flour on one cheek. Lily came with a card she had made herself, which was mostly glue.

Before the cake, Theo walked across the grass, holding Papa's two fingers, in big wobbly steps. Everybody clapped. Theo let go to clap too, and sat down.

Mama lit one candle and held the cake up high, away from the high chair. Everybody sang, and Olivia blew the candle out for him.

"Livy!" said Theo.

Mama took the candle out before the cake went anywhere near him. Then she set it on his tray.

Theo looked at the cake. He poked it. Then he put both hands in it, and then in his hair, and then in his mouth, in that order.

"He's wearing it," said Lily.

"Back in my day," said Grandpa Joe, "a first birthday cake was the size of a barn."

Theo had cake in one ear.

That night, after a bath, Mama laid him in his crib on his back, as always. He was one now, so the cow with the bell went in with him.

It rang once, and then not.
