Night 300

Night Three Hundred

Month 10: Words Are Magic

"Three hundred," said Papa. "Three hundred nights since you turned three."

"How do you write it?"

Papa got a crayon and a piece of paper. He made a 3. "Then two zeros."

Olivia looked at the empty space after the 3. "Zeros are O's."

"They look like O's."

"I do dem."

She made two big round ones, right next to his 3. The second one was bigger than the first, and a little crooked.

"Three hundred has two of my letter in it," said Olivia.

Papa decided this was not the night to explain zeros.

"Is three hundred a lot?"

"It's all the nights since before the crocuses," said Mama.

"How many till I'm four?"

Papa counted on his fingers, and then on the calendar. "Sixty-five."

"Is dat a lot?"

"It's until the brook comes unstuck," said Mama.

Olivia knew about that. The brook came unstuck on the day she was born.

Papa taped the paper to the wall by her bed.

She was asleep before he reached the lamp.
