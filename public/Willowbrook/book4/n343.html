Night 343

The Year in Pictures

Month 12: Almost Four

Olivia laid the whole year out on the living room floor, one drawing after another, from the door to the couch.

The first ones were from last spring. They were mostly circles.

Papa bent over one. "What a nice potato."

"Dat's YOU, Papa."

"Ah." He looked closer. "I see it now. The glasses."

"Dose are your ears."

The next one had a round head with lines coming out all over it.

"A spider," said Papa.

"MAMA. Dose are her arms."

"Her arms come out of her head?"

"Dey did back den."

Mama laughed until her eyes watered.

They walked along the line. The circles grew bodies. The bodies grew legs. Somewhere around the summer, the people got fingers, a lot of them.

At the end of the line was the newest one, from yesterday: a girl with a head and a body and two legs, all her curls, and a red bow.

"Dat's me," said Olivia. "Almost four."

"No potato," said Papa.

"No potato."

Mama put that one on the fridge.

The potato went back in the folder.
