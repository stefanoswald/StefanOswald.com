Night 96

The Under-Bed Question

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

"Papa. Is dere something under my bed?"

Papa did not say no. He said, "Let's look. You hold the flashlight."

Papa thought the flashlight was in the kitchen drawer. Olivia knew better. It was already in her bed, where she had put it after the shadow.

They lay on their bellies on the rug. She clicked the light on and pointed it into the dark.

Dust. A great deal of dust, in soft gray rolls.

"It's fluffy under dere."

The beam went further. A crayon. A raisin, very old. And in the far corner, something long and striped and lumpy.

"A monster," said Papa in a low voice. "A stripy one. Look at its tail."

"Dat's my SOCK, Papa!"

"Your sock?"

"My sock!"

Papa reached in and pulled it out. It was the one that had been missing since the weekend.

"It was hiding," said Olivia.

"Socks do that," said Papa.

She shone the light in one more time, all the way to the wall, slowly, to be sure. Then she gave Papa the raisin, which he did not eat, and put the sock under her pillow to keep it from wandering off again.

Papa left the flashlight by the bed, pointing under. Her eyes were already closing.
