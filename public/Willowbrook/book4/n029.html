Night 29

Two Captains

Month 1: Pretend!

Theo was napping at home with Mama, so it was just Olivia and Papa at the park. Mateo was there too, from two porches down, with his tool belt on.

The climbing frame was a pirate ship. Mateo had the wheel.

"I'm the captain," he said.

"I want to be captain," said Olivia.

"You can't. The captain has to be four."

Olivia's mouth fell open. "Dat's not a rule!"

She climbed down the rope ladder and ran to Papa's bench. "Mateo got da wheel all day."

Papa listened. "You want a turn. And Mateo made up a rule you don't like."

Olivia nodded hard.

"What could you do?"

Olivia chewed her lip. Then she went back and climbed the rope ladder, one rung at a time. Papa watched from the bench and did not get up, though he wanted to.

At the top she put her hands on her hips. "New rule. Da captain has to be three."

Mateo scratched his head with a twig from his tool belt. Then he looked at the wheel.

"It has two sides," he said.

So there were two captains, one on each side of the wheel, steering two different ways. The ship did not seem to mind.

Papa drank his coffee, cold, and watched them sail.
