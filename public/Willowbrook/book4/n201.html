Night 201

The Yellow Boots

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

The red boots pinched, so on Saturday Olivia got new ones. Yellow like the school bus, yellow like a yolk, so yellow that Papa said he needed his sunglasses.

Then it did not rain.

Sunday, sun. Monday, sun. Tuesday, sun, and not one puddle anywhere.

She wore them anyway. She wore them to breakfast. She wore them to the park and crunched through every dry leaf on the path, crunch, crunch. She wore them with her pajamas.

Theo found the old red boots in the hall. He stepped into them, both feet, all by himself. Then he walked three big steps, clomp, clomp, clomp, and sat down with a thump.

He laughed. He got up. Clomp, clomp, thump.

After supper Papa went out on the back step and whistled for rain. Nothing came out but air. No rain came either.

Olivia sighed at the window. "My boots are SO ready."

"They've been ready for days," said Papa. "Waiting is hard."

"Will it rain tomorrow?"

"Maybe."

"Not DAT maybe. A YES maybe."

At bedtime she lined the boots up by the front door. They waited there, facing out.
