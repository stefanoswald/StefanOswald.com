Night 200

Two Hundred Nights

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

"Two hundred," said Mama, tucking the quilt. "Two hundred nights since you turned three."

Olivia sat straight up. "Den I get two hundred stories."

"You get one," said Papa.

"Two?"

"One."

"One and a half?"

"One," said Mama, "and a very long goodnight."

Olivia flopped back down. Two hundred was a lot of nights. She could not see the end of it.

"What was in dem?"

"You tell us," said Papa.

She looked at the ceiling. "Da dark got a name. I goed to school. Fee-oh walked." She held up one more finger. "And da farm came here."

"You went to school," said Mama. "And Theo walks everywhere now."

"He falls down a lot."

"He does."

Papa read the one story. It was the moose book again, with the growl back in it.

Then came the very long goodnight. Goodnight lamp. Goodnight jar. Goodnight moose, and the cow down the hall, and the tomatoes on the sill, and every sock in the drawer.

Olivia was asleep by the tomatoes.

Papa said goodnight to the socks anyway.
