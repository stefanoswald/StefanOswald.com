Night 35

Theo Pulls Up

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Olivia heard him first. Theo was awake in the yellow room, making a new noise, a grunting, working noise.

She looked. Then she ran for Mama.

"Mama! Fee-oh is STANDING!"

He was. Both fists on the crib rail, feet under him, bouncing at the knees, he looked very surprised about it.

Mama's whole face lit up. "Look at you," she said. "Look where you are."

Then Theo tried to get down, and he did not know how. He held on, and held on, and his face began to crumple.

"He's stuck," said Olivia.

"Up is the easy part," said Mama. "Down takes practice."

She put her hands over his and bent his knees for him, slowly, until he sat. He looked at them. Then he grabbed the rail and pulled himself straight back up.

Stuck again. Crumple again.

"I'll show him," said Olivia. She stood where he could see her and bent her knees, down, down, until she was sitting on the rug.

Theo watched. His knees wobbled. He sat.

"You went slow, so he could see," said Mama.

Papa came in, still in his socks, and checked that the crib mattress was on its lowest notch. It already was.

All morning Theo pulled up on things and got stuck on them.

Olivia got down to show him again.
