Night 128

When He Ruined It

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

It was the tallest tower Olivia had ever built. It came up to her chin. She had used every block, even the round ones.

"Mama! Come see!"

Mama came, drying her hands. Theo came too, cruising along the couch, fast.

At the end of the couch, he let go and put one hand in the middle of the tower.

Down it came, even the round ones.

Theo laughed.

"NO!" Olivia's whole body shook. "He breaked it! He always does!"

Theo stopped laughing. His face began to crumple too.

Mama sat right down on the rug between them and gathered them in, one in each arm.

"You worked so hard on that," she said to Olivia. "You're really mad."

Olivia cried into one side of Mama and Theo into the other. Mama held on.

When the crying got smaller, Mama said, "He broke it. He doesn't know about towers yet."

"He's bad at towers."

"What could we do next time?"

Olivia wiped her nose on Mama's sleeve and looked at the blocks. Then she stacked three in front of Theo.

"Dis one's yours. For knocking."

Theo knocked it down. She was still a little mad, and she built it again anyway.

The next day, she built the big one on the table.
