Night 365

The Fourth Small Star

Month 12: Almost Four

It was the first night of March, and the brook was running. It had come unstuck that morning, the way it does, and now it ran loud and cold all the way down the hill.

Down by the water, the peepers rang.

On Maple Lane the porch lights went out, one at a time. Miss Rosa's bakery went dark. The Hendersons' orange cat came in off the wall. Only the Lantern Library stayed lit, the way it always does.

Up on the hill, the Great Willow held its three gold words in the dark: HOME, FAMILY, and GROWING.

Oliver Owl sat on the highest branch with his notebook, looking down at one small window.

"Who is four tonight?" he asked, though nobody was awake to answer.

He wrote one word. Then he turned to a clean page and closed the notebook.

Stella came down low over the roofs, lower than usual. Just above one roof on Maple Lane, she set a small new star.

Then she rose back up to her place.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.

Sweet dreams. Same time tomorrow.
