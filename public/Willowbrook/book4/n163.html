Night 163

The Painting Phase

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Every day a painting came home in the backpack, still a little damp.

By the second week the front of the refrigerator was full. By the third week the paintings were going up the side.

Papa stood in front of the newest one. It was purple, mostly.

"What a fine duck," said Papa.

"Dat's YOU."

"Me? Why am I purple?"

"Da purple jar was full."

Papa moved to the next one. It was purple too. He was more careful this time.

"And here I am again."

"Dat's Mama."

Mama laughed into the dish towel.

Papa came to the last one very slowly. It had a beak, and orange feet, and a pond.

"I'm not saying duck," said Papa. "Not ever again."

"It IS a duck!"

Olivia lay on the kitchen floor and laughed until she got the hiccups.

Papa said he would like one painting of himself that was not purple, please.

The next day a new one came home. It was orange. Papa hung it himself, at eye level.
