Night 246

Mateo's Ramp

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Mateo built a ramp on the back walk. It was one board on one brick, and he had written RAMP on it, in case anyone wondered.

"It needs a test driver," said Mateo.

Olivia put on her red helmet. That was Rule fourteen: helmets on for ramps. She had made it up that morning, but it counted.

Papa sat on the back step with his coffee. "Back walk only," he said. "You drive, and I'll watch."

First Mateo tested it with a toy truck. The truck went up, flew off the end, and landed upside down in the leaves.

"It goes very well," said Mateo.

Olivia pedaled to the ramp and stopped. It looked much taller from the trike.

She backed up. She pedaled to it again and stopped again.

"You could just do the front wheel," said Mateo. "That's a test too."

So Olivia rolled forward until her front wheel bumped up onto the board. She sat very still, half up and half down.

"How is it?" said Mateo.

"Bumpy," said Olivia. "Dat's enough for today."

She rolled back off. Papa went on drinking his coffee.

Mateo wrote something on his hand with a pen. "Tomorrow, back wheels."

Olivia parked the trike by the step, facing the ramp.
