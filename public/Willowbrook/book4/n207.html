Night 207

The Spilled Seeds Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Some flowers like to be planted in the fall, Papa said. The seeds sleep under the leaves all winter and come up when it gets warm.

Olivia carried the packet out to the fence. It rattled. She walked very carefully, and then she tripped over the hose.

The packet flew. The seeds went everywhere, into the long grass along the fence, tiny as dust.

"Dey're gone! All da flowers!" She was crying before she was even up.

Papa knelt in the grass beside her. "You carried them so carefully. And then they all got away."

"Get dem back!"

Papa looked. He ran his hand through the grass. "They're too small, Livy. I can't find them."

Olivia cried some more. Papa stayed right there in the wet grass until she was done.

"Maybe," said Papa.

"Maybe what?"

"We'll know in the spring."

"Dat's SO long."

"Seeds don't mind," said Papa.

He looked at the strip of grass along the fence. "Well," he said. "That's the Spill Garden now."

"But dere's nothing."

"Not yet."

That night the wind brought down the first maple leaves, and they settled along the fence, on top of the seeds.
