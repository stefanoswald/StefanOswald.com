Night 147

The Whistle Apprentice

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

After supper they sat on the back steps, where it was a little less hot, and Olivia whistled.

It was a small, thin whistle, like a bird that isn't sure. But it was real, and Theo stared at her mouth.

"You want to learn?" she asked him. "Lips small. Like dis. Blow soft."

Theo made his lips small. He blew. What came out was a raspberry, long and wet, all down her arm.

"Dat's not a whistle."

Theo did it again, louder, with his whole face. He was very proud of it.

Papa came out with a glass of water and sat behind them. "Let me show him how," he said. He puckered up and blew. Only air came out, like a bicycle tire going flat.

"Papa. Dat's not one either."

"Mine's not a whistle yet," said Papa. "I've been practicing for thirty years."

Theo looked at Papa for a long moment. Then he blew Papa a raspberry, his biggest one.

"I think that was advice," said Papa.

Olivia lay back on the step and laughed up at the sky.

So they practiced together, Olivia whistling, Papa puffing, and Theo spraying everybody.

The fireflies came up to listen.
