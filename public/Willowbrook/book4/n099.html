Night 99

The Fear That Was Fun

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

After supper, Papa got down on his hands and knees. "I am the Rug Monster," he growled. "I eat socks and small girls."

Olivia shrieked and ran behind the couch. Then she came right back out, because that is how monster games work.

"Get me again!"

The Rug Monster crawled after her, slow and growly. Olivia ran around the couch, around the chair, around the Rug Monster himself.

Theo was on the rug too. He did not run away from the monster. He crawled straight at it, laughing, and grabbed its beard.

"Ow," said the Rug Monster. "Theo, that's not how this works."

Theo pulled again.

Then Olivia held up her hand. "Stop!"

The Rug Monster froze with one paw in the air.

"Go!" He growled and crawled. "Stop!" He froze. "Sit!"

The Rug Monster sat, like a dog, with his tongue out. Theo clapped.

"You're the boss of this monster," said Papa.

Then the monster tried a new voice, deep and slow and very close, and Olivia's face changed.

"Not dat voice."

Papa went back to the regular growl at once. "Not that voice. Got it."

They played until the monster was out of breath and lay down flat on the rug. Theo crawled up onto its back and sat there, pleased.
