Night 227

Pumpkin Insides

Month 8: Helping Hands

Papa cut the top off the pumpkin. That was a Papa job, because of the knife.

Then he lifted the lid by its stem and put it on his head.

"Dat's not a hat," said Olivia.

"It's a very small hat," said Papa.

Scooping was Olivia's job. She reached in with the big spoon. It was cold and slippery and full of orange strings.

"It's pumpkin spaghetti!"

Seeds stuck to her fingers and her sleeves, and one stuck to her eyebrow for the rest of the evening.

Theo got his own bowl of strings to squish, with every seed picked out. He squished them hard. Then he put one on his head, like Papa.

Papa sang while he scraped. "Five little pumpkins, sitting on a goat."

"A GATE, Papa!"

"Sitting on a coat?"

"GATE!"

When the pumpkin was empty, Olivia drew its face with a marker: two eyes, a nose, and a mouth that went up on one side and down on the other.

"He's happy but a little worried," she said.

Papa carved it just the way she drew it.

That night the pumpkin sat on the front step with a little battery light inside. It looked happy, and a little worried.
