Night 299

The Name Cards

Month 10: Words Are Magic

At circle, Miss Dana had a new game. Everybody's name was on a card, and all the cards were in a basket.

"Pick one," said Miss Dana. "Then take it to its person."

Lily picked first. Her card had a J at the front, so she took it straight to June.

Marcus picked his own card and sat on it. Miss Dana said that counted.

Then Olivia picked. Her card started with a letter she didn't know. It looked like two birds flying down, side by side.

"Two V's," said Olivia. "Stuck together."

She looked around the circle. Nobody said it was theirs.

Wren was sitting very still, with her hands in her lap. Wren hardly ever talked at circle.

"Is dis yours?" said Olivia, very quietly.

Wren looked at the card. She gave a very small nod.

"W," said Miss Dana. "For Wren."

Olivia held it out, and Wren took it in both hands. She looked at it for a long time. Her ears went pink, and she smiled at the card.

When it was Wren's turn, she picked a card with an O at the front. She walked it all the way across the rug and gave it to Olivia.

At supper Olivia drew a W on her napkin. It was two birds, stuck together.
