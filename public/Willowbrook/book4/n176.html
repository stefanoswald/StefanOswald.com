Night 176

The Family Program

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

On Friday, the families came to school for the family program. The grown-ups sat on the little chairs. Papa's knees came up almost to his glasses.

The class sang the hello song. Then each child said who was in their family.

Olivia's part was a secret. All week she had said, "You hear it at da show."

Marcus said, "My mama and my dad and my new baby." Wren didn't say anything. She waved at her grandma, and that counted.

Then it was Olivia's turn. She stood up and said it loud, to the second row.

"Mama. Papa. Fee-oh. Bobby Bear. And Buttons."

It went quiet for a moment. Miss Dana nodded.

Then Theo, on Papa's lap, pointed straight at her and shouted, "LIVY!"

Everybody laughed. Olivia laughed too, and waved at him all through the next song.

At the end, Papa tried to whistle. Only air came out.

Her paper crown rode home on Theo's head.
