Night 77

Why Do Songs Get Stuck in Heads

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

"Mama! Da hello song won't stop!"

The hello song is Olivia's own. It is mostly names. Hello Mama, hello Papa, hello Fee-oh, hello toast. She had sung it at breakfast, and now it was singing itself.

"It's in my head. Why?"

"Your brain liked it, so it plays it again," said Mama. "Songs that go round and round are the stickiest."

"How do I make it stop?"

"Sometimes it stops if you sing it all the way to the end."

Olivia sang it all the way to the end, very loud. Hello, cat. Hello, spoon. Hello, window. The end.

She waited.

"It's still going."

"Then try a different song," said Mama, and started the spider one, the one with the waterspout.

Olivia sang the spider song. The hello song went away.

By lunch, the spider song was stuck.

By nap time, Mama was humming it at the sink.

And Theo, in his high chair, bounced every time anybody got to the part where the rain comes down.

"Now you got it too," said Olivia.

"I have it too," said Mama, and hummed the rest.

The spider climbed the spout all afternoon.
