Night 103

The Lost Plan

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

At the market, Olivia held the cart and asked a question she had been saving. "What if I get losted?"

Mama stopped the cart. "If you got lost here? Let's make a plan, right now."

"First, stop right where you are and be a statue," said Mama. "I'll come back the way we walked."

"Den what?"

"Then shout. Shout 'Mama!' and shout your own name, loud as you can. I know that voice anywhere."

"And den?"

"If I don't come quick, find a helper. Somebody behind a counter, or a mama with kids. Tell them, 'I'm lost.'"

They practiced right there, like a game. Olivia was a statue beside the apples, so still that a man reached right past her for a bag. She did the shout, the quiet version, which was mostly her face.

"Now show me a helper," said Mama.

Olivia looked around. She pointed at the man behind the cheese counter. Yes. She pointed at a mama pushing a stroller. Yes. Then she pointed at the baby in the stroller.

"Dat baby?"

"Too small," said Mama. "Try his mama."

"I got three jobs," said Olivia. "Statue. Shout. Helper."

She said them again at the checkout, and again at the car, one finger each.
