Night 37

What the Bear Knows

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Baba was on her dresser before Olivia could even sit up. He had seen everything. So in the meadow, she asked him.

"Baba. What do you know?"

"Lots," said Baba. "I know you don't like da flush."

"Not anymore."

"I know you chewed my ear."

Olivia stopped walking. "I did not."

"You were a baby. You chewed everything. Papa's thumb. A spoon. Me."

She looked at his ear, the flopped one. "Is dat why it flops?"

"It always flopped," said Baba. "You helped."

Then she asked the big one. "What's Mama's secret?"

Baba thought for a long time. "I don't know Mama's secrets. I only know yours."

In the morning Olivia went straight to the kitchen. "Mama. Did I chew Baba's ear? When I was a baby?"

Mama laughed. "Every day. You chewed that ear flat."

So Baba had been right. Olivia looked at Mama over her cereal.

"Do you have a secret?"

"I do," said Mama. "It's in the cupboard, and it's for Sunday."

Olivia looked at the cupboard for the whole rest of breakfast.

Baba didn't know either.
