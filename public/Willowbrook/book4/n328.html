Night 328

January Mud

Month 11: Winter Wonders

The thaw kept on, and the yard gave up and turned to mud.

Mama looked at it from the back step. Then she got out the old clothes.

"Outside clothes," she said. "Boots off on the porch after. Deal?"

"Deal!"

The mud went shloop when Olivia stepped in and shlop when she pulled out. On the fourth step it kept her boot. She stood on one leg as long as she could, then put her sock foot down in the mud, because what else was there to do.

"My sock is chocolate now," she said.

Theo sat right down in the middle of it and slapped it with both hands. "Mud!" After that, everything was mud: the fence, the hose, Mama.

Olivia found a stick and dug a ditch from the big puddle. The water ran along it, brown and fast, and she followed it to the fence.

When it was time to go in, Theo said no to the porch and no to the door.

Mama pulled off his boots. "Theo, put this on the mat."

He carried it, dripping, to the mat. Then he came back for the other one.

Two pairs of boots stood on the mat, full of yard.
