Night 320

The Frost Ferns

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Olivia woke to a window that had grown a garden.

Frost covered the glass from the corners in, silver-white, in ferns and feathers and curls.

"Mama! Come see! Dere's plants on my window!"

Mama came in her robe. Theo was still asleep. It was only the two of them, noses close to the glass.

"Who drawed it?" said Olivia.

"The cold drew it," said Mama. "The warm room left a little water on the glass, and in the night the cold froze it into feathers."

"All by itself?"

"All by itself. Ice likes to grow in shapes like that."

Olivia put her mouth close and breathed out. Haaaah. A round hole opened in the middle of a fern, and through it she could see the white yard.

"Oh no. I ate a fern."

"You made a peephole," said Mama.

Olivia looked through it. The feeder had a hat of snow. Then she looked at all the ferns around the hole.

"Just one hole," she said. "Da rest stays."

They stood at the window a while longer, one peephole between them, taking turns.
