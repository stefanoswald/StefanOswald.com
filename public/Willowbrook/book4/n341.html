Night 341

Theo's Secret

Month 12: Almost Four

Behind the couch, Mama and Theo were making something.

"Shh," said Mama. "It's a secret, for Livy."

"SHH," said Theo.

He had a fat purple crayon and a folded card, and he scribbled on it with his whole arm. Then he held it up high, climbed out over the arm of the couch, and ran to the kitchen.

"Livy! Livy!"

Olivia saw purple. She clapped both hands over her eyes.

"I didn't see nothing!"

Mama came and steered Theo back behind the couch.

A minute later he came out the other end. "LIVY!"

Olivia turned her whole chair around to face the wall. "Not looking! I'm not looking!"

"He doesn't know what a secret is yet," Mama said to the back of the chair.

"I know," said Olivia, to the wall.

The third time, Olivia hid under the blanket on the couch before he could find her. Theo found her. He lifted the edge, pushed the card underneath, and pushed his own head in after it, grinning.

It was dark under there. Olivia shut her eyes anyway.

"Still didn't see it," she told Mama, from under the blanket.

Mama put the card on top of the fridge.

Theo pointed at the fridge until bedtime.
