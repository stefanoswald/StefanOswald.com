Night 215

The Bakery Hour

Month 8: Helping Hands

On Saturday morning Olivia worked at the bakery for one hour.

Rosa's whole face went up when Olivia came in. She tied an apron on her, a small one covered in strawberries. On the pocket, in red thread, Rosa had stitched OLIVIA.

"Dat's my name," said Olivia. She touched the O. "Dat one's me."

Lily was in the back with the raisins, which mostly meant eating them.

Olivia had two jobs. She held the paper bags open with both hands, so Rosa could slide the bread in. And she said "Good morning" to everybody who came through the door.

"It's nearly afternoon," said a man in a hat.

"Good nearly afternoon," said Olivia.

Then a roll bounced off the tray onto the floor. Olivia froze.

"Floor rolls are for the birds," said Rosa. "Go crumble it on the step."

Olivia crumbled it on the step. Two sparrows came before she was done.

"This is the last tray," said Rosa, carrying out another tray. It was not the last tray.

When the hour was over, Rosa untied the apron. "Every person got a good morning," she said. "Even the man in the hat."

Olivia saw herself in the bread case glass. There was flour on one cheek, the same cheek as Rosa's.

She left it there.
