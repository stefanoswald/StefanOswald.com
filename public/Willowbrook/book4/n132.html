Night 132

The First Real Game Together

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Olivia had played peekaboo with Theo since he was small enough to lie on a blanket. She hid behind her hands, and he laughed when she came back. That was how it went.

After supper, it went a new way.

Theo cruised along the couch to the window. He took hold of the long curtain and pulled it across his face. All of him was hidden, except his feet.

He waited.

Olivia looked at the feet. The feet waited too.

"Where's Fee-oh?" she said, loud. "Is he under da table?" She looked under the table.

A giggle came out of the curtain.

"Is he in da toy box?" She looked in the toy box. "Is he in Papa's shoe?" She looked in Papa's shoe, carefully.

The feet did a little dance.

Olivia tiptoed up close. "Is he HERE?" She pulled back the curtain.

Theo shrieked so loud the curtain shook.

Then he pulled the curtain back over his face. His turn again.

Olivia stood still. He had started it. He had hidden, and he had waited for her to come and find him.

"Mama," she called. "He's playing WITH me!"

"I heard," said Mama from the kitchen.

They played until the feet got tired. Then the feet sat down, still hiding.
