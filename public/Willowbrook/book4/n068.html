Night 68

Why Do We Say Sorry

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Mama sat at the kitchen table with a pencil, trying to remember what they needed from the store. Theo was napping at last, and Olivia sat beside her with a why every few seconds.

"Why is da pencil yellow? Why do erasers smell? Why do we need eggs?"

"Olivia. Enough whys!"

It came out loud and sharp. Olivia's mouth shut. Her chin went wobbly.

Mama put the pencil down. She turned her chair and got low. "I used a sharp voice, and you were only asking. I'm sorry."

Olivia looked at the floor. "Why do we say sorry?"

"So you know I know what I did," said Mama. "Then we can fix it."

"How do we fix it?"

"You ask me one why, and I listen with both ears."

Olivia picked carefully. "Why do we need eggs?"

"For Saturday pancakes," said Mama. "And because Papa ate the last two."

"Papa ate dem?"

"Standing up. With the fridge door open."

Olivia gasped. Then she looked at Mama's hands, and at the table, and back at Mama.

"You forgot da flower."

"I did," said Mama.

She went out to the yard in her socks and came back with a dandelion, a little squashed from the lawn.

Olivia put it in an egg cup on the windowsill. It leaned toward the light.
