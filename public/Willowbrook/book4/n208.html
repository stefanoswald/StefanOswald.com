Night 208

The Maybe Theo Taught

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

They got to Dandelion Park just as the rain stopped. Everything was wet. The slide was wet. The swings had little puddles in their seats. The sandbox was mud.

Olivia stopped at the gate. "Everything's WET! We can't play!"

Her voice went up and up. Mama crouched down beside her. "The slide was the whole plan," she said. "And it's all wet."

Olivia leaned on Mama's knee with her lip out.

Before anyone could say another word, Theo let go of Mama's other hand. He pointed at the big puddle by the bench. He looked at Olivia. He looked back at the puddle.

Then he walked straight into it with his arms up and stamped.

SPLASH.

He laughed so hard he sat down in it. He got up, wet to the knees, and stamped again. SPLASH.

Olivia looked down at her yellow boots. She looked at the puddle. She looked at Mama.

"Theo says maybe," said Mama.

Olivia jumped. SPLASH. Theo jumped too, which for him meant bending his knees. SPLASH.

They did every puddle in the park, and the slide stayed wet, and nobody minded.

Theo squelched all the way home.
