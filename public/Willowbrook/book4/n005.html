Night 5

The Puddle Ship

Month 1: Pretend!

It rained all night, and in the morning the big puddle by the curb was back. Last year the puddle was for stomping. This year Olivia crouched at the edge of it in her boots and put a finger to her lips.

"Shhh. Dat's not a puddle. Dat's da sea."

She found a ship, an oak leaf from last fall, brown and curled at the edges. She set it on the water.

"Dere's a storm coming. Da sailors are scared."

"What does the captain say?" Mama asked. She stood close, between Olivia and the street.

"Da captain says hold on."

A stick made the waves, huge ones, nearly an inch high.

"Mama, you be da wind."

Mama blew. The leaf spun. It tipped. One side went under.

"Blow softer!"

Mama blew softer. The leaf rocked, and straightened, and drifted to the far side, where a pebble was waiting to be the harbor.

"Da sailors made it," Olivia said.

"All of them?"

"All of dem. Dey're tired now."

The sea dried up by Thursday. The leaf went home in her pocket.
