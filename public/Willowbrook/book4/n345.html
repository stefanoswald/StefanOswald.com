Night 345

The Invitations

Month 12: Almost Four

The party rule in this house is simple: the whole class, or nobody.

Mama wrote the words. Olivia did the rest. On every one she drew a picture for that person, and at the bottom she wrote OLIVIA in big letters, one at a time.

June's got a bug. Wren's got one small star in the corner, because Wren likes quiet. Marcus had a new baby at home, so Marcus got a baby. Mateo got a ramp.

"Mama, is dis a good ramp?"

"In a minute, love," said Mama. She was wiping Theo's hands.

Olivia waited. She drew a car on the ramp. She drew another car. She drew a third car flying off the end.

"That was a long minute," said Mama, when she came back. "I'm sorry."

"That car is going to land in the next town," said Mama.

The last one was for Mr. Petrov, at the mending shop. Olivia drew him a zipper. It came out like a caterpillar.

"He'll know," said Olivia.

By then her hand was tired. She shook it out and wrote the last A.

"Your hand got tired, and you finished anyway," said Mama.

On Monday Olivia put them in the cubbies herself. Mr. Petrov's went through his mail slot.

He came to his window and held up the caterpillar.
