Night 245

The Deep End Crossing

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In November, swimming moved to the indoor pool, the big warm room with the echo. Outside it was already dark.

The blue and white rope floated across the water. Past it was the deep end.

"In June da rope said not yet," Olivia told Mama.

"What about your skills?"

Olivia held up her fingers. "I kick. I blow bubbles. I turn around and grab da wall."

"Those went over a while ago," said Mama. "Want to follow them?"

The plan was the wall. Olivia would walk her hands along the edge, past the rope, all the way to the corner. Mama would be in the water right beside her.

At the rope, Olivia's hands stopped. Under her feet there was no floor, only blue.

"My tummy is flippy."

"Mine too, a little," said Mama. "I'm right here."

One hand went past the rope. Then the other hand. Then she was on the deep side, holding on, and her legs kicked by themselves.

Hand, hand, hand. All the way to the corner.

"I'm in da DEEP," said Olivia, and the room said it back.

Mama stayed beside her all the way back, one arm ready.

In the car her wet hair dripped on her collar. Her fingers were still curled from holding on.
