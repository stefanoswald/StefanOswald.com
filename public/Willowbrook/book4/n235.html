Night 235

The Broken Truck

Month 8: Helping Hands

Theo's wooden truck lost a back wheel. The wheel rolled under the couch, and the truck went bump, bump, bump on three.

"Uh-oh," said Theo. He held the truck up to Papa. Then he held it up to Olivia.

Olivia found the wheel under the couch, with a raisin and a crayon. Its little peg had snapped.

"Mr. Petrov can fix it," said Olivia. "He fixes everything."

On Saturday Papa took them to the mending shop. The sign in the window said SHOES, ZIPPERS, AND MOST THINGS. Olivia was sure a truck was a most thing.

Mr. Petrov looked at the truck over his glasses. "Only a peg," he said. "But somebody has to hold it still."

"Me," said Olivia.

She held the truck on the workbench with both hands. Mr. Petrov cut a new peg from a thin stick of wood, put a dot of glue on it and pushed the wheel on.

"Now we wait for the glue. Don't move."

Olivia didn't move. Theo reached for the truck, and Papa caught his hand.

"You kept it still the whole time," said Mr. Petrov. "The glue had a good chance."

At last he set the truck on the floor. Theo gave it a push. It rolled on four wheels all the way to the door.

Theo went after it.
