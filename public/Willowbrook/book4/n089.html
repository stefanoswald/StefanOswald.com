Night 89

Papa Gets the Whys Wrong

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

At bedtime Papa opened the green why notebook.

"Why is the sky blue?" he read. "Because somebody painted it."

"No! Da air bounces it!"

"Why do worms come up in the rain? To go swimming."

"No, Papa. Dey need air."

"Why does it get dark? Because the sun goes to bed."

"We turn! Like da orange!"

"And the cat stayed on," said Mama, from the foot of the bed.

Papa got the two eyes wrong, and the bread, and the twinkling stars, and Olivia fixed every one. She showed him Grandpa's smiling lines. She said her whole name for the names page. She clapped and waved for the page about Theo copying, and told him about the sorry that needed a flower, and the stairs Mama carried her up.

Papa didn't read the ambulance page silly.

"Wee-oo," said Olivia, very small.

"Very small," said Mama.

June's abuela had a page too, with a drawing of two girls on it. Papa touched it and turned it.

Some questions had nothing under them at all. The willow. Where Olivia was before. The star that twinkled once. Those were staying that way.

"Why is water wet?" read Papa.

"If it's not wet, it's air!"

"I got more," said Olivia.

"I know," said Papa.

Mama turned off the lamp.
