Night 148

The Unposed Portrait of Two

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

The aunts wanted a picture of the two of them for their mantel. A nice one.

So Olivia put on her red dress, and Theo put on a shirt with a collar, and Papa sat them on the couch by the big window.

"Big smiles," said Papa.

Olivia smiled with all of her teeth at once. Theo licked the window.

"Theo, look here, buddy."

Theo slid off the couch. Papa put him back. He slid off again. Then he pulled off one sock and offered it to the camera.

"Can everybody just SIT STILL?" said Papa, much too loud.

Everybody sat still. Olivia's smile went away. Theo's lip came out.

Papa put the camera down and sat on the floor. "Sorry," he said. "I got grumpy about a picture."

Then he lay down flat on the rug, the way he does when he gives up. Theo crawled over and sat on his tummy. Olivia climbed on too. Papa said "Oof," and Theo laughed his hiccup laugh, and Olivia rolled off laughing, and Theo tumbled after her, into her lap.

Papa reached for the camera without getting up and took one picture, from the floor.

That was the one. It shows a collar, a sock, and two open mouths.

Nobody in it is looking at the camera.
