Night 175

The Cow in the Wash

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Theo's cow had to go in the wash. It had been in the honey at the harvest party.

Theo stood at the dryer and cried. He pointed at the round window, where the cow went round and round. Clonk. Ding. Clonk. Ding.

Mama picked him up. "You want your cow. He'll be dry soon."

Theo cried harder. Soon does not mean much when you are thirteen months old.

Olivia watched from the doorway with Bobby Bear. She looked at Theo. She looked at Bobby Bear. She looked at Theo again.

Then she walked over and held Bobby Bear out.

"You can hold him. Till da cow's dry."

Theo stopped crying so fast he hiccupped. He took Bobby Bear by the flopped ear and pushed his face into Bobby Bear's tummy.

Mama waited a moment. Then she said, "You gave him Bobby Bear. You didn't have to."

"I want him back, though," said Olivia. "Soon."

When the dryer stopped, Mama took the cow out, warm. Theo let go of Bobby Bear at once and reached with both hands.

Olivia caught her bear before he hit the floor.

He smelled a little like Theo.
