Night 275

The Name Written Whole

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Olivia could make the O. She had been making it all week, on everything.

On Thursday she sat at the kitchen table with the red crayon and made the rest.

The L was a stick with a foot. The I was a stick by itself. The V was a bird flying down. Then another I. The A was the hardest, a pointy roof with a line across the middle, and the line kept missing.

She tried the A three times. On the third try the line landed.

"Mama! Papa! Come look. It's me."

There it was, big and wobbly, all the way across the paper. The L's foot pointed the wrong way, back toward the O.

"Every letter," said Papa. "You went slow on the A."

"Da L is looking at da O," said Olivia. "Dey're friends."

"They do look friendly," said Mama.

It went on the fridge, up high, where Theo couldn't reach.

For a week she wrote her name on everything. The grocery bag. A napkin. The back of Papa's hand, in marker, while he napped on the couch.

It was not the washable kind.

Papa wore it to the store.
