Night 54

The Far Gate

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Far out past the firefly field, past where the cow had got lost, Olivia saw something gold.

"Baba. What's dat?"

It was a gate. It was so far off that it looked small, and it was shut.

Baba was quiet for a moment, which is not like him.

"Dat's da far gate," he said.

"What's behind it?"

"I don't know. Nobody goes dere yet."

"Not even you?"

"Not even me."

Olivia looked at it. It did not look scary. It looked like a gate that was waiting for somebody.

"When can we go?"

"Not for years," said Baba. "When it's time, I'll tell you. Bears keep promises."

Olivia thought about years. Years was a lot of birthdays.

Then Baba turned his back on the gate. "Race you to da brook."

They raced.

He let her win.
