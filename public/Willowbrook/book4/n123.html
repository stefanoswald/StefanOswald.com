Night 123

Teaching the Jacket Flip

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

A warm rain came down all morning, and Olivia's red rain jacket came out of the closet.

Olivia could do the jacket flip. She had done it since winter. Today she would teach it.

"Fee-oh. Watch."

She laid the jacket on the floor, upside down. Arms in the sleeves. Up and over her head. Whoosh. On.

Theo clapped.

"Now you."

She laid it down for him. Theo crawled onto the jacket and sat in the middle of it.

"No. Arms in."

She put his hands in the sleeves herself. Theo pulled them out, lifted the whole jacket, and put it on his head. He was a small red tent.

"Dat's not da flip."

The tent sat very still. Then Theo pulled the jacket off and looked at her, amazed to find her still there.

Olivia laughed. She put the jacket back on his head. He pulled it off. On. Off. He shrieked every time.

"Okay," said Olivia. "You do da head part. I do da arms."

Mama came by with the laundry and stopped. "You made the job his size," she said.

By lunch, Theo could do the head part all by himself. When Papa came home, he did it with Papa's jacket.

It took Papa a while to find him.
