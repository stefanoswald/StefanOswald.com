Night 293

The Word for Theo

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Theo stacked three blocks. He put a fourth one on top, very carefully, with his tongue out.

It fell. All of it.

Theo screamed. He threw a block at the couch, sat down hard, and cried.

Olivia got down in front of him, the way Mama does. Eye to eye. "Fee-oh. You're frustrated."

Theo cried.

"Frus-tray-ted," said Olivia. "Say it."

Theo stopped to look at her. He was still breathing hard. Then he patted his own chest.

"Deo," he said.

"No. Frus-tray-ted."

He patted his chest harder. "DEO."

Olivia sat back on her heels. She looked at his hand on his chest.

"Deo," she said slowly. "Is dat you?"

Theo patted his chest. "Deo." He wasn't crying anymore.

"It's not Deo," Olivia told him. "It's Fee-oh."

From the kitchen doorway, Papa said, "Theo," to nobody in particular.

Olivia put the bottom block down and held it with both hands. "You put one."

Theo put one on top. It stayed.

He patted his chest.
