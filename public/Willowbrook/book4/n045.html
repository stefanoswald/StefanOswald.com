Night 45

Baba's Backstory

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

"Baba. Where were you before me?"

Baba sat down on a rock. He had the face of a bear with a long story, and he had one.

"First I was a wild bear," he said. "In da woods. I wrestled a moose."

"You did not."

"A little moose."

"Den what?"

"Den a shelf. In a shop. A whole shelf of bears, all waiting. I was da bravest bear on da shelf."

"How come dey picked you?"

Baba touched his flopped ear. "Dis. Da udder bears had two ears dat stood up. I had dis one."

Olivia looked at the ear. It hung down over his eye in the dream wind, the way it always did.

"Den a bag," said Baba. "Bags are very dark. Den you."

Olivia reached up and held the flopped ear between her finger and her thumb, gently, like the soft corner of a blanket.

"I like dis ear best," she said.

Baba let her hold it.

In the morning she told Theo about the moose.
