Night 110

The Solo Sleepover, Packed

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Tomorrow Olivia was going to sleep at Lily's house. Her first night away, just her, no Mama and no Papa. Miss Rosa had said so, and Lily had said so about forty times.

Tonight Olivia was packing. Grandpa Joe had come by with eggs and stayed. He sat on the little chair, which was much too little for him, with his knees up high.

Olivia packed her pajamas. Her toothbrush, the purple one. Her star sweater, in case. Bobby Bear went on top, facing out, so he could see.

Mama unplugged the gold nightlight and handed it over. "Lily's house has different dark," said Olivia.

Grandpa nodded.

Mama folded a small copy of the brave list and tucked it in the side pocket.

Olivia sat down on the rug next to the bag. She was quiet for a while. "I'm excited and scared."

Grandpa Joe looked at her for a long moment. "Me too," he said.

"About what?"

He thought. "Most things."

Then he reached into his shirt pocket and took out a smooth gray stone from the brook, warm from being carried. He put it in her hand and closed her fingers over it.

"For holding."

Olivia held it. It fit exactly.

The bag waited by the door, bear on top.
