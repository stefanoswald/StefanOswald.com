Night 266

The Wrong Turn Walk

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

On Thursday Papa and Olivia walked home from the library in the dark, because in November it was dark at five.

At the corner, Papa turned the wrong way.

"Papa. Dat's not our way."

"Isn't it?" said Papa. "I think I'm lost."

Papa knew exactly where they were. He wanted to see if Olivia did.

He pointed at a yellow house. "Is that our house?"

"No! Ours is white."

He pointed at the bakery. "Is that our house?"

"Dat's ROSA'S, Papa."

He pointed straight up at the moon. "Is that our house?"

"Dat's da MOON!"

"Then I'm very lost," said Papa. "Can you get us home?"

Olivia turned all the way around. Behind them, over the roofs, the lantern on the library was glowing.

"Da library is dere. So home is dis way."

She pulled him past the bakery, where the air smelled like bread, and around the corner. There was Mrs. Henderson's door with the lion. There was the maple with no leaves.

"Dere's OURS."

"You found it," said Papa. "You used the lantern, and your nose."

Olivia looked up at him. "You weren't lost."

"I was a little lost," said Papa. "Mostly I was following you."

At the door he asked if this was their house. Olivia opened it to show him.
