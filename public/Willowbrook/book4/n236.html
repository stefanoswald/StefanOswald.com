Night 236

Willowbrook Cleanup Day

Month 8: Helping Hands

On cleanup day half the town came to Dandelion Park with gloves and bags. The children got grabbers, long sticks with a trigger at one end and a claw at the other.

Olivia's grabber could pick up a wrapper. It could pick up a bottle cap. It could pick up Papa's nose.

"Put me back," said Papa.

Olivia filled her bag fast. Mostly with leaves.

"Leaves can stay," said Papa. "They're the park's blanket."

"Den what's trash?"

"Anything the park didn't make."

Olivia held up an acorn. "Park made dat." She held up a wrapper. "Park didn't." A feather: park. A straw: not park. A sock: not park, and nobody's either.

Lily and Papa fished a spoon out of the reeds. Why a spoon was in the reeds, no one could say.

Theo carried one big yellow leaf all morning. It was his, and it was not going in any bag.

At noon the bags stood in a row by the path. Olivia looked around the pond.

"It looks da same," she said.

Papa pointed at the path by the water, where the wrappers had been. It was just path now, and leaves.

Olivia looked for a long time. Then she put her acorn in her pocket.
