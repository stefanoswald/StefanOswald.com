Night 88

Why Does the Day Have to End

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

It had been a long, good Saturday, with the park in it, and the swings, and a picnic lunch on the grass. Now the sky over the Hendersons' roof was going from gold to blue.

"Why does da day hafta end? I wasn't done."

"The earth keeps turning," said Mama. "Our side is turning away from the sun."

"Like da orange."

"Like the orange."

"Tell it to stop turning."

"It won't listen to me," said Mama. "It never has."

Olivia pressed her forehead against the glass.

"You're sad it's over," said Mama. "It was a good one."

Olivia nodded against the window.

"What else do you see?" asked Mama.

Olivia looked. Mrs. Henderson was turning on her porch light. A bat went zigzag over the street. One small star was out, low down. And in the glass, very faint, there was a girl with curls.

"I see me."

Mama put her chin on top of Olivia's curls, and they watched together.

Then the window was all the way blue.
