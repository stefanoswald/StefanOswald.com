Night 221

The Sock With No Friend

Month 8: Helping Hands

Matching socks was Olivia's job. It was on the list, next to the drawing of a sock.

The clean laundry went on the rug in a heap as big as Theo. Olivia found a sock. Then she looked for its friend.

Papa's socks were as long as her arm. Theo's fit on her thumb. It was slow work, because every sock had to be held up to every other sock.

Papa sat down beside her. "Let me," he said.

Zip, zip, zip. He matched six pairs while she held up one sock.

Olivia put her sock down. "Dat was MY job."

Papa looked at the sock balls. Then he looked at her. "You're right," he said. "I was hurrying, and I took your job."

He began unrolling them. "I'm sorry."

"Not ALL of dem!" said Olivia.

So he left two pairs rolled, and she did the rest.

At the bottom of the heap there was one striped sock with no friend. They looked everywhere.

"Where did his friend go?"

"No one knows," said Papa. "It's one of the great mysteries."

Olivia went and got Mr. Socko. She laid the striped sock down beside him.

"Now he's got a friend," she said.

Papa folded the last towel.
