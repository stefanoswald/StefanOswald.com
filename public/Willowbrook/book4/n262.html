Night 262

The Glove on the Walk

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Mrs. Henderson dropped a glove on her way in from the mailbox. It lay on her front walk across the street, red on the white frost.

Olivia saw it from the window. "Mrs. Henderson dropped her hand!"

"Her glove," said Mama. "Want to take it back?"

Olivia wanted to. She also didn't. Mrs. Henderson's door was very big, with a knocker shaped like a lion.

"You can come," said Olivia. "But I knock."

Mama held her hand across the street. Olivia picked the glove up off the walk. It was stiff and cold.

At the bottom of the steps Mama stopped. Olivia went up by herself. Three steps. At the top, the lion looked at her.

She knocked once with her fist, very small. Nothing happened. She looked back at Mama.

Mama nodded.

Olivia knocked again, bigger.

The door opened. Mrs. Henderson looked down, and her eyebrows went up. Olivia's words had gone somewhere, so she held up the glove.

"My glove! I looked all over the house for that."

"It was on da walk," said Olivia. "Being cold."

Mrs. Henderson put the glove on. Then she held that hand against her cheek for a moment, the way you hold something that came back.

Olivia came down the three steps much faster than she had gone up.
