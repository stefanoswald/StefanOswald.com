Night 279

The Library Card

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The librarian slid a small white card across the desk. "This one is yours," she said. "It needs your name."

Papa lifted Olivia up so she could reach. She held the pen tight and made her O, and the L, and the rest, going a little downhill. The A had to squeeze in at the very edge.

The librarian didn't fix anything. She put the card in a little machine, and it hummed, and the card came out shiny and warm.

"It's warm!" Olivia held it against her cheek.

"Your card," said the librarian. "Three books today."

Three was hard. Olivia wanted the snow book, and the pig in the hat, and a truck book, and the moose book for Papa.

"You want them all," said Papa. "Three today, and the rest wait."

She picked the snow book and the pig in the hat. Then she held up the moose book and looked at Papa.

Papa did a small growl, right there at the desk.

At the end of the low shelf, Molly Mouse was looking at Olivia's card, her acorn-cap satchel on her back. Olivia held it up so she could see. Molly gave it one serious nod and was gone.

On the walk home, the card rode in Olivia's mitten.
