Night 83

Why Do We Get Older

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Papa carried Theo up the stairs for his nap, and Olivia watched them go all the way up.

Then she went and stood at the kitchen door frame, under her highest mark.

"Why do we only get bigger?"

"Bodies only grow one way," said Mama. "A tiny bit every day."

"I want to go backward."

"Backward to where?"

"To little. Like Fee-oh. Den Papa carries me."

Mama came and got down next to her. "You want to be carried, like Theo," she said. "It's hard, watching him get carried everywhere."

Olivia nodded, hard.

"You can't go backward," said Mama. "But you're not too big to carry."

"I'm too heavy."

"Let's see."

Mama scooped her up. It took a bit of a heave. Olivia's sneakers dangled down past Mama's knees.

"See?" said Mama, only a little out of breath.

They went up the stairs like that, slowly, one step at a time, with Olivia's sneakers bumping Mama's legs.

Olivia counted every stair out loud.
