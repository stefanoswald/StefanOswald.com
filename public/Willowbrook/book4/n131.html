Night 131

Copycat

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

It was the hottest day of July, so Papa put the sprinkler on in the back yard. It went tick, tick, tick across the yard, and then swished back.

Olivia ran through it and shrieked. Theo went through holding Papa's hand and shrieked the same shriek.

Olivia sat down in the wet grass. Theo sat down too.

She patted her head. He patted his head. She stuck out her tongue. He stuck out his tongue, with some lunch still on it.

"Papa! He's copying me!"

"He wants to do everything you do," said Papa.

Then Theo pulled off his sun hat and threw it in the grass. He had been doing that all day. Papa put it back on. Theo pulled it off. Papa put it back on. Theo threw it farther.

Olivia watched. Then she took off her own sun hat, very slowly, so he could see. She put it back on her head. She patted the top, twice.

Theo looked at her. He crawled to his hat, put it on, a little sideways, and patted the top.

Papa stared. "Do that again," he said.

Olivia did it again. So did Theo.

Then Olivia looked at Papa's bare head. "Where's your hat?"

Papa went inside to get it, with a wet child on each hand.
