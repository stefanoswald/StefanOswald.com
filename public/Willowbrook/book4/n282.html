Night 282

The First Snow

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The first snow came in the night. In the morning the mailbox wore a white hat.

Theo had seen snow before, when he was a baby. He did not remember it.

Mama zipped him into his snowsuit. He could hardly bend. He went down the front steps holding her hand, stiff as a little robot.

At the bottom he let go, took three steps, and sat down hard.

Snow went up his sleeves.

"No!" said Theo, with both arms up.

Mama thought he meant he didn't like it. She bent to pick him up.

"No," said Olivia. "He means snow."

"No!" Theo grabbed a fistful and held it up to show them. "No! No!"

"See?" said Olivia. "He sitted in it. He likes it."

"He sat right down in it," said Mama.

Olivia showed him how to stomp. Crunch. Theo stomped once, fell over, and said "No!" again, very pleased.

He tried to kick the snow. He missed and sat down again.

Across the street, Mrs. Henderson waved her shovel. Theo held up his fistful for her to see. "No!"

"Well, I'm sorry to hear it," called Mrs. Henderson.

When his mittens got soaked, Mama carried him in, still holding his snow.
