Night 162

Mateo at the Big School

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Mateo started at the big school, the brick one with the bus. He was four and a half, and he was in the room for the big fours.

That afternoon he came to the fence to tell them about it. Olivia came to hear, and Theo came too, holding on to the fence with both hands and bouncing.

"There's a gym," said Mateo. "It's a whole room just for running."

"On purpose?" said Olivia.

"On purpose. And you carry your own tray."

Olivia thought about carrying a tray. It sounded like the most grown-up thing a person could do.

"Is dere a hamster?"

"There's a fish. His name is Captain."

Theo pointed at Mateo's lunchbox. "Ball?"

"It's a lunchbox," said Mateo.

"Ball," said Theo, sure of it.

Then Theo pulled off his sock and held it out through the fence. Mateo took it. "Thank you." Theo held out his hand for it back. Mateo gave it back. Theo held it out again.

"Will I go dere?" Olivia asked.

"When you're big like me," said Mateo. "I'll show you where the bathroom is. That's the main thing."

The sock went back and forth until supper.
