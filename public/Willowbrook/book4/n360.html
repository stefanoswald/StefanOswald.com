Night 360

Four Tall?

Month 12: Almost Four

Olivia had been four for two whole hours. She went to the kitchen doorframe.

"Measure me. I'm four now."

She stood flat, heels on the wood, no tiptoes. Papa laid the cookbook on her head and slid it back to the frame.

The book stopped at the February line. Right at it.

Olivia stepped away and looked. Then she looked up at the place she had pointed to, way up by Mama's shoulder.

"I didn't grow," she said. Her chin wobbled. "I'm four and I didn't grow."

Papa got down low. "You thought four would make you taller. All at once."

"I DID think dat."

"Growing happens a tiny bit every day," said Mama. "Too tiny to see."

Olivia looked at the line as if it had tricked her.

"Can we check tomorrow?"

"We can check every day," said Papa. "It will be very boring."

Then Theo came and backed up against the frame beside her, arms straight up like a tree, and waited to be measured.

Papa drew a tiny line at his fingers. Olivia laughed a little.

Theo walked away, satisfied.
