Night 84

Why Does Theo Copy Me

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Theo cruised along the couch, one hand over the other, all the way to the end. Then he let go and sat down with a bump.

Olivia clapped.

Theo looked at her. Then he put his hands together and clapped too. Mostly his hands missed each other.

"Mama! He copied me!"

"He watches everything you do," said Mama.

"Why?"

"You're his favorite show."

Olivia tested it. She clapped fast. Theo clapped slow. She clapped over her head. Theo clapped in his lap. She hopped on one foot. Theo looked at her very seriously and bounced on his bottom.

"Dat's not hopping."

"It's his kind," said Mama, folding socks.

Then Mama asked Olivia to get her sneakers from the hall, for the park. At the door Olivia turned around.

"Bye-bye, Fee-oh," she said, and waved.

Theo's hand came up. It opened. It closed. It opened again.

Mama stopped folding. "He waved," she said, and her whole face lit up.

"He waved at me!"

Olivia went out of the room and came back in, so she could leave again. "Bye-bye, Fee-oh."

Theo waved again, a little late, mostly at the lamp. Nobody went for the sneakers.

Mama sat there, still holding a sock.
