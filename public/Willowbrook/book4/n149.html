Night 149

His Me-Do-It Dawns

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Mama held out a spoonful of yogurt to Theo, the way she always did.

Theo did not open his mouth. He grabbed the spoon.

Mama let go.

The spoon went up, and sideways, and into his ear. Yogurt went on his cheek. Some went in his hair. A little bit, at last, went in his mouth.

"Mama, he's doing it wrong," said Olivia, and reached for the spoon.

Theo pulled it away and made a loud noise that meant mine.

"He wants to do it himself," said Mama. "Remember that feeling?"

Olivia remembered. Last year she had wanted to do her own shoes, her own buttons, her own pouring, all of it, all by herself.

Her hands still wanted to help. Then she saw that Mama was sitting on hers.

So Olivia sat on her hands too.

Theo worked. The spoon went into the bowl and out, and toward his mouth, and past it, and toward it again. It took ages. Nobody helped.

When the bowl was empty, mostly onto the tray, Theo held the spoon up high. "Da!"

"You did it," said Olivia, from on top of her hands.

His ear got washed at bath time.
