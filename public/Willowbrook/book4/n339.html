Night 339

What Four Can Do

Month 12: Almost Four

"Write dis," said Olivia. "What Four can do."

Mama found a long strip of paper and a pen, and sat on the edge of the bed.

"One. Four can whistle."

Mama wrote it. Beside it, small, she wrote NOT YET.

"Why not yet?"

"Because I can't whistle either," said Mama. "Yet."

"Two. Four can reach da cookies."

Mama wrote it.

"Three. Four stays up all night. Da whole night."

"Hm," said Mama, and wrote it.

"Four. Four can ride a big bike. Four can read a whole book. Four can go in da deep end."

"Three did the deep end," said Mama.

"Four does it too."

Mama wrote them all down the long paper. Then she added one more line at the bottom and didn't read it out.

"What's dat one?"

"Four can still sit on a lap."

Olivia looked at the list. She couldn't read most of it, but she knew where that line was.

"Write it bigger," she said.

Mama wrote it bigger.

Olivia tried that one right away.
