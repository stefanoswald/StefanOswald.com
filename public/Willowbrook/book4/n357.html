Night 357

The Party

Month 12: Almost Four

The guests came in a crowd, stamping and unzipping, and their boots made a heap by the door. Then the house got loud. Musical cushions. The crown with the hinge. Somebody crying about a turn.

Then Olivia was gone.

Mama found her in the hall, behind the coats, with her hands over her ears. Mama took one slow breath of her own and sat down on the floor.

"It's very loud," said Mama. "It's your party, and it's too much."

Olivia nodded behind the coats.

They sat. The coats smelled like snow.

Then Theo came around the corner at his stiff run, holding his cow by one leg. He pushed it in between the coats, into her lap.

"Moo."

Olivia laughed, a small wet laugh. The cow's bell went ding.

"He bringed me his cow."

"He brought you his best thing," said Mama.

From the kitchen the lights went down, and Miss Rosa called for the birthday girl.

Olivia gave Theo back his cow. Then she came out.

The cake was the sky, dark blue with gold stars, and a little yellow snowplow driving across it. Four candles. Lily blew the fourth one, the way they had planned. Olivia blew the other three, and her wish.

Theo slept under the table with his cow.
