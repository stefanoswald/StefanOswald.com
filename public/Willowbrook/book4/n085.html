Night 85

Why Is Water Wet

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Bath night. Papa sat on the stool beside the tub with his sleeves rolled up.

"Papa. Why is water wet?"

"Because it's water."

"But why is it wet?"

"Wet is what water does when it touches things."

"Is it wet before it touches?"

"I," said Papa. "Hm."

He looked up at the ceiling for help. The ceiling had nothing.

"Wet is how water feels," he tried.

"What if nobody feels it?"

"Okay. Water is wet the way silly is silly."

"Dat's not a why, Papa."

"I know it's not a why."

Olivia giggled until the bubbles shook, and Papa lost the washcloth. He looked in the water. He looked under the bubbles. He was sure it had gone down by her feet.

It was on his shoulder.

"It's on your SHOULDER!"

Papa looked at his shoulder as if it had tricked him on purpose.

Then Olivia answered her own why, up to her chin in bubbles. "If it's not wet, it's air."

Papa thought about that for a while. "I can't beat that," he said.

He pulled the plug, and the drain gurgled. Then he held the towel open wide.
