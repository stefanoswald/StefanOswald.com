Night 285

Grandpa Joe's Reading

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Grandpa Joe was reading the feed store paper in the barn, sitting on an upside-down bucket. Olivia leaned on his knee and watched.

His finger went under the words. His lips moved a little. He went slow, one word and then the next.

"Grandpa. You read with your finger."

"Yep."

"Like me."

Grandpa looked at his finger. "Like you."

"How come you go slow?"

Grandpa thought about it for a while. The Duchess came across the barn floor, stepped over his boot, and kept going.

"Letters came hard," he said. "Always have."

Olivia thought about that. The barn was cold and smelled like hay. Somewhere a hen was talking to herself.

"When I'm big, I'll read to you," said Olivia. "Fast."

Grandpa's mustache moved at the corners.

"Slow's fine," he said.

He held the paper out between them. His finger went back under the words, and Olivia put her finger next to his. They went along together, his finger reading and hers following, all the way down the page.

Over on the hay, the Duchess settled down to sleep.
