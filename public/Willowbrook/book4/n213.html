Night 213

The Real Job List

Month 8: Helping Hands

"I want a real job," said Olivia. "Not a pretend one."

So after supper Mama got the big paper, and Papa got the crayons, and the family made a job list.

Olivia could not write words yet, so she drew. A fern, for watering. A sock, for matching. A spoon, for setting the table. Mama wrote the words underneath in careful letters.

"Why do we need a list?" asked Olivia.

"Because the house needs all of us," said Papa. "Everybody who lives here helps here."

Olivia looked at her brother. "What's Fee-oh's job?"

"He's still little," said Mama. "He'll get one."

Theo reached across the table, took the purple crayon in his fist, and drew a long line under everything.

"Fee-oh! Dat's da LIST!"

"He's signing it," said Papa.

That night Olivia set out the spoons: one for Mama, one for Papa, one for her, and one on Theo's high chair tray, where he could drop it.

Theo dropped it.

The list went up on the fridge under the duck magnet. Before bed, Olivia went down in her pajamas to check that the fern was still there.

It was. So was the purple line.
