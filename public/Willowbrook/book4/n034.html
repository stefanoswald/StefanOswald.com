Night 34

The Waking Test

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

If Baba could walk at night, why not in the day? Olivia meant to catch him at it.

First she pretended to sleep. She shut her eyes, snored a big fake snore, and snapped one eye open.

Baba was sitting right where she had left him.

Next she turned her back, counted to three, and spun around. Same bear. Same sitting.

Then Mateo came over from two porches down with his tool belt, and they built a trap. Mateo tied a jingle bell to a string, and the string to Baba's paw, and set him on the rug. The two of them hid behind the door.

"If he walks, it rings," said Mateo.

They waited. They waited so long that Olivia had to sit down.

Jingle.

"CAUGHT you!"

It was the cat, with one paw on the string and a face that said nothing had happened.

"That counts," said Mateo.

It did not count.

That night in the meadow, Olivia asked him straight. "Why don't you walk in da day?"

Baba thought about it. "In da day I'm busy."

"Doing what?"

"Sitting still. It's harder dan it looks."

Olivia had tried that once, at story time.

She let him sit.
