Night 313

The Long Nights

Month 11: Winter Wonders

By suppertime it was already night. The window over the sink was black, and the kitchen looked back at itself in it.

"Why is it dark at supper?" said Olivia. "It's not bedtime."

"In winter the sun takes a short trip across the sky," said Papa. "It comes up late and goes down early."

"Da sun has short legs."

"Very short legs."

Olivia buttered her bread. "Is da night longer now?"

"Much longer."

"Den do I get more dreams?"

"Same bedtime," said Mama. "Same dreams."

"Den who gets da extra night?"

Mama looked at the window. Then she got up and turned off the kitchen light.

The yard came out of the dark all at once, white and blue, and over the fence hung the moon, bright on the snow.

"Moon!" said Theo, and pointed with his spoon.

"The moon gets it," said Mama. "And the owls."

"Grandpa says the days are getting longer again," said Papa. "One hen step a day."

"A hen step is little," said Olivia.

They finished supper by moonlight. Theo waved his spoon at the moon until his applesauce was gone.
