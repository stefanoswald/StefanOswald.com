Night 337

The Doorframe Mark

Month 12: Almost Four

The kitchen doorframe has pencil lines on it, each with a name and a date in Mama's writing. Olivia's lines climb up one side. Theo's start low on the other.

"Shoes off," said Papa. "Heels on the wood."

Olivia stood flat, no tiptoes. Papa laid the cookbook on her head and drew a line under it. She turned around to look.

It was above last year's line, but not by as much as she wanted.

"At four, I'll be HERE," she said, and reached up, way up, higher than Mama's shoulder.

"We'll measure on your birthday," said Papa, "and see."

Then it was Theo's turn. Theo did not want a turn. Theo wanted to run, and he ran, around the table and back, twice.

"Stand like a tree, Fee-oh!" said Olivia. "Like dis."

She put her arms straight up. Theo put his arms straight up. He backed against the frame, very proud, a tree.

Papa drew the line at the tips of his fingers.

"Is dat fair?" said Olivia.

"It's a tree line," said Mama. "Trees get to count their branches."

Theo ran off before anybody could argue.

Under the new line Papa wrote THEO. Then, smaller, ARMS UP.
