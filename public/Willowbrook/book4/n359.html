Night 359

The Just-Us Morning

Month 12: Almost Four

Before Theo woke on the birthday morning, it was just the three of them. That is the whole point of the just-us morning.

The kitchen was still half dark. Papa made pancakes by the stove light. Mama brought two envelopes to the table.

"One from each of us," said Mama. "Like every year."

Olivia found her name on both. She knew those letters now.

Mama read hers first.

"Dear Olivia. This year you asked me why about everything. Why the moon follows the car. Why Theo eats socks. I liked every one. Love, Mama."

Then Papa read his.

"Dear Olivia. You are four. I still can't whistle. You said you would teach me. I am holding you to it. Love, Papa."

Olivia laughed with syrup on her chin.

On the sill, Bella opened her wings. Mama lifted the window. The first warm morning came in, and Bella went out into it, toward the brook.

After pancakes they went to the letter drawer in the hall. Inside were the letters from her other birthdays. Olivia counted them with her finger. Six. The new two went in on top. Eight.

"When can I read dem?"

"When you can read them yourself," said Papa.

Olivia narrowed her eyes. "Dat's a trick."

"A little bit," said Papa.

Upstairs, Theo started to sing.
