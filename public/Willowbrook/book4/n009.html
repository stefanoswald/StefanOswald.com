Night 9

The Wrong Costume Box

Month 1: Pretend!

Mama and Olivia made a dress-up box out of the old toy chest. In went Mama's worn-out scarves. Papa's old hat. The bee costume, too small now. Sunglasses with one lens missing. A crown off somebody's cake. And the red cape the aunts had mailed for her birthday.

Olivia climbed in up to her knees and dug.

When she came out, she had on the cape, the bee wings, Papa's hat, the one-lens sunglasses, and one rain boot.

"Guess who I am."

"A mail carrier?" said Mama.

"No!"

"A bumblebee going to a wedding?"

"NO."

"A pirate?"

Olivia shook her head so hard the hat fell over her eyes.

"Who, then?"

Olivia pushed the hat back up. "Everybody."

Mama looked at her for a long moment, the whole wonderful heap of her. "Hello, Everybody," she said.

Everybody flapped the cape, buzzed, said "Arr," and clumped off to find her other boot.

The box never shut properly again. Something was always hanging out of it.
