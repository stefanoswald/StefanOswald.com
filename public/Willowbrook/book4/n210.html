Night 210

The Uncaught Ones

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

By the end of September, the windowsill tomatoes had turned red, one by one. Papa ate one like an apple. Mrs. Henderson got two.

All but one. The last tomato stayed hard and green. Olivia turned it to the sun every morning, and it stayed green anyway.

At the lamp that night, she asked. "Mama. Do all da maybes turn?"

"No," said Mama. "Some just stay how they are."

"Like da green one."

"Like the green one."

Olivia thought. "Is Buttons a maybe?"

Mama took her hand. "No. Buttons died."

"His body stopped working."

"It did. That one is just sad."

"Still sad?"

"Still sad, sometimes. We can miss him."

Olivia was quiet. Mama stayed where she was.

"Can da green one go in?"

"It can go in anyway," said Mama.

So in the morning the green circle came off the counter at last, and Mama wrote two words under it: STAYED GREEN. It went into the jar with the others.

It fit just fine.
