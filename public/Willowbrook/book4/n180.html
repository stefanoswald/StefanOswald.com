Night 180

Circle Time at the Lamp

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

"Circle time," said Olivia, from her bed. Mama and Papa sat down on the rug with their legs crossed, which took Papa some doing.

"Who remembers da backpack?"

Mama put up her hand. "Red. With room for Bobby Bear."

"My cubby is next to Lily's," said Olivia. "Who remembers da crying day?"

"You held on to my shirt," said Mama. "Then what did I say?"

"I always come back," said Olivia. "And you did. Every day."

"And you fed Buttons," said Papa.

Olivia was quiet. "Buttons got out. We were statues. Den Buttons died."

"Yes," said Mama. "He's under the maple, with your dandelions."

Olivia sat up. "Who did puzzles with Wren?"

Papa put up his hand. "Me."

"No! ME."

After that it came in a rush: the coat hooks and the door she held, the napkin for Sam, the two mamas, the feather on Wren's shoe, the step at Marcus's party, maybe pizza, Mama's new friends at the fence, and Papa's sandwich song.

"And at the program you said Buttons," said Papa.

"He's in my family," said Olivia.

The teacher lay down and shut her eyes.
