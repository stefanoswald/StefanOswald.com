Night 302

When the Books Go Back

Month 10: Words Are Magic

"Tomorrow the library books go back," said Mama.

Olivia held the snow book tighter. "Not dis one. Dis one is mine."

"It's the library's," said Mama. "It lives there."

"But I love it." Her chin went wobbly. "I don't want it to go."

"You love that book. It's hard to give it back."

Olivia cried a little, into the snow on the cover. Mama kept her hand on Olivia's back and waited.

"Why does it have to go back?"

"So somebody else can have a turn," said Mama. "Somebody's waiting for it."

"Who?"

"I don't know. Somebody who likes snow."

Olivia thought about the somebody. She thought about them waiting and waiting, with no snow book.

"Can I get it again? After?"

"When it comes back, you can take it out again."

Olivia wiped her nose on her sleeve. "Read it one more time."

So Mama read it one more time, slowly, and Olivia said the parts she knew. At the last page she told the book goodnight.

Bobby Bear got to hold it till morning.
