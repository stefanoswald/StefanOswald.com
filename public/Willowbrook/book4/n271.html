Night 271

The Brave Teacher

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

At the park there was a little slide for little kids. It had three steps, and the slide part was shorter than Papa.

Theo toddled straight to it.

Olivia ran after him. "Fee-oh, wait! I teach you." She crouched down by the steps. "It's scary. So you do one step. Just one. Dat's da rule."

Theo climbed the first step. Then the second. Then the third. He did not stop at one. He did not look scared at all.

"Fee-oh! One! I SAID one!"

Papa stood right behind him, one hand near his back. "He's not scared," said Papa.

"Den I'm scared for him," said Olivia. "Somebody has to be."

Theo sat down at the top. He slid, very slowly, the way you slide when you are small and your coat is puffy, and landed at the bottom in Olivia's arms.

"More!" said Theo.

He went up and down the little slide all morning. Olivia caught him at the bottom every single time, whether he needed catching or not.

On the way home Olivia pushed the stroller, slowly, with Theo in it.
