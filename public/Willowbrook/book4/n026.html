Night 26

The Day Everything Was Something Else

Month 1: Pretend!

On Wednesday, nothing stayed only itself. Breakfast was a feast for a king. The stairs were a mountain.

And the hallway was a canyon.

"Dere's a bridge," Olivia said. "A rope one. Very wobbly."

The bridge was the long hall rug. Olivia crossed it with her arms out, and turned around at the far end.

"Now you, Mama."

Mama put one foot on the rug. She pulled it back.

"It's very wobbly," she said. "I'm scared of the canyon."

Olivia came back across. She took both of Mama's hands.

"Don't look down. Look at me."

Mama looked at her.

"One foot," said Olivia.

Mama took one step. The bridge wobbled, because Mama wobbled it.

"Den da other foot."

Step. Wobble. "Ooh." Step. Wobble. "Ooh." Olivia walked backward the whole way, very serious, and did not let go.

At the far end, Mama stepped off onto the safe carpet and sat right down.

"You held on da whole time," Olivia told her.

"I did," said Mama. "You helped."

Mama stayed on the safe side for a while, holding the doorframe.
