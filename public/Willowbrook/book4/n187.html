Night 187

The Sick Thursday Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Rosa called on Thursday morning. Lily had a cold and a pink nose, and she was staying home. Thursday was supposed to be Lily day.

Olivia's face crumpled.

"Maybe!" said Papa, right away, brightly.

"NO MAYBE!" Olivia threw a cushion at the couch. Then another one.

Papa stopped. He got down on the floor, low.

"Too fast," he said. "Sorry. You've been waiting for Lily all week."

"All WEEK." She climbed into his lap and cried the rest of it out.

When she was done, Papa said, "Want my maybe now?"

She thought about it. "Okay."

"Maybe the fort needs fixing before Lily sees it."

They pulled every cushion off the couch. They made walls and a roof and a dish-towel flag. Then Theo walked straight into the east wall. The east wall fell down, and Theo sat down too.

They built it again. Theo walked into it again.

So Olivia made the walls short, Theo-short, with a little door at the bottom just his size.

"You made it low, so it stays up," said Papa.

On Friday Lily came with her nose still pink. She stopped in the doorway.

"It has a FLAG."

She went in through the Theo door.
