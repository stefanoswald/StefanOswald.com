Night 222

The Job Nobody Assigned

Month 8: Helping Hands

Nobody asked Olivia to fix the shoes.

They were in a heap by the front door, the way they always were. Papa's boots were on top of Mama's garden clogs. Olivia's red rain boots were upside down. Theo's sneakers were somewhere underneath.

"Da shoes are fighting," said Olivia.

She pulled them apart. She stood them in a line, biggest to littlest: Papa's, Mama's, hers, Theo's, like a family going for a walk.

Then Theo came, picked up one of his sneakers, and walked away with it.

Olivia got it back. She put it in the line. Theo came and took it again.

"Fee-oh! Put him back!"

Mama watched from the kitchen doorway, drying a pot. She didn't say anything until Olivia had put the sneaker back a third time and sat on it.

"Nobody asked you to do that," said Mama.

Olivia looked up, worried. "Is it wrong?"

"No. You saw the shoes needed a line, and you made one."

When Papa came home, he stopped in the doorway and looked down at the row. He thought Mama had done it. Mama let him think so until supper.

In the morning the line was still there. The cat was asleep in Papa's boot.
