Night 254

The High Branch

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

The climbing oak at Dandelion Park had dropped all its leaves. Now you could see every branch.

Olivia had sat on the low branch all summer. Today she looked at the next one.

"Papa. Da second one."

"You climb," said Papa. "I'll spot."

She stood up on the low branch, holding the trunk. That was one step. She reached up and hooked her mitten over the second branch. That was two. Then her boot found a knot in the bark, and she pulled and wiggled, and there she was on the second branch, arms around the trunk.

"I'm UP."

"You're up," said Papa. "Your foot found the knot."

From up there she could see the duck pond, the kite hill, the brook, and far away on its own hill, the Great Willow.

"Da willow can see me too."

Then she looked at the third branch. It was a long way up, and her knees said so.

"Da third one is a not yet."

"That's fine," said Papa. "Down is the careful part."

She came down backward, the way he'd shown her, one foot and then the other, feeling for the knot. Papa's hands stayed up the whole way, not touching.

At the bottom she patted the oak twice.
