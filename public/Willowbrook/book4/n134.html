Night 134

The Brook Together

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

Papa took them to the brook early, before the heat. The stepping stones are for bigger feet, so they went to the shallows, where the water runs over flat pebbles and only comes up to your ankles.

"Da rule," said Olivia. "Hands on. Always."

"Always," said Papa. "Even in water this shallow."

Papa crouched in the shallows and held Theo under the arms, so only his feet went in. Olivia stood beside them with one hand on Papa's shoulder.

The water was cold. Theo's whole body went stiff, and his eyes went wide.

"It's cold first," Olivia told him. "Dat's just hello."

Theo pulled his feet up out of the water. Then, slowly, he put them back down. Then he kicked. Water flew everywhere, mostly onto Papa.

"Da water's going somewhere," said Olivia. "It doesn't stop."

On the big rock, Freddy Frog pushed back his lily-pad hat. "That's nothing," he said. "I have been to the sea. The sea is much colder."

"No you haven't," said Olivia.

Papa looked around. "Who are you talking to?"

"Freddy. He says he saw da sea."

"Freddy says a lot of things," said Papa.

Theo kicked until Papa was wet to the elbows. Then he stopped, and leaned his head on Papa's arm.

He was done.
