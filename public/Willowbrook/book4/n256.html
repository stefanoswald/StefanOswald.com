Night 256

The Basement Light

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

On the first really cold night of November, Mama needed the box of winter hats from the basement.

"I'll get it," said Olivia. Then she opened the basement door, looked down into the dark, and changed her mind.

"You can take the flashlight," said Mama. "And I'll stand right here at the top."

Olivia switched on the flashlight. She went down one stair and stopped. Then another.

Halfway down, something in the dark went WHUMP, and then a long, low roar.

Olivia froze.

"That's the furnace," Mama called. "It's waking up for winter."

"Furnace," said Olivia, to the dark, so it would know.

At the bottom, the basement smelled like dust and laundry soap. The flashlight found the washing machine, a bicycle pump, Papa's old skis, and a box with HATS written on it.

She tucked the flashlight under her arm and lifted the box with both hands. Now the light pointed mostly at the ceiling.

Up she crawled, one stair at a time, pushing the box ahead of her.

Mama was still in the doorway. "You went all the way down."

"Da furnace is loud," said Olivia. "Loud and warm."

They opened the box on the kitchen floor. On top was her striped hat from last winter.

It didn't fit. It sat on her curls like a bird.
