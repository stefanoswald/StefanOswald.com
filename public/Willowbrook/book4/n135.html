Night 135

The Bear Meets His Match

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

That night, in the dream meadow, Bobby Bear met the cow.

He had seen the cow before, on Theo's dresser. But they had never met properly, and Bobby Bear believed in doing things properly.

He stood up straight. One ear stayed flopped. "Good evening," he said. "I am Bobby Bear."

"Ding," said the cow.

Bobby Bear looked at Olivia. "What did he say?"

"He said hi," said Olivia.

"Ah." Bobby Bear bowed. "Do you like being Fee-oh's cow?"

"Ding."

"Dat means yes," said Olivia.

"Does he kick in his sleep?"

"Ding. Ding. Ding. Ding."

"Dat means a lot," said Olivia.

Bobby Bear nodded, very serious. "Mine kicks too." He looked at Olivia. "Dat's da hardest part."

"Ding," said the cow.

"He agrees."

Then Bobby Bear asked the most important question of all. "What do you do all night?"

The cow thought about it. The meadow grass moved in the warm wind. A long way off, the willow glowed at its edges.

"Ding," said the cow at last.

Bobby Bear looked at the cow, amazed. "Me too," he said.

In the morning, Theo's cow was on the floor. He had been somewhere.
