Night 249

The Shot Day

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Olivia's checkup had a shot in it. Mama told her at breakfast, because in this family nobody gets surprised by a shot.

"Will it hurt?"

"Yes. Like a pinch, for about three seconds."

"Den what?"

"Then it's done, and your body learns to fight a whole sickness."

Olivia thought about that all the way there. "Can I watch it?"

"You can watch or look away. You choose."

At the doctor's she sat on Mama's lap on the crinkly paper, holding Mama's hand. The nurse had a kind face and cold fingers.

"Watch," said Olivia.

She watched. She counted, out loud. "One. Two. Three."

It pinched, and then it stopped.

"Dat's it?"

"That's it," said the nurse.

"Dat was like da Duchess," said Olivia. "A little Duchess."

The Duchess was Grandpa Joe's oldest hen, and she did not like hands in her nest box.

"Da Duchess pecked me in da summer," Olivia told the nurse. "It hurted all morning."

"It hurt all morning?" said Mama. "That was a big peck."

"Dis was a little one."

At home Olivia drew a new picture on the brave list: an arm with a dot on it. Under it Mama wrote, The shot. She watched and counted.

The bandage stayed on until bath time. After the bath, she asked for it back.
