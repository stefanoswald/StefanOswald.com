Night 57

Everybody on the Map

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Olivia's meadow map was still on the kitchen wall, and it needed people.

"Everybody who comes," said Olivia. Mama held the crayon box, and Olivia knelt on a chair and got to work.

First Baba, brown, with one ear down. Then Olivia, with a red bow. "Dat's me." Then the cow, who came out looking a lot like a potato.

"And Franklin?" said Mama.

Olivia thought about how you draw somebody nobody can see. Then she pressed the orange crayon down in one spot and went round and round until it was a warm smudge.

"Dat's where he sits."

Lily's rabbit went near the edge. "He came a different way." Then Theo, tiny, crawling after the cow. Then the moth, up in the corner. "He's nice now."

Olivia leaned back to look. "It needs a sign. Like da town."

Willowbrook has a sign at the edge of town, with a number on it for how many people live there.

"How many live in the meadow?" said Mama.

Olivia counted on her fingers. She ran out and counted Mama's. She ran out again.

"Lots," she said.

So Mama wrote LOTS across the top, in big letters.

Olivia added one yellow dot for Gerald.
