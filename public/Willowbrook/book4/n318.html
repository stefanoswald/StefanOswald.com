Night 318

The Icicle Orchestra

Month 11: Winter Wonders

After the snow, the sun came out hard, and the back porch grew icicles. Little ones hung along the rail like a fringe. Big ones hung from the roof edge, as long as Olivia's arm.

"Not under the big ones," said Papa. "They can drop."

He knocked them down with the broom, from the side, while Olivia watched from the door. They landed in the snow like dropped spoons.

The little ones stayed. Olivia tapped the smallest one with her mitten.

Tink.

She tapped a fatter one. Tonk.

"Papa! Dey sing!"

She went down the whole row. The thin ones sang high. The fat ones sang low.

"Why are da fat ones low?"

"Big things shake slower," said Papa. "Slow shakes make low sounds."

Olivia looked up at him. "You're a big one."

"I sing very low," said Papa, very low, and she giggled and hit the next icicle too hard. It snapped off and dropped in the snow.

"Oops. Dat one quit."

She played the rest softer. Tink, tink, tonk. She found three notes of the hello song, and Papa sang the rest, wrong.

The little ones on the rail kept dripping in the sun, one note at a time.
