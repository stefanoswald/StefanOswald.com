Night 157

The Line Leader Chart

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

By the classroom door hung the job chart. Every job had a picture: a watering can, a light switch, a hamster, a pair of feet. Every child had a clothespin with a name on it, and each morning Miss Dana moved the pins down one.

The feet were line leader. Line leader is the most powerful job in any preschool, and it lasts one day.

Olivia checked the chart every morning with Lily. "Marcus is today. Den Priya. Den me."

On her day, she stood at the front with her chin up. Everybody waited. She led them past the easel, around the snack table, and into the coat hooks.

She stopped. "Where do lines go?"

"Line leaders can ask," said Miss Dana. "Today, outside."

So she led them outside with walking feet, and held the door with her whole body until the last child was through.

The next morning her pin had moved. Wren was on the feet.

"I want line leader every day," she told Papa at dinner. "I only got one day."

"You liked being at the front," said Papa. "The pins keep going round."

Olivia looked at her peas.

The next day she walked in the middle of the line. June held her hand.
