Night 199

The Late Nap Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Theo fell asleep in the car at four o'clock and did not wake up until half past five. At bedtime he was not sleepy at all. He walked laps around the kitchen with his cow, ding, ding, ding, and every time he passed Papa he laughed.

Papa sat down on the kitchen floor. "I'm so tired," he said. "And he's not even a little bit tired."

Olivia sat next to him. She patted his arm and waited.

"Maybe, Papa," she said.

Papa laughed. "Okay. Maybe."

He picked up Theo and the cow, and they all went out to the back step. The sky was going from blue to dark blue. The crickets had started. Theo had never been outside this late in his whole life.

Then one light came out over the trees, small and gold.

Theo pointed with his whole arm. "Up!"

"Dat's Stella," Olivia told him. "She's ours."

Theo waved at her. Bye-bye, open and shut.

"No, Fee-oh. Dat's hello."

He waved again, exactly the same. Stella did not seem to mind.

Theo fell asleep on Papa before the second star.
