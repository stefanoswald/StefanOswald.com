Night 105

The Deep End Line

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Saturday was just-us: Mama and Olivia at the town pool, which had opened that week, with the water still cold enough to make everybody say, "Oh!"

Olivia stood in the shallow end, Mama right beside her. Across the pool, a rope of blue and white floats lay on the water. Past it, the water went dark and deep, and big kids jumped in with their knees tucked.

"What's da rope for?"

"It shows where the deep end starts," said Mama. "Deep water is for swimmers with more skills than you have yet."

"Is it scary over dere?"

"Not scary. Just not yet."

Olivia looked at the rope. "Da rope says not yet."

"When can I go?"

"When your kicking can get you there. Your skills go over the rope first, and then you."

So they worked on kicking. Mama held her under the tummy, and Olivia kicked until the water splashed Mama's face, and Mama said, "Oh!" again. Olivia put her face in and blew bubbles and came up blinking.

"You blew out before you came up," said Mama. "That's the trick."

On the far side, a big kid cannonballed, and a little wave rolled all the way across to them.

"Da splash went over first," said Olivia.

Mama's hands stayed under her tummy.
