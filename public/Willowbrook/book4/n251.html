Night 251

Theo Sweeps

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

After lunch Mama swept the kitchen. Swish, swish, swish. The crumbs went into a pile.

Theo watched from under the table. Then he came out with Olivia's old toy broom, the one with the red handle, and swept too.

Swish. He swept the crumbs out of the pile.

Swish. He swept the cat.

The cat left.

"Fee-oh! Not da CAT!"

Swish, swish. He swept Mama's feet. He swept his own feet, which made him sit down.

"I'm da dustpan," said Olivia. She crouched down low with the dustpan and waited.

Theo swept a raisin at her. It went right past the dustpan and under the fridge.

"Uh-oh," said Theo.

Mama swept the pile back together. Theo swept it apart. Mama swept it together again. Theo swept it apart again, very pleased with himself.

"He's helping," said Olivia.

"He's sweeping," said Mama. "It's not quite the same thing."

In the end Mama swept, Olivia held the dustpan, and Theo swept the hall, where there was nothing at all, for a long time.

"Clean," said Theo, to nobody.

At nap time Papa found him asleep under the kitchen table, still holding the broom.
