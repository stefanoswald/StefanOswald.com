Night 15

Franklin's Birthday

Month 1: Pretend!

"It's Franklin's birthday," Olivia said at breakfast.

Franklin, the invisible guest. Two sugars.

By dinner there was one real cupcake from Miss Rosa's bakery, with one candle in it. Olivia put her birthday crown on the seat of Franklin's chair. She drew him a card with a duck on it, because Franklin likes ducks.

Then Papa pulled out the chair with the crown on it and started to sit down. He thought it was empty.

"PAPA! Dat's Franklin!"

Papa jumped up. "Sorry, Franklin," he said to the chair. "I didn't see you there."

Mama lit the candle, and everybody sang. Theo banged his spoon on his tray and sang too. "Da-da-da-da!"

"Happy birthday, dear Franklin..."

"He's shy about fire," Olivia said, and blew out the candle for him.

"How old is Franklin now?" Mama asked.

Olivia held up three fingers. "A thousand. And three."

Franklin could not eat his cupcake, so Olivia ate it for him, slowly, so he could watch.

The crown stayed on his chair all evening. Papa sat somewhere else.
