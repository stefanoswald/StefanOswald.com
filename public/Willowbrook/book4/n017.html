Night 17

The Pretend Argument

Month 1: Pretend!

Miss Rosa dropped Lily off after lunch, with flour on one cheek. Lily had been three for almost a week, and she said so at the door.

Then both girls made an announcement at the same moment.

"Da floor is water! Sharks!"

"Dis is a castle! I'm da queen!"

"Water."

"Castle."

"WATER!"

"CASTLE!"

Lily grabbed the big cushion for her throne. Olivia grabbed the other end. They pulled. Neither one let go. The cushion slipped and fell on the rug between them, and they both sat down hard, on opposite sides, with their arms crossed.

It was quiet for a while.

Olivia looked at the cushion. It was lying in the middle of the rug. In the middle of the water.

"Dat's an island," she said.

Lily looked at it. "My castle's on dat island."

Olivia thought about this. "Den my sharks guard it."

Lily considered the invisible sharks. "Okay. But dey hafta bow."

So the sharks bowed, and the queen climbed onto her island, and nobody touched the water. They called it Flood Castle.

When Miss Rosa came back for Lily, she had to wait at the door. The floor was water.
