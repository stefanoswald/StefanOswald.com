Night 237

The Top Button

Month 8: Helping Hands

It was a heavy day.

At preschool Olivia slipped off the bottom of the slide and scraped her knee. At pickup Lily took the red shovel and would not give it back. At home Olivia spilled the fern water on her own socks.

"Are you all right?" asked Mama.

"I'm fine," said Olivia, in a voice that was not fine.

At bath time she was fine. At pajama time she was fine, and she wanted to do her own buttons.

The top button would not go in, no matter how she pushed.

"Want help with that one?" asked Mama.

"NO. I'm FINE."

Mama sat down on the couch and waited. She did not reach for the button.

The button still would not go. Olivia's eyes went hot. Then she walked over and stood in front of Mama, holding the button out.

Mama did it. It took one second.

"Da whole day was bad," said Olivia.

"Tell me," said Mama.

So Olivia told it. "First I falled at da slide. Den Lily took da red shovel. Den my socks got wet."

"You fell at the slide, and lost the shovel, and got wet socks," said Mama. "That's a lot for one day."

Olivia climbed onto the couch. She put her head on Mama's arm.
