Night 169

The First Friend Fight at School

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

It started in the house corner.

"I'm da mama," said Olivia, and picked up the baby doll.

"No, I'M da mama," said Lily, and picked up the other end of the baby doll.

Nobody wanted to be the baby. They both pulled, and the doll's arm came off.

"You're not my friend!" said Lily.

"You're not MY friend!" said Olivia.

They didn't talk for the rest of the morning. At snack they sat at different ends of the table. At rest time their mats were side by side, and they both faced the wall.

At pickup, Mama and Rosa found two girls standing apart by the fence with their arms crossed. Rosa raised her eyebrows at Mama. Mama raised hers back.

They got down low.

"What happened?" said Rosa.

"She took da mama," said Olivia.

"She took da baby," said Lily.

"You both wanted to be the mama," said Mama. "And there was only one doll."

Olivia peeked at Lily. Lily was already peeking.

"What could you two do?" said Rosa.

Lily thought. "Two mamas?"

Olivia thought too. "And Fee-oh is da baby. A real one."

"Sorry I pulled," said Lily.

"Sorry I said not friend."

On Saturday there were two mamas in Olivia's kitchen, and one real baby. He ate the pretend soup.
