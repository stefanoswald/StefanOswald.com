Night 303

The World in Words

Month 10: Words Are Magic

Papa carried Theo in to say goodnight, and Theo pointed at everything in the room.

"Dat?"

"Dat's three hundred," said Olivia. "Da O's are mine. Dey're on everything, Fee-oh. Even your cereal."

"Dat?" The thin book on the shelf.

"My book. Da bear lost his whistle."

"And found it on his pillow," said Mama.

"Dat?" Lily's letter, on the nightstand.

"I said da words. Papa wrote dem. I did my name."

"Half the page," said Papa.

"Dat?" The old bear book, a little crooked.

"Mr. Petrov fixed it. And da last page, I can do it." Papa handed it down, and she put a finger under each word. "Goodnight. Little. Bear."

Theo pointed at the dark window. "No!"

"Snow comes down, snow comes slow," said Olivia. "Da end is still lost."

"It might come back," said Mama.

"Dat?" Her library card, by the lamp.

"My card. It came out warm." She thought. "At da diner I said please. I got triangles."

"That's a word for the jar," said Papa.

Theo held up his fist. The T block was in it.

"Dat's your letter," said Olivia.

Theo patted his chest. "Deo."

Papa carried him off to bed. Olivia said goodnight to her two O's.
