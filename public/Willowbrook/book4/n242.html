Night 242

The Paper Hand

Month 8: Helping Hands

Near the end of October, Papa traced around Olivia's hand with a crayon.

"What did this hand do this month?" he asked.

Olivia wiggled her thumb. "Dis one forgot da fern."

"And then?"

"Den it moved da can."

"Where I trip on it," said Papa.

Her pointer finger had raked Mrs. Henderson's leaves and jumped in them. Her middle finger had worn Rosa's apron, held the bread bags open, and matched Papa's giant socks.

"Dis one sat quiet," said Olivia, holding up the next finger. "When Mama was sick."

"The quiet helped the most," said Mama.

"And this little one?" asked Papa.

The pinky had waited on the rug while Theo did his puzzle. "It sat on itself," said Olivia.

"Which one lined up the shoes?"

"All of dem. Nobody asked."

"Were they pretend jobs?"

"No. Real ones."

"How many coins did the hand get?"

"Just da acorn. And I got it back."

"It paid me in naps," said Mama. She touched Olivia's other hand, the one that had held out a button. "And this one let me help."

Olivia laid her real hand on the paper one. It fit.
