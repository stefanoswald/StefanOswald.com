Night 168

What She Teaches Theo About School

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

After school, Olivia played school. Bobby Bear was the class. The cow with the bell was the class. Theo was the class too, but he kept cruising off along the couch.

"Circle time," said Olivia, and she sang the name song. "Bobby Bear. Cow. Fee-oh."

Theo bounced at the end of the couch.

"Line up!" Olivia knelt at the far side of the rug and held out her arms, the way Miss Dana does at the door.

Theo looked at her. He let go of the couch.

For a moment he stood there with nothing to hold. Then he took a step, with his arms up high. And another. And one more.

Then he sat down hard, right in front of her, and laughed.

"MAMA! PAPA!" Olivia shouted. "He walked! To me!"

Mama and Papa came running from the kitchen, Papa still holding a dish towel. Theo pulled up on the couch and did it again for them, two steps this time, and sat down again.

"He wanted to get to you," said Mama.

Olivia held both of Theo's hands and stood him up. "Walking feet, Fee-oh," she told him. "Dat's da rule."

Theo sat down on the cow. Its bell rang.
