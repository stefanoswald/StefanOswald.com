Night 104

The Giant Duck Problem

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

The market had a new side door, and for its opening day it had balloons and a duck.

Not a real duck. This one was as tall as the door, with an orange beak as big as a mailbox and feet like flippers.

Olivia loved ducks. Her first word had been duck. Her doll was named Duck. She took one look at this one and went behind Mama's legs.

"But you love ducks," said Mama, and tugged her hand forward.

Olivia's feet dug in. Her face said no.

Mama stopped and crouched down. "I pulled you. Sorry. A duck that big is strange, and we can watch from right here."

So they watched. The duck did a dance, badly. Then it tripped on its own flipper and said, "Oof," in a boy's voice.

"Ducks don't say oof," said Olivia.

"No, they don't."

Then the duck took off its own head. Underneath was a boy, red in the face, fanning himself. It was a hot day to be a duck.

"Dere's a kid in da duck!"

"There's a kid in the duck," said Mama. "It's a costume, like your bee one."

Olivia stayed behind the legs a while. Then she stepped out, one step, and waved.

The duck waved back, very slowly, so as not to startle anyone.
