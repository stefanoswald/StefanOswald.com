Night 120

Oliver Owl, Who Loves the Dark

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Summer nights come late and leave early. When the sky over Willowbrook was finally dark, the Great Willow's branches glowed faintly gold at the edges.

Up on his branch, Oliver Owl opened his eyes. For an owl, this is morning.

Stella dipped low. "Good morning, Oliver."

"Is it?" said Oliver Owl. He fluffed his feathers and looked down at the town. "Who is still awake down there?"

"Only the fireflies," said Stella. "And a brand-new one-year-old, who had cake."

"Cake?" Oliver Owl thought about this. "On him, or in him?"

"Mostly on," said Stella.

A breeze went through the willow. Down on Maple Lane, a small gold light glowed in one window, low near the floor.

The Sleepy Moon, above them all, hummed a little.

"Do you hear that?" said Oliver Owl.

They listened. Crickets. The brook. A very small snore, somewhere, and a cow bell that rang once.

"Shall we let them sleep?"

Stella twinkled once, for yes.
