Night 259

The Page Turner

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

In November the library lantern was already lit by story hour.

"I need a page turner tonight," said the librarian. "Anybody?"

Nobody moved. Olivia's hand stayed in her lap. Then it went up, before she had quite decided.

She stood at the front, beside the big reader chair, with all the faces looking. The first page was the hardest. Her fingers couldn't find the corner.

Then they found it. She turned it.

"Thank you," said the librarian, and read.

Olivia turned the next page, and the next. It was a book about a pig who wanted to fly.

Then two pages stuck together, and she turned them both. Suddenly the pig was in the sky, and nobody knew how he got there.

A boy on the rug laughed. Olivia's ears went pink. She wanted to sit down, right now.

"Oops," said the librarian. "We skipped the balloons."

Olivia peeled the pages apart and turned back to the balloons. Her hands were a little shaky. She kept going.

When everyone got up, a small gray nose came out from under the reader chair.

"There's another pig book," said Molly Mouse, very softly. "Bottom shelf, by the wall. Don't tell."

Olivia didn't tell. She just went and got it.
