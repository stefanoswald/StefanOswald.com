Night 44

The Rainy Meadow

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

It rained all day in Willowbrook, the soft April kind that turns the grass green overnight. And that night, it rained in the meadow too.

The dream rain was warm. "Like bathwater," Olivia said later. She and Baba ran for the willow and stood under it, where the branches hang down like a curtain.

The glowing fish came up to the top of the brook to sip the drops. Every drop made a ring, and every ring glowed.

Baba stuck his tongue out to catch some rain.

"What does it taste like?"

He thought about it. "Blue."

Olivia stuck her tongue out too. It tasted like water. She did not tell him.

They stood under the willow and didn't say much. The rain came down on the leaves, tick, tick, tick, and the fish kept making rings.

Then Olivia woke up, and it was still raining, the real kind, on the real roof. Tick, tick, tick. She lay there with Baba and listened to it on both sides at once.

"It's raining dere too," she told Baba.

She fell asleep again before it stopped.
