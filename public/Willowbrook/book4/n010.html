Night 10

The Quiet Pretend

Month 1: Pretend!

Not every pretend is loud. On a gray afternoon, Olivia lay down on the rug and curled up small. She tucked in her hands. She tucked in her feet.

"What are you?" Mama asked.

"Shhh. I'm a seed."

"Does the seed need rain?"

A very small nod.

So Mama made rain with her fingertips, on Olivia's back. Pitter, patter. Pitter, patter. Her warm hands, laid flat on the seed, were the sun.

Slowly, slowly, the seed began to grow. One arm crept out for a root. The other reached up. Her knees came, then her back, then her head, lifting. She rose so slowly that the cat walked all the way around her, sniffed her elbow, and left.

At last she was standing, arms out wide, fingers spread.

"I'm a tree now. It took a hundred years."

It had taken about four minutes. Mama did not say so.

"Trees can't walk," Olivia said. "So you bring me water."

Mama brought a cup of water, a real one, with a straw. The tree drank it without moving her branches.

Then the tree stood by the window a long time, swaying a little, watching the rain.
