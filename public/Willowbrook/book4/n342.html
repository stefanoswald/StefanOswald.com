Night 342

The Hand-Me-Down Box

Month 12: Almost Four

While Theo napped, Mama brought down the box of his baby clothes. He had outgrown every single thing in it.

"For June's baby sister," said Mama. "She's getting bigger."

Olivia held up each thing before it went in.

"Dis is da hat. He wore it home."

"He did."

"Dis one he spitted on. Da whole front."

"He spit up on everything," said Mama. "That shirt was brave."

Olivia rolled the tiny socks into pairs, the way she does the family socks. The cat got into the box and lay down on the sleepers.

"Not you," said Olivia, and lifted the cat out.

At the bottom of the pile was the bee costume. It had been Olivia's, and then Theo's, and its antennae had been bent and fixed twice.

Olivia held it and didn't say anything.

"Dat was my bee first."

"It was," said Mama. "It's a hard one to give."

Olivia put the antennae on her own head. They were too small for her now. She took them off and laid the bee on top of the pile, wings up.

The next day they carried the box to June's porch. June's mama held up the bee. June laughed. The baby kicked both feet.
