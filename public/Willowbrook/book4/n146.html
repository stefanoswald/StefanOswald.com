Night 146

The Berry Law

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

The raspberries at Honeywell Farm were ripe, and Grandpa Joe's berry law was in force: half for the pail, half for the picker.

Theo sat on a blanket at the end of the row, away from the prickers. Grandpa brought him the softest berries.

"Fee-oh," said Olivia. "Half for da pail. Watch." She put one berry in the pail. She ate one. "Pail. Me. Pail. Me."

Theo put one berry in the pail. Olivia clapped.

Then he reached in, took it back out, and ate it.

"Fee-oh! Dat was da pail's!"

Theo reached in for another one.

"No! HALF!"

Theo ate that one too. His face was red from his chin to his eyebrows.

Grandpa Joe laughed then, the quiet whole-body kind, with his hat off. "Back in my day," he said, "a raspberry was the size of a hen, and we still couldn't get one to the pail."

Olivia looked into her pail. There were four berries in it. Theo had none left, except on his face.

"New law," she said. "For Fee-oh, da pail gets some."

"How much is some?" asked Grandpa.

"One," said Olivia.

So Theo put in one berry, and Olivia guarded it, and nobody ate it.

It made it all the way home.
