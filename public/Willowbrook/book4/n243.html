Night 243

Frost on the Pumpkins

Month 8: Helping Hands

On the last night of October the frost came early.

It settled on the roofs of Maple Lane. It settled on Mrs. Henderson's raked lawn, and the little feeder on its hook, and the pumpkins on the porches.

One by one the pumpkin lights went out.

The happy, worried pumpkin on Olivia's step went out last.

Up in the Great Willow, Oliver Owl fluffed his feathers against the cold. "Who is still awake?" he asked.

No one answered. Down on Maple Lane the windows were dark, all but one porch light, and then that one went out too.

Stella dipped low over the rooftops. The Sleepy Moon pulled a thin cloud up to its chin.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
