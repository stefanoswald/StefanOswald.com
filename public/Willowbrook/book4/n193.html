Night 193

The Flat Tire Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

The car went thump-a-thump-a-thump on the way home from the store. Mama pulled over and got out to look.

"Flat tire," she said. "We'll walk home, and the garage will come for the car."

"Walk? My legs are too little!" Olivia plopped down on the sidewalk. "I want da CAR."

Mama sat down on the curb beside her. "Your legs are tired just thinking about it."

They sat. A squirrel went by with an acorn in its mouth.

"Maybe," said Mama, and held out her hand.

These were car blocks, the ones they only ever drove past. On foot they were full of things: a cat on a step, a gate painted blue, and a little wooden house on a post, with a glass door, full of books.

"Take one, leave one," Mama read from the sign.

Olivia took one, with a bear on the cover. "Leave one?"

"Next time," said Mama.

Next time was the next morning. Olivia stood at her own shelf for a long while. She did not pick the one with the torn cover. She picked the pig book, the good one.

"That's one you love," said Mama.

Olivia nodded. They walked back, and she put the pig book in the little house and shut the door very gently.
