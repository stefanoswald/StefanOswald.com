Night 263

The Big Wind

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

On Sunday the wind came through Willowbrook like it was late for something.

Papa and Olivia went up the kite hill without a kite. "Just to feel it," said Papa.

At the top the wind grabbed Olivia's coat and shoved. She took a step backward without meaning to.

"It's pushing me!"

"It's a strong one," said Papa, and took hold of her hood. "Want to push back?"

Olivia leaned into the wind. It held her up, like a big hand on her front. She leaned more, and it held her more.

"Papa! I'm leaning on da AIR!"

Then the wind took Papa's hat.

It went up, and over, and rolled down the hill like a wheel. Papa ran after it, and Olivia ran after Papa, both of them yelling, "HAT!" It stopped in the bare hedge at the bottom.

Papa put it back on and tied it down with his scarf, under his chin. He looked like somebody's grandma.

"You look silly," said Olivia.

"I look windproof."

They climbed back up and leaned some more. Olivia leaned with her arms out wide, until her cheeks were cold and her nose was running.

On the way home the wind was behind them. It pushed them all the way to Maple Lane, and they let it.
