Night 182

Spilled Milk, Yellow Bus

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Mateo rode the big yellow bus to school now. It came down Maple Lane at eight o'clock every morning, and at eight o'clock every morning Olivia was already at preschool, hanging up her bag. She had never once seen it.

On Tuesday she reached across the table for the toast, and her cup went over. Milk ran across the table, into her lap, and down into one shoe.

"My shoe!" She was crying before the dripping stopped. "Now we're late!"

Mama crouched down with a towel. "Oh no. Wet and late, and you like being first."

Olivia cried into the green cardigan. Mama held on and let her. Then came dry socks, the other shoes, and the walk to preschool, late.

"We're so late," Olivia said at the front gate.

"Maybe," said Mama.

A rumble came around the corner. The bus was huge and yellow and closer than she had ever seen it. It stopped two porches down with a long sigh of brakes, and Mateo climbed on. His face popped up in a window. He waved with both hands.

The driver waved too.

Olivia waved so hard her bow slipped. "Like da snowplow!"

"Just like the snowplow," said Mama.

The milk shoe dried on the step all day.
