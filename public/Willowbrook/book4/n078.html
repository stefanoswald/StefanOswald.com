Night 78

Why Is the Willow Gold

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

The Sunday willow walk. At the top of the hill the Great Willow was doing what it does at dusk, glowing faintly gold all through its hanging branches.

Olivia had seen it glow her whole life. Tonight she asked.

"Why does da willow glow?"

Mama and Papa looked at the tree. The tree went on glowing.

"Nobody knows," said Mama. "Grandpa's grandpa asked that same question, on this same hill."

"Nobody?"

"Nobody," said Papa. "Not the library. Not Grandpa. Not me."

Olivia frowned at the tree, which did not explain itself.

"What do you think it is?" asked Papa.

She thought. "Da willow's night-light."

Papa nodded slowly, as if he were putting that somewhere safe.

"Can we find out?"

"We could try," said Mama. "Or we could just look at it."

Olivia looked from Mama to the tree. Then she sat down in the grass.

So they looked. A breeze went through the branches, and the gold moved with them, like water. Down in town the library lantern came on.

They stayed until the gold went out.
