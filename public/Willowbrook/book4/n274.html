Night 274

The Letter That Followed Her Home

Month 10: Words Are Magic

It started with the bakery sign.

Olivia stopped on the step and pointed. "Mama. Dat's my O."

"That's your O," said Mama.

Miss Dana had shown her on Monday. O was the first letter of Olivia. It was round, like a mouth saying oh.

On the way home the O kept turning up. It was on the post office door. It was on a license plate, twice. It was right in the middle of the STOP sign.

"Mama! Dere's one on da STOP!"

"There is."

Across the street from their house, Mrs. Henderson had hung a wreath on her front door. Olivia stood on the sidewalk and looked at it for a long time.

"Dat whole thing is an O."

"It's a big one," said Mama.

"Was it always on everything?"

"It was always there. Now you know it."

At home, Theo was in his high chair with a pile of little round cereal. He picked one up with two fingers and put it in his mouth.

Olivia stared at him.

"Fee-oh is eating my letter."

Theo ate another one.
