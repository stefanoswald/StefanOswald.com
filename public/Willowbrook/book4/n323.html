Night 323

Valentines for Everybody

Month 11: Winter Wonders

Miss Dana sent a list home in Olivia's backpack, with every name in the class on it. Valentine's Day was not until February. But if you brought cards, you brought one for everybody.

"Dat's a lot of everybody," said Olivia.

So they started early, a few each night, at the kitchen table.

Mama folded the red paper in half and drew half a heart on the fold. Olivia cut along the line, slowly, and opened it.

"It was half! Now it's a whole one!"

She did it again. It kept working.

Theo wanted in. He got the fat red crayon.

"Mine!" he said, and scribbled on Marcus's card, and then on Priya's.

"Fee-oh! You're wrecking dem!"

Mama slid the finished ones out of reach. "He wants to help too," she said. "Any ideas?"

Olivia looked at the scribbles. They were very red. "Everybody gets one from Fee-oh," she said. "On da back."

So everybody did. Olivia put her O on the front of each card, and on some of them the whole OLIVIA, with the L facing whichever way it liked. Wren's front had only the heart, because Wren liked things quiet.

Theo's own card, red all over, went on the fridge under the duck magnet.
