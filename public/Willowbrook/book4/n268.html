Night 268

The Splinter

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

Olivia got a splinter from the back step railing. It was in the fat part of her hand, and it hurt.

"We can take it out with the tweezers," said Mama. "A pinch, about ten seconds."

"Or?"

"Or we leave it, and it works itself out in a few days. But it might get sore."

"Out," said Olivia. "But I watch."

At the kitchen table, under the lamp, Olivia laid her hand on a towel.

Then Mama went for the splinter, quick, before Olivia was ready.

"NO!" Olivia snatched her hand back. "I wasn't READY!"

Mama put the tweezers down. "You're right. I went too fast."

Olivia held her hand to her chest, eyes full.

"I'm sorry," said Mama. "You tell me when."

They waited. The clock ticked.

Then Olivia put her hand back on the towel, flat. "Now."

She watched. It pinched, and she said, "Ow," and then it was out.

"Dat little thing? It bited me so hard."

"It bit hard for something so small," said Mama.

Mama washed the spot with soap and water and put a bandage on it. Olivia wanted to keep the splinter, so it went in an envelope marked SPLINTER.

It went in the kitchen drawer, with the rubber bands.
