Night 75

Why Do We Have Names

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

"Why do we got names?"

"So we can call each other," said Mama. "If I shout 'Hey, kid!' at the park, twenty kids look up."

"If I shout 'Olivia,'" said Papa, "one kid looks up. Watch."

"Olivia," said Papa. Olivia looked.

"Theo," said Papa. In the high chair, Theo turned his whole head and looked at Papa, with a squashed pea on his chin.

"Spaghetti," said Papa. Nobody looked.

"He knows his name!"

"He does," said Mama.

"Why am I Olivia?"

"We said it out loud before you were born, and you kicked," said Mama. "So we picked it."

"I picked it?"

"You helped."

Olivia said her whole name, first and last, the way they practice it in the car. Then she said it again, louder, to make sure it still worked.

"Why is Franklin Franklin?"

"You'd have to ask Franklin," said Papa.

Olivia turned to the empty chair and asked. She listened. "It's a secret."

After supper, with the dishes running, Papa said "Theo" very softly from the sink.

Theo looked.
