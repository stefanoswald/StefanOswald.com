Night 93

The Little Gold Light

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Now that the dark had a name, the room needed its night settings, and Olivia wanted to choose them herself.

They tried the big lamp first. It was like trying to sleep in the kitchen.

"Too bright. Da day can't rest."

They tried no light at all. Olivia lasted to a count of five.

"Too much dark. For now."

"For now is fine," said Mama. "Lots of people like a little light. Me too, in hotels."

Then Mama took something small out of her pocket and plugged it in low, by the bed. It glowed gold, about the size of a strawberry.

"Like Della came inside!"

Then the door. Wide open was too loud, because the kitchen radio came in. Shut was too shut. They tried it open one finger wide, and the hall light lay down on the floor in a thin gold stripe, and the house sounds came through small.

"Dat one," said Olivia. "One finger."

She made it a rule, out loud, so it would count. "Rule twelve. Door stays one finger."

"Deal," said Mama, and they shook on it with one finger each.

The nightlight made its tiny hum, the way they do. Bobby Bear was tucked in on the left side.

The door stayed open one finger.
