Night 267

Wren's Loud Day

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

At circle, Miss Dana held up a green leaf and a yellow one. "Who knows why leaves change color?"

Lots of hands went up. Olivia's went up. Lily's went up so high she was nearly standing.

Wren's didn't, but Wren knew. Wren had told Olivia all about it at the puzzle table.

Olivia looked over. Wren's hand came up off Wren's knee, a little. Then it went back down. Then it came up a little again.

Olivia put her own hand down. She scooted over on the rug until her knee touched Wren's knee.

"I'm right here," she whispered.

Wren's hand went up, halfway, and stayed.

"Wren?" said Miss Dana.

The answer came out so quietly that the whole circle leaned in to hear it.

"Da green goes away. Da yellow was hiding."

"That's just what happens," said Miss Dana.

For a second nobody said anything. Then Sam said, "Whoa." And Wren smiled the whole-face smile, the one Olivia had only seen at the puzzle table.

At supper Olivia told it in order. "First Wren's hand went up. Den down. Den I sat close. Den it went up and stayed."

"What did you do?" asked Papa.

"I did my knee," said Olivia.

Under the table, Papa put his knee against hers.
