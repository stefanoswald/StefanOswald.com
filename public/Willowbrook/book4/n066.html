Night 66

The Why Nobody Could Answer

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

All week Olivia carried one question around, and nobody could answer it.

"Where was I before?"

"Before what, love?" said Mama.

"Before I got borned."

"Before you were born, you weren't here yet," said Mama. "We were waiting for you."

"But where WAS I?"

Papa said that was the part nobody knows, not grown-ups, not doctors, not even Grandpa Joe.

So on Sunday, at dusk, she took the question up the hill to the Great Willow. Papa waited on the bench halfway down.

Oliver Owl was on his low branch. Children see him plainly and hear every word he says. Grown-ups see an owl.

"Oliver. Where was I before?"

The owl blinked his slow blink. He only ever speaks in questions.

"Whoo was waiting for you?" he asked.

"Mama and Papa. And Grandpa."

"And what did they get ready?"

She thought. "My crib. My red headband." She thought some more. "My name."

"Hoo," said the owl. "So was there a place for you, before you came?"

"A me-shaped place," said Olivia.

The owl shut his eyes.

She walked back down to the bench. "Da owl asked me questions."

"He does that," said Papa, who had heard only hooting.

Behind them the willow was going gold.
