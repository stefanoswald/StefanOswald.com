Night 173

The Class Learns Maybe

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The class was going to Honeywell Farm, where Olivia's grandpa lives. Everybody had their shoes on. Everybody had a paper bag for eggs.

Then the little yellow bus made a noise like a sneeze, out at the curb, and would not go.

Marcus cried. June cried. Sam lay down flat on the floor, which is what Sam does.

Miss Dana sat down with them. "You wanted the farm so much. This is disappointing."

She waited. Nobody got up. Olivia leaned over to Lily.

"Maybe da bus is tired," she said.

Miss Dana heard. "Maybe? What does maybe mean at your house?"

Olivia thought about it. "It means we don't know yet."

After that, everybody wanted to say it.

"Maybe pizza!" said Marcus.

"Maybe a dinosaur!" said Sam, from the floor.

"Maybe," said Miss Dana, "we could make a farm right here."

So they did. The blocks were the barn. Lily was the farmer, in a paper hat. Olivia was the Duchess, and pecked everybody's shoes.

On Thursday the bus was fixed, and they went to the real farm. Grandpa Joe let every child hold an egg, and then they all washed their hands at the pump.

That night, when Papa said it was time for bed, Olivia said, "Maybe."

Papa carried her up anyway.
