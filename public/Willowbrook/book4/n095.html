Night 95

The Storm Fear Returns

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

The thunder fear had been quiet for a long time. After a hot June day, it came back full size.

The flash turned the whole room white, and the boom rattled the window.

Olivia was out of bed before the boom was done. Mama was already at the door, and Papa came behind her in one sock.

"That's a big one," said Mama, kneeling. "You're scared. I'm right here."

Olivia knew how to count a storm. You count from the flash to the boom, and a big number means far away. The flash came. "One..."

BOOM.

"Dere's no numbers left!"

"It's right over us tonight," said Papa. "It will move along."

They sat on the rug by her bed, all three of them, with Bobby Bear in the middle. Mama's hand was on Olivia's back, going up and down with her breathing.

Papa sang the rain song and got it wrong. "Rain, rain, go to... Spain?"

"No! Go AWAY, Papa!"

The next flash came. Olivia counted. "One. Two."

Boom. A smaller one.

"It's walking away," said Mama.

After that it grumbled away over the farm, and the rain was only rain. Olivia got back into bed. Mama sat on the edge of it.

Papa was asleep first, on the rug, in one sock.
