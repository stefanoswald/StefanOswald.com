Night 188

The Too-Small Sweater Maybe

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

The first cool morning came, and Mama found the old star sweater at the bottom of the drawer. The little one, with two stars, from when Olivia was two.

Olivia pulled it on. Her head got stuck in the middle. Her arms came out short, and the stars went sideways.

"It shrinked!"

"It feels small because you grew so big," said Mama, and helped her wriggle out.

Olivia held it against her chest. "But it's my star one." Her eyes filled up. "Da aunts made it."

"They did. You love that one."

Mama sat on the bottom stair, and Olivia leaned on her, and they looked at the two stars for a while.

"Maybe," said Mama.

Theo came down the hall just then, going nowhere in particular. He took the sweater off the stair and put it on his head.

Mama helped him the rest of the way in. It came down to his knees. The sleeves hung past his hands and flopped when he walked, and he walked everywhere, flop, flop.

"Fee-oh has elephant arms!"

Theo flapped them at the cat. The cat left. Olivia sat down on the stair, giggling.

"It's still mine," she said. "He's just borrowing."

"Just borrowing," said Mama.

Olivia rolled his sleeves up, three times each.
