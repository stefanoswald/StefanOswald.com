Night 171

The Rest Time Truce

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The hardest part of preschool, Olivia said, was rest time.

"Everybody lies on mats. For a hundred hours."

"Forty minutes," said Papa.

"A hundred hours. On da floor."

Olivia said she did not nap anymore, and had not for a long time, and she was very firm about it.

Miss Dana had a plan for children who didn't sleep. They could rest with one bear and one book, as long as they stayed on their mats and kept quiet.

So Olivia lay on her mat with Bobby Bear. She read her book twice. There was a crack in the ceiling shaped like a river.

"Your body rests," said Mama, when she heard. "Your head can go anywhere it likes."

On Monday her head built a castle on the ceiling river. On Tuesday the castle got a bridge to Lily's mat. On Wednesday it got a boat, and a moat, and a door just Bobby Bear's size.

"I builded a castle at rest," she said. "With my eyes open."

"You built it lying down?" said Papa. "That's a lot of building."

On Thursday, Miss Dana said at pickup, Olivia rested so hard she woke up at snack.

"I did NOT sleep," said Olivia. "I was building."

There was a mat crease on her cheek.
