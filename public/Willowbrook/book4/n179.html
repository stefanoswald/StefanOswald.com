Night 179

The End-of-Summer Sing

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

One warm evening near the end of summer, the whole preschool sang in Dandelion Park. There were three classes, a hundred blankets, and the ducks, who had not been invited and came anyway.

Olivia's class sang the summer song. There was a part where you waved your arms like the sun coming up, a part where you crouched down like the sun going to bed, and a part nobody could remember.

Papa sang along from the blanket. He sang it wrong on purpose.

"The sun goes down, the sandwich hums."

"PAPA," said Olivia, right from the row. "It's da MOON hums."

Everybody near the blanket laughed, and so did Miss Dana.

The song ended with everybody crouched down low. The sky went pink, and then gray. A few fireflies came up out of the grass.

Theo was asleep before they got to the car. Olivia was asleep in her car seat before the end of the lane, with grass on her knees.

Papa carried her up the stairs. "Good sing?" he whispered.

With her eyes shut, from his shoulder, she said, "Da moon hums."
