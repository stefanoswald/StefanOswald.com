Night 280

The Three-Word Page

Month 10: Words Are Magic

The bear book ends the same way every night. The little bear is in bed with his button, and at the bottom of the page there are three words.

Mama always reads them. Tonight Olivia put her hand flat on the page first.

"I do dis one."

Mama let go of the book.

Olivia put one finger under the first word. "Good." She moved it. "Night." She moved it. "Lit." She moved it. "Tle." She moved it again, off the words and onto the white. "Bear."

She frowned at her finger. "Da words ran out."

Mama waited.

Olivia went back to the start. This time she said "Goodnight" all in one breath, and her finger stayed put. Then she moved it. "Little." Then she moved it. "Bear."

Her finger landed on the last word just as she said it.

She looked up at Mama.

"Goodnight, little bear," said Mama, the way she says it every night.

Papa was in the doorway. He had seen the whole thing. He didn't say anything either. He just leaned there a while longer than usual.

Olivia did it again, for Bobby Bear. Then she closed the book herself.
