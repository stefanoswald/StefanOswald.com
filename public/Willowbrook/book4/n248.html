Night 248

The Words Went Away

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

On Friday the class was singing for the families, and Olivia had one line to say by herself: "The leaves came down, and the geese flew south."

She practiced on the footstool with a wooden spoon for a microphone. Mama and Papa were the audience. The cat was the audience too, until the second time, when it left.

Papa clapped before the end.

"Not yet, Papa! I'm not DONE."

"Sorry. I got excited about the geese."

When Friday came, the room was full of grown-ups in coats. Olivia stood up. She looked at all the faces, and her words went away.

Nothing came. Miss Dana waited. Then Lily said the line for her, loud, and the song started, and Olivia sang with everybody.

At home she lay on the rug with her face in it. "My words went away."

Mama lay down beside her. "That's a hard feeling. You practiced so much."

"Why did dey go?"

"Lots of faces can scare words off," said Papa. "Mine went away at my own wedding."

Olivia turned her head. "Did you get married?"

"Mama said my part for me. It still counted."

Olivia thought. "Next time I look at one face."

"Whose?"

"Yours. Not da whole room."

The wooden spoon stayed on the footstool.
