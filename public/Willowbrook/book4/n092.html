Night 92

The Shadow That Moved

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Something slid across the wall by the dresser. It stopped. Then it slid again.

"Papa! Something moved!"

"It's nothing, sweetheart. Go to sleep," Papa called from the hall. He had an armful of laundry, and he kept walking.

Olivia pulled the blanket over her head. Under there it was hot and very quiet.

Then Papa's feet came back, without the laundry, and his face came around the door. "I said nothing without even looking. Sorry. Show me."

She pointed, from under the blanket. They watched the wall together. Nothing. Nothing. Then the shape slid, long and gray, and stopped.

"Dere! See it?"

"I see it. Now watch the window with me."

A car turned at the corner. Its lights swept across the curtain, and the curtain's shadow swept across the wall.

"Da curtain did it?"

"The curtain and the car, together."

Papa wiggled the curtain. The shadow wiggled. Then Olivia climbed out and wiggled it herself, and the shadow did whatever she said. It slid. It danced. It bowed.

"I'm doing da spooky!"

When the next car came, she was ready for it. Papa set the lamp where she could reach it, and she lay back down.

One hand stayed up, ready.
