Night 30

What Went Back in the Box

Month 1: Pretend!

Everything was going back in the dress-up box, and Papa was helping. He got every single thing wrong on purpose.

He held up the wooden spoon. "Remember when this was a horse?"

"NO. A dragon. He rawrs at jam."

He held up the red cape. "And you flew to Grandpa's in this."

"It doesn't fly! Only when I run."

"What did you do, when it didn't?"

"I was sad. Den I runned."

"You ran the whole yard," Papa said. "It flapped behind you like a flag."

He held up the paper crown. "Whose birthday? Mine?"

"FRANKLIN'S. He's a thousand and three."

Mr. Socko went on Papa's hand. "Does the drain take socks?" he croaked.

"Only water fits," Olivia told him.

"And the bear in your closet?"

"I sent him home. Mama came too."

Some things were not in the box, but they remembered them anyway. Duck's mama, who always came back. Theo crawling to his cow, and riding in the truck. Circle time, and Mama waiting for her raisin.

Baba did not go in the box. Olivia held him close.

"He walks in my dreams," she said.

She took him up to bed.
