Night 277

The Skipped Page

Month 10: Words Are Magic

It had been a long day, and Mama was reading the bear book with her eyes half shut.

"The little bear looked under the bed," she read, and turned two pages at once. "And there was his button!"

Olivia sat straight up. "No."

"No?"

"You skipped da chair."

"What chair?" said Mama.

"He looks under da chair. Den in da boot. Den under da bed. Den da button." Olivia held up a finger for each one.

Every parent who has tried this has been caught.

Mama turned back two pages. There was the chair. There was the boot.

"You caught me," said Mama. "I skipped, and I'm sorry."

"How come?"

"I'm sleepy. Let's do it right."

Olivia put her finger on the chair page, under the words. "It says it right dere. Every time."

So Mama read the chair page, and the boot page, slowly. Olivia's finger went along under the words, a little ahead of her.

At the button page they said it together.

Then Mama closed the book and yawned a very big yawn. Bobby Bear had fallen over on his face.
