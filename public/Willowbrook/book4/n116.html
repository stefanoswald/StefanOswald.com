Night 116

When the Big Kid Was Scared

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Mateo was four, a whole year bigger, and he could do everything. On Saturday he was riding in the little kids' bike race at the summer fair, with a number pinned to his shirt.

But on Thursday, at the fence, he didn't look it.

"What if I fall?" he said. "In front of everybody?"

Olivia and Lily looked at each other. Mateo was the big kid.

"My papa got scared," said Olivia. "On da ladder."

"Your papa?"

"His tummy flipped. He went up anyway."

"I falled off two times," said Lily. "I got back on."

The little folded brave list had been in Olivia's pocket since the sleepover. She took it out and held it a moment. Then she held it out.

"You can borrow it. Till Saturday."

Mateo unfolded it: a shadow, a storm with a lump, a tall duck, a ladder going off the top.

On Saturday, Mateo fell once, at the turn, and got back up, and finished with his helmet on crooked.

On Sunday the list came back, a little bent. At the bottom was a new drawing: a bike, upside down, and a very big M.

"You let him borrow your list," said Papa. "That was a hard one to hand over."

Olivia folded it up small again.
