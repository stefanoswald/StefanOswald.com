Night 177

The First Rain at School

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Wren saw it first. In the middle of circle time she stood up and pointed at the window without a word.

Rain. The first rain since school started. Big warm drops tapped on the glass, and then they came faster.

Everybody ran to the window. Circle time was over, and Miss Dana did not even try to save it.

"Boots on," she said. "We're going out."

Everybody had rain boots in their cubby. Everybody but Olivia.

She stood in the doorway in her sandals and watched the others stomp. Her mouth went small. Miss Dana stood with her under the roof, and they held out their hands to catch the drops.

At pickup she told Mama. "I didn't have boots. I had to watch."

Mama stopped on the sidewalk. "I forgot your boots. I'm sorry."

Olivia looked down at her sandals.

Mama looked at the big puddle by the gate. It was a very good puddle.

"Want to try it in sandals?"

They splashed through every puddle on the four blocks home, both of them, Mama in her good shoes. It took forty minutes.

Olivia's sandals squished on every stair.
