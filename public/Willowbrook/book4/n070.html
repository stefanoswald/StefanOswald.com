Night 70

The Why That Was Really a Worry

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

On Tuesday an ambulance went past the park with its siren going. Olivia held her ears until it turned the corner.

On Wednesday she asked, "Why do ambulances go fast?"

"So the help gets there quick," said Papa.

On Thursday it was, "Why do people need ambulances?"

"When somebody is hurt or very sick, it takes them to the doctors," said Mama.

On Friday, at bedtime, with the lamp on and Bobby Bear tucked under her chin, she asked the real one.

"Do mamas go in ambulances?"

Mama sat down on the bed. "I think you've been worried since Tuesday," she said. "Worried about me."

Olivia nodded into the bear.

"I'm not sick," said Mama. "I'm right here, sitting on your blanket."

"Da siren was so loud."

"It's loud so the cars move over," said Mama. "Loud means help is hurrying."

Olivia thought about that. "Can you do it quiet?"

Mama did the siren very small. "Wee-oo. Wee-oo."

Olivia did it smaller. Mama did it smaller still, until it was mostly air.

"Wee-oo," whispered Olivia, hardly at all.

Mama stayed. After a while, Olivia's hand let go of the bear's ear.
