Night 61

Why Is the Sky Blue

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Theo was napping at home with Mama. It was just-us time, Papa and Olivia flat on their backs in the new May grass, with the whole sky on top of them.

"Papa. Why is da sky blue?"

Papa had been waiting three years for that one. "Sunlight has every color mixed up in it," he said. "When it hits the air, the blue bounces around the most."

"Da sky is bounced?"

"The sky is bounced."

"Off da air?"

"Off the air. Everywhere you look is bounced blue."

She looked everywhere. A bee went over them, not interested in any of it.

"Den why is sunset orange?"

"At sunset the light comes the long way, sideways," said Papa. "The blue bounces off before it gets here, and the orange is what's left."

"Da orange is da leftovers."

"The leftover light."

"I like da leftovers best."

So does most of Maple Lane, which comes out onto its porches every evening in May for exactly that.

They lay there until the grass had printed its lines on their arms. A cloud took its time going over.
