Night 32

The Second Dream

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

The meadow came back.

It was greener than last time, April green, and a brook ran through it that Olivia had never seen before. The fish in the brook glowed like little lanterns.

Baba walked beside her on the bank, on his own two feet, his flopped ear bouncing.

"Da fish know me," he said. "Dat one's Gerald."

"Which one?"

"All of dem are Gerald."

Olivia looked at the fish. They did not look like Geralds.

Baba picked up a flat stone. "Watch. Bears are good at skipping."

He threw it. It went plop, once, and sank.

"Nine," said Baba.

"Dat was one."

"Nine is what I meant."

She threw hers. It went plop too. They laughed until the edges of the dream began to wobble, which is what dreams do near morning.

"Come back tomorrow?" she said.

"I'm always here," said Baba. "Dream me in."

She woke with her arms around him. His eyes were glass again, and his feet were still. She looked at him for a long time.

"I saw you," she whispered.

She kept her arms where they were.
