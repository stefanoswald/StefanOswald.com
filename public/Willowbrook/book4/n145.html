Night 145

Grandpa's Two-Kid Sunday

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

On Sunday, Mama and Papa went out for breakfast by themselves, and Olivia and Theo went to the farm.

Grandpa Joe met the car. "Mornin'."

He carried Theo on one arm and the egg basket on the other. The Duchess was waiting at the henhouse, looking annoyed.

Olivia knew about eggs. She reached under the straw and found one, warm and brown, and set it in the basket.

Grandpa set Theo down against his knee. He took an egg out of the basket and put it in Theo's hand.

"He'll drop it," said Olivia.

"Yep," said Grandpa.

Theo looked at the egg. He squeezed it. He held it up high, to show everybody. Then he dropped it.

"Uh-oh," said Theo.

Most of the egg was on his shoe.

"I TOLD you," said Olivia.

Grandpa wiped the shoe with a handful of straw. Then he took another egg from the basket and put it in Theo's hand.

Olivia stared at him. "Why?"

Grandpa thought about it. "How else?"

Theo held the second egg as if it were asleep. He held it against his cheek. Then he gave it to Olivia, and she put it in the basket, very gently, next to hers.

Afterward, everybody washed their hands at the pump, even Grandpa.

The Duchess sat down on the rest.
