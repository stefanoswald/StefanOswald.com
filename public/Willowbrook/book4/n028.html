Night 28

The Pretend Rest Day

Month 1: Pretend!

One day there was no pretend at all. Nobody planned it.

Olivia dug in the real garden, in the real mud, for nothing. She ate a snack that was only a snack. She built a tower out of blocks, and it was a tower. She napped without being a bear or a seed.

After her nap she found a worm on the wet path. She watched it stretch and bunch up, stretch and bunch up. It did not turn into anything. It was a worm.

"No games today?" Mama asked.

"Da games are resting," Olivia said. "Dey get tired too."

Mama nodded and did not ask anything else.

Then, at the edge of the garden, Olivia saw something purple, no bigger than her thumb, poking up through last year's leaves.

"Mama. What's dat?"

"A crocus. The first one this spring."

"Why it's purple?"

"So the bees can find it," Mama said.

Olivia crouched down close. She did not say it was a cup, or a hat for a mouse. She just looked.

Mama crouched down too. They looked at it together until their knees were cold.
