Night 111

The Solo Sleepover, Night

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

Miss Rosa's house smelled like cinnamon. There had been a bath with too many bubbles, and one cookie each. "The last one," said Miss Rosa, and then there was another one, which was also the last one.

It was still light when the girls got into bed. Miss Rosa plugged in the gold nightlight where Olivia pointed.

Lily talked about ponies, and then, in the middle of a word, she was asleep.

The window went blue, then dark. Olivia stayed awake. A clock she didn't know ticked. A dog somewhere barked twice.

Her chin started to wobble. "Miss Rosa?"

Miss Rosa came right in. She had been listening for that.

"I want my mama."

Miss Rosa sat on the edge of the bed. "You miss your mama. Of course you do. Do you want to call her, or do you want me to stay?"

"You stay," said Olivia.

So Miss Rosa stayed and hummed the bread song, the one with no words.

Olivia held Bobby Bear and Grandpa's stone and looked at the little gold light. "Dat's mine," she whispered. "From home."

"It looks nice here," said Miss Rosa.

The clock ticked. Lily snored, a tiny snore, like a kitten. Olivia's hand on the stone went loose.

Miss Rosa stayed a while longer anyway.
