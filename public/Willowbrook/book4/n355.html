Night 355

Grandpa's Almanac Entry

Month 12: Almost Four

Grandpa Joe keeps an almanac. The weather goes in the front. The family goes in the back.

He opened it on the farm kitchen table to the page with Olivia's name at the top. There was one line for every year, in his slow square writing, and one line empty.

"Now?" said Olivia.

"Yep."

He wrote. He writes one letter at a time, with the tip of his tongue in the corner of his mouth. Olivia watched every letter go down.

It took a long time. The clock ticked. The Duchess looked in at the window and went away again.

"What does it say?"

Grandpa turned the book around and read it to her, slow.

"Brook ice going out. Sunflowers up on the sill. Carried the egg basket both ways herself. Two eggs broke. Carried it anyway."

Olivia looked at the line. "You put in da broken ones."

"Yep."

"Why?"

Grandpa thought about it. "True," he said.

He gave her the pencil. At the end of the line, in big letters, she wrote OLIVIA. The V went a little uphill.

Grandpa nodded once.
