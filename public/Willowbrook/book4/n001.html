Night 1

The Spoon That Was a Dragon

Month 1: Pretend!

The morning after she turned three, Olivia held the wooden spoon over her head at breakfast and found out it was not a spoon at all.

"Rawr! Dis is a dragon. He flies over da jam!"

The dragon swooped around the toast. He skimmed the rim of Papa's cup. He landed on the butter dish to rest his wings.

Mama moved the butter knife out of his way. "Is he a friendly dragon?"

"He's shy," Olivia said. "He only rawrs at jam."

"Could he pass the salt?" Papa asked.

"Papa. Dragons don't got hands."

So Papa offered him a crumb instead. The dragon took it very politely and flew it straight into Olivia's mouth. Dragons, it turns out, eat through whoever is holding them.

"He's full," Olivia said, with her cheeks full too.

At lunch the dragon was a spoon again. Mostly. He stirred the soup and said nothing.

But every so often that week, Olivia opened the spoon drawer and put her ear close. From the back came a very small rawr.
