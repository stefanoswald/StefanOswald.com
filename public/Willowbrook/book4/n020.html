Night 20

The Cape Does Nothing

Month 1: Pretend!

Olivia had worn the red cape every day for three weeks. On Friday she had a question.

"Papa. Can I fly in dis?"

"Let's find out," said Papa.

They went out to the back porch. Olivia climbed onto the bottom step, which is the step for jumping. She bent her knees. She held the cape out wide.

"One, two, three!"

She jumped. The cape flew out behind her. Olivia came straight down, thump, both feet on the ground.

She looked at the cape. She climbed up and jumped again. Thump.

"Da cape doesn't fly."

Her face went crumply. She had been so sure.

Papa sat down on the step. "You really wanted to fly."

Olivia nodded and leaned on his knee.

"Why it doesn't work?"

"People are too heavy for capes," Papa said. "Capes are light, so they fly when you run."

Olivia wiped her nose on her sleeve. Then she ran across the yard as fast as she could, and the cape flew out straight behind her, flapping.

"It's flying! Da cape flies! Not me!"

She ran until the cape was tired. At last she sat down next to Papa, and the cape sat down too.
