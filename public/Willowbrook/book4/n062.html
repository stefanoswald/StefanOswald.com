Night 62

Why Do Worms Come Out in Rain

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Warm rain on Maple Lane, the kind that smells like wet dirt and lilacs. The worms were out all over the sidewalk.

Olivia crouched down. "Why do worms come up?"

Mama crouched too, raincoat and all. "They live in tunnels in the dirt. When the rain fills the tunnels, they come up for air."

"Dey can't breathe down dere?"

"Not when it's full of water. So they travel while everything is wet."

One worm was crossing the entire sidewalk, very slowly, toward nothing Olivia could see.

"Where is he going?"

"Somewhere softer, I think," said Mama.

Olivia set her boot down right beside him, carefully, like a wall. The worm touched the boot, thought about it, and went the long way around.

"He goed around my boot!"

"He went all the way around," said Mama. "I don't think he wanted to climb a boot today."

Olivia stood up. "Rule nine," she said. "Look down on rain days."

They walked home looking down, stepping over and around, while the rain ticked on their hoods. Home took twice as long.
