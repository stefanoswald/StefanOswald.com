Night 291

How to Spell a Whistle

Month 10: Words Are Magic

It was snowing too hard to go out, so Papa and Olivia collected sounds instead.

Olivia shut her eyes and listened. Papa held the marker.

The kettle was starting. "Ssssss," said Olivia. Papa wrote it on the whiteboard.

The radiator. "Tonk. Tonk." Papa wrote that too.

Snow slid off the roof and landed outside the window. "Whumpf!" Papa wrote it with a very big W.

Down the hall, Theo found a pot and a spoon. "Bang," said Olivia. "Bang bang bang bang bang." Papa ran out of room.

"Now you whistle," said Olivia. "I'll tell you how it goes."

Papa puckered up and whistled. It came out the way it always comes out.

Olivia opened her eyes. "F," she said. "Lots of F."

Papa wrote FFFFFF. He looked at it. "That's not how it goes."

"Dat IS how it goes."

Papa could not argue with that.

"Do your laugh now," said Olivia, and shut her eyes again.

Papa laughed, the big one that shakes the table. Olivia listened all the way to the end of it.

"Dat one doesn't go in letters," she said.

"No?"

"It's too big."

Papa didn't write anything for that one.

The whistle stayed on the board all week.
