Night 224

The Book Box Keeper

Month 8: Helping Hands

At the corner of Maple Lane stood a little box on a post, with a glass door and a sign: TAKE ONE, LEAVE ONE.

Olivia had taken books from it all summer. Today she was leaving one.

Not the duck book. Not the moon book. At last she took down her color book, the one with the red balloon.

"You can pick a different one," said Mama.

"No. Dis one."

"That's a hard one to give," said Mama.

At the box Olivia stood on her toes and slid it in with the cover facing out, so it could see.

Then they sat on the bench across the street and waited. A dog looked, but dogs don't read.

"Nobody wants it," said Olivia. Her mouth went wobbly.

"You wanted someone to love it right away," said Mama. She put an arm around her and waited.

After a while Mama said, "Maybe someone comes tomorrow."

The next day the color book was gone. A truck book stood in its place.

On the way home, a small boy on a bench was reading the color book to his stuffed dog. He held it upside down, very seriously.

Olivia did not tell him it was hers. She walked past very slowly.
