Night 330

The First Warm Wind

Month 11: Winter Wonders

On the last warm evening of the thaw, Olivia and Papa sat on the front porch after supper with their coats open, which felt like getting away with something.

Then the wind turned. It came up Maple Lane from the south, soft, and pushed her curls around.

"Papa. Da wind isn't biting."

"That's a south wind," said Papa. "It's been somewhere warm."

Olivia sniffed. The air smelled wet and brown, like the bottom of a flowerpot.

"What's dat smell?"

"That's the ground. When it thaws, it smells."

She breathed it in again. "It's da undersmell."

Papa looked at her over his glasses. "Is that a word?"

"It is now."

Papa wrote it on a slip of paper for the word jar. Then Olivia asked the other thing.

"Is winter over?"

"Not yet," said Papa. "The cold comes back tomorrow."

"Oh." Her shoulders went down.

"You were hoping for spring," said Papa.

"A little."

They sat a while longer in the soft wind anyway. Across the lane, Mrs. Henderson's wind chime rang for the first time in weeks. Then, slowly, it stopped.
