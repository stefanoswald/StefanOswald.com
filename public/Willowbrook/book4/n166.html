Night 166

The Sick Day

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

Olivia woke up with a cold. Her nose ran, her voice was scratchy, and Mama said she had to stay home and rest.

"But it's Marcus's line leader day. I won't see da pin move."

She lay on the couch under the thin blanket and watched the clock she couldn't read yet.

"Is it circle now?"

"Could be," said Mama.

"Is it easel now?"

"Could be."

Theo cruised along the couch and put his cow on her tummy. Then he took it back. Then he put it on again.

"School is going without me," said Olivia, and her voice went thin.

Mama sat down by her feet. "You're missing your class."

At three o'clock there was a knock. It was Lily, with Rosa waving from the sidewalk.

Lily held out a painting. "Dis is for you. It's a dog." Then she held out a card. It had everybody's names on it, and some scribbles that were names too.

"Everybody drawed on it," said Lily. "Wren made a puzzle piece."

Olivia held the card with both hands and looked at every scribble.

"Da school missed me."

She held the card all afternoon.
