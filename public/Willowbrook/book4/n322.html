Night 322

The Blanket Fort Library

Month 11: Winter Wonders

The week it was too cold to go out, Olivia opened a library under the dining table.

Blankets made the walls. Clothespins held the roof on. The shoe rack was the shelf. Every book in the house went on it, sorted by color, because the blue ones looked nice together.

Olivia sat at the desk, which was a shoe box, and made library cards. Mama's card had a flower. Papa's had glasses. Theo's had a cow.

"Shh," she told everybody who came in. "Da books are listening."

Theo came in the most. He carried one book in, clear across the rug, and carried it out again. He chewed the corner of his card, and it still worked.

On the third night, Papa needed the table for supper. He pulled the blankets off in one big swoop.

"My LIBRARY!" Olivia burst into tears.

Papa stopped, blanket in his arms, and knelt down.

"I took it down without asking," he said. "I'm sorry. That was your library."

"It was open," said Olivia.

"It was open. Where can it go?"

They carried it to the corner by the couch, shelf and shoe box and all. It was smaller there. Bobby Bear still had the best seat.

The next morning, the cat had moved into the returns basket.
