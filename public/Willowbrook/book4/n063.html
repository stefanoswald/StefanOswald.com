Night 63

Why Does Grandpa Have Wrinkles

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

On Sunday, Grandpa Joe's rocker was going slow on the farm porch, and the lilac by the steps was all the way purple.

Olivia climbed up beside him and studied his hand on the armrest.

"Grandpa. Why do you got wrinkles?"

Grandpa Joe didn't answer right away. He never does.

He laid his hand flat on his knee, next to hers. Hers was smooth. His had lines running every which way, like a field after the plow.

He made a fist, slowly. The lines bunched up. He opened it. They lay back down.

Olivia made a fist. Nothing bunched. She opened it and checked, and made another one to be sure.

"Do dey hurt?"

He shook his head.

She reached up and touched the lines beside his eye. "What about dese ones?"

Grandpa Joe looked at her, and those lines went deeper, because he was smiling.

"Oh," said Olivia. "Dose are smiling ones."

He winked.

After a while he said, "Sun." The rocker went back and forth. "And laughing."

Olivia scrunched up her whole face and laughed at nothing, to get some started.

Under the steps, a hen settled.
