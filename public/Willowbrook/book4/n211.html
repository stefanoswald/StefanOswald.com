Night 211

What Came Out of the Jar

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Papa carried the Maybe jar up to bed, and Olivia tipped it out on the quilt. The drawings came out folded and crumpled, and one had been chewed, which was Theo.

She held up the first one. "Da bus. I was late, and it waved."

"Your shoe was full of milk," said Papa.

"Mr. Socko on da post." She held up the next one and gave Papa a look. "Dis is Papa's too-fast one."

Papa looked at the ceiling. "I said sorry."

"You did."

"Here's Theo in the puddle," said Mama. "What did he say?"

"Nothing. He just jumped in."

There was the fort with the flag, and the pig book, and the moose with no growl. "Wren's party went yes, no, yes."

"Marcus's bridge is at school."

Then the green circle. STAYED GREEN. Olivia smoothed it flat on her knee. "Dat one stays in."

"What about the Spill Garden?" said Papa.

"We don't know dat one yet." She yawned. "In da spring."

"And who said maybe to me on the kitchen floor?"

"Me."

They put the drawings back in, one at a time. The lid went on with a small clink.
