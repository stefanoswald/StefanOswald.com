Night 18

The Sad Game

Month 1: Pretend!

That week, Duck the doll played the same game over and over. Duck's mama went away. Duck cried. Duck's mama came back. Away. Crying. Back. Again and again.

Mama watched for a while. Then she sat down on the rug and talked to the doll, not to the girl.

"Duck, your mama keeps leaving. How is that?"

Duck answered in a high doll voice. "Duck is sad. Da mama goes, da baby cries."

"Which baby?"

"Fee-oh. When you go in da kitchen. He cries and cries."

Every time Mama left the room, Theo cried. Olivia had heard every time.

"He does cry," Mama said. "He thinks I'm gone."

"Why he think dat?" asked Duck.

"He's too little to know I come back. He doesn't know it yet."

Duck thought about that for a long time.

Then Duck's mama went away again. This time, when Duck cried, Olivia bent down and whispered in her ear. "She's coming back. Wait."

Duck's mama came back.

That evening, Mama went to get the laundry, and Theo started to cry on the rug. Olivia sat down beside him.

"She's coming back, Fee-oh. Wait."

Theo cried anyway. Then Mama came around the corner with the basket, and his whole face changed.

"See?" Olivia told him.
