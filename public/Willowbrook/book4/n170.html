Night 170

The Chime at Home

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

At school, Miss Dana never shouted "cleanup." She rang a little chime, and everybody changed what they were doing.

"Can we have a chime?" Olivia asked. "At home?"

Mama found a small brass bell at the market. It went on the low kitchen shelf, and Olivia made the rules.

"One ding means get ready. Two dings means change."

Theo pointed at the bell every time he went past it. "Ba!" That meant ball, or bell, or yes, depending.

Cleanup came with the bell, and dinner, and once Papa started the dishes because Theo rang it by accident.

Then came a long, loud evening. There were blocks everywhere. Theo was crying. Papa stepped on a block, and then on another block.

He went to the shelf and rang the bell hard. DING DING DING. "Cleanup! Now!"

Everybody went still. Theo cried louder.

"Dat was a mad ding," said Olivia.

Papa stood there holding the bell. "I need a slow breath," he said, and took one. Then he sat down on the floor, and on a block, and moved the block.

"You're right," he said. "I rang it mad. I'm sorry."

Olivia rang it once, softly. They picked up blocks together. Theo put one in the basket and took it out.

The bell went back on its shelf.
