Night 326

Snowman Snow

Month 11: Winter Wonders

"Snowman snow," said Papa.

The thaw had made the snow heavy and wet, so it stuck to itself. There wasn't much of it left. When Olivia rolled a ball across the yard, it picked up leaves and a twig and left a stripe of brown grass behind it. The snowman came out small, about as tall as Theo.

Theo knew what it was. "Man!"

The head fell off. Theo laughed and laughed. "Uh-oh!"

Papa put the head back. It fell off again. Theo laughed harder.

"It needs a stick inside," said Olivia. She pushed one into the middle, and Papa set the head on it, and this time it stayed.

The carrot was busy on the kitchen windowsill, so the snowman got a pine cone nose. Theo pulled off his red mitten, put it on the snowman's head, and patted it down.

Then he looked at Olivia. "Melt?"

"Yes," said Olivia. "When it's warm."

Theo looked at the snowman. He patted it once more. Then he took his mitten back, because his hand was cold.

By supper the snowman was leaning a little, like somebody listening.
