Night 181

Stella Over the School

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The last warm night of summer settled over Willowbrook. Gold light gathered in the branches of the Great Willow.

Stella drifted low, lower than usual, over a small roof by the maple tree.

"What's down there?" asked Oliver Owl.

"The preschool," said Stella. "Nobody sleeps there. But the cubbies are full."

A sweater on a hook. One rain boot. A paper sunflower crown with a bent petal. A clothespin with Olivia's name on it, waiting for its turn at the feet.

Under the maple, the grass was growing over a small place with dandelions on it.

"And in the morning?" asked the owl.

"In the morning they all come back," said Stella.

The Sleepy Moon slid a little lower, to see.

Down on Maple Lane, a red backpack waited by a door, and the cat was in it again.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
