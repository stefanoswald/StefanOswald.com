Night 158

The Snack Trade

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

At snack time Lily had apple slices, and Olivia had crackers, the little fish kind.

Olivia liked apples best. Lily liked crackers best.

"Trade?" said Lily.

"Trade," said Olivia.

They pushed their napkins across the table at the same time. At first it was very quiet and very crunchy.

Then Olivia watched Lily eat a fish cracker, tail first. Then another one.

"I want my crackers back."

"No trade-backs," said Lily, with her mouth full.

Olivia looked over at Miss Dana, and Miss Dana came and folded herself onto a tiny chair.

"You traded them, and now you miss them," said Miss Dana. "That's a hard feeling."

"I want dem back."

"I know. Tomorrow you can choose again."

Olivia ate an apple slice with a very sad face. It was a good apple slice. Being sad about it took some work.

The next day she traded four crackers for four apple slices, and kept one cracker in her pocket, just in case.

It came home a little bent.
