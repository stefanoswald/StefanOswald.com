Night 76

Why Can't We See the Wind

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

A gusty day on kite hill. Olivia's hair kept blowing into her mouth.

"Why can't we see da wind?"

"Air is invisible, even when it moves," said Papa. "But we can see what it pushes."

They looked. The grass bent over in long silver waves. Dandelion seeds went sailing by like tiny umbrellas. The flag on the library pointed one way, hard.

"It's pushing my hair!"

Then the kite. Papa held it up, and Olivia ran with the string, and the kite bumped along the grass behind her on its face.

"It's broken!"

"It's not broken," said Papa. "The wind was behind you. Try running toward the pond."

She ran toward the pond. The kite lifted, wobbled, and flopped.

Papa whistled for the wind. It was a terrible whistle, mostly air and one squeak. The wind came anyway, probably for its own reasons.

Olivia ran toward the pond again. This time the kite caught. It went up, and up, and pulled.

"It's pulling me!"

"That's the wind holding it up," said Papa, and put his hand over hers on the string.

The kite hung up there, very small and red, and shook.

The string tugged, and she tugged back.
