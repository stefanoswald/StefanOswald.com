Night 270

The Scared-of-Nothing Night

Month 9: Brave Is Scared Plus One Step

At bedtime Olivia made an announcement. "I'm not scared of anything now. Not da dark. Not da basement. Not thunder."

"That's a lot of nots," said Mama, and turned out the lamp.

The gold nightlight glowed by the door. Outside, the wind got up. The bare branch by the window moved, and its shadow reached across the ceiling like long fingers.

Tap. Tap-tap. The branch touched the glass.

Olivia's heart went fast. She pulled the blanket up to her nose.

"Mama." Her voice was small. "I'm scared of da branch."

Mama came back and sat on the bed.

"But I'm not scared of anything," said Olivia, and she started to cry. "I SAID."

"Scared comes back sometimes," said Mama. "Even after a brave month."

"Does yours?"

"Mine does. When I have to sing in front of people."

Olivia thought about that. She looked up at the shadow fingers, waving.

"What is it, really?" said Mama.

"Da branch. Da wind is shaking it."

"What could we do?"

"You stay. For a little."

So Mama stayed. She put her hand flat on the blanket, on Olivia's middle, and they listened together. Olivia's breathing went slow under Mama's hand.

The branch kept tapping. After a while, Olivia didn't hear it.
