Night 74

Why Does the Moon Follow the Car

Month 3: Why? Why? Why?

Driving home from the farm in the last of the light, Olivia was buckled into her car seat, and the moon came along.

"Da moon is following us," she said. "Lily told me."

Out the window the moon kept up with the car exactly. Trees went whipping past. The moon didn't.

"Watch the fence posts," said Papa. "They're close, so they zoom by."

They zoomed by.

"Now the hills. They're far, so they go slow."

The hills went slow.

"Now da moon."

"The moon is so far away it hardly moves at all," said Papa. "That's why it looks like it's coming with us."

"How far?"

"If you drove there and never stopped, it would take months."

"Months? In da car?"

"Months."

"With snacks?"

"A lot of snacks," said Papa.

Olivia watched the moon. It looked close enough to touch.

"Lily says da moon likes us."

"Could be," said Papa. "Nobody's ever asked it."

The moon kept pace past the barn and the pond and the long field, and it turned onto Maple Lane right behind them.

It was waiting over the driveway.
