Night 25

Miss Olivia's Circle

Month 1: Pretend!

Mateo went to school in the mornings now, with a backpack. So Olivia had a school too.

The cubbies were the bottom shelf of the bookcase. Every stuffed animal got one. Baba's had a bear drawn on it, so he would know.

"Everybody sit criss-cross," said Teacher Olivia. "Now we sing da hello song."

The hello song was names, to a tune that wobbled. Hello, Baba. Hello, Duck. Hello, cow. Hello, Franklin. It took a while.

Mama came to school too. She sat in the circle between Duck and the cow, with her knees up by her ears.

"Teacher, can we have snack?"

"You hafta raise your hand."

Mama raised her hand. "Teacher, can we have snack?"

"In a minute," said Teacher Olivia, and went on singing.

Mama recognized that minute. She had said it herself, many times. She looked at the clock.

Then Baba called out without raising his paw. Olivia did his voice and then her own.

"Baba. It's Duck's turn. You wait."

Baba waited. Duck talked about her hat. The cow said moo, which was allowed.

Snack came nine minutes later. It was pretend raisins for the class, and one real raisin for Mama, who had waited longest.

Mama ate it very slowly, to make it last.
