Night 178

Seeds on the Fence Post

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

The sunflowers at Honeywell Farm had grown so heavy they hung their heads. Grandpa Joe cut one off with his pocketknife. It was bigger than Olivia's face.

He sat down on the bench by the fence and laid it across both their laps. He showed her once how to push a seed out with her thumb. Then he didn't say anything.

They pushed out seeds. The seeds went into Grandpa's hat.

"Buttons liked seeds," said Olivia.

Grandpa nodded.

"Buttons died."

"I heard."

Olivia pushed out three more seeds.

"Is he still dead?"

"Yes."

"For always?"

"For always."

Her eyes got full. Grandpa put his big hand flat on her back and left it there.

After a while she took a pinch of seeds out of the hat and put them on top of the fence post.

"For da birds."

The Duchess came strutting along the fence and ate every one.

Olivia laughed, a wet laugh. Grandpa put out another pinch. The Duchess ate those too.

The third pinch, a chickadee got first.
