Night 94

What Baba Does All Night

Month 4: The Dark Is Just the Day Resting

In the dream meadow, the grass came up to Olivia's middle and up to Bobby Bear's ears.

"Baba. What do you do all night?"

Bobby Bear stood up straighter. One ear stayed flopped. "I work."

"What work?"

"First I hold still. Dat is very hard. Sometimes my nose itches for a hundred years."

"Den what?"

"Den I watch da door."

"For what?"

He thought about it for a long time. "Mostly for da door."

Olivia fell over in the grass, laughing.

"Also I keep your elbow warm," said Bobby Bear. "And I listen to da house. Da house says tick. Da pipes say glug. I say nothing. Dat is my part."

"Do you ever sleep?"

"Never," said Bobby Bear, and yawned the biggest yawn in the meadow.

They walked up the hill to where the willow hung down, gold at the edges. Bobby Bear said he would watch the meadow now. He lay down in the grass to do it, very still, with both eyes shut.

In the morning he was under her chin, right where she had left him. His nose did not itch.

She checked.
