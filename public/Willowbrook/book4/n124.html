Night 124

The Slow Walk, Slower

Month 5: Little Brother Tags Along

After supper, when the heat let go a little, they walked to the corner. Theo walked too, holding Mama's two fingers.

It took a long time. Every crack in the sidewalk had to be stepped over, and then stepped back over, to be sure. Every dandelion had to be picked and handed to somebody. One leaf had to be sat down next to.

"Fee-oh, come ON," said Olivia. She had walked to the corner a hundred times.

"He's never walked to this corner before," said Mama. "It's all new down at his height."

Two porches down, Mateo was on his steps with a hammer and a board. He waved.

Theo waved back. He waved the way he always did, with his palm turned in, as if he were waving at his own face.

"He's waving at himself," said Mateo.

"No, Fee-oh. Like dis." Olivia turned his hand around. Theo turned it back and waved at himself again, very pleased.

"He does it wrong."

"That's how waves start," said Mama. "They turn around later."

Olivia looked at her own hand. Then she waved at Mateo the Theo way, palm in. Mateo did it back.

At the corner, the first firefly blinked on in the hedge. Theo waved at it, backward.
