Night 336

Papa's Birthday Questions

Month 12: Almost Four

Papa came to bedtime with a fat notebook and a pencil.

"Birthday questions," he said. "The same ones every year, starting now."

Olivia sat up straight.

"Favorite color?"

"Red. And gold. Red-gold."

"Favorite food?"

"Pancakes. With da syrup in da holes."

"Best friend?"

"Lily. And Bobby Bear. And Fee-oh, sometimes."

Papa wrote it all down. "What do you want to be when you grow up?"

"A snowplow."

Papa laughed out loud.

Olivia's face went still. "I mean it."

Papa stopped laughing. "You meant it, and I laughed. I'm sorry."

Olivia nodded. "Da plow. Not da driver. Da part dat pushes."

Papa wrote PLOW in big letters and underlined it.

"What's scary right now?"

"Da dark, a little. Not da deep end."

"Last one. What do you want to tell Four?"

Olivia thought hard. Then she leaned over the notebook, close, so it could hear.

"Hi, Four. I'm coming."

Papa wrote that down exactly.
