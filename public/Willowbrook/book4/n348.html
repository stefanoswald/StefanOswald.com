Night 348

At Rosa's Counter

Month 12: Almost Four

The bakery was closed, which meant it was open just for them. Miss Rosa had flour on one cheek and a pencil in her hand.

"The sky, like always," she said, and drew a big circle on the parchment. "Now what goes on top?"

"A dragon," said Olivia.

Rosa drew a small dragon.

"And da Duchess. And a snowplow. And Bella. And a castle."

Rosa stopped drawing. "One special thing, sweetheart. It's a cake, not a parade."

Lily leaned in. "A dog."

"It's not your cake," said Olivia.

"A small dog."

Olivia thought very hard. The dragon was good. The Duchess was good. The castle was very good.

"Da snowplow," she said. "A little yellow one. Driving in da sky."

Rosa rubbed out the dragon and drew a tiny plow, pushing through the stars.

"Why a snowplow?" said Lily.

"It's from da Maybe story. It waved."

Rosa reached into the case. "Here. The last cookie."

She gave them each one. Then she found another last cookie for herself.

Olivia went home with flour on her cheek.
