Night 153

Visit Day

Month 6: Preschool, Turns, and Shares

A week before school, the preschool had a visit day, so everybody could see the room before anybody had to stay in it.

Miss Dana met them at the door. She had a ring of keys and a voice like a library.

Olivia stood behind Mama's leg and asked her questions from there.

"Where does my backpack go?"

"On your hook, in your cubby," said Miss Dana.

"Where is da potty?"

"Through the blue door."

"Can Bobby Bear come?"

"He can wait on the high shelf with the other bears."

Then Olivia saw a name over a cubby. Mama read it out. It was hers, and right beside it was another one. LILY.

Lily was already standing under it. "I checked first," she said.

In the corner, in a cage on a low table, a hamster was stuffing sunflower seeds into his cheeks.

"That's Buttons," said Miss Dana.

Olivia came out from behind Mama's leg. Buttons put in one more seed, and one more, until his face was twice as wide.

"He's saving dem," said Olivia. "For later."

On the way out, she patted her cubby.
