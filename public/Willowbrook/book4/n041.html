Night 41

Introducing Baba to Franklin

Month 2: Bobby Bear Wakes Up

Tea was at four, at the little table by the window, and the Sunday secret came out of the cupboard. It was lemon cookies.

Olivia set out three cups. One for her. One for Baba. One for Franklin, who takes two sugars and has never been seen by anyone.

"Baba, dis is Franklin. Franklin, dis is Baba."

She listened to them both.

"Franklin says you're very soft. Baba says Franklin is very quiet."

Papa came in looking for his glasses. "Is this a tea party? Can I come?"

"Yes. Sit dere."

Papa sat down in the empty chair.

"PAPA! You're on Franklin!"

Papa shot up so fast his knee hit the table and all the cups jumped. "Oh no. Franklin, I'm so sorry. I thought that chair was empty."

Olivia listened to the chair. "He says you're heavy."

"That's fair," said Papa, and he sat on the floor.

Olivia poured the air tea. Papa drank his in one gulp and said it was very good air. Baba did not drink his. Franklin drank all of his.

Then one lemon cookie was gone. Olivia looked at Franklin's plate. Then she looked at Papa's beard.

"Papa. Dat was Franklin's."

"He offered," said Papa.

Olivia listened to the chair for a moment. "He says he didn't."

So Papa gave Franklin his own cookie.
