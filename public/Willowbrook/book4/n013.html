Night 13

The Invisible Door

Month 1: Pretend!

This game needed nothing at all. Olivia drew a door in the air with one finger, a rectangle with careful corners. She turned an invisible knob. Click. She stepped through.

"Where are you now?" Mama asked.

"Da snow land. Brrr. You hafta do da door."

Mama drew the door. She turned the knob. Click. She stepped through and started shivering at once. In the snow land, shivering is not optional.

"Dere's a bear," Olivia said. "A wild one. He's friendly."

"Does he know Baba?"

"He knows Baba. He don't know you."

Mama waved at the bear, to be polite.

The snow was deep, so they walked with big high steps. The wind was cold, so they huddled. Mama's teeth chattered on purpose, and Olivia laughed so hard she had to sit down in the snow, which was the rug.

After that there were other doors. A jungle door. An under-da-sea door, where you had to hold your breath.

When Mama came back from the snow land, she blew on her hands.

Olivia held them and rubbed. They were cold for real.
