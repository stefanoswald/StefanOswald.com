Night 191

The Maybe Jar

Month 7: The Maybe Lessons

Papa scraped out the last of the jam, washed the jar, and was carrying it to the recycling when Olivia stopped him.

"Wait. I need dat."

"For what?"

She frowned. She had been trying to keep all the maybes in her head, and they kept falling out. "I forgot da crayon one. For a whole day."

So the jam jar became the Maybe jar. Mama wrote MAYBE on a strip of tape and stuck it on the front. Olivia drew a duck on the tape, for no reason.

Then she drew the maybes on little papers. A yellow box with a face in the window was the bus, and Mateo. A sock on a stick was Mr. Socko on the post. There was a fort with a flag, and Grandpa asleep in the big chair. Under each one, Papa wrote a few words, so nobody would forget which was which.

Theo's job was putting them in. He put them in. He also took them out. Both jobs seemed important to him.

Then Olivia started a green circle and stopped.

"Not dat one. Dey're still thinking."

The green circle waited on the counter by itself.

After supper the jar sat on the windowsill, next to the tomatoes.
