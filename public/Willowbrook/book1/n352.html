Night 352

The Dusk Feed

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The dusk feed was the one that had lasted. Every other part of the day had been rearranged twice since March, but at dusk Mama still drew the curtain in the plain room and sat, and Olivia still came to her, across the floor now, on her feet, and climbed up, and it went on as it had.

At a year, the question arrives whether it is invited or not. Keep going, or stop. The doctor would have a view and the aunts would have several, and they were welcome to keep theirs in the north. Both answers are right. A family that goes on past one is right, for as long as it likes. A family that stops is right. When it stops, it stops slowly, one feed at a time, over weeks, and the dusk feed goes last, and the last one is held a little longer than the clock asks.

Mama had not decided. Not deciding was allowed too.

The light went down in the plain room as it had gone down all year, and the weight on her arm got heavier, and Olivia's hand, which had been opening and closing on the cardigan, opened and stayed open.

Nothing in the room was in a hurry. Neither was the question. It could wait in the hall.
