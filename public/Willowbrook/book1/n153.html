Night 153

The First Spoonful

Month 6: The Feast Begins

Noon. The chair was loaded, the bib tied on at its heaviest setting, the phone propped and recording, and history was mashed with a fork. Iron first, as the first menu asks: a fortified cereal thinned with her own milk, the new thing carrying the old taste for company. Other tables start with mashed meat or lentils and do just as well. Iron leads everywhere.

The spoon came in half loaded and unhurried, and touched down.

The face that followed deserved a page in the kitchen notebook and got one. The eyes went wide. The brow gathered itself for an emergency. Outrage and interest at full volume, over one gram of cereal. Then the mouth opened again.

The second spoonful went in under the same suspicion. The third was met partway. First meals are measured in grams and over in minutes. Three spoonfuls is a banquet, one is a start, and a flat refusal is Tuesday, tried again Thursday and held against nobody. Milk goes on feeding her for months yet. Milk is still the dinner. Solids are guests.

The banquet closed at four spoonfuls. The fifth was steered into the bib by a fist.

"Welcome to food," said Papa, wiping her down. The aunts, who had lobbied for this since May, got the video and replied inside a minute.

Olivia hammered the tray flat-handed. More.
