Night 279

No

Month 10: First Snow

The head shake came first, at the broccoli. A turn away, and back, and away, so plain that Papa laughed and Mama set the spoon down.

Then the sound went with it. "Nuh." The head and the word together, aimed, at the second spoon.

It is not really about broccoli. All year the self has been arriving, in the reach and the roll and the crawl, and now it has a word, and the word is no. It is the first tool she owns that works on other people.

The bear got it at bedtime. "Nuh," to the bear held out, and in the same breath both arms up for the bear, the no and the want going in opposite directions at once. Both. Papa gave her the bear. She gave it back, and took it.

At the table the split was simple and stayed simple. Mama decides what comes to the tray. Olivia decides what goes in.

"No? Okay," said Mama, and put the spoon down, and ate her own dinner.

After that the spoons stayed in the drawer. The broccoli sat on the tray in a small green heap, ignored, and then, later, with the room's attention elsewhere, it went in off her own fingers, one finger at a time.
