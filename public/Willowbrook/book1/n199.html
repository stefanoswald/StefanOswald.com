Night 199

Clink

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

For six months the traffic had run one way. Out. Out of the cupboard, out of the basket, out of the hand and off the tray. Out is the first thing hands learn.

On Wednesday morning she was banging a block on the rim of the steel mixing bowl, a fine sound, and the block slipped, and went in.

Clink.

She stopped. She looked into the bowl for a long time. The block was in there. It had not gone out. It had gone in, and it was still there, and it had made a different noise about it.

She took it out and banged the rim again, and it did not go in, and she banged for a while, and then went back to the cupboard, and that was Wednesday.

Nobody showed her. That is the whole of it. In is a thing the hands find by accident, months before they can do it on purpose, and the day it happens is a day like any other, and one clink is kept behind those eyes with all the other clinks, waiting.

Mama, who had seen it from the sink, said nothing and put the bowl where it could be reached again tomorrow.

Tomorrow it did not happen. Nor Friday.

The bowl stayed on the floor all month, ringing when she hit it.
