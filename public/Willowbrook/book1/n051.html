Night 51

Papa's Hour

Month 2: Smiles and Sleep

There was an hour that belonged to Papa alone, and it started when Mama left the house.

That was the trick, found by accident. As long as Mama was in the building, Olivia held out for Mama, and Papa was the understudy. The moment the door shut, for a walk or a haircut, Olivia took the arms she had. And in the taking, something grew.

He had his own methods now. A burp technique that looked wrong and worked. A bouncing walk with a half turn at the open window. A radio station wrong for babies according to nobody who was home. Olivia was getting to know him, the rumble in his chest, the different way his arms decided things, and somewhere in the third week he stopped checking over his shoulder for the real parent.

It runs two ways. The Papa takes the solo hours, because a person only gets good at a thing alone with it. And the Mama leaves. Truly leaves, not from the driveway with a phone. He will do it differently. Different is not wrong. Different is a second whole parent.

Mama came home to Olivia asleep on her back in the bassinet and Papa asleep on the couch beside it, one big hand hanging down over its rim. She took the picture. She left the radio on. She did not fix anything.
