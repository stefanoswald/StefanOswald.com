Night 25

The Laundry Mountain

Month 1: Arrival

On the couch there arose a mountain, and the mountain was made of laundry, and the laundry was unbelievably small. Papa folded socks shorter than his thumb. How could a person of eight pounds generate this? Six outfits a day, cloths beyond counting, and a sleep sack that had come out of the wash inside out and somehow also backwards.

Nothing would be what it was, for a while. The trick was to stop holding it to the old standard. Fed, held, loved. That was the whole list, and the whole list was done by breakfast. The rest got sorted like this. Anything a visitor offers to do, say yes. Anything that can be done with one hand gets done while swaying. Anything else can be moved to the chair.

The cat arrived, damp from the yard, considered the mountain, and lay down on the warmest part of it, which was the part Papa had just folded.

He folded eleven more things, declared victory, and moved the mountain to the chair, cat and all. Beside him, the reason for all of it made a sound like a door creaking in a dollhouse, and slept on.
