Night 288

The Tree Comes Home

Month 10: First Snow

The tree lot is behind the fire station. They went on a Saturday that hurt to breathe in.

The puffy coat came off at the car door, as it has since it arrived on its hook in August with the rule already attached. Straps snug over the thin layers. The coat laid over the buckled harness like a blanket, never under it. A puffy coat is mostly air, and a strap that is tight over air is loose over a child the moment the car stops hard.

The tree was small. Papa said it was the right size for the corner. Mama said the corner would decide. It rode home on the roof, tied with rope and the word probably.

Then the corner, and the box of ornaments, and the division of the tree. The top two thirds got the glass and the hooks and stayed above her reach. The bottom third got the felt ones, big and soft and hookless, and was hers.

She pulled a felt star off, looked at it, and put it on the couch. The tree went on with one bare branch at the bottom, and the star was not moved back.

The tree leaned a little toward the window, the way Papa had tied it, and stayed that way.
