Night 233

The Balloon Over the Willows

Month 8: The Velcro Weeks and the Leaves

Sunday at the farm went by its usual order. Olivia was on Grandpa Joe's knee on the porch step for the last of the light when she stopped everything.

The arm went up. The point locked. It aimed past the pasture, at the willow line where the low sun was doing its gold work, and above the willows, like a note pinned to the sky, a red balloon.

"Well now," said Grandpa Joe.

The balloon moved along the willow line at a walking pace, the pace of something large and gentle and in no hurry underneath it. Opposite the fence it paused. It dipped once, and the point tracked every inch of the dip, and Olivia looked back at Grandpa Joe. He had seen it. He did not explain it. He watched it with her.

Ellie wears her balloon on special days. Nobody on the porch knew what the day was. Ellie did.

Olivia had last seen that balloon in June, at this same fence. Four months is a long time when you have had eight. The point said she had kept it anyway.

"Ba!" she told the willow line.

The balloon went on behind the green and was gone. Grandpa Joe shifted her to the other knee and said nothing about any of it, at length.
