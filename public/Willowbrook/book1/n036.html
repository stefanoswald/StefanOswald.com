Night 36

The Four Steps of Bedtime

Month 2: Smiles and Sleep

The routine was four steps long and it never changed. That was why it worked.

Bath first, or on a tired night a warm washcloth. Then quiet: lamp down, curtains drawn, voices on the low shelf, diaper and pajamas and the sleep sack zipped like an envelope. Then one small book in the lap. Olivia could not follow a word and followed all of it, the voice going up and down the hills of the sentences. Then the song. The same song every night. Papa sang it a little flat in the same places, and Mama had stopped mentioning it.

Then lights out. Crib, on her back, nothing in there with her, one hand resting on her chest a moment like a name signed at the bottom of a letter. The same words every night. "Goodnight, little one. We're right here."

Babies cannot read clocks. They read order beautifully. Bath means quiet means book means song means dark, one open door leading to the next. The same order every night, not most nights. Every night is what makes it work.

By the third week Olivia had started to yawn at the sound of the bath running. Papa heard it from the hall and kept still, in case it stopped.
