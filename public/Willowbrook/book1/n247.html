Night 247

Practice in Private

Month 9: One Step, One Word

Watched, she performed: three steps and the catch, the wobble bigger for the audience, the fall arriving sooner. Unwatched, seen from the hall corner, she trained. Laps of the yes space at seven strides and nine. The squat and rise, in sets. The turn attempted, failed, landed, for nobody.

It happened often enough to make a law. Watcher noticed, training stopped, sit-down prompt, and then either the show came on or she crawled off to a less observed corner.

Mama broke the law on Thursday. Nine strides, the best yet, and it came out of her mid-stride with both arms up: "Yes! Look at you go!"

Olivia looked up, lost the room, and went down on her face. She cried the surprised cry. Mama had her up in a second. "Sorry. That was me. I cheered too soon." They sat on the hall floor a while, doing nothing, until the crying wound down.

The walker's rules, after that: cheer at the end, not the middle. Look from doorways. And the one who will not show her grandfather the walking is not behind. She is keeping her practice where practice belongs.

That evening's private session, seen from the dark kitchen: eleven strides, a turn on the third try, a squat and rise with the bear as cargo.

Mama bit her knuckle and said nothing.
