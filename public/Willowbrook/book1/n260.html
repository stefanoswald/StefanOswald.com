Night 260

The Box

Month 9: One Step, One Word

The winter boots came on Thursday, and the snowsuit, ordered big, and within the hour the household confirmed the oldest fact in the toy business: the boots were received politely and the box was received like a country.

Day one. Entered, exited, entered. The babble sent into it came back bigger, so more babble was sent. The bear was imported. One book hauled aboard.

Day two, percussion. Every panel drummed and every panel had its own note. The long side went bong. The short side went bok. The floor of it, with a baby on it, went nothing at all, and this was studied.

Day three, peekaboo. The flaps worked as curtains. The hide, the held pause, audible breathing, one sock pumping, then the eruption. Mama was got four times running and acted amazed four times running, which is the job.

Days four to six, the conversions. Pushed, it was a wagon. Tipped, a cave. Worn, a hat of enormous size, and the photograph exists.

Day seven the roof went under heavy drumming and the walls went soft as cloth, and the box went out to the recycling with one panel spared for the toy basket, where it serves as a tunnel and is used daily.

The boots are in the closet.

The cat is in the panel.
