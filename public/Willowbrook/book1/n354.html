Night 354

The House Made Ready

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The house got ready on a Saturday, and it took until lunch.

The banner was one strip of paper with ONE on it in Mama's lettering, and under the letters a scribble Olivia had made with the green crayon while nobody was watching the crayon. Papa hung it across the window. It was crooked. Mama looked at it and, for once in her life, said nothing, and Papa noticed her saying nothing and straightened it.

The coffee table went against the wall. The gate on the yes space came off, since the yes space was about to be the whole room. The tarp from under the high chair was folded by the door, for the cake. Beside it, on the low shelf, stood the jars of September applesauce with ribbons on them, one for every guest to take home, the thank-yous Mama had been meaning to walk round the lane since January and never had. Now the lane was coming to them.

Olivia pulled the ribbon off one jar, and the jar rolled, and she went after it on her feet, and caught it, and sat down on it.

"That's the party," said Papa, looking at the room. "That's all of it."

"That's all of it," said Mama.

It looked like their living room, with one word over it.
