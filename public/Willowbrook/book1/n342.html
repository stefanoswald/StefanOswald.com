Night 342

The Cake Trial

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

Rosa had baked the practice cake before anybody asked her to, which is how Rosa does most things. It stood on the counter beside its own drawing: one layer, plain vanilla, a thin white frosting, and on top a star, cut for now from gold paper.

"Half the sugar," said Rosa. "No honey. None for a baby under one, and I'm not starting the morning she turns it."

"The pieces?"

"The size of your fingernail. She's got six teeth and a lot of confidence."

Lily was asleep in the next room in her crib, on her back, where the crawling had run out.

"Still not a step," said Rosa. She did not say it lightly. "Eleven days younger and not a step."

"Nine months to eighteen," said Mama. "The doctor said it about Olivia and it counts for Lily. Yours crawls faster than mine walks."

"She does," said Rosa, and wiped her cheek, and put the flour back on it.

Olivia got a crumb. She held it, considered it, ate it, and went very still.

"Muh," she said.

"That's a yes," said Rosa. "Write that on the drawing."

The gold paper star went into an envelope for the real one. Lily slept on. Olivia had her second crumb, and looked at the envelope, and said it again.
