Night 178

The Crackling Paper

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The package arrived Tuesday: a toy of pedigree, gift of the far aunts, endorsed on its box by two institutes. The toy was received with mild interest and set aside. The packing paper was received like a coronation.

The first crackle stopped her mid-roll, that dry lightning. The first crush, two-handed, ran every fine-motor lesson at once: resistance, collapse, the paper holding the shape of her decision. The tear, found by accident, was repeated with the devotion of a scholar confirming results. Waved, the sheet thundered. Held up, it made a peekaboo wall. Sat upon, it announced her.

Forty minutes, unassisted and undiminished, with the institutes' toy watching from the shelf like an understudy. The paper stayed supervised, the mouth rule standing, since paper tastes better than it should.

Then the steel bowls and their endless wrong nestings. The cardboard box, every vehicle at once. The funnel, again. The scarf drawer's one sacrificial scarf. Things with give and noise and no single correct use, because the toy that does one thing teaches one thing, and the paper that does anything teaches her.

The institutes are not wrong, merely beaten. The pedigreed newcomer went into the rotation basket. The paper outlasted three catalog toys that month, crackling to the last.

The aunts were sent a photograph of the coronation and, being wise aunts, framed it.
