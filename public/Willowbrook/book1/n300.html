Night 300

Three Hundred Nights

Month 10: First Snow

Three hundred nights.

At one hundred, bedtime had become a habit. At two hundred she had begun helping with it. Now the whole thing runs like a conversation.

At the word bath she heads for the hall. She offers her arms for the sleep sack before Papa finds the zipper. At the basket she chooses the bear book and carries it to Mama's lap.

They have read it so often that she knows where every surprise lives. Mama pauses before the button and Olivia supplies it. "Buh." She gasps before the page that earns a gasp. At the last picture she touches the moon.

Then Papa's song, wrong words in the usual places. She hums underneath it.

The crib is what it was on the first night. Her back, the flat mattress, the sack and nothing else, the bear on the dresser where he watches from, sixty-five nights from the candle now, and not one night sooner.

The lamp clicked off.

"Goodnight, Olivia," Mama whispered.

"Nigh-nigh."

Three hundred nights have built a small language. Book means lap. Song means safe. Dark means sleep, and goodnight comes back now from the other side of the rail.

Papa drew the star on the kitchen calendar, the three hundredth, and did not make it any bigger than the others, though he thought about it.
