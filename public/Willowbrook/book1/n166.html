Night 166

The Pincer Preview

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The tray held a single puff, the dissolving kind, and above it hovered the finest small machinery in the house, halfway through a change.

The old method was the rake: the whole hand swept inward, the puff gathered into the palm, the fist delivered to the mouth. But this week the thumb had separated from the crowd.

The forefinger went out solo. The two came down on the puff like a crane with opinions, closing, nearly, nearly, the puff escaping twice and caught on the third pass in a grip half rake and trending jeweler. The pincer proper tends to arrive around eight or nine months. This was the preview.

A few targets at a sitting, three or four, since a scattered dozen only summons the rake back. The misses go unhelped. Every failed pinch teaches the next one, and the parent who pops the puff in for her has done the homework in her place. Papa's hand went out once. Mama's hand went over it, and it came back.

The fourth try of the evening closed on the puff, pinched, mostly, and steered home, where it dissolved into astonished satisfaction.

Her eyes came up and found her audience, and the whole face preened. The applause was for the fingers. They had names to write, some day.

Tonight, one puff, then another, until the tray was clear.
