Night 191

The Snack Diplomacy

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

The stroller was parked at the bench, brake on, Mama on the bench in front of it, because puffs are eaten sitting up, with a grown-up watching, and never on the move. The puffs are the kind that go soft on the tongue in a second, which is the point of them.

Mama put one on the tray. Olivia went at it with her whole hand, all five fingers closing like the bucket of a digger, and got it into her fist. Then the fist went to her mouth and the puff stayed inside the fist, and she sucked her knuckles for a while and looked puzzled.

The small pinch between finger and thumb that would solve this was a month away, or two.

Puff eleven came out of the fist wrong. She held it up. Soggy at the edges from prior consideration, gripped in four fingers, lifted slowly toward Mama's face, and stopped there, and her eyes came up to Mama's.

Was it a gift? In Willowbrook a soggy puff held up at your mouth is a gift. That is the rule there.

Mama ate it.

The face across the tray did something new. Attention, delight, and under those, the first small satisfaction of having made a thing happen in somebody else.

She held up another. Mama ate that too.

Then a leaf, which Mama declined.
