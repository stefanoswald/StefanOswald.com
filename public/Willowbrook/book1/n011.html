Night 11

A Bath Before Swimming

Month 1: Arrival

Until her belly button healed and the little stump of cord waved goodbye, Olivia could not soak in a proper bath. Bathtime, for now, was a sponge affair: a warm room with the radiator ticking, a basin, a washcloth, and two parents moving like careful jewelers.

Face first, with plain warm water and nothing else. Then the folds and creases, under the chin where milk hides, inside the tiny fists that never open, behind the ears where mysteries collect. Wash, rinse, and wrapped up again quickly, because a small body loses heat fast. Olivia was against the entire project at first, then confusingly in favor of it, then against it again when the washcloth reached her feet.

Two or three baths a week is plenty at this age. More than that dries out skin so thin and new it still smells like the beginning of the world. Afterward she lay in her hooded towel like an astonished monk, furious and clean.

"Same time Thursday," Papa told her. She could not agree, but she did fall asleep, which everyone took as a yes.
