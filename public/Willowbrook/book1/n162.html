Night 162

Hands Free

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The rug hosted, this week, something so quiet it was nearly missed. Olivia sat. Not propped, not leaning on her own arms. Sat, back straight, both hands off the floor and free for the first time in her life.

The ring went from left hand to right, the July trick, old news. Then to the mouth. Then out to arm's length and back, and she watched it travel.

Then she leaned for the block and went over sideways, slowly, onto the cushion Mama had set there for this. She lay on her side with the ring still in her fist and made a noise of pure opinion.

Mama did not scoop her up. "Over you went," she said, and sat her back up, and moved nothing. The second lean went over too. The third lean put a hand down first, on the rug, and held.

The whole art of the sitting month is supply and stand back: a cushion, things worth reaching for, a floor to reach across. Rescue every topple and the hand never finds out it can go down.

By afternoon's end she had the ring, the block, and a sock, and needed no hands for the sitting. She held the ring out toward Mama. Not to give it. To show it.

Mama looked, and the ring went back in the mouth.
