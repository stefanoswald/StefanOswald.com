Night 146

Duchess and the Deputy

Month 5: Nearly Sitting, Nearly Everything

The Duchess had never once acknowledged Olivia.

Other hens came to the wire for the introductions. The Duchess kept to the far side of the run and gave the visitors the back of her head.

This Sunday Grandpa Joe did not run the introductions. He stood at the wire with Olivia on his arm and said nothing at all, which for him was not unusual, and did not move, which was.

The other hens came, looked, lost interest. The Duchess, at length, crossed the run. She stopped one regal pace off and turned one amber eye on the baby, and then the other eye, and then the first again.

Olivia, schooled in reaching, put out her hand. Not a grab. The hand went out flat and open and stayed there, and at five months that is a bow.

Hen and baby regarded each other across the wire for a long moment. Neither moved. Grandpa Joe, above them, breathed like a man who has waited out weather before.

The Duchess ended the audience with one shake of her feathers and went back to the far side. Olivia pumped both arms and sent one long vowel after her.

The fence line took them slowly home.

"Come on, deputy," said Grandpa Joe at the gate, and that was all he said till supper.
