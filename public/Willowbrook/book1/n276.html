Night 276

The Lights on Maple Lane

Month 10: First Snow

The lane puts its lights up on the first Saturday after the first snow. Nobody decided this. Mrs. Henderson's go up Friday, because she has a system, and then the rest of the street feels looked at.

The box came down from the attic. The strings were tested on the kitchen floor with Olivia in the high chair, Mama's one condition. Every year Papa says the tangle is worse than last year. Every year Mama says that is not possible.

Then the ladder, and the whistling. Papa whistles the way a door hinge does, and halfway through the second string Mrs. Henderson called across to ask whether the cat was all right.

Olivia watched from Mama's arms, hat on, mittens on, her breath going up in small clouds she kept trying to catch. She had no idea what the man on the ladder was for.

Then the plug went in.

The porch came on, red and green and gold along the rail, and she went still. Both hands came up. Her mouth opened and stayed open.

"Lights," said Mama, in her ear.

Up and down the lane the porches answered, one, then two, then all of them. Inside, the string past the nursery window made small colors on the ceiling, and she lay on her back and looked at them until she was not looking at anything.
