Night 151

The Oak Tells the Sky

Month 5: Nearly Sitting, Nearly Everything

Trees send their news up on the night wind, and the oak of Dandelion Park had a summer's worth to send.

Stella caught it at her post, one green leaf let go on the warm air, and read it to the Sleepy Moon, who hummed to show it was listening.

That the blanket babies of spring had become sitting babies, all across the park. That the small one from Maple Lane, the one the oak had watched since her first dandelion seed, sat under its branches now like somebody with work to do, running its leaves through her hands.

That her voice had gone to chains and growls. That her parents had spread a blanket in the last gold light and lain there checking their happiness against each other, and the oak took that as thanks for the shade.

"Five months," said Stella.

The Moon hummed, the hum that means every month is a busy one, but this is the one where you can watch the person arriving, like a boat coming in from far off, a little clearer every week.

The leaf turned once more and came down on the empty blanket.

Stella settled to her watch, low over Maple Lane, where the bedroom window held its small correct darkness, and the bear kept the dresser, and the person slept with both busy hands at rest.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
