Night 267

The Coat on the Hook

Month 9: One Step, One Word

The puffy coat had hung on its hook by the door since August, waiting for exactly this: the first properly cold drive to the farm, the porch thermometer below freezing at breakfast.

It went on at the door and looked wonderful. She looked like a small red sofa.

At the car it came off again. That is the rule the coat came with, and Papa said it out loud at the open door: the puffy coat comes off before the straps go on. A puffy coat under the straps leaves them loose, because the puff squashes flat in a crash and the straps are then a coat's thickness too big. So, sweater. Straps snug at the collarbones. The pinch test, no slack to take up between two fingers at the shoulder. Then the coat laid over her backwards, arms in the sleeves if she will allow it, like a blanket she is wearing.

She did not allow it. The coat went over her anyway. The heater was aimed at the back.

At the farm the coat went on again at the car door and she was a sofa again, and Grandpa Joe looked at her for a while and said, "That's a lot of coat."

The pinch test ran again on the way home.

The coat went back on its hook by the door, ready.
