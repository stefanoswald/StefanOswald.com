Night 311

The Whole Milk Rehearsal

Month 11: Mittens and the Early Dark

The change that comes after the first birthday was already being talked about at the table: milk in a cup, instead of a bottle or nursing, at the center of every meal.

But the birthday had not arrived, so whole cow's milk would wait. Before twelve months, breast milk or infant formula remained her main drink. The rehearsal was about the cup, not what would be in it later.

At the afternoon snack, Mama poured a little formula into the open cup. A nursing family could use expressed milk. A few sips of water with a meal made good practice too. Olivia took both handles, tipped too far, and painted her chin to the collar.

She tried again. The second sip landed. By the fifth she was holding the cup nearly level and wearing a white mustache from one cheek to the other.

She pointed at it in the spoon's reflection. "Muh."

"Milk," said Mama. "You did the cup."

Mama did not wipe it. Some things are worth leaving on a face a while.

After the first birthday, if her doctor agreed and her diet was ready, whole milk could join the table, and the family would make that change slowly. For now, Olivia practiced the cup while her familiar milk stayed exactly where it belonged.

The cup went in the sink. The mustache went to the bath.
