Night 165

Follow the Leader

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The household's first sport was invented the way the great ones are: by accident, at dusk, in a hallway. Papa, down for a sock, found himself regarded from four feet away by a baby on her stomach with narrowed eyes and a rising grin. Some old instinct made him thump the floor once and scuttle backward on his hands.

The effect was total. She shrieked the hunting shriek and came on, over and over, rolling.

He fled at quarter speed around the couch. She went under the coffee table by accident, came out the far side, and caught him at the bookshelf, where he was boarded and climbed. The hallway rang like a playground.

Chase is the first game two people play by moving together, the babble months gone to the whole body: my move answers your move, turns taken at the speed of joy. The being-chased half teaches its own lesson, the delicious safe danger of a monster who is also the safest person alive, fear in its play edition, in tiny doses.

Papa whistled chase music to go with it, or tried. The sound was mostly air.

Three chases ran that night. The monster lost all three and lay flat in the hallway while Olivia drummed on his shoulders like the end of a song.

Bath ran late. Nobody minded but the bath.
