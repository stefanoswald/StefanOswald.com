Night 303

The Photos, Backward

Month 10: First Snow

Papa's phone held four hundred photos of December, and on the year's second-to-last night he scrolled them backward at the kitchen table, one sentence each.

The mitten on the ice, her ear down to the brook under its lid. The three hundredth star. Grandma on the screen with a rubber duck pressed to her face. The bear book, eleventh reading, and a moo at the barnyard page.

No photo of the fever night, so Mama said that one. "The list, and the call, and we didn't guess."

The bear with the empty cup at his mouth. The second cup on its side. Lily and Olivia and one red block, nose to nose. Her mittens on the tractor wheel. A paper cut, kissed. The bump gone plum, and two faces that had stayed level.

The cup mid-air off the tray. "That was the night I shouted." "And came back."

The cardinal, a red dot, and her face turned round. The head shake at the broccoli. The bear hat on Mama's head and two hands reaching for it. Her palm out on the step, one flake on it.

Then one from March. A bundle in a knitted hat who could not lift her own head.

"When you were little," said Mama. No one had said that before.

The phone went dark, and neither of them turned it back on.
