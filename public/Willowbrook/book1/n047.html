Night 47

The Library Lantern

Month 2: Smiles and Sleep

The first outing with a destination was three blocks long, and the destination was made of books.

The Lantern Library sat round in the middle of town, and its light came on as Mama reached the path. She stopped and held Olivia up to see. Olivia looked at the light a long moment, then at Mama, then at the light again, and gave it a slow blink. From a two-month-old, that is high praise.

Inside was the good hush, the paper smell, and a librarian who did not coo or grab but smiled at her like a new neighbor. Olivia got a library card. It had to be signed for her, her signature being a fist.

They came home with three board books, thick-paged and chewable at the corners. At this age a book is a thing to hear, to stare at, and eventually to gum, and all three count as reading. The library is free and warm, it gets a housebound parent out the door, and it tells a baby early that there is a building where the stories live and the light is always on.

Something small with an acorn cap watched them go from a gap in the wainscoting, and said nothing, nobody present being the right age.

On the walk home Mama read the first one aloud to a baby mainly interested in the streetlights.
