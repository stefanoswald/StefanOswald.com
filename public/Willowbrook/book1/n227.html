Night 227

The Bucket Swing

Month 8: The Velcro Weeks and the Leaves

The first bright cold Saturday of the month was spent, by the demand of one pointing arm, at the swings.

The bucket swing took its passenger. Legs threaded, hands set on the chains as high as they reached, jacket zipped to the chin. The first push was the gentlest one, and it was given from in front. Pushing from in front keeps the faces meeting. It turns the ride from a thing done to her into a game played between them: away, and back, and away, and back, peekaboo on a pendulum.

The laugh started on the second arc and did not stop. It was the whole-body kind, renewing itself at the top of every swing, the kind the other benches turn toward. The wind got into her hair properly for the first time.

At the first pause both arms went up. At the second pause, both arms and a noise.

Lily arrived mid-session, eleven days younger, and was installed in the next bucket. The two of them swung side by side at slightly different speeds and studied each other at every crossing with the seriousness of their whole acquaintance, now conducted at velocity.

The ride ended one swing past the top of the fun, the wise place to end it. The dismount was protested by both buckets and turned down by both mothers, gently, with crackers.
