Night 142

The Babyproofing Expedition

Month 5: Nearly Sitting, Nearly Everything

Papa went down on his hands and knees at the living-room door and traveled the house at her height. Mama followed with the list.

Outlets capped, because at rug height they multiply. Cords vanished upward or into boxes, and the looped blind cords retired for good.

Then the things that tip. The bookcase was already on its stud. The dresser got its bracket that hour, and the television its strap, since a tipping piece of furniture is the quiet leader of this year's real dangers and the anchor kit costs less than lunch.

Then the swallowables. The floor swept, then swept again lower. A coin. A button. Anything that could fit in her mouth went away.

Then the chemicals: a lock on the under-sink cupboard, the medicines moved to a high cabinet, since a childproof cap is a second wall and never the first.

Then the stairs, gated top and bottom, the top gate screwed into the wall, because a pressure gate is for a doorway and never for the top of a stair.

Two hours. One sore back. She would be taller next month, and they would do it again next month, and the month after. New height, new reach, new check, for two years. Mama pinned the list inside the cupboard door.

Olivia napped through all of it and woke on a safer planet.
