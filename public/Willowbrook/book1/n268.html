Night 268

The Photograph

Month 9: One Step, One Word

The far aunts had been asking since the festival for a proper one, the nine-month photograph, for the mantel. So on the last Sunday of the month, the light gone by four and the good blanket spread under the lamp, the household made the attempt.

The setup: the blanket, the good sweater, one bear on leave from the dresser. The subject: a walking, pointing, one-word citizen with a rug's worth of her own business and no opening for portraits.

Frames one to eighteen: the back of her head.

Frame nineteen: Papa said "duck" from behind the camera in his duck voice, and she turned, and the laugh arrived before the face had arranged itself, the whole nine months of her in it.

Frame thirty-three: the attempt abandoned, the sweater half untucked, the subject back at her own business on the rug, and the camera, lowered, going off once more from the hip at the lamplight of it. The blocks. The frown. One bare foot.

The mantel got thirty-three. The aunts got nineteen. The first month's page had said the unposed ones would be the ones, and the mantel proved it.

Mama is in neither. She is in the mirror in frame twenty-six, mid-sneeze, and that one has been deleted, she was told.

Then the camera went in the drawer and the rug got watched instead.
