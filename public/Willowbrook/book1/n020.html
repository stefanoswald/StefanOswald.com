Night 20

Advice From Everywhere

Month 1: Arrival

Everyone had a book, and every book disagreed. Feed on a schedule, said one. Feed whenever she asks, said another. Never rock her to sleep, warned a third, while a fourth promised that rocking her to sleep was the one thing she needed. The neighbor swore by cabbage leaves. The internet, consulted at two in the morning, swore the sky was falling, which is the internet's one trick. Even the man in the checkout line had instructions, delivered over the eggs in a wet coat.

Mama read, nodded, thanked, and stacked. The stack on the table grew until it leaned. Every book, she noticed around page forty of the sixth one, was written about babies in general, and she was raising one baby in particular.

The rule became this. Safety advice was followed to the letter: how she was laid down to sleep, the car seat, the doctor's orders. Everything else got run through the family test. Did it work for Olivia, and did it keep them mostly sane? Keep what fits. Wave kindly at the rest.

"You're the expert on her now," the doctor had said. It was becoming true, feed by feed. Olivia yawned at the whole library, and the stack leaned a little more.
