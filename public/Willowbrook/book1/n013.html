Night 13

Growth Spurt Week

Month 1: Arrival

Suddenly Olivia was hungry all the time. She fed, dozed for a blink, and woke up outraged that no one had ever fed her, not once, in her entire life. Mama checked the calendar. Two weeks old, nearly to the day.

Growth spurts arrive like storms, at two or three weeks, again near six weeks, again around three months. For a few days a baby seems to want to eat the whole world. It is not a supply problem. It is a building site: bones lengthening, a brain wiring itself by the mile, and every bit of it built out of milk.

Mama stopped fighting the week and built a nest instead. Water within reach. Snacks in a bowl. A blanket against the draft. Phone, pillow, footstool, and the remote, which had been missing since the baby came and turned up under a burp cloth. Feed, doze, repeat, and let the week be what it was. Papa brought the snacks and took away the bowls and did not ask what day it was, because it did not matter.

By Friday the storm had passed. Olivia slept a long, heavy, workmanlike sleep, and woke up visibly more baby than before, longer in the sleeve, heavier on the arm, and hungry again by lunchtime.
