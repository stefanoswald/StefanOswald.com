Night 294

The Cup, Neat

Month 10: First Snow

The open cup began in August with two ounces of water and a chin that took most of it. It has run a long campaign since, through the chin cascades and the towels, the straw cup from Rosa for the stroller, the open one for the table.

At the afternoon snack on the last Thursday, it came together.

Mama poured two ounces and stepped back. Olivia lifted the cup, both hands, steered it, tipped it, drank. Water went in. None came out the sides. Then the part saved for last: the cup lowered, aimed, and set down upright on the coaster, and let go of.

Two ounces, neat.

A mouth that can manage a cup is doing more than drinking. The lips round for the rim the way they will round for the vowels that are coming, and lift, tip, sip, return is the finest thing hands and mouth have done together yet.

Milk is still the main food until one. The cup is water, at the table. Whole milk comes at the candle, the bottle retires across the spring, and the cup will be ready.

"Wa," she said. Not yet a word by the household rule. It will be.

The second cup went over. She looked at the water on the table a long while, and then put one finger in it.
