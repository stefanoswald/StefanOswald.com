Night 194

The Rainy Day Fort

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

It rained on Tuesday, and Wednesday, and by Thursday it was a week that was raining, and the house could not go out and so it went in.

Papa built the fort at ten in the morning. Couch cushions for walls, the good blanket roofed across to the armchair, the lamp moved to shine in through one gap. He has been building forts since he could lift a cushion and he is not modest about it. Olivia was carried in through the flap and had the effect the fort has on everyone. The room shrank to one warm pocket, the rain went far away, and everything in reach was hers.

Camp ran all day. The book basket came in. The big pot from the kitchen came in and was played, badly. Peekaboo ran at the flap, the best peekaboo door ever built.

Boredom was expected and never turned up. A rainy day at floor level has a shape if you give it two anchors, one in the morning and one after the nap, a fort, a bath moved early, anything with a name. The hours between look after themselves.

The fort came down at bath time under protest, one wall left standing by agreement for tomorrow. It was still raining.

The pot stayed in the fort overnight, like a thing that lived there.
