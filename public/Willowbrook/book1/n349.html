Night 349

The Mirror's Almost

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The baby in the mirror had been Olivia's friend since the tummy-time days, and had never once been rude. This morning the baby in the mirror had a dot of yogurt on her nose.

Olivia looked at it. She patted the glass where the dot was. The other baby patted back, exactly, and the dot stayed on Olivia.

Mama, behind her, made a face. The mother in the mirror made the same face, and Olivia turned round to check, and Mama was already ordinary again.

"Who's that?" said Mama. "That's you."

Olivia leaned in and kissed the other baby. The other baby kissed her. It was a wet exchange on both sides.

Then the cat walked through the room in the mirror, and Olivia turned all the way round to find it, and it was there, going out the door, and she looked back at the glass to see if it had gone from there too. It had. That was interesting enough to try twice.

Somewhere around a year and a half she will see the dot and reach for her own nose. Until then the dot belongs to the other baby, who is welcome to it.

She licked the mirror. Mama wiped it later.
