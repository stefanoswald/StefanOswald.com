Night 365

The Bear Moves In

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The house was three again by dark. Bath, and the yawn at the water. Quiet. The bear-and-button book. The song. Lights down.

Then Papa lifted Bobby Bear off the low shelf across from the crib, out of reach, where he had sat since January.

He had waited the whole year, because nothing soft belongs in a baby's crib in the first twelve months: no bear, no blanket, no pillow, no bumper. No one can name a night that is perfectly safe, but a small stuffed toy is generally thought to be low risk for a healthy child after twelve months, and a family may still wait longer, or ask the doctor first.

Tonight he went in.

Olivia found the flopped ear without opening her eyes, pulled him against her, and sighed like a person who has waited a year for something and is too tired to say so.

"Buh," she said, into the ear.

Outside, in the wet field past the lane, the peepers had started. The brook went under it.

Stella came down low in the window. The Sleepy Moon was already there.

On the oldest branch of the willow, Oliver Owl asked the question he asks once a year. "And what will they read tomorrow?"

"The next one," said Stella, and twinkled once, for yes.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.

From One to Two begins tomorrow.

Sweet dreams. Same time tomorrow.
