Night 163

The Dropped Toy Rule

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The dropping game had run for months on its original terms: release, clatter, assistant retrieves. This month Olivia rewrote it. It showed in the gaze. The ring went over the tray's edge as ever, but the eyes that followed it down stayed down, fixed on the landing site, waiting.

Not the old blank blink of a thing subtracted from the world. A stare with a theory in it. The ring is not gone. The ring is on the floor. The floor is where the ring goes on being. Hidden and vanished are different words. Things, and soon people, carry on offstage.

The peekaboo cloth had been teaching it since spring. The high chair's edge was where she proved it. Half hidden under the cloth, the toy was seized at once. Fully covered, a frown, a pause, then the cloth yanked and the matter settled with a shriek.

Tonight the ring went over at supper's end, and she leaned and looked, and then looked up for her assistant. The assistant was reading his phone. She looked at the floor. She looked at him. "Da," she said, with some heat.

Papa put the phone face down. "Sorry. I missed my cue." He held up the ring. "Down there the entire time."

She took it back, and the confirmation with it, and looked at the floor once more before she let go.
