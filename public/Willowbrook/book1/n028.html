Night 28

A Letter From Your Bear

Month 1: Arrival

On the dresser, across the room from the crib, sat a honey-brown bear with one flopped ear and a patchwork heart on his foot, keeping the night watch. If bears could write, his letter would go like this.

Dear Olivia. I am not allowed in your bed yet. This is correct. Your bed is for you alone, bare and boring and perfectly safe, so I guard from the dresser, where I can see the whole crib and most of the window.

After your first birthday, and not one night before, I will move in, and you will chew my ear, and it will be an honor. For now, my job is watching, and yours is growing, and your parents' job is everything else. Between us, they are doing better than they think. Tell them, when you learn how. Babies know these things first.

The moon comes by at eleven. The star checks in around three. You are, at all hours, guarded by an entire household of soft and shining things. Sleep, small one. I have the watch. Your bear, B.

Across the room, Mama and Papa slept without knowing a word of it. On the dresser, the bear did not blink until morning.
