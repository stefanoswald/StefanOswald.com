Night 48

Five Points of the Harness

Month 2: Smiles and Sleep

Before the engine, always, came the ritual.

Rear-facing. Reclined to the angle the little level sticker on the side allowed. Straps threaded through the slots at her shoulders or just below them, the rule for a baby riding backward. Chest clip level with her armpits, every time. Harness snug enough that Papa's pinching fingers slid off the webbing at her collarbone instead of catching a fold of it.

No puffy coat between her and the straps, ever. A warm car instead, or a blanket tucked over the buckled harness, never under it.

It took ninety seconds when done right, and Papa had decided early to be the tedious sort of father who grows old bones. He did the pinch test for the four-block drive to the market. Especially for that one, short trips being where corners get cut.

One more habit rode along, picked up from a story too sad for a book like this one. His shoe went in the back seat beside her, or his phone in the diaper bag at her feet. Something back there that had to be fetched before he could walk away from the car. Not a decision, a reflex, so that on his most tired day it would still happen.

Click. Pinch. Level. Go.

Olivia was asleep before the second stop sign, one fist gone loose against the strap.
