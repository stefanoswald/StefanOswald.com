Night 357

The Party Map

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

Two nights before, with the aunts in the guest room and Olivia asleep upstairs, they walked the party through the living room with a coffee each and the plan on the back of an envelope.

"Morning is just us," said Papa. "Pancakes, the three of us, nobody else till half past ten."

"Cake at eleven. Before the nap, not instead of it."

"Pieces the size of a fingernail. No honey in anything. No nuts, no whole grapes, no popcorn, no hard candy."

"And one grown-up whose only job is mouths."

"Me," said Papa. "I'm mouths." He wrote it down. MOUTHS.

"Tarp for the cake. Jars at the door on the way out." Mama looked at the envelope. "An hour and a half, and then we start saying goodbye, and we mean it."

"First birthdays end before they end."

Mama was doing the voice by then, the list voice, and Papa said so, and she heard it and put the coffee down.

"Sorry. I want it to be right for her."

"It's a floor and some people she likes. She'll want the box."

"She'll want the box," said Mama, and laughed, and the voice went.

Papa put the envelope on the table under the lamp. It said pancakes, cake, mouths, jars, goodbye.
