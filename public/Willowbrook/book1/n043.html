Night 43

Follow the Bear

Month 2: Smiles and Sleep

Bobby Bear had a new job: moving target.

Papa held him a long arm from Olivia's face and waited for her eyes to catch. They caught. Then, slow as honey off a spoon, he sailed the bear to the left. The eyes went after him in hops, losing him and finding him, like a reader losing the line. Back to the middle. Now to the right, and this time the whole face went along, chin and all, committed.

The second month is the season for this. Following a slow thing side to side is how two eyes learn to work as a team, and it takes no lessons, only a bear and a patient arm. A few passes here and there in happy awake time is plenty, and the game stops while it is still a game, before the first fuss, never after it.

"Once more," said Papa, "and then the bear must rest."

The bear rose. The bear hovered. The bear came down, wobbling, and landed nose to nose, and Olivia made her new sound, the hiccup of delight that was not quite a laugh yet.

Bobby Bear went back to his place on the dresser, one ear flopped, where he could see the crib. Olivia watched the spot he had left for a while, and then watched Papa instead.
