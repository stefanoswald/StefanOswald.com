Night 332

The Lights Come Down

Month 11: Mittens and the Early Dark

The week after New Year, the lights come down on Maple Lane. Ladders at every porch, neighbors up them in hats, the strings of December coiled into boxes for eleven months.

Papa took Olivia out on his arm to watch, because she had watched them go up.

The Hendersons' eaves went dark first. Olivia pointed at where the gold had been, and looked at Papa, and her whole face asked.

"All gone," said Papa. "The lights went in the box. They'll come back next winter."

She looked at the box. She looked at the dark eave. She looked at the box again. A lot of thinking, for one eave.

Mateo's mother was up the next ladder, Mateo on the bottom step holding the coil like a snake. "Bye, lights," Mateo told each string as it came down. Olivia waved at him. He waved back with the snake.

Porch by porch the lane went from December to January. But the plain strings along the porch rails stayed lit, as they do on Maple Lane, all winter and most of spring, because the dark at four wants something to look at.

"Those stay," said Papa. "Those are ours."

She pointed at the porch string as they turned for home. Still there. She checked twice on the way up the step.
