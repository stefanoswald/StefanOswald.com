Night 339

The Remember Game, Part Four

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

Olivia wanted every face patted before a page could turn, so the people took the longest.

Grandpa Joe, hat on. Grandpa Joe, hat off, which she looked at a long time. "Sundays," said Papa. "Eggs. One word a visit, and he means it."

Rosa, with flour on her cheek, holding Lily, who was holding Olivia's foot. "Eleven days younger," said Mama. "She'll be at the party. She'll take something of yours. That's friends."

Mrs. Henderson on her porch with the gold dog. The plant house, with the four others in a row on the rug like loaves. The librarian behind the desk. The far grandparents on the screen, waving with both hands, Grandma mid-song. The aunts at the wedding, all four of them, all talking. Mateo at the fence in November, mouth open, shouting the word he shouts.

"Your people," said Mama.

"Most of them," said Papa. "The rest are coming."

The last page in the book was empty. Olivia patted that too.

"That's the cake," Mama said. "It isn't in there yet."

Papa closed the album. "After the cake, it will be."

She was already asleep against Mama, one hand still out, patting nothing.
