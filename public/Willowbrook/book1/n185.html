Night 185

The Crawl-Through

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

Five foot six is the height the house was checked from in June. This month they checked it from ten inches, which is a different house.

Papa went first, on his hands and knees, and stopped at an outlet he had never noticed. Mama went the other way. They met under the coffee table with the faces of people who had been wrong about their own living room.

Two outlets without covers. The lamp cord, hanging in a loop at exactly grabbing height. The bookcase, which had never seemed like a thing that could fall until you were the size of what it would fall on. A bead from somewhere. A coin.

The cardboard tube from a toilet roll was the last test. Anything that fits through the tube can fit down a throat that size, and anything that fits through the tube leaves the floor for good. The bead went through. The coin went through. A block did not, and stayed.

The outlets got their covers. The cord was wound up and tied behind the lamp. The bookcase was screwed to the wall with two brackets. The bead and the coin went into a jar on the top shelf, the first things she ever collected.

Olivia crawled the route behind them, checking their work.

She found a crumb they had missed.
