Night 345

The Library's Gift

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

Thursday was library day, three books out and three books back, the whole year through, and this Thursday the librarian had something under the desk.

"Once a year we give one away," she said. "For the ones who signed their card with a fist."

It was a board book about a bear who loses a button. Inside the cover, a bookplate, the gilt-edged kind, in the librarian's careful hand: For Olivia, one this March, from the Lantern Library, where her story started early.

"Yours," said the librarian. "Not a loan. You can chew it."

Olivia took the book in both hands, looked up into the dome, and said "buh!" at the top of her voice.

It went up, and around, and came back down off the round ceiling as "buh," in a smaller voice, and she looked at Mama to ask who that was. In the gap in the wainscoting by the low shelves, something with whiskers had come to the edge to see, and went back.

"That's the room," said Mama. "It says everything back."

"Buh," said Olivia, quieter, testing it, and the room agreed.

At home the bear-and-button book went into the bedtime pile, third from the top, where the new ones start.
