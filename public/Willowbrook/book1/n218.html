Night 218

The Cheerio Standard

Month 8: The Velcro Weeks and the Leaves

Olivia's thumb and forefinger had been practicing for weeks. At breakfast, they finally became a team.

Mama placed a few pieces of plain round oat cereal on the high-chair tray. Olivia was sitting upright and eating under close supervision. The cereal was made for beginning eaters and dissolved easily, but Mama still checked the package and tested a piece before serving it. No food is magically free of choking risk, and readiness, texture, position, and supervision all matter.

Olivia reached. At first her whole hand swept across the tray and sent two pieces to the floor. Then her thumb met her fingertip around the edge of one small circle.

Up it came.

She studied her prize, carried it to her mouth, and smiled. The next piece went into her water cup for reasons known only to scientists. A third traveled into Papa's open mouth, which produced such a ridiculous cheer that she immediately tried the experiment again.

The pincer grasp had arrived. Soon it would find lint, crumbs, leaves, and every pebble on every path in Willowbrook. For tonight, the floor received the misses and Olivia kept practicing until the last little circle was gone.
