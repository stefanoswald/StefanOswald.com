Night 203

The Spoon Rebellion

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

Breakfast was yogurt, and the spoon arrived with it, and Olivia put her hand on the spoon.

Not the mouth turned away. Not the lips shut. Those were June. This was a hand around the handle, above Mama's hand, holding on. Mine.

Mama had read about the second spoon and had one in her pocket, like an umbrella. Olivia got the second spoon, empty, and Mama kept the first, and the arrangement held for four seconds, until the empty spoon was found to be empty and was waved at the bowl.

So Mama loaded it. A dip, not a scoop, the scoop being months off, and handed the painted spoon over, and let go.

Up it went, turning like a slow propeller, and landed, on the first run, on the eyebrow. The second run found the ear. The third found the mouth, more or less, and she looked at Mama with yogurt on her eyebrow and in her ear and the face of a person who has just driven a car.

Yogurt eaten: some. Yogurt worn: most. Mama's spoon worked the gaps between runs and got breakfast in.

The rebellions will wear this face for years, the shoes she will insist on, the juice she will pour. It starts with a spoon that turns.

The bath was happening anyway.

She kept the spoon through the bath.
