Night 124

The Bedtime Dividend

Month 5: Nearly Sitting, Nearly Everything

Bath, quiet, book, song, lights out. Five months of the same five steps, and in July the steps started paying back.

It looked like this. The bathwater ran, and Olivia's body slowed at the sound, the way it had since the third week. The book came out and she batted the page she always batted. The song went wrong on the note it always went wrong on, since Papa had never once found the second verse. Then the crib at the foot of the bed, the sleep sack zipped, the yawn arriving on the zipper's cue, and down she went, awake, on her back, and found sleep on her own. Most nights now. Not every night.

Parents who keep the steps through the months when nothing seems to happen sometimes ask when the routine begins to work. It was working the whole time, underground, the way bulbs work. The fifth month is only when it shows.

Each step hands her down to the next, and the order itself does the soothing, so the order travels: a strange room, a grandparent's house, a bad week.

Tonight the whole thing ran eleven minutes. The July light was still in the window and the blind was down against it, and she was gone before the wrong verse, one hand curled on the sleep sack.
