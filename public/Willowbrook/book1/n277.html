Night 277

The Spelling Era

Month 10: First Snow

Mama did not mean to test her. It happened on its own, over the dishes.

"Where's the cat?"

The head turned to the radiator, where the tail was.

"Where's Papa?"

The stairs. Papa was at work, but the stairs were where Papa went, and at ten months that is the same thing.

"Give Mama the spoon."

The spoon arrived in Mama's hand. Then it left again, since at this age giving and taking back are one transaction.

"Is it bath time?"

And she was gone, down the hall on hands and knees at a speed the hall had not seen before, and had to be brought back.

That is where the spelling begins. Every house arrives at it through the same four letters.

"Is it b-a-t-h time?" Papa tried that night.

She looked at the hall.

"She can't spell," said Mama.

"She can spell tone," said Papa.

The words she said fit on one hand with fingers to spare. The words she followed ran off the page. Bring, give, where, bear, up, cat, cup, no, and the four letters, in any voice at all.

So at half past five the word went unsaid. Mama turned on the tap and let the water do the talking, and Olivia came down the hall with the spoon and sat outside the door to wait.
