Night 340

The Tower of Three

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

Two blocks she could do. Two blocks had been done since November and were no longer news. The third one was the trouble.

She put it on. It went off. She put it on, and the second one went with it, and she looked at the two blocks on the rug as if they had done it to her.

Mama was at the sink and said nothing, which was hard.

Third try: the bottom block was crooked, so the whole thing leaned, and fell before she let go. Olivia said "uh-oh," flat, not surprised. She sat back. She picked up the bottom block and set it down again, square, and pressed on it.

Then the second. Then the third, held over the top a long time, her mouth open, her hand shaking a little from trying not to shake.

She let go. It stood.

"Mama! Mama!" and the finger, pointing, and the face turned to the sink.

Mama was drying a pan and missed it.

The face fell about an inch. Mama put the pan down.

"I missed it. I'm sorry. Show me."

So Olivia knocked it down with one sweep and started again from the bottom, square, and Mama sat on the floor and watched the whole thing, twice.
