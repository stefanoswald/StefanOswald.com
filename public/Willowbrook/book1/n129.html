Night 129

The Yes Space

Month 5: Nearly Sitting, Nearly Everything

Olivia could roll from the mat to the low shelf in four turns now, and from the shelf to the lamp cord in two, and the living room had to answer.

The answer was one corner where nothing would ever have to be taken away from her. The play yard gates from the spring went wide around it. Inside, a bare rug. The low mirror on its wall. Three toys, on rotation. Room in every direction to roll.

It took an afternoon on hands and knees to make the corner true. Outlets capped. The lamp cord up and gone. The bookcase screwed to the wall stud, because furniture tips, and a tipping shelf is the quiet danger of this whole year, and the rule is anchor everything now, not later. The rug swept at pebble size, then swept again lower. The gate latched.

A baby in a room with no no's needs no hovering, and a baby unhovered plays deeper and tries the harder thing. The parent, meanwhile, gets the rarest thing the summer sells: kettle minutes. Bathroom minutes. Minutes of sitting down entire.

Papa sat on the couch and read a whole page of something, for the first time since March.

Olivia lay in the middle of her corner working the ring, rolled to the mirror, greeted herself, and rolled back.
