Night 301

The Small Party Rule

Month 10: First Snow

They sat down to plan the birthday on a snowing night. Nine weeks off. Catalogs open, the list between two coffees.

A first birthday is where a family decides how it will do celebrations from then on, usually without noticing.

The catalog offered favors for guests who will not remember attending, and, for a fee, a person in a costume. Papa turned that page face down.

The list stayed inside the fence line. The houses she knows by voice. Lily and Rosa. The farm. The far aunts, who had written to say they were coming. The sitter from the plant house. The total came to a number the living room could hold without anyone in a doorway.

The food would be theirs. A cake with one candle, the smashing agreed in advance, the tarp found. When they were done the catalogs went into the recycling, and one page of balloons was spared. Red, for reasons the whole year had made plain.

The candle box had been bought weeks early, on an errand of Papa's own invention, and it had sat on the shelf since.

"Small," said Papa.

"Small," said Mama. "And ours."

Two cold coffees, one page of balloons, and a box on the shelf with one candle in it.
