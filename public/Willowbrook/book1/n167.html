Night 167

Babble Goes to Church

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The Thursday library run had a shape now, three board books out and three back, and this Thursday, in the high wooden hush of the children's corner, Olivia discovered echoes.

"BA." Pause. The room gave it back, rounder, from the ceiling.

Her face went through the whole of discovery. "BA-BA-DA." The ceiling returned the chain with good manners. "MA-MA-BA-GRRR," and she was off, delivering to the reading room the loudest address of her career, while Mama did the parent's oldest dance, pride and shush in one gesture.

The librarian, consulted by apologetic eyebrow, waved it off. "That's language happening," she said. "We're pro-language here."

Babble in the sixth month is coming into its main season, the ba-ba-ba and ma-ma-ma of the books, and the volume experiments are the point: loud, soft, squealed, whispered, the young voice trying its instrument against every room it meets. Bathroom, stairwell, library, each a different answer to one question. What does my voice do here?

From the gap in the wainscoting by the low shelf, unnoticed as ever, Molly Mouse checked the young patron against her notes: loud, curious, book-adjacent. The library's future was secure.

Three books were stamped. The address had an encore on the steps, in the warm evening, under the lantern just lit, to applause from a porch across the square.

One syllable, repeated, all the way home.
