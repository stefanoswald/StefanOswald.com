Night 295

The Bear Is Fed

Month 10: First Snow

It happened at the dresser, at dusk, on the month's last Sunday.

The bear was fed. Nothing was in the cup. Everything was in the cup.

She stood at the dresser, one ear of his flopped, the heart on his foot, and she pressed the rim of the cup to his mouth and held it there. Then the mmm. Then the wipe, one small hand across his face, as a face gets wiped after a cup.

It was the whole routine, given back. Nearly three hundred nights and a thousand meals of being looked after, coming out the other side, aimed at a bear.

This is the first pretend. A cup with nothing in it, held to a mouth that cannot drink, by somebody who knows both of those things and does it anyway. It is the door the stories will come through, and the games, and the long talks with people who are not there.

The bear took the meal with his usual composure. He will spend six more books returning the favor.

"Mmm," she told him.

The bathwater started down the hall, and she yawned at the sound of it. She pressed the cup to his mouth once more and left it on the dresser, in case.
