Night 186

Two Steps Up

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

The gates went up the same week as the crawl, one at the top of the stairs and one at the bottom, screwed into the wall and not the pressure kind, because a pressure gate at the top of a staircase is a gate that leans. Papa tested the top one by leaning on it himself, hard, twice.

For three days the gates were furniture. Then she found the stairs.

Through the bars, up. The best climbing thing in the house and the only one she could not have, a sum every crawler does in a second. She sat at the gate every morning like a small dog at a butcher's window and told the stairs what she thought of them.

Papa's answer: the gate stays shut every hour of the week except one. On Sunday morning the bottom gate opened, and Papa sat on the second step, and Olivia climbed the first one on her hands and knees with his hand flat on her back the whole way. Then the second. Then she sat on the second step and looked at the hallway as if she had built it.

Coming down was not on the lesson yet. Papa lifted her, and the gate clicked.

Two steps. Every Sunday. The gate the other six days, rattled and never once open.

The stairs stayed where they were.
