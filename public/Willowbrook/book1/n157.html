Night 157

Water, Two Ounces, Neat

Month 6: The Feast Begins

With the feast came its companion, poured one thumb deep: water. Before this line it had no seat at all. The milk carried every drop she needed, and small kidneys prefer it that way. Even now, ounces and not bottles, a few swallows with meals, the milk still doing all the real work while water learns its trade at the edges.

The cup was the second half of the lesson. The open cup is hardest first and teaches truest, lip and tilt and sip, the whole grown-up business, wet. The straw cup takes a fair second chair. The old spouted sippy, beloved of diaper bags everywhere, has been sent by the dentists and the speech people to travel duty only, since it mostly teaches the bottle's old lesson over again.

Grip achieved, two-handed, white-knuckled. Tilt achieved, then overdone, then corrected by one guiding finger from Mama. Sip achieved, one true swallow, witnessed, followed by the chin cascade, the drops sitting on her bib like medals.

She pounded the cup on the tray. Everything she owned got pounded that month. The cup had not expected to be exempt.

Refill demanded. Refill granted, two ounces, neat. The milk waited on the counter, unbothered, still the thing that fed her.

One wet chin. Then another.
