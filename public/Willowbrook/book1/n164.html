Night 164

The Republic Expands

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The yes space had been one rug since July. By mid-August it was the whole living room, because its citizen had taken up travel.

Not the crawl. Not yet. She got up on hands and knees and rocked, forward and back. Then she launched, went backward, away from the ring she wanted, fetched up under the armchair, and roared. It is a hard thing to want a ring and be delivered the far wall.

So she rolled. Rolling was the transport of the month: over, and over, a log with plans, across the room to the ring or the cat or the square of sun.

Mama moved the gate wide to the near end of the hallway and swept the floor to pebble size, since everything down there still went in the mouth. No wheeled walker in this house, ever. They carry a baby to the stairs and the stove faster than any crawl and teach nothing about walking. Floor, floor, floor. Then she sat on the couch and did not fetch the ring. "It's right there," she said. "You go." A hard thing to watch, a baby wanting, and harder not to fix it.

Three launches backward. One roar. Then the roll, four turns, and a fist on the ring at last.

She lay on her back and chewed it, breathing hard, like somebody home from a long trip.
