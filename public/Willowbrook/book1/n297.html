Night 297

Again

Month 10: First Snow

The bear book, again.

She had not said again. She did not have to. She closed it, both hands, and handed it back, and looked at him, and that is the whole word.

Eleven times, on the eve of the holiday, with the porch lights doing their colors on the window. Papa has read the bear book more times than he has read anything, including his own wedding vows, which he read once.

Somewhere around the sixth reading he tried skipping the meadow page. She opened it back. Around the eighth he tried the duck in a different voice, and she looked up at him as if he had brought a stranger into the room, and he went back to the old duck.

Every reading is the same book and she is not the same. On the first she watched the pages turn. Now she turns them, two at a time, and knows the button is coming a page before it comes, and gets ready, and says "Buh" a half second early, and is pleased. The gasp page gets a gasp. The moon gets touched.

That is what the repeating is for. Nothing is being wasted. It is going in.

Then the twelfth time, from the hall, was Mama's. By heart, with the lamp off.
