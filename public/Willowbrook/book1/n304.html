Night 304

The Cold Moon

Month 10: First Snow

The cold moon came up on the last night of the year, small and white and very high, and the willow stood under it with snow along every branch, holding still so as not to drop any.

Nobody gathered. It was too cold for gathering. The brook was under its lid. The hedgehog was under his hedge. Far off in the dark pasture a red balloon did not move, because Ellie was asleep and had been since supper.

Oliver Owl sat on the oldest branch with snow on his spectacles and asked the only question he had.

"And is she warm?"

Stella hung low over Maple Lane, over the porches with their lights, over one window with its small dark, sixty-one nights from the candle.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.

The Sleepy Moon, who had not opened his eyes all winter, hummed the short hum, the one for a town that has already gone to bed.

Down on the lane the porch lights went off one by one, the Hendersons' last, because she has a system.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
