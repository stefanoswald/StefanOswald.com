Night 152

Is It Time?

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The six-month line arrived, and with it the question the high chair had been asking since Papa built it with three screws left over. Was it time?

Mama and Papa went through the list over coffee while Olivia, in the chair, did most of it for them unasked. She sat steady with barely a hand behind her. Her head had been solid for months. The old reflex that pushes everything back off the tongue had faded, tested with an empty spoon, mouthed and not shoved out. And the wanting. Food had crossed the table under her whole turned head all July, the reach arriving with real aim.

They read the six-month page again. Around six months the milk-alone era closes, the iron she was born with runs low, and the swallow and the gut are ready where at four months they were not. It is a readiness rule, not a birthday rule. The signs outrank the date in either direction, and the pediatrician holds the tiebreaker for early movers and late bloomers alike.

Decided over the mugs, two votes yes, one abstention from Olivia, distracted by a dog. Tomorrow's lunch, in the alert hours and not the tired dusk, with days ahead to watch how the first course settled.

The tray got wiped that night with more care than a tray deserves. The tiny spoon stood by.
