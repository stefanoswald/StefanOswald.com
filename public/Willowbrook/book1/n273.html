Night 273

Stella and the First Flakes

Month 9: One Step, One Word

The wind had gone quiet at last, and in the quiet something else had started. Stella came down through the cold clear dark to her low place over the lane and found the air full of small slow things that were not leaves.

"Oh," she said. "Already."

The Sleepy Moon hummed. It was the hum he keeps for things that come every year and still surprise him.

Below, the town settled. The barn at Honeywell Farm was shut with everything inside it. The leaf pile by the back fence held its sleeper. The porch mums were black, the pond had nine ducks in the reeds with their heads under their wings, and the willow on its hill moved a little, bare, and went still.

"She walked," Stella said. "And she said one of them. Duck. The other eight didn't mind."

The Moon hummed for that too.

On Maple Lane one window was dark and correct. The crib, flat and bare. The bear on the dresser with his one flopped ear, and beside him a green tomato, going nowhere. Olivia asleep on her back with one foot through the bars.

The flakes came down on the wagon, left out one more night, and did not stay, and then did.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.

By morning the rail was white again.
