Night 351

The Last Bottle

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The bottles had been given notice in January and had taken it well. Through February the daytime ones went over to the straw cup one at a time, a week each, no hurry, the morning one first and the afternoon one last. The book on the shelf said the bottles go by a year, or a year and a half at the latest, the bedtime one last, and there is no prize for doing it all in a week.

They were not going for early. The bedtime bottle was the oldest thing in the routine. It had been there for the three a.m. kitchens and the paced feeds.

Tonight Mama settled into the chair with it, and Olivia took three pulls, stopped, and pushed it away with the flat of her hand, and turned her head to the dresser, where the straw cup was.

Mama gave her the cup. She drank. She gave the cup back and put her head down in the crook of the arm, where the bottle used to go, and stayed.

That was the last one. The bottle did not know. It went on the rack, dried on the little plastic tree, and in the morning went up to the keepsake shelf beside the hospital band.

The crook of the arm was not on the shelf. It was in the chair, with her in it.
