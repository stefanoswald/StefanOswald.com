Night 158

The Sweet Potato Landslide

Month 6: The Feast Begins

Every feast finds its first love, and in the third week the answer came back orange.

The first spoonful silenced the table. The second was met halfway with the whole torso. By the third, all order collapsed. Both fists went into the bowl, the spoon was disarmed and dropped overboard, and Olivia met the sweet potato directly, palm to mouth to hairline, orange to the elbows, saying yes with every limb she had.

"Mmmmm," she said. Not babble. Commentary. Both parents would swear to it.

A first love in food is an anchor. The loved thing becomes the safe harbor the strange things get tried from, sweet potato lending its good name to the lentil riding beside it on the same spoon. The sweetness is no scandal. The milk was sweet all along, and the walk toward earthy is made in months of small crossings, not forced in a day.

The other side of the tray: broccoli had lost ground since its first week, and green beans were worn rather than eaten. Everything would be offered again, because a taste at this age is a mood, and the food refused seven times is loved on the eighth.

Papa ran the warm cloth from hairline to chin, and it came away orange, and he went back for more.
