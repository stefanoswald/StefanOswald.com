Night 6

The Kitchen at Three A.M.

Month 1: Arrival

There is a country only parents know. It is called Three in the Morning, and Papa had just moved there. He stood in the kitchen and swayed. Parents sway even when the baby is somewhere else, in the shower, in line at the bank, and cannot stop. He fed Olivia while the refrigerator hummed its one long note.

Night feeds go easier when everything is ready before bed. A glass of water where a hand can find it. A burp cloth on the arm of the chair. A lamp that can whisper instead of shout. Papa had done none of this on the first night and all of it by the fourth.

The strange thing about the country of Three in the Morning is its secret sweetness. The whole town is asleep, and one very small person is wide awake in your arms, looking up as if you were the only thing in it. There will come a night when no one needs you in the kitchen at three, and there will be no warning that it is the last one.

Papa yawned an enormous yawn. Olivia finished, burped, and slept. He stood a while longer in the dark with her, too tired to move and in no particular hurry to.
