Night 234

The Helping Crew

Month 8: The Velcro Weeks and the Leaves

The copying reached housework on an ordinary chore morning. Mama was wiping the coffee table. Olivia arrived at her elbow, hauled herself up by the table's edge, and held out one demanding hand toward the cloth.

Mama looked at the cloth, and at the hand, and at the clock, and gave up the clock. A second cloth was found. The table was wiped twice, at two levels of skill.

The helping spread through October from there. The push wagon was put on deliveries: blocks to the basket, two of five arriving, and two is a start. Dinner got an apprentice with one wooden spoon and one empty pot, stirring beside the real cooking.

None of it was efficient. That is the point of it. A baby who offers to help is asking for a job, and a baby who keeps being turned down for being slow and messy stops offering, and it is a long way to the years when the help is real. So the slow help was hired. The extra minutes were allowed for. The sock delivered to the wrong room was received as a sock delivered.

"New hire," Papa said at dinner. "Terrible at this. Keeping her."

The second cloth was folded, by Mama, and unfolded, by the crew.
