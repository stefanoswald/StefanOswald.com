Night 346

The Balloon Order

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The party had one decoration on its list, and Papa took Olivia to the Saturday market to order it. The winter market was three stalls and the balloon man, who came every Saturday of the year whether the weather made sense or not.

"Red," said Papa. "Nine. First of March."

"Course it's red," said the balloon man. He wrote it down. "Maple Lane, the porch with the wagon. I know the house."

Then he wrote something else at the bottom of the order, and turned the pad so Papa could see: plus one, long string, no charge.

Papa waited for an explanation. None came. In Willowbrook you do not ask who the tenth balloon is for. There is a pasture past the willow line, and the town is polite about it.

Olivia had found the bunch. Her mitten got a string, and pulled, and a red balloon came down and bumped her on the nose and went back up, and she looked at the balloon man, hard.

"Mine," she said, to the whole bobbing bunch.

"First of March," said the balloon man. "They'll be there."

Papa carried her home on his shoulders. She rode backward the whole way, watching the red get smaller until the corner took it.
