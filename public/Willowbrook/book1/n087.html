Night 87

Three Months of Nights

Month 3: Rhythm

Around now, the nights turned into a craft. Not easy. A craft.

The difference showed in the hands. Watch Mama run the late shift tonight. The sleep sack zipped in one pass. The lights already low. The feed taken in the quiet chair with the practiced pillow. The burp drawn out with two minutes of patient jazz. The lowering into the crib done in the old smooth motion, bottom first, then the head, hands sliding free like a magician finishing.

Three months ago every step of that was a cliff. Tonight it was a path, walked in the dark, known by the feet. A thousand small motions, each done badly, then less badly, then so well they vanished into the hands.

The nights had found a shape too. The long first stretch mostly held. One honest feed in the small hours. Dawn, early now, arriving more politely than it used to.

Not every night. Enough nights.

Olivia stirred once, found the world correctly arranged, and went back down without further comment. Mama stood a moment by the crib, then crossed to her own bed on the other side of the room, which had never seemed farther on the bad nights, or nearer on the good ones.

The moon, an old hand at night shifts, tipped its light through the window and went back to its own.
