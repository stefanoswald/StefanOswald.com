Night 140

The Almost-Crawl

Month 5: Nearly Sitting, Nearly Everything

Hands planted. Knees under. Belly, for the first time in her life, lifted clear of the earth. And then: rocking. Forward, back. Forward, back.

The position turned up this week, unannounced, a rocket running its countdown and declining, every time, to go.

It is early for it. Some babies rock like this for weeks before anything travels, and all of that is right. The strength is there, five months of tummy time paying out. The steering is not.

Papa did the one thing the almost-crawl asks of a parent. He set the ring just past her fingertips and sat on his hands.

She rocked. She reached. The ring stayed where it was. She growled, a new sound, from the back country, and rocked harder, and the ring stayed where it was, and Papa said, "I know. It's right there," and did not move it.

A baby annoyed at a toy she cannot reach is a baby building the reach. Rescue every reach and you rescue away the reaching. Within reason. The fourth lunge with tears on it gets the ring delivered and the campaign called off for the night, because the point is fuel, not despair.

Tonight it was the third lunge. Papa slid the ring the last inch. She fell on it like a hawk and lay chewing it, belly down, done.
