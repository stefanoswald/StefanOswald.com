Night 193

Arm's Reach

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

She outgrew the little tub overnight. Her heels were on the rim and her elbows were on the rim and there was no room for the duck, and a bath without Esquire is not a bath.

So on Tuesday the little tub was retired and Olivia went into the big one, sitting up, in water that came to her hips and no higher, with Papa's hand at her back and the duck bobbing at her knee.

She hit the water flat with both palms and it hit her back. There is a great deal of water in a big tub, seen from inside it, and she got through the afternoon's supply of squeals in a minute.

The rule from the first bath had not changed and will not. A hand on her or within reach of her the whole time, and the grown-up in the room does not leave it. Not for the towel, hung on the door before the tap goes on. Not for the phone, left in another room. A baby who sits well still tips, and water does its work quietly.

Papa sat on the bathmat and got wet to the shoulder.

After, in pajamas on his damp knee, she looked at the little tub as if they had never met.

The cat found it in the basement by morning, and sleeps in it now.
