Night 359

Papa's Letter

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The next night it was Papa's turn at the table, and he sat a while before he started.

Dear Olivia,

Your mother wrote hers last night. I am not going to be able to do it like she does, so this is mine.

Week two, you fell asleep on my chest with your hand round my thumb, and I sat in the chair for an hour and did not move, and that was the moment I stopped being scared of dropping you. I never did drop you, and I would like that remembered.

I sang you the socks song and the burping song. I made the eyebrow thing permanent. I built you a fort out of every blanket in the house and you crawled straight out the back of it. I stood outside the window in November pretending the car needed looking at, because my eyes were doing something I had not planned.

You walked into your mother's hands and I heard about it on the phone, and I have seen every step since, and I mean to see the rest, including the ones away from us.

The bear moves in next week. Be gentle with his ear.

I cannot whistle. You will find that out.

Papa

He folded it, and put it in the drawer beside the other one, and shut the drawer on the two of them.
