Night 258

Three Meals and the Cup

Month 9: One Step, One Word

All that walking arrived in the kitchen as appetite.

Three meals had held since October, at the table, at the family's times. Milk, breast or bottle, was still the main food and would be until one. The meals were practice, taste, and company. Some days what went in would not have fed a sparrow, and the milk carried her.

The open cup came out at lunch. Small, a splash of water in it, no lid, both hands on it and Mama's hand under. The first try went down her collar. The second up her nose. The third into her mouth, and her face was that of a person who has discovered water. Straw cup for the stroller. Open cup at the table, a splash at a time, a towel under everything.

Between lunch and supper the walking made a hole, and the hole got a small fourth meal at the low table: seated, cheese and fruit, over when she stood up. A snack that follows a walker around the house is a graze, and grazers do not arrive at supper hungry.

The cup went over twice. Once by accident. Once, Mama was fairly sure, to see.

"More," she signed at the cheese, both fists together, and got more.

At supper she drank from the cup with both hands and nobody's under it, and set it down on its side.
