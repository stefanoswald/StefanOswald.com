Night 159

Sitting Up for the Doctor

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The six-month visit came round, and Olivia attended it sitting up, her own doing, her first appointment conducted from the vertical.

The sit was confirmed live on the table. The reach was demonstrated on the doctor's stethoscope, taken twice. The babble chains were performed for the doctor. There was the name-turn, and the stranger-study, the doctor inspected at length and provisionally approved.

Then the feast news. Solids landed, iron leading, allergens going in one at a time, gag met and survived, water in a cup. Keep going with all of it, said the doctor, and gave them the season's advice. The crawl was coming, so floor time onward and downward. The first tooth was on approach, the gums read like weather, with the fluoride talk attached and the first dental visit set for after the first tooth and no later than the first birthday.

Then the shots, the six-month round, the third verse of the old song. The wail ran sixty seconds and was operatic. Then the nursing, and the parking-lot sleep of the twice-protected.

"Halfway to one," said the doctor at the door. Doctors get to say the thing parents cannot quite say aloud. Twice her birth weight, said the scale. Papa asked if that made her twice the work. The doctor left that to the family.

Olivia slept the whole drive home, a bandage on each thigh.
