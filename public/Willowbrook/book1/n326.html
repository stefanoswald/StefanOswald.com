Night 326

The Yogurt Incident

Month 11: Mittens and the Early Dark

The spoon had been hers to hold for a month, not to use. One morning Mama loaded it with yogurt, set it on the tray, and stepped back, and what happened next is known in the family as the yogurt incident.

The spoon went up. The spoon went sideways. The yogurt went into the left eyebrow, where it stayed, thoughtfully. The second load went into the hair on the way to the mouth, and the third reached the mouth by way of the ear.

Mama said nothing, because that was the rule she had set herself. Watch. Do not grab the spoon. Do not wipe until it is over. This is how a person learns a spoon, and it cannot be clean.

Load four went in. Mouth, first try. The face that came up out of the yogurt was so surprised it forgot to chew.

Load five went on the cat.

By the end of the pot there was yogurt on the wall behind the high chair, at a height nobody could explain, and on the floor tarp, and in the folds of a neck, and one clean circle on the tray where the bowl had been.

"You did the spoon," said Mama, from a safe distance.

The bath was moved up an hour. The cat did not wait for it.
