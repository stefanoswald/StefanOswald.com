Night 208

The Windy Day

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

The wind came up on Saturday from the hill and took the first leaves off every maple on the lane.

Papa took Olivia out in it because the house was tired of them both.

Wind in the face does a thing to a baby. She gasped. She shut her eyes and opened her mouth and the wind went in, her curls went flat, and she gasped again and laughed at the gasp. Every gust the same. Gasp, laugh, gasp. Papa turned the stroller into it on purpose, which he is not proud of, and she said "Ah!" at the sky.

A leaf landed on her front and stayed. She looked down at it as if it had been delivered.

Then the hat.

Papa's hat came off at Mrs. Henderson's gate and went down the lane at a low fast run, and he went after it, and it stopped, and he bent, and it went again. Mrs. Henderson watched from her porch and did not help. Olivia watched from the stroller, turned to face the show, brake on, and made her sound at every escape.

He got it at the corner by stepping on it.

He came back with the hat in his hand and leaves in his beard. She looked at the beard.

The leaf was still on her sweater at bath time.
