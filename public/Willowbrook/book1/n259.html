Night 259

Reading on the Move

Month 9: One Step, One Word

Three hundred nights of the book, and mid-month the lap was abandoned. Mid-page. Papa's knee vacated at stride three, the bear book holding its meadow open to nobody, the audience orbiting the armchair.

Story time was not over. He kept reading, at full production, to the orbit, the pages angled outward at the passing trade. And the orbit, it turned out, was listening. It landed on his lap as the good page came near, stayed two spreads, left, and came back for the button. At the mallard page she said "duck" from across the room, sight unseen, from memory.

Movement at this age is not the opposite of attention. It is the age. So the low shelf of books stayed within orbit, the lap stayed open at no charge, and the stays got longer on their own.

Papa read a whole page of the bear book to an empty chair on Wednesday before he noticed she had gone to the kitchen. He finished the page. It was a good page.

One part of the night did not change at all. The last book, the crib-side one, was still read at the rail, bath-warm, sleep-sacked, the orbit's fuel spent.

By the month's end she climbed up for the whole button page and stayed.

He read on and did not remark on it.
