Night 67

The Baby in the Mirror

Month 3: Rhythm

There was another baby in the house, and she lived in the mirror by the play mat.

Olivia had noticed her before, vaguely. This month the noticing turned into a friendship. Tummy time, once a chore endured for a parent's sake, flew by when the mirror baby came. Every head-lift was rewarded with a face rising to meet hers. Every smile was answered on the instant by the friendliest baby in Willowbrook.

She had no idea it was her. That discovery, the strange one where the friend turns out to have been home all along, waits until well past the first birthday. For now the mirror baby was simply excellent company: reliable, expressive, and always as pleased to visit as Olivia was.

This is why an unbreakable low mirror is quiet gold in a play space. It doubles the faces, and faces are a baby's favorite scenery. It makes tummy time worth doing. And nobody has to be entertaining, which after three months is a mercy.

"Who's that?" Mama asked, the oldest question there is.

Olivia smiled at the baby. The baby smiled back, perfectly timed, as always. Then Olivia put her whole mouth on the glass, and the other baby, being the same sort of person, did too.
