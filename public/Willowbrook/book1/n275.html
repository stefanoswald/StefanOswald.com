Night 275

The First Snow

Month 10: First Snow

It came in the night, and by morning Maple Lane had been put away under it. Papa saw it first, at the window with his coffee, and said the thing he had been saving since March. "Olivia. Come and see."

She came at her new speed, hand over hand along the couch, and stopped at the glass. The lane had gone. In its place was a white one with the porches sunk into it, and the air was full of something coming down slowly and landing nowhere she could see.

The suit took eleven minutes and some opinion. Then the front step.

He held her hand and she stood, and a flake came down onto her bare palm, the mitten being off already. It sat there, a small cold star, and then it was water, and she looked up at him as if he had done it.

He caught one on his tongue to show her how. She watched this with great seriousness and put her wet palm in her mouth instead. Same idea, at ten months.

Cold, said her face. Then, plainly, more.

They stayed out until the mitten had gone back on and come off twice more, and went in to the radiator. The snow kept falling past the window all morning, and she watched it from the couch with one hand flat on the glass.
