Night 5

Skin and Song

Month 1: Arrival

The evening was cold and fussy, and so was everyone in it. So Mama tried the oldest remedy on the shelf. She unwrapped Olivia down to her diaper and laid her on her own bare chest, skin to skin, a blanket over the two of them, and sat back in the rocking chair.

And there it was. Olivia's breathing found Mama's breathing. Her quick, scribbly heartbeat slowed to match the steady drum underneath it. A warm chest and a familiar thump are the sound of home to a newborn. It is the first music she ever heard, from the inside. Skin to skin steadies a baby's temperature, calms her heart, and settles her whole small weather. It does the same for the person holding her, and hardly anybody mentions that part.

Mama kept her eyes open, or nearly. The rule of the chair was simple: the moment the holder felt sleep coming, the baby went to her crib and the holder went to bed.

Papa took a turn later, Olivia curled on his chest like a comma in a long sentence. No skill required. No song required either, though he whistled one anyway, and Papa's whistling sounds like a kettle that has changed its mind. She liked it very much.
