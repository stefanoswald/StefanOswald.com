Night 319

Bobby Bear's Fourth Letter

Month 11: Mittens and the Early Dark

Dear Olivia,

Fourth letter. Probably the last from the dresser, and a bear has never had so much to tell.

You stand. From up here I saw you let go of the couch and take two steps into nothing and sit down surprised. I would have clapped. Bears cannot clap. I flopped my ear instead.

You say something at me at dusk. It has a b in it. I am taking it as my name.

Last week you fed me from your cup. There was nothing in the cup. I have never been so full in my life.

Now the news. In six weeks and a bit, you will be one. I hear everything from the Moon, and the Moon says that on that day I am allowed in. Until then the crib stays bare and flat and boring, exactly as a crib should be, and I have kept watch on it from up here, and I would do it again.

There is talk of a promotion before then. Something about a shelf nearer to you. I am ready. I have been practicing being softer. My heart is on my foot and my ear will not stay up, and I am told both are on purpose.

Sleep, little one. Talk to me at dusk. I hear everything.

B. Bear, the dresser, for now
