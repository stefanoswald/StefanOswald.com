Night 59

The Moon Hums Month Two Closed

Month 2: Smiles and Sleep

High over Willowbrook the Sleepy Moon was humming, low, as it did when a month was nearly folded up and put away.

Stella came down through the rinsed April dark to the window on Maple Lane, the one she had found on the very first night. The lights below were steadier than last month. Fewer lamps burned confused at three in the morning. More of them burned calm, one window here, one there, doing the quiet work: a feed, a sway, a song flat in all the right places.

Inside this window was the crib, bare and correct, in the corner of the room where Mama and Papa slept. The bear sat on the dresser with one ear flopped, keeping his watch. The baby was bigger than last month by about a moon's worth, asleep on her back, with both hands, the new ones, the found ones, resting open on her chest.

Mama was asleep. Papa was nearly. The cat was somewhere. Down the lane, Rosa's kitchen light went out too.

"They're doing all right," Stella said, to the roofs.

"Mmmmm," hummed the Moon, low and long.

Stella twinkled once, for yes. The hum went softer, and softer, and softest, and settled over the roofs.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
