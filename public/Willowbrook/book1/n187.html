Night 187

The Swoop

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

Tied things come loose. The lamp cord was behind the lamp on Monday, and on Wednesday evening it hung down again, a loop of pure invitation, and Olivia went for it.

"No!"

It came out of Mama louder than she meant, and Olivia stopped, and looked at her, and cried. Not because of the cord. Because of the sound.

At six months the word no is nearly all weather and no meaning. She heard thunder from the person she loves best and did what anyone would.

Mama got down on the floor. "That was loud. I'm sorry." She picked her up, turned her, and set her down at the far end of the rug beside the wooden spoons, and the spoons won.

The swoop is most of it. Lift, turn, put down somewhere better, and hardly a word. The cord went back behind the lamp, tied twice this time, and the fern on the low shelf went up on top of the fridge, where it lives now and is doing well. Every no said daily is a thing you could have moved instead. The room does the work, and then there is nothing to say no to.

Thirty seconds later she went for the cord again and found lamp.

She looked at the lamp for a while and went back to the spoons.
