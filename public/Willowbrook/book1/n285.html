Night 285

The Fortress of Cozy

Month 10: First Snow

The plow did not come on Tuesday. Papa did not go to work. The lane filled up quietly all morning, and by lunch the living room had become something else.

Two chairs, the couch, the good blanket, six clothespins, and one spare string of lights from the attic box, run along the inside of the roof and plugged in behind the couch where no small hand could follow it, and Mama checked that twice.

Papa is a master of blanket forts. This was the first one, and it was a fortress, and he named it so.

The cat went in first. This is true of every fort ever built.

Olivia went in second, on hands and knees, and stopped in the doorway with her face lit small colors and her mouth open. Then she went the rest of the way in and sat, and looked up, and did not come out for some time.

Papa, who had built it, did not fit. He got his head and one arm in and read the bear book from there, sideways, with his socks in the hall. Mama fit, and said so more than once.

The snow came down past the window all afternoon in the blue light. Nobody shoveled anything.

Inside, the small lights, and three people, and the cat, who had been there first.
