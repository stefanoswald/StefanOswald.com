Night 316

The Snowed-In Saturday

Month 11: Mittens and the Early Dark

The snow came sideways all Saturday, and the family stayed put. The car was a hill. A day at home with a baby is either very long or very good, and this one went the good way because Papa built a fort before breakfast.

A couch fort, blanket over the cushions, chairs for roof beams, the book basket dragged inside. Olivia crawled in and out of it forty times, each entrance a discovery.

The day kept its shape anyway. At the word "nap" she looked at the stairs. At the sound of the bathwater she yawned, as she has since April. And when Mama said "book" in the fort, she went to the basket, dug, and came up with the duck book, held out flat in both hands.

"That one? The duck?"

"Duh," said Olivia. The whole word, at this address.

The duck book was read four times. At bedtime Mama held up two books at the crib, the duck and the bear, and one finger went straight to the duck.

She knows what comes next, and has opinions about it. Nothing the family did all year works harder than the same order every night. This is what it buys.

Papa took the fort down after lights out. The chairs went back. The snow kept coming.
