Night 278

The Hat Refusal

Month 10: First Snow

The hat.

It went on. It came off. It went on, and came off, and the third time she looked straight at Mama while she did it, and that is not a hat falling off.

The walk waited in the open door, white and bright. Suit on, boots on, stroller ready, and the whole outing had come down to four inches of red wool and a will.

Mama got down on the floor.

"I know, you hate it. Outside is hats. This one or the bear one?"

The bear one had ears. The red one had been the whole argument. She looked at both for a long moment, reached for the red one, and pulled it down over her own eyes.

Then Mama did the thing that works about one time in three. She put the bear hat on her own head.

Olivia stared. Then she wanted it. Both hands, the sound she makes for injustice, the red hat abandoned on the floor. So the bear hat came off Mama and went on her, and stayed on, because now it had been taken from someone.

A ten-month refusal runs on a ten-month attention span. Four minutes, start to gate.

She wore the ears round the block, and at the corner she pulled the hat off and held it up so Mama could see it.
