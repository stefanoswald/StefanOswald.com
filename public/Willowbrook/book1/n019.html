Night 19

Tummy Time Begins

Month 1: Arrival

The blanket went down in the morning's one patch of sun, like a flag on a new planet, and Olivia went down on her tummy upon it. She objected. Loudly, and at length. Tummy time is baby weightlifting, and the gym is hard to love at first.

Those minutes on the belly build the neck, and the neck holds up the head, and the head is in charge of everything after that: rolling, sitting, crawling, and one distant day running away from the bath. Mama kept the sessions tiny and kind. A few minutes, a few times a day, always awake, always watched. She lay down on the rug herself, nose to nose, which improved morale considerably. On days Olivia would not have it, the gym was simply Olivia on Papa's chest while he lay on the couch, and that counts completely.

Today she lifted her head. One wobbling inch, like a bulldozer starting up, and she held it there, astonished at herself, looking straight at Mama. Mama looked straight back, said "Hello, you," and did not reach in to help.

Then Olivia put her head down and told the rug exactly what she thought of it. Mama scooped her up, laughing, and the workout was over.
