Night 356

The Town Delivers

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

Nobody in Willowbrook was asked to bring anything. That has never stopped Willowbrook.

On Monday the farm truck came up the drive with three dozen eggs. "Henrietta's," said Grandpa Joe. "The Duchess sends nothing. She's against parties." He was gone before the coffee.

Tuesday it was Rosa at the door with two boxes of rolls and flour on her cheek, in the cold, in her apron. "For the day. Cake's Saturday, and I'm not saying more."

Wednesday Mrs. Henderson swept their walk before anybody was up, the last grey snow and the grit, with the gold dog lying across the bottom step to make sure it was done properly.

Thursday the plant house sent home four cards with a handprint on each, from the other four, in four colors, one of them mostly on the envelope.

Olivia went to the door for all of it, on her feet, fast, and at Rosa's boxes she said "hi," and at the cards she said "hi" again, so it went in the notebook by the kettle, a new line, given to a box of rolls.

"Everybody," said Mama at the door, every time, and stopped explaining it after Tuesday.

Friday there was a stick on the mat. The gold dog had not been asked to bring anything either.
