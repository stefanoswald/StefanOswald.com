Night 230

The Bath Fleet Review

Month 8: The Velcro Weeks and the Leaves

On Saturday night Papa held a review of the bath fleet. Nobody had asked for one.

The fleet was lined up along the rim of the tub in order of seniority. Esquire the duck, first, on account of his rank. The pouring cup. The three stacking cups with the holes in their bottoms. One small red boat that had come free with something. And a washcloth, which had asked to be included.

Olivia sat in her three inches of water and watched the line-up with the face of somebody who has been promised something.

"Esquire," said Papa. Esquire was inspected and found floating. He went into the water and did so.

"Cup." The cup was launched, took on water at once, and sank with dignity. Papa saluted it.

"Boat." The boat was launched and rammed by the duck.

"Stacking cups." Papa did not get to finish. One arm came across the rim in a single sweep and the whole fleet went in together, cups, boat, washcloth, and a rubber ring nobody had reviewed, and the tub took them all with a slap.

Rain fell out of the stacking cups. The duck came up first, as he always does.

"Review over," said Papa, from within the hand's reach, wet to the elbow.

Afterward, in pajamas on his knee, she reviewed the fleet again, drying on the rim, Esquire first.
