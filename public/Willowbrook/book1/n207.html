Night 207

The Straw Cup

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

Water with meals had begun in August, a few sips, milk still nearly everything she lives on, from a cup Mama held to her lip and tipped, which got most of the water into the bib.

This month the straw cup arrived, a gift from Rosa, who said Lily had taken to it in a day. Rosa says a lot of things.

The first attempt was to chew the straw. The second was to chew it harder. The third was to turn the cup upside down and watch the water come out of the straw onto the tray, which was interesting.

Mama's trick, from a video she will not admit to: dip the straw, cover the top with a finger, put the wet end in her mouth, let go. Water arrives. The face that gets water from a straw for the first time has been handed a magic trick.

Then nothing for two days. Then, on Thursday, a suck of her own, and a mouthful, most of it down her chin, and she looked at the cup with enormous respect.

A few sips at meals. That is all it is for. The rest is play, and the tray is a lake by the end of breakfast, with a bib in it.

She banged the empty cup on the tray.

Mama filled it again, a little.
