Night 18

The Night Nest

Month 1: Arrival

Olivia's bed was almost empty, and that was the best thing about it.

One firm, flat mattress. One fitted sheet. One baby, laid down on her back. No pillows. No bumpers. No loose blankets. No stuffed bear. Bobby Bear kept watch from the dresser, where he could be charming without being a hazard. Safe cribs are boringly, wonderfully bare.

For now the crib stood in Mama and Papa's room, close enough for feeding and comforting, but separate from their bed. Room sharing without bed sharing is safest, ideally for at least the first six months. If Olivia fell asleep in the car seat, the swing, the stroller, or the carrier, they moved her to this flat bed as soon as they could, even when it meant waking her, even when Papa had to do it with his eyes mostly shut.

The room was cool and dim. Olivia wore a sleep sack, and nothing covered her face. It looked plain as a bare field, that little bed. It was really a fortress, built out of everything her parents had carefully left out.

She slept in the middle of it with her arms flung wide, taking up all that safe empty space as if she had planned it.
