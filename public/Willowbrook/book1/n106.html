Night 106

Filmed Before a Live Audience

Month 4: Rolling and Laughing

The camera roll passed four thousand that month. Every laugh got filmed. The rolls, the ba-ba-ba, the foot-catch, the duck going under, filmed and filmed. These months evaporate. The videos are the dew collected, and some February night years off, three seconds of this laugh will be worth more than either of them could say.

So there was Papa on his knees on the blanket with the phone up, and Mama off to the side doing the tickle spider, and Olivia laughing at the ceiling.

Then the phone died, mid-laugh.

Papa lowered it, and she was looking at him. Not at the small black square. At his face, now that it was back. Her laugh went softer, went private, the way it does when the joke is between two people and nobody is watching. He had been slightly elsewhere, behind the glass, and she had known before he did.

"There you are," he said, which seemed to be what she was waiting for.

The house made a bargain after that. Thirty seconds of filming, generous. Then the phone goes face down on the arm of the chair and the encore plays live, to its intended audience of two.

Tonight ran about one to nine, filmed to not. Later, watching her sleep, the phone still face down where he had left it, neither of them wished the numbers the other way.
