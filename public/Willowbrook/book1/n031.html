Night 31

Stella Takes the Watch

Month 1: Arrival

High over Willowbrook, where the wind goes to slow down, a small gold star noticed a new light on Maple Lane. The low, warm, three-in-the-morning kind of light.

"A new one?" Stella asked.

The Sleepy Moon hummed, which means yes, and also means most other things.

Stella dipped lower, as stars do for babies, until her glow rested on one particular window ledge. Inside was a crib, plain and bare. A bear on the dresser. Two grown people asleep in improbable positions, one of them still holding a burp cloth. And one very small person flat on her back in the middle of the crib, arms out, like a starfish who owns the place.

Stella settled in for the long watch, the one that lasts all the years of growing up. The window light went out. The town turned over in its sleep.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
