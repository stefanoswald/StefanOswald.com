Night 176

The Gallery of Food Faces

Month 6: The Feast Begins

By the end of the month the house had assembled its finest art collection: the food faces.

In the Betrayal Wing hung green beans, the founding piece, the shudder running shoulder to shoulder. Beside it, lemon's accidental masterpiece: one licked wedge, the whole face folding inward, then reaching for more. The Suspicion Wing held egg at its first sitting, examined in midair with mistrust, and yogurt, approached across three meals, a little nearer each time.

The Rapture Wing is the permanent collection. And peach, August's own, which required a moment alone afterward, all parties.

The parent's job here is the curator's. Keep hanging the show, one bite's worth, no pushing, no bargaining, the beloved anchor alongside, and let opinion drift as it drifted for broccoli, back in favor by week five, no explanation offered.

The second rule is softer, and Papa broke it on Wednesday. Green beans came around again, the shudder ran, and he laughed out loud, and her face crumpled. Not at the beans. At the laugh.

He put the spoon down. "That was me. I laughed at you. Sorry, love." He waited until she came back, and was deadpan as a judge for the rest of the beans, and saved the laughing for the hall, with Mama.

Green beans stayed in the Betrayal Wing all month. Admission was still one bite.
