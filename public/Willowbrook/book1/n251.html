Night 251

Duck

Month 9: One Step, One Word

The pond at Dandelion Park keeps its ducks into the cold, the fat ones who do not care for travel, and on Tuesday there were nine in a huddle at the reedy end, complaining. The stroller stopped there as always. The finger went out as always at anything moving.

Then, aimed straight down the line of the finger, in the clear voice of her morning announcements: "Duh. Duck."

Mama stood very still.

Then, steadier than she felt: "Duck. Yes. Duck." Olivia aimed again, satisfied, and said it again, at the next duck, and the next. All nine were named, the word spent like a new coin and honored every time.

Ma-ma and da-da had come months ago and mostly meant the lamp. This was different: a word for a thing in the world, the whole kind of thing, and it went home with them. Esquire got it in the bath. The mallard page in the bear book got it at bedtime.

The advice is the oldest in the house. Answer everything. Every duck confirmed, every syllable caught and handed back. You said something. It mattered. Someone is on the other end.

Mama wrote DUCK in the notebook by the kettle, on the page that had waited blank since summer, and did not manage anything else.

Papa read it standing up, in his coat.
