Night 90

Stella at the Window

Month 3: Rhythm

Three months is a season, and stars know their seasons.

Stella drifted down through the May dark to the window on Maple Lane, the way she had on the first night, and looked in. The crib, plain and bare, beside the big bed. The bear on the dresser, one ear down, on duty. Two grown people asleep in ordinary positions now, which was new. And one small person in the middle of the crib, fast asleep, a little longer than last month, a little rounder, one hand open on the sheet.

Stella had heard the laugh through the glass. She had seen the mat lose its borders. She had watched the lamp burn briefer and calmer each week.

On her way past she looked in at a window across town, where another small person slept with one sock off. She keeps an eye on them in pairs when they arrive that close together.

"All quiet," she said up to the Sleepy Moon, no louder than a breath.

The brook whispered over its stones. The Great Willow on the hill glowed faintly gold and let the night pass.

Stella settled at her post above the roof, dimmed a little, and stayed.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
