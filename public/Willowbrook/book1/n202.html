Night 202

The Laughing Game Named Again

Month 7: Explorers and Guardrails

The cloth went up, over her own face this time, held in two fists, and the room waited.

"Where's Olivia?"

Under the cloth, audibly, the mischief gathered. One sock foot pumped. The breath went quick. Then, before anybody could do it for her, she pulled the cloth down herself.

"There she is!"

The laugh came up from her feet, the full-body one, and the cloth was already going back up.

The oldest game in the house has changed hands. For four months it was done to her, Mama hiding, Mama appearing, Olivia the audience. This month she took over the appearing, and works it like a person who has waited all her life for a job. Cloth up. Wait. Down.

The wait is the thing. Under the cloth she is practicing the strangest fact she knows, that she is still here while hidden, and Mama too, and that she can hold that for a second, two, and then spring it. It is the shape of every joke she will ever tell.

Nine shows that evening, the waits getting longer. The ninth went so long that Mama broke and peeked first, and the cloth came down on a face that found this the funniest thing that had ever happened anywhere.

The tenth was called by a yawn, mid-hide.

The cloth came down slowly over a director already half asleep.
