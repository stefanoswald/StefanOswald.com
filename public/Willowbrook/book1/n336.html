Night 336

The Remember Game, Part One

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

At the book part of bedtime Olivia reached past the bear book and pulled the album off the low shelf instead. It was heavy. She sat down with it. So the album came up onto the laps, and that is how the remember game started, by accident, like most family things.

The first page was the hospital band, small enough for a doll. Olivia patted it.

"That was yours," said Mama. "You came home in a cold rain, in March, and Papa carried you up the steps like a bowl of soup."

"Very full soup," said Papa.

"And you didn't know a single word, so we named everything for you. Lamp. Rain. Cat. Papa's nose." Mama turned the page. "You looked at a thing, and we said its name, and you looked at us, and we said it again."

"That's the whole game," said Papa. "You look, we say."

Olivia was looking at a photograph of Esquire the duck in a bath.

"Duh," she said.

"There it is," said Mama. "That's the one that came back."

The pages were thick and slow. Olivia turned three at once, and nobody corrected her. Under the lamp Papa said the names again, quietly, the lamp, the rain, the cat, and by the cat her eyes had closed.
