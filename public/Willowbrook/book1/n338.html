Night 338

The Remember Game, Part Three

Month 12: The Brook Comes Unstuck

The third night was the firsts, and they went fast, because there were a lot of them and Olivia was standing up.

"First smile," said Mama, at a page. "A Tuesday in April, over a diaper, at me."

"First laugh, May. I was there. I spent June trying to buy another one."

"First roll. Onto the cat."

"The cat has forgiven nobody."

The high chair page, with sweet potato in her eyebrows. The page where she sat up alone on the rug with both hands free and a face like a person who has just been handed the keys. The September page, crawling out of the frame. The rail of the crib in October, and the mattress lowered the same night.

"And a Wednesday," said Mama, "before lunch. Three steps into my hands."

"I heard it on the phone," said Papa. "Then duck, and the empty page in the notebook wasn't empty."

Olivia let go of Papa's knee. She took one step to Mama, then one back to Papa, then stood in the middle with her arms out, deciding.

She patted the pond page, twice, where the ducks were.

Then she sat down, hard, her usual ending, and stayed there, and the album stayed open on the rug beside her.
