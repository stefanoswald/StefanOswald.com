Night 168

The Car Seat in August

Month 6: The Feast Begins

The puffy coat came in August, because aunts plan ahead. It went on the hook by the door for October with its rule already attached: coat off at the car door, straps snug over thin layers, coat over the top like a blanket. A puffy coat is mostly air, and a strap tight over air is loose over a child the moment the car stops hard. The thin layers underneath press down honestly, so they are the ones that stay on.

August had its own question, and it lived in the driveway.

The car had stood in the sun all morning. Mama's hand went to the buckle first, and the buckle bit. So the doors stood open while a cup of water went over the hot metal, the sunshade went up on her window, and the harness was pinch-tested at the collarbone against one thin onesie. Snug on the actual child. Rear-facing, still.

The chest check from the newborn pages: a hand down the collar at the far end. Damp at the neck meant one layer lighter next trip, since a buckled baby heats faster than the driving parent suspects. And she was never left in the car, not for a minute, not with the windows down.

Olivia rode to the farm that Sunday in shade of her own, watching the corn go past.

The coat waited on its hook.
