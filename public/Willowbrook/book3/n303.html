Night 303

The Before-and-After Game

Month 10: Try Again

At the lamp, Papa said, "Before and after.

I say before. You say after."

"Once, a girl couldn't pedal."

"Now I pedal! Bring-bring!"

"Once, a kite wouldn't fly."

"Den it hummed."

"Once, a puzzle was too hard."

"Too hard today," said Olivia. "Den I did it."

"Once, a girl said pasketti."

"Spaghetti," said Olivia, perfectly.

Mama had one.

"Once, a girl crumpled her ducks."

"Now dey in da folder."

Olivia had one too.

"Once, Papa's shelf fell."

"And Papa was loud," said Papa.

"And you did da breaf."

"Once, a girl held the pool edge so tight," said Mama.

"Now I float. Mama's hands under."

"Once, a girl couldn't whistle," said Papa.

Olivia tried. Air, and a little spit.

"Still can't."

"Yet," said Mama and Papa, at the same time.

She was asleep before the next one.
