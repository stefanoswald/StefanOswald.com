Night 145

Papa's Double Shift

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Saturday. Mama was tired all the way down to her toes.

"Go," said Papa. "Sleep, or sit at Rosa's."

Mama went.

So it was Papa, Theo in the carrier, and Olivia by the hand,

at the market on the lane, early, under the awnings.

Theo needed a new diaper by the peaches.

Papa laid the changing mat on a bench.

Right in the middle of it, Theo made a fountain.

It got Papa's shirt.

"FOUNTAIN!" said Olivia, delighted.

Then Theo was hungry.

Papa sat on the bench and gave him the bottle Mama had left.

Olivia held the burp cloth ready, with both hands.

A woman stopped to look.

"Both of them? Brave man."

"Just Saturday," said Papa.

They came home at noon with peaches, one wet shirt,

and a baby asleep on Papa's chest.

Mama had slept all morning.

"How was it?"

"Wet," said Papa.

"Fee-oh made fountain!"

Papa went to change his shirt, whistling badly.
