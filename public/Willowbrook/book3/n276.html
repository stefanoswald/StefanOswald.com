Night 276

Freddy Tries Again

Month 10: Try Again

Papa and Olivia went back the next day.

Freddy gave back the mitten.

It was a little damp.

"Today," he said, "the little jump."

Stone to stone.

He landed it.

"The middle jump."

He landed that too.

"The famous one."

He backed up. He wiggled.

Splash.

Olivia clapped.

Freddy came up blinking.

"Why clap? I missed."

"You jumped!" said Olivia.

Freddy thought about that.

He climbed out.

He did the middle jump again, to warm up.

Then the famous one.

The far bank caught him.

Both feet.

"TA-DA!" said Freddy.

"I have been to the sea, you know."

Tessa had been hearing about the sea for years.

Then Freddy dug down into the soft mud under the bank,

the way frogs do in winter.

"Night, Freddy," said Olivia.

One bubble came up.

Then none.
