Night 92

Talking to the Tummy

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Mama's tummy was big and round now, and Mama said a surprising thing. The baby could hear.

"In dere?"

"In there," said Mama. "It's getting to know your voice."

Well. Then the baby needed the news. All of it.

Olivia pressed her mouth right up against the tummy.

"Hi baby. It me."

Then the news, in order.

"I had toast."

"Wif jam."

"Wiwi come over."

"We make fort."

"Doggie bark. Loud."

She stopped, to let the baby catch up.

"I save you spot."

She had, too. There was a spot in the fort, and Bobby Bear was keeping it warm.

Then, quieter, the goodnight she made up herself.

"Nigh-nigh in dere."

Mama's shirt had a small damp patch.
