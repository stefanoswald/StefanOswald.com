Night 212

The First Yellow Leaf

Month 7: Potty Power

The first cool night came over the hill.

Oliver Owl fluffed his feathers.

"Who is still awake?" he said.

Stella dipped low to look.

On Maple Lane, one window was yellow.

Then it was dark.

At Honeywell Farm the hens were in and the barn was quiet.

On top of the Lantern Library, the lantern stayed lit.

Down at the brook, Freddy was telling the reeds about the sea.

The reeds did not believe him either.

High in the willow, one leaf had turned yellow.

Just one.

Oliver looked at it over his spectacles.

"Already?"

The Sleepy Moon hummed, low.

The leaf stayed on.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
