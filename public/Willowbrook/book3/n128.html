Night 128

The Hard Afternoon

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Four o'clock, and hot.

The fan pushed the warm air around.

Theo had been crying for a long time.

Mama walked him. Swayed him. Shushed him.

He cried anyway.

On the rug, Olivia built a tower.

One block. Two. More.

Six. Six had never happened before.

"Mama! Look at me! Look my tower!"

"In a minute."

The minute did not come.

The crying ate it.

Olivia stood by her tower and waited.

Then she sat down by it.

Then she lay down on the rug with her face in her arm.

Mama saw.

She stopped swaying. She shut her eyes and let out one long breath.

Then she came and knelt on the rug,

Theo still crying on her shoulder.

"You made six, and I missed it. I'm sorry."

Olivia looked out from under her arm.

"Six."

"Show me?"

Mama counted, and Olivia touched each block as she went.

Theo cried through all six.

Mama stayed on the rug anyway.
