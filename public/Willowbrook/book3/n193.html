Night 193

The Flush Monster That Wasn't

Month 7: Potty Power

The little potty was fine.

The big toilet was another matter.

Its flush was loud.

A roar, then a gulp, then a long gurgle,

like something big swallowing.

"Too roary," said Olivia, from the hall.

So Papa did what he did with the dark corner at night.

They went and looked at it together.

One step back, holding hands.

Papa pushed the handle.

RRROAR-glug.

Olivia's free hand went to her ear.

"Where roar come from?"

"Water, in a hurry," said Papa.

"Like the brook in March."

Olivia remembered the brook in March.

Loud, and not mad at all.

Second flush, one step closer.

Third flush, her hand on the handle,

Papa's hand over hers.

RRROAR-glug.

Olivia giggled.

"Go, water!"

The next morning, the roar was big again.

Papa came and stood one step back with her.
