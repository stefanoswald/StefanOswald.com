Night 116

Bella Comes Back Again

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

The garden was going in. Olivia had planted the lettuce herself, mostly in one hole.

Mama rested on the bench, round as a watermelon.

Then, over the fence, wings.

"Bella!"

It was Bella, for the first time since Olivia's birthday. She flew once around the garden, as she always does. Then she landed.

Not on the fence.

Not on the tomato stake.

On the bump. Right on top.

Nobody moved. Olivia held her breath until her cheeks went round.

Bella's wings opened and closed, slow as breathing.

"Hi, baby," Olivia whispered. "Dat Bella."

Then the wings lifted, and Bella was over the fence and gone.

Mama let out her breath. Olivia let out hers, with a pop.

"Baby got Bella'd," she told Papa at supper.
