Night 136

Theo Meets the Bench

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Dandelion Park, in the shade of the big tree.

Mama sat on the bench, the same bench where she sat

when Olivia was a baby.

Rosa sat beside her. The other mothers scooted up.

"How's the sleep?" one of them asked.

Mama laughed. That was the answer.

Theo lay in Mama's arms.

Olivia stood at Mama's knee.

"Dis my brudder."

She held out both hands, like a show.

"Ta-da!"

Theo was asleep.

The mothers said, "Ohhh."

She put her hands down. She held them out again.

"Ta-da!"

Still asleep.

"Ohhh," said the mothers.

"He sleep," Olivia told them. "He eat. He cry."

"That's all they do at first," said Rosa.

"Dat all," Olivia agreed.

One more time.

"Ta-da!"

And Theo opened his mouth in the biggest yawn in the park.

The whole bench laughed.

Olivia put one finger on her lips.

"Shh," she told the whole park.
