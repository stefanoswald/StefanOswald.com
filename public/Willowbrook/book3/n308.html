Night 308

A Flower for the Duchess

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Nothing grows at the farm in January.

So Olivia made a flower out of red paper,

with a yellow crayon middle.

"For da Duchess," she told Grandpa.

Grandpa looked at the flower,

then at the Duchess,

and raised his eyebrows.

The Duchess sat on the fence rail,

fat and speckled,

glaring at the snow.

"Hi, Duchess. Dis for you."

The Duchess looked at the flower.

She looked at Olivia.

Then she turned around

and showed Olivia her tail.

"Hmph."

Olivia's lip went out.

"She no like it."

"She's like that with everybody," said Grandpa.

Olivia chewed her mitten.

Then she stood the flower up in the snow by the coop door,

right where the Duchess walks.

"For you anyway."

Going home, Olivia looked back.

The Duchess was walking around the flower,

the long way.
