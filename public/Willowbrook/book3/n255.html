Night 255

Freddy Counts to Five

Month 9: Counting to Five

The brook was cold and loud.

Papa kept a hand on the back of Olivia's hood.

Papa heard a frog. Olivia heard Freddy.

"Tomorrow I sleep in the mud till spring," said Freddy. "But first, I will count to five!"

"One! Two! SEVEN!"

"No!" said Olivia. "Free!"

"At the sea," said Freddy, "seven comes next. I have been to the sea."

"You have NOT."

Nobody at the brook believes Freddy has been to the sea.

"Slowly," said Tessa. "With me."

"One," said Tessa.

A long time went by.

"Two."

"Too slow!" said Freddy.

"Slow is also a speed," said Tessa.

"FREE!" said Olivia.

"Four, five!" said Freddy.

"FIVE!" they said together.

"I did it," said Freddy. "Goodnight, everybody."

He jumped into the brook.

Plop.

Bubbles came up.

One. Two. Free.
