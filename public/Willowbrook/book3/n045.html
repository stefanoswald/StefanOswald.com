Night 45

Making Her Own Sandwich

Month 2: Me Do It

Mama set out the pieces for lunch.

Two slices of bread, flat.

The soft spread, lid off.

The little spreader, the one with no edge.

Banana, already in coins.

"Yours," said Mama, and went to wash the pan.

Olivia spread the spread.

Most of it went on the bread.

Some went on the table, some on her,

and she licked that part off.

Then the banana coins, one at a time,

in a ring, then a line, then a pile in the middle.

Then the top slice.

Press. Press harder.

A sandwich.

Tall in the middle. Flat at the edges.

Made by her.

She ate it with both hands, crust and all.

She eats crusts now, it turns out,

when they are hers.

"Me made it."

Mama came back and made her own,

which was neater,

and which nobody looked at.
