Night 337

Three Fingers

Month 12: Almost Three

Everybody asks a birthday girl the same thing.

"How old are you?"

Olivia wanted her answer ready.

At the hall mirror, Mama showed her.

Pointer up. Tall one up. The next one up.

The thumb holds the little one down.

The little one popped up.

"Four!" said Mama.

"NO four!"

Thumb down, hard.

"Free."

The little finger popped up again.

It wanted to come to the birthday too.

"Hug it with your thumb," said Mama.

Olivia hugged it down.

Three fingers in the mirror.

Three fingers back.

At the store, the man at the till asked.

Up went two fingers, the old way.

Then she remembered.

"No. Free. At da cake."

She held them up all the way to the car.

At home she found Theo's pointer finger

and uncurled it from his fist.

"You get one. In summer."

He put it in his mouth.
