Night 335

The Last Month of Two

Month 12: Almost Three

Papa turned the kitchen calendar to its last page.

February. A snowy barn on top.

And down in the far corner, past the last square,

a crayon cake with three candles.

"Dat my cake?"

"That's your cake."

"Now?"

"After all these squares."

Olivia counted the squares.

"One, two, free, four, five, lots."

"Lots," Papa agreed.

She patted the cake.

Then she stood very still by the wall,

the way she stands when a thought is too big to walk with.

"Me still me at free?"

Mama put down the spoon.

"Still you. Just more of you."

"More me?"

"More you."

Olivia looked down to check.

Same socks. Same tummy.

In the morning, the first square got an X.

She made it herself, very hard, in red crayon.
