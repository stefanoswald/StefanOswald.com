Night 332

The Duchess Brings a Feather

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Every Sunday in January,

Olivia brought the Duchess a new paper flower.

Every Sunday, the Duchess walked around it.

This Sunday, at the spot by the coop door,

something was lying in the snow.

A feather.

Speckled brown and gold,

with a green shine when she tipped it.

Only one hen had feathers like that.

She was on the fence rail,

looking the other way.

"Gampa! Look!"

Grandpa came and looked.

He took off his hat.

"Da Duchess gave me!"

Olivia held the feather to her coat.

"Me so happy.

She like me now?"

Grandpa looked at the Duchess.

The Duchess looked at the sky.

"Maybe," said Grandpa.

"Hmph," said the Duchess.

She put the pink flower down anyway.
