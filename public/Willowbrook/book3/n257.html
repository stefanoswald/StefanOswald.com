Night 257

The Five Senses Walk

Month 9: Counting to Five

A frosty morning. Papa held up one finger.

"Look."

Olivia breathed out, and a little cloud came out of her.

She stared at it. "Smoke!"

"Your breath," said Papa. "It's that cold."

She breathed out clouds all the way to the corner.

Two fingers. "Listen."

Crunch, went her boots on the white grass.

Crunch. Crunch.

"Crunchy," she said, and did it more.

Three fingers. "Smell."

Her nose went straight to the corner.

"Cimmanon," she said. Miss Rosa was baking.

Four fingers. "Touch."

The fence rail was white all over.

She took off a mitten and put her hand on it.

"Cold! Wet!"

Five fingers, and they were at Miss Rosa's door.

"Taste," said Olivia, before Papa could.

Miss Rosa came out with a warm piece of roll.

"The last one," she said. It was not the last one.

Papa got the next last one.
