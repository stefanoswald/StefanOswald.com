Night 38

I Carry It

Month 2: Me Do It

Bags in the car. Papa handed them out.

"Me carry."

She picked the milk. The whole jug.

"Bread is lighter," said Papa.

"Milk."

Milk, then.

She hugged it to her front with both arms.

Her back went bendy. Her steps went tiny.

The path was wet from the rain,

with a puddle in the middle,

and she went around the puddle

the long way, still hugging.

Papa walked beside her with his hands out,

not touching, like a man carrying air.

The jug slipped. She caught it on her knee.

The jug tipped. She leaned it back.

The step up to the door was the hardest part.

Up. Both feet. Still holding.

The counter was too high. The floor was fine.

Thud.

"Heavy," she said, red in the face.

"It is," said Papa. "You carried it the whole way."

She went back out for the bread.
