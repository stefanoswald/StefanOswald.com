Night 4

Mad Is a Thunderstorm

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Lily went home.

Olivia wanted more Lily.

Here comes the loud one.

It booms in the feet. Stomp!

It flashes in the face. Red!

It knocks the tower flying.

Crash.

Mama does not yell at storms.

That only makes two.

She stays close, like ground under thunder.

"I'm here," she says. That's all.

The booms go on a while, because storms do.

Then they slow.

Then comes the rain part, wet and small,

and two arms reach up.

In she comes.

"You're mad," says Mama, later. "Wiwi went home."

"More Wiwi," says Olivia.

"Being mad is fine. Kicking the blocks is not."

Olivia looks at the blocks.

One is under the chair.

She goes and gets it.
