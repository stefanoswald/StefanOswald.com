Night 254

Grandpa's Egg Count

Month 9: Counting to Five

Papa's car went down the farm lane without her.

Olivia cried at the gate.

Grandpa Joe stood beside her.

He did not say anything, and he did not hurry her.

After a while he held out the egg basket.

She took the handle, still crying a little.

The henhouse was dim and smelled of straw.

The first box had an egg in it. Brown. Warm.

"One."

The next box. "Two."

The next box had the Duchess in it, not laying.

Olivia found the last one in the straw.

"FREE."

She looked in every box for four.

She looked for five.

The hens of Honeywell Farm lay fewer eggs when the days get short.

Nobody had told them about five.

Olivia counted them again.

"Mama. Papa. Me."

She thought. "Fee-oh drink milk."

Grandpa Joe looked into the basket.

"Plenty," he said.

They carried it up to the house,

four hands on one handle,

and washed all four at the sink.
