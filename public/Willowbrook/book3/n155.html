Night 155

My Baby, My Show-and-Tell

Month 6: Big Sister School

Thursday, at the park, early, before the heat.

Olivia stood on the blanket.

Lily sat in front.

Theo lay on his back between them.

"Fee-oh show," said Olivia.

Lily clapped, before anything.

"Fee-oh smile now. Watch."

Olivia leaned down. "Hi, Fee-oh!"

Theo looked at a leaf.

"Hi, FEE-OH!"

Theo looked at the leaf harder.

"He smile at home."

"He does," said Mama. "I've seen it."

"Fee-oh talk now. Watch."

Olivia waited.

Lily waited.

A duck came up from the pond and waited.

Theo hiccuped.

Lily clapped anyway.

Then Theo turned his head,

found Lily's face,

and said, "Ahhh-goo."

Both girls held their breath.

"He TALK!" said Lily.

"Me tell you!" said Olivia.

She bowed.

Lily bowed too, just in case.
