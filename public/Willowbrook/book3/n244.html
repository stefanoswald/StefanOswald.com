Night 244

The Counting Caterpillar Returns

Month 9: Counting to Five

The frost was off the garden by lunch.

On the flagstone path, something fuzzy was walking.

Black at both ends. Brown in the middle.

"Caterpillar!"

"There's one every fall," said Mama.

"Remember last year's?"

Olivia did not remember last year's.

"I 'member," she said.

We look. We don't touch. Fuzz can itch.

So they crouched close, hands on knees.

Scrunch.

"One."

Scrunch.

"Two."

Scrunch.

"FREE!"

Then her counting ran on without him.

"Four! Five!"

The caterpillar was still on three.

A caterpillar in November is in no hurry. He has all winter.

"Wait for him," said Mama.

Scrunch. "Four."

Scrunch. "Five."

At the end of the path he went under the leaf heap,

and was gone.

Olivia put one more leaf on top.

Behind them, the porch light came on.

It was not even supper.
