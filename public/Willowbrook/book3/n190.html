Night 190

The Long Ride to the Orchard

Month 7: Potty Power

The apple orchard was a long ride away.

Mama packed a bag.

Spare pants, spare socks, a towel,

and the little seat for big toilets.

"Try now, or try at the orchard?" said Mama.

"Orchard."

Papa whistled them out of the driveway, badly.

Theo slept in his car seat.

The road went past fields,

and more fields,

and one cow.

Then Olivia sat up very straight.

"Potty. NOW."

"Hold on," said Papa. "Gas station."

He turned in.

Mama grabbed the bag.

Olivia walked fast and stiff,

like somebody carrying soup.

They made it.

"You told us in time," said Mama.

At the orchard it was still summer-warm,

and the apples were red,

and Mama cut one into thin slices,

and Olivia ate every slice under a tree.

On the way home she said, "Potty."

Same gas station.

Papa was already turning in.
