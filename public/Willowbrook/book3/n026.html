Night 26

The Overwhelmed Bubble

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

A birthday party. A big one.

Balloons. Children. Music. Cake.

Wonderful.

Wonderful.

Wonder... too much.

Olivia's eyes go glassy.

Her hands come up over her ears.

"Too loud."

Mama sees.

She asks no questions.

She scoops Olivia up and carries her out to the porch.

The porch is cold and quiet.

The music is a hum through the door.

One lap. One breeze. One girl.

"That was a lot," says Mama. "All at once."

Nothing is wrong. She is just full.

Good things overfill too.

They sit.

Her hands come down, one at a time.

The glassy goes out of her eyes.

"Cake?" says Olivia.

"Cake," says Mama.

They go back in for it.
