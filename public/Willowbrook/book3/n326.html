Night 326

The Blanket for June's Baby

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

June's mama is having a baby.

So all the grown-ups on the lane

were knitting a blanket,

one square each.

Mama knitted theirs, red.

Olivia held the yarn in her lap

and let it out, a little at a time.

Then a lot at a time.

Then she pulled the wrong end.

The square came out crooked,

with a lump in the middle.

"Oh no. Me broke it."

"That's the part you did," said Mama,

and left it in.

Mrs. Henderson sewed all the squares together.

They carried the blanket to June's house in the snow.

June's mama held it against her round tummy

and didn't say anything for a while.

June found the lump right away.

"Dat yours?"

"Me did it."

Then June whispered,

"Baby get all da mama?"

Olivia remembered the candles.

"My mama did candles. Ask her."

June looked up at her mama.

Her mama knelt down in the doorway.
