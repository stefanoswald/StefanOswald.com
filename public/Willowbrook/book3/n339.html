Night 339

The Remember Game: The Crash and the Brother

Month 12: Almost Three

Part two was the big one.

Tonight Olivia told it, and Papa did the noises.

"Me and Wiwi. Da store."

"With carts," said Papa. Rattle, rattle.

"We go fast."

"Much too fast."

"And den BONK!"

Papa fell over on the pillow.

"Da carts get mixed up."

Papa mixed up the pillows.

"Den noodles at Wiwi's."

Papa slurped.

"Then the paper chain," said Mama.

"One link every night."

"Me tear dem."

"And one morning, before the sun,

Rosa woke you up, and the phone rang."

Papa's voice went funny here.

It always does at this part.

"And there was your brother.

You said hello, and he turned his head to find you."

"He know my voice."

"He knew it already."

Papa took off his glasses and wiped them.

Olivia patted his beard.

"Papa wet."

"A little," said Papa.

She patted until it was dry.
