Night 350

The Almost-Three Checkup

Month 12: Almost Three

"The doctor will be here in a minute," said the nurse.

Olivia looked at Mama.

"Mama minute?"

"I hope not," said Mama.

It was a real minute.

The doctor listened to Olivia's heart.

Thump-thump, thump-thump.

"My turn," said Olivia.

The doctor handed her the stethoscope,

and Olivia listened to the doctor.

"Dere a drum in you."

Then a cuff went on her arm.

It squeezed, tighter and tighter.

"Too tight!"

Mama held her other hand.

"It's squeezing. It lets go soon."

Pssss. It let go.

Olivia's shoulders came down.

"It hug too hard."

"It does hug hard," said the doctor. "That's all it does."

On the scale, she stood very tall.

"Free big," said Olivia.

At home, Duck got a checkup.

Duck had a drum in her too.
