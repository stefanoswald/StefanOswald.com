Night 211

The Whole Month Dry-ish

Month 7: Potty Power

Saturday, and a mountain of clean laundry on the rug.

Everybody folded.

Olivia pulled out the duck pants.

"Remember the first day in those?" said Mama.

"Ducks wet. Den stars."

"And what do we say?"

"Wet pants. Dry pants. All done!"

A night diaper went to the shelf, not the pile.

"Night one," said Olivia. "Legs growing."

"And the big roar?" said Papa.

Olivia covered her ears, then uncovered them.

"Go, water!"

"How do you know when it's time?" said Mama.

Olivia patted her tummy.

"It tell me. Me go."

"Where was the lemon soap?" said Papa.

"Shoe shop!"

Theo kicked on his blanket by the pile.

Olivia leaned over him, tongue out.

He laughed.

Papa folded the last duck pair

and put it on top.
