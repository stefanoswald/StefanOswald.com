Night 63

The Swap

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

After a scare, mamas talk.

The talking shakes it out.

So the carts stood side by side,

and the mamas talked over the top of them,

each one holding a small hand tight.

That left two free hands, down at cart height.

Lily's free hand went into Olivia's cart

and came out with the apples.

In they went, into Lily's cart.

Olivia thought about that.

Then her free hand went into Lily's cart

and came out with a long box of spaghetti.

A jar of red sauce went one way.

The beans went the other.

A wedge of cheese, a loaf of bread,

a little green plant in a pot.

It was a quiet game.

"Well," said Rosa at last, "we'd better finish up."

At the checkout both mamas were still talking,

and the groceries went into the bags

without anybody looking.

Two girls walked out holding hands with their mamas,

swinging them.
