Night 37

The Toothbrush Turn

Month 2: Me Do It

At the sink there are two turns.

Turn one is hers.

"Me brush."

She brushes her own way,

which is mostly chewing the brush

and humming at the mirror.

Some bristle finds some tooth.

Turn two is Mama's.

"My turn. Open big."

The real scrub. Fronts, backs,

and the new big teeth at the back

where the crumbs like to hide.

Quick, and done.

Some nights turn two is not popular.

"No. Me."

"Your turn was first. Now mine."

Mama does not argue with a mouth.

She counts to it instead, one to five, slow,

and the mouth stays open for the counting.

Spit. Wipe.

Olivia gets the brush back

for one more chew, her way,

and puts it in the cup herself,

upside down.
