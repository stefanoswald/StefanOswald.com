Night 35

The Wrong-Buttoned Sweater

Month 2: Me Do It

The morning was cool, so, sweater.

Olivia did the buttons.

She picked the holes. Mama held each one open.

Olivia pushed all five through herself,

and every one went in the wrong hole.

One side hung long.

The collar pointed at the window.

She stood very straight in it.

"Me did buttons."

Mama's hand went out toward the top button.

Then it came back.

She looked at the sweater.

She looked at the face above the sweater.

"Five buttons," Mama said. "You did every one."

The sweater stayed like that all day.

It went to the market, crooked.

It went past the tulips on the corner, crooked.

Rosa saw it at the bakery door and said, "Nice sweater,"

and got a little flour on the long side.

At bedtime they undid them together.

That part was faster.
