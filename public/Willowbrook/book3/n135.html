Night 135

The Double Outing

Month 5: Theo Arrives

The first walk with all four of them.

They waited until evening, when the heat let go.

Theo rode on Mama, in the carrier.

Olivia pushed the stroller.

Nobody was in it but the diaper bag, a blanket, and Duck.

"Me push da fings."

Papa pushed too, from the back, a little.

Four people walk slower than three.

Olivia stopped to fix Duck.

Mama stopped to fix Theo's hat.

Papa stopped because everybody else had stopped.

Mrs. Henderson waved from her porch across the street.

Mateo waved a stick from his.

The gold dog came to the fence and wagged at the carrier.

"Dis Fee-oh," Olivia told him. "He sleeping."

The gold dog lay down, so as not to wake him.

At the corner, Olivia jumped a crack in the sidewalk with both feet.

Then she jumped it again, in case anybody missed it.

They turned for home, even slower.

Over the Hendersons' hedge, the first firefly came on.
