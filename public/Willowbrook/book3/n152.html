Night 152

The First Smile Lands

Month 6: Big Sister School

For weeks Olivia had tried everything.

Puffed cheeks. The belly pbbbt.

The talking sock.

Theo only stared.

On Tuesday morning she was too sleepy to try.

She climbed up on the big bed in her pajamas,

where Mama had Theo on her knees,

and put her face close.

"Hi, Fee-oh."

Theo looked at her.

Then his whole face opened.

His mouth went wide and his eyes went small,

and it was for her.

She did not even need the saved face.

Olivia held very still.

"Mama. Look."

"Hi, Fee-oh," she said again.

He did it again.

Papa came to the door with his coffee.

"Wind?" said Mama.

Papa looked for a long time.

"Not wind," he said.

He put his coffee on the dresser

and sat down on the end of the bed to watch.
