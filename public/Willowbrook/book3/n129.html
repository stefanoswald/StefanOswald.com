Night 129

The First Smile Goes To...

Month 5: Theo Arrives

After his evening milk, Theo fell asleep in Mama's arms.

In his sleep, one corner of his mouth went up.

"He smile!" said Olivia. "At me!"

"That's wind," said Papa.

"No. Me."

"Wind."

"ME."

The corner went up again, both sides this time.

"See? Me!"

Papa leaned in. "His eyes are shut."

Olivia leaned in too. "He smiling inside."

Then Theo burped. A big one, for somebody so small.

"Wind," said Papa.

Olivia folded her arms.

Theo's mouth curled up one more time, in his sleep.

"You're so happy he smiled," said Mama.

"At ME."

"Maybe," said Mama. "We'll see when he's awake for it."

Papa did not say wind again.

He only thought it, very loudly.

Olivia watched Theo's mouth until bath time.
