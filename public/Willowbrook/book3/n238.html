Night 238

The Self-Forecast

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

No Lily today. Lily was still unpacking.

Olivia was drawing a big red sun.

Snap.

The red crayon broke in two.

She held the halves together.

They did not go back.

Her face went red. Her hands went tight.

She stood up and put one hand flat on her chest.

"Hot here."

Then: "Me go corner."

And she went.

Nobody sent her.

Mama stayed in the kitchen doorway

and dried the same plate.

Squeeze, went the pillow.

Whirr, went the pinwheel.

Squeeze.

Whirr.

When the hot was gone, Olivia came out.

She picked up both halves of the crayon

and looked at them.

"Two red."

She drew two suns.

Mama was still drying the plate.

It was very dry by then.
