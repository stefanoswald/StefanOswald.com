Night 107

Reading to the Bump

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

At the Lantern Library the librarian had said that big sisters read to babies. Olivia said yes on the spot.

So tonight she was the reader. The book was the bear one. The listener was the bump, and Bobby Bear listened too.

She leaned against Mama, opened the soft old covers, and read it her own way.

"Bear lost button. He look and look."

She turned the book around so the bump could see.

"See, baby? Sad bear."

She turned the pages one at a time, very carefully.

"Dere button! He finded it!"

She waited for the baby to gasp. The baby did not, so she did it for them. "Gasp."

Thump.

"Mama! Baby clap!"

It was a kick. But everybody on that couch agreed it was a clap.

"Da end," she told the bump.

The bump saw every picture.
