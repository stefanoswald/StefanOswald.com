Night 297

The Snow Castle's Five Falls

Month 10: Try Again

Snow came again in the night,

the wet, sticky kind.

At the farm Olivia had her summer sand bucket.

Grandpa had an upturned crate to sit on.

She poured the snow in loose.

It came out a pile.

Grandpa scooped a little snow in his big mitten

and patted it firm.

He said nothing.

She packed the next one a little.

It slumped.

She packed the next one hard

and yanked the bucket.

The top broke off.

"Slow," said Grandpa.

That was all.

The fourth one she packed and patted

and lifted slow.

It stood.

Then the Duchess walked right through it,

on her way to somewhere else.

"DUCHESS!"

Olivia kicked the snow. Her eyes went hot.

Grandpa waited. He did not fix it.

After a while she picked up the bucket.

Pack it. Pat it. Slow, slow, wiggle.

The fifth one stood.

Grandpa nodded once.

The Duchess came back and climbed on top.

It held her.
