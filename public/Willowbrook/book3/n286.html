Night 286

The Pool Lessons Begin

Month 10: Try Again

Saturday mornings were pool mornings now.

Just Olivia and Mama.

Theo stayed home with Papa.

Outside, snow.

Inside, the pool was warm and loud and very big.

At the far end the blue went dark and deep.

Olivia held Mama's hand so hard her knuckles went white.

"It's very big," said Mama. "You're holding tight."

Mama went down the steps into the water first.

One step, for Olivia.

Two.

Kicking at the edge was the best part.

Splash, splash, splash, up to the ceiling.

Blowing bubbles was the strange part.

She snorted water. She tried again.

"Dandelion blow in da water!"

The float was the scary part.

On her back, Mama's hands underneath,

the ceiling lights far away.

Her eyes never left Mama's face.

In the towel after, she shivered.

"Pool big. Me coming back."

Then a bun at the bakery, just the two of them.

Her wet hair dripped on it.
