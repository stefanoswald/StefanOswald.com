Night 140

When Will He PLAY?

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Olivia dragged the whole block bin over to Theo

and tipped it out beside him.

Theo lay on his back on his blanket and looked at the ceiling.

"Play, Fee-oh."

Theo looked at the ceiling.

"PLAY."

Theo yawned.

"Mama. When Fee-oh play?"

"Not for a long time," said Mama. "He's still brand new."

"Tomorrow?"

"Longer."

"Two tomorrow?"

"Lots of tomorrows. When the leaves come down, he'll hold a toy."

Olivia looked out the window.

The leaves were very green, and very up.

"He can play one game now," said Mama.

"Put your face close."

Olivia put her face close, as close as a bear book.

Theo looked at her. She looked at Theo.

Neither one blinked.

"Dis a game?"

"His first one," said Mama.

Theo blinked.

"Me win!" said Olivia.

She let him win the next one.
