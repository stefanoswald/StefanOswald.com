Night 266

Teaching Theo the Thunder Count

Month 9: Counting to Five

Thunder in November does not happen often.

It happened on Tuesday.

BOOM.

Theo jumped in Mama's arms. His face crumpled, and he cried.

Olivia climbed up on the couch beside them.

"Fee-oh. Watch."

Flash.

"Now count. One. Two. Free. Four."

BOOM.

Theo cried a little less.

He was looking at her mouth.

Flash.

"One. Two. Free. Four. Five."

Boom. Smaller.

"It going," she told him.

Theo had stopped crying.

He held her finger and watched her face.

"He likes your counting," said Mama.

Flash, far off.

Olivia counted to five, and then some more fives.

The boom was far away now,

like somebody moving a chair upstairs.

Theo's eyes went heavy.

Mama took him to the bassinet and laid him on his back.

On the couch, Olivia whispered one more count.
