Night 305

The Muscle Nobody Sees

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

At breakfast Olivia pushed up her sleeve

and made a fist.

A small bump came up.

"Muscle! From da trike."

Mama felt it.

"All that pedaling."

"You have one more," said Mama.

"Nobody can see it."

"Where?"

Olivia looked up her sleeve.

She looked behind her knee.

She looked down the neck of her pajamas.

"Not dere."

Mama tapped her own chest.

"In here. A kind muscle."

"What it do?"

"Helps. It grows when you use it."

Olivia tapped her chest.

Nothing bumped.

Then Mama's napkin slid off the table.

Olivia climbed down,

got it,

and climbed back up.

"Dere."

"Thank you," said Mama.

Olivia pressed her chest, hard.

"Bigger?"

"A little, I think."

She kept one hand there

and ate her toast with the other.
