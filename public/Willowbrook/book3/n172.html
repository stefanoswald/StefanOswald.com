Night 172

Teaching the Dog About the Baby

Month 6: Big Sister School

On the walk, they stopped at the fence

where the gold dog lives.

The gold dog came over slowly.

He is old, and he does everything slowly.

"Dis Fee-oh," said Olivia.

"Him little. Be nice."

Papa held Theo close, and a little low.

The gold dog put his nose through the fence.

Sniff. Sniff.

He sniffed one small sock.

Theo's toes curled up.

"Gentle," Olivia told the dog.

She showed him her flat hand.

He sniffed that too.

He knew that hand already.

Then he sniffed Theo's sock again,

for a long time,

the way you read a page twice.

Theo stared at the big gold face.

The big gold face stared back.

Everything held still.

Then the dog sighed through his nose,

turned around three times,

and lay down against the fence,

right where Theo was.

"Him like Fee-oh," said Olivia.

His tail went thump on the grass.
