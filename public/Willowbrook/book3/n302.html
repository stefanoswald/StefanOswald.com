Night 302

Theo's First Yet

Month 10: Try Again

Theo sat against the big cushion like a small king.

Olivia set the red block on the rug in front of him.

He reached.

His hand went right past it.

He reached again.

His hand came down flat on the rug.

"Ehh," said Theo. His face began to crumple.

Olivia got down on her tummy, nose to nose.

"Fee-oh can't get it," she told Mama.

Then she remembered.

"Yet."

"Yet," said Mama, from the couch, folding socks.

Theo reached one more time.

His fingers bumped the block.

It rolled a little closer.

He grabbed it.

He looked at it.

He passed it to his other hand.

Then he put it in his mouth.

"Not for eating," said Olivia.

Theo chewed on it anyway, very pleased.

The red block came back shiny.
