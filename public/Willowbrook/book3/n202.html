Night 202

The Wiping Apprenticeship

Month 7: Potty Power

Wiping was the hard part.

Olivia went first.

She pulled the paper.

And pulled.

The roll spun,

and the paper kept coming,

over her knees,

onto the floor,

out the door a little way.

"That's enough for a whole farm," said Mama.

Olivia tore off one piece.

"Front to back," she said. "Always."

She did it, front to back.

Then Mama checked, and finished,

the way she finished buttons.

"Me do it all?"

"Not yet," said Mama.

"Your arms are still growing the reach."

Olivia stretched one arm out as far as it went.

Then the other one.

Mama wound the long tail of paper back on the roll.

It went on fat and lumpy.

It sat there like that all week.
