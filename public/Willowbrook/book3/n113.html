Night 113

Lily's Promise

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Thursday. The fort. Lily had come over with something on her mind.

Lily knew about babies. She had a cousin.

"Baby come, you busy."

"Me big sister," said Olivia.

Lily picked at the blanket.

"Still play wif me?"

Olivia looked at her. This one was easy.

"Yes. Fursday."

Lily thought about that. Thursday was a very good answer. It always had been.

Then she made an offer of her own.

"I help baby. I know babies."

"You know babies?"

"My cousin."

Olivia nodded. That was true.

"Pinky," said Lily. Rosa had shown her that.

Two small pinkies hooked and held, a good long second, in the blue light of the fort.

Rabbit kept the door.
