Night 329

The Gentle Inspection

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Theo sits by himself now.

Mostly.

Today he leaned too far after a block.

Tip.

Bonk.

His head bumped the rug, and he howled.

Olivia reached him before Mama did.

She got down low, the way Mama does.

"Where owie?"

She looked at his head. A pink spot.

She looked at his hands. Fine.

She looked in his mouth,

which was wide open, howling.

"Mama! Him got a white fing!"

Mama picked him up and looked too.

There, on his bottom gum,

one tiny white edge.

"His first tooth," said Mama.

Theo stopped crying to see what everybody was looking at.

Olivia kissed the pink spot,

softly.

Mama brought the teething ring from the fridge,

cold, not frozen.

Theo chewed it, frowning.

Olivia touched the tooth with one finger.

Sharp.
