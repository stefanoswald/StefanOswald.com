Night 273

Stella Counts to Five

Month 9: Counting to Five

A cold clear night.

Frost was coming on the roofs of Maple Lane.

Stella came down low over one house,

and counted.

One. A girl in a big bed,

one foot out of the covers.

Two. A baby on his back in the bassinet,

both fists up by his ears.

Three. Mama, asleep on her side of the big bed,

one hand hanging down toward the bassinet.

Four. Papa, asleep with his glasses on.

Five. The cat, asleep in Papa's slipper.

Stella looked out at the rest of the town.

More windows. More sleepers.

Too many for one star.

She did not count them.

She just looked.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
