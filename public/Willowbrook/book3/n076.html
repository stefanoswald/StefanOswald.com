Night 76

What Lily Knows

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Lily is eleven days younger than Olivia.

She knows things anyway.

"Watch."

Lily made her lips very small and blew.

Fffff, and some spit.

"Whistle."

Olivia made her lips small and blew.

All spit.

Papa cannot whistle either,

so she was in good company.

"Big stick dere."

Behind the oak lay a stick with a fork at the end,

the best stick in the park.

They went to visit it.

They did not take it home. It lives there.

Olivia knows things too.

She took Lily to the drinking fountain

and pushed the button, and the water jumped.

"Again!"

Again.

Lily pointed up.

The moon was out already, pale, in the afternoon sky.

"Moon come car."

That evening, driving home, Olivia kept her face at the window.

The moon came too.

It came the whole way home.
