Night 217

The Storm at the Market

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

The grocery on Maple Lane had little pumpkins in a bin by the door

and a cereal with a duck on the box.

"Duck one!"

"Not that one," said Mama. "We have oats at home."

Down went Olivia, right there in the aisle, boots kicking, loud.

Mama crouched beside her.

She slid one hand between Olivia's head and the corner of the shelf.

"I'm staying with you," she said, and then nothing else.

She didn't look up at the people.

An old farmer with a bag of onions went by

and gave her one slow nod.

Mrs. Henderson steered her cart around them

the way you'd steer around a puddle.

The storm wore itself out, and Olivia's arms went up.

Mama lifted her. The duck box stayed on the shelf.

On the way home, the fountain in the square was shut off for winter,

full of leaves.

Olivia dropped one more in.

"Fountain sleeping?"

"Till spring," said Mama.
