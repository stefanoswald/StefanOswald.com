Night 313

The Secret Snowman

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

"Let's make Lily a surprise," said Papa.

"A secret one."

"Secret?"

"She won't know who."

Two streets over, on the step by Lily's blue door,

they built a snowman as tall as a boot.

Pebble eyes.

A twig nose.

Papa rang the bell.

Then they ran and hid behind the hedge.

Olivia held her breath.

It was hard.

Her whole tummy wanted to giggle.

The blue door opened.

Lily looked down.

"MAMA! A snowman came!"

Olivia pressed both mittens on her mouth.

She squeezed her eyes shut.

She squeezed everything.

Then she popped up out of the hedge.

"Me did it! Me and Papa!"

"So much for secret," said Papa.

Lily didn't mind one bit.

She bent down and kissed the snowman,

and his nose fell off.
