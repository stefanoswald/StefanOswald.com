Night 32

The Declaration

Month 2: Me Do It

It happened at the shoe bench,

on a morning with rain on the window.

Mama reached for the shoe.

Two small hands flew up.

"Me do it."

Mama's hands went back to her lap.

Hands that have helped for two years

do not like a lap. They fidgeted.

They went under her knees.

The shoe went on the wrong foot.

Off. The other foot. The heel folded under.

Off. On. A push. A push with a grunt.

Mama looked at the ceiling.

There is a spider up there.

She counted its legs. Twice.

Then a stamp, and the shoe was on.

Not tight. Not straight. On.

"Me did it."

Olivia stamped once more, to hear it,

and went off to find the other shoe.
