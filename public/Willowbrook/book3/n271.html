Night 271

Counting Jumps

Month 9: Counting to Five

The back steps, on a cold dry day.

Olivia could jump now. Both feet at once, off the bottom step.

"Watch me!"

Papa watched.

Theo watched from Papa's chest.

Jump. "One!"

Up the step. Jump. "Two!"

Up. Jump. "FREE!"

Up. Jump. "Four!"

On five she landed on her bottom.

She sat there.

Her face went one way, and then the other.

"You landed on your bottom," said Papa.

Olivia stood up. "Five again."

She climbed up.

She bent her knees low, lower than before.

Jump.

Both feet.

"FIVE!"

Theo kicked his legs against Papa, hard.

"Fee-oh jump too?"

"Not yet," said Papa. "He's working on rolling."

"Me count him," said Olivia.

Then she climbed back up for five more.
