Night 6

The Name Game

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

A new game, on the rug.

Mama makes a face.

Big smile, bright eyes.

"What's this one?"

"Happy!"

Right.

Droopy mouth. Slow blink.

"Sad."

Right again.

Scrunched brow, puffed cheeks.

"Mad!"

Yes. Now don't laugh.

Olivia laughs.

Mouth open, eyebrows up.

"This one's surprised," says Mama.

Olivia tries it.

Her eyebrows go all the way up.

Now Olivia makes one.

Nose wrinkled, tongue out a little.

"Dis one?"

Mama looks close.

"Hmm. Is that sleepy?"

"No."

"Silly?"

"No. Cookie face."

A cookie face.

Mama did not know that one.

She knows it now.

They go and see about a cookie.

Olivia keeps the face on the whole way.
