Night 355

Mama's Letter at Three

Month 12: Almost Three

Late, when the house was asleep,

Mama sat at the kitchen table with a pen.

Every birthday she writes one letter.

It goes in the drawer for someday.

Dear Olivia,

This year your feelings got their names, and you used them. Most days you said "mad" instead of throwing.

You tore one link off the paper chain every night until your brother came. Then you sang to him, and he turned to find your voice.

This week he said your name, or something very close to it. We are keeping it.

On the way home from the pond you told me, "I like this me." Please keep that too.

I like being in a room with you. I always will.

Love,

Mama

She folded it,

and it went in the drawer beside the other two.
