Night 250

Five More Minutes

Month 9: Counting to Five

In November the park gets dark before you're done with it.

"Time to go," said Mama.

"Five more minutes!"

Olivia held up the whole hand.

"Five," said Mama. "I'll count them for you."

One more slide. "That's one."

The swing, high. "Two."

The swing again. "Three."

A stick, very important. "Four."

"Five. Time to go."

Olivia's face went stormy.

"More five!"

"You wish it was longer," said Mama. "Me too."

She held out her hand.

"Walk, or ride on my back?"

Olivia stamped once.

Then she took the hand.

They walked home under the lamps.

At the door, Mama said she would start supper in a minute.

Olivia held up five fingers.

Mama studied the hand.

She started supper.
