Night 299

The Second Sorry

Month 10: Try Again

Both girls brought their braid bread to Lily's window seat.

Lily unwrapped hers from its napkin.

It had one big lump on the end.

"It got a nose!" Olivia laughed.

Lily's face went down.

"Sorry, Wiwi," said Olivia fast,

already reaching for the blocks.

Lily turned to the window.

Her shoulder went up.

The sorry had bounced right off.

Olivia looked at that shoulder.

She looked at the snow for a while.

Then she climbed back up on the window seat.

"I laughed at your bread."

Lily didn't turn around.

"Your face got sad."

Lily's shoulder came down, a little.

"Mine got lumps too."

She held hers out. It did.

"Want half?"

Lily looked at the lumps.

Then she turned around.

Lily ate the nose first.
