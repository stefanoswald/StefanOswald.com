Night 182

The Little Throne Arrives

Month 7: Potty Power

One warm evening there was something new

in the bathroom, by the tub.

It was small and white, and just her size.

"What dat?"

"Your potty," said Mama.

"For whenever you want it."

Olivia picked it up.

She turned it over.

She put it on her head.

"Hat!"

"Not a hat," said Mama.

Olivia took it off and looked inside.

Nothing in there.

She set it down by the tub

and sat on it,

pajamas on, socks on,

the way you try out a new chair.

"Comfy."

Mama brushed her teeth right there,

top ones, then bottom ones.

A cricket sang outside the open window.

Nobody said anything else about the potty.

In the morning it was still by the tub,

and Bobby Bear was sitting on it.
