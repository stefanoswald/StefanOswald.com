Night 36

I Can Climb In

Month 2: Me Do It

Rain on the car roof, tock, tock, tock.

Papa opened the door and reached in for her.

"No. Me climb."

One knee up. The other knee.

A turn around. A sit.

She pulled the straps over her own shoulders

and looked at Papa.

"You climbed in," said Papa. "That's new."

Then his hands did the rest.

The buckle. The tug. The snug.

He does that every time,

and he checks it every time,

because the buckle is a grown-up job

until she is a lot bigger.

"Me do buckle?"

"Not yet. That one is mine."

She tried it once with her thumbs anyway.

It did not open. It is made not to.

Papa got in the front and whistled,

which is not a thing he can do.

Olivia kicked the seat in time with it, more or less,

all the way to the market.
