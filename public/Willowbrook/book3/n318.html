Night 318

The Unkind Day

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

It was a no-nap day.

At the park, Olivia and June dug in the snow

on top of the sandbox.

Olivia put the red shovel down.

Just for a second.

June picked it up.

"MINE!"

Olivia grabbed it back and pushed.

June sat down hard in the snow and cried.

Mama was there fast.

She took one breath,

knelt, and held Olivia's hands.

"I won't let you push."

Olivia cried too, loud,

then small, into Mama's coat.

June's mama was holding June.

When it was quiet, Mama said,

"You were so tired. You pushed June."

June's face was all wet.

Holding Mama's hand, Olivia went over.

"Me pushed you. Sorry."

She held out the red shovel.

June took it and turned her back.

At bedtime Olivia held Baba tight.

"Me mean?"

"You pushed," said Mama. "Then you went back."

"June still my friend?"

"We'll ask her on Saturday."

Baba's ear went in Olivia's mouth.
