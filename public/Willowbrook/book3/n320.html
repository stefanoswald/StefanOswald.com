Night 320

The Dish Comes Back

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Mrs. Henderson came across the street

with the noodle dish, washed and shiny.

Inside were oatmeal cookies.

"All better," she said.

"May I hold that baby?"

Mama set Theo in her lap.

Theo looked up at Mrs. Henderson's face.

He looked and looked.

His lip went down,

and down,

and then he wailed.

He twisted around and reached out both arms.

Not for Mama.

For Olivia.

"Him want ME!"

"He's getting shy of new faces," said Mama.

"It's not you."

"Well," said Mrs. Henderson, "I've been told."

Mama sat Olivia on the couch

and put Theo in Olivia's lap,

Mama's hand on his back the whole time.

Theo stopped.

He held on to Olivia's sweater.

Hiccup. Hiccup.

From there he peeked at Mrs. Henderson.

Mrs. Henderson peeked back over the dish.

Theo stared.

Then, very small, he smiled.

"I'll take that," said Mrs. Henderson.

Olivia gave her a cookie, for trying.
