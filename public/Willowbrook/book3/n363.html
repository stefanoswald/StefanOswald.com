Night 363

Bobby Bear's Letter at Three

Month 12: Almost Three

The moon was already in the window

when Olivia came up to bed.

On her pillow, next to Bobby Bear, was a letter.

Bobby Bear looked as if he knew nothing about it.

Mama read it to her.

Dear Olivia,

Happy birthday. You are three, and I was there for all of two.

I held on in the big bed when it was new. I kept watch in the fort. I heard every secret, and they are all still safe.

Now here is a secret of mine.

This year, in your dreams, I am going to get up. I am going to walk, and I am going to talk to you.

Watch for me.

Love,

Bobby Bear

Olivia looked at the bear.

The bear looked at the moon.

She tucked him under her chin, floppy ear on top.
