Night 72

The Sleepover Attempt

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Lily came to sleep over.

She brought pajamas, a toothbrush,

and a floppy rabbit called Rabbit.

Dinner, fine. Bath, splashy. Three stories.

Then the lamp went down, and the giggling came up.

Whisper. Giggle.

A foot in a face. Giggle.

Mama came in once. "Sleepy time."

Giggle.

Mama came in twice. "Heads down."

Giggle, and Rabbit went up at the ceiling.

The third time, Mama's voice came out too big.

"ENOUGH. SLEEP. NOW."

Two faces went still.

Mama stood a moment in the doorway.

Then she came and sat on the floor by the bed.

"That was too loud. I'm sorry."

Lily's chin wobbled. "Want Mama."

"Of course you do."

Rosa came at nine, in her slippers.

"We'll call it a very long playdate," she said.

Olivia sat up to wave. "Wiwi back?"

"Not yet," said Mama. "When you're bigger."

Olivia was asleep before the car door shut,

one hand on Lily's side of the bed.
