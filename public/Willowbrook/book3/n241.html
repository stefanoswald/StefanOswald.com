Night 241

The Gentle Week

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Papa's notebook had only one new line that week:

Wednesday. Bath. Short.

On Saturday, Papa took Theo to the farm,

and it was just-us morning.

Mama and Olivia walked to Rosa's for two warm buns,

and then Olivia got out the duck puzzle.

The last piece would not go in.

She pushed. She pushed harder.

Her face went pink.

Then she held the piece up.

"Dis NOT go! Me mad at it!"

"You're mad at that piece," said Mama.

"MAD." Olivia stomped one foot, once.

Then: "Help me?"

Mama didn't take it.

She turned it around in Olivia's hand, just a little.

Click.

Olivia stared at it.

"It was upside."

"It was upside," said Mama.

They ate the buns at the low table, the puzzle between them,

and Olivia kept one finger on the last piece, just in case.
