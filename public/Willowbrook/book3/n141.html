Night 141

The Photo of Four

Month 5: Theo Arrives

The aunts wanted a picture of all four of them.

So in the gold light before supper,

everybody sat on the porch steps,

and Papa stood the phone on the mailbox.

"Ten seconds," he said, and pressed it, and ran.

Click.

Papa was halfway up the steps. Theo was yawning.

"Again." Press. Run.

Click.

Papa made it. Olivia was waving at the phone with both arms.

"Hi, phone!"

"Again." Press. Run.

Papa tripped on the bottom step and sat down on it hard.

Mama laughed.

Olivia laughed at Mama laughing.

Theo yawned again.

Papa looked at the three of them.

Click.

That was the one.

Nobody in it was looking at the phone.

The aunts put it on their fridge up north.

Olivia got one for her low shelf.

"Dat us," she said, pointing.

"Mama. Papa. Me. Fee-oh."

She stood it next to the red balloon string.
