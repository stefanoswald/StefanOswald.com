Night 291

Theo Says Duck

Month 10: Try Again

Every morning Olivia showed Theo her duck,

the one she drew for him.

"Duck, Fee-oh. Say duck."

Theo said, "Ah."

Theo said, "Da."

Once he tried to eat the corner.

On Thursday he stared at the duck.

His mouth went round.

"Buh."

Olivia dropped the paper.

"He said DUCK!"

"He said buh," said Mama.

"Buh is duck."

"Buh!" said Theo, to the lamp.

"Buh!" to his own foot.

"Dat one's a foot," said Olivia. "But okay."

She went straight to the fridge,

to the word list under the strawberry magnet,

and drew a mark next to DUCK.

Papa looked at the mark.

"That was buh."

"Buh is duck," said Olivia.

Theo said buh to his toes.
