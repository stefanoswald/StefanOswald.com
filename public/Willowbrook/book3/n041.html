Night 41

The Big Girl Bed Arrives

Month 2: Me Do It

Big boxes in Olivia's room.

Papa has the tools out.

Something is being built.

It is a bed. A low one,

with a little rail on one side,

close to the floor.

Olivia helps.

Papa asks for the screwdriver

and she brings him the hammer.

He asks for the small screws

and she brings him the big ones.

"Thank you," he says, every time,

and puts them where he can reach.

"Bed ready?"

"In a minute," says Mama from the doorway,

with the quilt.

Papa looks at the clock.

Mama's minutes are long ones.

Everybody knows it.

"Bed ready?"

"Nearly."

The last screw. The mattress, dropped in.

Olivia pats it. She bounces it once.

She climbs on, up the open side,

and lies flat with her boots still on,

and looks at the ceiling

from her new place in the room.

The crib stands across the room and says nothing.
