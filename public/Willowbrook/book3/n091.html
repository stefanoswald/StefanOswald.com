Night 91

How Long Is Summer

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

On the first of June, Olivia checked the strawberries. Green. Every one.

"Baby come today?"

"Not today," said Mama.

"Tomorrow?"

"Not tomorrow."

"After tomorrow?"

Papa looked at Mama. Time is a slippery thing at two.

"The baby comes in summer," said Mama.

Olivia pointed out the window at the sunshine. "Summer now!"

This was, unfortunately, true.

"Summer is long," said Papa. "I'll show you how long."

So Papa made a paper chain. Link after link after link, one for every sleep, all along the kitchen wall to a drawing of a baby with a star on it.

"One link each night," he said. "When they're all gone, the baby is close."

That night, after teeth, Olivia pulled the first one herself.

Rrrip.

"One gone!"

She took the torn link to bed.
