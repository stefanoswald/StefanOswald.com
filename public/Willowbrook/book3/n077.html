Night 77

The Third Friend Question

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

On the blanket at the park, two girls were building.

Blocks from the park bag, one on one on one.

A little boy came over from the bench.

He was walking now, just.

He knew about four words, and one of them was "mine."

He looked at the tower.

He knocked it flat.

He laughed. It was the best thing he had done all day.

"No!"

"Us building!"

They built it again. He knocked it again.

The boy's lip began to wobble.

Mama sat on the corner of the blanket and waited.

Olivia looked at the blocks.

She looked at the boy.

Then she stacked two blocks, just two, down by his shoes.

"You knock."

He knocked. Bliss.

So Lily built the tall one, far away,

and Olivia built little ones, close by,

and the boy knocked the little ones flat,

one after another.

The blanket was big enough.
