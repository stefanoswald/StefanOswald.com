Night 334

The Coldest Night

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

The coldest night of the winter.

Snow on every branch of the Great Willow.

Even the stars looked cold.

Oliver Owl pulled his head down into his feathers.

Below, Willowbrook was going dark.

One porch light stayed on at the Hendersons',

for somebody not home yet.

At the farm, the Duchess slept on her roost,

one feather short.

"Who is still awake?" asked Oliver.

Stella dipped low over Maple Lane to look.

In the yellow room, Theo slept on his back.

In the next room, Olivia had one hand on her chest.

Then the hand went soft.

Stella came back up, slow.

The Sleepy Moon hummed, low and long.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
