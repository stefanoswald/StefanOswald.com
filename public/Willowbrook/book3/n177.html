Night 177

The Morning After the Wish

Month 6: Big Sister School

The next morning, Olivia came in quietly.

Not tiptoe quiet. A different quiet.

Theo was awake in his bassinet,

on his back,

looking at the cow on the dresser.

Olivia stood on tiptoe at the edge.

She did not say anything at first.

Then, small: "Hi, Fee-oh. It me."

Theo's eyes came around to her.

He looked and looked.

Then the smile came,

the whole face of it.

Olivia looked at Mama.

Mama was sitting up in bed, watching.

Nobody said anything about yesterday.

"Him smile ME."

"He did," said Mama.

"Hi, Fee-oh," said Olivia again, to be sure.

He was sure.

She stayed there,

chin on the edge of the bassinet.
