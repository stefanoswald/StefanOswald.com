Night 265

The Domino Line, Counted

Month 9: Counting to Five

The workshop was cold. They kept their coats on.

Grandpa stood five wood blocks up in a line on the bench.

Leftovers from chairs, smooth as eggs.

"One, two, three, four, five," he said. "Now. One finger."

Olivia pushed the first one.

Click-click-click-click-click.

"AGAIN!"

"Back in my day," said Grandpa, "my line went all the way to the barn."

"Nuh-uh."

"Past the barn," said Grandpa.

Then Olivia stood them up herself.

She put them far apart, with lots of room, so they wouldn't bump.

Push.

One fell down.

Four stood there.

She turned to Grandpa.

Grandpa was very busy with his hat.

She pushed the next one. It fell by itself.

She stood them all up again, closer this time,

touching-close.

Push.

Click-click-click-click-click.

Grandpa went to find more blocks.
