Night 160

The Sitting Sentinel

Month 6: Big Sister School

Mama was cooking.

Theo was in his bouncer on the kitchen floor.

Olivia sat down beside him with Bobby Bear.

"Me guard."

A guard sits very still.

Olivia sat very still.

Almost.

The cat came in from the hall.

"No, cat. Fee-oh mine."

The cat sat down in the doorway.

It looked at Theo.

Olivia looked at the cat.

The cat washed one paw, slowly,

to show it had never been interested.

Theo's eyes got heavy.

Then they closed.

"Asleep?" said Mama. "Bassinet, then."

She lifted him out of the bouncer

and carried him down the hall.

Olivia stayed at her post.

She guarded the empty bouncer.

It was very well guarded.

When Mama came back,

the cat was asleep in the bouncer,

and the guard was asleep beside it.
