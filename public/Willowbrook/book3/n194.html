Night 194

Duck Gets Potty Trained

Month 7: Potty Power

Duck was learning the potty now.

Olivia had decided.

Duck's potty was a block.

"No hurry, Duck," said Olivia.

Duck sat.

Olivia waited, arms folded.

"Nofing? Dat okay."

The next time, Duck did it.

"Duck! Somefing!"

Olivia showed Mama the block.

Mama looked into the block very carefully.

Then Duck was too busy playing,

and Duck forgot.

"Oh no, Duck."

Olivia got down low.

"Wet pants. Dry pants. All done."

She changed Duck's pants,

which were a washcloth.

In the doorway, Mama heard her own voice,

a size smaller.

Duck sat on the block till supper.
