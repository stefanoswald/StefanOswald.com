Night 114

The Just-Us Day

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

One morning Mama said, "Today is a just-us day. You and me, all day."

"Just us?"

"Just us. Ducks first, or the library?"

"Ducks!"

So first the ducks at the pond, every single one said hello to. Then Rosa's bakery, where one bun was split in half and then split again, in Olivia's favor.

"Take your time today, you two," said Rosa.

Then the Lantern Library, one story on Mama's lap under the high round window.

They did not hurry once. Mama walked at Olivia speed all day long.

Then home for a nap in the big bed, both of them, with the bump between them like a third pillow.

"Just-us again?"

"Every month," said Mama. "Even after the baby."

Mama fell asleep first.
