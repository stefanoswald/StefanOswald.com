Night 201

All Yours

Month 7: Potty Power

Bath time.

Warm water.

The big yellow duck, upside down again.

Mama knelt by the tub.

"Your body belongs to you. All of it. Did you know that?"

"My body?"

"Yours. Your arms, your tummy, your private parts, the underpants parts. All yours."

Olivia patted her tummy.

"Mine."

"Yours," said Mama.

"Nobody touches your body without asking."

"Nobody?" said Olivia.

"Not kids. Not grown-ups. Not even family."

"At doctor visits, Mama's there. With bath help, you can say 'I do it.'"

"Me wash." Olivia washed her own knees.

"And if anybody ever makes your body feel weird or secret-y, you tell me. Or Papa. Right away."

"Right away."

"Even if somebody said not to. Especially then. You will never, ever be in trouble for telling."

"Even grown-up?"

"Especially then, love."

Mama will say it again,

on other nights, in other words.

The duck swam to the tap and back.
