Night 309

The Give-Away

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

A box sat by the front door,

half full of things for the library toy corner.

On top were Olivia's stacking cups.

"DAT MINE!"

She pulled them out.

"Mama put dem in!"

Mama came and looked.

"I should have asked. I'm sorry."

Olivia held the cups tight.

"They're yours," said Mama. "You choose."

Olivia sat down on the mat with them.

She built one tower,

red at the bottom,

up to the little blue.

Then she knocked it down.

She looked at the box.

She looked at the cups.

Then she put them back in,

one inside the other.

"Bye, cups."

Her chin wobbled.

On Thursday, at the library,

a baby in a green hat sat in the toy corner

banging two of the cups together and laughing.

Olivia stood still.

"Dat my cups."

"They were," said Mama.

The baby put the little blue one on her head.
