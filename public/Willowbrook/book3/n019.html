Night 19

Brave Isn't Not-Scared

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The doctor's office.

The little ear light is coming.

Olivia does not like the ear light.

Her hand finds Mama's finger and holds on hard.

Her chin wobbles.

"No light," she says.

"I know," says Mama. "I'm right here."

She holds still.

As still as she can, which is not very.

The light peeks in one ear.

Two ears.

"All done," says the doctor.

In the parking lot, Mama crouches down.

"You were scared," she says. "You held still anyway."

Olivia nods.

"That was brave," says Mama.

"Brave," says Olivia.

She has scared. Now she has this one too.

In the car she checks her ears with both hands.

Both still there.
