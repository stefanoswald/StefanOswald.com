Night 259

Counting the Uncountable

Month 9: Counting to Five

In November the stars come out before supper.

Papa and Olivia sat on the back steps in their coats,

under a blanket, to see them.

"One," said Olivia.

The gold one, low down.

It twinkled when she counted it. It likes that.

"Two." A small one.

"Free." Another.

Then there were more.

"Four, five, five, FIVE..."

And more behind those. And more between.

She held up all her fingers.

There were not enough fingers.

"Papa. Too many."

"Way too many," said Papa.

"You count," she said.

Papa held up his hands too.

Still too many.

She put her hands back under the blanket, where it was warm.

They looked for a while without counting.

Then Mama called them in for supper.

Olivia waved to the gold one from the door.
