Night 123

Loud Is His Only Word

Month 5: Theo Arrives

The first night home, the wall woke her up.

"Waaah."

Olivia put her pillow over her head.

"Waaah," said the wall, through the pillow.

Down the hall a light went on.

Feet. Shushing. Mama's low hum.

Then quiet.

Then, later, "Waaah" again.

At breakfast everyone looked rumpled.

"Baby LOUD," said Olivia.

"He is," said Mama.

"Why he cry?"

"Crying is his only word so far," said Mama. "He uses it for everything."

Olivia thought about that, with her spoon in the air.

"Hungry, waaah?"

"Waaah," said Mama.

"Sleepy, waaah?"

"Waaah."

"Wet, waaah?"

Papa nodded. "Especially that."

"Dat hard," said Olivia.

That night the wall cried again.

Olivia did not reach for the pillow.

"Fee-oh talking," she told Baba,

and turned over.
