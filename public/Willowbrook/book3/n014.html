Night 14

Love Is the Warmest One

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Papa is making toast.

Just standing there, minding his toast,

whistling something that is not a tune.

Olivia comes down the hall at full speed

and hugs his knees.

Full strength. No reason.

"Well, hello," says Papa. "What's this for?"

She does not have the big words yet.

She has three.

"Wuv you, Papa."

"Love you too," says Papa.

He puts a hand on her curls.

The toast pops.

He does not get it.

This is the warm one.

It fills up and fills up until it has to go somewhere,

and it picks the nearest knees.

The toast burns a little.

Papa eats that one himself.

Olivia gets the good one, with honey.
