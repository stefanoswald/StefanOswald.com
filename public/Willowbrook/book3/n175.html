Night 175

The Double Bedtime

Month 6: Big Sister School

Bedtime goes twice now.

Theo first.

Milk. Song. Bassinet.

Olivia helped with the song.

"Why Fee-oh first?"

"Littlest first," said Papa. "Last gets the long one."

Then Olivia's turn.

Bath. Pajamas. Book.

Papa sat on the big bed with the book,

and Olivia turned the pages, one at a time.

Three pages in, Theo cried.

"Hang on," said Papa, and went.

Olivia sat with the book open.

She turned a page by herself.

She turned it back.

She waited and waited.

When Papa came back, her arms were crossed.

"Papa GONE."

Papa sat down.

"I left in the middle. I'm sorry."

"Middle," said Olivia, and held up the book.

"From the start?"

"From da START."

So he read it from the start,

every page, slowly.

Nobody was waiting after her.
