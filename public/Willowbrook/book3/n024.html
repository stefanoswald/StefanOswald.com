Night 24

The Cozy Feeling

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Rain outside, tapping the fort roof.

The fort roof is the kitchen table.

Cocoa inside, cooled, half drunk.

Bobby Bear in the crook of an arm.

Mama on one side. Papa on the other.

The blanket just heavy enough.

Papa builds the best forts.

This one has a flashlight in it

and a door made of a chair.

"What's this feeling called?" says Papa.

Hmm.

Happy? More than happy.

Calm? Cozier than calm.

Olivia has her own word.

She says it now, burrowed in.

"Home."

Papa looks at Mama over her head.

"That'll do," he says.

The rain keeps on.

The cocoa gets finished.

The fort holds.
