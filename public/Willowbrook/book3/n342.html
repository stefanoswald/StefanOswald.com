Night 342

Bella Lands on Theo

Month 12: Almost Three

The first warm noon came.

Snow slid off the shed roof. Whump.

Mama sat on the back step with Theo in her lap,

and Olivia stomped the puddles to see how deep they went.

Over the fence, bright as a box of crayons,

came Bella.

Bella comes back every year when winter lets go.

Nobody knows where she waits.

"Fee-oh," Olivia said. "Still. Like me."

She held so still her hat shook.

Bella went round the yard once.

She went round the step.

She landed on Theo's mitten.

Theo looked at his mitten.

He looked at Olivia.

Then he made a sound nobody had heard him make before.

"Pbbbbbt."

It was a very wet sound.

Bella did not seem to mind.

"Hello, almost-three," she said, to Olivia only,

and lifted away over the fence.

"Dat not a word," said Olivia.

"No," said Mama. "But it's new."

Theo said it to the fence all afternoon.
