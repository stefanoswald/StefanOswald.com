Night 106

The Worry Sneaks Back

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Warm rain all afternoon, and no nap.

At snack time the cracker broke in two. That was the end of everything.

"NO! Broke!"

Olivia threw herself down on the kitchen floor and roared.

Mama got down on the floor too, close, and said only, "I'm with you."

The roar went on. Then it turned into crying, and the crying got smaller, and a hand came out, reaching.

Mama took the hand. Then all of her.

In the quiet after, the real thing came out, wet and small.

"Baby get my crib."

A hiccup.

"Baby get Mama."

So that was it. The worry was back.

"You were so worried," said Mama. "About the baby and me."

Olivia nodded into her shoulder.

"That worry might come again. When it does, come find me."

"Find you."

Mama picked up the two halves of the cracker. She gave Olivia the big one.

Mama ate the little half.
