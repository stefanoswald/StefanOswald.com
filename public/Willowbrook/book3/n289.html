Night 289

The Kite Day

Month 10: Try Again

Then one afternoon the wind came in wild.

It banged the gate.

It rattled the windows.

It bent the bare treetops right over.

"Kite wind?" said Olivia.

"Kite wind," said Papa.

On the hill the wind shoved them both.

Papa held the kite up high.

"Ready... go!"

He let go.

The kite went up.

And up.

No nose-dive. No spin.

Higher than the oak.

Higher than the hill.

Small and red against the whole gray sky.

The string pulled in her hands.

Then it hummed.

"Papa! It humming!"

Papa put his hands around hers,

and they held it together.

The wind pulled.

They pulled back.

They stayed till their noses were red and the light went low.

At bedtime her hands still felt it.
