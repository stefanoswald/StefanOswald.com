Night 111

The False Alarm

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

In the night, the lights came on. Voices. Papa with the bag. Mama finding her shoes.

Rosa came in her slippers, and Olivia rode to Lily's house in her pajamas, buckled into the car seat, her own small bag on her knees.

But it was not time. The baby had knocked, sort of, and changed its mind. A doctor checked Mama. All was well. It was not time yet.

By lunch everybody was home, and the big bag went back by the door.

Olivia stood in the hall with her fists tight.

"Baby trick us!"

"You're mad," said Mama. "You got all ready, and no baby."

"MAD."

Mama sat on the bottom stair and waited. Olivia leaned on her knee until her fists came open.

Later, by the bag, she asked. "Dat bad?"

"Maybe," said Mama. "What could we do now?"

"Check duck."

She unzipped the corner. The duck was still in there.

She zipped it back up herself.
