Night 50

The Water Fountain

Month 2: Me Do It

The first warm day.

Coats open. The park full of big kids.

And the drinking fountain, the one with the button.

Big kids use it all day, one hand, not even looking.

"Me do it."

Up onto the ledge. It is just her size, that ledge.

The button. Push.

Water, a whole arc of it.

She leaned in and the arc went up her nose.

Splutter. Regroup.

Push. Lean, wrong. Wet chin.

Push. Lean, late. Nothing.

Push, and lean at the same time,

and there it was. A sip.

"Cold!"

Real park water.

She drank like that for a while,

push, lean, sip,

push, lean, sip,

with her hair wet at the front,

while three big kids waited behind her

and did not say a thing,

because they had all been two once

at that same fountain,

and Mama did not hurry her either.
