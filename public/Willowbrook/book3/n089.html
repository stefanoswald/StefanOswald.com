Night 89

The Scribble Picture

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

After dinner Olivia made a picture of everything.

It took all the crayons.

At bedtime she brought it to the big bed.

"What's in it?" said Mama.

A red round one. "Bonk! Wiwi!"

"The store. And then?"

A long wiggly one. "Sketti."

"The wrong groceries. The best dinner, twice now."

A tower, lying down. "Mad."

"You were both mad. Then?"

"Build again."

A brown one. "Gold dog."

Olivia held her hand out flat, to show.

"Wiwi's hand, too," said Mama.

A blue one, straight across. "She play!"

"Lily stood right in front of you."

A green one. "Pickle."

That one needed a laugh.

"And the quiet day?"

"Shh. Books."

"And the wagon?"

"Witch!"

Olivia pointed at Lily's duck on the wall.

"Wiwi duck."

"From Lily's mailbox to ours," said Mama.

In the corner there was one more,

very small and very round.

"Baby."

Mama turned the lamp down on all of it.
