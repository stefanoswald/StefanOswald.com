Night 206

The Saved Face

Month 7: Potty Power

Theo smiled at everybody now.

But nobody had made him laugh.

Olivia tried.

Puffed cheeks. A smile.

The talking sock. A smile and a coo.

"BOO!" A blink.

She sat back on her heels.

Then she remembered.

Papa, on the porch steps, back in June.

Save that one for the baby, he had said.

She leaned in close.

Tongue out.

Eyes squeezed shut.

Both ears pulled.

Theo stared.

Then something bubbled up out of him.

"Ah-heh! Heh-heh-heh!"

A real laugh, all the way from his tummy.

Olivia's eyes flew open.

"MAMA! Fee-oh LAUGH!"

Mama came to the doorway.

Olivia did the face again.

Theo laughed again, and kicked both feet.

Papa came running with a dish towel.

"That one," he said. "That's the face."

At bedtime Olivia showed Bobby Bear the face.

Bobby Bear did not laugh.

"Only Fee-oh," she said.
