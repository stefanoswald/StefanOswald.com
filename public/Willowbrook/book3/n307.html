Night 307

The Casserole Run

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Across the street, Mrs. Henderson had a bad cold,

and Mr. Henderson had one too.

"Who make dey dinner?" said Olivia.

"Nobody feels like cooking with a cold," said Mama.

So Mama made the noodle one.

She wrapped the warm dish in a towel

and set it in the sled.

"Me pull it!"

Olivia put the rope over her shoulder.

Crunch, crunch, went the sled.

At the street they stopped and looked both ways,

and Mama held her hand across.

Up the walk.

Slow.

Slower.

Mama carried the dish up the icy steps.

Olivia did the knock.

Mrs. Henderson opened the door in her robe.

Her nose was pink.

"Noodle one," said Olivia. "For you."

Then she added, "No cooking."

Mrs. Henderson laughed, and coughed,

and laughed some more.

"Yes, ma'am."

She held the dish against her robe

and shut her eyes for a moment.

Going home, Olivia rode in the sled.

Mama pulled.
