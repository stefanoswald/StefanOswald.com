Night 234

The Squeal Tour

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

The whole lane had to hear it.

First, Mrs. Henderson, raking leaves on her lawn.

"Do it, Fee-oh!"

Theo looked at Mrs. Henderson.

He yawned.

Next, the bakery door.

Rosa came out with flour on one cheek and bent over the stroller.

"Do it, Fee-oh!"

Theo sneezed.

Next, the gold dog, wagging at his fence.

"Fee-oh. Pwease?"

Theo looked at the sky.

"He's saving it," said Mama.

Olivia sighed an enormous sigh.

She gave up on the whole lane

and leaned in over the side of the stroller,

so her face was just under the hood.

"EEEEEEE!"

His whole self kicked.

Up and down the lane, heads turned.

The gold dog barked once.

Mrs. Henderson waved her rake.

Theo went back to looking at the sky.
