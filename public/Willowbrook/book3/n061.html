Night 61

The Great Escape

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Mama reached for the apples.

One apple. Two apples. A bag to put them in.

When she turned back there was a sticky spot on the cart

and no hand on it.

Olivia was running.

Past the beans.

Past the soup.

Past a man with a mop, who said, "Whoa there."

The floor was shiny, the aisle was long,

and her shoes went slap, slap, slap.

"So good!" she told the tomatoes.

"OLIVIA!"

That was Mama's big voice, the one she almost never needs.

Olivia did not stop.

Stores are big and girls are small.

That is why hands get held.

This day turned out well. Hold the hand anyway.

Mama swung the cart round and went after the slap, slap, slap.

Round the corner at the end of the aisle, a bump.

Then quiet.

Then laughing, two kinds.

Mama knew both of them.
