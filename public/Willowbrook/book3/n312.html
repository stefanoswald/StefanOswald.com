Night 312

Fixing Mateo's Chain

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Mama was sweeping snow off the steps.

Down the sidewalk, Mateo's bike stopped.

Clunk.

The chain hung down in a greasy loop.

Mateo pushed the pedal. Nothing.

He sat down on the curb, chin on his knees.

Olivia had never seen Mateo stuck.

Mateo fixes everything.

"Fix it?"

"Can't," said Mateo. "My hands are too little."

Olivia ran to his porch

and came back with his tool belt.

Twig hammer. String. A clothespin.

The twig did not help the chain.

So Olivia ran back up his steps

and banged on the door with both mittens.

"Mateo mama! Chain fell off!"

Mateo's mama came out in her slippers.

She turned the bike over,

hooked the chain on,

and turned the pedal.

Click, click, click.

Mateo looked at Olivia.

"You got my mama."

"And da belt."

He wiped his black hands on his coat.

His mama decided not to see.

Then he rode to the corner and back,

so she could watch.
