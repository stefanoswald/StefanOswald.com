Night 166

He's MY Brother

Month 6: Big Sister School

The market on the lane was busy.

Theo in the stroller, under the sunshade. Olivia holding on.

A lady swooped in.

"Oh, look at this darling!"

Her face came right down at Theo.

Olivia stepped in front of the wheel.

She crossed her arms.

"Dat MY brudder."

The lady stopped.

"Ask me," said Olivia.

The lady straightened up.

She was a Willowbrook lady,

so she did it exactly right.

"May I say hello to your brother?"

Olivia thought about it.

"Yes. No touch."

"No touch," said the lady.

"Wave."

The lady waved from a good way off.

Theo looked at her,

and then at the peaches.

"He's well guarded," the lady said to Mama.

"He is," said Mama.

Olivia kept one hand on the stroller

all the way home.
