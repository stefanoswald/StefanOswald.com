Night 99

The Hospital Bag

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

A bag appeared by the front door. Zipped. Waiting.

"What dat bag?"

"The hospital bag," said Papa. "When the baby's ready, we grab it and go."

Inside went tiny clothes, the camera, crackers for Papa, and a list taped to the front.

Olivia looked it all over. "Where my stuff?"

"You get your own bag," said Mama. "For a sleepover at Lily's, when the baby comes."

"Real sleepover?"

"A real one."

So Olivia packed. Pajamas. Toothbrush. Bobby Bear. One shoe. Papa added the other shoe later, when she wasn't looking.

Then she went back to the big bag, unzipped one corner, and pushed something deep down inside. Her yellow bath duck, the big one.

"For baby."

Papa left it right where it was.

Now there were two bags by the door.
