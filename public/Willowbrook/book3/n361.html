Night 361

The Wheelbarrow

Month 12: Almost Three

After the cake, Grandpa Joe took Olivia out to the garage.

Something stood there under a sheet.

It had a wheel.

She could see the wheel.

Grandpa Joe pulled the sheet away.

A wheelbarrow, just her size,

oak, sanded smooth,

with handles right where her hands were.

And on the side, carved deep, a star.

Everything Grandpa makes has that star.

Olivia pulled the curl of wood out of her pocket.

She had kept it all week.

"Dis from it?"

Grandpa Joe nodded.

"For your garden," he said. "Come spring."

She pushed it all the way round the garage.

The wheel went squeak, squeak.

"Fee-oh ride?"

"Duck first," said Grandpa.

So Duck rode, sitting up very straight.

Then the cat got in.

The cat did not get out.

By the time the guests went home, the cat was asleep in the wheelbarrow,

on Duck.
