Night 66

Life Happens FOR Us

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Olivia woke a little on the stairs,

the way you do when you are carried.

In the big bed, Mama sat on the edge.

"What a day."

"Cookie."

"A cookie. And then?"

"Me run."

"You ran, and I couldn't find you. That part was scary."

"Hold hand."

"Hold hands. And then?"

"Bonk! Wiwi!"

"And then the carts got all mixed up."

"Wiwi game."

"And then?"

Olivia smiled with her eyes shut.

"Sketti."

Mama tucked the blanket up under her chin.

"A crash, and the wrong groceries, and then the best dinner we ever had."

She said the next part very low.

"Life happens for us."

"Maybe?" said Olivia, nearly asleep.

Mama kissed her forehead.

There was still a little sauce behind one ear.

Mama left it.
