Night 275

Freddy's Famous Miss

Month 10: Try Again

Frogs sleep in the mud all winter.

Freddy had one jump to do first.

"The famous one," he told Olivia.

"All the way across."

Papa held Olivia's hand on the bank.

Papa heard a croak.

Olivia heard the rest.

Tessa, on her slow way to bed for the winter,

stopped to watch.

Freddy backed up.

He wiggled.

He jumped.

Splash.

Not on the far bank.

In the middle, in the cold, cold water.

He came up in the reeds with his hat on sideways.

He did not say "Ta-da."

He did not say anything.

"Freddy?" said Olivia. "You okay?"

"I missed," said Freddy, very small.

"I never miss."

Olivia pulled off one red mitten.

"Want dis?"

Freddy looked at the mitten.

He looked at the far bank.

He climbed out and got inside the mitten.

Only his hat stuck out.
