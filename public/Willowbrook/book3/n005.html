Night 5

Scared Has Big Ears

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Thump.

A sound in the hallway, in the dark.

Olivia freezes.

Her eyes go big.

Her shoulders go up.

Her feet grow roots.

Papa comes out and turns on the hall light.

He does not say it was nothing.

"Big thump," he says. "You got scared."

"Scared," says Olivia.

It is a new word. It fits.

"Let's go look together."

Hand in hand they creep around the corner.

Slowly. Slower.

There on the floor is a heap.

A dark heap with arms.

It is Papa's coat.

It slid off its hook, and that was the thump.

"Papa coat!"

Olivia laughs the wobbliest laugh.

Papa hangs it on a better hook.

Back in bed, her ears stay big a while.

Then they go small.
