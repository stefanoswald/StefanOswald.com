Night 44

The Jacket Flip Trick

Month 2: Me Do It

Rosa knows a trick. Lily does it at Rosa's.

Rosa showed Mama at the bakery door,

with a bag of rolls under one arm,

and Mama brought it home.

Rain, so, raincoat.

Mama laid it on the floor upside down,

collar at Olivia's toes.

"Arms in."

Both arms, into the sleeves.

"Now up, over your head."

Whoosh.

Olivia stood in the hall in her coat.

Right way round. Arms in. Done.

She looked at her sleeves

as if they had done it without her.

"Again."

Off. Floor. Arms. Whoosh.

"Again!"

Nine more.

That coat has never gone on nine times before breakfast.

By the last one she did not want Mama's hands near it,

not even to lay it down.

She laid it down.

Collar at her toes.

Arms.

Up, and out the door into the rain,

hood up, by nobody.
