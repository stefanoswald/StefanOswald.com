Night 117

The Real Night

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

A hand on her shoulder, in the dark.

"Olivia, sweetheart." Mama's voice was calm as the pond. "It's baby time. The real one."

Olivia sat straight up.

The house was moving, quietly. Papa had the bag.

"Duck in?"

Papa checked. "Duck's in."

At the bottom of the stairs Mama stopped, one hand on the rail, and breathed slowly. Flower in. Dandelion out. Olivia breathed it with her.

Headlights turned into the drive. Rosa, in her slippers again.

At the door, Mama bent down as far as she could bend and held Olivia's cheeks.

"Next time you see us, you'll be a big sister."

"Already big sister."

Rosa told that one for years.

Rosa buckled Olivia into the car seat. Papa's car went one way down the lane, and Rosa's went the other.

The taillights turned at the corner.
