Night 353

The Maybe Night

Month 12: Almost Three

Mama sat on the edge of the big bed.

"Maybe night," she said.

Olivia knows where her part goes.

"Once, when you were very small," said Mama,

"the milk spilled all over the table,

and we were late, late, late."

"Uh-oh."

"And because we were late, the snowplow went by,

and the driver waved at you.

So was the spill bad?"

"MAYBE!"

"And once, you and Lily crashed carts at the store,

and the groceries got all mixed up."

"BONK!"

"Was that bad?"

"MAYBE! We got noodles!"

"And once, your tower fell down."

"Me so mad."

"You were so mad. And then?"

"It a bridge."

Mama turned the lamp down low.

"And in a few more sleeps, you'll be three. Is that good?"

Olivia opened her mouth to say yes.

Then she grinned.

"Maybe."

Mama laughed.

Then she turned the lamp all the way down.
