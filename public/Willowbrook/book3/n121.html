Night 121

The Meeting

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Papa carried her down a long, bright hall to a door.

Inside, morning came gold through the blinds.

Mama was in the bed, tired and smiling,

with something small in her arms.

But Olivia saw the windowsill first.

"Duck!"

Her yellow duck from the hospital bag sat there.

"Duck kept us company all night," said Mama.

"Come meet your brother."

Olivia held Papa's leg.

She had her big sister shirt on,

and she was suddenly shy.

Papa lifted her onto the bed.

She scooted. She leaned.

A red, squashed face. A tiny hat. One fist out.

He was crying a thin, new cry.

"Hi, Fee-oh," she said. "It me."

The crying stopped.

Theo's head turned a little, toward her voice.

"He hear me."

"He knows your voice," said Papa.

Olivia touched his cheek with one finger,

the way she practiced on Baba.

Then she leaned on Mama and looked at him a long time.

The duck watched from the sill.
