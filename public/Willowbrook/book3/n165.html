Night 165

Nurse Olivia's Rounds

Month 6: Big Sister School

Theo had his first cold.

Snuffle. Sniff. Snork.

He was very little,

so Mama called the doctor first thing,

and the doctor told her what to watch for.

Olivia went on duty.

She brought tissues, at a run.

She watched the humidifier puff cool mist.

"Fee-oh cloud."

Then Duck got a cold too.

Olivia wrapped her in a dish towel.

She wiped Duck's nose.

She gave Duck milk from an empty spoon.

"Poor Duck. Sick."

"Poor Duck," said Mama.

"Fee-oh better?"

"Soon, I think. We're watching him."

Olivia sang to Duck,

some of the words and most of the tune.

Theo, on Mama's shoulder, listened too.

His snuffles went slower.

After a week, Theo's nose was clear.

Duck's took one more day.
