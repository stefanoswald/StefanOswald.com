Night 317

The Listening Bench

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Lily would not play.

She sat on the bench by the frozen pond

with her mittens in her lap.

"Gamma went home," she said.

"On da train."

Olivia had ideas.

She did her silly dance.

Lily didn't laugh.

She held out Duck.

Lily didn't take her.

She made her best fish face.

Nothing.

Olivia stood in the snow with no ideas left.

Then she climbed up on the bench

and sat right next to Lily,

so their coats touched.

She looked at the pond too.

Nobody said anything.

The snow came down, slow.

Right behind the bench, Mama and Rosa talked quietly

and let them be.

After a long time,

Lily leaned over

and put her head on Olivia's shoulder.

"Your hat itchy," she said.

She didn't move.
