Night 109

The Chain Gets Short

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

That morning the first strawberry in the garden turned red. Olivia ran inside with it.

"Strawberry red! Baby come!"

The baby did not come. Strawberries, it turns out, do not know about babies.

"A paper chain is only a guess," said Papa. "The baby decides."

Olivia ate the strawberry. That helped a little.

At night the tearing had become a whole show. The chain that once ran the length of the kitchen was down to a few links, and the drawing of the baby with the star was close enough to touch.

She climbed onto the chair while Papa held the back of it. She chose a link. She counted.

"Free. Two. One."

Rrrip.

The torn link went in the bowl on the counter with all the others.

Papa counted what was left. Olivia helped by pointing.

The bowl was nearly full.
