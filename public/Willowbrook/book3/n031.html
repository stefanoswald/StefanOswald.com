Night 31

Stella and the Weather Report

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The town was dark.

Stella dipped low.

"Rain this morning," she told the Moon.

"Mud on the lane."

"The brook is high and loud."

"Clear now."

"Mmm," hummed the Moon.

"One lamp still on at the farm."

"One sock on a shelf."

"The small one is asleep, arms out."

"Mmm."

That was the whole weather.

Stella dipped a little lower.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
