Night 268

The Stairs Go Past Five

Month 9: Counting to Five

Bedtime stairs.

Olivia went up with one hand on the rail, counting.

"One. Two. Free. Four. Five."

Then there were more stairs.

"Five," she said, on the next one. "Five. FIVE."

"Mm-hm," said Papa.

He was reading something on his phone.

"Papa. What next?"

"Mm."

Olivia plopped down on the stairs and crossed her arms.

"You not looking."

Papa looked up.

He put the phone in his pocket.

"I wasn't listening. I'm sorry."

He sat on the step below her.

"After five?" she said.

"Six," said Papa.

"Six!"

"Seven. Eight."

She had never heard of these.

They went up together,

Papa saying the new numbers and Olivia saying them after him, one step behind.

"Eleven. Twelve."

The top.

"More numbers?"

"They keep going," said Papa. "Forever."

Olivia sat on the top step to think about forever.

Papa sat beside her.
