Night 81

The Laughing Fit at Nothing

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

It started with a word.

Lily said it after lunch, about lunch.

"Pickle."

Olivia laughed.

So Lily laughed.

So Olivia said "PICKLE!" and they both slid off their chairs.

Pickle is not a funny word.

They knew that.

That made it funnier.

They tried to stop.

They sat up very straight.

They looked at each other.

That was a mistake.

Down again.

"Pi," said Olivia, and never got to the rest of it.

In the kitchen the mamas were laughing too,

and not one of them could have said why.

Hiccups. Wet eyes. Sore cheeks.

Then crackers, and some quiet, and crunching.

Lily looked at her empty plate.

"Pickle," she whispered.

One more slide to the floor, a small one.

Then they lay there side by side

until the hiccups stopped.
