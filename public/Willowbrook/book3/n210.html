Night 210

Block Seven

Month 7: Potty Power

Mid-morning, and the tower was six blocks high.

Olivia had block seven in her hand.

Then she stopped.

She put block seven down.

She walked to the bathroom.

She didn't tell anybody.

The door went to its inch.

The step scraped.

The roar came.

Then the tap, and the whole washing song,

all the way to "Bye!"

She came back with her hands still wet

and wiped them on her shirt.

Mama looked up from her book.

"Did you go?"

"Me goed."

Mama went to look anyway.

It was fine, mostly.

Olivia picked up block seven

and put it on top.

It held.
