Night 245

The Whole Hand

Month 9: Counting to Five

At breakfast, Olivia counted her hand.

The thumb. "One."

The pointer. "Two."

The tall one. "Free."

The next one would not stand up by itself, so she held it up. "Four."

The little one. "Five!"

The whole hand.

She folded it into a fist.

"All gone."

She opened it.

"All back!"

"Do mine," said Papa, and held up his big hand.

She counted it. One, two, free, four, five.

"Wrong," said Papa. "I have seven."

"No you DON'T."

He counted them himself. "One, two, three, nine, seven."

"PAPA."

She bent his fingers down one at a time, firmly,

the way you have to with papas.

"One. Two. Free. Four. Five."

"Five," said Papa. "Who knew."

He picked up his toast with all five.
