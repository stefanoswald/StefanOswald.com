Night 325

Rosa's Surprise

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Rosa bakes for everybody.

Buns for just-us mornings.

Bread for every new baby.

Loaves that are the last one, and never are.

"Who bakes for Rosa?" said Mama.

Lily and Olivia looked at each other.

So on Tuesday, in Olivia's kitchen,

they made Rosa a braid.

Over. Under.

"Which one under?"

"Dat one."

It came out lumpy,

with a big knob on one end.

It baked up brown and smelled like Rosa's.

They carried it two streets over,

Mama holding the warm plate from underneath.

Lily knocked.

Olivia knocked louder.

Rosa opened the door.

She looked at the braid.

"Lumpy braids..." she said.

"BAKE UP DA SAME!" said both girls.

Then Rosa put her dish towel over her whole face.

"Mama sad?" said Lily.

"No," said Rosa, from behind the towel.

"Mama is the other thing."

She carried the braid inside like a baby.
