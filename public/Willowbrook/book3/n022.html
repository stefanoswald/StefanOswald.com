Night 22

Silly Is Medicine

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

A hard day.

A scraped knee. A lost mitten. A nap that did not take.

By dinner everyone was flat.

So Papa put the fruit bowl lid on his head.

"I am ready for dinner," he said.

Olivia stared.

Then the giggle.

Then the big laugh, the one with no breath in it.

Mama put socks on her hands.

The socks ate her peas.

Then Papa's game.

"Please pass the elephant."

"No! Bread!"

"Thank you for the snowplow."

"No! Water!"

"Olivia, eat your shoes."

"No! Peas!"

Peas went everywhere anyway.

The knee still hurt a bit.

The mitten was still lost.

The lid stayed on Papa's head through the washing up,

and nobody told him.
