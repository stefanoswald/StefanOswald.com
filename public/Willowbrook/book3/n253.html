Night 253

The Snack Subtraction

Month 9: Counting to Five

Five crackers on the plate.

Mama counted them out. "One, two, three, four, five."

Olivia ate one. "One!"

She ate another. "Two!"

"Free!"

The numbers went up.

The crackers went down.

She stopped and stared at the plate.

"Mama. Dey going."

"Where are they going?"

She patted her tummy. "In dere."

"Four." Munch.

"Five." The plate was empty.

"More five?"

"Two more," said Mama. "Supper's soon."

"TWO? Dat not five."

"Two," said Mama.

Olivia counted them. One, two.

She counted them again, slower, in case.

Still two.

She ate the first one in very small bites,

to make it last.
