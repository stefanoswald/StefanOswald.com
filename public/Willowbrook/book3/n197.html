Night 197

Up on the Big One

Month 7: Potty Power

At home, Olivia still used the little potty.

Today she pointed at the big one.

"Dat one."

So Mama set the little seat on the big toilet

and brought Olivia's step over from the sink.

Step up.

Turn around.

Hold on.

Sit.

"Me high!"

Her feet sat flat on the step.

"Feet on the step, every time," said Mama.

Feet on something solid make the job easier,

so the step stays.

When she was done, Olivia climbed down.

She looked at the handle.

She looked at Mama.

Then she pushed it herself,

no hand over hers.

The roar came.

Olivia kept her hands at her sides.

Over in the corner, the little potty waited.

The cat was asleep in it.
