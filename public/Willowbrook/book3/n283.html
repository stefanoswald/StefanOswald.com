Night 283

The Yet Word

Month 10: Try Again

The puzzle came down from the shelf every day.

Today four pieces went together,

the duck's whole head.

The other eight would not.

"I can't do it," said Olivia.

Mama said, "You can't do it yet."

"Yet?"

"Yet means it's still coming."

Olivia tried the word on.

"I can't do it... yet."

She put the box back on the shelf herself.

At supper Papa said,

"I can't whistle right."

"Yet!" said Olivia.

Papa tried.

It sounded like a kettle with a cold.

"Fee-oh can't sit up," said Olivia.

"Yet," said Mama.

"Cat can't talk."

Papa looked at the cat.

The cat looked at Papa.

"That one might stay a no," said Papa.

The cat washed one paw.
