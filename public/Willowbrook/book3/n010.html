Night 10

Calm Is the Pond

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The first mild day.

Coats open. Mud on the path.

Mama takes Olivia to the pond.

Look.

No wind.

The water is flat and soft.

The ducks leave slow lines behind them.

The sky lies on top.

"This is calm," says Mama. "The pond is calm."

Then Mama shows her a trick.

She holds up one hand like a flower.

She sniffs it, slow. In.

Then she blows, slow, like a dandelion. Out.

Mama does it first, twice, by herself.

"Flower in," says Mama.

Olivia sniffs.

"Dandelion out."

Olivia blows. Her cheeks go round.

Again.

Again.

The third time she blows so hard she falls over.

The ducks do not mind.

The water stays flat.

So does Olivia, on her back in the mud.
