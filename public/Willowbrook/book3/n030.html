Night 30

The Month of Names, Kept

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Bedtime, at the lamp.

Papa is telling the month back.

He is getting it wrong on purpose.

"Remember the balloon that went small? You were so happy."

"No! Sad."

"Sad. And when Wiwi came to the door?"

"Happy!"

"And the tower, when Wiwi went home. You were shy."

"No! Mad."

"Mad," says Papa. "Boom."

"And the thump in the hall?"

Olivia's shoulders go up, remembering.

"Scared. Papa coat."

"Papa coat," Papa agrees.

Mama adds the rest, one each.

The farm day, jacket backwards.

The zipper, wiggled till it zipped.

The bucket, handed to Lily.

The ear light, held still for.

The pond, and the flower, and the blow.

The fort, and her word for it.

"Home," says Olivia.

"And the clouds?" says Mama.

"Bye, cloud."

"They went by," says Mama. "You stayed."

The lamp clicks off.

Olivia is already asleep, arms out wide.
