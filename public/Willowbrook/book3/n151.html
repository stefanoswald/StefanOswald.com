Night 151

Two Windows

Month 5: Theo Arrives

A warm night on Maple Lane.

The fireflies were out, low over the grass.

Stella came down the sky, as she does,

to look in on the house.

One window: Olivia in her big bed,

Bobby Bear under one arm and Duck under the other.

Asleep.

The next window: Mama and Papa's room.

The bassinet by the big bed, and in it, Theo,

on his back, with his fists up by his ears.

Asleep, for now.

Last summer Stella had one window here to watch.

Now she had two.

She moved over a little, to hang between them.

"Mmm," hummed the Sleepy Moon.

One by one, the lamps on the lane went out.

The fireflies kept blinking.

Goodnight, Willowbrook.
