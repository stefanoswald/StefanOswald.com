Night 62

The Crash

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Here is the part Mama didn't see.

Two runners.

One corner.

Nobody looking.

Bonk.

Down they went in a heap by the cereal.

One red bow, crooked.

One blue jacket, half off.

They blinked at each other.

It was Lily.

Lily from the bench, Lily from the park, Lily from every week since they were babies,

running away from her own mama,

with the same cookie from the same man in her fist.

"Wiwi!"

"Livy!"

The heap began to shake.

Then the heap was laughing, the big kind with no breath in it.

Two carts came round the corner too fast and bumped nose to nose.

Rosa scooped up Lily.

Mama got down on the floor and held on,

and took one slow breath before she said anything.

"I couldn't see you. That scared me."

Olivia's laugh went small.

"In the store, we hold hands. Every time."

"Hand," said Olivia,

and gave her the sticky one.
