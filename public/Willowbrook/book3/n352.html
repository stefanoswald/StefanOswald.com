Night 352

The Just-Us Morning

Month 12: Almost Three

Before every birthday, Olivia and Mama take one morning just for them.

Papa keeps Theo.

Nobody hurries.

First, Rosa's, for a bun.

Rosa had put three sugar stars on top,

and there was flour on her cheek, as usual.

Then the Lantern Library,

one story on Mama's lap in the round room.

Then the pond, holding hands.

The ice had pulled back from the edge,

and the ducks were paddling in the open water.

"Dey know me," said Olivia.

The ducks mostly knew the bun.

On the way home, the gold dog was lying in a square of sun by his fence.

His tail went thump, thump.

Olivia stopped there.

"Same me at free?"

"Same you," said Mama. "Plus one candle."

Olivia thought about that.

"I like dis me."

"So do I," said Mama.

The gold dog put his chin back down in the sun.
