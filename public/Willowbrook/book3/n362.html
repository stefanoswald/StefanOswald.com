Night 362

The Willow Walk at Three

Month 12: Almost Three

When the party was over,

the four of them walked up the hill to the Great Willow.

They go every birthday.

The stepping stones were under the high brown water.

"Brook too big," said Olivia.

"Then we go by the bridge," said Papa.

They went by the bridge.

At the top, the whole town lay below them,

the lane and the library lantern and the farm,

all going gold, then blue.

Olivia patted the willow's trunk, flat-handed, hello.

In the bark was last year's word.

"Home," Mama read.

And under it, where nobody had carved anything,

was a new word.

"What dat say?"

"Family," said Mama.

Olivia looked at Mama. At Papa.

At Theo, asleep on Papa's shoulder.

"One, two, free, four," she said.

They went home down the hill in the almost-dark,

and behind them the willow glowed, a little.
