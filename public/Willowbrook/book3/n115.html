Night 115

Papa's Version

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Porch steps at dusk. Papa tried to whistle and made a sound like a bicycle tire going flat.

"Papa, no."

Then Papa told her something she had never heard.

"I was a big brother once. I still am."

Olivia stared at him. Papa?

"Your uncle was my baby," said Papa. "He was very, very loud."

"Loud?"

"Loud. He got all the laps, and I was cross about it."

"Den?"

"Then one day I made a face." Papa made it now, eyes crossed, cheeks puffed out. "And he laughed at me first."

Olivia laughed too. She couldn't help it.

"Me make face!"

She made one. Tongue out, eyes squeezed shut, both ears pulled.

Papa nearly fell off the step.

"That one," he said. "Save that one for the baby."

She practiced it on the lane until dark.

The string lights came on.
