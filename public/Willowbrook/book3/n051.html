Night 51

Buckling the Doll Stroller

Month 2: Me Do It

A doll stroller came across the street this week

from Mrs. Henderson, whose grandchildren are big now.

It has a little buckle.

A toy one. Simple, nothing to pinch.

Olivia sat the bear in it.

She looked at the buckle a long time.

She has been watching buckles all month.

The car seat one is still Papa's, and will be for years.

This one is her size.

The two ends. Aim. Press. Miss.

Aim. Press. Miss.

Aim.

Click.

Click!

She undid it and did it again, to hear it.

Then she looked at the bear, strapped in,

his flopped ear over the strap.

"Safe now, Baba."

Off they went down the hall,

around the corner by the shelf,

one wheel squeaking,

the bear leaning to one side

and not falling out.
