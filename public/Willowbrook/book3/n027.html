Night 27

The Feelings Are Weather, You Are the Sky

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Blanket on the park hill.

The grass is still wet from the thaw.

Look up.

Clouds today. All kinds.

A big grey grumbly one.

A little white puff.

A long thin one, going somewhere.

"Watch them," says Mama.

They move. They cross. They go.

"Feelings are like that," says Mama.

"Mad clouds, sad clouds. They come and they go."

"And you?"

Olivia waits.

"You're the sky. The clouds go through you."

The grumbly one slides off east.

"Bye, cloud," says Olivia.

"The sky stays," says Mama.

The blue behind it was there the whole time.

Behind every one of them.

Olivia lies on the blanket and looks at it a long while.

Then she points.

"Duck."

It is a cloud shaped like a duck.
