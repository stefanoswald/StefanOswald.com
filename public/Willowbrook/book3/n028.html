Night 28

The Feelings Toolbox

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Olivia has a shelf now.

The low one, by the window.

Look what is on it.

The red string, from the balloon that went small.

"Sad," says Olivia, and touches it.

The little lamp, from the corner.

"Corner," she says. "Sock."

The sock is there too. Papa let her keep it.

A dandelion in a cup, the first one from the lane.

She sniffs it. In.

She blows it. Out.

It is the yellow kind. Nothing flies.

She blows anyway.

Bobby Bear, for forts.

And the fruit bowl lid, which Papa wants back.

"That's a lot of things," says Mama.

"Mine," says Olivia.

Papa looks at the shelf a while.

"Where do I keep mine?" he says.

Olivia moves the sock to the front.

That's better.
