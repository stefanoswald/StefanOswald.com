Night 138

The Cry Decoder

Month 5: Theo Arrives

When Theo cried, Olivia knew why.

She always knew.

The monitor crackled. "Eh. Eh. Eh-eh."

"Hungry," said Olivia.

Mama went up. He was hungry.

After his nap: "Ehhh."

"Hungry," said Olivia.

Papa went up. He was wet.

At suppertime: "Eh-eh-EH."

"Hungry."

He was hungry.

"See?" said Olivia.

"You were right two times," said Mama.

"Free times."

"Two," said Papa.

"Free," said Olivia, and that was that.

Mama wiped her hands.

"We don't always know either," she said. "We go and find out."

The next day, the monitor crackled again.

Olivia opened her mouth to say hungry.

Then she shut it.

She took the rail, and went up the stairs behind Mama

to find out.
