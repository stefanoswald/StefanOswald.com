Night 290

The Last Piece

Month 10: Try Again

Some days one piece went in.

Some days none.

Today only the sky was left.

One piece.

"Mama, look!"

Mama was on the couch, burping Theo.

"In a minute."

Olivia waited.

Mama's minutes are never a minute.

Papa has checked.

Olivia waited some more.

Then she pushed the sky in herself.

Click.

The duck pond was whole.

When Mama came, it was done.

"You missed it," said Olivia.

Her lip went out.

Mama sat down on the rug with Theo.

"I did miss it. I'm sorry."

Olivia looked at her for a long moment.

Then she took the sky piece out

and put it back in.

Click.

"Again," said Mama.

So Olivia did it again, slowly.

This time Mama watched the whole thing.
