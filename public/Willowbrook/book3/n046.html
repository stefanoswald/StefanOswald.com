Night 46

The Bath Runs Deep

Month 2: Me Do It

Bathtime, and Papa turned the tap,

and a small hand went for it too.

"Me turn it."

Papa kept his hand over the taps.

"The taps are mine. Hot lives in there."

Then the storm.

The arch. The stamp. The whole bathroom full of it.

Papa stayed on the floor by the tub,

close, not talking.

"I'm here."

The loud part went on a while.

Then it went to hiccups.

Then to a wet face against his shirt.

"You were so mad. You wanted the taps."

He waited.

"Mad is okay. Taps are still mine."

Then, "The plug is yours."

The plug?

The plug. On its chain, at the end of the bath,

the big gurgle, the water going round and away.

Hers. Every night.

"Boat or duck tonight?"

"Duck."

In she went.

At the end she pulled the plug herself,

and the water went down its hole,

and she leaned over and told it, "Bye."
