Night 169

The First Chase

Month 6: Big Sister School

Shh-shh-shh.

Papa shook the rattle for Theo.

Theo looked past it.

Mama held up the cow.

Ding.

Theo looked past that too.

His eyes were going across the room,

slowly, slowly,

all the way to the couch,

where Olivia was making a nest of cushions.

Olivia went to the window.

Theo's eyes went to the window.

She went to the door.

His eyes went to the door.

"Fee-oh looking me!"

She ducked down behind the couch.

Theo's eyes stayed on the empty spot.

The corners of his mouth went down.

Up she popped. "Here me!"

The corners came back up.

"He's chasing you," said Papa.

"With his eyes," said Mama.

Olivia ran to the kitchen and back,

to the kitchen and back,

and Theo's eyes went with her every time,

until one of them got tired.

It was Olivia.

She lay down on the rug where he could see her.
