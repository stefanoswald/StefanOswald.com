Night 294

Tessa and the Big Rock

Month 10: Try Again

Tessa was still going to bed for the winter.

She was nearly there.

"One more warm first," she told Olivia.

"On the big rock."

The big flat rock by the brook gets the last of the sun.

Freddy can get up it in one jump.

Tessa never has.

One foot up. Rest.

Another foot. Rest.

Then she slid back down, halfway.

Olivia gasped.

Tessa didn't.

Mama poured cocoa from the thermos,

warm, not hot,

and they sat on the log and watched.

Feet again. Rest.

Then the long slow middle,

where nothing seemed to move at all.

"Go, Tessa," Olivia whispered.

And then she was on top,

in the thin gold sun.

"You did it!"

One eye opened in the mud by the bank.

"Took you all afternoon," said Freddy,

and shut it again.

"Slow is also a speed," said Tessa.

The last sun slid off the water.
