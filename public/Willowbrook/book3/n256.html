Night 256

The Fifth Kiss

Month 9: Counting to Five

Bedtime. Olivia was in charge of the kisses now.

"Five," she said. "I say where."

"One." Her forehead.

"Two. Dis cheek."

"Free. Dat cheek."

"Four." Her nose. It squished.

"And five..."

She thought about it for a long time.

"Five for Fee-oh. You take it."

"He's asleep," said Mama.

"On his head."

So Mama carried the fifth kiss down the hall

to the bassinet by the big bed,

where Theo slept on his back with nothing in there but Theo.

She put it on his head, soft, where the hair sticks up.

When she came back to say it was done,

Olivia was asleep,

with Bobby Bear's ear in her fist.
