Night 125

Diaper, Please

Month 5: Theo Arrives

"Olivia, can you get a diaper and bring it to Papa?"

Two jobs. She did both.

Down the hall, and back with a diaper held high.

"Diaper!"

"Thank you," said Papa. "That was fast."

"Burp cloth, please!"

Off again.

Back with a dish towel.

"Dat burp cloth."

Papa looked at the towel. Theo looked at the ceiling.

"It'll do," said Papa, and it did.

Then a very small sock went missing.

Mama looked. Papa looked.

Olivia lay flat on the floor and looked under the couch,

which is where socks go.

"Dere!"

It was grey with dust and had one crumb on it.

"You found it," said Mama. "I never look there."

Olivia put the sock on Theo's foot herself.

Upside down.

It stayed on, mostly.

Theo kicked.

The sock flew under the couch.

Olivia got back down on the floor.
