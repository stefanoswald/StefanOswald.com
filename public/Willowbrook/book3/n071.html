Night 71

Introducing the Gold Dog

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

The evening walk, in the long May light.

The gold dog was at his fence, the way he always is.

Old. Slow. Tail going.

Lily stopped walking.

Lily got behind Rosa's legs.

Mama and Rosa stood right behind the girls.

Olivia went up to the fence the way Papa showed her, many walks ago.

"Watch, Wiwi."

Hand out. Flat. Low.

Then wait.

The gold dog put his nose to her fingers.

Sniff. Tail.

"See? Nice."

Lily looked out from between Rosa's knees.

One step. Another.

Her hand came out in a fist.

"Flat," said Olivia, and showed her again.

Lily's fingers opened.

Low. Slow.

A warm nose. Warm breath.

Lily snatched her hand back and laughed,

and then she put it out again.

The gold dog had done enough for one evening.

He lay down along the fence and sighed.
