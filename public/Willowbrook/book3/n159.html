Night 159

Dinner Show

Month 6: Big Sister School

At supper, Theo lay in his bouncer

by Olivia's chair

and watched her eat.

She held up a noodle.

His eyes went up.

She put it in her mouth.

His eyes went in with it.

"He like noodle."

"He likes watching you," said Mama.

So Olivia did a show.

One pea, very slowly, all the way in.

One noodle on her lip, like a mustache.

"Me Papa," she said.

Papa laughed into his water.

"Fee-oh have pea?"

"Not yet," said Papa. "Milk is his whole dinner for now."

Olivia looked at her peas.

She looked at Theo.

"Me eat his," she said.

She ate them all, one at a time,

for him.
