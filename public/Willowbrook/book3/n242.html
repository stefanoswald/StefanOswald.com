Night 242

Pocket Things

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Mama emptied Olivia's jacket pockets onto the blanket at bedtime.

A flat gray pebble.

"Roar spot!"

She roared, a small bedtime roar.

A red leaf from Lily's street.

"Wiwi window."

"And how far is it?" said Papa.

"Dat close."

An acorn from the farm.

"Gampa dent."

Half a red crayon.

Olivia touched her chest. "Mad here."

"And then?" said Mama.

"Me go corner."

A squashed orange petal.

"Fee-oh cry. Me flower."

"You picked it all by yourself," said Mama.

Nothing in the pockets came from Papa's storm.

"Papa yelled," said Olivia anyway. "Papa sorry."

"I was," said Papa.

One sock. Everybody laughed.

"Socks. Short nap," said Papa, who knew his notebook by heart.

"And Theo?" said Mama.

Olivia lay flat and rolled over, tummy to back.

"Only me see."

Then she did his laugh, and his squeal, the one that's hers.

Everything went on the low shelf, sock and all.
