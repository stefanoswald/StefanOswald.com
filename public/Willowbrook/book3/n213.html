Night 213

The Season of Storms

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

The first big wind of October came at bedtime.

It pushed at the window.

It rattled the gutter.

Whoo-OOO.

Olivia sat straight up. "What dat?"

"That's the wind," said Mama.

"Wind mad?"

"Wind is big tonight. It's shaking the leaves down."

Olivia pulled Bobby Bear up under her chin.

"Me scared."

"It's a scary sound," said Mama. "I'm right beside you."

After a while, they went to the window together,

Mama first, Olivia holding a fistful of cardigan.

Out on the lane, the maple was letting go of its leaves,

whole handfuls, spinning past the streetlight.

"Leaves goed!"

Mama put her hand flat on the glass.

The glass was cold, and it did not move.

Olivia put her hand next to Mama's.

Whoo-OOO, said the wind.

"Whoo," said Olivia, back, much smaller.

In bed, she listened to the wind knock and knock.

Bobby Bear listened for her.
