Night 226

Lily's Moving Day

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Moving day.

Boxes on the porch, boxes on the steps, boxes in the truck.

Olivia and Lily carried the pillow box between them.

It was very light.

They carried it like it was very heavy, with noises.

"That's the last box," said Rosa.

It was not the last box.

It was not even close.

Lily stood at her empty window one more time.

"Bye, old house."

The new house had a blue door and a pumpkin on the step.

Upstairs, Lily's room had a window seat.

Rosa put the window guard up first.

The girls climbed in and looked down at the street.

"Mine," said Lily. "And Livy."

Then Papa and Olivia walked home, hand in hand, the way he promised.

Past the gold dog's fence.

Around one corner.

Crunch, crunch, through the leaves.

Around the other corner,

and there was her own porch.

"Dat close," said Olivia.

"That close," said Papa.

She turned right around to walk it back.
