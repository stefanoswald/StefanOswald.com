Night 47

The Slow Errand

Month 2: Me Do It

Bread and milk. That was the list.

Mama left twice the time, on purpose.

Olivia pushed the small cart.

It goes where it wants. She went with it.

Around the tulips in their bucket by the door.

She chose the bread. The round one.

Mama got the milk down from the high shelf,

and Olivia put it in the cart with both arms.

At the counter, coins.

One. Into the grocer's hand.

Two. Three.

He counted them back into the till, out loud, slow,

the way he does for her,

and he did not hurry, even with a line.

The bread rode home in her arms.

It got a little squashed on the corner

where she stopped to look at a puddle.

Half an hour, that errand.

It used to be ten minutes.

Mama walked behind, looking at the tulips,

in no hurry that anyone could see.
