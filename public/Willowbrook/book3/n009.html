Night 9

Lap for Two

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The neighbor came over with her new baby.

Small as a loaf.

And Mama held it.

Mama held it.

Something squeezed inside Olivia, small and tight.

Her arms crossed.

Her lip came out.

Her feet came over and pushed at Mama's knee.

"My mama," said Olivia.

"Me up."

Mama read her face.

"You want my lap," she said. "Come up. There's room."

There was.

Lap for two, baby on one side, girl on the other.

The squeeze let go, slowly.

Olivia looked at the baby a long time.

"Baby small."

She put one finger on its hand.

"Gentle," said Mama. "Like that."

The baby yawned.

Then Olivia yawned.

Then Mama.
