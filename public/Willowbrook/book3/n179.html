Night 179

What He Gave Her Back

Month 6: Big Sister School

All day, Olivia gave Theo things.

She gave him a song.

He gave her a hiccup.

She gave him a look at Duck.

He gave her a yawn.

She gave him a kiss on his foot.

He gave her a sneeze, right on the nose.

At the end of the day,

Mama sat in the rocking chair

with Theo on her shoulder,

and Olivia sat on the footstool, close.

"Me sing. Him SNEEZE."

Pat, pat, went Mama's hand on his back.

Theo gave everybody a burp.

A big one.

Olivia laughed so hard she slid off the footstool.

From the rug, she looked up.

Theo turned his head

and looked down at her,

and said, "Ahhh."

Just to her.

Olivia lay on the rug and said "ah" back.
