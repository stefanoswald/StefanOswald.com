Night 167

The Quiet Practice

Month 6: Big Sister School

Theo was napping.

Nap time is soft time,

and Olivia was learning soft.

Tiptoe past the door.

Whisper, not talk.

Pots stay in the cupboard.

The pots did not like it.

Neither did Olivia.

She tiptoed, mostly.

She whispered, mostly.

Then she found the big pot

and the wooden spoon.

Just once.

BONG.

Down the hall, Theo woke with a wail.

"OLIVIA!" said Mama, much too loud.

"He JUST went down!"

Olivia dropped the spoon.

Her lip shook.

Mama stood still.

She took a slow breath, and then one more.

Then she knelt down.

"I yelled. I'm sorry. Soft is hard for me too."

Olivia leaned on her.

"I'll get him," said Mama.

"Then we'll find a place for loud."

Later, out in the yard,

the pot went BONG as loud as it liked.

Two porches down, Mateo banged back.
