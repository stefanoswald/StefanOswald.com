Night 130

Teaching Him the Signs

Month 5: Theo Arrives

When Olivia was small, before she had many words,

her hands did the talking.

Fingers tapped together meant more.

Hands waving meant all done.

Theo had no words and no hands that talked.

Olivia decided to fix that.

Mama fed him in the big chair.

Olivia stood right beside it.

"Fee-oh. Look. Dis more." Tap, tap.

Theo drank. His eyes were shut.

"More. Dis one." Tap, tap, tap.

Theo drank.

"He not looking."

"He's busy," said Mama.

Olivia tapped her fingers right by his ear.

Theo drank.

At last he stopped, with a milky mouth.

Mama put him on her shoulder and patted.

Theo burped, and his little fists wobbled in the air.

"All done!" said Olivia. "He say all done!"

Mama looked at the fists.

"I'll count it," she said.

Olivia waved all done back at him, to be polite.
