Night 205

Not Everybody's Ready

Month 7: Potty Power

At the park, near the bench where Mama and Rosa sat,

there was a little boy

from the next blanket,

smaller than Lily, in a diaper,

digging.

A big boy of four came by and pointed.

"Diaper! Dat's for babies."

The little boy stopped digging.

His face went red.

Olivia stood up.

"Me diaper," she said. "At night."

The big boy looked at her.

"Me too," said Lily. "Night."

The big boy thought about that.

Then he shrugged and ran off to the slide.

The little boy looked at Olivia for a long time.

Then he went back to digging,

right next to her.

After a while he leaned on her shoulder,

sandy and warm,

and stayed there.
