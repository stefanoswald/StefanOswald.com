Night 83

Two Families, One Table

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

On Friday the kitchen smelled of red sauce again.

On purpose, this time.

Rosa brought the bread.

Mama made the sauce.

Papa did the noodles, and the singing.

"Row, row, row your boat, gently down the sauce."

"No!" said Olivia. "Stream!"

"Merrily, merrily, merrily, merrily, life is but a meatball."

"NO!" said Lily.

The table was loud.

Two girls knelt up on their seats to reach.

One noodle went on the floor, and the cat took it away.

"Remember the carts?" said Rosa.

"Wiwi game!" said Olivia.

"Me game," said Lily, very pleased.

The grown-ups laughed as if it were new.

Then Rosa said, "Every first Friday?"

"Every first Friday," said Mama.

After, Papa washed the big pot.

Olivia dried one spoon.

She dried it very well.
