Night 229

The Choice Umbrella

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

"Red boots or frog boots?" said Mama.

It was raining, and Olivia's face had been getting ready for a storm.

She looked at both.

"Frog."

"Your umbrella or Papa's big one?"

"Big one."

Under Papa's big umbrella there was a lot of Papa

and a little bit of Olivia.

At the park, it was time to go.

"Hop to the gate or stomp to the gate?" said Papa.

Olivia stomped,

every puddle, in the frog boots, all the way.

At supper, the squash was not a choice.

"Big spoon or little spoon?"

"Big."

At bedtime, Papa held up two books.

"The duck one or the moon one?"

Olivia looked at them.

"Both."

"Both isn't one of the choices," said Papa.

"Both," said Olivia.

Papa read both.
