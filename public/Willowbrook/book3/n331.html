Night 331

Kindness to the House

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Snow came in on everybody's boots.

By supper there were puddles all down the hall.

"Me wipe it."

Olivia got the old towel.

Wipe, wipe, wipe.

Puddle gone.

She lined up the boots on the mat.

Papa's big ones.

Mama's.

Hers.

Theo's tiny ones, which have never touched the ground.

Then the toys went home.

Blocks in the basket.

Duck in her chair.

Olivia shut the closet door with two hands,

slow, so it didn't bang.

"Why so slow?" said Mama.

"Rule two. No banging.

House sleeping."

Mama looked at the house.

Then she shut the kitchen drawer without a sound.

At bedtime Olivia patted the wall by her bed.

"Night, house."

The radiator ticked.
