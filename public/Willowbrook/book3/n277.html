Night 277

The Red Tricycle

Month 10: Try Again

Mateo came up the sidewalk pushing a red tricycle.

It had a silver bell.

"Too little for me now," he said. "You have it."

Olivia rang the bell.

Bring-bring!

She loved the bell right away.

Papa brought a small red helmet.

She loved that less.

He buckled it on anyway.

The pedals were another thing.

She pushed down with one foot.

The trike rolled backward.

She pushed with the other.

It rocked, and stayed.

"Round," said Mateo.

He took her boots in his hands

and moved them for her.

Up, over, down.

Mateo is almost four. He knows about pedals.

The wheels went half a turn.

"I moved!"

Papa clapped from the porch steps.

Olivia rang the bell for him.

Bring-bring!

By lunch she had rolled from one crack in the sidewalk

to the next one.

Papa parked it in the hall.
