Night 119

The Phone Call

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Dawn in Rosa's kitchen. The phone lay on the table on speaker, and out of it came Papa's voice, tired and shining.

"Olivia? You there?"

"Papa! Baby here?"

"He's here. A little brother."

Lily bounced. "I help!"

Rosa cried into a dish towel.

"What name?"

"Theo," said Papa. "His name is Theo."

Olivia tried it. "Fee-oh."

"Close enough," said Papa. "I'm coming to get you."

"No trick?"

"No trick."

Rosa sat down beside her. "What will you tell him?"

Olivia thought. "Chain all done."

"No more tearing," said Rosa.

"Two brights."

"One for you, one for him," said Rosa. She knew all about the candles.

Olivia picked up Rabbit, one hand under his head. "Hold head. Like dis."

"He knows your voice already," said Rosa. "All that reading."

The worry had come back more than once in these weeks, and each time somebody held her. The next just-us day was already promised.

Rosa buttered toast for the car.
