Night 170

Sprinkler Day

Month 6: Big Sister School

Today was supposed to be the pool.

Olivia had worn her swimsuit since breakfast.

Then the sky went dark,

and the thunder came,

and the pool closed.

Olivia stood at the window in her swimsuit.

Her face crumpled all the way.

"POOL!"

Mama sat down by her.

"You wanted the pool so much. And the thunder came."

Olivia cried against the glass.

Mama stayed.

The rain came down hard,

and then it slowed.

"Maybe," said Mama.

"Maybe what?"

"Maybe the day isn't done yet."

By four o'clock the sun was back.

Papa came out whistling, very badly,

with the sprinkler.

Tsst. Tsst. Tsst.

Olivia ran through it.

She jumped it with both feet.

"Look at me!"

Theo watched from the porch shade in Mama's arms.

"Dis pool," said Olivia, dripping.

The grass stayed wet until supper.
