Night 68

Sharing Is Hard

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

The red wagon lives in the hall.

It is Olivia's.

Today Lily had the handle,

and then Olivia had the handle too.

Four hands on one handle.

"Mine!"

"Me turn!"

Pull. Pull back. Two faces going pink.

Mama came and sat on the floor beside the wagon.

She did not take the handle.

"One handle and two pullers. That's hard."

Lily's lip went out. Olivia's went out further.

"Lily pulls first, one lap round the table," said Mama.

"Olivia rides. Then we switch."

Olivia climbed in.

Lily pulled. It was heavy.

She pulled anyway, round the table legs, past the cat, back to Mama.

"Switch."

Olivia pulled. Lily rode and held both sides.

"Switch."

By the fourth lap the girls were saying it too,

a beat behind Mama.

"Witch!"

The wagon went round and round.

Mama stayed on the floor, keeping the laps.
