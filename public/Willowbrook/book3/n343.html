Night 343

The Party List

Month 12: Almost Three

"Who comes to the party?" said Mama.

She had a pencil and a long paper.

Olivia had her fingers.

"Wiwi."

Mama wrote LILY.

"June. Mateo. Gampa."

"The aunts?"

"Da aunts. Rosa, for cake."

"Mrs. Henderson?"

"Yes. And da gold dog."

Papa laughed out loud.

Olivia's face went tight.

"Not funny. Him my friend."

Papa stopped laughing.

"I laughed, and you meant it. I'm sorry."

He thought.

"The dog can't come inside. He could get a wave at the fence."

"And a cake crumb."

"And a crumb."

Mama wrote GOLD DOG, WAVE.

"Fee-oh," said Olivia.

"Theo lives here, love."

"Still come."

Mama wrote THEO.

"What do we eat?"

Olivia took a big breath.

"Spa. Ghet. Ti."

Every piece in the right place.

Papa clapped.

Mama stuck the list on the fridge,

right next to the word list.
