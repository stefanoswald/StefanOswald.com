Night 248

Five Ducks Exactly

Month 9: Counting to Five

The duck pond, in the cold.

The ducks were puffed up round, ready for winter.

Olivia pointed at each one.

"One. Two. Free."

Mama pointed at the rest. "Four. Five."

Five ducks. Exactly.

Then the last duck tipped up.

Head under. Tail up. Feet paddling at the sky.

And then he was all the way under.

"Four," said Olivia. Her chin went wobbly.

"Where duck go?"

"Under," said Mama. "You're sad he went."

They watched the flat gray water.

Olivia held on to Mama's sleeve.

"Gone?"

"Maybe," said Mama. "Let's watch."

Bloop.

Up he came, with a bit of green weed on his head.

"FIVE!"

The duck shook his head. The weed stayed on.

Olivia waved to him all the way to the gate.

His name was Five now.
