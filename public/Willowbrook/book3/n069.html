Night 69

The Same and Different Game

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Snack, at the little table.

Lily ate a berry.

"Me too," said Olivia, and ate a berry.

Lily ate another.

"Me too."

Nobody started the game. It was just there.

Mama put out mushrooms, sliced thin.

Lily ate one.

Olivia studied hers.

"No fank you."

Lily ate that one too.

In the next room, Papa switched on the vacuum.

Both girls were on one chair in one second,

four hands over four ears.

"Too loud!"

It stopped. They climbed down.

Out on the lane, a big dog barked.

Olivia ran to the window. "Doggie!"

Lily did not run anywhere.

"No like doggie."

Olivia looked at Lily.

She looked out at the lane.

"Gold dog nice," she said. "Me show you."

There were two berries left.

One each.

Two chins, both purple.
