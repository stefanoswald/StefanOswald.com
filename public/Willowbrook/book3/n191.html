Night 191

The Rainy Week

Month 7: Potty Power

It rained all week.

It rained on the swings and on the lane

and on Mrs. Henderson's green tomatoes.

Inside, it rained too.

Wet pants on Monday.

Wet pants on Tuesday.

On Wednesday, wet pants in the middle of lunch.

Mama sighed.

A big one, out loud.

Olivia's face went small.

Mama stopped.

She took one slow breath, for herself.

Then she sat down on the floor beside her.

"That sigh was my tired, not you," she said. "I'm sorry."

Olivia leaned on her.

All week the house had been full of visitors,

cooing at Theo.

That night Mama and Papa looked at each other

over Olivia's head.

On Thursday Papa kept Theo,

and Mama and Olivia took the big umbrella

down Maple Lane,

into every puddle.

"Just us," said Olivia, under the umbrella.

Nobody said potty all day.

On Saturday the sun came out.

Two pairs of boots dried by the door.
