Night 347

The Whistle

Month 12: Almost Three

It happened on a Tuesday, over cocoa.

The cocoa was warm, not hot,

but Olivia blew on it anyway, the way Papa does.

Her lips went round.

Out came a whistle.

A real one.

Thin and wobbly, one note,

and not one drop of spit.

Olivia stopped.

She blew again.

Wheeee.

"MAMA! My whistle come!"

Mama came fast, dish towel and all.

Wheeee, went Olivia.

Mama sat right down on the kitchen floor.

"Since the lilacs," she said. "And there it is."

Papa heard it on the phone.

Theo heard it up close, and said "Pbbbt" back.

On Thursday, at the window seat,

Olivia whistled for Lily.

Lily whistled back.

"Me here," said Lily.

"Me here," said Olivia.

One whistle, and one back.

That was theirs now.

Papa tried it at supper.

Out came air.
