Night 298

The Banana Face

Month 10: Try Again

Theo was almost six months old.

Tonight he sat in the high chair for the first time,

strapped in, with Mama right beside him.

In the bowl, banana,

mashed as soft as pudding.

Olivia had the seat across.

When the spoon came close,

she opened her mouth wide to show him.

"Ahhh."

Theo opened his mouth too.

In went the banana.

Then out it came again,

pushed by his tongue, down his chin.

And his face!

Eyes wide. Mouth square. Nose up.

Olivia laughed till she got the hiccups.

"He not like it."

"It's new," said Mama. "New is strange at first."

Mama tried again.

A little stayed in.

Theo smacked his lips, thinking hard.

Olivia held out a blueberry from her plate.

"Not that," said Mama. "Fee-oh's food is all mush."

"Mush," said Olivia, very serious.

Theo opened his mouth for more.

Most of it went on his bib.
