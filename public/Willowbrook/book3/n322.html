Night 322

Grandpa's Invisible List

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Sunday, before chores,

Grandpa put Olivia's car seat in the old truck's back seat,

buckled her in, coat off,

and tucked the coat over her like a blanket.

First, the library.

The bird feeder by the door was empty.

Grandpa filled it, one big scoop.

"Hungry," he said.

The chickadees came before they were back in the truck.

Then the library steps, all ice.

Grandpa shook sand on them from a coffee can.

Olivia shook too.

Shh, shh, shh.

Then a little yellow house.

Grandpa set a carton of eggs by the door.

He didn't knock.

"Why no knock?"

Grandpa winked.

"How you know where?"

Grandpa tapped his hat.

Driving away, they saw a lady come out

and find the eggs.

She looked up the street and down the street.

Grandpa kept both hands on the wheel

and his eyes on the road.

Olivia ducked her head.

At the farm, Grandpa tapped his hat again.

Olivia tapped hers.
