Night 328

The Wave Exchange

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

It was too cold to go out,

so Olivia waved from the front window.

Mrs. Henderson came out for her mail.

Olivia waved.

Mrs. Henderson waved back, mail and all.

The mail carrier.

Wave.

He waved a whole bundle of letters.

Then the plow came down the lane,

its orange light going round and round.

Olivia waved with both arms.

The driver lifted one hand off the big wheel.

"Him WAVED!"

Mama watched the plow all the way to the corner.

A man went by with his hood up and his head down.

Olivia waved.

And waved.

He didn't see.

"Him no wave."

"He didn't see you," said Mama.

Olivia waved at his back till he was gone.

On Mama's lap, Theo patted the glass with both hands.

Bap, bap.

Mama said that counted.

A crow landed on the snowbank.

Olivia waved at the crow.

The crow walked away, very busy.
