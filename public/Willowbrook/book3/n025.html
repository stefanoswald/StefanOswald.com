Night 25

The Impatient Wiggle

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The cookies are out of the oven.

They are right there.

They are too hot.

"In a minute," says Mama.

It is never a minute. Papa says so.

Here comes the wiggly one.

It hops on one foot.

"Now?"

"Not yet."

It sighs like a whale.

"Nowww?"

"Almost."

It lies down on the kitchen floor.

"NOW?"

"You're waiting," says Mama. "Waiting is hard."

Then a job.

"Help me wash the bowl?"

They wash. They dry. They put away.

The waiting is still there.

It is just quieter, with wet hands.

Ding.

The first cookie is warm in the middle.

Olivia eats it in small bites, sitting on the floor.
