Night 55

The No-Thank-You Try

Month 2: Me Do It

Tonight, mushrooms.

On the plate next to the noodles,

brown and shiny.

The old way was the plate shoved

and a "No!" the whole street could hear.

This week there are new words.

Mama has been saying them at everything.

"No thank you," to the cat, who was on the table.

"No thank you," to Papa's second helping.

"No thank you," to the rain.

Olivia looked at the mushrooms.

Her hand went toward the plate, flat, ready to push.

Then it stopped.

"No fank you."

Mama nodded.

The mushrooms moved to Mama's plate.

No coaxing. No one more bite.

That is the deal.

No thank you means no thank you.

Olivia ate her noodles.

She watched Mama eat the mushrooms.

She watched very closely.

"Dat good?"

"Pretty good."

They will come back another night, the mushrooms.

Mama ate the last one slowly,

and Olivia watched it go.
