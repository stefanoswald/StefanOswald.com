Night 310

Kind to Theo When He's Terrible

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Olivia built a tower on the rug.

Six blocks, the red one on top.

Theo sat beside her,

no cushion needed now,

round and wobbly and very interested.

He reached out one hand.

Swat.

Blocks everywhere.

"NO, Fee-oh!"

She built it again.

He swatted it again,

and laughed his big laugh.

"FEE-OH!"

Olivia stamped both feet and wailed.

Mama came and sat down on the rug, close.

When the wail was small,

Mama said, "He knocked it down twice. You're so mad."

"Him bad!"

"He's little. He thinks it's a game."

Theo looked at the blocks, hopeful.

"What could we do?" said Mama.

Olivia thought.

She built a tall tower up on the low table,

out of reach.

Then a little one on the rug.

Three blocks.

"Dis one yours, Fee-oh."

Swat.

Theo laughed.

Olivia built it again.

The tall one stood all afternoon.
