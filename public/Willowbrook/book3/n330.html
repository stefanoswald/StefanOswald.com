Night 330

The Kindness Jar Fills

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

All month a jar stood on the kitchen counter.

When somebody did a kind thing,

Mama wrote it on a slip of paper,

and Olivia drew a circle on it

and dropped it in.

Tonight the jar was full.

"Count dem!" said Olivia.

"Me got most!"

"It's not a counting jar," said Mama.

"What kind?"

"A remembering kind."

Mama took out a slip and read it.

"Theo gave Olivia his spoon."

"Dat spoon wet!"

Olivia took one out.

She can't read yet.

"Dis one say... snowman."

Mama peeked.

"It says Papa was kind to the phone man."

"Dat too," said Olivia.

Papa took one.

"Olivia sat on the bench with Lily."

Olivia nodded.

"My hat itchy."

The slips went back in.

The lid went back on.

On the sill, the bean had two new leaves.
