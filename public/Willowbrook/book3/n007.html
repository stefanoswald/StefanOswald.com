Night 7

Excited Can't Sit Down

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Farm day.

Grandpa day. Hen day.

Olivia woke up fizzy.

Fizzy cannot sit down.

It bounces on its toes.

It runs to the window. Again.

It puts its own jacket on, backwards,

an hour before anybody's boots.

"Now?"

"Not yet," says Mama.

"Now?"

"Soon."

"You're excited," says Mama. "Excited makes the clock slow."

So she gives it a job.

"Pack apples for Grandpa?"

Olivia packs.

"One. Two. Lots."

She zips the bag.

She carries it to the door.

She stands guard on the mat.

Outside, mud, and the brook loud with thaw.

At last, keys.

The jacket is still backwards.

It goes to the farm that way.
