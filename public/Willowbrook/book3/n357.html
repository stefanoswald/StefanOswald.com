Night 357

The Party Eve

Month 12: Almost Three

A banner hung over the kitchen door.

Rosa's cake sat under a cloth on the high counter.

It was the night before the birthday.

"No peeking," said Papa.

Olivia pushed her step from the sink to the counter.

She climbed up and reached for the cloth.

"OLIVIA! NO!"

Mama's voice was very big.

Olivia froze, and her face crumpled.

Theo started crying too.

Mama lifted her down.

Then Mama took one slow breath, in and out.

She got down low.

"That was too loud, and I'm sorry. I got scared you would fall."

Olivia cried into her cardigan.

Mama waited.

"You wanted to see it so much."

"So much."

"The step stays at the sink. The cake waits for tomorrow."

Olivia sniffed.

"Rule free," she said. "Cake stay up."

"Rule three," said Papa, bouncing Theo, and wrote it on the fridge.

At bedtime, the kitchen was dark.

The cake sat under its cloth, waiting.
