Night 65

The Spaghetti Night

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

They came at six.

Rosa carried the apples and beans.

Lily carried a fork, already in her fist.

The kitchen got loud the good way.

Water rolling. Bread in the oven.

Papa whistling into the steam, which is not the same as whistling a song.

"Dat not song," said Olivia.

"It's a spaghetti song," said Papa.

Then the table, and on the table, a mountain.

Two girls side by side.

Slurping is allowed on spaghetti night.

Nobody made that rule. By the second bite it was one.

Sauce on chins. Sauce on cheeks. Sauce on one eyebrow, somehow.

"Wiwi long one!"

"Livy short one!"

Papa fished out the two longest noodles in the pot.

Zzzzip. Zzzzip.

The grown-ups stayed with their elbows on the table.

The girls went under it with a blanket.

By eight the blanket was quiet.

"Some Tuesday," said Rosa at the door.

Lily went home over her shoulder,

still holding the fork.
