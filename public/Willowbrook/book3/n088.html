Night 88

Telling Everyone

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

By Sunday, the whole lane knew.

She told the bench. "Baby in Mama!"

She told the bakery. "Baby in Mama!"

At the farm, Grandpa Joe was in the barn doorway.

"Gampa! Baby!"

Grandpa took off his straw hat.

He looked at her for a while.

He put the hat back on.

"Well now."

He turned two buckets upside down.

He sat on one.

He patted the other.

She sat.

In the straw by the wall sat Henrietta, on her nest,

fluffed flat, not going anywhere.

Olivia reached out.

Grandpa's big hand came over hers, slow,

and put it back in her lap.

He shook his head, a little.

So they looked.

"Egg?"

Grandpa nodded.

"Chick come?"

He nodded again.

He held his hands flat and still on his knees.

"Waiting," he said.

Olivia put her hands flat on her knees too.

The swallows went in and out of the barn.

Henrietta blinked.

Nobody said anything else.
