Night 360

The Constellation Cake

Month 12: Almost Three

There it was.

One gold star in the middle, like every year,

and all around it, little stars,

so many they bumped into each other.

One for everybody in the room.

Rosa had counted.

Three candles, lit.

Everybody sang.

The aunts sang in two parts.

Theo sang in no parts, loudly.

Olivia looked around the table while they sang,

at every face, one at a time.

She was in no hurry at all.

Then she took the biggest breath of her life.

Whoooo.

All three went out.

"What did you wish?" said Papa.

"Secret."

She told Bobby Bear later.

It had Theo in it.

Rosa, flour on her cheek even today,

cut the gold star piece for Olivia.

She ate the star first.
