Night 168

The Bell on the Arch

Month 6: Big Sister School

Papa had set up the play gym:

an arch over the mat,

with a felt moon, a crinkle star,

and a little bell, dangling.

Theo lay under it.

His arms waved.

His fists went up and down, up and down,

the way new arms do, going nowhere in particular.

Ding.

Everybody froze.

Olivia's mouth fell open.

"Fee-oh RING it!"

"By accident, I think," said Mama.

"Again, Fee-oh!"

Theo's arms waved.

Up. Down. Nowhere.

No ding.

"AGAIN!"

Theo looked at the felt moon.

Olivia lay down beside him and waited.

She waited a long time, for her.

Nothing.

So she reached up and rang it herself.

Ding.

Theo turned his whole head to look.

"Dat da bell," she told him.

She rang it for him until lunch.
