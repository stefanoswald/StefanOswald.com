Night 53

The Table Setter

Month 2: Me Do It

A new job at dinner.

Table setter.

Napkins first. One at every seat.

Some flat, some folded into wads,

because wads are a way to fold.

Spoons next. Everyone gets one.

Papa's spoon goes backward, every night.

He does not fix it.

He says that is how they do it in fancy places.

Her cup, the little one, at her spot.

She moves it, and moves it back,

until it is right.

Then the best part.

"Dinner ready!"

Down the hall, as loud as she likes.

It is the one time of day when loud is the job.

Papa comes and finds his spoon.

He turns it round. He turns it back, her way.

He sits.

There is no sticker for this.

There is soup.

It comes to a table she got ready,

and Papa eats his from the wrong side of the spoon

and does fine.
