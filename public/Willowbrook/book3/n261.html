Night 261

The High Five

Month 9: Counting to Five

Papa taught her the high five.

"Hand up. Flat. Eyes on my hand."

Smack.

She liked the sound.

She high-fived Papa, the fridge, the cat, who left,

and Papa again.

"Now Fee-oh."

She held her flat hand over Theo on the rug.

"Hand up, Fee-oh. Flat."

Theo reached up.

He grabbed her fingers

and pulled them down into his mouth.

"No! Not eat it!"

Theo laughed with her fingers in.

"That's a baby high five," said Papa.

"Wet," said Olivia.

She tried it again, slower,

low down, where he could get it.

He grabbed. He chewed.

She let him.

Later, when Theo was asleep in his bassinet,

Papa held up his hand in the hall.

Olivia gave it the smallest smack there is.
