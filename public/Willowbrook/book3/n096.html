Night 96

Practicing on Baba

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Today Bobby Bear was the baby. He was not consulted.

Mama showed the holding first.

"Babies' heads are sleepy. We hold under the head."

"Always?"

"Always."

Olivia held Bobby Bear with one hand under his head. His ear flopped anyway. Ears do what they like.

"Like dis?"

"Just like that."

Then the rocking. Sway, sway, shhh.

Then the small voice. "Hi baby. Me big sister."

Then the diaper, which was a washcloth, which went on his head.

Mama made a small noise into her hand.

"Oops. Wrong end."

She tried again. This time it went on the right end, mostly.

Then Bobby Bear rode in the doll stroller, buckled in, because you always buckle. Then he napped on his back, like a real baby.

When Papa came home and dropped his keys, she met him in the hall.

"Shhh. Baby sleeping."

Papa tiptoed for the rest of the evening.
