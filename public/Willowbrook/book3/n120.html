Night 120

The Town Hears First-ish

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Very early. The sky over Willowbrook was going pale at the edges.

The brook heard first. The brook usually does.

The brook told the willow, and the willow's long branches stirred, though there was no wind.

High in the Great Willow, Oliver Owl opened one eye.

Stella hung low over a certain bright window across town. She had been up all night.

"A boy," said Stella. "Theo."

"Who?" said Oliver Owl, who had heard perfectly well and wanted to hear it again.

"Theo."

Down on Maple Lane a porch light went off. At the bakery a light went on.

The Moon, pale in the morning sky, said nothing at all.

And the Sleepy Moon hummed.
