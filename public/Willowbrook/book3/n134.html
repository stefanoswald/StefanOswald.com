Night 134

The Aunts Arrive for Theo

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Two weeks after Theo came, the aunts came too,

all the way from up north.

"Aunties!"

"Wash hands. Den baby."

The aunts washed. The aunts always do what Olivia says.

Out came the knitting.

A tiny cotton hat for Theo, with a star on it.

It fit. They had guessed his head over the phone.

Then a parcel for Olivia.

"Dis me?"

Inside was a knitted doll with dark curls and a red bow.

"A baby of your own," said the aunts, "while Mama has hers."

Olivia held the doll the way you hold a baby, head first.

"What's her name?"

Olivia did not have to think.

"Duck."

The aunts looked at each other.

"Duck," said one.

"Duck," said the other.

"DUCK," said Olivia.

So the doll was Duck.

That night Duck got a clean diaper, a bottle, and a long burp.

Then Duck went to sleep in the big bed, next to Baba.
