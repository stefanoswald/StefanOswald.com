Night 231

The Apology Flower

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

"Park!" said Olivia, with her boots already on.

But it got dark so early now.

"Too dark now," said Mama. "Tomorrow morning."

The storm came fast and loud,

right beside the play mat.

Theo flung out both arms, startled.

His face crumpled,

and he cried a thin, scared cry.

Olivia stopped yelling.

Mama picked him up, and he quieted on her shoulder.

Olivia stood still, looking at him.

"Fee-oh cry."

"The loud scared him," said Mama. "He's all right now."

Olivia thought.

Then she went to the back door and pointed.

Mama opened it.

In the pot by the steps was the last orange marigold.

Olivia picked it and knelt by the mat, where Theo lay again.

"Sorry, Fee-oh. Dis you."

Theo reached for it.

"Looking, not tasting," said Mama,

and held it where he could see.

Theo looked at the flower.

Then he looked past it, at her,

and smiled with his whole face.
