Night 112

The Waiting Games

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Any day now, everybody said. Any day turned out to be very long.

So in the mornings, while Mama rested, Papa took Olivia to the park.

Olivia had a new trick. She stood on the bottom step of the slide, bent her knees, and jumped off with both feet.

"Papa! Look at me!"

Papa was looking at his phone, in case of news.

"Mm-hm."

She jumped again. Both feet. Thud.

"Look at ME!"

"Mm-hm."

She marched over and pushed the phone down with one finger.

Papa looked at her. Then he put the phone in his pocket and got down low.

"I missed it. I'm sorry."

"You miss it."

"I did. Show me again?"

She showed him. Knees, jump, both feet. Thud.

"Both feet," said Papa. "And you stuck the landing."

She did it again. And again. And again, until the ducks lost interest.

She rode home on his shoulders.
