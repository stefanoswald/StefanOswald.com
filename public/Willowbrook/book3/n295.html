Night 295

The Nose Blow

Month 10: Try Again

Olivia had a cold.

Her nose ran and ran.

"Blow," said Mama, holding the tissue.

Olivia sniffed.

In, not out. Every time.

So Mama put a small feather on the table.

"Mouth closed. Make it move with your nose."

Olivia sniffed.

The feather flew up and stuck to her nose.

She sneezed.

The feather sailed off the table.

"Dat count?"

"That was a sneeze," said Mama.

They tried again.

Mouth closed.

Air down, and out the nose.

The feather trembled.

Then it slid, all the way across the table.

"Nose wind!"

Then the tissue.

Out, both sides, the real thing.

Something came out.

"Ew!" said Olivia, delighted.

She ran to show Papa.

"Please don't," said Papa.

The feather stayed out, in case.
