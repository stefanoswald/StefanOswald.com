Night 354

The Town Delivers

Month 12: Almost Three

All week, the town came to the door.

On Monday, Grandpa Joe brought eggs.

"Back in my day," he said, "one egg made a cake for the whole county."

On Wednesday, Rosa brought a crate of noodles.

On Thursday, June and her mama brought red balloons.

"She said red is yours," said June's mama.

On Friday, Olivia looked out the window.

Mrs. Henderson was sweeping the slush off their front walk.

Nobody had asked her.

She swept slowly, bent over the broom.

Olivia watched for a long time.

"Her back hurt?"

"It's hard work in the cold," said Mama.

Olivia got her crayons.

She drew Mrs. Henderson and the broom,

and a big yellow sun over both of them, to warm her up.

Papa walked her across the street.

"Fank you. For da sweeping."

Mrs. Henderson held the drawing in both hands.

Her whole face went soft.
