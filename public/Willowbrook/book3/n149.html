Night 149

Hunting the First Smile

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Papa said the sleepy smiles were wind.

So Olivia went hunting for an awake one,

just for her.

Every day she tried.

Puffed cheeks, like Papa's.

Theo stared.

A kiss on his belly. Pbbbt.

Theo stared harder.

A sock on her hand, talking.

"Hewwo, Fee-oh."

Theo looked at the sock, and then at her.

"Not yet," said Mama. "He's still very new."

"When?"

"When he's ready. You'll see it."

So Olivia got out the big one,

the face she had been saving for him.

She squeezed her eyes shut.

She stuck her tongue out.

She took hold of both ears.

Then she opened her eyes to see.

Theo was asleep.

Olivia let go of her ears.

She would save that face a little longer.
