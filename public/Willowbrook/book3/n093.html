Night 93

The Kick

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Evening, on the couch. Mama sat up very straight.

"Quick! Hand here!"

Olivia put her hand flat on the round tummy.

Nothing.

Nothing.

Thump.

She snatched her hand back and stared at it.

"Baby kick me!"

"Near you," said Mama. "Possibly at you."

Hand back on. Waiting. Waiting.

Thump.

"Again!"

After that every thump got a squeal, and every squeal seemed to get another thump, as if somebody in there liked the noise.

"Baby dancing."

"Something like that," said Mama.

At bedtime Olivia gave the tummy two small pats, like the pats Mama gives her back. Nothing came back. The baby was asleep, or pretending.

She kept her hand there anyway.
