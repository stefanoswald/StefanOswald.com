Night 189

Theo Watches

Month 7: Potty Power

Olivia had an audience now.

Mama set Theo's bouncer in the bathroom doorway,

and Theo watched.

His head didn't wobble now.

It stayed right up, pointed at her.

"Fee-oh. Look."

She sat on the little potty.

"Dis my potty."

Theo looked.

"Sit. Wait."

She waited.

Theo waited.

Nothing happened.

"Nofing, Fee-oh."

Theo batted the ring on his bouncer bar.

Clack.

Then the sink.

Olivia climbed onto her step.

"Soap. Bubbles. Scrub."

She held up her hands to show him.

Theo's mouth made an O.

Out came a coo, and a lot of drool.

"You too," Olivia told him. "When big."

"Much bigger," said Mama.

By the towel part, Theo's eyes were closing,

and Mama carried him off to his bassinet,

fists and all.
