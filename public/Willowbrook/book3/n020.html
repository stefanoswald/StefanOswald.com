Night 20

The Grumpy Morning

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Some mornings arrive wrong.

Olivia woke up grumpy.

Scowl at the ceiling.

Scowl at the stairs.

Scowl at breakfast, even the good breakfast.

Even the red cup got one.

"Somebody's grumpy," said Mama.

"No!"

The scowl went on.

Mama had not slept much either.

"Enough," she said, too loud. "Eat."

Then she heard herself.

She came round the table and got low.

"That was my grumpy," she said. "Sorry."

"Grumpy," said Olivia, and scowled at her.

"Two grumpies," said Mama. "Let's go slow today."

So the day went slow.

Quiet games. An early snack. Low lights.

Grumpy is mostly tired, wearing a costume.

By lunch the costume slipped.

By nap it was off.

"Hi, Mama," she said, waking.

"Hi, you."

The red cup was a good cup again.
