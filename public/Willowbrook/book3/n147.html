Night 147

The Fort Gets Its Resident

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Thursday, and too hot for the park.

So Olivia and Lily built a fort

out of the couch cushions and the big blanket.

Then Olivia made an announcement.

"Fee-oh come in."

"Baby in fort!" said Lily.

"Babies come in with a grown-up," said Mama.

"So I'm coming too."

Mama got down on her knees with Theo in her arms.

The fort was not built for Mama.

The roof came down on her head.

The girls held it up with both hands.

"Mama too big!"

"Mama is exactly fort size," said Mama, from under the blanket.

Inside, the lamp shone gold through the weave.

Theo blinked up at it.

"He like it," said Lily.

"He LOVE it," said Olivia.

"Dis fort Fee-oh's too. Us free."

"And Mama," said Lily.

Olivia thought about it.

"Mama visiting."

Mama stayed until both of her feet fell asleep.
