Night 227

The Body Map of Mad

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

It rained all afternoon, too wet for the roar spot.

Mama rolled a long sheet of paper across the floor.

"Lie down on it."

Olivia lay down.

Mama drew all the way around her.

It tickled at the elbows. It tickled at the knees.

Olivia got up and looked.

"Dat ME!"

"Where does mad go?" said Mama.

Olivia took the red crayon and scribbled on the chest, hard.

"Mad here."

"And sad?"

Blue, on the eyes. Drip, drip, down the cheeks.

"And scared?"

She thought about it.

A little purple on the tummy.

"Wobbly."

"And happy?"

Olivia took the yellow.

She colored the arms, the legs, the feet twice,

then the paper next to her,

and then a bit of the floor.

"Happy BIG."

Mama looked at the floor for a moment.

She let it go.

The paper Olivia went up on the wall by the cozy corner,

yellow feet and all.
