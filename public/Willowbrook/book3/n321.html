Night 321

The Thank-You Round

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Something new at supper:

one thank-you each,

to somebody at the table.

"Thank you, Mama, for the soup," said Papa.

"Thank you, Olivia, for the napkins," said Mama.

Olivia's spoon stopped in the air.

"Fank you, Mama. For da crust."

Papa laughed.

Olivia's face went hot.

"No laughing! Me mean it!"

Papa put his spoon down.

"You meant it. I'm sorry I laughed."

He looked at her with his whole face.

"Say it again?"

"Fank you, Mama. For da crust."

"You're welcome," said Mama.

"It's my favorite part too."

Olivia looked at her crust.

She broke it in two

and gave Mama the bigger piece.

Then it was Theo's turn.

"Da-da-da-da!"

"He thanked me," said Papa. "By name."

"Dat just baby talk," said Olivia.

Theo banged his spoon.
