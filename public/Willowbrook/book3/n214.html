Night 214

The Wrong Banana

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Mama broke the banana in half,

one piece for Olivia and one for Mama.

Snap.

Olivia looked at her half.

Her face changed.

"No! Put back!"

Mama held the two halves together.

They did not go back.

A banana in Willowbrook, once broken, stays broken.

Everyone has tried.

The storm came all at once:

Olivia on the floor, heels drumming on the tiles.

Mama pulled a chair close and sat,

the halves on a plate on her knee.

Nobody fixed anything.

Nobody laughed, though one corner of Mama's mouth wanted to.

The loud part went on, and on,

and then it got wet and small,

and then Olivia was climbing into her lap.

"You wanted it whole," said Mama.

"Whole," said Olivia, hiccuping.

"And it broke," said Mama.

Olivia looked at the plate a long time.

Then she ate both halves, on Mama's lap.
