Night 23

The Sorry Feeling

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

It happened fast.

Olivia swung the bucket, whee,

and it bonked Lily on the arm.

Lily cried.

Olivia stood very still.

A new feeling sank into her middle,

heavy and squirmy, with hot cheeks.

No one put it there.

It came by itself.

Mama got low, between them.

"You didn't mean it," she said. "And her arm hurts."

Olivia's lip wobbled.

"Both are true," said Mama.

"That's the sorry feeling. What helps?"

Olivia knew.

She patted the arm. Gentle.

"Sorry, Wiwi."

Then her own idea.

She held out the bucket.

The whole bucket.

Lily sniffed. Lily took it.

Lily's face came back, bit by bit.

She scooped the wet spring sand.

Then she handed Olivia the shovel.
