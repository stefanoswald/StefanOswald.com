Night 139

The Tummy Time Coach

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Tummy time.

Mama laid Theo on his tummy on the blanket

and lay down on the floor herself, face to face with him.

Theo did not like tummy time.

He complained into the blanket.

Olivia lay down on the floor too, on his other side.

"Fee-oh. Up. Look me."

Theo's head wobbled.

It came up the smallest bit.

Then it went down again, soft, onto the blanket.

"Up!" said Olivia.

Theo turned his head to her side,

cheek on the blanket, eyes on her.

"He look ME!"

"He heard you," said Mama.

Then he complained louder.

"That's plenty," said Mama,

and turned him onto his back, and he stopped at once.

Olivia stayed on her tummy.

"Me do more."

She lifted her own head up, very high.
