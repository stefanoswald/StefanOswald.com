Night 300

Three Hundred Nights of Book Three

Month 10: Try Again

"Story three hundred," said Papa, at the lamp.

"Free hundred?"

"Three hundred nights. Since you turned two."

Olivia counted on her fingers.

One, two, free, four, five.

She ran out of fingers.

She borrowed Papa's.

She ran out of those.

"Dat lots."

"Lots," said Mama.

"How long to free candles?"

"All the rest of winter," said Mama.

Down the hall, Theo was sleeping in the yellow room tonight,

for the first time,

in the crib that used to be hers,

its mattress set down low,

away from the window cords.

On his back, with nothing in there but him.

The cow with the bell watched from the dresser.

He was six months old today.

"Me read Fee-oh one?"

"He's asleep," said Papa. "Tomorrow."

So Papa read story three hundred,

and Olivia listened to all of it.

Then she said goodnight through the wall.
