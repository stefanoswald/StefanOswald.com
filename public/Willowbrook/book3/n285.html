Night 285

Spaghetti, the Word

Month 10: Try Again

There was a word Olivia could not say.

"Pasketti."

She could hear it was wrong.

That was the hard part.

So at the bathroom mirror she broke it into pieces.

"Spuh." Easy.

"Ghet." Easy.

"Tee." Baby stuff.

Then all together, fast.

"Spuh-ghet... pasketti!"

The pieces stood up fine alone.

Together they fell over, like a tall tower.

Mama said it once in the mirror, slow.

"Spa. Ghet. Ti."

Olivia's mouth copied Mama's mouth.

Big on the "ghet."

"Spuh-GHET-ee... pasketti."

She stamped one foot.

"I can't say it."

Then she remembered.

"Yet."

At bedtime she practiced on Bobby Bear.

"Spuh-ghet-ee, Baba. You try."

Baba did not try.

She whispered it into his flopped ear.
