Night 137

Acting Small

Month 5: Theo Arrives

The just-us day was only an hour this month.

Papa took Theo downstairs.

"What would you like?" said Mama.

"A book, or a bath with bubbles?"

Olivia put her thumb near her mouth.

"Rock me. Like baby."

"Come here, my baby," said Mama.

She sat in the rocking chair in the yellow room,

and Olivia climbed up, all knees and elbows.

Mama wrapped the baby blanket around her.

It covered about half.

Her feet stuck out the bottom.

"Waaah," said Olivia, in a small voice.

"Oh, my baby," said Mama. "Shh, shh."

Rock. Rock. The chair creaked.

Mama hummed the tune she used to hum

when Olivia was new.

Olivia's thumb went in. Her eyes went half shut.

For a while she was very small.

Then the thumb came out.

"Me big now."

"All right," said Mama.

Olivia climbed down and went to her own bed.

Mama sat on in the chair, holding the empty blanket.
