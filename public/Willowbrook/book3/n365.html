Night 365

Goodnight, Two

Month 12: Almost Three

Below the hill, the brook ran high and loud,

unstuck.

Down in the reeds, the peepers started up.

Peep. Peep. Peep-peep.

On Maple Lane, the lamps went out one by one.

Theo slept in his crib, on his back, one fist by his ear.

Olivia slept in the big bed, three years old,

with Bobby Bear under her chin.

In the Great Willow, Oliver Owl opened one eye.

"Who is three tonight?" he asked the dark.

Stella dipped low over the roof

and twinkled once, for yes.

The Sleepy Moon hummed.

Goodnight, two.

Sweet dreams. Same time tomorrow.
