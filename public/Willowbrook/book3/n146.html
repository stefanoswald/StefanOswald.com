Night 146

The Fog, Weathered Together

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Everybody in the house was tired.

Theo woke in the night, and then he woke again.

Mama put the milk in the cupboard.

Papa put his glasses in the fridge.

Olivia found them there, next to the butter.

"Papa. Glasses COLD."

Only Olivia remembered what came next every night.

Bath. Pajamas. Book. Song.

"Same," she said. "Same, same."

So it was the same.

One night Papa read the bear book and skipped a page.

"No! Button page!"

Down the hall, Theo started to cry.

"Olivia, SHH," said Papa, too sharp.

Olivia's face crumpled.

Papa put the book down and rubbed his eyes.

"I was too loud. I'm sorry."

Olivia looked at him.

"Papa tired."

"Very," said Papa.

He turned back to the button page

and read it slowly, all the way through.

Then the song, the right words, even.

By the last line Papa's eyes were closing,

his back against her bed.

Olivia patted his head.

"Nigh-nigh, Papa."
