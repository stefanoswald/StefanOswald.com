Night 232

Almost Dada

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

The list was still on the fridge, under the strawberry magnet.

DADA. MAMA. DUCK.

Papa lay down on the play mat beside Theo.

"Da-da. Da-da."

Theo looked at him. "Ahhh."

"Almost," said Papa.

"Dat ah," said Olivia.

Her turn. "Duck, Fee-oh. DUCK."

Theo kicked both legs. "Eeee!"

"Almost duck," said Olivia.

"That was eee," said Papa.

Mama didn't try. She just leaned in.

"Hello, you."

Theo gave her a long "Oooo" and a bubble.

Then Theo reached up

and took Papa's glasses right off his face.

He held on.

Papa squinted. "Nobody move."

Theo shook the glasses and laughed, a real laugh, all the way down.

Olivia looked out the window at the bare maple.

Then at Theo's fist.

Papa got his glasses back, a little wet.
