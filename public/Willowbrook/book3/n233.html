Night 233

Her Squeal

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Olivia had been out all afternoon with Mama.

She came in the front door with cold cheeks

and a leaf stuck to one boot.

Theo was in his bouncer by the couch, next to Papa.

He saw her.

His arms went up. His legs went.

And out came a sound nobody had heard him make before,

high and happy, like a kettle that is glad to see you.

"EEEEE!"

Olivia stopped on the mat.

"Fee-oh?"

"EEEEE!"

Papa got up, went out the front door, and came back in.

Theo looked at him politely.

Mama came in with the bags.

"Ah," said Theo, to be nice.

Olivia went out the door and came back in.

"EEEEEEE!"

"Dat mine," said Olivia.

"That one's yours," said Papa.

She went out and came in again, and again,

until the mat was covered in leaves.

Then she sat down by the bouncer, boots still on.
