Night 218

Holding the Line, Kindly

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

"Cookie," said Olivia, at the cookie jar. "Cookie NOW."

"After dinner," said Papa.

"NOW!"

"You really want it now," said Papa. "It's for after."

The storm was a big one.

Papa stayed close, down on one knee by the jar.

He did not hand over the cookie.

He did not go away.

When the loud part was over

and her wet face was in his sweater,

Papa put one cookie on the windowsill.

"That's yours. It waits right there."

All through dinner, Olivia watched the windowsill.

A bite of fish.

"Still dere."

A carrot.

"Still dere."

A long drink of milk.

"After now?"

Papa looked at her empty plate.

"After now."

He lifted her up so she could reach the sill,

and she took it down herself.
