Night 282

The Drawing That Wouldn't Duck

Month 10: Try Again

Olivia wanted to draw a duck.

She drew one.

"Not a duck."

Crumple.

She drew another.

"Head wrong."

Crumple.

The third one got crumpled before it had feet.

Her eyes knew exactly what a duck looks like.

Her hand was slower.

Mama did not draw one for her.

She picked up the crumpled ducks

and smoothed them flat on the table,

one, two, three, in a row.

"Look," said Mama.

Olivia looked.

Duck one was mostly a potato.

Duck two had a head.

Duck three had a head and the start of feet.

"Dis one got feet," said Olivia.

"It does," said Mama.

Olivia drew duck four.

A head. Two feet. A wing like a spoon.

"DUCK!"

Mama found a folder and wrote DUCKS on the front.

All four went in, the potato too.

Olivia drew one more, for Fee-oh.
