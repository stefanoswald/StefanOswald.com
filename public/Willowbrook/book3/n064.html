Night 64

The Wrong Groceries

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Home, and the bags on the counter.

Olivia on her step, to help.

Mama took out a long box.

"Hm. I didn't buy spaghetti."

A jar of red sauce. "Or this."

Cheese. Bread. A little plant that smelled green.

Mama looked at the pile.

Olivia looked at her shoes.

"Uh-oh."

"Uh-oh?"

"Wiwi game. Me too."

Mama sat down on a kitchen chair and laughed.

"Thank you for telling me."

"Mama mad?"

"Not mad. Surprised."

She looked at the pile again. Spaghetti. Sauce. Cheese. Bread.

"That's a whole dinner. It's Rosa's dinner."

"Uh-oh," said Olivia again.

"You're worried," said Mama. "Maybe. What could we do?"

Olivia pointed at the phone.

"Call Wiwi."

So they did.

Rosa was laughing before she even said hello.

She had a bag of apples and beans that were not hers.

"Come for supper," said Mama. "Bring your half."

In the empty grocery bag on the floor,

the cat turned around twice and lay down.
