Night 207

Theo Finds His Hands

Month 7: Potty Power

Theo found something.

He was lying on the play mat,

and his hand went up past his face,

and he saw it.

He stopped kicking.

He looked at it.

He opened it.

He closed it.

He looked at it some more.

"Fee-oh find hand," said Olivia.

She lay down beside him

and held her hand up too.

She opened it. She closed it.

It was a pretty good hand.

Then Theo tried to get his into his mouth.

It went to his eye.

It went to his nose.

He fussed.

"Mouf, Fee-oh. Mouf."

On the third try, he found it.

Fist in.

Sucking.

Drool everywhere.

Papa looked up from his book.

"He's been working on that all week."

Olivia wiped Theo's chin with her sleeve.
