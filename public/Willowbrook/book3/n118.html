Night 118

The Longest Sleepover

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Lily's room. The spare mattress on the floor. The first real sleepover, on the biggest night there was.

Neither girl slept much.

They whispered under the blanket.

"Baby here?"

"Not yet," said Lily. "Babies slow."

Her cousin had taken all night. She knew.

"What if brudder?"

"Teach him fings."

"What if sister?"

"Teach her fings."

"What if duck?"

Lily thought hard. "Teach it quack."

They laughed into the pillow until Rosa put her head around the door.

"Sleep, chickens," said Rosa. "Big day tomorrow."

At two o'clock Rosa looked in again. The girls were asleep in one heap, tangled up like the day the carts crashed, with Bobby Bear and Rabbit piled on them.

Rosa left the hall light on.
