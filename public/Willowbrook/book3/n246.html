Night 246

One, Two, FREE

Month 9: Counting to Five

Theo could roll one way now, tummy to back,

so he lived on a blanket on the floor.

Olivia lay down beside him on her tummy.

"Fee-oh. Watch."

She held up fingers.

"One."

Theo kicked.

"Two."

He pushed up on his arms and wobbled,

and grunted like Papa lifting the couch.

"FREE!"

Over he went.

Flop, onto his back, arms out,

his face right next to hers.

He looked surprised. Then he laughed.

"Mama! He goed on free!"

"You counted him over," said Mama.

"Again. One. Two. FREE!"

Theo looked at the lamp.

He had done it once. That seemed like plenty to him.

Olivia rolled onto her back beside him

and looked at the ceiling too.
