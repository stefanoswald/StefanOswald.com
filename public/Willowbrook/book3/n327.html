Night 327

Papa on the Phone

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Papa was on the phone forever.

The shelf brackets had come wrong.

Again.

Olivia stacked blocks by his feet,

quiet, with her ears on.

"No, I know it's not your fault," said Papa.

"Take your time."

Then, "Thanks for trying. Hope your day gets easier."

He hung up.

He put his forehead down on the table.

Then he drank his cold coffee.

After lunch, Olivia made a call on a banana.

"Hello? Da duck came wrong?"

She listened, nodding.

"Not your fault. Take your time."

She nodded some more.

"Hope your day easier. Bye-bye."

Papa put his cup down.

"Who was that?"

"Da duck man."

Papa was glad she had missed last Tuesday's call.

After supper she called the duck man back,

to check on him.
