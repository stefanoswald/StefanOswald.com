Night 163

When the Loud Is Too Loud

Month 6: Big Sister School

Every evening now, Theo had a fussy hour.

Tonight it was a fussy two hours.

Papa walked him up and down, up and down.

Theo cried and cried.

Olivia put her hands over her ears.

Her face crumpled.

Not mad. Not sad. Too much.

Mama saw.

She got down low.

"So loud."

Olivia nodded, hands still on.

"Want a quiet spot?"

They made one behind the couch:

two chairs, the big blanket on top,

a flashlight, and Bobby Bear.

Olivia crawled in.

Mama came in after her.

Inside, the crying sounded smaller.

Olivia took her hands off her ears.

"Fee-oh LOUD."

"Very loud. Your ears got full."

"Me love Fee-oh."

"I know," said Mama. "I love him too, and my ears get full too."

They sat in the blanket light.

Down the hall, the crying stopped,

then started again, smaller.

Olivia put Bobby Bear's floppy ear over her own

and stayed in a little longer.
