Night 252

Hide and Seek Unlocked

Month 9: Counting to Five

Thursday at the park. The mamas on the bench, with a thermos.

Olivia put her hands on her eyes at the oak.

"One. Two. Free. Four. Five!"

She peeked a little at four. Everybody does.

Lily hid behind the bush.

In summer, that bush hides a whole Lily.

In November it is sticks.

Olivia could see all of Lily.

The blue jacket. The bangs. The giggle.

"Where Wiwi go?"

She looked on the slide.

She looked under the bench, and Miss Rosa, flour on one cheek, lifted her feet.

She looked in her own pocket.

The bush giggled.

"FOUND you!"

Shrieking. Falling down. Again!

Then Lily counted,

and Olivia hid behind the same bush,

and shut her eyes tight,

so nobody could see her.
