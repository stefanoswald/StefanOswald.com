Night 222

The Roar Spot

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Some mad is too loud for a house with a sleeping baby in it.

So Papa took Olivia out to the back of the yard,

past the leaf pile, to a flat gray stone by the fence.

"This is the roar spot," said Papa.

"Out here, you can roar as big as you like."

Olivia stood on the stone.

She looked at the house.

She looked at Papa.

"Big?"

"Biggest."

"RAAAAAH!"

Leaves jumped off the maple.

Papa covered his ears.

A crow left.

Two yards over, the gold dog lifted one ear, and put it down again.

Two porches down, Mateo roared back.

Papa tried one too.

It came out like a sneeze.

Olivia laughed so hard she had to sit down on the stone.

On Wednesday, she was mad about her hat.

"Corner or roar spot?" said Mama.

"ROAR spot."

She came back in with pink cheeks

and one leaf in her hair.
