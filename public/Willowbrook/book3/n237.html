Night 237

The Storm Diary

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Since the banana, Papa had kept a little notebook on the fridge.

After each storm, when it was all over, he wrote one line.

Tuesday. Socks. Short nap.

Thursday. Cookie. Before dinner.

Saturday. Yellow spoon. Supper late.

Sunday. The farm. One stomp.

Tonight Mama and Papa read it together at the table.

Mama ran her finger down the page.

"Short nap," she said. "Short nap again."

"It's never the socks," said Papa.

Olivia climbed onto his knee.

"Me write."

Papa gave her the pencil.

She wrote a long, loopy line across the bottom of the page,

with her tongue out.

"What does it say?" said Papa.

"Fee-oh cry."

Papa read it back, very seriously.

"Theo cried."

The next day, the nap started half an hour sooner.
