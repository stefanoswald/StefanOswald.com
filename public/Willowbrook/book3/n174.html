Night 174

The Just-Us Promise, Kept

Month 6: Big Sister School

Saturday. Papa took Theo.

Mama took Olivia,

just Olivia,

the old way.

Mrs. Henderson waved from her porch.

"Just us!" Olivia told her.

At the bakery, Rosa had flour on one cheek.

"Just you two?" said Rosa.

"Then it's the just-us bun."

It was the regular bun, with a bow on the bag.

At the Lantern Library, one story,

on Mama's lap, with nobody else's feet in it.

At the pond, they looked at the ducks.

The ducks looked at the bun.

"Next month?" said Olivia.

"Every month," said Mama.

On the path home, Olivia found a feather,

gray and white.

She held it up.

"For Fee-oh."

"You thought of him," said Mama.

At home, Olivia held the feather over Theo on his mat.

His eyes went left with it, and right, and left.

Then she stood it up on the dresser,

next to the cow.
