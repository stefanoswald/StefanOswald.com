Night 240

The First Window-Seat Thursday

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Thursday at Lily's new house.

Two streets over. That close.

Lily's room smelled like bread,

because Rosa's big new kitchen was right underneath it.

The girls climbed into the window seat with two books and a blanket.

Below them, the street did things.

A man raked his leaves into a pile.

The wind blew them all back across the lawn.

He raked them up again.

"Again!" said Lily.

The wind did it again.

The girls cheered for the wind.

A squirrel sat on a pumpkin and ate its stem.

"Bad squirrel," said Lily.

"Hungry squirrel," said Olivia.

Rosa came up the stairs with two cookies.

"The last two," she said.

There were more downstairs.

Then the girls read their books,

not talking, just turning pages,

side by side in the window seat.

By the second book, Lily's head had tipped onto Olivia's shoulder.
