Night 225

The Words Come Back

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

A storm came out of nowhere after dinner.

Olivia lay face down on the rug, crying hard,

and nobody knew why.

Papa almost asked.

What's wrong? Use your words.

Every papa almost asks.

He didn't.

He sat on the floor near her, not touching,

and waited.

The clock ticked.

The dishwasher hummed.

The crying went on, then went ragged, then went quiet.

Then, from the rug, very small:

"Wiwi go away."

Oh.

Lily was moving.

Rosa had told them at spaghetti night.

Two streets over, a bigger house, a room with a window seat.

"You're sad Lily's moving," said Papa.

Olivia nodded into the rug.

"It's two streets," said Papa. "On moving day, we'll walk it."

"Walk it?"

"You and me."

Olivia crawled over and lay across his leg.

His leg went to sleep.

He let it.
