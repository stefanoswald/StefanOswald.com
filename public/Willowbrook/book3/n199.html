Night 199

The Handwashing Anthem

Month 7: Potty Power

After the potty come the hands.

Every time.

That was the rule from the very first something.

And the hands have a song.

Olivia made up the words.

"Wash da fingers,

wash da fumbs,

wash da backs,

bubbles, bubbles,

down da drain.

Bye!"

The doctors say to scrub for at least twenty seconds.

Fingers, thumbs, between, the backs.

This song, sung twice, is just long enough.

Papa sang it from the hall,

wrong on purpose.

"Wash the noodles,

wash the nose!"

"NO! FUMBS!"

"Wash the thumbs,

wash the cheese!"

"PAPA!"

Rinse on "Bye!"

Then the towel.

It went back on its hook, crooked.
