Night 105

The Practice Visit

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Lily's aunt stopped by with the baby cousin. A real baby, two months old.

Olivia watched everything.

Babies are small. Smaller than Bobby Bear.

"Me dat small?"

"Smaller," said Mama.

Olivia did not believe this.

Babies are loud. The cousin showed them how loud.

"Baby mad?"

"Hungry," said Lily's aunt, and fed him, and the loud stopped.

Babies hold on. Olivia held out one finger, slow and gentle, as Mama had shown her. The tiny fist closed around it and held tight.

Olivia did not move. The baby did not move. They looked at each other.

"He hold me."

"He does," said Mama.

Then babies fall asleep, anywhere, all at once. This one fell asleep holding on, so Olivia stood very still for a long time.

At home she showed the finger to the cat.
