Night 94

The Baby's Room

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Olivia's old crib had stood empty in her room since the big bed came. On Saturday Papa took it apart.

"My crib!"

"Your old crib," said Papa. "It's going to the baby."

Olivia thought about that. Then she picked up one long slat.

"Me carry dis."

She carried it all the way down the hall, very slowly, into the little yellow room by the stairs. Papa put the crib back together there, bolt by bolt, and Olivia checked every bolt with a pat.

"Good crib," she said. "I sleeped great."

Mama opened the dresser. Tiny clothes, her old ones, washed soft. One tiny sock went on Olivia's thumb. It fit.

"Fumb sock!"

Last came the cow with the bell, from the fair. Nothing goes in a baby's crib but the baby, Mama said, so the cow went up on the dresser, where it could see.

"Cow watch baby."

Ting, went the bell.
