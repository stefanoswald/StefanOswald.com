Night 33

The Sock Standoff

Month 2: Me Do It

Today, the sock.

Socks look easy. Socks are not easy.

Toes in. Heel stuck.

Pull. The sock came off again.

Toes in. Everything twisted.

Pull. The sock was on her hand.

"No, sock."

Mama sat in the doorway on her hands.

Her hands wanted that sock very much.

She sat harder.

Olivia lay on her back

and put her foot up in the air,

which is one way to do it.

Toes in. Heel. A long, long pull.

On.

Inside out, with the heel on top of her foot.

On.

She stood up to feel it.

Two steps. The heel rode along on top.

"Dat one," she said, and held up the other foot.

Mama's hands came out from under her.

Olivia was already sitting down with the sock.
