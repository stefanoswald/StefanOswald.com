Night 82

The Comfort Call

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Warm rain came down on Thursday,

and so did a phone call. Lily had a cold.

Olivia already had her shoes on.

She sat down by the front door in them.

Mama sat down by the door too.

"You were all ready to go."

"Sad."

"Sad," said Mama. "Me too, a little."

Then Mama got the phone,

and there on the screen was Lily.

Pink nose. Blanket. Rabbit.

"Hi Wiwi!"

"Hi Livy," said Lily, through her stuffy nose.

Olivia gave the tour.

The fort.

The tower.

Bobby Bear, held so close to the screen

that all Lily could see was nose.

Lily held up Rabbit.

One long ear filled the whole screen.

Then one story, the bear book,

held up to the phone while Mama turned the pages.

Lily was asleep on her pillow before the last page.

Olivia told it to her anyway, very quietly.
