Night 349

Theo's Turn Next

Month 12: Almost Three

On the rug, Olivia played turns with Theo.

She said a thing.

Then she waited for his turn.

"Moo," said Olivia.

"Ba," said Theo.

"Dat not moo."

She waited anyway.

"Duck," said Olivia.

"Da," said Theo.

"Close."

"Livy," said Olivia, and pointed at herself.

Theo looked at her.

"La-la," he said.

"No. LIVY."

Theo blew a bubble.

Then he leaned all the way forward.

"Li-li," said Theo. "Li-li-li."

Olivia sat straight up.

"MAMA! He say Livy!"

Mama had heard something from the couch.

It had been "li-li," mostly.

Then she saw Olivia's face.

"He said your name," said Mama.

Olivia lay back down, nose to nose.

"Again. Livy."

"Li," said Theo.

That night Papa wrote LIVY on the fridge list,

above DADA and MAMA and DUCK.

"Nobody won," he said, and did the dishes.
