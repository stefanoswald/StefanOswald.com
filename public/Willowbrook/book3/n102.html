Night 102

Grandpa Builds Again

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Sunday. The workshop door stood open to the June morning, and swallows went in and out over their heads.

New oak lay on the bench. Small pieces. Chair-shaped.

"Anodder chair, Gampa?"

Grandpa nodded.

He took the star template down from its nail and traced it onto the new back rail, the same star as on her own little chair.

"Same star?"

"Same star."

Then he held out the sanding block. Her sanding block.

She sanded. Shff. Shff. Shff. The dust hung gold in the light from the door.

Now and then Grandpa's big hand came down over hers, pressed a little harder, and went away again.

"Baby sit here?"

Grandpa nodded.

She ran her thumb along the rail, like Grandpa.

"Smoove."

He ran his thumb along it too, and said nothing. Then he carried her little chair in from the porch and set it beside the new one, star next to star.

Grandpa blew the dust off the new star.
