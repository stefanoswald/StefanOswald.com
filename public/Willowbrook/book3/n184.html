Night 184

The First Try

Month 7: Potty Power

After the bath, it was her idea.

"Me try. For real."

So she sat.

Mama handed her the bear book,

and she read it to herself,

mostly the pictures.

They waited.

The bathwater went down the drain.

Glug. Glug.

The towel dripped.

Olivia turned a page.

Then another.

Nothing came.

"All done," she said. "Nofing."

"Nothing this time," said Mama.

"You sat a good long while."

Olivia stood up and looked in the potty, to be sure.

Empty.

She did the big shrug, both hands up.

"Tomorrow."

She pulled up her pajamas by herself,

mostly.
