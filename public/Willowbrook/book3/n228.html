Night 228

The Storm That Turned Into a Nap

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

The socks were wrong.

Both of them.

"NO socks! No socks EVER!"

Olivia hit the rug,

feet pounding, one sock flying off across the room.

Mama glanced at the clock.

The nap had been short. The morning had been long.

She leaned in the doorway to wait it out.

The pounding slowed.

The yelling slowed.

Then, in the middle of a kick, it stopped.

Olivia was asleep.

Face down on the rug. One sock on.

Her mouth was a little open.

Mama looked at her for a while.

It was a very funny thing to see,

and she laughed without making a sound.

She tucked the throw blanket over her, right where she lay,

and went to find the flung sock.

Forty minutes later, Olivia sat up,

pink and creased from the rug.

"Why me on floor?"

"You fell asleep," said Mama.

Olivia looked at her feet.

She put the other sock on herself.
