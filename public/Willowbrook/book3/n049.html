Night 49

The Choosing Power

Month 2: Me Do It

The rule now is, Olivia dresses Olivia.

Today she chose

the striped shirt,

the polka-dot pants,

one red sock,

one duck sock,

and the rain boots.

Mama looked at all of it.

Her mouth opened to say a thing about the socks.

It closed again.

Clothes are hers.

The weather still gets a say.

"It's cold out. Blue coat or yellow coat?"

"Yellow."

Yellow, then, over the stripes.

Off to the market like that.

The librarian, passing, said, "Good boots for puddles."

The grocer nodded at the socks, slow,

like a man who knows socks.

Rosa said, "One duck. I like it."

Nobody said one word about matching.

Nobody in Willowbrook ever does.

Olivia found the puddle by the bench

and stood in it in the boots

and watched her two different socks

go under.
