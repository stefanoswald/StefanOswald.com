Night 209

Come Here a Minute

Month 7: Potty Power

It had been a month of big things.

That evening, for no reason,

Mama sat down in the rocking chair

and held out her arms.

"Come here a minute."

Olivia came.

She climbed up, knees first,

elbows everywhere.

She did not fit the way she used to.

She fit anyway.

They rocked.

Creak, forward.

Creak, back.

The first cool air of the fall came in at the window,

and Mama pulled the blanket round them both.

"Rock like Fee-oh," said Olivia.

So Mama rocked her like Theo,

and sang Theo's song,

and Olivia made one small baby noise, to help.

Mama had said a minute.

Nobody looked at the clock.

The chair went on creaking.
