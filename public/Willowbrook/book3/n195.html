Night 195

Lily Borrows the Ducks

Month 7: Potty Power

On Thursday, Lily came to play.

Miss Rosa dropped her off, flour on one cheek.

Lily was learning the potty too,

at her house, on a potty that was green.

"Me ducks," said Olivia, and showed her.

"Me green," said Lily.

"Me big flush," said Olivia.

"Me no flush," said Lily. "Too roary."

They both nodded. They knew.

They built a zoo out of blocks.

Right in the middle of the giraffe,

Lily went still.

She looked down.

Wet.

Lily's lip wobbled.

Olivia looked at Lily's pants.

Then she ran to her drawer

and came back with the spare duck pair.

"Wiwi. Dis."

Mama helped Lily change.

Lily looked down at the ducks.

Then she looked at Olivia,

and her whole face came back.

They finished the giraffe.

Lily went home in the ducks.
