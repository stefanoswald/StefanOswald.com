Night 110

The Night Everything Was Quiet

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

It was a slow evening. The windows were open. The fan turned its head one way, then back.

Mama lay on the couch, round as the moon. Papa's hand rested on the bump. Olivia's hand rested beside it.

For a long while there was no talking at all.

Thump.

"Soon," said Mama, to the ceiling.

Outside, Mrs. Henderson was watering her tomatoes. A screen door banged somewhere down the lane.

Olivia put her mouth close to the bump. "Baby come now. We ready."

Thump.

"Dat mean yes."

It might also have meant hiccups. Mama kept that to herself.

The light went long and gold, then blue. Olivia's eyes closed with her hand still on the bump.

In the kitchen, the cat was asleep in the fruit bowl.
