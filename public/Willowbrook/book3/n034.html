Night 34

Pouring Her Own Milk

Month 2: Me Do It

A small jug came to breakfast.

It is blue, and it holds about one cup,

and it is hers.

"Me pour it."

Two hands. Tongue out.

The jug tipped, slow, slow, then all at once.

Glug.

Milk in the cup.

Milk beside the cup.

One drop on her sock.

Olivia looked at the little lake,

then at Mama.

"The cloth is on your chair," said Mama.

Olivia wiped. The lake went, mostly.

She wiped the dry part of the table too, to be fair.

Then she drank the milk she poured,

all of it, in one long go,

and put the cup down hard.

"More."

Mama filled the small jug halfway

and slid it over

and picked up her own coffee,

which had gone cold,

which is what coffee does now.
