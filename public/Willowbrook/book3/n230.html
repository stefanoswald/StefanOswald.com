Night 230

Grandpa on Weather

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Sunday at the farm, nearly lunch.

Olivia did not want to leave the hens.

She stomped up the porch steps,

sat on the top one,

and crossed her arms tight.

Grandpa Joe sat down in his rocker.

Rock. Rock.

He didn't say anything.

After a while he held out one finger.

Olivia took it.

He walked her to the barn door

and put her hand on a dent in the wood,

low down, old and smooth.

"Mine," said Grandpa Joe.

He lifted one boot and set it down again, gently, by the dent.

Olivia looked from the dent to Grandpa.

"Gampa mad?"

"Once."

Then he looked up.

Clouds were going over the barn, fast, gray and then white,

and behind them the sky was blue.

He pointed.

Olivia looked up too.

Back on the porch, he rocked,

and she climbed up and rocked beside him.

The clouds went on over the hill.
