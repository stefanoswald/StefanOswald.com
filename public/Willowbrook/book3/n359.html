Night 359

The Party of Three Candles

Month 12: Almost Three

At noon, the door kept opening.

Lily came first, running.

Then Mateo, Grandpa Joe, the aunts,

and Mrs. Henderson with her card from the window.

June came last, holding on to her mama's coat.

Olivia took June's hand and pulled her into the middle of everybody.

"Dis June!"

June stopped looking at her shoes.

Then spaghetti.

Everybody slurped.

Grandpa Joe got sauce on his mustache and left it there.

Then hide and seek.

Olivia covered her eyes and counted.

"One, two, free, four, five! Ready!"

Grandpa Joe was behind the couch.

His straw hat stuck up over the top.

"GAMPA!"

"Can't hide a thing from you," said Grandpa.

On the rug, Theo got up on his hands and knees

and rocked, forward and back, as if to go find everyone.

He stayed where he was.

Then Papa turned the lights down,

and Rosa lifted the cloth off the cake.
