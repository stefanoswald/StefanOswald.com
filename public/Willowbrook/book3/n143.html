Night 143

Theo's First Sunday

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Sunday evening, when the heat went out of the day,

they drove to Honeywell Farm.

Grandpa Joe met them at the gate

and took Theo in one arm,

like a man who has held many new things.

"Well now," he said.

At the fence, Olivia did the talking.

She stood on the bottom rail.

"Fee-oh. Dis da farm.

Dat horse. Big. Nice.

Dat Duchess. She say no."

The Duchess turned her back.

Then she peeked.

Along the fence came Henrietta,

and behind her, in a line, her chicks.

Peep. Peep-peep.

The light was going.

One by one the chicks ran in under Henrietta,

and she fluffed out wide,

and they were gone.

Only a few small feet showed underneath.

"All dem fit?"

Grandpa nodded.

Olivia looked at Henrietta for a long time.

Then she climbed down

and leaned against Grandpa's leg.

He put his free hand on top of her head and left it there.

Henrietta settled, very wide.
