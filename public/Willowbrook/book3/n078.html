Night 78

Rosa's Kitchen Rules

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Thursday at Lily's house means Rosa's kitchen,

and Rosa's kitchen has rules.

"Aprons on."

Two aprons, tied around twice.

"Hands washed."

Two stools at the sink.

"Nobody touches the oven but me."

Two nods.

"And everybody tastes."

That is the best rule.

Today was bread.

Each girl got a lump of real dough.

Push it. Squash it. Punch it.

"Dough likes a good argument," said Rosa,

with flour on one cheek.

Olivia's lump turned into a ball, more or less.

Lily's turned into a snake, a long one, on purpose.

Onto the tray, beside the big loaves.

Rosa put the tray in the oven. Nobody else.

The girls sat on the floor, three big steps back,

and watched the ball and the snake get puffy

through the oven window.

Out came two small breads, brown and lopsided.

Rosa broke them open. Steam. A little butter.

Everybody tasted.

Lily ate the ball.

Olivia ate the snake.
