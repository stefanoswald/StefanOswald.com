Night 40

Doing It for Bobby

Month 2: Me Do It

At the little table, Olivia is teaching.

The pupil is the bear.

The lesson is drinking from a cup.

She wraps his paws around it.

"Baba do it."

The cup goes up. The cup goes to his nose.

The bear does nothing, because he is a bear,

and she pats his head anyway.

"Baba drink."

Next, the sock.

She starts to pull it onto his foot,

the one with the heart,

and stops herself.

She puts the sock in his paws.

She folds her own hands in her lap

and sits on them.

She waits. He waits.

The clock in the hall waits.

"Okay," she says at last. "Me help."

The sock goes on with a little help,

the way socks do,

and Baba gets a nap for his trouble,

sock on,

one ear flopped over his eye.
