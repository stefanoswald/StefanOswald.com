Night 344

Invitations by Trike

Month 12: Almost Three

Mama wrote the words on the cards.

Olivia drew the ducks.

The cards went in the trike basket.

Mama buckled her helmet.

"Me bring dem."

Papa had swept the sidewalk.

Snow stood along both sides of it, like walls.

Bring-bring went the bell.

Two porches down, Mateo was on his step.

"Party," said Olivia, and held up a card.

"I'm coming," said Mateo. "I'm four, you know."

"Me free. Almost."

Mama carried the trike across to Mrs. Henderson's.

Olivia held her hand the whole way.

Mrs. Henderson came to the door in her slippers.

She read the card twice.

"For me?"

Her cheeks went pink, and she stood the card up in her window

where the whole street could see it.

The cards for Lily and June and the farm were too far for a trike.

They went in the mailbox by the gate.

Olivia put the red flag up herself.
