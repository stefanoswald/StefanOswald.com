Night 247

Counting Everything

Month 9: Counting to Five

Everything on Maple Lane needed counting now.

Their porch steps. One, two, free.

Mrs. Henderson's pumpkins, going soft.

"One. Two. Free. Squishy one."

The gold dog's legs, which he allowed.

Then honking, from the sky.

Geese, going over in a long wobbly V.

"One! Two! Free! Four! Five!"

The geese kept coming.

"Five! FIVE!"

The geese kept coming.

"LOTS!"

"Lots," said Mama. "They're going south."

"Where south?"

"Somewhere warmer."

"Me too."

"Not this year."

Olivia counted Theo's feet, in the carrier on Mama's front.

"One."

There was only one sock.

They walked back the way they had come, looking.

The porch lights were coming on down the lane.

Mrs. Henderson was at her gate.

She was holding up a very small sock.
