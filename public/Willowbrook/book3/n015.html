Night 15

Two Feelings at Once

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The best playdate ever, and now it is over.

Olivia is at the window.

Lily is in the stroller, going away up the lane.

Olivia waves. She is smiling.

She is also crying.

Both.

Wave, smile, tears, all together.

"Oh, love," says Mama, picking her up.

"You're happy and you're sad. Both at once."

"Happy," says Olivia. "Sad."

"A heart is a big house," says Mama. "Two lights can be on."

Lily's mitten waves from the corner.

Then the stroller turns and is gone.

Olivia's breath makes a cloud on the cold glass.

She wipes it with her sleeve.

Then she waves once more, at nothing, in case.
