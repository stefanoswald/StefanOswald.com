Night 56

The Do-Over

Month 2: Me Do It

The pour went wrong.

Not a lake this time. The whole jug.

Over the edge, off the table,

on the chair, on the floor, on the sock.

Olivia froze.

Her face started to fold.

"Oh, Olivia."

That was Mama, and it came out sharp,

and the face folded all the way.

Mama heard herself.

She took one slow breath, out.

Then she got down on the floor, in the milk.

"That was my grumpy voice. Sorry."

She waited.

"Big spill. Want a do-over?"

A do-over.

That is the family word for it.

Not a scolding. A fresh go.

They wiped, one cloth each.

Mama did the floor. Olivia did the chair

and a bit of the table leg.

Then the jug came back, filled halfway,

into her two hands, steadier now.

Tip. Slow. Glug.

Into the cup. All of it.

"Me did it."

"Do-over," said Mama, and drank the milk,

because it was her cup.
