Night 269

The Turn-Taking Count

Month 9: Counting to Five

Theo could grab now. Both hands. Anything.

This morning he grabbed Olivia's big yellow bath duck

and put its head in his mouth.

"MINE!"

Olivia pulled. Theo held on.

He was stronger than he looked.

"He's got a good grip," said Mama. "You want it back."

Olivia thought. "Fee-oh turn. Five."

She counted, fast.

"One-two-free-four-five! My turn!"

Theo did not let go.

Four-month-olds don't do five.

"Hm," said Mama. "What could we do?"

Olivia looked in the toy basket.

She got the crinkle star and waved it at him.

Theo let go of the duck to grab the star.

"Trade!"

The duck was wet all over.

"Eww. Spitty."

She dried it on her shirt.

It was her turn now.
