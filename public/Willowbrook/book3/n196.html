Night 196

I Need Privacy

Month 7: Potty Power

This week there was something new at the bathroom door.

Olivia went in.

She turned around.

She held up one flat hand.

"Mama out."

"Oh," said Mama, and stepped out.

"You want privacy."

"Pri-sy."

Olivia pushed the door.

Not shut. Nearly shut.

One inch of light stayed in the crack.

Mama waited in the hall.

Then, without thinking, she pushed the door open to check.

"MAMA. OUT."

Mama stepped back.

"You're right. I didn't knock."

She pulled the door back to its inch.

"Sorry," she said through the crack.

Knock, knock.

"Okay in there?"

"OKAY."

Quiet.

Then, "Mama? Button."

Privacy does not cover buttons.

Mama went in and did the button,

and went back out,

and the door went back to its inch.
