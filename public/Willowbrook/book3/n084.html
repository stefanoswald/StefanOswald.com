Night 84

What Makes a Best Friend

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

The end of the afternoon, at the gate.

The sky had gone the color of lilacs,

and so had the lilacs.

"Time to go home," said Rosa.

Nobody went.

They hugged.

They let go.

They hugged again.

Lily went three steps down the lane

and came back for one more.

Rosa leaned on the gate.

"Olivia. Who's your best friend?"

Olivia did not look up.

She pointed at Lily,

who was right there, close enough to touch.

"Dis one."

"And yours, Lily?"

Lily pointed. "Dat one."

"Well," said Mama. "That's easy, then."

Then Lily went down the lane on Rosa's hip,

looking back over the shoulder every few steps,

and waving, and waving.

Olivia stayed at the gate

until the blue jacket went round the corner.
