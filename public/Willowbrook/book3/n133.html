Night 133

The Bath Audience

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Theo's bath was not a real bath yet.

Not until the little stump on his belly came off.

So Mama laid him on a towel on the changing table

and washed him a bit at a time, with a warm cloth.

One of her hands stayed on him the whole time.

Olivia watched from her step stool.

She told everything as it happened.

"Face now. Just water. No soap face."

"Just water," said Mama.

"Now neck. Neck cheese."

"Milk," said Mama. "Milk in the creases."

"Cheese," said Olivia.

"Where belly button?"

"Not yet," said Mama. "Soon."

"Now quick-quick wrap. Babies get cold fast."

Mama wrapped him quick-quick, snug in the towel.

"Burrito," said Olivia.

"Me do toes?"

Mama gave her one corner of the towel.

Ten toes, each smaller than a pea,

dried one at a time.

Then Olivia sniffed the top of his head.

"Cheese," she whispered.

Mama let that one go.
