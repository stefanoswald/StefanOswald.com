Night 178

The First Word Bets

Month 6: Big Sister School

At supper, Papa had an idea.

"Let's guess Theo's first word."

"He won't talk for about a year," said Mama.

"Then I have a year to practice," said Papa.

"Dada," said Papa.

"Mama," said Mama.

"DUCK," said Olivia.

Everybody looked at her.

"Duck?"

"Duck. Me teach him."

Mama wrote all three on a list for the fridge:

DADA, MAMA, DUCK.

"Loser does the dishes," said Papa.

Theo, in his bouncer, said, "Ahh."

Everybody stopped.

"Almost Dada," said Papa.

"Almost DUCK," said Olivia.

"That was ah," said Mama.

Olivia leaned down to the bouncer.

"Duck, Fee-oh. DUCK."

Theo blew a small bubble.

The list went up on the fridge,

under the strawberry magnet,

and it stayed there a long, long time.
