Night 324

The Interrupting Fix

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

June came to play.

June talks slow.

She looks for every word first.

"I want," said June,

and stopped to find the next word.

"Da RED one!" said Olivia,

and gave her the red block.

June didn't want the red one.

She put it down.

"Can we," said June.

"Play TRAINS!" said Olivia.

June stopped talking.

She held her sleeve and looked at the floor.

Mama sat down by Olivia.

"June has words in there," she said softly.

"They come slow."

She pressed Olivia's two hands together.

"When June is talking, squeeze."

"I like," said June.

Olivia squeezed.

Her whole face squeezed too.

June found it.

"Da cat."

The cat was asleep in the doll stroller.

"Me too," said Olivia.

June talked all afternoon after that.

Mostly about the cat.
