Night 345

The Cake Consult

Month 12: Almost Three

Rosa had a pencil behind her ear

and a pad on the counter.

"Third cake," she said. "Tell me."

Olivia told her.

"Free candles."

Rosa drew three candles.

"Gold star on top."

Rosa drew the star. She always does.

"And little stars. Round it."

"Little stars?"

"For Fee-oh. For June."

"And?"

"EVERYBODY."

Olivia's arms went wide.

Rosa put down her pencil.

She looked at Olivia for a while.

"A star for everybody at the party."

"Yes."

Rosa drew them in, one by one, until the cake was crowded.

"Last thing," said Rosa. "One crust end, the last one."

She cut an end off a warm loaf.

Olivia ate it.

Rosa cut another end.

"This one's the last one."

It was not.

Olivia sat on the tall stool,

chewing, feet swinging.
