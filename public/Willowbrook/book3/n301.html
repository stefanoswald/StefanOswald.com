Night 301

Spaghetti

Month 10: Try Again

Rosa and Lily came to supper.

Spaghetti.

All week Olivia had been practicing.

"Pasketti." "Spuh-ghet-ee."

Mostly pasketti.

Mama put the big bowl down.

"What are we having?" said Rosa, who knew.

Olivia took a big breath.

"Spa. Ghet. Ti."

Then all together.

"Spaghetti!"

It came out whole.

Lily clapped with her fork in her hand.

Rosa cheered. Mama cheered.

Theo, in his high chair with his mashed carrot,

banged his spoon. "Ba!"

"He said spaghetti!" said Lily.

"No," said Olivia. "Dat ba."

"Buh was duck," said Mama.

Olivia thought about that.

"Dat different."

She said spaghetti all through supper,

mostly right.

There was sauce on her eyebrow again.
