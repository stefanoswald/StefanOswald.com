Night 219

The Repair

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

The tower was tall.

Then it wasn't.

Olivia threw a block.

It hit the door. Thunk.

Then down she went, loud.

Mama moved the other blocks out of reach

and waited, close by.

When it was over, Olivia sat up.

Her hair was stuck to her cheeks.

She looked at Mama, to see.

Mama opened her arms first.

Olivia walked into them.

"That was a big mad," said Mama. "Your tall tower crashed."

"It falled," said Olivia, into the cardigan.

"You can stomp when you're mad. Blocks stay on the floor."

Olivia looked at the block by the door.

She went and got it.

The rest of the tower lay in a long, wobbly line across the rug.

Mama tipped her head.

"Hmm. Maybe that's a bridge?"

Olivia looked at it.

Then she went for her little red car.

Brrm, over the bridge.

Brrm, back.
