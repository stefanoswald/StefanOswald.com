Night 314

Kind to Herself

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Olivia poured her own milk

with the little blue jug, like always.

This time it tipped.

Milk ran across the table and over the edge.

Drip. Drip. Drip.

Olivia put her face down on her arms.

"Me bad. Me always spill."

Mama didn't argue.

She sat Duck on the table

and tipped Duck's arm against the jug.

"Oh no," said Mama, in Duck's voice.

"I spilled."

Olivia looked up.

"It okay, Duck. Just milk."

She patted Duck's head.

"We get da towel."

"What about Livy?" said Mama.

Olivia looked at her wet sleeve.

Then she patted her own head,

twice.

"It okay, Livy. Just milk."

They wiped it up together.

Duck helped, mostly by falling over.

Then Olivia poured again,

two hands on the blue jug this time.

Most of it went in.
