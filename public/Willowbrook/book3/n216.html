Night 216

The Cozy Corner

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

While Theo napped, they built a corner.

Olivia chose the place: behind the big chair, by the window,

where the radiator ticked behind its wooden cover.

She chose the cushion, the green one,

and dragged it there herself.

Papa brought a squeeze pillow.

Olivia squeezed it. It sighed.

She squeezed it again, to hear it sigh.

Mama brought the pinwheel from Rosa's.

Olivia blew. It whirred and spun.

Two soft books went in.

Duck went in.

A spoon went in, for reasons of her own.

"Livy corner," said Olivia.

"Nobody sends you here," said Mama. "You come when you want."

Olivia tried it out.

She sat. She squeezed. She blew.

She lay down with her feet up the wall.

"Good," she said.

By supper, the cat had moved in.
