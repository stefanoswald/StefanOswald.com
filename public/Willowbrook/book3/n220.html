Night 220

Papa's Storm

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

It had been a long day.

Theo had been up in the night. The car was making a noise.

Then Olivia reached for the brush,

and the paint jar tipped over on the new rug.

"OLIVIA! I said CAREFUL!"

Olivia went very still.

Her chin started to wobble.

Papa stopped.

He looked at her face, and put the rag down,

and got down on his knees.

"I yelled," he said. "I'm sorry."

Olivia studied him.

"Papa storm?"

"Papa storm. Spills are just spills."

She thought about that.

Then she got the other rag and dabbed at the paint,

dab, dab, very serious,

while Papa scrubbed.

The rug kept a spot the size of a pancake.

Olivia gave it a pat on her way to bed.
