Night 304

Asleep Yet?

Month 10: Try Again

Snow lay along every branch of the Great Willow.

Oliver Owl fluffed his feathers against the cold.

Below, Willowbrook was putting out its lights.

The bakery. The farm. Mateo's porch.

The library lantern stayed lit.

On Maple Lane, one window was still yellow.

"Is she asleep?" asked Oliver.

Stella dipped low to look.

She did not twinkle.

Oliver waited.

Owls are good at waiting.

Snow came down, small and slow,

on the roofs and the barn and the brook.

The yellow window went dark.

"Asleep yet?" asked Oliver.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.
