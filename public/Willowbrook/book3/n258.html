Night 258

Five Berries for Two

Month 9: Counting to Five

Thursday, at Olivia's house.

Mama put five strawberries in a bowl.

They came from the market. The garden was asleep.

Olivia gave them out.

"One for me. One for Wiwi."

"Two for me. Two for Wiwi."

One left.

They both looked at it.

Lily put her hand near the bowl.

Olivia put her hand near Lily's hand.

"For Fee-oh?" said Lily.

"Fee-oh no teef," said Olivia. "He drink milk."

This was true.

Mama came with a knife.

"One berry, two girls. I can cut it."

She cut it. One half came out big. One came out little.

Olivia looked at the big half.

She looked at Lily.

She gave Lily the big one.

She held up the little one. "Baby berry."

Lily grinned at her with red juice on her chin.
