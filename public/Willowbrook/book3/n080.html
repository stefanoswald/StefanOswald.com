Night 80

The Defending

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

There was a bigger boy in the sandbox.

Four, maybe.

He had a truck, a plan, and a great many roads.

Olivia dug a hole near the edge of one road.

"No," said the boy. "She's too little. She can't play this."

Olivia's shovel stopped.

Her face began to fold.

On the bench, Mama half stood up,

and then sat back down.

A blue jacket came across the sand

and stopped right between them.

Lily. Feet planted. Chin up.

"She play."

The boy looked at Lily.

"She dig good," said Lily. "My friend."

The boy thought about this for a long time,

which for a four-year-old is about two seconds.

"She can do the river," he said.

So Olivia did the river,

with water from the bucket,

and Lily helped with the river,

and the boy did the roads.

When the river got to a road,

he made the road a bridge.

Three heads, bent over one river.
