Night 293

The Seed Is a Yet

Month 10: Try Again

Outside the window, snow.

On the sill, a paper cup of dirt.

Olivia pushed one bean down with her finger.

Mama helped her give it water.

"Now what?"

"Now we wait."

Day one: dirt.

Day two: dirt.

Day three: dirt.

"It broken?"

"Not broken," said Mama. "It's a yet."

"Where?"

"Down in the dark, busy."

Olivia put her ear on the cup.

Dirt does not say much.

Every morning she gave it a little water.

Day six: dirt.

Day seven: dirt.

Then one morning,

a green hook,

bent over, pulling its head up out of the dark.

"It came!"

She told Theo first.

Theo said, "Ba."

It leaned toward the snowy window.
