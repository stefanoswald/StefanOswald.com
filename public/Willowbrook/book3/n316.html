Night 316

Theo's First Kindness

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

On Thursday, Lily whistled.

A real one.

Olivia tried on the walk home.

Air. Spit. No whistle.

Now she lay on the rug

with her face in Duck.

"Me sad," she told Mama. "Can't whistle."

"You wanted one like Lily's," said Mama.

Theo sat on the rug beside her,

chewing the big wooden spoon.

"Ba-ba-ba-ba," he said.

Olivia didn't look up.

Theo took the spoon out of his mouth.

He moved it to his other hand,

slow and wobbly.

Then his arm swung out,

and the spoon landed on Olivia's hand.

He let go.

Olivia sat up.

"Fee-oh gived me it!"

The spoon was wet all over.

"His best spoon," said Mama.

Olivia held it for a bit.

Then she gave it back,

and Theo put it right in his mouth.

She tried the whistle again, for him.

Air, and a little spit.

Theo laughed.

So she did it again.
