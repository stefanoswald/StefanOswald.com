Night 17

Papa Has Feelings Too

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Papa was looking at an old photo.

An old man in it, with kind eyes and a big hat.

Papa's face had gone droopy.

The sad face, from the game.

Olivia climbed up.

"Papa sad?"

Papa nearly said no, no.

Grown-ups do that.

Then he did not.

"Yes," he said. "A little sad."

Olivia looked at the photo.

"That's my grandpa," said Papa. "He died a long time ago."

Olivia looked at Papa.

"I miss him today."

She knew what to do.

Pat, pat, on his hand.

Then a kiss on the knuckle.

"Okay, Papa."

"Thank you," said Papa. "That helps."

They looked at the photo together.

"Hat," said Olivia.

"He wore it every day," said Papa. "Even inside."

Papa put the photo on the low shelf, where she could see it.

He left it there.
