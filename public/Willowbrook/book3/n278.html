Night 278

The Circle Won't Circle

Month 10: Try Again

It was too icy outside,

so Papa made the hall a road.

Helmet on, same as outside.

Olivia's legs knew running.

They knew stomping.

They knew jumping, both feet.

They did not know round.

Push. Lurch. Stop.

Push. Lurch. Stop.

Half the hall took forever.

Then her foot slipped off

and her knee bumped the handlebar.

"Trike broken!"

She smacked the bell.

It rang anyway, which made it worse.

Papa did not tell her the trike was fine.

He sat on the floor at the end of the hall.

When she was quieter, he said,

"Those pedals are tricky."

Then, "Stop, or one more?"

"One more."

Push. Lurch.

Then a pedal came up under her foot by itself,

and she pushed it,

and it went all the way round.

One whole circle.

"Papa! Did you see?"

"I saw," said Papa.
