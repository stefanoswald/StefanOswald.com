Night 108

The Welcome Basket

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Something was happening at Rosa's kitchen table. The mothers from the park bench were all there, and a big basket was filling up.

Little knitted socks. A soft cotton blanket. Suppers for the freezer, each one labeled, stacked like bricks.

"For the first weeks," said one of the mothers. "Nobody cooks in the first weeks."

Rosa, flour on one cheek, set in a jar of her bread starter. "It rises while you don't."

Olivia had something too. She had worked on it all morning.

"For baby."

She put it on top. It was a duck. It was mostly a duck.

Mama picked up the drawing and held it. Her eyes went shiny. For a moment she did not say anything at all.

"Thank you," she said at last. "All of you."

"Duck," said Olivia, in case anyone had missed it.

The basket went home in the red wagon, heavy as anything.

The duck rode on top.
