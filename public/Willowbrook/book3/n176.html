Night 176

The Hard Wish

Month 6: Big Sister School

It was a long afternoon.

Theo cried and cried.

The nap was short.

Lily was sick, so no Thursday.

And a grown-up foot, carrying a baby,

knocked Olivia's tower down.

Olivia stood in the middle of the rug

with her fists shut.

"Put him BACK."

The room went quiet.

Even Olivia's eyes went wide.

Mama did not gasp.

She sat down on the floor.

"You're so tired of the crying."

Olivia's chin wobbled.

"Loud. All day."

"All day," said Mama.

Olivia climbed into her lap, fists and all.

Mama held her.

Down the hall, Theo cried some more.

"Him staying?" said Olivia, into the green cardigan.

"He's staying," said Mama. "So are you. So am I."

After a while, the crying down the hall stopped.

After another while,

Olivia's fists came open.
