Night 264

Three Fingers for the Birthday

Month 9: Counting to Five

At the hall mirror, Olivia did her age.

Two fingers.

"Me Olivia. Me two."

"After the winter," said Mama, "you'll be three."

"Free?"

She tried to put up three fingers.

Four came up.

The little one would not stay down by itself.

She tried again. Four again.

Mama showed her the trick.

The thumb holds the little one down.

Three stand up.

"FREE!"

She let go of the little one to look at it,

and it popped right back up.

"Four."

"When me free?"

"After the snow," said Mama. "At the very end of the cold."

Olivia did not know when that was.

She practiced anyway.

She practiced for the mirror until supper.

The mirror got it right every time.
