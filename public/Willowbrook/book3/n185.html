Night 185

The Something

Month 7: Potty Power

Four mornings of trying.

Four nothings.

Then, on a plain Tuesday after breakfast,

Olivia said, "Me try,"

and went by herself.

She sat.

She hummed.

Then her face changed.

She looked down.

She looked up.

"MAMA! PAPA! Somefing CAME!"

They came at a run,

Papa with his toast still in his hand.

They looked.

It was true.

Olivia stood up very tall.

"Me did it."

"You felt it coming, and you sat," said Mama.

Mama tipped it into the big toilet.

"Bye, somefing!"

Whoosh.

Olivia's hands flew to her ears.

When the pipes were quiet, she took them down.

"Me happy."

"No more diapers, ever!" said Papa.

Mama looked at the little potty for a while.

"Maybe," she said.

Papa finished his toast.
