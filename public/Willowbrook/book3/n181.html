Night 181

One More Light

Month 6: Big Sister School

A warm night at the end of summer.

The last fireflies were out over the Great Willow,

fewer than before.

Oliver Owl sat on his branch.

Stella came down low beside him.

Below, the lamps on Maple Lane went out,

one, and then another.

"Is everyone asleep?" asked the owl.

Stella looked.

One window lit up again, yellow.

A baby was awake in there,

and someone was walking him,

back and forth, back and forth.

Stella waited.

The Sleepy Moon hummed, very low.

The owl waited too. He is good at it.

Back and forth.

Back and forth.

The window went dark.

"Now?" asked the owl.

Stella twinkled once, for yes.
