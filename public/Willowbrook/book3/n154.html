Night 154

The Tummy-Time Race

Month 6: Big Sister School

Tummy time.

Mama lay down on the floor.

Theo lay on his tummy on the mat,

cheek down, grumbling.

Olivia lay down too, nose to nose.

"Race, Fee-oh. Heads up!"

Her head went up.

Theo pushed.

His head came up, wobble, wobble,

up off the mat,

and down. Cheek.

"Me win!"

"Again," said Mama.

Up. Wobble, wobble.

He held it longer this time,

long enough to look right at her.

Down.

Olivia held hers up and up.

The rug had fuzz on it.

She looked at the fuzz.

She picked at the fuzz.

Her cheek went down on the rug

to see it better.

"Who's winning?" said Mama.

Olivia looked over.

Theo's head was up.

"Fee-oh win."

He put his cheek down,

and so did she.
