Night 284

Theo's Hundred Falls

Month 10: Try Again

Theo could almost sit.

Mama set him on the rug

with his hands down in front of him, like a little tent.

He wobbled.

He held.

He toppled.

Sideways, slow,

onto the cushion Olivia had pushed there.

Flump.

Theo laughed.

Mama sat him up again.

Olivia's job was the cushion.

Left side? Right side?

"Dis way!"

Flump. Right on it.

Then he went backward,

where no cushion was,

and Mama's hand was there instead.

He laughed at that too.

Olivia watched him for a long time.

"He falled. He not sad."

"He's not," said Mama.

"Why?"

Mama thought about it.

"I think he likes the cushion."

Olivia pushed the cushion closer.

Theo toppled onto it, laughing.

Olivia lay down on the cushion too.

"Flump."
