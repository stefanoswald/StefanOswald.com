Night 364

Everything Three Holds

Month 12: Almost Three

Olivia had one more goodnight to give.

Papa carried her to Theo's crib.

Theo was still awake, on his back, looking up.

"Li-li?"

"Me free now, Fee-oh."

"Tell him about this month," said Papa.

"Da brook unstuck."

"It went crack, and then it ran."

"Bella land on you."

"And somebody said pbbbt."

"My whistle came!"

"One whistle, and one back, for Lily."

"Everybody came to party."

"With eggs and noodles and red balloons."

"And what does three get?"

"Big swing. Trike to corner."

"A night at the farm, just you. The cubby room, with Lily."

"Long stories."

"The kind that stop till tomorrow."

"June's baby coming."

"With the blanket and its crooked square."

"And when the weather comes?"

"Me da sky."

"And if it rains on your barrow?"

"Maybe."

Olivia leaned down over the rail.

"You get one. In summer."

She gave him the fifth kiss, on his head.
