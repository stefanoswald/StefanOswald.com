Night 74

The Copying Compliment

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Thursday morning, Olivia pointed at her own head.

"Hair like Wiwi."

Lily's hair is black and straight,

cut straight across the front.

Olivia's hair is curls, all the way down.

Mama wet the front curls and combed them flat.

She let go.

Boing.

Mama tried a clip.

Boing.

Olivia put her own hand flat on top of her head

and held them down herself.

She went to answer the door like that.

There stood Lily.

In a red bow.

In her black hair, a red bow, just like Olivia's.

Behind her, Rosa held up four fingers.

"Four tries. It kept sliding."

"Like Livy," said Lily.

Olivia looked at the bow.

Lily looked at the hand on Olivia's head.

Nobody asked about it.

Then Olivia let go.

Boing.
