Night 70

The First Fight

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Inside the fort there was a tower.

Olivia's tower.

Lily put a block on top.

"No! Me do it!"

Olivia took the block off.

Lily put it back.

Hands got pushy. The tower leaned, and went.

"Wiwi did it!"

"Livy did it!"

Then there were two girls in two corners of the fort,

backs turned, arms folded,

and the blanket roof sagging between them.

Mama did not rush in.

She sat down on the rug outside the fort,

where they could see her socks,

and said nothing at all.

Sniff, from one corner.

Sniff, from the other.

"Two mad girls," said Mama at last, "and one tower down."

Then, "No pushing. You can say stop."

"Stop," said Olivia, trying it.

"Stop," said Lily.

Then, very small, "Sorry push."

"Sorry."

"Build again?"

"Build."

The new tower was shorter than the old one.

It had Lily's block on top.
