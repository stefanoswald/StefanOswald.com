Night 73

Missing on a Monday

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Monday. Thursday was three sleeps away.

Olivia stood at the window with her nose on the glass.

"Wiwi?"

"Not today," said Mama, folding towels. "Thursday."

"Miss Wiwi."

"Thursday's soon," said Mama, and kept folding.

Olivia lay face down on the rug and stayed there.

Mama looked at the rug.

She put the towel down.

She lay down on the rug too, nose to nose.

"Sorry. I was busy with the towels."

Then, "You miss Lily."

Olivia nodded into the rug.

"Want to make her something?"

Crayons. Blue, because Lily likes blue.

Round and round and round.

"Dis duck."

It did not look like a duck. It looked like Monday.

On the walk it went into Lily's mailbox,

and Olivia put the flag up herself.

On Tuesday morning their own flag was up.

Inside, folded twice: blue scribbles, a great many of them.

"Wiwi duck!"

Mama taped it by the big bed, low,

where Olivia could reach it.
