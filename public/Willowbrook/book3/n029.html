Night 29

Henrietta Has a Bad Day

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Even hens have days.

Henrietta is having one.

Her feathers are ruffled the wrong way.

The Duchess took her nest spot.

Her bok has gone flat.

Bok. Bok.

Olivia knows this weather.

She has seen it all month.

"Hen-etta sad?"

Grandpa Joe leans on the fence.

"Reckon," he says.

Olivia kneels at the wire.

A soft voice first. "Okay, Hen-etta."

Then slow blinks, the kind the cat does.

Then one yellow dandelion, pushed through the fence.

The first one on the whole farm.

Henrietta looks at the flower.

Bok?

She pecks it. Keeps it.

Struts off, a little straighter.

Grandpa says nothing.

He winks.

He puts a second dandelion in Olivia's hand,

and nods at the Duchess.
