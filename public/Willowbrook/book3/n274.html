Night 274

The Twelve-Piece Mountain

Month 10: Try Again

The aunts sent a puzzle in the mail.

Twelve pieces. A duck pond.

Her old one had three.

Olivia tipped it out on the rug.

Two pieces went together right away.

"I did dat!"

Then nothing went.

Not the reeds. Not the sky.

She turned them. She pushed them.

She pushed harder.

"Dis puzzle BAD!"

Her arm swept the pieces across the rug.

Then came the loud crying,

then the small crying,

and then she was climbing into Mama's lap.

Mama had sat down on the rug, close, the whole time.

When the crying was little,

Mama said, "That puzzle made you so mad."

"Too hard."

"Too hard today."

They picked up all twelve together.

Mama stood the box on the low shelf,

duck side out, where Olivia could see it.

"Tomorrow?" said Olivia.

"Maybe," said Mama.

The duck watched from the shelf.
