Night 161

Ah and Goo

Month 6: Big Sister School

Theo had a new sound.

Not crying. A round one.

"Ahhh," said Theo.

Olivia lay down on the rug, nose to nose.

"Really?"

"Goo."

"No," said Olivia. "Him did NOT."

"Ahh-ooo."

"Me know. Me SAID."

Mama stopped folding laundry to listen.

"Ahh," said Theo, softer.

"Uh-huh."

"Goo."

"Den what?"

Theo looked at the ceiling fan.

He looked back at her.

"Ahhh-goo."

"Oh no," said Olivia. "Poor duck."

Mama never did find out what happened to the duck.
