Night 239

Only Olivia Saw

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Theo was doing his tummy time on the play mat.

He pushed up on his elbows and looked at Olivia.

Olivia lay on her tummy too, facing him.

"Hi, Fee-oh."

Mama was on the couch right behind them,

folding towels.

Theo reached for Olivia's curls.

He reached too far.

He tipped,

and then, slowly and all at once,

he was on his back, looking at the ceiling.

He looked very surprised.

Nobody had told him that could happen.

Olivia shot up onto her knees.

"Fee-oh FLIPPED!"

Mama looked up.

Theo was on his back,

as if he had been there all day.

"He rolled?"

"Over! Like dis!"

Olivia rolled across the rug to show her, and back.

Mama turned Theo onto his tummy again, and they waited.

Theo sucked his fist.

He did not do it again.

"I missed it," said Mama. "You saw it."

Olivia lay back down beside him, to wait.
