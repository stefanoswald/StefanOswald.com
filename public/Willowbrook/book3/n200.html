Night 200

Two Hundred Nights of Book Three

Month 7: Potty Power

"Two hundred," said Papa,

and held up the book.

Olivia looked at it.

"Two hunnerd?"

She held up all her fingers.

"Dis many?"

"More," said Papa.

He showed her.

The pages they had read made a fat stack under his thumb.

The pages still to come were a thinner one.

Olivia pinched the fat side.

"All dese?"

"One every night since your birthday," said Mama.

Papa let the pages fall back, slowly,

and stopped at one.

"Theo came here."

Olivia put her finger in that page and kept it there.

Down the hall, Theo was asleep in his bassinet

by Mama and Papa's bed.

Papa read tonight's.

"Next one," said Olivia.

"Tomorrow night," said Papa.

She fell asleep with her finger still in Theo's page.
