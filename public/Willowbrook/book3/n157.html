Night 157

Teaching Him Peekaboo

Month 6: Big Sister School

Olivia had a burp cloth

and a plan.

Cloth up.

Cloth down.

"PEEKABOO!"

Theo flung out both arms.

His face crumpled.

He cried.

"Big voice," said Mama from the couch.

"He's still new at loud."

Mama came and patted his tummy

until he settled.

Olivia looked at the cloth.

Cloth up.

She waited behind it.

Cloth down.

"Peekaboo," she said, small.

Theo stared at her.

Cloth up.

Wait.

Cloth down.

"Peekaboo."

His mouth went wide.

There it was.

"Again," whispered Olivia.

Cloth up.
