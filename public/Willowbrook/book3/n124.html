Night 124

The Green Pinch

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Theo was eating again.

Theo was always eating.

Olivia stood across the room with Bobby Bear

and watched Mama's arms be full.

Something pinched inside her.

Her arms crossed. Her lip went out.

"Mama. Put him DOWN."

"He's eating, love."

"Put him down NOW!"

She stamped. Bobby Bear fell on the floor.

Mama did not scold, and she did not put him down.

She held out her free arm.

"Come here. There's a spot."

Olivia did not come.

Then she came, slowly, and leaned on Mama's knee.

Then she was in the spot, squashed against Mama's side,

and she cried a little, and Mama waited.

"You wanted me all to yourself," said Mama, after.

"That pinchy feeling is called jealous."

"Jell-us."

"Some people call it the green pinch."

"Green pinch," said Olivia, into Mama's sleeve.

Theo kept eating. He had no idea.

After a while Olivia climbed down and fetched Bobby Bear.

Then she climbed back into the spot.
