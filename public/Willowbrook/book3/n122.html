Night 122

The Finger Grab, Part Two

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Theo came home on a hot afternoon.

He lay in Mama's lap on the couch, under the fan.

Olivia climbed up beside them.

She knew what to do. She had done it once before,

with Lily's baby cousin.

One finger. Slow. Gentle.

She laid it in his palm.

The tiny fist closed.

Tight.

"He catched me!"

"He did," said Mama.

Every new baby comes with that grip.

Nobody had told Olivia, and she would not have believed it.

After a while she tried to take her finger back.

Theo held on.

She tried again.

He held on harder.

"Me stuck."

"Looks like it," said Mama.

So Olivia stayed stuck.

Her arm got tired. She propped it on a cushion.

The fan went round.

Then Theo's mouth opened wide, like a bird's,

and his hand let go all at once.

"Milk time," said Mama.

Olivia looked at her finger.

Then she put that finger in her pocket.
