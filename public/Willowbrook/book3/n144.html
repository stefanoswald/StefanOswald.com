Night 144

The Song Works on Him

Month 5: Theo Arrives

The fussy hour came.

Papa walked Theo up and down the yellow room.

Sway. Shush. Pat.

Theo fussed.

"Sing," said Olivia from the doorway, in her pajamas.

"Da song."

So Papa sang.

"Twinkle, twinkle, little sock."

"STAR!"

"Little car?"

"STAR, Papa!"

Theo fussed louder. He did not care for it either.

"Me sing."

She stood right by Papa's knee and sang up at Theo.

"Twinkle, twinkle, wittle star."

Theo stopped.

His eyes went looking for her voice.

She sang all of it, with some of the words

and most of the tune.

Theo's eyes got heavy.

"Again?" whispered Papa.

She sang it again, softer.

Papa carried Theo to his bassinet.

Olivia followed, still singing under her breath.
