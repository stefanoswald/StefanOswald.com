Night 270

The Number Hunt

Month 9: Counting to Five

Mama showed her that numbers are written on things.

On mailboxes. On doors. On the bus stop sign.

After that, every number on Maple Lane was a five.

The mailbox: "Five!"

"That's a two," said Mama.

"Five."

The bus stop: "Five!"

"That's a three."

"Five," said Olivia, kindly.

Two porches down, Mateo was on his step.

"How old you?"

Mateo held up four fingers. It took two tries.

"Almost four."

"Dat five," said Olivia.

"FOUR."

"Five."

Mateo went inside to get his mother.

At their own door, Mama lifted her up to the brass number.

"This one is ours. Seven."

"Seben."

Olivia patted it. It was cold.

She patted it goodnight on the way in.
