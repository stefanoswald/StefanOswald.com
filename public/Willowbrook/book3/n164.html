Night 164

The High Shelf

Month 6: Big Sister School

"One day," said Mama, "Theo will want your things."

Olivia looked at Theo on his mat.

She ran and got her sparkly rock,

the pine scrap from Grandpa's workshop,

and her three best blocks,

and piled them in Papa's lap.

"Hide."

"He can't even roll over," said Papa.

"Later he can."

Papa looked at the little rock.

It was just the size of a baby's mouth.

"Later he can," he said.

On Saturday Papa got out the drill.

Theo, who had never met his own feet,

watched the whole thing from the mat.

Papa put the shelf just where Olivia could reach,

and not one inch lower.

Up went the rock,

the pine scrap,

and the blocks, in a row.

"Up dere mine," said Olivia.

"Down here Fee-oh's."

She looked at the rattle.

"And ours," she said, after a minute.

She lay down on the mat next to Theo,

and they both looked up at the shelf.
