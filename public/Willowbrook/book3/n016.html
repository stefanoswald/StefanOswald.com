Night 16

Where Does Mad Go?

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

This morning there was a big mad.

The boots were wrong.

Both of them, one at a time.

It boomed for a long time.

And now, look.

Gone.

Olivia is on the rug with her cup, calm as the pond.

Boots on. The right feet.

"Mama?" she says. "Where mad go?"

Mama thinks about it.

"Same place rain goes. It rains out."

"Where?"

"Through you, and away. You're still here."

Olivia checks.

She looks under the couch.

Not there.

She looks behind the curtain.

Only window.

Under the couch, the cat is asleep on a mitten.

That is not where the mad went.

The cat would say so, if asked.
