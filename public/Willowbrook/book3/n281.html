Night 281

Harry and the Bubble

Month 10: Try Again

Olivia blew bubbles on the back porch,

though it was so cold the snow squeaked.

Mama watched through the kitchen door, where it was warm.

Out from under the porch came Harry.

Hedgehogs sleep through winter.

Harry wakes up now and then to check on things.

Harry loves bubbles.

Bubbles do not love Harry.

Pop, on his prickles.

Pop, on his leaf.

Pop.

"Harry," said Olivia. "Sit. Still."

Harry sat.

Olivia blew one slow bubble.

It floated down onto the porch rail

and did not pop.

It went cloudy, then sparkly,

like frost on a window.

"Frozen!" said Olivia.

She blew another.

It drifted down, down,

and landed on Harry's nose.

It froze there.

Harry went cross-eyed looking at it.

Nobody moved.

Then, very softly, it cracked into sparkles.
