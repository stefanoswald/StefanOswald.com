Night 251

The Calm-Down Five

Month 9: Counting to Five

The mitten would not go on.

The thumb went in the wrong hole,

then no hole, then out.

"NO MITTEN!"

She threw it, and threw herself down after it,

and the storm came all at once.

Mama sat down on the floor, near.

"I'm here."

She did not ask anything. She waited.

The loud went on, and then it got smaller.

Then it was crying with hiccups,

and Olivia crawled into her corner,

and Mama came too.

"That mitten made you so mad," said Mama.

"Fumb hid."

Mama took Olivia's hand.

She squeezed the thumb, gently, and let go. "One."

The pointer. Squeeze. "Two."

"Free," said Olivia, for the tall one.

Four. Five.

Her hand lay soft in Mama's.

"Again. Me do it."

She squeezed her own fingers, all five.

The mitten was waiting on the mat.

Mama held it open, wide.

Olivia pushed her thumb in.

It went.
