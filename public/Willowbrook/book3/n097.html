Night 97

The Names Game

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

At dinner the grown-ups tried out names for the baby. They said each one slowly, the way you taste soup.

Olivia had names too.

"Duck."

"Duck," said Papa, nodding. "Strong name."

"Pancake."

"Also strong."

"Wiwi Two."

"One Lily is plenty," said Mama.

Then Papa had an idea. "What about Broccoli?"

"No! Dat a food!"

"Spoon?"

"Dat a SPOON!"

"Grandpa?"

"Dat GAMPA!"

She laughed so hard she slid right under the table, and Papa had to fish her out.

There was one name Mama and Papa kept saying to each other, softer than the rest. They did not tell anyone yet.

At bedtime Papa wrote the list on the fridge. At the bottom, very small: Duck.
