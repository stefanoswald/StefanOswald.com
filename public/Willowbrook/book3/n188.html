Night 188

The Bathroom Tour of Willowbrook

Month 7: Potty Power

That Saturday, every door on Maple Lane

got the same question.

"Potty here?"

"We got one!" Mateo called from his porch.

The bakery had one,

with a muffin painted on the door.

Olivia sat for a moment.

"Fawse awarm."

She had heard Mama say that.

The shoe shop had one behind a curtain,

with a soap shaped like a lemon.

Olivia sat.

Nothing.

She washed her hands twice anyway.

At the park, the bathroom door was locked.

Olivia pulled.

She pulled harder.

"Door stuck. NOW."

"Then we go by the library," said Mama.

Across the grass they went,

Olivia bouncing on Mama's hip,

up the round steps,

in, and just in time.

The library dryer was so loud

it blew her hair straight up.

At supper Papa said, "Which potty was best?"

Olivia held her hands up to his nose.

"Lemon."
