Night 351

The Aunts Arrive with the Sweater

Month 12: Almost Three

The aunts' car came up the lane through the slush

and honked twice, the way it always does.

"NAN-NANS!"

Olivia ran down the hall.

The aunts came in stamping snow off their boots,

and bent down both at once for her.

The older aunt had a soft parcel.

Inside was a red sweater with three gold stars across the front.

"One for every year," she said.

Olivia touched them.

"One. Two. FREE."

The other aunt had a parcel for Theo.

A little green sweater with no stars on it at all.

"His first star comes in summer," she said.

She lifted Theo up high to say hello.

Theo said hello from the other end.

Everybody heard it.

"He's glad you came," said Mama.

Olivia wore the sweater to supper, and to bed.

In the kitchen, the aunts' needles went click, click,

long after the lamps were out.
