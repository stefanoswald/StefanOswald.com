Night 267

This Little Piggy, Second Generation

Month 9: Counting to Five

After his bath, Theo lay on a towel on the floor of the yellow room

with bare toes, while Papa, at the drawer, found the pajamas.

"Me do piggies."

Olivia took his foot.

"Dis piggy market."

Theo watched her.

"Dis piggy home."

"Dis piggy..." She forgot. "Crackers."

"Dis piggy none."

She kissed that one, because it had none.

"And dis piggy..."

She waited. Theo waited. His whole face waited.

"Wee wee wee!"

Her fingers ran all the way home, up to his tummy.

Theo laughed so hard he hiccuped.

Wee is also a potty word. Olivia had to lie down.

When she sat up, Theo had his foot in his hands.

He pulled it to his mouth

and put the market piggy in.

"Fee-oh! Dat my piggy!"

He was still eating it when Papa came with the pajamas.
