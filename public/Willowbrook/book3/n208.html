Night 208

The Sunday Suit Question

Month 7: Potty Power

Sunday was farm day,

and Olivia wanted the dress.

The twirly one, from the aunts.

The farm had mud.

The farm had hens.

After all the rain, the farm had a puddle by the barn

nearly as big as the duck pond.

"Dress!"

Mama didn't say no.

"The dress over play clothes?" she said.

"Or the dress for supper after?"

Olivia thought.

"Dress. Boots. Old shirt."

She pointed out the window at the car.

"Mud? Dress dere."

Mama considered this.

It was a better plan than hers.

So Olivia went to the farm twirling,

in red boots, with her oldest shirt underneath.

At the barn puddle, the plan went into action.

Off came the dress.

Into the car it went.

Into the puddle went Olivia.

After supper, clean again,

she put the dress back on

and twirled once, for Grandpa.
