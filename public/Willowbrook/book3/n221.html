Night 221

Theo's First Storm

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Theo had had enough of everything.

He fussed. He fussed louder.

Then his face went red, his fists went tight,

and the whole of him was crying.

"Fee-oh storm," said Olivia.

She brought him her squeeze pillow.

Theo did not squeeze it.

She brought the pinwheel and blew it at him.

Theo cried at the pinwheel.

"Those are for you," said Papa. "He needs walking."

So Papa walked.

Up the hall. Down the hall.

Shh, shh, shh.

Olivia marched behind him in her pajamas, one finger on her lips.

"SHHH!" she said. "SHHHH!"

"A smaller shush," said Papa.

"Shh," said Olivia, very small.

Up the hall. Down the hall.

The crying went hiccupy, then slow.

Theo's head went heavy on Papa's shoulder.

Papa laid him in the bassinet, on his back,

and they both leaned over to look.

Olivia tiptoed out first.
