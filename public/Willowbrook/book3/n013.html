Night 13

Frustrated and the Zipper

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The zipper would not zip.

Try. No.

Try. No.

Try. No!

The hot one is back, the banana-day one.

It has a name now.

"Mad," said Olivia, and threw the jacket.

Mama was in a hurry.

She picked the jacket up and put it on Olivia and grabbed the zipper.

"Me do it!"

A wail. A big one.

Mama stopped.

"You wanted to do it," she said. "I grabbed, sorry."

She gave the zipper back and sat down on the floor.

Olivia looked at it. Close.

The little teeth were not lined up.

"Stuck at the bottom," said Mama. That was all she said.

Wiggle. Wiggle.

Pull.

Zzzzzip.

All the way up.

Over her chin. Over her mouth.

"Too far," said Olivia, through the jacket.

She wore it like that to the park.
