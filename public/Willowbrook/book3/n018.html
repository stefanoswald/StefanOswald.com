Night 18

The Feeling Check at Dinner

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

A new thing at dinner.

One feeling each, from the day.

Then the bread.

Papa goes first.

"Mad. The car wouldn't start."

Nobody fixes it. Everybody nods.

Mama.

"Happy. The crocuses are up by the step."

Nods.

Olivia.

The table waits.

The whole day goes past her, the puddle, the crash, the goodbye.

"Happy," she says. "Sad."

"Wiwi goed home."

Nods, all round.

Nobody says but at least.

Nobody says it's okay.

Papa passes the bread.

That is the whole game.

One feeling. Nods. Bread.

Olivia takes two rolls.
