Night 296

The Braid Lesson

Month 10: Try Again

Thursday at Rosa's was bread day.

Snow on the window. The oven warm.

Flour on Rosa's cheek, as usual.

Rosa's hands went over, under, over,

and a braid fell out of her fingers

like it was nothing.

"Now you two."

Three dough ropes each.

Over. Under.

Which one was under?

Lily somehow had four ropes.

Olivia had one long knot.

"Dat not a braid."

"That's a knot," said Rosa.

"Everybody's first one is a knot."

They rolled the ropes out again.

Rosa put her hands over theirs.

Over, under, over.

Slow.

A pattern came.

Fat, then thin, then fat, but a pattern.

"Dat a braid!" said Lily.

"A lumpy braid," said Olivia.

"Lumpy braids bake up the same," said Rosa.

She slid them into the oven beside her smooth ones.

"This is the last loaf today," said Rosa,

and rolled out another.

Both girls watched the oven light from the table.
