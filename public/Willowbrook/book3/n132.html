Night 132

Lily Meets the Baby

Month 5: Theo Arrives

On Thursday, Lily came to see the baby at last.

Olivia met her at the door.

"Wash hands."

Lily washed.

"Sit couch."

Lily sat.

"Pillow."

Olivia put a pillow on Lily's lap and patted it flat.

Then Mama laid Theo on the pillow

and kept both hands right under him.

Lily looked down.

Theo looked up, sort of.

"He so small," said Lily.

"He get bigger," said Olivia.

Then Theo hiccupped.

"Hic."

Lily's eyes went round. "Hic?"

"Hic," said Theo.

"HIC!" said both girls, and fell against each other laughing.

Theo went on hiccupping. He did not mind.

"I help?" said Lily.

"You can pat him," said Mama. "One finger, soft."

Lily patted. Theo hiccupped.

She patted again, right on the hic.

Hic, pat. Hic, pat.

Olivia patted the pillow, to help.

When the hiccups were done, Mama lifted Theo back into her arms.

Lily kept her hands in the pillow shape a while longer.
