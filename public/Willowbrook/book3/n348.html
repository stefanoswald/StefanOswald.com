Night 348

What Three Can Do

Month 12: Almost Three

"What free do?" Olivia asked at bedtime.

"Dat two can't?"

Mama thought about it.

"Three can pedal to the corner, with Papa walking."

"Free can drive da car?"

"No."

"Free can fly?"

"No."

"Free stay up all night?"

"Nice try."

"Three can go on the big swing sometimes, with a grown-up."

"And?"

"Three can sleep over at the farm."

"All by me?"

"All by you. Grandpa keeps asking."

"And at the end of summer, three can go to the room with the cubbies."

"Wiwi too?"

"Lily too."

Olivia lay still and thought about all of it.

"Free is big."

"Three is big," said Mama.

Olivia put three fingers on the pillow,

ready.
