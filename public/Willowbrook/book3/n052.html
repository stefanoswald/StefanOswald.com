Night 52

The Both-Ways Day

Month 2: Me Do It

In the morning it was "Me do it" for everything.

The coat, flipped. The milk, poured.

The sandwich, pressed flat by her.

The bed, climbed on and off and on.

The stairs, one hand on the rail, Mama one step behind.

By dusk she was smaller.

The day had used her up.

At the bottom of the stairs she stopped

and put both arms up.

"Carry you."

She means carry me.

The words swap over when she is tired.

Mama knows which one she means.

So Mama carried her.

Up the stairs, slow, with a sway on every step,

the way she did when Olivia was a baby

and there was nothing else to do with her.

Olivia's head went onto her shoulder.

It stayed.

"Big in the morning," said Mama, into her hair.

"Small at night."

The sway went on a while past the top of the stairs.
