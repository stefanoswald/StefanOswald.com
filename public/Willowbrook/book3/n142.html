Night 142

Spaghetti Night, Party of Six

Month 5: Theo Arrives

Spaghetti night is the first Friday of every month.

On the first Friday of this month,

nobody in Olivia's house knew what day it was.

So Rosa moved it.

She brought it over, pot and all, with Lily.

Six at the table now.

Theo slept in his bassinet by Papa's chair,

on his back, fists up by his ears.

The noodles went round.

Lily and Olivia slurped theirs at the same time, on purpose.

Sauce went up to the eyebrows.

"Remember?" said Lily. "Da carts?"

"CRASH!" said Olivia.

"And den spaghetti."

"And den..." Olivia leaned over and looked in the bassinet.

"Fee-oh," she whispered.

"This is the last meatball," said Rosa.

It was not. There were four more.

Theo slept through the meatballs,

and the slurping,

and Papa's second helping.

At the door, Rosa looked back.

"Six," she said. "I'll need a bigger pot."

She carried the empty one home.
