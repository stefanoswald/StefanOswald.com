Night 236

When Mama's Weather Comes

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

A hard afternoon for Mama.

The phone rang twice. The washing machine made a new noise.

Theo's nap was over almost before it started.

Mama put a pot down on the counter. Clang.

Olivia looked up from the floor.

"Mama hot?"

Mama went still.

"Yes," she said. "I'm getting hot inside."

She put both hands on the edge of the sink and closed her eyes.

She breathed in, slow, like smelling a flower.

She breathed out, slow, like blowing a dandelion.

"I need one minute on the porch," said Mama. "I'm not mad at you."

Mama stood out on the back porch with her hands in her cardigan pockets.

Olivia watched her through the glass.

A few leaves blew across the yard.

Mama watched them go.

Then she came back in.

"Better?" said Olivia.

"Better," said Mama.

The washing machine made its noise again.

They both looked at it.

It went on making its noise.
