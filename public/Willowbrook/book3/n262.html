Night 262

Molly's Five-Book Rule

Month 9: Counting to Five

Just-us afternoon. Theo stayed home with Papa.

At the Lantern Library, a small card gets five books.

Olivia picked.

A tractor one.

A duck one.

One about the dark, because it comes so early now.

One she chose by smelling it. It smelled old. That was the reason.

And a counting one.

Then she picked up one more, with a bear on it.

"Five on a small card," said the librarian.

Olivia held the bear book tight to her coat.

"Mine too."

Her lip went.

"You want them all," said Mama. "Pick one to come back for."

From a gap by the low shelf came a small voice.

"It's my rule, actually," said Molly Mouse. "I'll keep that one warm for you."

Olivia put the bear book on the low shelf, by the gap.

Stamp, stamp, stamp, stamp, stamp.

At the door she looked back.

Molly was reading it.
