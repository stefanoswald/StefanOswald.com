Night 126

Reading to Theo, In Person

Month 5: Theo Arrives

All spring, Olivia read to the bump.

Now the bump had a face.

Afternoon in the yellow room.

Theo lay on his back on the play blanket, awake.

Mama folded tiny shirts in the rocking chair.

Olivia lay on her tummy beside him with the bear book

and turned the pages one at a time.

"Dis bear. He lost button."

Theo looked at the ceiling.

"LOOK, Fee-oh. Button gone."

Theo looked at her.

Not at the book. At her.

She moved the book closer. He looked at her.

She moved it farther. He looked at her.

"He no like bear?"

"Babies like faces best," said Mama. "Yours especially."

Olivia thought.

Then she held the book up against her cheek,

her face and the bear's face side by side.

"Dere. Bofe."

Theo blinked at the two of them.

So Olivia read it again, from the beginning,

with the bear by her ear.

Mama had stopped folding.
