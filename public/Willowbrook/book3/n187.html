Night 187

The Tiny Underpants

Month 7: Potty Power

After a week of dry mornings,

Olivia pointed at the laundry basket.

"Me pants. Like Mama."

So they walked to the shop on Maple Lane.

There were stripes.

There were stars.

There were ducks.

"DUCKS."

She took one pair of stars too.

"For party."

She carried the bag home in both arms,

the way you carry a cake.

"Tonight?"

"Not at night yet," said Mama.

"Night comes on its own time."

In the morning Olivia put on the ducks herself.

One leg in.

The other leg in.

The first leg out again.

Mama helped with that part.

They felt light, like nothing on.

She checked them twice before breakfast.

Once that day, she got there in time.

Once, she didn't.

"Ducks wet," she said.

Mama brought the stars.

It was not a party.

Olivia wore them anyway.

The ducks went in the wash.
