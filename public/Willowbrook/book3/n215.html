Night 215

The Zipper

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Jackets on. Boots on. Out to the park.

Mama zipped Olivia's jacket, quick, all the way up.

"ME do it!"

Too late. It was done.

Olivia pulled the jacket off and threw it on the mat.

Then she threw herself down beside it, loud and red.

Mama sat on the bottom stair, close enough to touch,

and said nothing at all about zippers.

It was a short storm.

When it was over, Olivia climbed onto the stair

and into Mama's lap, still sniffing.

"You wanted to zip it," said Mama. "I'm sorry I did it for you."

Olivia put a hand on her chest.

"Hot. Here."

"Hot in there," said Mama. "Like soup."

Olivia nodded.

"Do-over?" said Mama.

She fitted the zipper together at the bottom, the tricky part,

and held it still.

Olivia pulled.

Zzzzip, all the way to her chin.
