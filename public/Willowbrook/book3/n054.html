Night 54

Grandpa's Workshop Rules

Month 2: Me Do It

Sunday at the farm. The workshop door is open.

Olivia is allowed in today.

Grandpa's workshop has two rules.

He says them slow.

"Ask before touching."

"Everything back where it lives."

The sharp things live high. The rules live low.

Her spot is the low bench.

Her tools, one sanding block and one scrap of pine.

Her job, make it smooth.

Shff. Shff.

Grandpa works beside her.

His shff is bigger. Same song.

She points at the small hammer on its nail.

He shakes his head, once.

She goes back to the pine.

A long time, the two of them,

the dust going gold in the door light,

the Duchess looking in from the yard

and thinking better of it.

At the end she holds up the scrap.

He runs his thumb over it. Nods.

"Reckon so."

The sanding block goes back where it lives.

The pine goes home in her pocket.
