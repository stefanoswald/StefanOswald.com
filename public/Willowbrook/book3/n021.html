Night 21

The Worry About the Dark Corner

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Bedtime.

Olivia points.

"Dere."

The corner. By the dresser. Where the shadow is.

"What's in the corner?" says Papa.

She does not have a word.

Just something.

Papa does not say nothing's there.

"Let's go see," he says.

He brings the lamp over.

The corner lights up.

There is the dresser.

There is the wall.

There is one sock.

"Sock!" says Olivia.

"Just a sock," says Papa.

They check behind the dresser too.

Dust. No something.

The lamp goes back.

The shadow comes back, the same shape.

Now it is the dresser's shadow, and it has a sock in it.

The next night she points again.

"Dere."

Papa brings the lamp again.
