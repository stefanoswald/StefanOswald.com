Night 104

When the Baby Comes, What Stays

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Bedtime. The lamp. Mama sat on the edge of the big bed and started a game.

"When the baby comes, whose bed is this?"

"Mine."

"Whose bear?"

Olivia squeezed him. "Mine."

"Whose Thursdays with Lily?"

"MINE."

"Whose Mama?"

A pause. Then, from under the sheet, smaller: "Mine?"

"Yours," said Mama. "Still yours."

Olivia came out from under the sheet.

"What change?"

Mama gave it real thought. "It gets louder. And some days I'll be busy with the baby."

"Me want you?"

"Then you tell me, and I'll come."

Olivia turned that over for a while. "Loud baby."

"Very loud," said Mama. "Like a small trumpet."

Olivia did the trumpet. Mama did the trumpet. Then they were quiet.

She tucked Bobby Bear under her chin.
