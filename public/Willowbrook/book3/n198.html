Night 198

Theo Pushes Up

Month 7: Potty Power

In the middle of all the potty news,

there was other news.

Mama put Theo on his tummy on the blanket.

Olivia lay down on her tummy too,

nose to nose.

"Up, Fee-oh."

Theo pushed.

His elbows went under him.

His chest came up off the blanket,

and his head came up,

and stayed up,

wobbling only a little.

He looked right at her.

"Mama! Fee-oh UP!"

"He's pushing with his arms," said Mama.

Olivia pushed up on her arms too.

Two seals on a rug.

Then Theo's arms shook,

and down he went, nose first,

into the blanket.

He fussed.

He pushed.

Up again, not quite as far.

Olivia stayed up with him

till her own arms shook.

Then Theo put his cheek on the blanket

and looked at her sideways.
