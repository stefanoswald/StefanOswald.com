Night 148

The Cow Takes the Watch

Month 5: Theo Arrives

At bedtime Olivia went to say goodnight to Theo.

He was asleep on his back in his bassinet, by Mama and Papa's bed.

She looked at him.

She looked at Bobby Bear.

Very carefully, she tucked Bobby Bear in beside him.

"Baba watch."

Papa lifted Bobby Bear out again, gently.

"Nothing soft goes in with Theo until he's past one. That keeps his bed safe."

Olivia held Bobby Bear tight.

"Fee-oh need guard."

"He does," said Papa. "Where could a guard go?"

Olivia thought.

Then she ran to the yellow room.

She came back slowly, with the cow from the fair,

holding its bell in her fist so it would not ring.

Papa lifted her up to the dresser.

She stood the cow facing the bassinet.

Ding, went the bell, very small.

Theo slept on.

"Cow watch Fee-oh."

"It can see everything from there," said Papa.

The cow kept its eyes on the bassinet.
