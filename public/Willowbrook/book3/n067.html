Night 67

The Standing Playdate

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

After spaghetti night the mamas made a plan.

Thursdays. Every Thursday, one house and then the other.

The mamas had shared a bench for two years.

Thursdays would belong to the girls.

On the first one, Olivia was at the window straight after her nap.

"Wiwi come?"

"In a minute," said Mama.

The bush by the gate nodded.

A bird went by. A truck went by.

"Wiwi come?"

"In a minute."

The clock over the stove went through forty of them.

Mama's minutes are like that.

Then the bell.

"WIWI!"

"LIVY!"

They ran at each other and hugged so hard they sat down.

It had been two whole days.

Then they were gone, down the hall to the blocks,

and two mamas stood at the open door

with nobody left to say hello to.
