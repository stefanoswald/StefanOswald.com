Night 158

The Two-Kid Bath

Month 6: Big Sister School

Two baths at once, tonight.

Olivia in the big tub.

Theo in his little tub on the floor beside it.

Papa on his knees in between,

one hand under Theo's head.

That hand stayed there. Every second.

"Row, row, row your goat," sang Papa.

"BOAT!"

"Row, row, row your coat."

"BOAT, Papa!"

Papa washed the creases in Theo's neck,

where the milk hides.

"Cheese in dere," said Olivia.

Olivia gave the water a slap.

Splash. Right on Papa's glasses.

Theo's arms flew out.

"Small splash," said Papa. "Theo size."

Pat. Pat.

Theo kicked in his tub.

Olivia pulled the plug, her job.

Glug, went the drain.

Then two towels, two hoods,

one big pink girl and one small pink boy,

side by side on the bath mat.

"Same time tomorrow?"

"Same time," said Papa.
