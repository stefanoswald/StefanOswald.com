Night 356

Papa's Letter at Three

Month 12: Almost Three

The next night, it was Papa's turn at the table.

He chewed the pen for a while first.

Dear Olivia,

You are almost three. I have checked the calendar twice.

This year you whistled. You tried from the lilacs to the snow, and most of it was spit. Then one Tuesday it wasn't.

You fixed my songs every time I sang them wrong. I did it on purpose. You knew.

Your brother's first word was your name. Mama says it was mostly "li-li." We have decided it counts. I lost the bet, so I did the dishes, and I would lose it again.

I look in on you every night before I go to bed. I am not going to stop.

Love,

Papa

He licked the envelope

and made a face.
