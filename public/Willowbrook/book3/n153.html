Night 153

Gentle Hands

Month 6: Big Sister School

Theo lay on his blanket, kicking.

Olivia wanted to touch all of him.

"The top of his head is soft," said Mama.

"One finger. Barely."

One finger. Barely.

Theo blinked.

"His feet can have a pat."

Pat. Pat.

Theo kicked, which is a yes.

Then Olivia leaned in close,

and a curl fell down,

and Theo's fist found it and shut.

"OW! Fee-oh! Let GO!"

He did not let go.

New fists do not know how.

"He doesn't know he's got it," said Mama.

"Open his fingers, one at a time."

Olivia opened one finger.

Then the next.

Then the first one closed again.

"Fee-oh!"

Mama helped with the last two.

The curl came free.

Olivia rubbed her head.

She looked at his fist for a while.

Then she put her own finger in it instead.
