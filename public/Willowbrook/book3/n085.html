Night 85

The Friend Who Isn't Here Yet

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Bedtime, and the sky was still light.

Olivia had things to tell Bobby Bear.

"Baba. Shh."

Things were different at her house.

Mama's lap was getting smaller.

There was a big round tummy in the way.

"Mama tummy big."

Mama napped in the afternoon now, like a baby.

Papa had painted the little room by the stairs

the color of butter.

And in the laundry basket there were socks.

Tiny ones. Too small for anybody.

Olivia had one.

She put it on the bear's foot,

the one with the patchwork heart.

It fit.

"Baba sock."

Mama came in to say goodnight.

She sat down on the bed, slowly,

the way she sits now.

She saw the sock.

Her face went soft,

and she didn't say anything about it at all.

"Goodnight, Olivia."

"Nigh-nigh."

The bear slept in one tiny sock.
