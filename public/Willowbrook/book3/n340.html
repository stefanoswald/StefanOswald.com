Night 340

The Remember Game: The Tools Year

Month 12: Almost Three

Olivia told part three standing up on the bed.

It was the stormy part.

"Hot here," she said, hand on her chest.

"Den me go cozy corner."

"Behind the big chair," said Mama.

"With the pillow that sighs."

"And da roar rock. ROAR."

"It's bedtime," said Papa. "Roar small."

She roared small.

"Wet pants, dry pants," said Mama.

"All done!"

"One, two, free."

"And when the puzzle was too hard?"

"Yet."

"And the Duchess," said Papa.

"She gave me fevver."

"She did. That hen doesn't give anybody anything."

Olivia climbed back under the blanket.

"Da whole year?"

"The whole year," said Mama.

"You and a lot of weather."

Papa turned off the lamp.

In the dark, very quietly, somebody roared.
