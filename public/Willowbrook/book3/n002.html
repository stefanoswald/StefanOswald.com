Night 2

Happy Lives in the Whole Body

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Ding-dong!

Olivia runs to the window.

Boots on the step. Blue jacket.

"Wiwi!"

Here comes the golden one.

Look where it lives.

In the feet. They bounce.

In the hands. They clap.

In the cheeks. Up they go.

The door opens.

Lily has mud on both knees already.

Two runs, one hug, and both of them sit down hard.

"You're happy," says Mama from the kitchen.

"Happy," says Olivia.

It is the one word she has for it,

and today it fits everywhere.

Lily's boots come off.

One of them goes onto Olivia's foot.

It is the wrong foot.

It stays on.
