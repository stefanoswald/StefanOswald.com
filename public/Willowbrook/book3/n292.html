Night 292

The Try-Again Museum

Month 10: Try Again

Olivia cleared the low shelf by the window.

Not all of it. The sock stayed.

It had been there since March.

"Museum," she said.

In went the puzzle box.

In went the trike bell.

Papa had to unscrew it,

and promise to screw it back.

In went the kite, folded small.

In went the folder of ducks.

In went a bent bracket from the shelf that fell.

"Why the bracket?" said Papa.

"Dat one fell down."

Papa nodded.

Mama came to look.

Papa reached for the bell.

"Rule one," said Olivia. "No touching."

"Not even me?"

"Specially you."

Papa put his hands in his pockets.

Mama looked at everything twice, slowly,

the way people do in a museum.

That night the museum was closed.

The sock stood guard.
