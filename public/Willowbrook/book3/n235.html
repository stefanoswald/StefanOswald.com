Night 235

In a Minute

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Mama was putting away laundry in Olivia's room.

Olivia had Duck on her head.

"Mama! Look!"

"In a minute," said Mama, matching socks.

Olivia walked three steps. Duck fell off.

She put him back.

"Mama. LOOK."

"In a minute, love."

Out in the hall, Papa looked at his watch.

"MAMA!"

Olivia kicked the laundry basket.

Socks went everywhere.

Mama stopped.

She looked at the socks, and then at Olivia's red face.

She put the laundry down and knelt by the basket.

"I kept saying in a minute. That was a long minute."

"Long," said Olivia.

"I'm sorry. I'm looking now."

"Eleven minutes," said Papa, from the hall.

Olivia put Duck back on her head.

She walked all the way across the room,

slow, slow, arms out,

and Duck did not fall off once.

Mama watched every step.
