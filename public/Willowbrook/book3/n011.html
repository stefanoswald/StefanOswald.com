Night 11

Proud Stands Tall

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Five blocks high.

Five, all by herself.

The big one on the bottom, the small one on top.

Look what happened to Olivia.

Her chest puffed out.

Her chin went up.

Her hands landed on her hips.

She stood like a statue of her own self.

"Papa! Look!"

Papa looked. He really looked.

"You did that," he said. "All you."

"Me did it."

"You look proud," said Papa.

The tower wobbled.

Papa held his breath.

It stayed.

Olivia stood tall beside it.

She stood tall for a long time.

Papa's coffee went cold.

Then, crash.

She did that too.

"Again," she said, and reached for the big one.
