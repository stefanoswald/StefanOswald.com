Night 263

Five in the Family

Month 9: Counting to Five

At supper, Olivia counted the family.

"Mama. One."

"Papa. Two."

"Me. Free."

Theo was on Papa's arm, reaching for Papa's glasses.

"Fee-oh. Four."

Then she climbed down and ran for Bobby Bear,

and gave him the empty chair.

"Baba. Five."

Papa gave Bobby Bear a long, serious look.

"Five," he said. "He's been here longer than Theo."

"Five," said Olivia, and held up the whole hand.

Then the cat jumped up on the chair by the window.

Olivia looked at the cat.

She looked at her hand.

"Uh oh," she said. "Lots."

Theo got the glasses.

The cat washed one paw and stayed.
