Night 127

The Town Meets Theo

Month 5: Theo Arrives

The whole town wanted to see Theo.

At the door stood Olivia.

Rosa came first, with a warm loaf.

"Wash hands," said Olivia.

Rosa washed.

"Sit down."

Rosa sat.

"Hold head."

Mama laid Theo in her arms, and Rosa held his head.

Mrs. Henderson came with tomatoes,

because tomatoes are what Mrs. Henderson brings.

She washed without being told.

Olivia told her anyway.

On Sunday the truck came, and Grandpa Joe with it.

Hat off at the door. Hands at the sink.

Olivia watched every finger.

Then Grandpa sat, and Mama set Theo in his big brown hands.

Grandpa looked him over, slowly.

"Back in my day," he said, "babies came bigger. Size of a calf."

"No!"

"Size of a tractor."

"NO, Gampa!"

Theo sneezed.

Grandpa jumped. Theo's arms flew out.

Olivia laughed so hard she sat down on the floor.

Grandpa did not give him back for an hour.
