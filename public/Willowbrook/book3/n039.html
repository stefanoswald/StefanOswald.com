Night 39

The Help That Isn't Helping

Month 2: Me Do It

Mrs. Henderson came across the street with a cake

and stayed for tea, and watched the puzzle.

The star piece. The hard one.

Turn. No. Turn. No. Turn.

Mrs. Henderson is kind, and she could not stand it.

She leaned over and popped it in.

"There you go, sweetie."

Nothing happened for one second.

Then Olivia's face went dark, like the sky before rain.

"Me. Do. It."

The star piece came back out.

She threw it, not far. Just down.

"She likes the doing part," said Mama.

Mrs. Henderson put both hands around her tea

and kept them there.

Turn. No. Turn. No.

Turn.

In.

Olivia sat back and looked at Mrs. Henderson,

and Mrs. Henderson said, "Well, look at that,"

and did not touch one more thing

for the rest of the visit,

not even the cake.
