Night 223

Two Storms at Once

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Theo was drooly and cross.

Both fists went in his mouth.

He chewed them and cried around them.

Olivia was hungry and tired,

and her yellow spoon was in the dishwasher.

"YELLOW spoon!"

It was supper time, already dark at the windows.

Two storms. One kitchen.

Mama and Papa looked at each other over the noise.

That look is a whole conversation.

Papa took Theo.

He walked him and swayed him

and gave him the teething ring from the fridge, cold, not frozen.

Theo gummed it hard.

Mama took Olivia, who had gone under the table.

Mama went under the table too.

On the stove, the pasta cooked much too long.

By the time Theo was asleep in his bassinet,

it came to the table soft and brown at the edges.

Mama lit a candle, out of reach. "Fancy," she said.

Olivia ate hers with the blue spoon.

"Fancy," she said.

Theo slept through the whole fancy part.
