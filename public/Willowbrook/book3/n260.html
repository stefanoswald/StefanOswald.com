Night 260

Round the Couch

Month 9: Counting to Five

Cold rain all day. No park.

So Olivia ran round the couch.

Theo lay on his back on the blanket

and turned his head to find her.

There she was. Gone.

There she was!

Every time she came round, he squealed.

"Again?" she asked him.

He kicked both feet, which means yes.

Round. Squeal. Round. Squeal.

Then, while she was behind the couch,

Theo did something new.

He rolled the other way,

back to tummy,

to see where she went.

"Mama! He rolled AFTER me!"

"He wanted to see you," said Mama.

Olivia ran one more lap, slower,

and Theo held his head up and watched her the whole way round.

Then she flopped down on the blanket beside him, puffing.

He reached over

and grabbed a curl.
