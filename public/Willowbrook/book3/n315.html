Night 315

The Gold Dog Slows Down

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Every day, Olivia and the gold dog raced the fence.

His side. Her side.

All the way to the corner.

This week he didn't get up.

He lay by his gate in a square of thin sun,

and his tail went thump, thump.

"Race!" said Olivia. "Come on!"

The gold dog stood up slowly, back end last.

He walked three steps.

He sat down.

"Why him slow?"

"He's old," said Mama. "The cold makes his legs stiff."

"Why old?"

"Lots and lots of birthdays."

Olivia looked at the corner.

She looked at the gold dog.

Then she walked.

One boot.

Wait.

The other boot.

Wait.

The gold dog walked too,

his side, her side,

one post, two posts, three.

At the third post he stopped,

and so did she.

She held her flat mitten to the fence.

He sniffed it the long way, the way old dogs do.

His tail thumped once in the snow.
