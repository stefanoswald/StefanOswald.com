Night 42

The First Night in the Big Bed

Month 2: Me Do It

Bath. Pajamas. Story,

read in the new bed,

which fits three people and a bear,

which the crib never did.

Then Mama stood up.

"Climb in, love."

No lifting. Nobody's hands.

Olivia walked to the edge.

Knee up. In. Under the quilt.

She held out her arms for the bear,

and the bear got lifted. He has not asked to climb.

Across the room the crib stood empty.

Two years of nights in it. Empty now.

"Nigh-nigh, crib," she said.

Papa found something in his eye.

The small battery lamp from the corner

stays on the floor by the bed,

where Papa put it,

in case the corner comes back tonight.

Kiss. Lamp low. Door to its inch.

She stayed in the bed all night.

Nearly.

The floor got her once, softly, around three,

and she slept there too,

half the quilt with her.
