Night 103

The Big Sister Shirt

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

A box came from the aunts up north. Inside, in tissue paper, was a small shirt. On the front, in careful stitches, it said BIG SISTER, and at the shoulder sat one gold star.

Olivia touched each letter.

"Aunties sew dis?"

"Every letter," said Mama. "By hand, in the evenings."

Olivia touched the letters again, slower.

It went on at once, over her pajamas. Then the lane had to see it. From the front door she showed the gold dog, who wagged and could not read and approved anyway. She showed Mateo, two porches down. He showed her a stick.

"Big! Sister!" she told the mailman.

He said he had guessed.

After lunch she was busy with the crayons. Then she held up a big yellow star.

"For aunties."

Mama wrote THANK YOU underneath, and Olivia pressed her whole hand beside it.

She slept in the shirt.
