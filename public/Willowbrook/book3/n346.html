Night 346

Grandpa's Secret Project

Month 12: Almost Three

At the farm, the workshop door was shut.

That door is never shut.

Behind it, zzzt, zzzt went a saw.

Shff, shff went sandpaper.

Then a little squeak, round and round.

Olivia knocked.

Grandpa Joe opened the door a crack.

"Gampa. What in dere?"

"Sawdust."

The door shut.

She put her ear on the door.

Squeak, squeak.

She looked through the knothole.

She saw one suspender.

The Duchess came and stood beside her.

The Duchess wanted in too.

Olivia knocked again.

"Is it a duck?"

The door opened a crack.

"Nope."

The door shut.

"He shutted it again," she told the Duchess.

The Duchess said nothing. She is a hen.

The door opened one more time.

Grandpa Joe held out a long curl of wood,

pale and thin as a ribbon.

He put it in her hand.

He winked.

Olivia carried the curl all the way home,

so it would not come uncurled.
