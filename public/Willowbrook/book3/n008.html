Night 8

Shy Hides Behind Legs

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The first market of spring, on the lane.

Puddles between the stalls.

A new person at the honey table.

A nice one, but new.

"Well, hello there!"

Olivia is gone.

Behind Mama's legs.

One eye out.

Mama does not pull her round.

"She'll say hi when she's ready," says Mama.

Not sorry. Just so.

"Shy looks first."

The honey man nods.

He goes on selling honey.

He has met two-year-olds before.

The eye watches.

The eye becomes half a face.

The half a face becomes a girl, holding a leg.

A small hand comes up.

"Hi."

The honey man waves back with his whole arm.

And Olivia is behind the legs again.

One eye out.
