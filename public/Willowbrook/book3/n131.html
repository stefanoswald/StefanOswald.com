Night 131

The Nap Trade

Month 5: Theo Arrives

It was a hot, still afternoon.

Theo was asleep on his back in his bassinet,

beside Mama and Papa's bed.

Olivia kept watch from the rug, with her blocks.

She built. She yawned.

She built a little slower.

She put her cheek down on the rug,

just for a minute.

Mama came to the doorway with her coffee

and stopped.

Two children. Both asleep. At the same time.

This had never happened before.

Mama thought of all the things she could do.

A shower. A nap. A sandwich, with both hands.

She did none of them.

She leaned on the door frame and watched them breathe,

one small back going up and down, and one smaller.

Her coffee went cold.

It had been cold before, too.

Then Theo squeaked, and Olivia sat up

with a block stuck to her cheek.

Only the cat slept on, in the laundry basket.
