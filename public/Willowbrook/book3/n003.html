Night 3

Sad Is Heavy

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

The red balloon from her birthday

went small in the night.

All by itself.

Now it is wrinkled, and it does not float.

It just sits.

A feeling comes.

Not hot. Heavy.

It makes the shoulders droop.

It makes the bottom stair the only place to sit.

Olivia sits there, holding the balloon.

"My bloon," she says.

Papa sits down next to her.

He does not say it was only a balloon.

"That's sad," he says.

It is.

They sit with it.

The stair is cold.

Papa's arm is not.

After a while Papa says,

"Should we keep the string?"

Olivia nods.

The string goes in her pocket.

The flat balloon goes on the high shelf.

Papa sits one more minute anyway.
