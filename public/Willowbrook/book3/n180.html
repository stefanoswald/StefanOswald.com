Night 180

The Big Sister Badge

Month 6: Big Sister School

A small envelope came from the aunts up north.

Inside was a felt badge with a gold star,

and a note: For the big sister. Tell us everything.

So at bedtime Mama called them

and put the phone on the bed.

"What did Theo do?" said the aunts, both at once.

"Smile. At ME."

"First?"

"First."

"And what did you do?"

Olivia thought.

"Duck."

"She let Theo hold Duck," said Mama. "Bobby Bear stayed hers."

"Peekaboo. Small."

"Small?" said the aunts.

"He was new at loud," said Mama.

"Lady swoop. ASK."

She showed the phone one finger.

"Barely."

"Goo. Me say really."

Mama told them the rest:

the tissues, the bell, the feather by the cow.

Mama pinned the badge on the shirt.

"And the laugh?" said the aunts.

"Not yet," said Mama.

Olivia put both hands on her ears.

"Me saving."

The badge slept on the top shelf.
