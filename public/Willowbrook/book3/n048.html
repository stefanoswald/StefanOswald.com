Night 48

When Me-Do-It Can't

Month 2: Me Do It

Papa's old shoes came out of a box.

Enormous. Wonderful.

She put both feet in and shuffled down the hall

like a very small boat.

Then the laces.

"Me tie it."

She tried. The laces flopped.

She tried. They tangled.

She tried and got a knot,

the wrong kind, the kind that is only a lump.

Her face went hot.

Papa knelt down. He did not take the laces.

"Laces are hard. Hands get there later."

He made one loop, slow. Then the other.

Then he stopped,

and held the two loops out,

and put his other hand under hers,

open, waiting.

"You pull."

She pulled.

Two ears stood up on the shoe. A bow.

A loose one. A lopsided one. A bow.

"Me pull it."

"You did the pull," said Papa. "That part is yours."

She shuffled to the door and back,

the bow flapping,

and held up the other shoe.
