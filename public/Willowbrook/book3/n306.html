Night 306

The Door Hold

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Saturday was the pool, then the market.

Just Olivia and Mama.

The market door is heavy glass.

Mama reached for it.

Olivia got there first.

She leaned her back on it,

boots pushing, both hands flat,

and the door swung open.

"Me holding it!"

An old man with a cane came in out of the snow.

"Thank you, miss."

"Welcome!"

A mama with a stroller.

"Oh, thank you!"

"Welcome!"

A man with a big box of oranges.

"Thanks, door holder."

"Welcome," said Olivia, a little squashed.

Then the grocer's cat strolled in

and said nothing at all.

"Cat! Say fank you!"

The cat went and sat on the potatoes.

"Arms tired?" said Mama.

"Mama go first."

So Mama went in.

Olivia slipped in after her

and let go.

Whump.
