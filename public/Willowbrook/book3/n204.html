Night 204

The Farm Sleepover

Month 7: Potty Power

Olivia packed her own bag for the farm.

Duck pants. A night diaper.

The little seat for big toilets.

When Mama's car went down the lane,

Olivia cried at the gate.

Grandpa Joe stood beside her.

He held out his hand, and she took it,

and they watched the hens go in to roost.

By the time the coop door shut, her crying was done.

At bedtime, the farm bathroom was old.

The pipes groaned.

Olivia stood in the doorway.

She changed her mind.

She changed it back.

Grandpa put her little seat on.

He set a kitchen chair in the hall, by the door,

and sat down on it.

"Here," he said.

The pipes groaned again.

Olivia climbed up.

In the morning the night diaper had done its job,

and nobody said a word about it.

Olivia put on her duck pants

and ran out to help with the eggs.

Grandpa winked at the Duchess.
