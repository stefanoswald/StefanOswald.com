Night 95

Will Mama Still Love Me

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Bedtime. The lamp turned low. Then, out of the quiet, very small:

"Mama?"

"Mm?"

"When baby come," she said, and stopped.

Mama waited.

"You still love me?"

Mama did not hurry. She brought two candles from the kitchen and set them on the dresser, well away from the bed. Olivia sat on her hands, which is the rule with fire.

Mama lit the first one. "This is my love for you."

She tipped the second wick into the first flame, and it caught.

"This one is for the baby. Look at yours."

Olivia looked hard. "Same."

"Same bright," said Mama. "Love multiplies."

Two flames stood side by side, and neither one was smaller.

"Two brights," Olivia whispered.

They blew them out together. Whoo.

Two thin curls of smoke went up.
