Night 58

Ready!

Month 2: Me Do It

Time to go out.

Papa stood at the door with the umbrella.

"Boots first," said Papa. "Then socks."

"No! Socks."

"Socks first. Then the coat on your head."

"No. Floor."

Coat on the floor. Collar at her toes. Arms. Up.

On, by her.

"Remember the first sock?" said Mama.

"Sock on hand."

"On your hand. Then on your foot."

Boots. Right feet, this time.

"And the milk?" said Papa. "Who pours it now?"

"Me."

"Who spilled the whole jug?"

"Me. Do-over."

"Do-over," said Mama. "And the buttons that day. All wrong."

Olivia looked down at her coat, which has no buttons,

and laughed anyway.

"And mushrooms?"

"No fank you."

The doorknob. New this week.

Reach, both hands, turn, pull.

The door came open on the wet lane.

"Ready!"

She went out first, under the front edge of the umbrella,

and Papa came after with the rest of it,

one slow step at a time.
