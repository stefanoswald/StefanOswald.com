Night 311

The New Kid at the Blanket

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Story time at the Lantern Library.

Everybody sat on the big blue blanket.

Everybody but one.

A new girl stood at the edge,

holding her mama's hand.

Her mama had a round tummy,

the way Mama did before Theo.

The new girl looked at the blanket.

She did not sit.

Olivia saw.

Lily saw.

They looked at each other.

Olivia scooted over.

Lily scooted the other way.

Now there was a spot in the middle.

Olivia patted it, twice.

The new girl looked up at her mama.

Her mama let go of her hand.

She sat down in the spot,

touching nobody.

In a hole in the wall, Molly Mouse was listening too.

The story was about a goose who lost her mitten.

By the end, the new girl's knee was touching Olivia's knee.

"What your name?" said Lily.

"June."

"Me Olivia. Dat Wiwi."

"LILY," said Lily.

June's mama let out a long breath.
