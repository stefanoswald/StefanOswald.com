Night 183

Watching Everything

Month 7: Potty Power

These days, nobody went to the bathroom alone.

Papa went.

Olivia went too.

"Papa potty?"

"Papa potty," said Papa, who had hoped for a minute alone.

Mama went.

Olivia stood in the doorway.

"Where it go?"

"Down the pipes."

"Den where?"

"To a place that cleans the water."

"Gampa potty?"

"Everybody goes," said Mama.

"Duchess?"

"Hens just go wherever they are."

Olivia thought about the Duchess for a long time.

Then the cat walked by,

on its way to its box.

Olivia followed.

She sat down to watch.

The cat sat down too.

It looked at Olivia.

Olivia looked at the cat.

Nobody went.

A cat does not care for an audience.

It walked off, tail up.

After supper, while Mama was giving Olivia her bath,

it came back.
