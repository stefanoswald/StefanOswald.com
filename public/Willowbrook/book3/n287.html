Night 287

Papa's Broken Shelf

Month 10: Try Again

Papa built a shelf in the garage.

Wood. Brackets. The level with the bubble in it.

He put the paint cans on top.

CRASH.

Shelf, brackets, cans, all on the floor.

"GAH!" said Papa.

Olivia came to the door.

"What dat? Why it fall? Papa, why?"

"Not NOW, Olivia!"

Her face went small.

She backed up against the door.

Papa sat down on the cold floor.

"Hold on. I'm going to breathe."

Flower in. Dandelion out.

Then he held out his hand.

"I was loud at you. I'm sorry."

She came and sat by him.

"Why it fall?"

"Let's look," said Papa.

They looked.

"The brackets were too far apart," said Papa.

"The middle sagged."

"More brackets?" said Olivia.

"Let's try three."

Up went the shelf again, three brackets this time.

Papa put one can on it.

Then all of them.

It held.

By supper the cat was asleep on it.
