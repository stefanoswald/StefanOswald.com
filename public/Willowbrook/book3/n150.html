Night 150

Papa Tells It Wrong

Month 5: Theo Arrives

At the end of the month all four of them lay on the big bed

in the long evening light.

"I'll tell the Theo story," said Papa.

"First thing you saw was the baby."

"NO! Duck!"

"Right. Then he stopped crying for Mama."

"For ME. Me talked."

"Then he grabbed my nose."

"My FINGER."

"Then he smiled at me."

"Wind," said Olivia.

Mama laughed so hard that Theo bounced.

They told the rest together.

The sock under the couch.

The hiccups on Lily's pillow.

The day he waved all done, sort of.

"And the green pinch?" said Papa.

"Me told Mama."

"And there was a spot by me," said Mama. "There still is."

Papa held up one finger for Olivia and one for Theo.

"Still two brights?"

Olivia checked both fingers.

"Two brights. Same big."

Theo yawned, which was his part.
