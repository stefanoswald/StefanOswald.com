Night 162

Six Weeks of Him

Month 6: Big Sister School

Six weeks of Theo.

In this family, a muffin marks a thing.

So there was a muffin, with no candle.

Candles wait for the whole year.

"What can Theo do now?" said Papa.

Olivia held up one finger.

"Smile. At me."

Two fingers.

"Say goo."

Three.

"Look at fan."

Four.

"Cry LOUD."

Theo, in Papa's arms, did not argue.

There was one finger left.

Olivia looked at it.

"Poop BIG."

Papa sniffed.

"True," he said, and went to get a diaper.

Mama cut the muffin in three.

The candle stayed in the drawer.
