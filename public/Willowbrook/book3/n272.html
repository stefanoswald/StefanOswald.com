Night 272

Five Before Bed

Month 9: Counting to Five

"Baba wants five stories," said Olivia.

"Tell him the ducks," said Mama.

"One went under!"

"And came up with a weed hat," said Papa.

"Tell him the berries."

"Wiwi got big half."

"You gave it to her," said Mama.

"The mitten?"

Olivia squeezed her own fingers, one at a time.

"Fumb hid."

"Grandpa's eggs?"

"Free eggs. Plenty."

"The park gate?"

Olivia held up five fingers at Mama, and Papa laughed.

Somewhere in there she stopped counting.

She told Baba about the caterpillar under the leaves,

and Theo rolling over on free,

and the crunchy frost, and Lily behind the sticks,

and Molly keeping the bear book warm,

and Theo's wet high five, and the duck trade,

and the stairs that never stop.

Baba, who is number five in this family, did not mind.

"And the fifth kiss?" said Mama.

Olivia pointed down the hall.

"Fee-oh's."

Mama carried it down the hall.
