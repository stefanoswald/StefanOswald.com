Night 224

The Quiet Snack

Month 8: Tantrum Weather

Four o'clock is a hard hour in every house in Willowbrook.

The blocks were wrong.

Duck was wrong.

The window was wrong, for having rain on it.

"No, no, NO."

Mama didn't ask what was wrong.

She looked at the clock.

Lunch was a long, long time ago.

She didn't say so.

She cut an apple into thin moons,

put a thin slice of cheese beside them,

and set the plate on the low table,

not too close.

Olivia whined at the plate.

Then she sat down and ate a moon.

Then the cheese.

Then another moon, crunch, crunch.

The whine went somewhere else.

Duck was fine again.

The blocks were fine.

The rain was fine, even, and good for watching.

"More moons," said Olivia.
