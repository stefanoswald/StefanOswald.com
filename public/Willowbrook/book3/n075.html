Night 75

The Kiss on the Bandage

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

The park was green all over,

and two girls were running through it.

A root. A toe. Down.

Lily's knee had a scrape with real red on it,

and Lily had real crying to go with it.

Mama knelt with the little kit from her bag.

She washed the scrape with soap and water,

gently, while Lily held on to her sleeve,

and then she put the bandage on.

Olivia had been watching the whole time.

She sat down in the grass next to Lily.

She took Lily's leg into her lap, all of it.

She bent over, slow and serious,

and kissed the bandage.

"On the bandage," said Mama. "Not the scrape."

"Me know."

Pat, pat.

"Wiwi okay?"

Lily sniffed. She looked at her knee.

"Not all."

So they did not run again.

They walked to the swings,

one girl limping grandly,

the other one holding her sleeve.
