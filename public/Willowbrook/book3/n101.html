Night 101

The Ultrasound Picture

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Mama and Papa came home from the doctor with a photograph.

"The baby's first picture!" said Papa.

Olivia looked. She looked closer. She turned it sideways. It was grey fuzz.

"Dat not baby."

"That's the head," said Papa, pointing. "That's a hand."

The fuzz did not get any better.

Olivia studied it. "Baby look like oatmeal."

Papa had to sit down.

So Mama found Olivia's baby book and opened it to the first page. Grey fuzz there too.

"Dat me?"

"That's you."

Olivia looked at the fuzz. Then at her own arms, her legs, her good strong feet.

"Me was oatmeal?"

"Beautiful oatmeal," said Mama.

Olivia gave the new picture one pat. "Grow, baby."

The oatmeal went on the fridge.
