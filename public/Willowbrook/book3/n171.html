Night 171

The Two Chairs

Month 6: Big Sister School

Sunday evening at Honeywell Farm.

On the porch stood the two small chairs,

Olivia's, and the new one with the same star.

Olivia sat in hers.

Grandpa Joe sat in his rocker

with Theo in the crook of his arm.

The new chair was empty.

Olivia looked at it.

"Fee-oh too little."

Grandpa nodded.

She climbed down.

She fetched Duck from the bag

and sat her in Theo's chair,

very straight.

"Duck sit. For Fee-oh."

Grandpa looked at Duck.

He lifted his straw hat, just a little.

"Evening," he said.

Olivia climbed back into her chair.

The swallows went round the barn.

The rocker creaked.

Theo's eyes closed against the red flannel.

After that, the porch was quiet.

When it was time to go,

Olivia took Duck home.

Grandpa winked at the empty chair.
