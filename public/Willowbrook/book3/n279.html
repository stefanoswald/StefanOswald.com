Night 279

The Downhill Secret

Month 10: Try Again

On a dry cold day Papa wheeled the trike to the park.

The path by the kite hill slopes, just a little.

Just enough.

"New plan," said Papa. "Just ride."

Helmet on, Olivia put her feet on the pedals.

Papa gave the trike a nudge.

The little hill turned the wheels.

The wheels turned the pedals.

The pedals turned her feet,

round and round.

"My feet going round!"

Papa walked her back up.

Down again. And again.

His hand stayed near the seat.

At the bottom the path went flat,

and the hill stopped helping.

Her feet kept going.

Round. Round. Round.

All by themselves.

"Papa! Me doing it!"

"Your feet know the circle now," said Papa.

She rode to the bench.

She rode back.

Her cheeks matched the trike.
