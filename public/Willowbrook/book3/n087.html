Night 87

Asking Mama

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

At dinner there was the feeling check,

one feeling each, then the bread.

"Excited," said Papa.

"Tired," said Mama. "And excited."

Then it was Olivia's turn.

She put down her spoon.

She put the tiny sock on the table.

"Baby?"

Mama and Papa looked at each other.

Then Mama took Olivia's hand

and laid it on the round tummy.

"Yes. There's a baby growing in there."

"Your brother or your sister," said Papa.

Olivia looked at the tummy for a long time.

She lifted the edge of Mama's shirt

and found the belly button.

"Baby door?"

"Not exactly," said Papa.

"Baby come?"

"When the strawberries come," said Mama.

Olivia likes strawberries very much.

She looked at the window,

in case they were coming now.

Then she ate her bread

with one hand on the tummy,

just in case.
