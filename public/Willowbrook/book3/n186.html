Night 186

Accidents Are Just Rain

Month 7: Potty Power

Two days later, Olivia was busy.

Duck needed her lunch.

The puzzle needed its last piece.

Her body said something,

and Olivia was too busy to hear.

Then her pants were wet.

Her socks were wet.

Her face crumpled.

"Uh-oh. Uh-oh."

Mama got down low.

No sigh.

"Wet pants," she said, and off they came.

"Dry pants," and on they went.

"All done."

Olivia looked at the window.

Rain was running down it.

"Me rain," she said.

"A little rain," said Mama. "It dries."

"Papa wet?"

Papa put his head round the door.

"Once. On Grandpa's tractor."

Olivia's mouth fell open.

She went back to the puzzle in dry socks.
