Night 319

The Kindness Detectives

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

On the walk, Mama made up a game.

"Find somebody helping."

Olivia looked hard.

"Dere!"

A big boy was pulling his little sister up the lane on a sled.

"Helping," said Mama.

"Dere!"

The mail carrier set a box up on a porch, out of the snow.

"Helping."

"Dere!"

Somebody had shoveled Mrs. Henderson's walk,

right up to her door.

"Who?"

Mama didn't know.

Papa's shovel leaned on their own porch.

It wasn't telling.

"Dere! Da gold dog!"

The gold dog was asleep in the sun.

"Is he helping?" said Mama.

Olivia squinted at him.

"Him keeping da sun warm."

Then Olivia's boot slid on the ice,

and Mama's hand held her up.

Olivia looked at the hand.

She pointed at Mama.

"Dere. Helping."

Mama didn't let go till the porch.
