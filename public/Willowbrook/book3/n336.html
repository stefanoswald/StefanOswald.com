Night 336

The Door Frame

Month 12: Almost Three

Olivia backed up to the kitchen door frame.

Papa took the pencil from the drawer.

"Heels down."

"Dey down."

They were not down.

Then they were.

Papa laid a book flat on her curls.

Scritch went the pencil.

She stepped away to look.

There was last year's line, the almost-two one.

And there was the new one,

a whole hand higher.

"Dat space me?"

"All of it," said Mama.

"Soup and sleep and a lot of climbing."

Then it was Theo's turn.

Mama set him on the floor by the frame.

He sat up straight all by himself,

no hands, no pillow behind him,

and looked very pleased about it.

Scritch.

A tiny line, down low, where the cat could reach it.

"Fee-oh mark!"

"La," said Theo. "La-la."

Papa wrote THEO by the low line and OLIVIA by the high one.

Then he put the pencil back in the drawer.
