Night 1

The Feeling with No Name

Month 1: Big Feelings Have Names

Olivia's banana broke in half.

Then her sock went twisty.

Then her cup tipped over.

Something hot filled her up,

toes to hair.

Her fists went tight.

Her face went crumpled.

She did not have a word for it.

Mama knelt down on the kitchen floor.

She did not say stop.

She did not say shhh.

"I'm here," she said.

The loud part boomed.

Then it wobbled.

Then it went small and hiccupy,

and two arms came up.

Mama picked her up.

"The banana broke," she said. "You wanted it whole."

"Whole," said Olivia, into Mama's shoulder.

The banana was still in two pieces.

The sock was still twisty.

Olivia ate the big half first.
