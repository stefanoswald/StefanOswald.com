Night 280

Ring the Bell

Month 10: Try Again

The first snow came in the night.

In the morning Papa shoveled the sidewalk

all the way to Mateo's.

Then Olivia rode it, helmet on,

Papa walking one step behind.

Real pedaling. Nobody pushing.

Past every porch, ringing the bell.

Bring-bring! at Mrs. Henderson's.

Mrs. Henderson waved her shovel.

Bring-bring! at Mateo's.

Mateo knocked on his window.

Bring-bring! at the gold dog's fence.

The gold dog walked along beside her,

his side of the fence, her side of the snow.

At home, Mama was on the porch with Theo,

round as a snowball in his snowsuit.

Papa clapped.

Theo's arms went up and down,

and he squealed, the squeal he saves for her.

Olivia stopped at the steps.

She rang the bell just for him.

Bring-bring.

Theo squealed right back.
