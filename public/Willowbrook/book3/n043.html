Night 43

The Out-of-Bed Discovery

Month 2: Me Do It

Night three of the big bed.

Mama and Papa on the couch. The house quiet.

Too quiet.

Small feet in the hall.

And in the doorway, Olivia,

bear under one arm,

as if she were only passing through.

"Hi."

The big bed has no walls.

She has just found that out.

Papa walked her back. Tuck. Kiss.

Ten minutes.

"Hi."

Mama walked her back. Tuck. Kiss.

Ten minutes.

"Hi."

"BED, Olivia."

That was Papa, too loud.

Her lip went. The bear went up to her face.

Papa got down on the hall floor.

"That was loud. Sorry."

He waited until the lip came back.

Then he walked her, slow, no words,

and tucked her, and kissed the bear as well.

There was no fourth hi.

The hallway is dark

and has no bear in it.

The bed does.
