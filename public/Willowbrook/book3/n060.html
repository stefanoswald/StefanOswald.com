Night 60

The Cookie at the Grocery Store

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

Tuesday was grocery day.

The store had lilacs in a bucket by the door,

and the cart had a seat with a strap,

and Olivia rode in it, strapped.

Mama had the list.

Olivia had the pointing.

"Nana!" Bananas.

"Dat!" Beans.

"Dat!" More beans.

At the bakery counter a man in a paper hat

leaned over the glass with a cookie on a napkin.

"For the lady in the cart," he said.

Olivia looked at Mama. Mama nodded.

Both hands. Big bite.

"So good!"

"You're happy," said Mama. "Maybe."

And she pushed on toward the apples.

The cookie was sweet.

It was also the kind of cookie that goes straight to the legs.

By the last crumb Olivia was bouncing in the seat.

"Me walk. Me push."

"Hand on the cart," said Mama, "or hand in mine?"

"Cart!"

Out of the strap, down to the floor.

One sticky hand on the cart.
