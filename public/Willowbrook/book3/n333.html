Night 333

Down the Lane in the Sled

Month 11: Kindness Is a Muscle

Before supper, Papa pulled Olivia down the lane in the sled.

The porch lights were coming on.

"Who held the market door?" said Papa.

"Me. Cat no say fank you."

At Mrs. Henderson's: "Noodle one. Now she better."

At Mateo's: "Chain fell off. Me got his mama."

"And his tool belt," said Papa.

At the gold dog's fence she didn't ask to race.

She got out and walked beside the sled, slow,

and the gold dog walked too.

"Remember your cups?"

"Da baby wear one."

"And Lily's snowman?"

"Me told." She giggled.

"June at story time?"

"Me patted da spot."

Then, quieter, "Me pushed June."

"You did," said Papa. "Then what?"

"Sorry. And da red shovel."

"And the bench with Lily?"

"Me just sat."

The Duchess's feather was waiting on her shelf at home.

Theo's spoon was drying by the sink.

Back on their own steps, Olivia pressed her chest.

"Bigger?"

Papa pressed his mitten there too.

"Feels bigger."
