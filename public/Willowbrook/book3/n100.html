Night 100

Bedtime in the Daylight

Month 4: Waiting for Baby

Bedtime came. The dark did not.

The sun was still up, bright as lunch, pushing in around the curtains. A robin sat on the sill outside and sang as if it were morning.

"Not night!" said Olivia. "Sun up!"

"The sun stays up late in summer," said Papa.

"Why?"

Papa thought about it. "He's allowed. He's very old."

"Me old too!"

"Not that old."

He drew the curtains tight. The room went dim, with gold along the edges.

Then he sang the goodnight song.

"Twinkle, twinkle, little sun."

"No! STAR!"

"Twinkle, twinkle, little moon?"

"STAR, Papa!"

"I don't see any stars," said Papa, which was fair.

Olivia checked the gap in the curtains. No stars. Just gold.

So he sang it right, slowly, all the way through.

The robin sang on without her.
