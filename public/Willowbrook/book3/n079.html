Night 79

The Quiet Kind of Together

Month 3: A Friend Called Lily

One afternoon the house went quiet.

No shrieks. No crashes. No "mine."

In the kitchen the mamas looked at each other

and went very softly down the hall to see.

On the couch: two girls, two books.

Olivia had the bear book.

Lily had the one with the tractor.

Nobody was reading out loud.

Nobody was showing anybody anything.

Olivia turned her pages in big clumps.

Lily turned hers back to the tractor, every time.

Four feet stuck out over the edge of the couch.

Sometimes a foot bumped a foot,

and nobody minded.

The mamas backed out of the doorway,

the way you back away from a sleeping cat.

It lasted ten whole minutes.

Then Olivia sat up.

"Wiwi! Look! Button!"

Lily looked.

It was a very good button.

Then they went back to their own books.
