Night 173

Art for His Wall

Month 6: Big Sister School

Olivia looked around the yellow room.

"No ducks," she said.

This was a problem.

So she made one at the kitchen table,

with the fat crayons and her tongue out.

Yellow. Roundish.

One big black eye.

Three legs.

"Him running."

Mama found a real frame.

Art is art.

"Where?" said Mama.

Olivia pointed low,

right beside the changing table,

where Theo lies on his back and looks at the wall

while his diaper gets done.

Up it went.

Next morning came the showing.

Theo on the changing table,

Mama's hand on his tummy.

Olivia on the step stool beside him.

"Dat duck. Him running."

Theo looked at the duck.

He looked at the black eye and did not look away.

"Say duck."

Theo did not say duck.

"Duck," she told him,

every diaper after that.
