Night 156

The Hard Share

Month 6: Big Sister School

Bobby Bear was on the play mat.

Olivia had left him there, just for a minute.

Theo's hand flapped,

and found the floppy ear,

and shut.

"NO! Mine! Baba MINE!"

Olivia pulled.

Theo held.

She pulled harder.

Theo startled and wailed,

and Olivia wailed too.

Mama got down on the floor.

"He grabbed Baba. You want him back."

She opened Theo's fingers, one at a time.

The ear came free.

Olivia held Bobby Bear tight.

Theo went on crying.

Olivia watched him.

Nobody asked her for anything.

She went to her low shelf.

She came back with Duck.

She held Duck for a long moment.

Then she put Duck's knitted foot

into Theo's hand.

His fist shut on it.

He stopped.

"Duck for Fee-oh," she said.

"Baba for me."

She sat down close with Bobby Bear,

where she could keep an eye on Duck's foot.
