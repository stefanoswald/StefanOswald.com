Night 341

Spring Comes Back Around

Month 12: Almost Three

All night the roof went drip, drip, drip.

In the morning the icicles were shorter.

"Let's go see the brook," said Mama.

Theo rode in the carrier on her front,

bundled up, with his face peeking out.

Olivia walked in her boots, through slush,

holding Mama's hand.

Since the first snow, the brook had been white and still.

Now it was talking.

Under the ice, something went glug.

Then crack.

Then a whole plate of ice tipped up and slid away,

and the water ran out from under it, dark and fast.

"Brook unstuck!"

"It does this every year," said Mama.

"Right before your birthday."

"For me?"

"You were born on a day like this."

From the path, Olivia threw a stick in.

The brook took it, bump, bump, gone.

She threw one more, for Theo.

Theo slept through all of it, in his hat.
