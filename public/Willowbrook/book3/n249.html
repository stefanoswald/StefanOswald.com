Night 249

The Five-Block Tower

Month 9: Counting to Five

Olivia built a tower of the big blocks.

One. Two. Free.

Four took the tip of her tongue.

Five went on at full stretch.

Theo, on his tummy, watched it go up.

Then he reached with both hands,

farther than anybody knew he could reach.

Crash.

"NO, Fee-oh!"

Olivia shoved the blocks away, hard, and one bumped his hand.

"OLIVIA!" Mama was down on the rug fast, too loud.

Theo cried. Olivia cried louder.

Mama picked Theo up and took one slow breath.

Then she sat close to Olivia.

"I yelled. I'm sorry."

Olivia leaned on her.

"My tower."

"He wrecked your five," said Mama. "You were furious."

"MAD."

"You can be mad at him. Blocks don't go at him."

They built the next one on the far side of the coffee table,

out of reach.

Theo watched from Mama's lap, reaching anyway.
